# w2c-controller@.service — a subsystem's controller, on every server

**Role in the module.** Lesson 10. A template, one instance per subsystem of the catalogue: `w2c-controller@vms` (the cameras' placement), `w2c-controller@rec` (the recordings', one recorder per server by default, `rec/policy`). `w2c-run.sh controller %i` — the platform's `SpecController` from the spec, a platform process (ADR 0023) — as `w2c`, joining `w2c-%icontroller` (the role keeps the spec's name `<sub>controller`; its socket's group is the platform's): placement and nothing else; and `w2c-store` (the heartbeats it reads, its snapshot, objects on this server), `UMask=0007`, `ProtectSystem=strict`. One on every server, safe at several (every write a CAS); it starts no process — it publishes numbers and offers, and `w2c-spares.sh` starts. Its settings beyond its own lines come from `/etc/w2c/w2c.env` and `/etc/vms/vms.env`, read by `w2c-run.sh` UNDER what the unit says (no `EnvironmentFile=`: systemd would let a file override `Environment=`). `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=2`: a crash is systemd's to restart under the same name, on this server.

## Notes
- `tests/cluster/test_units.py`: each unit (an instance's, `%i` put in) names its role's socket (`PLATFORM_STORE=configstore:///run/configstore/<role>.sock`), joins the group the rights file gives that role, runs `w2c-run.sh <verb>`, comes after `configstore.service`, as `w2c` (a platform process) or `vms` (a subsystem's).
