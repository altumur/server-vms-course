# configstore-rights.json — the store's rights, generated from the spec and committed

**Role in the module.** Lesson 5. What `configstore` asks before anything is forwarded or applied: for each role — each socket `/run/configstore/<role>.sock` — its `group` (who may open the socket: `vms-<role>` for the VMS's processes, `w2c-<role>` for the platform's — the console's is `w2c-console`, ADR 0014) and the keys and prefixes it may `read`, `write` and `delete` (a trailing `*` a prefix, a leading `!` a denial; the product's format). Installed as `/etc/w2c/configstore-rights.json` (`install.sh`, on the data partition at `/data/platform/etc`) and read by the daemon at start (`-rights`).

Never edited by hand: `SPEC_DIR=Source/vms python3 -m w2cplatform.cluster rights > deploy/cluster/configstore-rights.json` (`w2cplatform/cluster/rights.py`, where each grant is explained), and `tests/cluster/test_policies.py` fails the day the file and the generator disagree, a write or read the stand makes is not granted, or a grant is one the code's lists do not ask for. The roles: `console` (it also makes the cluster's door key: `door/signer`, which it alone reads, and `door/keys`, which every worker reads), `resource`, a `<sub>controller` and a `<sub>worker` for every spec the catalogue loads, and М12's `domainagent` and `member`.
