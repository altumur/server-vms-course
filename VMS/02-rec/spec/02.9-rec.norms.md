# V/02.9 — Recording on the domain: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`02.9-rec.norms.md`](../02.9-rec.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 9 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../02-rec.md),
[instructions](../02-rec.README.md), manifest [`rec.subsystem.yaml`](../../rec.subsystem.yaml). The platform — the export `cammeta-specs`
`7a875c75` (the camera and the stream in the calls of the receiver — `b41527f`).

A domain is several clusters (P/08). A camera lives in its own cluster (a camera-cluster is a cluster to itself, V/01.6 Н1.5), but
another cluster records it: data cross between clusters, work does not. The chapter sets the norms of the recording side: the recording in the domain catalogue
and the assignment, the source of the recording of a foreign camera, the **receiver** — the door of the recorder into which a camera pushes the stream and through
which the recorder asks the camera for a range, several receivers of one cluster, the books `primaries` and `polls`, the reserve,
the **forwarder** of a relay to the centre and the silence witness `rec/polled`. The camera side (the contribution to the book `sources`, tokens,
the pusher) — [V/01.6](../../../01-cam/spec/01.6-cam.norms.md); the domain mechanisms (books, tokens, assignment, topology,
witness) — the platform, by reference.

Norm ids are `V/02.9-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

**Assignment.** Which cluster records a camera is the assignment `domain/placement/rec/<ref камеры>` (P/08.6 Н1.1), written by the operator of
the domain: `cluster` — the cluster of the primary recording, `origin` — the cluster of the camera, `copies` — the clusters of the backup recordings (`when:
offline` or the home on `edge`, chapter 6 Н1.4, Н1.7). The primary recording of a camera is written by one cluster. A transfer is a `PUT` of a new assignment record: the books
follow on the next pass (Н1.2), the recording of the previous cluster stops with a reason and stays with its archive until its retention expires
(the operator removes the row). The rows `rec/recordings/*` in the recording cluster are created by its operator (`cam: ref:<ref>`, `ref`);
the assignment creates no rows. **Limit.** While there is no row in the new cluster, nobody records the camera, and the reserve holds the ring: there are no
primaries, the transfer is a decision, not a failure (chapter 6 Н1.4). The operator's order — first the row in the new cluster, then `PUT`.

A recording of a foreign camera with a `ref` different from the `<ref>` in `cam` is not written: `waiting`, `reason` «ref differs from cam».

A recording of its own camera does not read the book `sources` (chapter 4 Н1.1) and stops by the assignment: its `ref` is assigned, and its own cluster is neither `cluster`
nor in `copies` — `waiting`, `reason` «assigned to another cluster». No assignment — the recording goes on: there is no transfer. **Limit:** the agent carries the record only to the named members (P/08.6 Н1.2), and `origin` is optional: a transfer without `origin` removes the assignment from the cluster of the camera, the recording there goes on, two clusters record the camera; the domain operator names `origin`. A former domain holder has no copy until the agent has received `carry`: a recording that stood in `assigned to another cluster` starts for these seconds: the same window as `contributes`. The assignment does not read — unread, not absent: a running recording is kept, one not started is not started (`waiting`, `reason` «assignment unreadable»), log, the next pass reads again.

The primary and the backup recording of one `ref` at the executors of the assignment (`cluster` and `copies`; `origin` is not an executor) are not
a conflict of the domain view ([P/08.1](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.1-domain.norms.md) Н3; ADR-0091 addendum (12), (13), (15)); a row
without `ref` is neither a unit nor a claim; an edit through the domain by `ref` at two executors without `?cluster=` — 409 `executors` with
a listing (P/08.6).

### Н1.2 The source of the recording of a foreign camera

A recording with `cam: ref:<ref>` takes the source from the book `domain/cam/sources` of its own cluster (a copy at a member, the row of its own cluster
at the domain holder — Н1; the contribution — V/01.6 Н1.2; the book — [P/08.5 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md)).
The book is read **once per pass** for all such recordings.

- Between clusters the stream goes only by pushing into the receiver of the recording cluster: the RTSP of the delivery is the loopback of the holder's server
  (V/01.2 Н1.3), a foreign loopback is not a source (chapter 4 Н1.1). The source of the recording is its own receiver (`ingest://ref:<ref>` — the key of the foreign camera, the main stream; Н1.3);
  `live_url` of the book element is for a recorder on the holder's server of the same cluster and for it only.
- The cluster of the camera pushes (V/01.6 Н1.4): a camera-cluster — by its holder; a server camera — by **the recorder of the cluster of
  the camera** on the server of its holder: the card recorder, if the camera has a recording on the volume `edge` of this server (chapter 6 Н1.7), it holds the card and is heard within `PEER_LOST_AFTER`; otherwise the first by slot name among the recorders of this server heard within
  `PEER_LOST_AFTER` = 30 s. It reads the holder's frames from the share `cam.shm` (chapter 4 Н1.1), audio — from the `mic` endpoints of the holder (Н1.3а), and pushes them by the same protocol
  as a camera (Н2), with the token `stream` (V/01.6 Н1.3, `grant: [camworker, recworker]`), to the receivers from the book `primaries`
  (Н1.6), — and into the receiver of **its own** cluster while that receiver wants it (a gateway or a recorder of another server, Н1.3), with the role leaf
  `recworker` without a token; into its own receiver — every camera stream that the receiver wants (`stream` of the poll answer, Н1.3 «Camera and stream»), each from its own share `cam.shm`; the gateway of its own server takes the RTSP of the loopback itself (V/03.2). No recorder on the holder's server — nobody
  pushes the camera (placement `near`, V/01.1). A scenario request from the poll answer (V/07.5 Н1.4) it submits to the holder as
  a request of `cam` — a submission by a worker (`worker.requests: [cam]`, P/03.6 Н1.5), the name by the submitter's key `rec.ask-<firing>-<i>`
  (`firing` — the firing key from the request, V/07.5 Н1.4; `i` — the action number, 0 for the scenario of a document;
  `<подсистема подателя>.<ключ>`, [request contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/request-mark.md) `rid`; ADR-0091 addendum (23) item 2):
  a repeat of the same firing is the same row, through another receiver and after a restart of the receiver as well (`again` by `unit` and
  `digest` of the kind `worker`); the outcome — by the next poll: the holder's outcome in `outcomes` as is — `performed`, `refused: <error>`,
  `expired`, `unknown`; a refusal of the submission — `refused: <почему>`.
- There is no row of its own cluster at the domain holder yet (the window after a transfer, no assembly: P/08.5 Н1.3 `contributes`) — the book is not assembled, rather than empty: what was read last since start is kept, no reason.
- The camera is not in the book, the element is out of form, the copy does not read — the recording is `waiting`, `reason` in words: «not in the source book», «source book entry unreadable», «source book unreadable», not «camera held by nobody». The store is silent —
  what was read last is kept.
- The domain is unreachable — the recording goes on by the last carried copy; the age of the element (`now − as_of`) is in the recording status.
  A camera that changed its address while the domain is unreachable waits for the domain to return (an accepted refusal).
- The camera left the book (a transfer) — the recording stops, `reason` «not in the source book».

**The `waiting` reason of a recording of a foreign camera** is the first fulfilled condition from the top; the words are a contract:

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| the `ref` of the recording ≠ the `<ref>` in `cam` (Н1.1) | `ref differs from cam` |
| the copy of the book `sources` (at the domain holder — the row of its own cluster) does not read | `source book unreadable` |
| there is no element `<ref>` in the book | `not in the source book` |
| the element `<ref>` is out of form | `source book entry unreadable` |

### Н1.3 The receiver

Each recorder holds a **receiver** — the door into which cameras push the stream (Н2). The address is in the heartbeat `ingest` (a string,
`heartbeat.strings`); the address rule and the limits — [P/03.4 Н1.7](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)
`door_address`, `door_limits`; the lesson constants (`door_limits.other`): `CONNECTIONS` = 256, `PER_ADDRESS` = 64, `PACE_FLOOR` does not
apply to the stream and to holding the poll (numbers without a basis: checked by measurement after the first generation). The receiver is the route `ingest` of the recorder door: `door.routes {name: ingest, role: [recworker,
liveworker, detworker], domain: [stream, poll, upstream, ask]}` (P/02.1; the kinds are declared in `domain.tokens` of the manifests `cam`,
`rec`). The joint between servers and clusters is HTTP by Н2 (a norm); the local joint of one server (a subscriber or a pusher on
the receiver's server) — «Implementation decides», like the frame share (V/01.2 Н1.3).
The receiver has no row of its own. A camera pushes to the receiver of a cluster, not to a process: a zombie recorder receives nothing,
moving a recorder is the cluster's business.

**Admission** — P/03.4 Н1.7 `door_admission` `role_domain`. The processes of its own cluster — a role leaf (class `mixed`): `recworker` (a neighbour, Н1.5;
the pusher of a server camera, Н1.2), `liveworker` (the gateway subscription, V/03.2), `detworker` (the detector subscription, V/04.2); without a kit — only the loopback, calls by role —
401 `no_role`. A camera-cluster, a pusher and a forwarder of another cluster — without a leaf, a domain token `Authorization: Bearer` of the kind
`stream` (stream, claims), `poll` (a poll without a stream, Н1.7), `upstream` (forwarder, Н1.8) or `ask` (scenario request, V/07) —
one call of the function `token` by the platform (`cluster` — its own; `expect.keep` — the kinds of the route whose catalogue manifest holds `keep: true`: `stream` is taken from `cam`, not from `rec` (P/03.4 Н1.7 `keep`)); the platform passes the token claims to the handler: the receiver checks `ref`
against the camera of the call. A client of another cluster verifies the receiver's server as a peer of a member of the assignment by
`Entry.ca` of the row `domain/member-list/<раздел>` (P/03.4 Н1.7 `cross_cluster`; `worker.reads` `cam`, `rec`, `auto`); there is no foreign section — the peer client requests it itself (the peer contract `client`). Refusals — each with its own reason (Н4): a foreign camera, a foreign cluster
(`wrong_audience`), an expired or revoked token (401), a foreign role (`wrong_role`); no live handler — 503 `no_handler`.
The token carries no rights (ADR-0031).

**Camera and stream** (V/01.1 Н1.1а). **The camera key** at the receiver is like the field `cam` of a recording and of a delivery: its own camera — its local
id (`<cam>`; `ref` of its own camera is optional and may be empty at many, V/01.1 Н1.1), a camera of another cluster —
`ref:<ref>`. Inside a cluster the subscription and the push (Н2) carry the key of its own camera `cam` and `stream` — the id of the stream that is needed
(for the gateway — the subscription stream, V/03.2; for the detector — its `stream`, V/04.2; for a recorder of another server — the recording stream, chapter 4
Н1.1); empty — the main one; the poll carries `cam`, and its answer — the state of each needed stream (`stream`, `push`, `have`).
The receiver keeps the wish, the poll answer, the tee, the pre-recording ring and the subscribers by the pair (camera key, `stream`): two
cameras without `ref` are two keys, the main stream of a camera and its grid stream are two streams of the receiver, the frames of one are not given
to the other. The clock offset belongs to the camera (one device): by the camera key, common to all its streams. **Between clusters** —
`ref` and only the main stream (V/01.6 Н1.1): calls by a domain token (`stream`, `poll`, `upstream`, `ask`) carry `ref`,
a non-empty `stream` is a refusal (Н4); at the receiver this is the key `ref:<ref>`, the stream is empty; the recording and the delivery of a foreign camera take its
main stream.

**Wish.** A camera stream is needed: by a recorder of continuous recording — while the recording is subscribed; by a recording on event — for its window;
by a viewer — while the gateway is subscribed (the call «subscription», Н2; V/03.2) and another `LINGER` = 10 s after unsubscribing. A wish at any receiver of the cluster is a wish at all of them (Н1.5).
Expired wishes are swept out and wake the held poll.

**The poll answer** (Н2) carries: `version`; `push` — whether the stream is needed; `audio` — the `mic` numbers needed by the subscribers of the stream (Н1.3а); `have` — up to where the recorders of the cluster **have recorded** the stream:
by the pair (camera key, `stream`) — the minimum of `written_through` (the field of the recording heartbeat, converted to the camera clock) over the recordings
of the cluster of this stream that **must** be written now (`enabled`, `until` — 0 or not passed; the reserve `when: offline` in
`standby` and the recording `replica`, chapter 6 Н1.9, do not take part); if such a recording has a heartbeat older than `LOST_AFTER` = 45 s, or the field is absent or out of form (the family
`rec-heartbeat-words`: unread, not absent) — there is no `have`; there are no due recordings —
there is no `have` (the camera sends everything it holds: the card is a ring, there is nothing to hold forever); at a relay — the smaller one together with the word
of the centre (`up_have`, Н1.8); the card debt — by `have` (V/01.6 Н1.4); a range claim (Н1.4); scenario requests (V/07); `uncovered: true` — a recording
must go, but here nobody takes it (only about cameras that push here according to the sources book; the held poll
rechecks at least once per 5 s). Version `-1` — answer at once; the same version — hold until the answer changes, no longer than
`POLL_HOLD` = 30 s and no longer than the nearest deadline of a wish or a claim over all receivers of the cluster. Versions start with the number
of the receiver start: a version of another start is answered at once.

**Frame time.** Every call of a camera tells its «now» (`camera_now`) and the road (round trip) of the previous call (`rtt`).
The offset «cluster clock − camera clock» is held as follows:

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| accuracy | `OFFSET_HOLD` = 0.25 s |
| down (the camera clock is ahead) | by any call, by frames: each new frame lowers the offset by no more than `OFFSET_SLEW` = ½ of the remainder to the target; while the stream goes towards the target, the offset is the target |
| up by less than `CLOCK_STEP` = 5 s | only if all calls within `OFFSET_RISE` = 30 s said so, at least `RISE_REQUESTS` = 3; by the smaller; one dissenting call removes it |
| up by more than `CLOCK_STEP` | at once, conditionally (the previous one alongside); `RISE_REQUESTS` agreeing — a step, firm, the count `clock_steps`, a log line; a dissenting one — the rise is removed by frames without a count |
| road | a rise of the offset explainable by a growth of `rtt` over the least is not counted: `proven = rise − max(0, rtt − rtt_least)`; a held poll measures once, on arrival |

The target is `now − camera_now` at the arrival of the call, without `rtt`. A call changes the offset only when it differs from the held one
by more than `OFFSET_HOLD`; on the descent the held one is the target, the frames go towards it by ½ of the remainder; the first word about the clock — the offset at once.

The receiver names a claim (Н1.4) only with a firm offset: during a conditional rise it waits; a claim named with the road of the first
call of the process fails (the recorder asks again). The pieces of the camera offset — the row `rec/ingest/clock/<ключ камеры>.json` (`<cam>` or `ref:<ref>`, Н1.3 «Camera and stream»)
`{v: 1, at, pieces}`: the newest `CLOCK_PIECES_KEPT` = 32 pieces `{from, srv_from, off_ms, old_off_ms?}` — whole ms: `srv_from` and
`at` by the cluster clock, `from` by the camera clock, a zero `old_off_ms` is not written; it is written on a change,
read once per camera at its first word about the clock; the newest restored piece is firm; a row out of form —
start anew, a count. The camera clock was set — the pieces before `CLOCK_FLOOR` (2000-01-01) go away, and so does a rise greater than `CLOCK_STEP` to whose
start the offset has returned.

**Frame order.** A frame later than the receiver clock by more than `FRAME_AHEAD` = 60 s or with a non-finite time — a refusal,
the count `ahead_losses`, a log line once; the bound stands on all inputs (camera push, forwarder, neighbour) and on
`written_through` from the heartbeat. A frame not newer than `have` is discarded. The tee: to recorders — all frames (the camera pre-recording ring
first), to viewers — the live edge; to a subscriber — only a frame newer than the last one given to it (otherwise `repeats_dropped`); a frame
further than the last one by `STREAM_JUMP` = 2 s **and** later than the receiver clock + 2 s is held back: the next frame within
`RING_AHEAD` = 10 s after it releases both, otherwise the held-back one is discarded (`ahead_losses`); a sparse stream (a frame once per
11–60 s) passes whole and at once. A sequence starts with a key frame.

**Recording from the receiver** (the source `ingest://<ключ камеры>[?stream=<id>]`, chapter 4: the key — Н1.3 «Camera and stream», `stream` —
the recording stream, none — the main one; for a foreign camera — `ingest://ref:<ref>`):
- start — a subscription and a wish forever, with the `audio` of the recording (Н1.3а; chapter 4 Н1.2а); stop — unsubscribe at once, without `LINGER`;
- it writes from a key frame; after a frame that the engine did not take — from the next key frame; `written_through` (cluster clock) —
  by closed blocks (chapter 4 Н1.8): taken is not yet written, an unclosed frame perishes with the disk, and it is not
  called written to the camera; the price — `have` lags by a flush, up to 15 s, the camera sends this tail again; before a heartbeat the recorder writes out
  what has arrived — the receiver answers the camera with the `have` from the heartbeat;
- not a single frame within `FIRST_FRAME_GRACE` = 20 s after start — `waiting`, `reason` `push_waiting`; the first frame — `running` under
  the same epoch; frames went and disappeared for `WATCHDOG` = 8 s — a work failure of the unit, a restart under **the same** epoch (chapter 4
  Н1.2; P/03.3 Н3 step 2);
- the reserve `when: offline` on the receiver writes what came, without the ring and without `waiting`: silence is the norm for it; no frames —
  `standby` (chapter 6 Н1.4), it does not take part in `have`; a frame came — `running`.

### Н1.3а Audio

ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 7. A frame of the kind `audio` (Н2) goes by all calls next to the video: push, subscription, neighbour, forwarder, pull, export. `t` is of the same line as the video frames of the camera: one offset, one conversion (Н1.3).

- **Which `mic`.** A subscriber names the number by the field `audio` of the subscription: the device number, 0 — the first `mic` of the camera (V/01.2 Н1.3, V/02.1 Н1.1). No field — there are no audio frames for it: the detector does not need them. The wish for a number lives with the wish for the stream, with the same `LINGER`; the poll answer lists the numbers in `audio` of the element of `streams`.
- **The pusher** sends the frames of the named numbers: a server camera — from the holder's endpoints `<live_url>/mic[/<n>]`, a camera-cluster — its own (V/01.6 Н1.4). The number 0 and the number of the first `mic` are one endpoint (`Content-Base` of the answer to `DESCRIBE`, V/01.2 Н1.3); the frames go once with the real `mic` and `first: true`, to the subscriber of 0 as well. Frames before the first RTCP Sender Report of the endpoint are not sent: `t` is the video line. The endpoint is not read, there is no `mic` — no frames, the video stream goes on; the next pass asks again.
- **The receiver and the forwarder** do not touch `sample` and `codec`: the subscriber transcodes (chapter 4 Н1.2а, V/03.4 Н1.5). The tee and the pre-recording ring hold audio frames next to the video; a subscriber gets only its number, the subscriber of 0 — the frames with `first`.
- **Audio does not hold the window.** The clock offset, `have`, `written_through`, `FIRST_FRAME_GRACE`, `WATCHDOG`, `STREAM_JUMP`, the cut to a key frame and the windows in frames (`FORWARD_FRAMES`, `PEER_BUFFER`, the 50 frames of neighbour lag) are judged by video frames only (`FIRST_FRAME_GRACE` at `no sound frames`, V/02.4, is a deadline for the audio word, not a window); the cut also throws away audio earlier than the cut frame. The limits in bytes (`FORWARD_BYTES`, `BODY`) count both kinds.
- **Order.** `FRAME_AHEAD` — as for video. `have` does not apply to an audio frame; a frame not newer than the last one given to the subscriber of the same number — `repeats_dropped`.

### Н1.4 A range claim through the receiver

Cameras without a playback door (pushing by themselves) give the card archive only by a claim: the recorder (catch-up recording, chapter 8;
copy, chapter 6) puts a claim `{rid, from, to, recording, piece, audio?}` on its receiver, the camera sees it in the poll answer and
exports it in pieces; for the other cameras of the box the card recorder answers in the same way — it is their pusher (chapter 6 Н1.7). The camera protocol — V/01.6 Н1.4; the receiver side:

- `piece` — the piece size, bytes; `rid` — the claim string; a poll answer carries one claim, the next one — after the previous one is closed;
- a piece carries `seq` and `more`; `PIECE_BYTES` = 1 MiB — the body of the call «export» whole (the door limit `BODY`, P/03.4 Н1.7;
  frames — base64, Н2); an empty export without `more` — the answer is whole; a piece out of order fails the claim; a repeat
  of the piece with `seq` = n − 1 is recognised as a repeat («taken already»); to each piece the receiver answers whether somebody else is still waiting for the range;
- `PIECE_BYTES` = 1 MiB; the waiting deadline is for the next piece: `POLL_ROUND` + `PIECE_BYTES` / `UPLINK_FLOOR` = 5 s + 1 MiB /
  64 KiB/s = 21 s, each arriving piece resets the count; expired — the claim has failed;
- a refusal of the camera (no card, it did not open, the segment was not read to the end) on any piece closes the claim, the arrived pieces are thrown away,
  the recorder gets the refusal at once — «the card could not», not emptiness; «not on the card» (gone by the budget) — the rest
  is discarded with the reason «no longer on the card» and is not asked for again;
- the answer is given once and forgotten; an unclaimed one — after `ANSWER_KEPT` = 60 s; the receiver remembers the last
  `LANDED_KEPT` = 64 landed ranges; the end of the recorder's waiting, whatever the outcome, removes the claim, the pieces and the answer;
- a claim is converted by the offset with which it was first named (Н1.3), the pieces — by the same one;
- a claim submitted at one receiver is answered at the same receiver (Н1.5);
- a repeat after a refusal — with a delay and a spread (chapter 8 Н1.3).

A range is an answer to a claim by its number, not the stream `…/backfill`; the recorder lands it into its volume as catch-up recording (chapter 8 Н1.3). Export frames are of both kinds: `audio` of the claim is the `mic` number of the recording, the source gives only this number; no field — no audio. The recorder lands the frames whose `mic` equals `audio`, at 0 — also those with `first`; the rest are discarded with the count `absurd_frames`.

### Н1.5 Several receivers of one cluster

The book `ingest` (Н1.6) lists the receivers of all recorders of the recording cluster; a camera pushes to the first one that answered.
The **neighbours** of a receiver are the recorders of its own cluster heard within `PEER_LOST_AFTER` = 30 s by its own clock and naming
another `ingest`; fell silent — not a neighbour; `ingest` out of form (the family `rec-heartbeat-words`) — not a neighbour on this pass
(unread, not «no receiver»: the next heartbeat — again).
A receiver pushes the stream to a neighbour that has a subscriber to the camera, by the same protocol
as a camera; the neighbour lagged by more than 50 frames (about 2 s at 25 frames/s) — the tail is dropped at once, continuation from the next key
frame, the dropped goes into the count `dropped`. A wish at a neighbour is a wish here (the poll answer). A claim at a neighbour is a claim here (the poll answer); it is answered where it was submitted: the receiver converts the camera export by the offset with which it named the claim (Н1.4), and carries it to the neighbour by the same call, `t` — by the cluster clock; the neighbour's answer is the answer to the camera; the camera moved to another receiver — the piece goes there too under its number, the claim is the same. A scenario request at a neighbour is a request here (the poll answer); the outcome is answered where it was submitted.

### Н1.6 The book of primaries `primaries`

`domain.books.primaries: {from: rec/primaries/, key: ref, pick: all, to: "*", show: [recording, running, role,
retention_days, when, home]}`. The recorder writes the contribution `rec/primaries/<свой слот>` (P/03.7 Н1.2) — an element per
held recording with `ref` that has an assignment `domain/placed/rec/<ref>` with this cluster in `cluster` or `copies`; the recordings of its own camera carry one `ref`, therefore `role: primary` among them is given only by `<cam>`; a recording of a foreign camera and a reserve enter as they are; a recording `replica` (chapter 6 Н1.9) is not a primary one and does not enter the book:

```yaml
ref:            the camera ref (the book key)
recording:      the recording id
running:        true — the recording is in the state running; false — held, but not writing
role:           primary | backup (when: offline or the home on edge, chapter 6 Н1.7)
when?:          offline — at a reserve; at a recording on edge — the word of the volume, always — no field
retention_days: the retention of the recording
home?:          the volume of the recording
server?:        the server of the recorder that holds the recording (SERVER_NAME): its life is the head of the book ingest; a recording not held has no field
push?:          true — the camera said it always pushes (the element of the book sources); for a cluster from star — always
since?:         since what moment the recording is enabled and not running (only with running: false)
as_of:          seconds; changes only together with the content of the element
```

`coverage` is not in the element (ADR-0113 addendum (26) item 1): it would change every 5–10 s, the book sections would go stale all at once, and the reserve would learn of the fall of the primary minutes later. Readers take it from the heartbeat: `backup_sources` and the sources of catch-up recording (chapter 6 Н1.6, chapter 8), the timeline — the door `/timeline`. The traversal time is counted by Quint.

The receiver addresses are not in the element (ADR-0113 addendum (29), (30)): they would change in all elements of the cluster at the death of one recorder, about 2900 sections at once with 5000 cameras, and this is exactly the failure that the reserve catches. The element names the server; the addresses and the life — the book `ingest` below.

**A book for all** (ADR-0113 addendum (25)). The field `to` of the contribution is not read (P/08.5 Н1.3 `to_all`), the element does not carry it. The head — `domain/rec/primaries/*`, the sections — `domain/rec/primaries/*/<раздел>`, a section — 12 bits of SHA-256 of `ref`, the keys are the same at the holder and at the copy. Without a request the book does not reach a member: a reader on any cluster, at the holder too, at its start and once per `BOOK_WANT_EVERY` puts `domain/book-want/rec/primaries/<раздел>`; the agent of the holder's cluster does not execute it, the keys are the same. Readers: the recorder — the sections of the `ref` of its recordings that judge the primary: the backup ones and those on the volume `edge`; the camera holder — the sections of the `ref` of its cameras with an assignment (the pusher, V/01.6 Н1.4). A cluster of one requests one section, a central one — a section per camera, about 2900 with 5000 cameras. **Price:** the recordings of all cameras are visible to the domain (ADR-0113 addendum (25)); a camera-cluster keeps on flash the row of the section with foreign keys, about 61 with 250,000 cameras, and rewrites it on a change of any of them; the value is to be measured.

A recording `enabled` with `ref`, an assignment and an empty `replica_from` that nobody holds is entered by **the first recorder of the cluster** (the first by slot
name among those heard within `PEER_LOST_AFTER`): `running: false`, `since` — from the first pass of this writer in which nobody holds it.
The fields «empty, false, none» are absent, except `running`. A camera-cluster reads the book from flash: an element is not rewritten without
a change.

**The book `ingest`** (ADR-0113 addendum (29)–(33)). `domain.books.ingest: {from: rec/receivers/, key: cluster, pick: all, to: "*", stale: true, show: [server, ingest]}`: `stale` without `to: "*"` is a catalogue refusal. Each recorder writes the contribution `rec/receivers/<свой слот>` — one element about itself, the row — only on a change:

```yaml
cluster:        CLUSTER of its own cluster (the book key)
server:         SERVER_NAME of its own server: one recorder per server
ingest:         [receiver addresses] — its own ingest of the heartbeat (Н1.3); for a cluster from star of the topology — the ingest of the centre from the element of the book upstream for its own cluster (Н1.8; a camera cannot get through to this cluster), empty — []
as_of:          seconds; changes only together with the content
```

A dead recorder does not change its element: the life is in the head of the book. A slot contribution without the heartbeat row of its worker is `stale`, like an overdue heartbeat (ADR-0113 addendum (37) item 2, P/09.1); the decommissioning of a server (`decommission`) removes the contributions of the slots of its workers in the same batch (addendum (39)). Its contribution `rec/receivers/<слот>` and the elements of `primaries` with `running: true` lie until another worker captures the slot or the server is decommissioned: the capture removes the rows under the slot (P/03.7 `claim`); an abandoned capture — a slot row with `released: taking` whose version has not changed for `SLOT_TERM` — is removed by the fate of the slot under the guard of this version, with decommissioning and without (P/04 Н3 «судьба(слот)»; ADR-0113 addendum (49), (50)); decommissioning removes them in the batch that gives up the slots under the `cas` of the slot, after giving up — under the guard of the slot row, and a break between the batches is picked up by each pass while the decommission mark and the request stand (P/04 Н3 «вывести_сервер» step 2; ADR-0113 addendum (44) items 1, 2, (45) item 1, (46)). A server that was cut off rather than dead, after decommissioning, takes the given-up slot as a foreign one: it clears the rows under the slot and writes anew all contributions of the subsystem under the slot — `rec/receivers/<слот>`, `rec/primaries/<слот>`, `rec/polls/<слот>`, `rec/upstream/<слот>` (Н1.8), as at start (addendum (44) item 3): the capture resets the memory of all written contributions, each contribution after it is a first write, the rule «the row only on a change» compares with an empty memory, otherwise the same instance after reunion would not write what has not changed (addendum (45) item 2); after the recordings move, a new element of the same camera lives alongside — the reserve judges by them together, «none is being written» does not occur. The head `domain/rec/ingest/*`, the element `""` — `{missing, missing_count, term, rev, stale, stale_count}`: `stale` — `[<член>/<сервер>]` by bytes, the first 500, a server that has in the domain view `pulse.rec.stale` — the heartbeat key `rec` in `stale` of the member's publication: the heartbeat of the role is older than the threshold of `rec` by the agent clock — `heartbeat.stale_after` = 30 s of the manifest, three heartbeat periods of 10 s, no key — `LOST_AFTER` = 45 s (ADR-0113 addendum (36), P/09.1); the head is written at once on a change, `rev` grows ([P/08.5 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md), [P/09.1](../../../../Platform/09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md); ADR-0113 addendum (31)–(33)). `pulse.rec` exists only while `rec` declares `domain.view_pulse` with at least one field: in the manifest this is `disks`; removing it would silently switch off the life of the recorders; `stale` is not declared in `view_pulse`. A recorder in `stale` — its element `ingest` is not an address, its recordings are not being written. The list is truncated, `stale_count` is greater than its length: the reader does not know about a server outside the list — for the reserve this is ignorance, like `missing`, for the pusher the address is tried. **Limit:** more than 500 dead servers in the domain — the reserves of primaries outside the list write out of ignorance. The section of a cluster changes only with a change of the set of recorders, the sections of `primaries` — only with a change of a recording. There are no more keys than clusters, no more elements in a key than servers of the member. Readers — the pusher (V/01.6 Н1.4: the section of the assignment cluster) and the reserve (the section of the cluster of the primary: without it the reserve has no head); the request `domain/book-want/rec/ingest/<раздел>`, a section — 12 bits of SHA-256 of the cluster name, as for `primaries`. An element out of form (the family `ingest-entry`) — unread, not absent: the last read one; nothing read — there is no address of this server.

**The reserve by the book** (the reserve on the card — the volume `edge`, chapter 6 Н1.7; the reserve that fetches by itself): first the stream —
the receiver of the primary told the camera `uncovered`, and the camera pushes into the reserve (V/01.6 Н1.4), then the book:

| Book | Reserve |
|---|---|
| the cluster of the primary (`cluster` of the assignment) is in `missing` of the head `domain/rec/primaries/*` (`silent`, `never`, `no_copy`, `bad_contribution`, `secret_unopened`; [P/08.5 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md)), with an element or without: it is judged before the element of the copy, the member is silent — the primary is silent (ADR-0113 addendum (27)) | writes — ignorance |
| the reader has no head `domain/rec/primaries/*` yet | writes — there is no knowledge at all; status «primaries not delivered yet» |
| there is no head of `ingest` yet (the section has not come, 503 until the first head of the term) | writes — there is no knowledge at all, as without the head of `primaries`, before the rows of the section of `primaries`: otherwise without the section and the head the reserve would hold the ring with a dead recorder; status «ingest not delivered yet» (ADR-0113 addendum (38) item 3, addendum (39)) |
| the head has come, there is no section of the `ref`, the server of the primary recording of the cluster is in `stale` of the head of `ingest` | writes: the recorder is dead (ADR-0113 addendum (38) item 2); **limit:** the server died and was not decommissioned — its contribution holds `stale`, a new reader writes the reserves of this cluster until the section arrives, until the operator decommissions the server (addendum (39)) |
| the head has come, there is no section of the `ref` (the request lies, `empty_part`), the cluster of the primary is not in `missing` of the head | holds the ring; status «primaries not delivered yet» (ADR-0113 addendum (30) item 4) |
| the element of the primary is out of form (the family `primaries-entry`: unread, not absent) | the previous state read since start (`lapse`); no previous one — writes: there is no knowledge at all (a decision of «Архитектор» 2026-10-29) |
| there is no element of the primary, the cluster of the primary is not in `missing` | holds the ring; status «no primary recording of this camera is known» |
| `running: true`, the `<кластер>/<сервер>` of the element is not in `stale` of the head of `ingest`, and the list is full | holds the ring |
| `running: true`, the `<кластер>/<сервер>` of the element is in `stale` of the head of `ingest` | writes: the recorder is dead — the primary is not writing (ADR-0113 addendum (29)); `stale` already means the threshold of `rec` of 30 s, `START_GRACE` is not waited for |
| `running: true`, the server is not in `stale`, but `stale_count` is greater than the length of the list | writes — ignorance, like `missing` |
| `running: false`, `now − since` < `START_GRACE` = 20 s | holds the ring |
| `running: false`, `now − since` ≥ 20 s | writes |

If there are several primaries of one stream in the book, the reserve writes only when **none** of those that must be written is being written
(the table says «writes» for each of them; chapter 6 Н1.4); `spread_by` does not affect the decision.
The freshness of a copy is judged by the data of the book, not by `domain/reached` (the reach time of the agent is not a measure of freshness; ADR-0091
addendum (13)): a member that does not contribute is in `missing` with a reason. The truncation cuts camera-clusters: servers are first in the list, `SERVERS_MAX` 500 equals `MISSING_SHOWN`, and the cluster of a primary is a server (P/08.6 `class`), therefore a silent cluster of a primary is always named. Raising `SERVERS_MAX` without `MISSING_SHOWN` would bring the step back: the reserves would start to write on a foreign failure. A copy that the agent does not update (the domain is unreachable
for the cluster of the reserve) stays the last carried one: the stream decides before the book (`uncovered`). **Limit** (ADR-0113 addendum (27), (28)): `running` lags by a circle of the view sections, and a wide member carries the window `BOOK_PARTS_SHOWN`: the fall of one recording in a live wide member is noticed within 175–450 s with 250,000 cameras, with the circle and the report (ADR-0113 addendum (34) item 2; a member counts as wide from 65 sections), plus `START_GRACE` 20 s; with a ring of 66 s — a loss of up to ≈ 6.5 min at a reserve that fetches by itself. The fall of a whole cluster is seen earlier: `rev` of the head grows on a change of the set of `class: server` in `missing`, the head is written by the holder's pass, about 53 s for any domain (addendum (34) item 1). The fall of a recorder — by the head of `ingest`: the threshold of `rec` of 30 s by the agent clock, the agent sees the change of the heartbeat by its pass, up to 5 s, «longer than the threshold» adds a pass, the agent report, the holder's pass, the `carry` of the reserve — up to 48 s, the margin with a ring of 66 s is 16–18 s (ADR-0113 addendum (38) item 1, Quint vres2); with `LOST_AFTER` 45 s it would be about 58 s, a margin of no more than 8 s (ADR-0113 addendum (36)); a heartbeat that did not reach the holder makes a live recorder dead — the reserve writes double, harmlessly (ADR-0113 addendum (31) item 3); there is no head of `ingest` yet — the reserve writes, there is no knowledge about the recorder (addendum (38) item 3). Until the first head of the term the section door answers 503, for `primaries` up to 20 min after a transfer: the copy is kept, the reserve sees only it. A new reader with a head that has come but without a section holds the ring, except when the server of the primary is in `stale` of the head of `ingest` — then it writes (addendum (38) item 2): what remains is the fall of one recording during these minutes, the same limit of 175–450 s; without a head it writes. A camera-cluster is never a primary recording for foreign ones; the head of `ingest` does not concern it. A reserve that fetches by itself opens a session only when the primary is not writing, and holds
it another `HOLD_AFTER` = 60 s after the primary returns (chapter 6 Н1.5).

**Row limit.** A section is one row, the ceiling is 524288 bytes ([P/08.5 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md) `write`, `parts`). Per key — about 400 bytes without a reserve, an estimate by the row form, not a measurement; the multiplier is a reserve of the same cluster. A section of the book `ingest` holds the keys of clusters, about 0.1 key per section with 500 members, an element — about 150 bytes per server. `ref` is spread by the hash: about 61 keys per section with 250,000 cameras, about 340 with 1.4 million; this is below the ceiling, and with a reserve. A row larger than the ceiling is not written: the previous one remains, log `domain.books.too_large`, `view.books.too_large` with `*/<раздел>`. One section freezes, with `running` and `since` of its keys:

- a new camera does not get into it: the reserve holds the ring with the status «no primary recording of this camera is known», the timeline does not see its recordings on other clusters; the pusher takes the addresses from the book `ingest`, but the role and `push` — from this section: without an element it does not push;
- the primary fell, but the copy has `running: true`: a reserve that fetches by itself will not notice the fall while the cluster of the primary is alive; nobody has the recording, there is no status. The reserve of a pushed camera is insured by the stream word `uncovered`;
- `running: false` older than `START_GRACE` froze in the copy: the reserve writes although the primary has returned; the recording is superfluous.

**Recording cluster limit.** A central recording cluster records as the primary recording no more than 10,000 cameras: the owner's word, the limit is sufficient, the layout of `book-keys` does not change (ADR-0113 addendum (40)). The row `domain/book-keys` of a member section holds about 10,000 keys of all members of the section together (P/08.5 Н1.3 `ceiling`): two central clusters in one section, 6000 each, will overflow it. From 80 % of the row — log `domain.books.near_ceiling` and an alarm; admittance of a server into such a section answers «pick another name» (P/08.3, addendum (42)). Overflow — contributions are removed from the end by name until the row fits, usually one, deterministically: the removed cluster is in `missing` with the word `bad_contribution`, log `domain.books.too_large` with the names, the reserves of all its cameras write, the other clusters of the section are not touched (addendum (41)–(43)). The growth of an already admitted cluster by assignments is not stopped by the door `/domain/placement`: a refusal of an assignment would leave cameras without recording (addendum (43)); the cure is transferring assignments, `copies` first.

### Н1.7 The poll book `polls`

`domain.books.polls: {from: rec/polls/, key: ref, pick: unique, to: to}`. A camera that nobody records but that must be polled
(an element of the book `sources` with `polls: true`, V/01.6 Н1.2) is polled by the receiver of its poll home (`poll_home`, P/08.7
Н1.1). The first recorder of the home cluster (Н1.6) writes the contribution `rec/polls/<слот>` — an element per such camera from
the book `domain/cam/sources` of its own cluster (Н1): `{ref, to: [член вклада камеры], urls: [ingest своего кластера], as_of}` (`to`: the member of the camera's contribution; `urls`: the `ingest` of its own cluster). The camera holds the poll
with the token `poll` (V/01.6 Н1.3) — without a stream: the answer carries only scenario requests (V/07). A separate book, because the book
of primaries is read by the reserve on the card: a polled camera must not look recorded. The poll home is a relay and a centre
exists — the forwarder holds the poll of this camera at the centre too (Н1.8, the token `upstream` by `unit`).

### Н1.8 The relay forwarder

A relay is a recording cluster through which others report (`relays` of the topology; [P/08.3 Н1.2](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md),
[P/09.2 Н1.1](../../../../Platform/09-agent/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md)); the topology — a copy `domain/member-topology` (at the domain
holder — the row `domain/topology`, Н1).
Control is flat: the report and the tokens belong to the agent (P/09.2); the hierarchy is needed only by media. From a camera the stream is received only by its
recording cluster; the copy to the centre is given by the **forwarder** — in the recorder that holds the recording of the camera (one per camera).

**The book `upstream`.** `domain.books.upstream: {from: rec/upstream/, key: relay, pick: unique, to: to}`. The first recorder
of the centre (`topology.centre` — its own cluster; first — Н1.6) writes the contribution `rec/upstream/<слот>`: an element per member from
`star` and per relay (`relays`, except the centre) — `{relay: <член>, to: [<член>], ingest: [приёмники своего
кластера: свой `ingest` и соседей, Н1.5], mode: pull (член в star) | push, as_of}` (`ingest`: the receivers of its own cluster — its own `ingest` and those of the neighbours; `pull` for a member in star); the row — only on a change. A relay reads the book
`domain/rec/upstream` (a copy or the row of its own cluster, Н1) — the addresses of the receivers of the centre and its own mode; the element for its own
cluster out of form (the family `upstream-entry`) — unread, not absent: the forwarder keeps the last read
addresses and mode; nothing read — there is no forwarder, `reason` «upstream book entry unreadable».

**When.** Its own cluster is a relay or in `star`, and the book `upstream` (Н1) has an element for it: there is a forwarder at every
held recording with `ref` and an assignment (Н1.6). The key — the token `upstream` (`placement: {sub: rec, caller: cluster,
target_fields: [centre]}`, P/08.7 Н3); the recorder requests it through the agent (P/03.7 Н1.5). No element (no centre,
the book is not carried) — there is no forwarder; the recording has gone (transfer, removal) — the stream ends, the subscription and the wish are released.

**Push (wish down, stream up; `mode: push`).** The forwarder holds a poll of the receiver of the centre for the camera; the centre wants it (its
recorder writes a copy, its gateway shows the camera) — a subscription to its own
receiver with the `audio` numbers of the centre's poll answer (the queue `FORWARD_FRAMES` = 300 frames) and a push by the same protocol as a camera; the centre does not want it — unsubscribe,
the unsent goes into the count `up_dropped`, the next wish — from the live edge. The untransmitted is held `FORWARD_HOLD` = 10 s and no
more than `FORWARD_BYTES` = 16 MiB, the cut — from a key frame; beyond that — `up_dropped`. A repeat that the centre has already taken is cut off by
the tee of the centre (Н1.3).

**Star (`mode: pull`).** Its own cluster is in `star` of the topology (it cannot be called into): the camera pushes into the centre (`stream` with
`star_centre`, V/01.6 Н1.3; the addresses — the `ingest` of the elements of the book `ingest` of its own cluster, which name the receivers of the centre, Н1.6),
and the recording of the relay takes the stream from the receiver of the centre by a **pull** with the token `upstream` and the `audio` of its recording; the centre does not
become a recording cluster and does not write a copy for the sake of transport (form C3b ADR-0074): the pull answer is
a batch with the label `(boot, n)`; in each pull the forwarder tells the label of the last received batch; the label did not match —
the centre gives that batch again before the new one, no more than `PEER_BUFFER` = 300 frames with a cut from a key frame; a label of another
start of the centre — the last batch again; a change of `boot` — a possible hole: the count `down_breaks` and a log line once per
restart (catch-up recording — chapter 8). Each pull is a wish for `LINGER`.

**The word of the centre.** The poll answer of the centre carries its `have`; the forwarder puts it into its own receiver (`up_have`), and the camera
is answered the smaller of its own and the centre's (Н1.3): the card does not let go of what the relay took until the centre has written it. The row
`rec/ingest/up/<ref>.json` `{v: 1, have: <мс по часам ретранслятора>, heard: <unix мс>}` (`have`: ms by the relay clock; `heard`: Unix ms) — the next process seeds
`up_have` before the first pass; it is written: the centre started to want — at once; `have` went back — at once; grows — no more often than
`UP_KEPT_EVERY` = 60 s; stands still — once per `UP_KEPT_REFRESH` = 3600 s; «don't want» — delete; older than `UP_KEPT_FOR` = 86400 s —
delete at restart, the count `up_expired`, a log line; a centre silent for a day expires the same way. A store refusal does not erase the word.

**The relay archive by a claim of the centre.** A claim of the centre's recorder on its receiver (Н1.4) for a camera behind the relay
comes to the forwarder in the poll answer; the forwarder exports the range from the archive of its recording (chapter 5 `/samples`, audio — `track=audio`, the `mic` of a frame — the `audio` of the claim) in pieces by
the camera protocol (Н1.4); the claim does not go to the camera.

**Scenario requests.** A request to a target behind a foreign relay (`up`) is carried by the forwarder to the centre, and it waits for the outcome — the protocol V/07.5 Н1.5.

**Failures.** The centre did not answer, the poll broke off — a repeat after `period × U(0.5, 1.5)`, `period` = 2 s, each camera at its own
moment; the camera state — «the centre stopped answering». The store did not answer — the circle failed: the count `errors` of the camera and
`forwarder_errors`, a log line once per new error and once at recovery; the stream goes on.

### Н1.9 The silence witness `rec/polled`

`domain.witness: {report: polled, member_field: cam}` ([P/10 Н1.8](../../../../Platform/10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md),
P/03.7 Н1). With every heartbeat the recorder writes the object `rec/polled/<свой слот>` (an object per receiver: one per cluster
would overwrite foreign polls): `{ts, cluster, units: {<член>: <секунды с последнего опроса>}}` (the value: seconds since the last poll). The member is the `sub` of the poll
token (a camera-cluster — its own member); per member — the smallest age; the age — `round(ts − polled_at, 3)` by the object clock;
a member that has not polled for `ALARM_WINDOW` = 86400 s leaves `units`. The poll is held by the camera pusher — a process other than its
agent: the camera is alive but the report is silent — the domain alarm `not_reporting` (P/10 Н1.8). `member_field: cam` — a `fixed` field
of the recording (load check, P/02.1); the member name at the witness comes from the token, not from the field.

## Н2 Doors

**The receiver** is the door of the recorder, the route `ingest` (the address and the admittance — Н1.3). The joint between servers and clusters is HTTP/1.1
by this norm: the pusher of a camera-cluster (V/01.6 Н1.4), the pusher of a server camera, the forwarder, the neighbour and the subscriber of another
server speak the same; the local joint of one server — «Implementation decides». Six calls — `POST` of paths under `/ingest/`
(the first segment is the route `ingest`, P/03.4 Н1.7), the bodies — a JSON object (`Content-Type: application/json`). **A frame** is
`{t, key, sample, kind?, mic?, codec?}`: `t` — seconds (a JSON number) by the sender's clock (the camera's — for the push and the camera export; the cluster's — for
the neighbour, the subscription and the pull), `key` — `true` for a key frame, `sample` — the frame bytes (`body` of the SMPL record, the contract `obsd`) in base64 (RFC 4648 § 4, with `=`). `kind` — `audio` for an audio frame, no field — video; for audio `mic` is mandatory — the real number, for the forwarder's export — the `audio` of the claim — and `codec` — `<имя rtpmap>/<частота>[/<каналы>]` (rtpmap name / rate / channels), `key` is always `true`, `sample` — a codec frame, AAC — an ADTS frame under the name `mpeg4-generic`; `first: true` — this `mic` is the camera's first (Н1.3а). A call with
`camera_now` — `t` is converted by the offset; the camera export — by the offset with which the claim is named (Н1.4); the rest — `t` as is. `camera_now` is sent by the camera and by a pusher into a foreign cluster;
the neighbour, the forwarder and the pusher into its own receiver do not send it with a push. The form
of the frame and of the poll body is also given by the VMS data families (the families class, the analysis of the V/01 review, question 5).

| Call | Request | Admission (Н1.3) | Answer 200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| poll | `POST /ingest/poll` `{cam?, ref?, version, camera_now, rtt?, outcomes?: [{ask, outcome}]}`: `cam` — of its own cluster, `ref` — by a domain token; `outcomes` — the outcomes of scenario requests | `recworker`; the tokens `stream`, `poll`, `upstream` | `{version, streams: [{stream, push, have?, audio?}], range?: {rid, from, to, recording, piece, audio?}, asks?: [{ask, action, n?, port?, firing, deadline}], uncovered?: true}` — the poll answer of Н1.3 (by a domain token — only the main one, `stream: ""`); a held one — up to `POLL_HOLD` |
| subscription | `POST /ingest/subscribe` `{cam, stream?, audio?}` (`cam` — the camera key, Н1.3; `audio` — Н1.3а) | `liveworker`, `detworker`, `recworker` | the stream `application/x-ndjson`: a line per frame (cluster clock) while the connection is open; a break — unsubscribe, the wish lasts another `LINGER` |
| push | `POST /ingest/push` `{cam?, ref?, stream?, camera_now?, frames: [кадр…]}` (`frames`: a list of frames) | `recworker`; the tokens `stream`, `upstream` | `{have?}` — the `have` of this stream |
| export | `POST /ingest/upload` `{ref, rid, seq, more, frames: [кадр…]}` or `{ref, rid, seq, refused: <почему>}` | `recworker` (neighbour, Н1.5); the tokens `stream`, `upstream` | `{wanted, again?: true}` — whether somebody is still waiting for the range; `again` — a repeat of the piece `seq` = n − 1 («taken already») |
| pull | `POST /ingest/pull` `{ref, last?: {boot, n}, audio?}` | the token `upstream` | `{boot, n, frames: [кадр…]}` — a batch (Н1.8 «Star») |
| request | `POST /ingest/asks` `{ref, action, n?, port?, firing, deadline, camera_now}` (`firing` — the firing key, 32 hex, V/07.5 Н1.4) | the token `ask` | `{ask, outcome?}` — the request number and the outcome when known (`performed`, `refused: <почему>`, `expired`, `unknown`); a request with the same (`by`, target, action, `firing`) — live or with an outcome in the memory `REMEMBER` — gets the same number, a repeat of the call learns the outcome (V/07.5 Н1.4) |

A batch of a push and of a pull is cut so that the body is no larger than the `BODY` of the door; a frame that does not fit into a body alone
is not sent by the pusher (the count `absurd_frames` at the sender). A call not by its own admittance (a subscription by a domain token, a pull by a role leaf and
so on) — 403 `{"error": "not allowed for this call"}`.

**Input parsing** — the common parsing of [P/01.1 Н2.1](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md) through P/03.4 Н1.7
`door_limits` (`BODY`, pace, connections; the lesson constants — Н1.3) with deviations: the path — byte for byte (`/ingest/poll/`, `%`-encoded and other —
404 `{"error": "no such path"}`); the method — only `POST` (another one — 405 `{"error": "method not allowed"}`); the query is not read;
the body — exactly one JSON object (UTF-8; a repeated key, a non-finite number — 400 `{"error": "bad body", "detail"}`),
unknown fields are not read; a call field out of form, a video frame out of form (`t` is not a number, `sample` is not base64) — 400 `bad
body` for the whole call, the frames of previous calls remain; a frame with a `kind` other than `audio`, an audio frame out of form — discarded, the count `absurd_frames`, the call goes on: audio does not hold video; a refusal before the end of the body — the body is read to the end within `BODY` or
the connection is closed (P/01.1 Н2.1). The order: path → method → admittance → body.

The chapter has no HTTP doors of its own beyond the receiver: books, tokens, assignment, witness are doors of the platform (P/08.5, P/08.6,
P/08.7, P/10).
