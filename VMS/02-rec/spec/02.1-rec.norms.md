# V/02.1 — Recording — the unit: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/02.1-rec.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 1 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/02-rec.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](02-rec.README.md), [acceptance](../acceptance/1/README.md), manifest [`rec.subsystem.yaml`](../rec.subsystem.yaml).
The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the recording stream `stream` — `41d3ec8`; the analysis of the streams review — `b41527f`); the references `P/…` lead into its `Platform/`.

The chapter sets norms for what the subsystem `rec` declares about a recording: the fields and their meaning, the link of a recording to the camera and the volume, the forms of the tables
`volumes` and `keeps`, the placement values, the end time of a recording, the snapshot and the rights of people. How the platform executes each declaration is
the norms of the platform by reference. The recorder — chapter 4; the engine `obsd` — chapter 2; the volume kinds — chapter 6; retentions and labels —
chapter 7; catch-up recording — chapter 8; the domain — chapter 9.

Norm ids are `V/02.1-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `until` — Unix seconds (a number); `min_depth_days`, `retention_days` — days.

**Data** (the shared families `<sub>/…` of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv); the chapter has no keys of its own
beyond them): the operator writes `rec/recordings/<id>`, the tables `rec/volumes/<имя>`, `rec/keeps/<ключ>` ([P/06.1](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)
Н1.1–Н1.3, Н1.6), the volume `edge` of a cluster of one — the box firmware (chapter 6 Н1.7); the orderers — `auto`: a recording row by a scenario (`worker.creates`, P/03.6 Н1.6; lesson V/07), `cam`: a recording row on the card (V/01.6 Н1.7); the places
`rec/holds/<том>` and the table `rec/wanted/<том>` — the recorder (P/03.2, chapter 4).

### Н1.1 Recording

The unit of `rec` is the recording of one camera to one place: the row `rec/recordings/<id>`, `unit.id: name` — the id is the name of the recording,
chosen by the operator (`7`, `7-cloud`); `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` — the events, grants and labels of the recording are the camera's. A
recording with `cam: ref:<ref>` has no target in its own cluster: the rights to it — by a grant on `*` ([gate and pass
contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Права по телу» `unresolved`), the events — under its own key.

| Field | Declaration | Meaning | Who reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, required | the name of the recording: the key, the epoch, the streams in the volume (chapter 3) | the recorder |
| `cam` | `string`, required, `fixed` | whose camera it records; two forms: `<id>` — the id of a `cam` camera of its own cluster (of the main stream, V/01.1 Н1.1а); `ref:<ref>` — a camera of another cluster by its `ref` in the domain (chapter 9 Н1.1); does not change | the recorder (the source, chapter 4), `about` |
| `ref` | `string` | the name of the recording's camera in the domain (V/01.1 Н1.1 `ref`): by it the domain finds the recording (`domain.ref`, chapter 9); the reconciliation with `cam` is kept by the recorder; not `fixed` — an order of a recording by a scenario names only the camera (V/07.4) | the recorder, the domain |
| `stream` | `string`, `ref: cam/cameras`, `must_match: {main: cam}` | which stream of the camera it records: the id of a further stream of this camera; empty — the main one (the id of the camera itself — 409: the camera's `main` is empty, V/01.1 Н1.1а); the user's choice (the page suggests the largest); editable: a change is an edit of the row, the recorder restarts the recording under the same epoch (P/03.3 Н3 step 2), the next sequence — from the new stream from a key frame (chapter 4 Н1.2), the previous ones remain; the frame size is a property of a sequence (the codec parameters are in each key frame): one recording carries sequences of different sizes, there is no mark of the change on the timeline | the recorder (the source, chapter 4) |
| `audio` | `int`, `0`, ≥ 0 | which `mic` the recording records: the `number` of a microphone of the camera's device (V/01.4 Н1.4а), whoever it belongs to; 0 — the first `mic` of the camera; there is no such one — the recording is without sound, not a refusal; editable: a change is an edit of the row, the recorder restarts the recording under the same epoch (P/03.3 Н3 step 2), as with `stream` (chapter 4 Н1.2а) | the recorder (chapter 4) |
| `retention_days` | `int`, `30`, `≥ 1` | how far back the recording's door shows video — a ceiling; what the volume has is decided by the ring (chapter 5) | the archive door |
| `enabled` | `bool`, `true` | the recorder records only an enabled one | the recorder |
| `labels` | `list` | where it may run: a recorder whose server carries these labels | placement (`constraint`) |
| `home` | `string`, `ref: rec/volumes` | the volume in which the archive of the recording lies; there is no volume — not a refusal (a reference does not require a target, the row check contract); then the recording has no home and no `affinity`, it is placed as a recording without a home (P/04 Н1.3); the volume `edge` (the card, chapter 6) — only for cameras of its own server: the guard is the recorder of the card (V/02.6 Н1.7: a recording of a camera whose holder is not on the server of the volume is not recorded, `reason`), the door does not refuse: the rows do not say whose server holds the camera | placement (`home`, `affinity`), the recorder of the card |
| `until` | `float`, `0` | the end of the recording; `0` — without an end (all manual recordings and card orders, V/01.6 Н1.7); a recording by a scenario ends by itself (Н1.3) | the controller (`ends`) |
| `min_depth_days` | `float`, `0` | the floor of the archive depth: less — the alarm `archive.shallow` (chapter 5) | the recorder |
| `when` | `always` \| `offline`, `always` | backup recording: only while the main one should record and does not (`offline`); on the card (`edge`) the word of the volume (Н1.2), the field is its copy from the order (V/01.6 Н1.7); on a volume `backup` the word `always` — `waiting` at the recorder (chapter 6 Н1.4), the door does not judge this; for a recording on another volume it is not read | the recorder |
| `replica_from` | `string` | a recording without a live stream (chapter 6 Н1.9): `device` — the device archive, a name — another recording of the same camera and the same `stream`; empty — the live stream; the door does not judge the word: the source, a cycle and the volume kind are other rows, the recorder judges; editable: a change is a new `revision`, the plan anew | the recorder |

**Several recordings of one camera.** A recording is a separate entity: a camera may have several of them (a main one and a backup one,
a local and a network volume; the recording `7` and `7-cloud`). To spread the copies over different servers — `placement.spread_by: cam`
(the filter of P/04 Н1.3); the shipped manifest does not have it: a network volume needs both recordings on one machine. An installation that
buys the survival of a server adds it for itself. The name of the first recording of a camera that the page suggests (`<cam>`,
`<cam>-<том>`) — lesson V/08.

### Н1.2 Tables

**`volumes`** — where to record: the row `rec/volumes/<имя>`, written by the operator (P/06.1 Н1.6), the volume `edge` of a cluster of one — the box firmware (chapter 6 Н1.7); the meaning of the kinds and what each of them
changes — chapter 6.

| Field | Declaration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, key | the volume name |
| `kind` | `local` \| `network` \| `backup` \| `edge` \| `incidents`, `local` | the kind (chapter 6) |
| `url` | `url`, required; `schemes: {file: {host: path}, s3: {}}`; `credentials: {login: access_key, secret: access_secret}`, `secret_in` | where the volume is: parameters, not an address with a key (chapter 3) |
| `server` | `string` | whose disk (`local`, `edge`, `backup`, `incidents`); `network` has none (any one takes it) |
| `quota_bytes` | `int`, required, `≥ 1` | the size of the volume ring (chapter 6) |
| `retention_days` | `int`, `≥ 1`, absent — 30 | the default of a new recording that `backend` creates; it does not change the orders `auto-<cam>`, `cam-<cam>-<том>` or existing recordings (ADR-0112 addendum (6) item 2) |
| `access_key` | `string` | the bucket access key — which key, not the key itself; shown |
| `access_secret` | `string`, `bound_to: [url]` | the bucket secret (P/06.2) |
| `enabled` | `bool`, `true` | whether the volume is declared for recording (places — only `enabled`, Н1.3) |
| `shrink_confirmed` | `int`, `0` | the confirmation of a quota decrease (chapter 6) |
| `when` | `always` \| `offline` | at `edge` — how to record the cameras of the server; no field — `always` for a camera without a main one in a cluster of one, otherwise `offline` (chapter 6 Н1.7); other kinds have no such field |
| `admits` | `bool`, `true` | whether it accepts recordings; `incidents` has `false` |

The table schema: `local` — `server` is required; `network` — without `server`; `incidents` — `admits: false`; `edge` — `server` is
required, `url` — `file://`, without `access_*`; `when` — only at `edge`. The table log —
`archive.volume.declared`, `archive.volume.withdrawn`.

**`keeps`** — the «keep» labels: the row `rec/keeps/{cam}-{from:int}-{to:int}`, the fields `cam`, `from`, `to` (seconds, > 0),
`note` (≤ 500), `recordings`, `released_at`; `stamp: [by, at, made_at]`; the log `archive.keep.made`, `archive.keep.lifted`.
The meaning, retentions and copies — chapter 7.

**`wanted`** — the reason for a volume holder: the row `rec/wanted/<том>`, the field `why`: `archive` — the volume has been opened, an archive may lie in it;
`keeps` — the volume `incidents` has not been opened yet, but labels are set. A worker table (`worker.writes`): the recorder writes it (chapter 4 Н1.3), the console only reads.

### Н1.3 Placement and end time

The keys and their meaning — [P/04 Н1.3](../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md); `rec` declares:

| Key | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | `{from: capacity, default: 50, unit_mb: 42, base_mb: 51}` | cameras per recorder — its word from the heartbeat (disks and network) |
| `constraint` | `labels-subset` | — |
| `requires` | `resource` | the only subsystem that has to be where the archive is |
| `places` | `{table: volumes, where: {enabled: true}, server_field: server, lease: strict, wanted: wanted}` | a volume is a place: it is held by one recorder (P/03.2 Н1.3, chapter 4); `wanted` — the table of reasons: who needs a holder (Н1.2) |
| `place_by` | `volume` | `distinct` and `home` are counted by disk, not by box |
| `servers` | `distinct` | one recorder per volume carries recordings (the others on the box are without a place) |
| `home` | `home` | the row field that names the volume of its archive; the home is kept |
| `affinity` | `{field: home, table: volumes, server_field: server, strict: {kind: [backup, edge], enabled: true}, exclusive: true}` | a recording on a backup volume or on a card — only on the server of this volume, and there — only such recordings (`exclusive`; chapter 6 Н1.1) |
| `headroom` | `{from: headroom}` | the recorder's headroom — a field of its heartbeat (P/04 Н1.3) |
| `grows` | `true` | on-demand workers: a recording that has nowhere to be recorded, and a volume with a `wanted` row — a shortage (P/04 Н3 «нехватка(), заказать()» for `servers: distinct` with a place from the heartbeat); there are no spares (ADR-0084) |
| `ends` | `{field: until, never: 0}` | the end of the recording (ADR-0091 item 2) |
| `tie_break`, `rebalance` | `most-free-capacity`, `{dead_band: 0.10}` | — |

**A volume without a holder.** The shortage counts the waiting recordings and the volumes with a row `rec/wanted/<том>` (P/04 Н3 «нехватка()»): such a volume orders
a recorder, and its holder is not a victim of a reduction (P/04 Н3 «сократить»). A volume without a reason — new, never opened, without labels — gives no order:
there is nothing to read in it; it is visible as the series `volumes_unserved`.

**The end time of a recording** (`ends`): a recording with `until` ≠ 0 and not later than the controller's clock is removed from placement; the order row
(a recording by a scenario, `made`) the controller deletes physically when its clock is strictly later than `made.keep`; a manual recording whose
end time has passed it removes from placement and does not delete (`expired_kept`), the operator deletes it (P/04 Н1.3, P/03.6 Н1.6).

### Н1.4 Snapshot and rights

`snapshot: [name, cam, ref, retention_days, enabled, until, labels, home, when]` — the form of P/04 Н1.3а; `ref` — the domain finds
the recording by the camera (chapter 9); `when` — the domain finds
the backup `offline` recording of a camera of another cluster (chapter 9). The rights of people ([P/06.3 Н1.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.3-console.norms.md)):
`unit_of: {keeps: cam}` — a label belongs to the camera; `routes: {edit: [keeps]}` — `edit` suffices to set a label (there is no camera or its row is unreadable — a grant on `*` is needed,
[gate and pass contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Права по телу» `unresolved`);
`reach: {cluster: [home]}` — moving a recording to another volume is the business of the cluster (ADR-0057).

## Н3 Pass

### Writing a recording row (door P/06.1)

```
body → the fields of Н1.1; name, cam are required
cam on an edit with another value → 400 `fixed`, `field: /cam`
home: no volume — not a refusal (no home); a volume edge of a foreign server — not a refusal of the door: the recorder of the card does not record
replica_from, when on a volume backup: any word — not a refusal of the door: the recorder judges (chapter 6 Н1.4, Н1.9)
retention_days < 1, when outside the enum → 400
id with the prefix «auto-», «cam-» → 400 (the names of the orderers auto, cam, P/06.1 Н1.3 made_prefix)
rights: reach.cluster — a grant on the cluster when home changes
```

**Races.** The volume was deleted after the row was written — the same as if there was no volume (ADR-0057): the recording keeps the former `home`,
it has no home: it is placed as a recording without a home, the row remains. The card was declared later than a recording on this volume — the same:
the door does not judge, the recorder of the card does not record a recording of a camera of a foreign server.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| there is no `name` or `cam` | 400 `required`, `field` (P/06.1 Н4) | the answer | — |
| `cam` changes | 400 `fixed`, `field: /cam` | the answer | a new recording |
| `stream` — a stream of another camera | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match` (P/06.1 Н4) | the answer | a stream of this camera |
| `stream` — the id of the camera itself | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match`: the camera's `main` is empty (V/01.1 Н1.1а) | the answer | the main stream — an empty `stream` |
| `home` — there is no volume | accepted; there is no home, the recording goes to any ordinary volume (P/04 Н1.3) | the placement reason | declare the volume or another one |
| `home` — a card, the camera holder is on another server | accepted by the door; the recorder of the card does not record it (V/02.6 Н1.7) | the heartbeat of the recorder of the card, `reason` | another volume |
| `when: always` on a volume `backup`; `replica_from`: a cycle, a source on `backup`, the volume is not `local`, `network` | accepted by the door; `waiting`, `reason` at the recorder (chapter 6 Н1.4, Н1.9) | the heartbeat, `reason` | fix the row |
| `retention_days` < 1, `when` outside the list | 400 | the answer | — |
| id `auto-…`, `cam-…` | 400 `bad_id`, `field: /name` (`detail` «names starting with "auto-" are made by auto», the same for `cam-`) | the answer | another name |
