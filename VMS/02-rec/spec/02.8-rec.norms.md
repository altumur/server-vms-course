# V/02.8 — Catch-up recording and the scan order: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/02.8-rec.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 8 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/02-rec.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](02-rec.README.md), manifest [`rec.subsystem.yaml`](../rec.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs`
`7a875c75`.

When there was no connection to the camera, the recording has a hole, while on the camera's card (or in the recorder) these minutes exist. Catch-up recording is a reconciliation
of two coverages: the recorder takes from the device what it does not have itself, into its own volume. The chapter sets norms for the catch-up plan, its budget and
window, download and landing, the request «закрепить» (catch-up recording on request), and the scan order: the same minutes that were not recorded did not
go through detection — the recorder that brought them orders the job `detjob`. The door of the device archive — V/01.5; the stream
`…/backfill` — [chapter 3](02.3-rec.norms.md) Н1.1; the copy from a backup — [chapter 6](02.6-rec.norms.md) Н1.6; visibility —
[chapter 5](02.5-rec.norms.md).

Norm ids are `V/02.8-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Unix seconds; the catch-up window is hours of the server's local time.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — the requests
`rec/requests/<rid>` (P/06.4), the rows `det/units/*` (the camera's detectors; `worker.reads`), the heartbeats of holders and recorders;
writes — video into its own volume (the stream `…/backfill`), the heartbeat field `fetched` (P/03.4 Н1.1), the order debt `rec/state/scan/<запись>` (Н1.5), the order rows `detjob/jobs/rec-<key>`
(`worker.creates`, [P/03.6 Н1.6](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)), the events `archive.backfill.refused`, `archive.backfill.unscanned`.

### Н1.1 Plan: holes by subtraction

A recording `replica` does not build this plan: its plan and source are chapter 6 Н1.9. Catch-up recording is the work of the recording's recorder; the sources, in order: the device archive (the camera holder by its heartbeat, `playback_url`
and `coverage`, V/01.5 Н1.1; not on a foreign loopback), then the backup recordings (chapter 6 Н1.6). No holder — no source. A recording with a non-empty `stream` has no device source:
the device archive is the archive of the camera, and it does not say whose stream is in it (V/01.5 Н1.1а).
The hole of a recording for a source:

```
ours  = the visible coverage of the recording in the volume — all its streams and epochs, seams ≤ 2 s glued (chapter 5)
lo    = max(start of the source coverage, now − recording retention, visible from (chapter 5 Н1.2), start of ours)   # only holes inside what is recorded
hi    = min(end of the source coverage, end of the visible ours, now − 900)                                       # the top — by what is visible
holes = [lo, hi) − ours − coverage of other doors − «source has none» (per pair recording × source) − dropped − landing, not yet visible
```

**Coverage of other doors** is the `/spans` of the recording at each live door with a volume, except its own (chapter 5 Н1.5; not on a foreign loopback): a recording
that has been in another volume lies there too (chapter 4 Н1.9), and a copy from the device under a new epoch would beat the live frames (chapter 5 Н1.3).
**Limit:** a door did not answer, or volumes without a live door — their coverage is not subtracted: the ring of the source will not wait, the minutes will arrive
as a second copy and go through the scan once more (Н1.5).

The plan is only holes **inside** what is recorded: a recording by event does not become permanent, the plan does not chase the tail of the ring.
The order — the oldest hole first: the ring of the source will give it up sooner; a hole that the source cannot close is held not by the
queue but by «source has none» (Н1.3).
An unreadable summary of the source or retention of the recording — no plan.

### Н1.2 Budget and window

- Per pass — `BACKFILL_BUDGET` (the subsystem file `/etc/rec/rec.env`) ranges (default 1; 0 — only requests), a range no longer than 600 s (the rest — on the next
  pass). The budget and the window below do not concern `replica` (chapter 6 Н1.9).
- The window `BACKFILL_WINDOW` (the subsystem file, example `22-6`) — by the **local** hour of the server, crossing midnight; without the variable — any hour;
  for a card source (`edge`) the camera word `stream.lagging` decides (free — allowed, lagging — not allowed, silent — the window).
- The download runs on its own thread, one at a time (the device has one channel), not on the thread of the loop and the leases; the pass does not wait for it longer than
  0.5 s.
- The volume accepts nothing (`archive_error`, no writer) — catch-up recording waits for the volume; it does not wait for a full disk: the volume is a ring,
  what was downloaded goes to the head of the ring (the ring will give up the hour of the day before yesterday later than yesterday's).

### Н1.3 Download and landing

- Only under `may_write` and the current epoch of the recorder (the epoch is «who recorded», not «when it was shot»); into the stream `…/backfill`
  of its own volume, into the volume on which the download started (the volume changed — do not land).
- In pieces of 60 s, cut at a key frame: a piece lands before the next one is requested; a failed piece breaks off the
  range — the rest is requested again.
- **Only holes** a second time — by group of frames (from key frame to key frame): a group that intersects what is already ours or landing is
  discarded (in the plan it is «none», below: the rest of a hole shorter than a group is not requested again); a group without a key frame is as an engine refusal: not taken, it goes into the count of three (below). A gap longer than 2 s closes the sequence, the next one starts with a key
  frame.
- **Sound** of a piece lands in `…/backfill/audio`: the sound frames of the export of its own number (`audio` of the claim, chapter 9 Н1.4), the sound of `/samples` of a backup (chapter 6 Н1.6), the sound track of the device bytes if it is there (V/01.5); the codec — chapter 4 Н1.2а. Only the times of taken groups land. A refusal of sound does not cancel the group, the count `samples_refused`; the source has no sound — not a hole and not «none».
- An engine refusal (`SEQUENCE_*`) — the group is not taken; a piece rejected three times is dropped (`backfill.refused_seconds` in the heartbeat),
  the count is reset when the piece has landed. `SEQUENCE_LOST` — the group is taken, but the previous sequence of the piece did not land: it is as a refusal.
- «Source has none» — only from a **full clean answer** of the source (the download reached the end of the range without an error, a break
  or an engine refusal): what it did not give and the discarded groups are remembered per pair (recording, source) until the `coverage` of the source
  (the heartbeat of the camera holder or of the backup, chapter 6 Н1.6) changes; growth of the coverage upward and the departure of its start are not a change: «none»
  below it remains; the sum is `nowhere_seconds` in the heartbeat. An error, a break, an engine refusal are not
  «none»: the source is deferred (5 s × 2 up to 600 s with jitter), the rest is requested again.

### Н1.4 The request «закрепить»

The section `requests:` of the manifest ([P/06.4 Н1.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md)): the body `{unit,
from, to, lost?}` (`unit` is the id of the recording), the key `{unit}-{from:int}-{to:int}`, `per_person: 7`, `settle: 60`, `ttl: 86400`,
`stamp: [by, at, about]`, the log `archive.backfill.asked`, **`outcome: fetched`** — the catch-up profile: the outcome is the rid in the field
`fetched` of the recorder's heartbeat, there is no label except the reaper's label (below); the reaper removes the row by a confirmed `fetched`
(P/06.4 Н3 «жнец»). It is filed by the operator (the button «закрепить» on a device interval) and by workers — the job `detjob` (V/05) and
detection over the device archive (V/06) — by worker filing (P/03.6 Н1.5).

The recorder holding the recording, for each of its requests (outside the budget and the window, under `may_act`; download — only when the volume
accepts, otherwise the branch «wait» below; a recording `replica` has one source — its `replica_from`, the request does not go past it):

```
range is not finite numbers | to ≤ from | longer than a day | shorter than a second | to ≤ visible from   → refusal (fetched + refusal)
all of [from, to) already exists (in the volume or at other doors, Н1.1) → fetched
request age ≥ ttl (86400 s from `at` of the stamp) → fetched with the refusal «too old»     (the recorder closes it, not the age)
  — in any branch «wait» below and after a downloaded part: the deadline counts from filing in all of them; a running piece is landed to the end
to > now + 60                                                                            → wait (not final)
the request bounds [from, to) are unchanged; the piece of this pass = [from', min(to, end of what is visible, now − 900)),
  from' — the first second in [from, to) shown neither by the volume nor by other doors; the piece is empty (recent, the tail not visible yet) → wait
no sources / the source failed / the volume does not accept → wait (the row stays)
sources in turn up to the first one without an error; a piece downloaded — remember it, wait for the rest;
  fetched — only when the whole original [from, to) is in the volume, at other doors or «none» at all sources (Н1.3; a discarded group is also
  «none»: a rest shorter than a group is closed here, not by the deadline) — final
```

A refusal: the rid into `fetched`, the word into `requests_refused` of the heartbeat (`{<rid>: <слово>}`, the last 50), the event `archive.backfill.refused`
(`request`, `error`, `of: cam/<камера>`). The words:

| Condition | Word |
|---|---|
| the range is not finite numbers | `from and to are not a range` |
| `to ≤ from` | `a backfill's range ends after it starts` |
| longer than a day | `a backfill is a range of at most 86400 s` |
| shorter than a second | `a backfill is at least 1 s of footage` |
| `to ≤ видно с` | `this range ends before anything the recording shows` |
| age ≥ `ttl` | `too old` |

The request has no lower bound by the start of the coverage. `lost: true` is a mark of the product «lost by the
recorder at the input»: a request with it is **accepted** and executed the same way (the same checks, the limit of a day applies —
longer than a day — a refusal); the mark does not affect the outcome, the course does not file it.

A request about a recording that does not exist (no row, or a tombstone) is not the recorder's business: the reaper closes it with the outcome `gone` and
frees the reserve `per_person` ([P/06.4 Н3](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md) «жнец» `gone`; ADR-0091 addendum (11) item 4).

A request that no one can close (the recording exists, it has no live recorder, so there is no one to say «too old») is closed by
the reaper: without a confirmed `fetched` at age ≥ `ttl` + `REAP_AFTER` — the label `expired`, then `settle`, removal of the row,
the reserve `per_person` is free; a late `fetched` is harmless (P/06.4 Н3 «жнец» `expired`; the platform norm B, step «1в», the request of
VMS «срок просьбы»). No one removes the label: a request with the same `rid` (the same range) after it is accepted (202), but
is closed via `settle`, not via `ttl` (C06-EXPIRED-REPEAT-SETTLE-01), and the recorder may not manage to download it.
The same goes for the label `gone` when the recording is created again under the same id (the permanent key `backend`, V/11.4 Н1.2; a scenario order, V/07.4 Н1.4).
One who files again after `expired` or `gone` changes the bounds: another range — another `rid`, the full deadline.

### Н1.5 The scan order

The minutes that catch-up recording brought did not go through detection: events are written by the holder of the camera's epoch, and it was not there at that time.
A recording `replica` from another archive has no order: the minutes went through detection at the source. From a device, an order is placed only for minutes without a holder `running`: there was a holder — the detector ran over the stream, a second scan would give double events. The recorder itself looks at the heartbeat of the holder of the recording's stream: `seen` of the state — up to where it looked, `gaps` — the segments without `running`, the interval between `seen` and the pass is itself a segment: there was no one to look. The first pass of the recording sets `seen`: minutes older than it are not ordered for scanning, otherwise the first copy would scan the whole archive although the detector ran over the stream. **Limit:** the detector did not run then — these minutes stay without a scan, a person orders it through the door (V/05.1 Н1.2). There are no more than 64 segments, an extra one is a merge of the two oldest: an extra scan, not a loss; segments below `to` are removed. The order range is the landed part ∩ `gaps` minus the `/spans` of other doors; empty — no order. The holder's heartbeat is not read — the pass counts as a segment: not read, rather than «the holder was there».
The recorder that landed catch-up footage (`kept > 0` — something new was brought; «already ours» — do not order) orders the job `detjob`
for each kind of the enabled detectors of the camera (`det/units/*` of the same `cam`, `enabled`) — a pair «range × `kind`», the key
does not name the detector, one job per window, as for a scenario (V/07.4 Н1.3). **The base** is the first by id detector of the kind without `made`;
if there is none — the first by id ordered one. **Limit:** a second detector of the same kind (another mask, other `params`, another stream)
does not scan the catch-up minutes with its own settings:

- `worker.creates: [{sub: detjob, rows: jobs, key: "{cam}-{rec}-{kind}-{from}-{to}"}]` — the row `detjob/jobs/rec-<key>`
  (P/03.6 Н1.6 `key`, ADR-0091 item 1; `from`, `to` of a `detjob` row are `float`, in the key — the notation `values_by_type` of the row check
  contract, the same at all implementations; the platform does not allow `:int` in `worker.creates[].key`, P/02.1): `cam`, `rec` — the recording, `kind` — the detector model, `from`, `to` — the bounds of the landed part of the pass range (Н1.2): one order per range, after the last landed piece; the range broke off (Н1.3) — an order for the landed part, the rest comes as a new range with its own order; the fields are
  the settings of the base (`params`, `labels`, `alarms` — at creation), the mask — `mask_of`: the id of the base (a reference to the row, the job — V/05.1); for a base with an empty `mask` and a set `mask_of` — its `mask_of` (there is no chain of references, V/04.1 Н1.1), for an ordered one without either — without `mask_of`;
- the order is written when what has landed is visible (closed blocks, chapter 5 Н1.1): the job builds its plan by what is visible, and invisible minutes
  would become a hole, and the outcome — `done` without a scan; visibility is judged by the `/spans` of live doors (V/02.5 Н1.4), not only of its own volume: after
  a move what landed lies in the landing volume;
- the order debt — the range and the event time in the unit state `rec/state/scan/<запись>` (`unit.state.scan`, P/03.6 Н1.7): it is written
  right after landing and grows with each landed piece, it is removed by the outcome of the order, except `refused` (below), it survives a restart and a move; a new holder orders with the former
  event time; **an edit of the recording row:** the debt does not survive a new `revision` (P/03.6 Н1.7), but the holder reads the state row before writing (P/03.6 Н3): with the same `born` it reports its `owed` of the former `revision` by the event below, `why: recording edited`, and writes an empty debt; **limit:** death between landing a piece and writing the debt, deletion of the recording row and a new life with another `born` — a hole left to a person, no event;
- the event time is the moment the last piece of the debt landed; the horizon `ORDER_HORIZON` 600 s (older — `expired`); `refused` keeps the debt: a repeat on the next pass
  **with the former** event time (a new time would revive a job deleted by the operator — the tombstone `made.gone` + 60), while the event is not older than the horizon; older — the debt is removed, as `expired`;
  **a hole in the detections left after `expired`** is for a person: when removing the debt, before writing the state, the holder writes under its epoch the event `archive.backfill.unscanned` (`cam`, `recording`, `from`, `to`, `model` — the `kind` of the detector, `why: expired`) for the range and every `kind` whose outcome `created` or `unchanged` is absent on this pass; a break between them — the new holder writes it again; a repeated scan is set up by a person through the door (V/05.1 Н1.2);
- the outcomes — `creates.<outcome>`, the rest of its own rows — `made_rows` in the heartbeat (P/03.4 Н1.1); deletion of a finished job —
  V/05 (`retire_when.delete_after`).

### Н1.6 Timeline

Four kinds of interval: recorded by us (the recording epoch); `source: backfill` — was on the device, now ours (the same ring,
the same doors); `source: device`, `yields: true` — exists only on the device (it will vanish by its ring; the place of the button
«закрепить»; a recording with a non-empty `stream` has no such interval, V/02.5 Н1.5); empty — not recorded anywhere («source has none» makes this a state). The subtraction «ours wins» is one
rule: the page decides what to show (lesson V/11), the recorder — what to take (Н1.1).

## Н3 Pass

```
catch-up thread (one at a time; the volume accepts): requests (Н1.4) → the plan (Н1.1) within the budget and the window (Н1.2) → download and
  landing of a piece (Н1.3) → kept > 0 → the order debt; the range ended or broke off → what landed is visible → the scan order (Н1.5)
```

**Races.** Two requests of one range — one rid by the key; a different body under the same rid — 409 `exists` (P/06.4). A live
recording and catch-up recording over one minute — the epoch rule (chapter 5): at an equal epoch the live stream wins; two landings into one
stream — chapter 6 Н3. The camera holder
moved in the middle of a download — the source failed, the range will be requested again.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the range is unreadable, empty, longer than a day, shorter than a second, beyond the ceiling | `fetched` + refusal | heartbeat `requests_refused`, the event `archive.backfill.refused` | another range |
| the range is in the future; the piece of the pass is empty (recent) | wait | — | — |
| a part of the request is downloaded | wait for the rest; `fetched` — only the whole `[from, to)` | — | — |
| no source, the source failed, the volume does not accept | wait | the row stays | — |
| the request is a day old (`ttl` from `at`), in whichever branch «wait» it stands | `fetched` + refusal «too old»; a running piece is landed to the end, no new ones are requested | the same | file again |
| the eighth unanswered request of a person | 429 (P/06.4) | the answer | wait |
| no recording (deleted) | the reaper closes with the outcome `gone`, the reserve `per_person` is free | — | the recording was created again — file with another range: the same `rid` will close via `settle` |
| no one to close: age ≥ `ttl` + `REAP_AFTER`, no `fetched` | the reaper closes with the label `expired`, the reserve `per_person` is free | the label `objects/rec/commands/<rid>` | file again with another range: the same `rid` will close via `settle` |
| scan order: the event is older than 600 s | `expired`, the debt is removed | `creates.expired`, the event `archive.backfill.unscanned` | a repeated scan through the door (V/05.1 Н1.2) |
| scan order: `refused` | the debt stays, a repeat with the former time; older than 600 s — the debt is removed | `creates.refused`, on removal `archive.backfill.unscanned` | — |
| an edit of the recording row with a debt (the same `born`) | the debt is removed | `archive.backfill.unscanned`, `why: recording edited` | a repeated scan through the door (V/05.1 Н1.2) |
