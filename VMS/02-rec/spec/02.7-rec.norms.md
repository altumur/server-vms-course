# V/02.7 — Holds and keeps: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/02.7-rec.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 7 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/02-rec.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](02-rec.README.md), [HTTP contract](02-rec.api.openapi.yaml), manifest [`rec.subsystem.yaml`](../rec.subsystem.yaml).
The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The ring and the retention ceiling do not know that some minutes are evidence. A keep (`keep`) says exactly that: it holds the
camera's events back from cleanup, makes the video visible beyond the ceiling and copies it into the evidence volume (`incidents`), where it is sealed with a digest and
verified. The chapter sets norms for the meaning of a keep, the hold of events, the copy, the seal and the verification, the loss, the recording side, the resource's request
to free space. The form of the table `keeps` — [chapter 1](02.1-rec.norms.md) Н1.2; visibility beyond the ceiling — [chapter 5](02.5-rec.norms.md)
Н1.2; the volume `incidents` — [chapter 6](02.6-rec.norms.md) Н1.8.

Norm ids are `V/02.7-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `from`, `to`, `made_at`, `sealed_at`, `keep_made_at` are Unix seconds.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the operator writes `rec/keeps/<ключ>`;
the recorder writes the objects `rec/sealed/<том>/<метка>`, `rec/taken/<том>` (`objects.rows`; P/05 Н1.1), the video in
`incidents` (the stream `<запись>/e0`, chapter 3), the events `archive.keep.*`, the log rows `archive.keep.verified`; the resource reads
`rec/keeps/*` (the hold, P/05), the recorder reads the requests `rec/requests/free-*`.

### Н1.1 Keep

- A keep is «these minutes of the camera are evidence»: the row `rec/keeps/{cam}-{from:int}-{to:int}` (chapter 1 Н1.2 — the form);
  setting the same minutes again is the same row (two people marked one thing — it is there once).
- `recordings` — the recordings of the camera at the moment the keep is set; a recording deleted later is copied, one created later is found by
  its own row.
- The operator sets and lifts a keep through the table door (`rights.unit_of: {keeps: cam}`, `routes: {edit: [keeps]}`: `edit` on the
  camera is enough; there is no camera or the row is unreadable — a grant on `*`, the gate and pass contract `unresolved`); who set it — `stamp.by`, when — `made_at` (the first write of the row; an overwrite keeps it — P/06.1 Н1.6); who
  lifted it — the log `archive.keep.lifted`.
- `released_at` — the keep is released: it stops holding (`holds.released`); the operator writes it (also for a garbled keep that otherwise cannot be lifted
  by deletion); the recorder does not write the field.
- The heartbeat fields `keeps` and `volume` are given out by the heartbeat door `GET /rec/heartbeats` (P/06.7 Н1.6 `pulse`): this is how `backend` reads the words of a keep (V/11.4 Н1.23).
- Lifting a keep erases nothing: the events return under their own retention, the copy in `incidents` lives until the ring overwrites it.

### Н1.2 Hold of events

`holds: {table: keeps, unit: cam, since: from, until: to, longest: 604800, released: released_at}` is executed by the resource
([holds contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/holds.md); the owner — [P/06.4 Н1.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md); ADR-0091 item 8): the events of the camera and of every unit
«about» it (`about`: recordings, detectors — deleted ones too) within the interval are not cleaned up until the keep is released and a week has passed
since the start of the interval. Scenarios `auto` are not held (they have no `about`). A garbled interval — a bound does not read as a finite number, or
`from ≥ to`. A garbled keep holds as far as the interval reads (without one bound — open towards it, without both — the whole
camera; `from ≥ to` — nothing, the holds contract); without `cam` — nothing.

### Н1.3 Copy into `incidents`

A recorder that holds the place `incidents`, once a minute, for every keep and every recording of it:
- copies into its volume the stream `<запись>/e0` and its sound `<запись>/e0/audio` (epoch 0 is nobody's; a live epoch is senior to it — chapter 5 Н1.3), only what is missing: what
  the source doors show and what is not yet in the volume. The source doors are every live door with a volume other than `incidents` (V/02.5 Н1.5;
  `/spans`, V/02.5 Н1.4; not on a foreign loopback): the minutes of a recording lie also in the volume it moved away from (V/02.4 Н1.9); the frames — `/samples`
  at the door that showed the minutes, the sound — `track=audio` of the same minutes: the sound is read before the landing and lands first, then the video; empty — there was no sound; a refusal of the reading or of the landing of any stream — the minute is not copied, the next pass lands it again, as a new sequence (time does not go back inside it, the contract `obsd`), the digest withstands a repeat of the sound, and the piece cuts it off by merging (V/02.5 Н1.6). A door did not answer — it has said nothing about its volume, the next pass asks again;
  «the source does not have it» is remembered per pair (keep, recording) until a restart or until the door shows these minutes;
- the minutes for which a door can say «there is nothing»: each of its epochs > 0 of a live stream from the first to the last instant,
  and `[held_since, ∞)` of the current holder (the door's clock converted to one's own); catch-up recording and `e0` do not move the bounds; for `replica` — only the minutes below `copied_to` of the answer (chapter 6 Н1.9): what has not been brought yet is still at the source, the door does not speak for it; a field out of form — the answer is not read, like a door that did not answer; what no door that answered can
  speak for — is not copied;
- a recording with a seal (Н1.4) is not copied;
- pieces by the minute; a gap closes a sequence, a new one starts with a key frame; after the copy — close the block (`seal`,
  chapter 5 Н1.1), otherwise the count in the same pass gives 0;
- the event `archive.keep.copied` (epoch 0; fields `cam`, `keep`, `recording`, `bytes`, `sha256` — the copy digest below, after the
  copy, `seconds`, `volume`); without a new copy it is not repeated;
- what is not copied — `keep_missing: {метка: секунды}` in the heartbeat (series `rec_keep_missing_seconds`); longer than 300 s — the alarm
  `archive.keep.uncopied` (`cam`, `keep`, `seconds`, `since`, `volume`), repeated once a day; the minutes above `copied_to` of a `replica` recording are still travelling (no less than 900 s, chapter 6 Н1.9): they enter the count only 900 s after the end of the keep.

Events `archive.keep.*` — the bucket `rec/<запись>/e0`; without a recording (`uncopied`, `garbled`) — the first recording of the keep by name; there are no
recordings — the camera of the keep.

**Copy digest** (the seal, the verification, `archive.keep.copied`, `state: kept` — one function at any holder of the volume on any
box): SHA-256 over the SMPL records of the stream `<запись>/e0` of the volume, then of the stream `<запись>/e0/audio`, in each those whose time is in `[from, to)` of the keep, in time order
(when equal — in the order of writing in the volume), each as the bytes of the SMPL record of the contract `obsd` (header, `sub`, body) in a row, without
separators; empty — SHA-256 of the empty string; written as 64 lowercase hex digits.

### Н1.4 Seal and verification

- **The seal** `rec/sealed/<том>/<метка>` is an object `{recordings: "<запись>=<sha256>,…", from, to, keep_made_at, sealed_at}` (JSON with
  ordered keys), per pair (keep, volume): every `incidents` volume has its own copy and its own digest, the holder reads, writes and removes only the seals of its own volume; the `incidents` holder sets it per recording **once**, when the copy is whole for the first time — there are frames in `[from, to)`,
  nothing of the recording is uncopied, `to` has passed (the first one — creation once, after that — CAS by the read version; the loser — the next pass completes the sealing); the event `archive.keep.sealed`.
  The recorder role writes, the console only reads: the operator cannot forge a seal.
- **The seal closes the copy of the recording.** The digest takes all the frames of `[from, to)`, and the seal is one: a frame added after it would give
  `damaged` over a whole copy. Therefore a recording whose seal belongs to the keep is no longer copied and is not counted in `keep_missing`:
  the minutes that the source showed after the seal (catch-up recording, chapter 8) do not become a copy and lie at the source until its ring; what is lost by the ring
  of `incidents` is not restored. A seal out of form — as if there were no seal (below); the store did not answer — the recording has no copy on the pass.
- A seal belongs to the keep only while its `keep_made_at`, `from`, `to` coincide with the keep: the keep was lifted and set again
  or overwritten with other `from`, `to` — «not sealed»; a seal without a keep or with another `made_at` is removed before a new seal.
  An overwrite with the same `from`, `to` keeps `made_at` (Н1.1), the seal stays: a recording removed from `recordings` lives on in it,
  an added one is sealed in addition. A keep with a garbled interval is not sealed; an unreadable seal is not overwritten.
- **Verification** — `POST /keeps/<метка>/verify?recording=<запись>` of the door of the `incidents` holder (V/02.5 Н1.5, the answer — OpenAPI
  `KeepVerify`, `volume` — the volume of this door whose copy and seal were verified): the digest «now» is the same copy digest (Н1.3). `result` of a recording: `ok` — matched; `damaged` — did not match,
  or there is a seal but the volume does not read; `pending` — there is no seal, there are frames or they will come; `unverified` — there is no seal and the volume does not read;
  `empty` — there is no seal, there are no frames, the interval has ended and no live heartbeat of a recorder (`fresh_for`) carries `keeps.<метка>`
  with this `recording`. For the verification an unreadable seal is as if there were no seal, with the count made; `damaged` — only by a seal that was read. `integrity`: `ok` — everything
  matched; `broken: …` — any `damaged`; `unknown: …` — the rest. The log row `archive.keep.verified` (`audit/recworker/e1`,
  the shared fields of the door log and `keep`, `volume`, `ok`, `integrity`, `recordings`; ADR-0091 addendum (7)).

### Н1.5 Loss and the recording side

- **Loss first, then the copy:** before the copy the `incidents` holder compares how much lies in the volume with what it said last time;
  a decrease by ≥ 3 s — the alarm `archive.keep.lost` (class `alarm`, epoch 0, `cam`, `keep`, `recording`, `seconds`, `volume`), one per
  loss; in the reverse order the loss would be hidden by re-copying. After a restart «how much lay» — per pair (keep,
  recording): there are events of this box — the last `copied` minus the `lost` after it; there are none — `took`, intersected with `[from, to)`
  of the keep; of this and of what lies now — the larger.
- `rec/taken/<том>` — what the holder has copied into the volume: `{"took": {"<запись>": [[from, to], …]}, "wrapped": bool, "formatted":
  <секунды>}` (integers without `.0`, keys in order, without spaces; `formatted` — `createdAtUnixSec` of `READER_INFO`; `wrapped` — true
  from the first loss found, not reset for this volume); the holder writes it at each copy and at the end of a pass, if it
  has changed; it is trimmed by the standing keeps; the next holder of the volume on any box reads it as a store row before the
  events; another `formatted` — not this volume. There is no object — as the first holder; the object exists but is out of form — it is not read,
  rather than absent: on this pass there is no comparison with the former holder (there is no alarm `archive.keep.lost` by its words), what was said by this
  holder and the events of this box are counted as usual, the alarm of its own copies goes; the volume is the same, the copy goes as usual (the keeps
  are protected), `taken` is written anew by its own copy, the log row «`rec/taken unreadable: <том>`» once per episode (the body of the row-object is judged by
  the reader; the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29). The store did not answer — the copy pass does not run: no loss count, no copy, `taken` is not written; the next pass reads again.
- The seals `rec/sealed/<том>/*` go with the volume: the next holder of the volume on any box reads the same ones. A read `taken` with another
  `formatted` is not this volume: its seals are removed as stale (Н3); until they are removed, `taken` is not rewritten and a seal does not close a copy. **Limit:** there is no one to remove the seals of a removed volume, there is no holder.
- The heartbeat of the `incidents` holder: `incidents_lost {метка: секунды}`, `incidents_at_risk [метка]` (the ring has closed and the
  oldest frame of the recording lies in the interval of the keep); empty ones are not written; on the page — `servers.status` `of: keeps`.
- **The recording side** — a non-`incidents` recorder says in the heartbeat `keeps` per keep (the last recording): `{recording, seconds,
  leaves_in_s, state}`, `state` — `pushed` (`incidents` has no holes), `pushing` (the `incidents` door answered), `here` (not close to the
  edge of the ring or of the ceiling, a margin of 6 h), `at risk` + `why`, `lost` + `lost_seconds` (the alarm `archive.keep.lost`, once a day),
  `garbled` + `why`; the `incidents` holder says `state: kept` (`seconds`, `missing`, `from`, `to`, `sha256`, `whole`, `empty`).
- A garbled keep at the `incidents` holder — longer than an hour: the alarm `archive.keep.garbled`, one per episode, repeated once a day; it did not
  read the keeps — the pass does not say «there are no keeps».
- `keeps_unprotected` — keeps stand, but there is no enabled `incidents` volume (a series); setting a keep still succeeds (201).
  A volume is declared but there is no holder — the series is silent: the order of a holder — V/02.1 Н1.3, while there is none, `volumes_unserved` is visible.

### Н1.6 Request to free space

The resource above the mark writes `rec/requests/free-<server>-<volume>` (`requests.free: true`; [P/05 Н1.4](../../../Platform/05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md)
`free_ask`) — to all `rec` workers on the server, `<volume>` is the resource's volume. A recorder, while the row stands, says in the heartbeat
`freeing: {<volume>: 0}` ([P/03.4 Н1.1](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)) and does not put the id into `fetched`: zero is
a decision, not a refusal — the ring gives up the old by itself and deletes no files.

### Н1.7 What the copy promises

It promises: the copy is verifiable (one seal per recording in the volume, verification at the door where the copy lies; the digest in `archive.keep.copied`, if
the event made it, Н3); the loss
and the threat are visible on every pass and by an alarm. It does not promise: eternity (`incidents` is also a ring: a keep buys time;
evidence that must outlive the volume is exported as a piece, chapter 5); a copy if `incidents` is not declared or no door
gave the minutes; the minutes that the source showed after the seal (Н1.4); a copy of a garbled keep; deletion of the copy when the keep is lifted.

## Н3 Pass

```
incidents holder, once a minute: read the keeps (not read — do not go on); remove the stale seals of its own volume;
  for each keep × recording: loss (Н1.5) → without a seal: copy of what is missing (Н1.3) → seal → count → the seal at the first whole copy
  heartbeat: keeps (kept), keep_missing, incidents_lost, incidents_at_risk; alarms by their deadlines; taken on change
a recording's recorder: the recording side (Н1.5) — for each keep of its own recordings
any recorder: requests free-<own server>-* → freeing 0 (Н1.6)
```

**Races.** The keep was lifted and set again between the copy and the seal — `keep_made_at` will not match, the seal does not belong to the keep and is removed.
Two copies of one keep by two `incidents` holders (a move of the place) — the `taken` of the next holder and `e0` with the epoch rule
(chapter 5): a moment once. A seal lost the CAS — the next pass completes the sealing. **Limit:** a break between the copy and
`archive.keep.copied` or between the seal and `archive.keep.sealed` — there is no event and there will be none: the next pass has nothing to copy and
to seal; the truth is the volume, `rec/taken/<том>` and the seal, the event is a trace.

## Н5 Acceptance

closes, and a door token — they are checked by reading; the table `keeps` is the door of P/06.1 (the form — acceptance 1 of V/02.1).
