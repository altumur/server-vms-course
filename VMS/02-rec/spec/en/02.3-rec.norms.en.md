# V/02.3 — A stream in the volume: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`02.3-rec.norms.md`](../02.3-rec.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 3 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../02-rec.md),
[instructions](../02-rec.README.md), [contract `obsd`](../02-rec.obsd.contract.md). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The chapter sets norms for how a recording lies in the volume of the engine: stream names, the segment from the index, volume parameters,
volume refusals and what the recorder does with them, opening a volume, a frame into the writer and its outcomes, a killed recorder and
a vanished engine. The protocol and the properties of the engine are the contract `obsd`; holding the volume as a place, leaving the volume and
stopping — chapter 4; visibility and the timeline — chapter 5.

Norm ids are `V/02.3-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** The time of a frame and of a segment is archive time (the contract «Время и запись SMPL»); outward (the door, the heartbeat) — Unix seconds.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the recorder writes — the objects
`rec/used/<том>` (`objects.rows`, P/05 Н1.1; the form — Н1.5), the event `segment.lost` into the bucket of the recording (Н1.6); reads — the volume row `rec/volumes/<имя>` (V/02.1 Н1.2), the place
`rec/holds/<том>` (P/03.2).

### Н1.1 Stream names

| Stream | What |
|---|---|
| `<запись>/e<эпоха>` | the live recording under the recording epoch (P/03.2 Н1.5) |
| `<запись>/e<эпоха>/backfill` | frames written into a hole of the recording from the device archive (chapter 8): ours, of our epoch, but where they came from is in the name |
| `<запись>/e<эпоха>/audio`, `<запись>/e<эпоха>/backfill/audio`, `<запись>/e0/audio` | the sound of these streams (chapter 4 Н1.2а): a stream of its own, the frame type inside a sequence does not change; coverage, the timeline, `written_through` and holes do not count it |
| `<запись>/e0` | a copy of a held interval in the volume `incidents` (chapter 7): epoch 0 is less than any real one |

- The first part is the **recording**, not the camera: a camera with two recordings has two streams (`7/e3`, `7-backup/e1`).
- A stream has no metadata besides its name: everything that must be known about it without reading frames is in the name.
- A name not by this grammar (`7`, `7/5`, `7/e5/x`) is a foreign stream: the recorder MUST NOT read it or touch it.
- **The epoch in the name separates writers:** a fenced holder writes into `…/e3`, its successor into `…/e4`; frames do not mix,
  nothing is overwritten or deleted: the video of a fenced epoch stays in the volume and is shown as fenced (chapter 5).

### Н1.2 The segment — from the index

What is recorded, the recorder reads every time from the index of the volume (`READER_TIMELINE`, `READER_SEQUENCES`); beside the volume there is
neither a list nor a cache — a copy of the index would diverge from it at the first failure.

### Н1.3 Volume parameters

The recorder opens the volume by parameters (`VOLUME_OPEN {params}`, the contract «Операции»), not by an address with a key: a file —
`{schema: file, path}` (the path from `url` of the volume row `file://…`); a bucket — `{schema, host, port?, region, bucket,
path, access_key, secret_key}` from the address `s3://<host>[:<port>]/<region>/<bucket>[/<path>]` (fewer than two path segments or
another scheme — the parameters are not assembled, `wrong`) — the access key from `access_key`, the secret is the opened envelope `access_secret` of the volume row
(P/06.2; who opens the envelope of a table row — inventory § 6 № 7, chapter 6). In the log and the heartbeat the address is masked by the [address contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/url-credentials.md) (the owner is P/06.2 Н1.1а).

### Н1.4 Volume refusals

The recorder MUST assign every refusal of the engine or of the client to one of three kinds:

| Kind | What | Codes |
|---|---|---|
| `wrong` | the volume is wrong: it will not open this way, however long one waits | `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `NOT_A_VOLUME`, `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`, `READ_ONLY`, `PATH_NOT_EMPTY`, `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, `PROTECTED_VOLUME`, `INVALID_CIPHER_KEY`; the parameters are not assembled; the answer is not read; the volume has no quota; `VOLUME_RECOVER` → 2 |
| `busy` | the volume is taken by a foreign writer | `ALREADY_LOCKED` |
| `away` | the engine or the address is unavailable | a break, silence; `UNKNOWN_HANDLE` after `HELLO` with a new `pid` (a new daemon — the handles of the session are gone, the contract «Сессия»); other codes, except the frame codes (Н1.6: `SEQUENCE_*`, `QUEUE_FULL`) |

The exception is the **server's own disk** (the place `own_place`, P/03.2 Н1.3): `IO_ERROR`, `GENERIC_ERROR` on it are `wrong` (a broken
disk on the box), on a network volume — `away`. What the recorder does with each kind (hold the place, give it up, how long to wait) —
chapter 4 Н1.4.

`ALREADY_FORMATTED` is a formatting race: the next opening sees the volume. `DATA_CORRUPTED`, `NO_SPACE` at opening are `away`:
a **limit** — such a volume holds the place without a deadline (chapter 4 Н1.4), the heartbeat names `archive_failure`, the operator removes it
(`enabled: false`). An unclean volume goes through `VOLUME_RECOVER`: result 2 — `wrong`.

### Н1.5 Open a volume

```
VOLUME_OPEN params → VOLUME_EXISTS
  no volume: the row has no quota_bytes → wrong; format (VOLUME_FORMAT {size: quota_bytes, tunables: {maxBlockSize:
    8 MiB, optimalReadSize: 1 MiB}, label: <volume>, checkSpace: false}) — only if the volume was not
    here before: there is no object rec/used/<volume> or its identity ≠ the identity of these parameters; the object exists with the same
    identity — the volume vanished from the address: wrong «volume was here and is gone», it is not made empty anew; the object is not read
    (not in form) — do not format: wrong «rec/used/<том> unreadable»; the store did not answer — do not format: away
  exists: VOLUME_MOUNT_RO, the size — READER_INFO.maxVolumeSize (from the daemon, not from the row), READER_CLOSE
VOLUME_MOUNT_RW {owner: rec:<volume>} (the place confirmed in the recording window, P/03.2 Н1.2)
  VOLUME_UNCLEAN → VOLUME_RECOVER only under a place confirmed in this second (chapter 4 Н1.3);
                   the alarm archive.volume.recovered with result at any result;
                   result 0, 1 → VOLUME_MOUNT_RW again, a second VOLUME_UNCLEAN → away; result 2 → wrong
  ALREADY_LOCKED → busy; reattached → its own writer after a restart (Н1.7)
WRITER_CONFIGURE {maxBlockSize, optimalReadSize, sequenceFlushPeriodMs, blockFlushPeriodSec} — a refusal is a log entry, not a volume refusal
write rec/used/<volume> {v: 1, at, identity} (that the volume was opened here): an object with the same identity is not rewritten;
  a refusal of the write or no answer to it — the volume is not opened: close the writer, away, the next pass opens again (without the object a vanished
  volume would be formatted empty); an object that this process has already read or written with this identity is not
  verified anew: a remount under a silent store does not depend on it (chapter 4 Н1.3)
```

The object `rec/used/<том>` is `{v: 1, at, identity}`: `at` — the Unix seconds of the first mount by these parameters, `identity` —
`schema://host/path` of the volume parameters without credentials — as the engine recognises the volume (the contract «Операции», `VOLUME_OPEN`):
a file — `file://` + the absolute `path` as is, a bucket — `<schema>://<host>[:<port>]/<bucket>/<path>`. «Here» is equality of
`identity` byte for byte, without normalisation: the volume row
was edited to another path — the `identity` is different, a new address is a new volume (it is formatted empty); the same address without a volume is a
vanished volume. **Limit:** the object remembers one `identity`: the address was edited to another one and returned — the former address is again a new
volume, the vanished volume there is formatted empty. The object is written only by the holder of the volume; its form is this norm (`objects.rows`, P/05 Н1.1), it is not judged by a VMS family;
«not read» means — not a JSON object in the form `{v, at, identity}`.

Block and read are `8 МиБ` and `1 МиБ` (block ≥ read + 3 MB, the contract «Свойства движка»); a volume block smaller than 4 MiB is the heartbeat field
`objectstorage.block_small` (bytes), not an event; the working block is the smaller of the volume block and the writer setting. The flush periods are
`SEQUENCE_FLUSH_MS` (10,000) and `BLOCK_FLUSH_S` (5) of the subsystem file; 0 — the engine default.

### Н1.6 A frame into the writer

The recording pipeline (chapter 4) gives a frame to the recording receiver; the receiver MUST ask for the **current** volume of the recorder on every frame
(a remount changes the volume, the pipeline is not restarted) and gives `PUT_MEDIA` into the stream of Н1.1:

| Outcome | What it means | What the recorder does |
|---|---|---|
| `OK` | taken | — |
| `SEQUENCE_LOST` | taken, an earlier sequence of the stream is lost | the count `samples_refused{SEQUENCE_LOST}`, the event `segment.lost` (below); no alarm: the hole is shown by the timeline and closed by catch-up recording (chapter 8) |
| `QUEUE_FULL` | not taken: the writer queue is full (pressure, not a loss of the engine) | the pipeline skips to the next key frame; the count `samples_refused{QUEUE_FULL}`; not `away`, not a remount |
| other `SEQUENCE_*`, `SEQUENCE_TOO_LARGE` | not taken | the pipeline skips to the next key frame; the count `samples_refused{status}` |
| the place is not confirmed in the recording window (P/03.2 Н1.2) | not sent | the frame does not go to the network volume and is lost: the pipeline skips to the next key frame; the count `samples_refused{FENCED}`; this is the fence, not a loss of the engine: the pass decides the fate of the place (chapter 4) |
| a break, silence | the engine is unavailable | the flag «engine lost»: nothing is handled on the pipeline thread, the pass remounts (Н3) |
| `WRITER_STOPPED` with «volume lock lost» | the volume lock is foreign | the place is lost, not the engine: a remount will not help — chapter 4 gives up the place |
| `WRITER_STOPPED` otherwise | the engine stopped the writer | a new writer on the next pass |
| `wrong` | the volume refuses writing for ever | chapter 4 |
| other codes of kind `away` | not taken | the pipeline skips to the next key frame; the count `samples_refused{status}`; not a remount; `UNKNOWN_HANDLE` — as a break |

**The event `segment.lost`** (ADR-0105 item 8) is the only trace of a loss in the log: `SEQUENCE_LOST` of a live recording stream. It is written by the recorder under the recording epoch into the bucket of the recording, `of` — `cam/<камера>` by `about` (V/02.1), the class `observation`; the fields `cam`, `recording`, `volume`, `occurred` — the capture time of the frame on which the engine said so. How much is lost, the row does not say: that is the timeline. It is read by the log and by the trigger condition of a rule (V/07): a rule selects the camera as a target by the field `cam` (V/07.2 Н1.3), the `unit` of the event is the recording. Repeats are collapsed by `events.suppress` of the manifest: a window of 60 s per pair `cam`, `recording`. Catch-up recording (chapter 8), `QUEUE_FULL`, the fence and skipping frames do not raise this event: the frames are not taken, the engine lost nothing. The event did not arrive — the loss stays in the count and on the timeline.

A sound frame (chapter 4 Н1.2а) goes through the same outcomes; each of its frames is a key frame, a skip is one frame. The frame time is the capture time; frames before the first key frame are not given; the frame receiver of the pipeline always answers «accepted» —
a refusal of the engine does not bring the pipeline down. A frame refusal is also counted in the heartbeat: `samples_refused` by recording and code, the series
`rec_samples_refused_total{unit,status}`. The alarm `archive.footage.dropped` is only a writer dropped or not closed on leaving
(chapter 4 Н1.5), one per recording and episode; a frame refusal is a count, not an alarm.

### Н1.7 A killed recorder and a vanished engine

- **A killed recorder** (SIGKILL): the engine detaches its writer and holds a grace period (`OBSD_WRITER_GRACE_S` 90, V/02.2 Н1.3);
  the recorder under the same slot takes the same place (the place follows the name, P/03.2 Н3), mounts with the same `owner` —
  `reattached`, without recovery and without losing what was recorded.
- **A vanished engine:** the volume stays the place of the recorder — this is `away`, not `wrong`: the place is not given up, the capacity is not
  reset to zero; in the heartbeat — `archive_error`, `archive_failure`, `archive_away_since`; every pass remounts; the daemon
  is back — a writer again (`reattached`, if the grace period has not expired).

## Н3 Pass

### Remount (the recorder's pass)

```
the flag «engine lost» is raised and the place is confirmed → close the old session (all connections without BYE, as below), open the volume anew (Н1.5)
VOLUME_MOUNT_RW without an answer (a break, the call timeout) — a possible writer without a handle:
  release the session without BYE: close all its connections, wait OBSD_SESSION_LINGER_MS (3000 ms) + 1000 ms of margin — the disappearance of the session:
    the writer, if there was one, is detached into the grace period with owner rec:<volume> (the contract «Отсрочка»)
  a new session, VOLUME_OPEN, VOLUME_MOUNT_RW {owner: rec:<volume>}:
    reattached: true → its own writer of the previous attempt; reattached: false → there was no writer, the volume is clean
    ALREADY_LOCKED → busy (a foreign writer)
the place is not confirmed → do not remount: chapter 4 decides the fate of the place
```

The margin: the engine counts linger from its own observation of the close; a mount before the detachment would get `ALREADY_LOCKED`, and its own
writer would be called foreign. 1000 ms is a value without grounding; it is checked by measurement.

**A trace with a lost answer.** The recorder sent `VOLUME_MOUNT_RW {owner: rec:disk-a}`, the engine created a writer, the answer
was lost with a break. There is no handle, a blind repeat is not allowed (the contract «Повтор»), `STATS` does not link the writer to the session.
The recorder closes the connections of the session without `BYE`; after `OBSD_SESSION_LINGER_MS` the engine detaches the writer into the grace period
with `owner: rec:disk-a`; a new session mounts with the same `owner` — `reattached: true`, the writer is its own again. The mount did not reach
the engine — there is no writer, the mount gives a clean volume. A mount completed by the engine after the disappearance of the session does not leave the writer
attached (the contract «Монтаж в исчезнувшей сессии»): otherwise every new mount would be `ALREADY_LOCKED`. The same `owner` at another recorder is only at the successor of the place (P/03.2):
the place is one, therefore `reattached` at it is its lawful pickup, not a seizure.

**Races.** Two writers of one volume on different servers (the place went to another one while this one was writing): the fence of patch 07 —
`WRITER_STOPPED` «volume lock lost» at the former one (the contract «Свойства движка»), the platform place — at the new one (P/03.2). A restart of the
recorder and the grace period: the same `owner` within the grace period — `reattached`; after it — a clean lock, the volume is mounted anew.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Kind | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the volume is wrong (codes `wrong`, no quota) | `wrong` | heartbeat `volume_error`, an alarm | fix the volume row |
| the volume is taken by a foreign writer | `busy` | heartbeat | chapter 4 |
| the engine or the address is unavailable | `away` | heartbeat `archive_away_since` | — |
| a frame is not taken | the count `samples_refused` | heartbeat, series | — |
| the volume lock is foreign | the place is lost | heartbeat, log | chapter 4 |
