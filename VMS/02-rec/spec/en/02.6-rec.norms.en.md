## Н1 Form

**Time.** `coverage` — Unix seconds; timeouts — seconds.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — `rec/volumes/*`,
`rec/recordings/*`, the heartbeats `rec/*`, the sections of the books `domain/rec/primaries/*/<раздел>`, `domain/rec/ingest/*/<раздел>` by its own request (chapter 9 Н1.6); writes — the heartbeat (Н1.4, Н1.5, the field `card` — Н1.7), video into its own volume (chapter 3), the state `rec/state/replica/<запись>` (Н1.9).

### Н1.1 Volume kinds

| Kind | Where | Who takes it | `home` of a recording | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `local` | the disk of the server `server` | the recorder of this server | a preference | — |
| `network` | an address (a bucket, a network path), there is no `server` | the recorder of any server | a preference | the bucket secret (Н1.3) |
| `backup` | the disk of the server `server` or an address | as `local` or `network` | a filter (`affinity.strict`) | backup recording (Н1.4) |
| `edge` | the card of the box `server`, one per server | only the recorder of the recordings of this card (the engine does not take the card) | a filter; a recording of a camera whose holder is not on the server of the volume is not written — the guard is the recorder of the card (Н1.7), the door does not refuse | a buffer of the server's cameras, not an engine volume (Н1.7) |
| `incidents` | a disk or an address | as `local` or `network` | recordings are not placed (`admits: false`) | copies of labels (chapter 7) |

- Being a backup is a property of the volume, not of the recording: a backup recording is an ordinary row `rec/recordings/*` with its home on `backup` or
  `edge`.
- Own disks first, then addresses; a disabled volume (`enabled: false`) is not a place.
- The places `backup` and `edge` are **exclusive** (`affinity.exclusive: true`, [P/04 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md),
  P/03.2 Н3 «место: взять»): only a recorder without places and recordings takes one; the one that took it takes no other places and accepts only
  recordings with their home on this place — a primary does not move to the backup volume, a backup does not share a process with a primary. For recordings without
  a holder of such a place — an order for the place to its server (P/04 Н3); a bucket `backup` without `server` has no order — the place
  waits for a holder (a recorder without places on any server).

### Н1.2 Quota — the ring size

- `quota_bytes` is the size to which the engine formats the volume (contract `VOLUME_FORMAT`); beyond that the volume is a ring, nothing is deleted
  by age; `retention_days` is the ceiling of the door (chapter 5). The recordings of one volume share time: a camera with a high bitrate
  shortens the depth for its neighbours. At the card the quota is the byte budget of the whole card, there is no per-camera share (Н1.7).
- On each pass `quota_bytes` is reconciled with `maxVolumeSize` of the volume (`READER_INFO`). Growth of the quota — at once, without stopping the writer
  (`WRITER_RESIZE`); a decrease — only when `shrink_confirmed` equals the new
  `quota_bytes`, until then — `quota_note` and `shrink_pending` (the declared quota, bytes) in the heartbeat, the series `rec_volume_shrink_pending`; an engine refusal —
  the log and `resize_error` in the heartbeat, the volume is written at the old size, the next pass reconciles again; an applied decrease —
  the event `archive.volume.shrunk` (`volume`, `was`, `quota_bytes` — before and after; the bucket `rec/<том>/e0`).

### Н1.3 Secret of a bucket volume

`access_secret` of the volume row (`bound_to: [url]`, P/06.2 Н1.1) is opened when the place is taken and when the row of a held
place changes, by the same rule as the secret of a unit row ([P/03.5 Н3](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.5-worker.norms.md)); the value goes
only to the engine (`VOLUME_OPEN params.secret_key`, chapter 3 Н1.3), in memory, the heartbeat and the log — the envelope; it did not open — the place is not
taken (a held one — leave the volume, chapter 4 Н1.5; `released` is not written, Н1.4), `place_error.<том>: sealed` in the heartbeat (P/03.4 Н1.1). `edge` has no secret (the table schema).

### Н1.4 Backup recording

The volume `backup` is only `when: offline`: it is a redirection of the live stream for the case of a failure of the primary. A recording on it with `when: always` is not written: `waiting`, `reason` `a backup volume takes when: offline`; the door does not judge this (the home is another row), the default of `when` is `always`, therefore a backup is declared by the word. A permanent copy of the live stream is a second primary. On the card (`edge`) the word is a field of the volume, the recording reads it and not its own `when`: `always` — it writes always, the card is itself the source; `offline` — as here.

`when: offline` is **for a failure of the primary, never for a decision**: it writes while **not a single**
primary (a recording of the same camera and the same stream `stream` with its home not on a backup volume and without `replica_from`, Н1.9) that **is due** to be written **is
being written**; there are no primaries, or none is due — the backup holds the ring. The backup insures the frames of the camera, not every recording:
a second primary is the operator's choice of a copy; `spread_by` does not affect the decision:
- due — the primary is `enabled` and its `until` is 0 or has not passed; a disabled primary, pauses of recording by an event are not a failure;
- being written — a live recorder (the readable heartbeat changed within 45 s by the clock of the one asking) holds the primary in phase `running`, and its
  `written_through` (chapter 4 Н1.8) lags behind `ts` of the same heartbeat by no more than 45 s: the phase does not see a volume that does not take
  frames. During the first 45 s of its `running` by the clock of the backup, `written_through` is not judged: the first block is not closed yet, a recording after
  a pause carries the former value. Longer: the field is absent or not in form — not being written, without grace; a primary
  that is starting (due, not `running` in a live heartbeat) gets the grace `START_GRACE` = 20 s from the first pass of the backup
  that saw it so; it ran and stopped (the recorder vanished) — the countdown from its
  last heartbeat, without grace;
- a new start of the primary is a reason to release the ring (Н1.5), even if it is being written again: in the live heartbeat its recorder
  or its `started` (P/03.4 Н1.1) changed, or it is starting again after `failed`. A break shorter than 45 s was not noticed, its minutes
  may be held by no one. A start after disabling or a pause is not a failure, the ring is held; the first look of the backup after its own start is not a new start;
- the decision — on each pass of the backup, no less often than `STANDBY_PASS` = 2 s (`POLL`, P/03.3 Н1.2);
- **limit:** a failure that is visible neither by the timeouts above nor by a new start (the volume does not take frames for less than 45 s) is not
  insured by the backup: the hole equals its length, catch-up recording closes it (chapter 8);
- the sources of knowledge — in order: the word of the camera stream (`stream`, chapter 9), the book of primaries (`domain/rec/primaries/*`; the cluster
  of the primary in `missing` of the head — not knowing, write) and the head of the book `ingest` (the server of the primary is dead — the primary is not being written; chapter 9 Н1.6), the heartbeats of its own cluster;
- the reason of the decision — in `reason` of the recording (the primary is being written / is starting / started anew / disabled / exists nowhere).

While the backup does not write, it is in phase `standby` with pre-recording (Н1.5; a backup at a receiver — without a ring, chapter 9 Н1.3). There are no primaries and the holder of the stream is in `idle` (V/01.2 Н1.2в) — also `standby`, no ring, `prebuffered` 0: the backup does not hold the stream, there is no one to release the ring for. A recording on the card (Н1.7) does not stay in `idle`: it is a consumer. The heartbeat of the backup: `coverage {from, to, fragments}` —
the summary of what is visible (only closed blocks; at the card — once per 10 s).

### Н1.5 Pre-recording

The backup `offline` holds in memory the last `PREBUFFER` = 66 s (a vanished primary is noticed after 45 s — without grace,
Н1.4): the ring leaks (the old goes away), the output is shut until the decision to write; the decision «write» releases the ring from
the first key frame (frame time — capture time), then the live stream; the decision «do not write» for longer than `HOLD_AFTER` = 60 s —
a return to hold under the same epoch: the new ring rises before the writing pipeline goes out, the frames of the restart do not
drop out (the archives overlap). On hold the stream is open while there is a primary, at the card — always (V/01.2 Н1.2в). **Limit:** the ring is 66 s. The noticing timeout by the heartbeat is 45 s, a margin of 21 s; the fall of the primary's cluster by the head of the book — about 53 s without a pass of the recorder and `STANDBY_PASS`, a margin of about 13 s (ADR-0113 addendum (34) item 1, chapter 9 Н1.6); the fall of the cluster with a pass of the recorder — 55 s, a margin of 11 s (ADR-0113 addendum (38)); the death of the primary's recorder with a live cluster — `stale` by the threshold of `rec` of 30 s, up to 48 s: the agent sees the change of the heartbeat by its own pass, up to 5 s, and «longer than the threshold» adds a pass, a margin of 16–18 s against the ring of 66 s (addendum (38) item 1); at `LOST_AFTER` 45 s it would be about 58 s, a margin of no more than 8 s, therefore the threshold is declared (addendum (36)); by `written_through` — up to 56 s (45, the heartbeat step 10, a pass), a margin of 10 s. The margin is shared by the pass (`STANDBY_PASS`),
the key frame and the unclosed tail of the primary (up to 15 s, chapter 3 Н1.5). The process died — the tail is closed by the engine (contract
«Отсрочка»), the ring covers the failure; the server died — the tail perishes with the disk, the hole is a pass and a group of frames. Phase `standby`, `reason` —
why it holds, `prebuffered` — how much the ring holds. A stop of the recorder writes out the released ring before closing (chapter 4
Н1.2; limit — Н1.6); the ring on hold is discarded. The ring also holds the sound of the recording, already in AAC: transcoding is at the reception (chapter 4 Н1.2а), on hold too; the release — from the time of the first key frame. `L16` adds nothing to the ring memory (before transcoding it would be ≈ 12 MB at 48 kHz stereo), its price is an encoder per backup; it is not in `unit_mb`, it is checked by measurement. The ring costs memory (≈ 32–34 MB per recording at 4 Mbit/s of video and 64 kbit/s of sound) — this is in `capacity.unit_mb` of the manifest; 42 is computed from the ring size and checked by measurement.

### Н1.6 Copy from the backup into the primary

The recorder of the primary fills the hole of the primary (chapter 8) also from the backup recording of the same camera and the same stream (Н1.4; `backup_sources`): the source is
a recording with its home on `backup` (the door address in its heartbeat, not on another cluster's loopback, V/02.5 Н1.7) or `edge` (the answer to a range
claim, chapter 9 Н1.4: the camera's; for the other cameras of the box — the card recorder's, Н1.7), with a live heartbeat with `coverage`, not on backoff. The order of the claim sources — the device archive (V/01.5), then the
backups. **Plan by the summary, copy by the door:** holes — by the visible coverage of the primary (chapter 5), frames — by `/samples` of the door
of the backup (V/02.5 Н1.4) in pieces of ≈ a minute, into its own volume by the stream `…/backfill` (chapter 3; a copy of frames, not a second recording), sound — by `track=audio` of the same pieces, by the rule of chapter 8 Н1.3;
each interval — by its own sequence. «The source does not have it» — only from a complete clean answer of the door (chapter 8 Н1.3):
it is remembered as empty for this source until its `coverage` changes (growth upward is not a change, chapter 8 Н1.3); an error, a break, an engine refusal are not «does not have»: the source
is on backoff (5 s × 2 up to 600 s with jitter), the range is requested again. The backup **takes from no one** — neither from the primary nor from
the card: one hole copied in both directions is one hole twice.

### Н1.7 The card

The decision is ADR-0108 and its addendum (1). The card is a buffer on the box, not an engine volume (the box has no memory for the engine): one row `edge` per server, the budget `quota_bytes` — for the whole card; it is held by the recorder of the server (a
cluster of one, V/01.6 Н1.5).
- **With all cameras of the server.** The operator writes only the volume row (a cluster of one — below); the recording per camera is ordered by its holder (V/01.6 Н1.7): `rec/recordings/cam-<cam>-<том>`, `home` — this volume, `until` 0. Two `edge` volume rows of one server — the door does not judge: the holder orders on the first by name, the second has no recordings, the recorder writes `edge volume unused` into the log. **Limit:** `exclusive` gives the recorder only the card, a camera beyond `capacity` is `unplaceable` without a separate word, and V/09.2 does not count, in its 2151 MB, the process memory for the 50 rings of the card.
- **The volume row of a cluster of one** (ADR-0110 addendum (3) item 3; ADR-0108 item 3, addendum (2)). It is created by itself: a collapsed member has no server on which the operator would create the volume (V/11.3 Н1.3).
  - **Who:** the firmware puts the row into `/etc/w2c/boot-rows.json` next to the row `cam/cameras/1` (V/01.6 Н1.5), the console at start creates it by the path «создать» (create), with the stamp `by: boot`; the file is not in form — code 2, reading failed — the start goes on, the log, the row at the next boot (P/02.4 Н1.10 `boot`). The holder and the recorder do not write the table `rec/volumes`: `worker.creates` creates only units, `worker.writes` would take it away from the console, and there is no recorder before the volume.
  - **Row:** `rec/volumes/card`: `kind: edge`, `server: <SERVER_NAME>`, `url: file://<каталог карты>`, `quota_bytes` — the size of the card by the word of the firmware; there is no `when`, the default is below. No card — no row in the file.
  - **A row exists — it is not touched.** **Limit:** a new card of another size keeps the former quota. A removed row comes back at the next start: to disable the card — `enabled: false`.
  - **An edit from another cluster** — the link `POST /domain/edits`, `domain.edit_tables` (P/08.6 Н1.3б, V/11.4 Н1.13, V/02.9 Н1.1): the agent applies it. To a collapsed member `backend` sends only `recordMode`, `sizeBytes` of the record `storage` (V/11.3 Н1.3).
- **`when`** is a field of the volume. No field: `always` for a camera without a primary in a cluster of one, otherwise `offline`. A cluster of one — there is the row `cam/cameras/1` with `source: cfw://local` (V/01.6 Н1.5); without a primary — there is neither a primary row (Н1.4) of its own cluster nor an element of the primary in the book (chapter 9 Н1.6). A primary appeared — `offline`. The camera row, the recordings or the book are not read — not read, rather than absent: the former word; there is no former one — `always`. An edit takes effect on the next pass of the card recorder without a new `revision`: the order does not change the `when` of the row (P/03.6 Н3 step 4). The field `when` of the recording is a copy from the order: `snapshot` and `view` of the domain see it until a new row, the contribution `primaries` (chapter 9 Н1.6) — the word of the volume.
- Segments: frame files, only appended; by the budget the oldest of what has been delivered goes, across the whole card, there is no per-camera share: a camera with a high bitrate shortens the depth for its neighbours; an open segment — never, a frame is not
  rejected; the last to go is what the server does not have (the pusher's debt, V/01.6 Н1.4). Within the debt the first to go are the segments of a recording whose row has been absent longer than `EDGE_UNTIL`: there is no one to copy them for; then the oldest across the whole card. `evicted_owed`, `evicted_unknown_ms` are counted per recording.
- **End of the row.** The row has no expiry; the row `cam-*` is removed by its holder, the recorder, by `worker.deletes` (the clock later than `made.keep`, 409 `early` — on the next pass, P/03.6 Н1.6). The volume is removed or disabled, the camera is not in `cam/cameras` — it removes it at once; the row of a removed or disabled volume is held by no one, it is removed by the first recorder of the cluster, the one that runs `rec/wanted`. The holder of the camera is not on the server of the volume longer than `EDGE_UNTIL` (V/01.6 Н1.7) — the card recorder removes it, when there is no debt of this recording. The volume, the camera row or its heartbeat are not read — not read, rather than absent: the row stands. **Limits:** the server of the volume has not come back and the volume is not removed — the row stands `waiting` until the operator removes the volume; `worker.deletes` gives the right to all `rec/recordings/*`; that only `cam-*` are removed is a norm, not a right.
- **The end of the row does not erase the card:** the segments of a recording without a row lie as debt. The copy source of Н1.6 is the recording row: no row — no copy, the debt waits for a new row of this camera on this volume or goes by the budget.
- The sound frames of the recording (chapter 4 Н1.2а) lie in the segments next to the video, under the same budget; the 512 frames of the queue count video, the 6 MiB — both kinds.
- The queue to the card per camera is ≤ 6 MiB (512 frames), beyond that the frames up to a key frame are dropped with a count; the memory budget of a camera is 40 MiB
  (the camera ring 32 and the pieces — V/01.6 Н1.5; the queue — here).
- The card state is the field `card` of the top of the heartbeat (one card place per worker, like `volume`; not in `status[]` of the recording):
  `card.state`: `opening | recording | failing | stalled | unavailable | none` (a closed set; there is no other word); the card did not
  open — the place is not taken (`place_error.<карта>: refused`, chapter 4 Н1.4), a retry after 30 s; writing has stalled for longer than 10 s + 1 s per
  MiB of the ongoing write call to the card — `stalled`, the same timeout ×3 — the alarm `card.failing` (`cam`, `state`, `error`); `none` — there is no card, no trouble.
  `card.live_skipped_s` — the seconds of live frames that the card skipped while catching up with the recording.
- The answer to a range claim — in pieces per recording; a closed card or a segment not read to the end — a refusal, not emptiness.
- **Other cameras of the box** (not `cfw://local`). The card recorder answers the claim: it reads the segments itself, they have no door `/samples` (the card is not an engine volume), it uploads by `POST /ingest/upload` by the camera protocol (chapter 9 Н1.4, Н2): with the leaf of the role `recworker` into its own receiver, with the token `stream` into another's. The claim comes in the poll answer to the pusher, therefore the pusher of a camera with a recording on the card is the recorder of this card (chapter 9 Н1.2). These cameras have no word `stream`: the export window is «молчит — окно» (silent — a window) of chapter 8 Н1.2.
- The card recorder does not write a recording of a camera whose holder is not on the server of the volume, even if the row exists (`waiting`, `reason` `edge volume serves its own server`; the debt is copied) — this is the guard: the tables door
  does not refuse such a row (`must_match` at `home` is removed, V/02.1). The holder's server — by the camera heartbeat (chapter 4 Н1.1); the heartbeat is not read — not absent: an ongoing recording goes on, a new one is not started.
- The card pre-recording = the camera ring; a shortage — the alarm `card.prebuffer.short` (`cam`, `reach_s`, `need_s`).
- The frame time line is the camera's (V/01.6 Н1.4); the card stores its note and marks the segments by it.

### Н1.8 Volume for evidence

`incidents` accepts no recordings (`admits: false`, P/04 Н1.3); the holder of the place copies the labelled intervals there (chapter
7, the stream `<запись>/e0`); the volume is also a ring (`archive.keep.lost`); the door shows it without a ceiling (chapter 5).

### Н1.9 Recording without a live stream

The decision is ADR-0107 and its addendum (2). A recording with a non-empty `replica_from` (V/02.1 Н1.1) does not take the camera stream: the recorder fetches video from the source into its own volume by the mechanism of Н1.6 and chapter 8. If the camera has no other consumer, the holder does not read the stream (V/01.2 Н1.2в).

**Where to and where from.** Where to — a volume `local`, `network`; there is no home — as for a recording without a home (P/04 Н1.3). Where from — `device`: the device archive of the camera (V/01.5); a name: a recording of the same `cam` and the same `stream` on a volume `local`, `network`, `edge`, including another `replica`. A recording on `backup` is never a source: its minutes come into the primary by Н1.6. A source on `edge` is the row `cam-<cam>-<том>` (Н1.7).

**The truth is at the recorder** (the tables door does not reconcile other rows; the order in which rows are written bypasses any earlier check): on each pass it walks the chain `replica_from` from its own recording and judges it. The first condition from the top that holds — `waiting`, `reason`:

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| a row of a recording in the chain or of its volume does not read (not read rather than removed: what is accumulated stands, the plan is not built) | `replica source unreadable: <имя>` |
| the home of the recording is a volume other than `local`, `network` | `replica home is not local or network` |
| `device`, `stream` is not empty: the device archive is the archive of the camera (V/01.5 Н1.1а) | `replica from device: stream is set` |
| `device`, `cam` is `ref:<ref>`: another cluster's loopback is not a source | `replica from device: other cluster` |
| the chain came back to a recording that has already been in it — at every recording of the loop | `replica loop: <имя>` |
| the source row is absent or is a tombstone | `replica source gone: <имя>` |
| the source is of another `cam` or another `stream` | `replica source is another stream: <имя>` |
| the home of the source is a volume `backup` | `replica source on a backup volume: <имя>` |

The source is removed — `waiting`, what is accumulated lives out its own retention, there are no new minutes. A source `enabled: false` is not removed: its minutes still exist, the copy takes them.

**Source coverage.** `device` — `coverage` of the holder (V/01.5 Н1.1); a recording on the card — `coverage` of its heartbeat (Н1.6); otherwise — `/spans` of the recording at all live doors (chapter 5 Н1.4; not on another cluster's loopback), their union: the minutes of a recording lie in several volumes (V/02.4 Н1.9). A door did not answer — it said nothing about its volume: this pass does not move the start of the coverage, «loss» (below) is not counted.

**Plan.** The hole is counted over the whole coverage of the source, not inside what is written:

```
ours  = the visible coverage of the recording in the volume (chapter 5)
lo    = max(start of the source coverage, now − retention of the recording, visible from, to)
hi    = min(end of the source coverage, now − 900)   # a value without grounds, checked by measurement
holes = [lo, hi) − ours − coverage of other doors − «the source does not have it» − abandoned − landing, not yet visible
        − landed since the last write of to
```

Frames, pieces, landing — Н1.6, chapter 8 Н1.3: `…/backfill` under the recorder's epoch, the oldest hole first. From a device the frames go by V/02.8 Н1.3, the stream is only the main one. `replica` has no budget `BACKFILL_BUDGET` and no window `BACKFILL_WINDOW`: this is the only path of its minutes. Per pass — one range per source, no longer than 600 s, from a device — one download (chapter 8 Н1.2); for a card source `stream.lagging` decides. «The source does not have it» is lifted not only by a change of `coverage`: minutes written inside do not change it, therefore once a day the coverage is reconciled by the listing of the source (`/recordings`, `/spans`). The plan of chapter 8 Н1.1 does not run for `replica`, it has one scheduler.

**Copy reached up to.** `rec/state/replica/<запись>` (`unit.state.replica`, P/03.6 Н1.7) is written by the holder of the recording and read by it too. `to` is the start of the oldest of the plan holes and of what is landing at the end of the pass; there is neither — `max(hi, lo)`: for a young source `hi` is below the start of the coverage; it only grows. What is landing may break off or die with the holder, therefore `to` does not overtake it: otherwise minutes of the source would be lost silently. What has landed since the last write of `to` is not a hole: the ring might have evicted it, and the re-copy would never end; this is process memory. What the ring evicted is not a hole: a minute below `to` does not enter the plan, therefore **a minute is copied a finite number of times**. The row is written on the pass where `to` grew; the death of the holder repeats once what has landed since the last write.

- The state does not survive a new `revision` (P/03.6 Н1.7). The holder reads the former row (P/03.6 Н3): the same `born` of the recording, the same `of` and `born` of the source — the values continue; otherwise the plan starts anew: a change of the source, the source created again. The minutes of the former source stay in the volume and are subtracted as `ours`.
- No row — `to` 0. The row is not in form — not read, rather than absent: there is no plan, the holder writes it anew by the rule P/03.6 Н1.7 `unreadable` with `to` = the end of `ours`: the ring might have evicted the minutes below it, and a re-copy without memory would never end.
- The recording moved to another volume: `to` moves with the state, the former minutes are at another door, the new volume does not fetch them.

**«Does not have» above `to`.** `to` goes past minutes that the source does not have, and the source may write them later: the primary came back and closed the hole (Н1.6). Therefore «the source does not have it» of this recording lives in the state too: `absent` — up to 64 intervals, the extra oldest one is removed and its seconds are added to `dropped` of the state, in the heartbeat `replica_absent_dropped_s`: a removed «does not have» the source may write later, such a minute will not come, but the loss is not silent. There is no merging: it would return into an interval what has landed. The daily reconciliation returns to the plan the intervals that the source now shows, below `to` too: the plan is the holes above plus `absent` ∩ coverage. What has landed is subtracted from an interval; below the start of the coverage an interval is removed and does not go into `lost`. The ring does not touch `absent`: these minutes were never in the volume, therefore finiteness holds: a minute from `absent` lands once.

**Loss.** The start of the source coverage overtook `to`, `to` > 0: `[to, начало)` (`[to, start)`) left the source ring before the copy. Their seconds are added to `lost`, `to` becomes the start of the coverage. The event `replica.lost` (ADR-0112 addendum (6) item 3): written by the recorder under the epoch of the recording into its bucket, `of` — `cam/<камера>` by `about`, class `observation`; the fields `cam`, `recording`, `from`, `to` of the lost interval, `occurred` — the moment the loss was seen. Repeats are collapsed by `events.suppress` of the manifest: a window of 60 s per pair `cam`, `recording`. The event did not arrive — the loss stays in `lost` and in the heartbeat. `lost` is an upper estimate: this interval includes holes of the source and minutes landed above `to`, and there is nothing to separate them by: the start is gone. The first plan (`to` 0) has no loss: what the source still keeps is taken.

**Source larger than the volume.** The size estimate — `bytes` of the spans (chapter 5 Н1.4), for a device and a card — the rate of what has landed (bytes per second) times the length of the coverage; there is no rate yet — no estimate. The estimate is larger than the volume ring (`volume_quota`, V/02.4 Н1.8) — `over_quota: true`: the recording says so in advance, the copy goes on, the ring decides the depth.

**Live and `replica`.** `replica_from` is filled in at a live recording — the stream is removed as at a change of `stream`: the pipeline ends (V/02.4 Н1.2), what is written stays, a new `revision`, the boundary of the door is `to`. It is cleared — the recording is live, the pipeline starts under the same epoch, catch-up recording closes the hole (chapter 8).

**Words of the recording.**

| What | At `replica` |
|---|---|
| `phase` | `running` — the source is fit: the copy is going or has caught up; `waiting` — the table above; a source on backoff (Н1.6) does not change the phase |
| `via`, `written_through`, `prebuffered`, `coverage` | no fields: there is no live stream; readers of `written_through` do not count it (Н1.4, chapter 9 Н1.3) |
| `last_frame_at` | the landing time of the last piece |
| `replica_lag_s` | `now − to`, a number ≥ 0; the state is not read — no field |
| `replica_lost_s` | `lost`, none — 0 |
| `over_quota` | `true` or no field |
| `when` | not read |
| `until`, `enabled`, `retention_days`, `min_depth_days`, `audio` | as for a recording: `audio` is not read, sound comes with the pieces (chapter 8 Н1.3) |

The new words are read by the series of the manifest and by the page; a value not in form — the series and the caption are silent: these are words about the copy, the copy does not depend on them.

**Limits.**
- A recorder for 16 cameras MUST receive the archive 16 times faster than real time, otherwise the lag only grows: there is one download per device, `max_playbacks` exhausted — 503, the source is on backoff up to 600 s, therefore the cameras of one device lag unevenly. It is measured on the first run with a live recorder. Between archives there is no such limit.
- A copy has a lag of no less than 900 s.
- The volume is the ring of the volume's recordings: the copy shortens the depth for its neighbours (Н1.2).
- A recording on `backup` is not a source: with a primary `P` and a `replica` `R`, the minutes of a failure come into `R` only through `P`. `P` is removed — there are no failure minutes in `R`.
- A label copies all recordings of the camera (chapter 7 Н1.1): a `replica` and its source give two copies of the same frames.
- A camera with a single `replica` has no live detection on the stream: the detector is itself a consumer and holds the stream (V/01.2 Н1.2в), the built-in one does not look into the stream (V/01.4 Н1.4в).
- `near` of the camera (V/01.1 Н1.3) may lead the holder to the recorder of a `replica`: the form `prefer` does not express exceptions; the primary of that camera goes through the receiver.

## Н3 Pass

```
places (chapter 4): edge — only the recorder of this card's recordings; backup/edge — exclusive: a holder without other places, only recordings of its own home
volume backup, when: always → waiting (Н1.4)
backup offline: decide write/hold (Н1.4) → release the ring or hold; HOLD_AFTER — restart on hold
replica: judge the chain (Н1.9) → source coverage → loss → plan → copy in pieces → to
primary: holes (chapter 8) → sources: the device, then the backups (Н1.6) → copy in pieces
quota: differs from maxVolumeSize → resize (growth) or wait for shrink_confirmed (decrease)
```

**Races.** The primary stopped writing between heartbeats — the backup starts from the ring (66 s cover the noticing timeout, Н1.4; limit — Н1.5);
the primary came back before the backup noticed — a new start, the ring is released (Н1.4); both write, the backup
returns to hold after `HOLD_AFTER`, the archives overlap. Two copies of one hole from two sources land in one
stream `…/backfill`, the key `(epoch, live)` is equal, and the epoch rule (chapter 5) does not separate them: a moment is held once by what is landing and by
«only holes» (chapter 8 Н1.1, Н1.3). What is landing is process memory: a restart under the same epoch loses it, a piece lands
twice (contract «Последовательность»); then the moment is held once by the merging of the piece (chapter 5 Н1.6), `/samples` gives both, the receiver
drops the group (chapter 8 Н1.3).
