# V/02.5 — Visibility, retention, timeline and the archive door: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`02.5-rec.norms.md`](../02.5-rec.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 5 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../02-rec.md),
[HTTP contract](../02-rec.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](../02-rec.README.md), [acceptance](../../acceptance/5/README.md),
[the contract `obsd`](../02-rec.obsd.contract.md). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The chapter sets norms for what is visible from a volume and to whom: the visibility of what is recorded, retention as a ceiling, the timeline of a recording with the rule
«one moment — one epoch», the archive door of the recorder — for processes (`/spans`, `/samples`) and for the page (`/timeline`,
`/segment`, `/keeps`), fMP4 pieces, the door address and the archive depth. The engine protocol is the contract `obsd`; streams in a volume —
[chapter 3](../02.3-rec.norms.md); the page — lesson V/11 (what it calls — here).

Norm ids are `V/02.5-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Outward — Unix seconds (`/spans`, `/samples`: `from`, `to`, `start`, `end`) and Unix milliseconds (`/timeline`:
`start_ms`, `end_ms`; the path `/segment`); `from`, `to` are finite numbers, the parsing — Н2.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — `rec/recordings/<id>`
(retention, the depth floor), the table `rec/keeps/*` (keeps are visible beyond the ceiling), `rec/epoch/<id>` (fencing), the heartbeats `rec/*` and
`cam/*`; writes — event rows `archive.shallow` (the bucket of the recording), log rows `archive.read` (Н1.8).

### Н1.1 Visibility

- **Recorded ≠ visible:** the door answers only about what is visible (the contract «Видимость»: the blocks closed by the moment the reader is
  mounted); every question is a fresh reader; what has just been recorded appears later. When what is recorded must be seen
  now (the keep copy, chapter 7), the recorder closes the block (`seal`: close the writer and open it again).
- The recorder plans by what is visible: holes are only up to the end of the visible coverage; what is copied but not yet visible is not a hole.

### Н1.2 Retention is a ceiling

- `retention_days` of a recording is how far back the door shows video: visible from `now − retention_days × 86400`; what lies in the volume
  is decided by the ring (the contract «Кольцо»), nothing is deleted by age.
- The ceiling applies to the **answer**, not to the question: every span and every frame is trimmed by the visible set — `[видно с,
  ∞)` ∪ the intervals of the keeps of this recording (`keeps`, chapter 7): what is kept is visible beyond the ceiling as well. At the lower edge of the ceiling a frame goes only if
  its whole group is visible (from key frame to key frame): the output starts from the first key frame not earlier than
  the edge, the lead-in (Н1.4) does not cross the edge. A keep interval does not cut a group: a group that crosses it is visible whole.
- The volume `incidents` (chapter 6) shows everything (there is no ceiling).
- The recording row is read for every question: no row or a tombstone — 30 days; the store is silent — the last row read
  (30 days — only if it has never been read); the value is not a finite non-negative number — visible from `now` (hiding
  is safe), the count `ceilings_garbled` in the heartbeat. The keeps were not read — the ceiling stands.
- The ceiling does not promise depth: the floor `min_depth_days` is an observation (Н1.9).

### Н1.3 Timeline and «one moment — one epoch»

- The timeline of a recording is all its video streams in the volume (`<запись>/e<n>`, `…/backfill`, `…/e0`, chapter 3), from the index (the contract
  `READER_TIMELINE`; a request over many days the recorder MAY cut into windows), sorted by `(start, epoch)`;
  touching pieces (seam ≤ 2 s) are glued into coverage.
- **A fenced epoch** (`epoch` < the epoch of the recording in the store, `rec/epoch/<id>`; the copy `e0` is not fenced) is shown and
  marked `fenced: true`; in the automatic choice of frames it **loses** (the rule below), it is not excluded. The row
  `rec/epoch/<id>` is not read — `fenced: false` at the door, on `/timeline` — the word of the door; a row not by the form — the log.
- **One moment — one epoch** (`authoritative`): the boundaries are `from`, `to` and all starts and ends of spans between them; each
  elementary interval is given to the senior of those covering it by the key `(epoch, live)` over all streams of the recording: the senior
  epoch wins, at an equal epoch the live stream wins over the catch-up recorded one; the copy `e0` loses to any real epoch;
  an equal key (one stream at two doors) — the door with the smaller slot name; adjacent intervals of one span are glued. A fenced epoch is younger than the epoch of the recording and therefore loses to its streams:
  it is given only where the senior one does not cover it (the frames of a fenced holder are a duplicate, not damage, V/02.4 Н1.9).
  The rule is one for automatic choice — `/samples` without `stream` and catch-up recording (chapter 8: such coverage is recorded).
- **Explicit choice** — `/samples` with `stream` and the piece `/segment/<запись>/e<эпоха>/…` (the epoch and the source — from the path, Н1.6):
  the frames of this stream, including a fenced one; the rule of Н1.3 there acts only between doors holding this stream.
- **Examples** (the epoch of the recording is 4): `e3` on `[100, 200)`, `e4` on `[150, 300)` — automatically `[100, 150)` from `e3`,
  `[150, 300)` from `e4`; `e3` on `[100, 200)`, `e4` from 200 — `[100, 200)` from `e3` (not covered by an unfenced one); `stream=3` on
  `[100, 300)` — the frames of `e3` for `[100, 200)` and nothing after.

### Н1.4 Archive door: routes for processes

The contract is [OpenAPI](../02-rec.api.openapi.yaml). Admittance — Н2.

| Route | Answer 200 |
|---|---|
| `GET /spans/<запись>?from&to` | `{unit, spans: [{start, end, epoch, source: live \| backfill, bytes, fenced}], current_epoch, held_since, now}` — what is visible (Н1.1, Н1.2); `bytes` — the sum of `size` of the span's intervals in the index, trimming by the ceiling does not recompute it; `current_epoch` — the epoch of the recording at this recorder; `held_since` — the wall clock of the recorder when it took the epoch (`null` — the volume `incidents`, the recording is not its own, or `replica`; without a volume the route answers 503); `copied_to?` — only at its own `replica`: the boundary below which the door speaks for the minutes (chapter 6 Н1.9, chapter 7 Н1.3), no copy yet — 0; `now` — the clock of the door: the reader translates `held_since` to its own clock |
| `GET /samples/<запись>?from&to[&stream=<epoch>[/backfill]][&track=audio]` | SMPL records in a row (the contract «Время и запись SMPL»), `application/octet-stream`; without `stream` — by the rule of Н1.3 (each stretch from the owner epoch, with a lead-in from a key frame not later than the start of the stretch, within what is visible, Н1.2); with `stream` — the frames only of this stream, a fenced one too (Н1.3 «Explicit choice»); `track=audio` — the audio frames of the same streams over the same stretches, without a lead-in; HTTP/1.1 — chunked, without `Content-Length`; empty — 200 without a body; a refusal after the first byte — a break without the last chunk |

The defaults and parsing of `from`, `to` — Н2. No volume — 503 `{"error": "no volume open here"}` («not possible now», not
«no recording»).

### Н1.5 Archive door: routes for the page

`door: {routes: [timeline, segment, keeps]}` of the manifest — the issue of the door and the token is [P/06.5 Н3](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.5-console.norms.md)
`/where/<id>`, `/where/<table>/<place>`; the token check is [P/03.4 Н3](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)
«токен двери у держателя», the door token contract (refusal — 403 `{"error": "forbidden"}`; on 403 the page re-requests
`/where` once).

| Route | Answer 200 |
|---|---|
| `GET /timeline/<запись>?from&to` | `{spans, unreachable, unavailable}`: `spans` — a list `[{start_ms, end_ms, epoch, source?, fenced?, yields?}]` by `(start_ms, epoch)` — the streams of the recording from all live doors of recorders (their `/spans`, question timeout 5 s, body ≤ 16 MiB) and, for a recording with an empty `stream`, the spans of the device archive (`{epoch: 0, source: device, yields: true}` from `coverage` of the camera holder, V/01.5 Н1.1; whose stream is in the archive it does not say, Н1.1а); `unreachable` — the slot names of the recorders whose door did not answer or answered incompletely; `unavailable` — `<том>@<сервер>` of the volumes of this recording without a live holder; both are lists, empty ones — `[]` (unavailability is in the body: the answer headers are not visible to a page from another origin, the contract `cors`) |
| `GET /segment/<запись>/e<эпоха>/<fromMs>-<toMs>[.backfill \| .device].mp4` | an fMP4 piece (Н1.6): `.mp4` — the live stream of the epoch (`e0` — the keep copy), `.backfill.mp4` — its catch-up recording, `.device.mp4` (only `e0`) — the device archive; HTTP/1.1 — chunked; a door that dropped off before the first byte — a piece without its frames (the page sees the incompleteness by `/timeline`, the log — the field `unreachable`, Н1.8) |
| `POST /keeps/<метка>/verify?recording=<id>` | reconciliation of the keep copy — chapter 7 |

A live door is a recorder whose readable heartbeat has changed within 45 s by the reader's clock (`fresh_for`, P/03.4 Н1.1). A volume of this recording
without a live holder is the `volume` of the heartbeat of a non-live recorder where the recording is in `status[]`, or its `home`; `<сервер>` is the
`server` of this heartbeat; no heartbeat — the `server` of the volume row, otherwise `?`.

### Н1.6 The fMP4 piece

Assembly: `/spans` at the live doors → the spans of the epoch and source of the piece → Н1.3 over them → frames at the owner door `/samples`
with `stream` in chunks of 60 s (timeout 30 s) → merge by time, each moment once (a frame earlier than the end of the previous one is
skipped) → before the first key frame — skipped → the file; no frames after skipping — 404 `nothing recorded`. On the wire:

- `ftyp` (`iso5`, minor 512, compatible `iso5 iso6 mp41 avc1`); `moov`: `mvhd` (version 0, creation and modification times 0,
  timescale 1000, duration 0, rate 1.0, volume 1.0, identity matrix, `next_track_ID` 2), one `trak`: `tkhd`
  (version 0, flags 3, times 0, `track_ID` 1, duration 0, volume 0, identity matrix, width and height — from `sub`,
  16.16), `mdia`: `mdhd` (timescale 1000, duration 0, language `und`), `hdlr` `vide` with the name `VideoHandler` (with
  a terminating zero), `minf`: `vmhd` (flags 1), `dinf/dref` (`url ` with flag 1), `stbl`: `stsd` → `avc1` → `avcC`, empty
  `stts`, `stsc`, `stsz`, `stco`; `mvex/trex` (`track_ID` 1, defaults 0);
- `avcC` — the profile from the SPS of the first key frame of the piece (not from the camera row): `[1, sps[1], sps[2], sps[3], 0xFF, 0xE0 | nSPS]`,
  SPS and PPS with a u16 length; width and height — from `sub` of its SMPL record; no SPS or PPS — 415 `not playable`; a change of parameters
  inside the piece is not handled;
- a sample is AVCC (each NAL with a u32 BE length; NAL 7, 8, 9, 12 are dropped); the duration is `max(1, end − begin)` ms;
- a fragment on every key frame: `moof` (`mfhd` seq — from 1, +1 per fragment; `traf`: `tfhd` with `default-base-is-moof`, `tfdt` v1 u64 ms,
  `trun` with flags `0x000701` — the data offset from the first byte of `moof`) + `mdat`; sample flags: key — `2<<24`, other —
  `(1<<24) | (1<<16)`;
- `tfdt` of the first fragment is 0, then — the sum of the durations of the previous samples, a gap does not shift the timeline (the position within the day
  is known to the page); the same interval — the same file byte for byte.

**Audio** (ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 7). In the interval of the piece there are frames of the audio stream of its source (`…/audio`, `…/backfill/audio`, `e0/audio`; `/samples` with `track=audio`) — the file carries a second track;
there are none — the bytes above without changes. A refusal of `/samples` for audio is like a refusal of frames. The differences of the file:

- `moov`: `next_track_ID` 3; a second `trak`: `tkhd` (version 0, flags 3, `track_ID` 2, volume 1.0, width and height 0), `mdhd` as for video, `hdlr` `soun` with the name `SoundHandler`, `minf`: `smhd`, `dinf/dref`,
  `stbl`: `stsd` → `mp4a` (channels and rate — from `sub` of the first frame, sample size 16) → `esds` (object type 0x40, stream type 0x05, `AudioSpecificConfig` from the ADTS of the first frame), empty tables; `trex` with `track_ID` 2;
- the duration of a video sample is the difference of `begin` of adjacent frames of the fragment, for the last frame of the fragment — `max(1, end − begin)` ms;
- an audio sample is a frame without the ADTS header, duration — `max(1, end − begin)` ms, flags — `2<<24`; a change of audio parameters inside the piece is not handled;
- a fragment carries the audio frames with time from the start of its key frame, within the length of the fragment on the timeline — the sum of the durations of its video samples; a frame that does not fit whole, and frames earlier than the first key frame — skipped. As the second `traf` of the same `moof`:
  `track_ID` 2, `tfdt` — `tfdt` of the video plus the offset of the first audio frame from the key frame, `trun` with the same flags, the offset — from the first byte of `moof`, the bytes — in the same `mdat` after the video; no frames — no `traf`.

**Limit.** Audio in a video gap is lost: there is no gap on the timeline of the piece (above), the audio track goes by fragments and does not overlap itself. Audio follows video: a stretch whose owner by Н1.3 has no audio is silent, even though another epoch stores audio.

`.device.mp4`: the recorder reads `/playback/<камера>?from&to` at the camera holder (V/01.5; the role certificate `recworker`) and
gives the bytes as they came; for a recording with a non-empty `stream`, without a holder or coverage — 404 `no device archive`; longer than 3600 s — 400 `range too long` (Н4); 503, no answer or another refusal of the holder — 503 `busy`, in `detail` — its word.

Piece limits: longer than 3600 s — 400 `bad range`; at the same time — 2 pieces per door, 1 per person (`user` of the token); beyond that —
503 `busy` with `Retry-After: 5`; connection limits — P/03.4 Н1.7 `door_limits`.

### Н1.7 Door address

The rule of the door address of a process is [P/03.4 Н1.7](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) (`kit`): with the kit of the role `recworker` the door listens on the addresses to which
`SERVER_NAME` resolves, and publishes in the heartbeat `url` = `https://<SERVER_NAME>:<порт>`; without a kit — the loopback,
`http://127.0.0.1:<порт>`; another `ARCHIVE_HOST` (the subsystem file, optional) or `0.0.0.0` — start refusal, code 2. The port is `ARCHIVE_PORT` of the subsystem file
`/etc/rec/rec.env` (not set or 0 — the OS port). Server names are a deployment premise (DNS or the operator's hosts).
The same rule is at the door of the camera holder (V/01.5 Н1.2); RTSP delivery — only the loopback (V/01.2 Н1.3).

### Н1.8 Serving log

Every serving of a piece `/segment`, including `.device.mp4`, is a row `archive.read` in the log `audit/recworker/e1` (the event log contract): `user`
(the token), `media` (`rec/<запись>/e<эпоха>[/backfill|/device]/<from>-<to>`, the boundaries — whole seconds), `addr`, `status`, `bytes`, `target`, `of` (`rec/<запись>`),
`sha256` (only for a whole file), `unreachable`, `broken`; a part of a file smaller than 1 MiB — no more often than one row per minute per pair (who,
`media`); `unreachable` — the recorders whose door dropped off while the piece was being assembled. The log of a door is `audit/<роль>/e1` with the common
fields, the kinds are named by the owner lesson. The CORS of the door is `cors` of the [role certificate contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/role-certificate.md)
(through P/03.4 Н1.7) as is, there is no list of its own: a preflight `OPTIONS` of an allowed origin — before admittance (including for
`POST /keeps/<метка>/verify`), the CORS headers — also on refusals (the page reads the refusal).

### Н1.9 Depth

Once a minute (not with a lost engine): `depth_days` of the recording = `(now − начало покрытия) / 86400` (holes are not subtracted,
the ceiling has no effect), into the status of the recording; the alarm `archive.shallow` (class `alarm`, the bucket of the recording under its epoch, fields `cam`,
`depth_days`, `min_depth_days`) — the floor is set, the ring has closed (`firstBlockId > 0`), the depth is less than the floor, the epoch of the recording is
at this recorder; repeat — once a day; removal of the condition clears the mark.

## Н2 Doors

The archive door of the recorder is [OpenAPI](../02-rec.api.openapi.yaml); one listener (Н1.7). The class `mixed`
of the [role certificate contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/role-certificate.md) as is (P/03.4 Н1.7): each
route of `door.routes` of the manifest has the admittance `role: […]` or `token`. With the kit of the role `recworker` — TLS; the client certificate
is requested optionally and is checked in the handshake. The process routes (`/spans`, `/samples`; `role: [recworker,
detjobworker, expworker]` — `expworker` of the export V/08.7, ADR-0117 item 1.3) — the words of the class `role`: no leaf — 401 `no_role`; not exactly one role in the SAN — 403 `no_role`; a role not among
the admitted ones — 403 `wrong_role`; a signature of the leaf or of the CA outside `sig` or a serial in `revoked` — 403 `bad_chain`; a chain
rejected by TLS — a handshake refusal. The page routes (`/timeline`, `/segment`, `/keeps`) — the door token (Н1.5; the unit of the token
is the recording from the path or `?recording=`: held by this recorder or lying in its volume, the door token contract «Токен места»;
for `verify` «lying» is the recording of the label, even if there are no frames yet): absent or invalid — 403 `forbidden`. Without a kit — the loopback:
the process routes are closed — 401 `no_role` (a log line at start), the page routes are open; for a recorder without a kit
the processes of other recorders, `detjob` and `exp` do not read its volume. The door has no page `/metrics` of its own (V/02.4 Н1.8). A cluster
of one before the first publication — 503 `not_ready` (P/02.4 Н1.10).

**Request parsing** is the common parsing of [P/01.1 Н2.1](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md) (through
[P/03.4 Н1.7](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) `door_limits`): query as
`application/x-www-form-urlencoded`, the request line and headers, the body frame, early refusal — as there (the body of `POST verify`
is not read: a refusal before the end of the body — by P/01.1 Н2.1, the connection is closed). The deviations of the archive door:
- **the path** is compared with the routes byte for byte, without decoding and normalization: `/spans/door/`, `//spans/door`,
  an empty segment, a `%`-notation in a segment (`/spans/do%6Fr`) — not a route, 404 `no such path`; the segment `<запись>` — as
  written, bytes not in UTF-8 — 404 `no such path`; a `/segment` path not by the form — 404 `no such segment`: `<fromMs>`, `<toMs>` —
  decimal digits, leading zeros are allowed; `toMs ≤ fromMs` or `.device` not at `e0` — not by the form;
- **a method** other than the one declared at the route (`GET`, for `verify` — `POST`; `HEAD` — as `GET`) — 405 `method not allowed`;
  an unknown path — 404 with any method;
- **`from`, `to`** — Unix seconds, the notation of a JSON number (RFC 8259 `number`), a finite value: a repeated parameter, an empty
  value (`from=`), another notation (`NaN`, `nan`, `inf`, `0x10`), a value not in UTF-8 or non-finite after parsing (`1e400`)
  — 400 `bad range`; `from > to` — 400 `bad range`; `from == to` — an empty interval (`/spans` — an empty `spans`,
  `/samples` — 200 without a body, `/timeline` — an empty `spans`); `stream`, `recording` — repeated or empty — 400 `bad range`; `track` — repeated or not `audio` — 400 `bad range`;
  other query parameters are not read;
- **defaults** (all routes with `from`, `to`): without `from` — 0, without `to` — the clock of the door + 86400.

The order of checks: path (404) → method (405) → admittance (handshake; 401, 403) → query (400) → no volume (503) → answer. For
`verify` the token unit is in the query: `recording` absent, empty or repeated — 400 `bad range` before the check of `unit`, after the check of the signature
and validity of the token.

## Н3 Pass

The door has no pass of its own: every question is a fresh reader of the volume (Н1.1), the recording row and the keeps — for every question
(Н1.2). Depth — once a minute by the pass of the recorder (Н1.9).

**Races.** The recording moved to another recorder between `/timeline` and `/segment` — the piece is assembled from all live doors
by their `/spans`; a door that dropped off before the first byte — a piece without its frames (`/timeline` will name it in `unreachable`); after
it — a break, the page asks again. Two questions
during recording — each sees what was closed by the moment of its mounting.
