# V/02.4 — The recorder: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/02.4-rec.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 4 of lesson V/02 «Запись» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/02-rec.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](02-rec.README.md), [acceptance](../acceptance/4/README.md), manifest [`rec.subsystem.yaml`](../rec.subsystem.yaml).

The recorder is the worker `recworker` that has the recording in its assignment. The chapter sets norms for what `rec` knows: where the recorder

a volume and stops, the writer watchdog, the heartbeat words. The worker runtime, places, recording windows, substitution, stop are
the norms of [P/03](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03-worker.README.md) by reference; the engine protocol is the [`obsd` contract](02-rec.obsd.contract.md);
the stream in the volume and the volume refusals are [chapter 3](02.3-rec.norms.md).

Norm ids are `V/02.4-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

### Н1.1 Source

The recorder takes the stream from the camera holder (V/01.2 Н1.3), not from the device. A `replica` recording takes no stream and is not covered by this section: its source and words are chapter 6 Н1.9:

- the holder is a live `cam` worker whose element `status[]` names the **recording stream** with `phase: running`: the `stream` of the recording row,
  empty — `cam` (the main stream, V/01.1 Н1.1а); the heartbeats are read once per pass, there is no cache longer than a pass; the holder
  is looked up by the fields of the recording row, not by its id;
- a stream not of this camera is not a source: the recorder reads the row `cam/cameras/<cam>` and, with a non-empty `stream`, the row
  `cam/cameras/<stream>` (its `main` = `cam`); otherwise it does not record: the row `cam` is a stream (its `main` is not empty) — `waiting`, `reason`
  `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>` (the door does not check this, V/01.1 Н1.1а); the `type` of the row `cam` is not `camera` — `waiting`, `reason`
  `camera <cam> has no stream`: it has no frames and no recording (V/01.1 Н1.1б), the door does not check this; a stream not of this camera — `reason` `stream: not a stream of <cam>`
  (the door checks the same `must_match`, but the target could disappear or change after the row was written); for a camera of another
  cluster (`ref:<ref>`) with an empty `stream` its main stream is recorded (V/01.6 Н1.1), with a non-empty one — `reason` «stream: other cluster»;
- the choice of the kind exists only for a holder on its **own** server: shared memory (`live_shm`, the share `cam.shm`, `worker.uses`;
  the address is exactly the heartbeat string `shm:///…` of the holder's own epoch, it is not built by the recorder, V/01.2 Н1.3);
  there is no `live_shm` address in the heartbeat or the share did not open — RTSP on the loopback of its own server (`live_url`); `live_shm` or
  `live_url` is in the element but not in the form of the family `cam-heartbeat-words` (V/01) — **unread, not absent**: do not switch
  to RTSP, keep an open source, do not open a source not yet opened, `reason` «`cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>`»,
  the next heartbeat — again; in the recording status —
  `via: shm | rtsp`; the holder and the recorder are on one server (`near`/`home` of the camera, V/01.1 Н1.3);
- a holder on **another** server is not a source: RTSP is loopback only (V/01.2 Н1.3, ADR-0075 window 2); between servers the stream
  goes through the receiver of the recording cluster (chapter 9), by a subscription to the recording stream — `cam` and `stream` (chapter 9 Н1.3 «Камера и поток», Н2); the status
  says this, and not «nobody holds it»;
- a camera of another cluster (the field `cam` is `ref:<ref>`) — the source from the book `domain/cam/sources` (the copy of its own cluster; for
  the cluster that holds the domain — the row of its own cluster, «Data» above; V/01.6 Н1.2; chapter 9);
- the store is silent — the last source remains; a recording never read does not start;
- there is no source (nobody holds the camera, there is no volume) — the start fails: phase `waiting`, `reason` «camera held by nobody»
  (not `why`: this is a word of the subsystem), the backoff — P/03.3 Н1.2. A camera `live: on-demand` is, for
  recording, like `always`: the recording is a consumer, its row wakes the stream (V/01.2 Н1.2в; ADR-0103 addendum (7) item 3, (8) item 2), until the first frames — `waiting`.

**The cause of `waiting`** is the first condition met from the top; the words are a contract:

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| a volume `backup`, `when: always` (chapter 6 Н1.4) | `a backup volume takes when: offline` |
| a volume `edge`, the camera holder is on another server (chapter 6 Н1.7) | `edge volume serves its own server` |
| a backup `offline` recording, the holder of the stream is in `idle` | not `waiting`: phase `standby` (chapter 6 Н1.4) |
| the row `cam/cameras/<cam>` is a stream (its `main` is not empty) | `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>` |
| the `type` of the row `cam/cameras/<cam>` is not `camera` | `camera <cam> has no stream` |
| a camera of another cluster, the `stream` of the recording is not empty | `stream: other cluster` |
| there is no row `cam/cameras/<stream>` or its `main` ≠ `cam` | `stream: not a stream of <cam>` |
| a camera of another cluster: the book `sources` | the words of the book (chapter 9 Н1.2) |
| a recording of its own camera: the assignment names another cluster (chapter 9 Н1.1) | `assigned to another cluster` |
| a recording of its own camera is not started, the assignment is not read (chapter 9 Н1.1) | `assignment unreadable` |
| `live_shm` or `live_url` of the holder of its own server is not in form | `cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>` |
| the holder is on another server | the source is the receiver; `push_waiting` (chapter 9 Н1.3 «Запись из приёмника») |
| the source frames are not H.264 (Н1.2) | `codec not supported: <кодек>` |
| there is no holder with `phase: running` or there is no volume | `camera held by nobody` |

### Н1.2 Recording pipeline

- H.264 frames come one per buffer (`alignment=au`), the codec parameters are in each key frame (a sequence
  plays by itself); RTSP — TCP only, latency ≈ 200 ms; shared memory — with the capture time. Input that is not H.264 —
  nothing goes into the volume: `waiting`, `reason` `codec not supported: <кодек>`, the next pass checks the source again.
- **No losses in the pipeline:** the pipeline queue (≈ 200 frames) does not leak — a slow `PUT_MEDIA` slows the pipeline down;
  `QUEUE_FULL` is the engine queue, a loss up to a key frame (chapter 3 Н1.6); 8 s without a frame — the pipeline is dead (`watchdog`): a work failure of the unit (P/03.3), the restart is under the same epoch
  (P/03.3 Н3 step 2, P/03.2 Н3, ADR-0091 item 6); the same holds for a recording from the receiver (chapter 9 refers here).
- **Frame:** the time is the holder's capture time (V/01.2 Н1.3): the share — the time of its frame, RTSP — the RTCP Sender Report, before the first report — the recorder's clock minus the latency in the pipeline, a time step at the first report closes the sequence, the next one is by the report (the `obsd` contract «Поток»); before the first key frame and after
  an engine refusal — skip to the next key frame; the frame receiver always answers the pipeline «accepted» (an engine refusal does not
  bring the pipeline down); the frame outcomes — chapter 3 Н1.6.
- **Backup ring** (`when: offline`, chapter 6 Н1.5): the last 66 s are held in memory, the pipeline stands on hold until
  the start; lifting the hold releases the frames from the first key frame.
- **End of the pipeline:** the end of the stream (EOS) and waiting for it — a live recording ≤ 2 s, a released ring ≤ 10 s, a ring on
  hold — 0; then stop the pipeline, then close the sequence (`FINISH_MEDIA`); all pipelines of the worker
  drain simultaneously.

### Н1.2а Recording sound

The decision is ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 7; the codecs of the descriptions are by DriverPack 2026-10-02, checked again against a new version. The recording writes, next to the video, one `mic`, wherever the frames come from.

- **Source.** A holder on its own server (`via: shm | rtsp`) — an RTSP endpoint (V/01.2 Н1.3), over TCP: the recording's `audio` is 0 — `<live_url>/mic`, otherwise `<live_url>/mic/<audio>`; `live_url` is that of the element of the recording stream,
  also with `via: shm`; the recording opens the endpoint at the start of the pipeline. The receiver (chapter 9) — a subscription with the recording's `audio`, frames of the kind `audio`, the codec is the `codec` of the frame (chapter 9 Н1.3а).
- **Video does not wait for sound:** the phase, `watchdog`, the epoch and leaving the volume are judged by video only. `DESCRIBE` 404 — `no mic`; a failure, a break, a read that did not get through — `mic unread`, not «there is no microphone»; while there is no sound, the recorder asks the endpoint with the backoff of P/03.3 Н1.2 (`retry`): the `mic` could have been switched on, the description could have arrived.
- **Codec, at reception:** the recorder transcodes G.711 (`PCMU`, `PCMA`), G.726 (`G726-*`), PCM (`L16`) and MP2 (`MPA`) into AAC, and repacks AAC (`mpeg4-generic`, `MP4A-LATM`) into ADTS without transcoding; another codec — sound is not recorded, `audio_reason` below. A change of `codec` on the fly is a new sequence of the sound stream.
- **Into the volume** — the stream `<запись>/e<эпоха>/audio` (chapter 3 Н1.1), the frames by the `obsd` contract «Время и запись SMPL», the time is the video line: the holder's capture time, the RTCP Sender Report of the endpoint, at the receiver — the cluster clock; frames before the first report are not recorded; the outcomes — chapter 3 Н1.6.
- **The backup ring** (chapter 6 Н1.5), catch-up recording (chapter 8), the backup and retention copies (chapters 6, 7), the forwarder (chapter 9 Н1.8) carry sound.
- **Words** (Н1.8): recorded — `audio_codec`, the `rtpmap` name of the source; not recorded — `audio_reason`: `no mic`, `mic unread`, `no sound frames`, `codec not supported: <кодек>`. `no sound frames` — at the receiver: there has been no sound frame for `FIRST_FRAME_GRACE` = 20 s (chapter 9 Н1.3) while video goes; the first sound frame clears it.

### Н1.3 Volume — place

Volumes are only the rows of the table `volumes` (V/02.1 Н1.2): a platform place is a row of the table of places (`placement.places`, P/04 Н1.3,
P/03.2 Н1.3). There is no «server volume» of its own without a row and no pinned volume bypassing the places: a box where no
volume is declared records nothing (`place_by: ""`); a server disk is declared by the row `kind: local, server: <сервер>`.

- Which places the recorder takes: the volumes of its own server (`local`, `backup`, `incidents`, `edge` with `server` = its own) and network ones; `edge`
  (the card) — only the recorder of the recordings of this card (`affinity`, chapter 6). The order: own disks first.
- Taking a place — by P/03.2 Н3 «место: взять, держать, отпустить» (CAS; the place follows the name; the windows `WRITE_WINDOW` 40 s,
  `CLOSE_BY` 50 s); opening the volume is part of taking: first the CAS of the row, then open the volume (chapter 3 Н1.5). `wrong` — not a place:
  the next candidate, the row expires by its deadline, a retry — at the next lease step; `busy`, `away` — the place is taken, further by Н1.4.
- Holding: a renewal at each lease step and in substitution (substitution renews the place — P/03.4 Н3); before each
  `VOLUME_MOUNT_RW` and `seal` — confirm the place: a renewal before the call; the store is silent — an `own_place` is mounted,
  a strict one — only in the recording window; before each frame into a network volume — the place is confirmed in the recording window
  (chapter 3 Н1.6).
- Who needs a holder: the first recorder of the cluster (the first by slot name among those heard within `PEER_LOST_AFTER`, chapter 9 Н1.6) on
  each pass reconciles `rec/wanted/*` with the volumes: for an enabled volume — the row `why: archive` if there is `rec/used/<том>`, otherwise `why: keeps` if
  the volume is `incidents` and labels are set; there is no reason — there is no row. Whether the volume is held is judged by the platform (P/04 Н3 «нехватка()»). It writes only a change, cas
  by what it read (P/03.4 Н1.6). The volume table, `rec/used/<том>` or the labels are not read — the row of this volume does not change.
- The store is silent: own disk (the place `own_place`, the row names my server) is held indefinitely (`lease.unconfirmed_max:
  forever`: video carries the epoch in its name — a second writer gives a duplicate, not corruption); the rest — in the window (P/03.2 Н3 «место: взять, держать, отпустить»). The server is not
  named (`SERVER_NAME`) — all places are strict (`server_unsaid` in the heartbeat, P/03.4).

### Н1.4 Volume refusals: what the recorder does

The kinds of refusal — chapter 3 Н1.4. The recorder:

| Kind and when | Action |
|---|---|
| `wrong` on taking | the place is not taken (it did not open — it is not a place): `place_error.<том>: refused` in the heartbeat (P/03.4 Н1.1), the words are in `volume_error` and are kept until the recorder opens the volume; the next candidate; none left — `place_by` as before (`""` if there was no place), the recordings leave by P/04 (ADR-0091 item 9) |
| `wrong` in the middle of work | leave the volume (Н1.5); the volume is in `refused` for `REFUSED_FOR` = 600 s (do not take it again); the cause is in the heartbeat |
| `away` on own disk | hold the place with full capacity without a deadline; `archive_error`, `archive_failure`, `archive_away_since` |
| `away` on a network volume (the engine is silent) | each pass — `PING`; silent longer than `ENGINE_SILENT_FOR` = 300 s (counted from the first non-answer, an answer clears it) — leave the volume, `refused`; do not take new network volumes through a silent engine |
| `away` on a network volume, `PING` answers | hold the place, open the volume anew on each pass, without a deadline |
| `busy` under its own place | longer than `BUSY_FOR` = 600 s — the alarm `archive.volume.busy` (class `alarm`), leave the volume, `refused`; any non-`busy` refusal resets the count |
| an engine without `WRITER_ABANDON` | do not take the volume (`wrong`, the cause «engine too old» in `unservable`; V/02.2 Н1.4) |
| the place is lost (`HOLD_LOST`, the volume lock is foreign) | leave the volume |
| there is no volume row or `enabled: false` | leave the volume (Н1.5) |
| `url`, the key or the secret changed | close the writer by Н1.5, open the volume anew (chapter 3 Н1.5) under the same place |

The recorder MUST NOT release a place in the middle of work (`released`) (P/03.2 Н3: only at an orderly stop): the place of
a volume that was left is freed by its deadline of 45 + `HOLD_SKEW` s (ADR-0091 item 9).

### Н1.5 Leave the volume

**The end of closing** is one deadline for leaving a volume and for stopping (Н1.6): `min(подтверждение места + CLOSE_BY, SIGTERM + 33 с)`, that is, the earlier of the place confirmation + `CLOSE_BY` and SIGTERM + 33 s
(`CLOSE_BY` 50 s — P/03.2 Н1.2; the confirmation is taken as a snapshot at the start of leaving; for its own place, the start of leaving stands instead of the confirmation;
`SIGTERM + 33 с` — only at a stop). Draining the pipelines, `FINISH_MEDIA` and
`WRITER_CLOSE` wait for an answer until the end of closing (`WRITER_CLOSE` — no longer than the 30 s of the `obsd` contract); other calls, except
`WRITER_ABANDON`, have the timeout `OBSD_TIMEOUT` (chapter 2), not later than the end of closing. The rule is one and it is here; chapter 2 Н1.5 and Н1.6 of this chapter refer to it.

```
1. All recordings of the volume at once: stop the pipeline (Н1.2, end of the pipeline; FINISH_MEDIA), phase waiting (no volume, Н1.1).
2. Close the writer (WRITER_CLOSE), wait for an answer until the end of closing;
  a. closed or answered with an error (an answer with an error is an answer, the contract «Повтор»), the lock is not foreign → the writer is closed (what was written is readable)
  b. the volume lock is foreign or the end of closing came without an answer → abandon the writer (WRITER_ABANDON, timeout — OBSD_TIMEOUT)
    there is an answer to ABANDON → the writer is handed over
    there is no answer → close the session (the engine's grace period; the successor mounts with the same owner, chapter 3)
3. The place: do not release it (Н1.4) — it expires by itself; place_by stays as before until the end of leaving, then ""
```

Leaving does not stop the unit: `stop_unit` is called by the runtime when the unit is not in the assignment (P/03.3 Н3 step 2), and the assignment is changed by
the controller by the `place_by: ""` of the end of leaving (P/04 Н3). Until then the unit and its epoch remain with the recorder, there is no new epoch;
the volume being left is, for step 4 of Н3, no volume: the pipeline does not start.

Frames that did not get through raise the alarm `archive.footage.dropped` (`epoch`, `volume`, `seconds`, `since`, `until`, `exact`, `of:
cam/<камера>`; in the heartbeat `archive_dropped_seconds`): `exact: true` — the bounds are from the index, `false` — an estimate; `since`, `until` —
Unix seconds.

### Н1.6 Stop by SIGTERM

`worker.stop_timeout: 47`: the end of closing (SIGTERM + 33 s: the end of a live recording 2, `FINISH_MEDIA` 1, `WRITER_CLOSE` 30 of the
`obsd` contract), `WRITER_ABANDON` within the timeout `OBSD_TIMEOUT` (33 + 10 s) and a margin for the last heartbeat and for handing over the slot. The 1 s of `FINISH_MEDIA` is
a value without grounding, checked by measurement. The order — P/03.4 Н3 «остановка по SIGTERM»: substitution and polling stop; stop the
pipelines and close the writers of the places by Н1.5 with the **end of closing** of Н1.5: the writers of all places are closed at once, with one deadline for
all, `WRITER_ABANDON` also at once (33 s fit into `CLOSE_BY`: SIGTERM is not later than the confirmation + `RENEW_EVERY`, P/03.4). Then for each place: the writer is closed or
`WRITER_ABANDON` answered — the last heartbeat and `released`, then hand over the slot; `ABANDON` without an answer (the session is closed, Н1.5) —
do not release the place: it expires by itself, as when leaving (Н1.4); a writer not closed does not hand over the place.

**Limit.** A released ring drains for up to 10 s (Н1.2): with `FINISH_MEDIA` and `WRITER_CLOSE` at the contract limit this is 41 s, more than
the end of closing. Such a stop abandons the writer, the ring may not get through: the alarm `archive.footage.dropped` (Н1.5). If the store was silent longer than 17 s before SIGTERM, the end of closing is earlier than 33 s, and the writer may be abandoned for a live recording too. The 33 s have no margin: `FINISH_MEDIA` of 2 s abandons a live recording; checked by measurement.

### Н1.7 Writer watchdog

- **The unit is bytes.** «Sent» is the sum of the lengths of the SMPL records (the header, `sub`, the body; the contract «Время и запись SMPL») in
  accepted `PUT_MEDIA` (the answer `OK` or `SEQUENCE_LOST`) per volume — live recording, catch-up recording, copies; «arrived» is the growth of
  `totalWritten` of the volume (`READER_STATUS` by a fresh reader at each query, bytes — the `obsd` contract). The base of both counts is the writer's mount (`VOLUME_MOUNT_RW`):
  after a remount the count starts anew.
- The states of `writer.state`: `stalled` — since the last growth of `totalWritten` more than three blocks have been sent (`3 × размер блока`
  of the volume, that is three times its block size, in bytes) and the growth has stood still longer than a minute; `losing` — over the last 5 min (a sliding window over the samples of the passes, only
  a full one) more than three blocks have been sent and less than 90 % of what was sent within the same window has arrived; `ok` — otherwise, including «nothing
  sent»; a closed set (`metrics.values`).
- The recorder MUST count what is sent: `writer.state` is always in the heartbeat while the volume is held; there is no `READER_STATUS` sample —
  the state is as before, initially `ok`.
- `stalled` or `losing` — reopen the writer (stop the pipelines of the volume, remount), no more often than once per 10 min:
  closing has the timeout `OBSD_TIMEOUT`, without an answer — the session without `BYE` (chapter 3 Н3), mounting at the next pass; the place and the units
  remain, the pipelines rise under the same epoch. `seal` waits for `WRITER_CLOSE` no longer than the 30 s of the contract; without an answer — the same.

### Н1.8 Heartbeat words and metrics

- `heartbeat.strings: [volume, volume_error, ingest, volume_missing]`; `servers.status` of the manifest names
  `volume_missing`, `volume_error`; the top of the heartbeat — `volume` (the held volume or
  `""`), `url` — the address of the archive door (chapter 5), `writer`, `card` (the card — one card place per worker, like
  `volume`; chapter 6 Н1.7), `disks` (the server disks, ADR-0111 item 2: `[{disk, size_bytes, system}]`, `disk` — the mount point of a file system on a block device of size greater than zero, without `/boot`, `/boot/efi`, `tmpfs`, `overlay` and other in-memory file systems; `system` — the root is on it; not read — no field), `volume_error`, `archive_failure`,
  `archive_error`, `archive_away_since`, `volume_missing`, `volume_wait`, `refused`, `unservable`, `volume_quota`,
  `quota_note`, `shrink_pending`, `resize_error` (chapter 6 Н1.2), `archive_dropped_seconds`, `freeing` (chapter 7); `ingest` — chapter 9. `volume_missing` is the volume name (a string);
  `refused`, `unservable` — `{том: причина}`; `volume_quota` — the bytes of the ring while the volume is open; `objectstorage.block_small` —
  bytes (chapter 3 Н1.5); `volume_wait` is not written: there is no pinning (Н1.3).
- Volume events — the unit `<том>`, epoch 0, the field `volume`, `of` — `rec/<том>` (the log contract, step 2); label events — chapter 7 Н1.3.
- The element `status[]` of a recording: `phase` (`running` \| `waiting` \| `standby` \| `pending` \| `failed`; `standby` — a backup recording on
  hold, chapter 6 Н1.5; `pending` — `enabled: false` or there has been no start yet: the element stays in `status[]`; `failed` —
  the last start or the work failed (the backoff — `stalled` of the platform); a row never read — the platform phase `unreadable`, P/03.3 Н3 step 2), `reason`, `via`, `audio_codec`, `audio_reason` (Н1.2а; the page reads it), `samples_refused`, `last_frame_at`, `depth_days`, `shallow` (chapter 5),
  `coverage`, `prebuffered` (a backup recording, chapter 6), `written_through` (a recording in `running`, wherever the frames come from: the end of what is visible of its
  live stream, closed blocks only, the cluster clock; it lags behind the taken frame by the flush, up to 15 s, chapter 3 Н1.5; read by `have`,
  chapter 9 Н1.3, and by the backup recording, chapter 6 Н1.4; a `replica` has no such field), for a `replica` — `replica_lag_s`, `replica_lost_s`, `replica_absent_dropped_s`, `over_quota` (chapter 6 Н1.9).
- The `rec_*` series of the manifest on live recording: `recordings_running`, `recordings`, `volumes_declared`, `volumes_unserved`,
  `volume_error`, `volume_wait`, `volume_shrink_pending`, `archive_away_seconds`, `archive_failure{kind}`,
  `writer{state}`, `last_frame_age_seconds`, `samples_refused_total{unit,status}`, `archive_depth_days`, `archive_shallow`,
  `unconfirmed_seconds`. The recorder has no `/metrics` page of its own (the series come from the heartbeat, P/06.5). The series on the current recording —
  `writer{state}`, `volume_error`, `archive_failure{kind}`, `archive_away_seconds`, `last_frame_age_seconds` — are declared with
  `live: true` (P/06.5 Н3 «Ряды манифеста»): the heartbeat of a recorder that has left does not remain in them; their reader is the health of V/09.4.

### Н1.9 Video is not moved

Video lies in the volume where it was recorded and is read through the door of the volume holder (chapter 5): the recorder does not send what was recorded
to another server either when the recording moves or when the server dies. A fenced-off holder writes under its own epoch (own disk —
through the silence of the store, a network volume — only in the window), the minutes of the fenced-off epoch are not deleted (chapter 3 Н1.1).

## Н3 Pass

```
1. resubscription: the recording is running, and the camera source changed (the holder moved) → stop the pipeline and start it with the new
   source (P/03.1 stop_unit / start_unit; under the same epoch); the source «none» is not a move (the holder could have restarted)
2. places: take what is missing (Н1.3), hold, refusals (Н1.4); the first recorder of the cluster — `rec/wanted/*` (Н1.3); the flag «engine lost» — remount (chapter 3 Н3)
3. writer watchdog (Н1.7); depth — once a minute (chapter 5)
4. reconciliation of the units (P/03.3): there are a source and a volume → start the pipeline; otherwise → waiting; replica — the plan of chapter 6 Н1.9, no pipeline
```

**Races.** The camera holder moved in the middle of recording — the frames between the stop and the start are lost (seconds), the gap
is shown by the timeline and filled by catch-up recording (chapter 8). Two recorders of different servers on one volume — the platform place and
the fence of patch 07 (chapter 3). The store is silent during a move of the recording — own disk keeps being written, the timeline has two epochs over
the same minutes (chapter 5).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `live_shm` or `live_url` of the `cam` heartbeat not in form (V/01 `cam-heartbeat-words`) | unread, not absent: the kind does not change, an open source is kept, a source not yet opened waits | the recording status, `reason` | the next heartbeat of the holder |
| nobody holds the camera | `waiting`, `reason` | the heartbeat, `/recordings` | the camera holder (V/01) |
| the holder is on another server | `waiting`, `reason` `push_waiting` — the stream only through the receiver | the heartbeat | placement (`near`) or the receiver (chapter 9) |
| the source frames are not H.264 | the recording does not go, `waiting`, `reason` `codec not supported: <кодек>` | the heartbeat, `/recordings` | an H.264 stream on the camera |
| no sound: there is no `mic`, the endpoint is not read, there are no sound frames, another codec | the recording goes without sound, `audio_reason` (Н1.2а) | the heartbeat, the page | `audio`, `enabled` of the `mic` setting |
| no volume can be taken | `place_by: ""`, `volume_error` | the heartbeat, the series `volume_error` | declare or repair a volume |
| the volume is wrong in the middle of work | leaving, `refused` for 600 s | the heartbeat, an alarm | fix the volume row |
| the engine is silent longer than 300 s (a network volume) | leaving, `refused` | the heartbeat | the engine |
| the volume is taken by a foreign writer longer than 600 s | the alarm `archive.volume.busy`, leaving | the event log | deal with the writer |
| the writer is stuck or losing | reopen (≤ once per 10 min) | the series `writer{state}` | — |
| the writer did not close by the end of closing | `WRITER_ABANDON`; without an answer — close the session, the place expires by itself | the log, `archive.footage.dropped` | the engine |
