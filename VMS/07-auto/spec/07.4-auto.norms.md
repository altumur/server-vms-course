# V/07.4 — Actions: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/07.4-auto.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 4 of lesson V/07 «Сценарии» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/07-auto.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](07-auto.README.md), manifest [`auto.subsystem.yaml`](../auto.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

A firing (chapter 2) of a fit scenario (chapter 3) turns into actions, and the evaluator executes none of them itself: the relay
and the preset are a request to the camera holder; a recording, a detector and a scan for a time are a row which the evaluator orders from the owner
subsystem. The chapter sets norms for each action, the checks before it, the expansion of targets, a run with waiting, the firing ceilings, the trace
of a firing, the requests `run`, `retry`, the door of the test run and the evaluator's heartbeat.

Norm ids are `V/07.4-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `occurred` is the moment of the closing event of the firing (chapter 2 Н1.3), the moment of the schedule, the moment of the pass of the recording window (chapter 2 Н1.6), the stamp `at` of a request `run`, `retry` (Н1.12); `minutes`, `before`, `after`, `valid_for` count from it, and a step after a wait counts from the end of the wait (Н1.11): below, the `occurred` of a step is this moment. The time
of the order event `t_E` (P/03.6 Н1.6: `ORDER_HORIZON`, the window above the tombstone) is the same `occurred`: `until` and
the scan window count from it, a repeat after a move gives the same `t_E`. For a scan order `t_E` is the `to` of the window: an equal window gives an equal `made.at`,
the order's answer is `unchanged` (P/03.6 Н3 «заказать» step 4).

**Data** (the common families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): it writes — requests `cam/requests/<rid>`
(`worker.requests: [cam]`, [P/03.6 Н1.5](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)), order rows
`rec/recordings/auto-<cam>`, `det/units/auto-<cam>-<kind>`, `detjob/jobs/auto-<…>` (`worker.creates`,
[P/03.6 Н1.6](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)), events `auto/<scenario>/e<эпоха>/`, alarms `auto.alarms/<scenario>/e<эпоха>/`, the run row `auto/state/runs/<scenario>` (Н1.11), chain rows `auto/chains/*` (Н1.13), the heartbeat; it reads — the tables `auto/schedules/*`, `auto/day_groups/*`, `auto/modes/all`, `auto/mode_current/now`, `auto/windows/*`, `auto/rule_marks/*`, `auto/answers/*`, the rows `cam/rule_state/*` (V/01.4 Н1.4д), the heartbeats `cam/heartbeats/*`, `rec/heartbeats/*`, the folders `platform/folders/*`, `platform/memberof/*` (`worker.reads`), the copy of grants `domain/grants` (Н1.9).

The `cam` of an action is a camera (the main stream, V/01.1 Н1.1а); the ordered recording and detector are on the camera's main stream (`stream`
is empty, V/02.1, V/04.1): the order keys (`{cam}`, `{cam}-{kind}`) do not name a stream, and one camera is one order row; a scan
job is on the stream of its recording (V/05.1). A relay and preset request goes to the camera; the holder rejects a request to a stream (V/01.4 Н4).

### Н1.1 Relay and preset — a request to the holder

`cam output` and `cam preset` are the worker's filing of a `cam` request row (P/03.6 Н1.5; the form and the execution — V/01.4): the body
`{unit, action: output, port, state?, pulse_ms?}`, `{unit, action: preset, n}` or `{unit, action: unit, n, operation}`, the actions of Н1.10 — their own, by the `cam` request schema (not by the schema — it is not
filed, `filings.refused`); `valid_until` = `occurred + valid_for` of the scenario (the deadline counts from the event, not from the filing; `0` — as the default, V/07.1); stamps — `by:
auto/<scenario>`, `at`, `group` (P/03.6 Н1.5). The request name is by the filer's key: `auto.<f>-<i>` (`<подсистема подателя>.<ключ>`): `f` is the first 32 hex
of `sha256(UTF-8(<сценарий> + "\n" + <id события>))`, for a run without an event — of `sha256(UTF-8(<имя сработки>))`; `i` is the step's path in the tree `then` (`0`, `2.no.1`, numbers from 0), for a step with the field `target` always `<путь>-<устройство>`, `<cam>` or the key of the child row (Н1.9): a folder of one camera and a folder of two give that camera one name, otherwise a change of composition between repeats would give a second command; the length of `f` does not depend on the names
(the grammar of `rid` — up to 200 bytes, without `'`; the name in `fetched` — up to 40 characters, `said_id`); `cam` has no `requests.key` of its own; [the request contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/request-mark.md)
`rid`, P/03.6 Н1.5): a repeat of the same firing — including by a new holder after a move within the cursor window — is the same row; the door
procedure reconciles `unit` and the `digest` of the kind `worker`: the same — `filings.again`, different — `filings.refused` (ADR-0091 addendum (21) item 2, addendum (23)
items 1–2). If the deadline has already passed by the filing, the request is not filed: the event `fired` with
the mark `late`, the count `late`; after a move this may also be said of a firing filed earlier: it is counted anew (a limit). `late` belongs to the actions: the orders of the same firing are filed by their own rules.

### Н1.2 Detector for a time — an order

`det detect` is an order of a detector row: `worker.creates: [{sub: det, rows: units, key: "{cam}-{kind}"}]` → `det/units/auto-<cam>-<kind>`
(V/04.1 Н1.2), the deadline `until` = `occurred + minutes × 60` (a second triggering extends it — the platform takes the largest, P/03.6
Н1.6). Before the order:

- **A second detector is not started:** if there is an operator's detector of the same pair (`cam`, `kind`) — enabled, without `made`, with `until` = 0 —
  there is no order, the field `watched` of the heartbeat element is the id of this detector. If the operator enabled their detector later than the order, both work until the order's `until`.
- **The base** is the operator's detector of the same pair first by id, if there is one: `params` (a string; the action's `params` replace it whole), `labels`, `alarms`,
  the mask — `mask_of: <id детектора оператора>` (the order does not copy bodies: the order's blob is empty, P/03.6 Н1.6; a reference to a body does not
  hold the body, P/06.1 Н3 — the order references the row; the model takes its mask at start, V/04.1 Н1.1 «Какая маска»). If there is no
  base — the detector's default fields, the action's `params`, without `mask_of`.

### Н1.3 Scan — an order

`det scan` is an order of a job over an interval: `worker.creates: [{sub: detjob, rows: jobs, key: "{cam}-{rec}-{kind}-{from}-{to}"}]` →
`detjob/jobs/auto-<…>` (V/05.1 Н1.2):

- the window is `[from, to)`: `from = floor(occurred − before)`, `to = ceil(occurred + after)` in binary64 (outward to whole seconds,
  as V/06 question 1 (d)), the defaults `before` = `after` = 60 s; in the key — the notation of `values_by_type` (`float`), as V/02.8 Н1.5
  (the platform does not allow `:int` in `worker.creates[].key`, P/02.1); a repeat on a fractional `occurred` is the same row, `unchanged`; `after` > 0 — the job follows the recording (V/05.4 Н1.3);
- the recording is the `rec` of the action; if there is none — the recording of this camera (the `cam` of the recording is this camera) with id = `cam`; if there is none — the first by name
  recording of this camera (a row without a tombstone, disabled and ordered ones too; an unreadable one is not chosen); if there is not a single one — a refusal (chapter 3 —
  `unfit`; at filing — the step `fail`, `target.noRecord`); if there is not a single one but an unreadable row exists, a recording may exist: `unchecked`, the step `fail`, `target.unread`;
- the base — as Н1.2: `params` (the action replaces it whole), `labels`, `alarms`, `mask_of` — the id of this detector; if there is no base — the default
  fields, the action's `params`, without `mask_of`.

One job per window `(cam, rec, kind, from, to)`: an equal window is the same job (`unchanged`), another window is another job; there is no extension
of a running job (the key is immutable). The job's fields (`params`, `labels`, `alarms`, `mask_of`) come from the first order: an order of another
scenario with the same window and other `params` does not change them (`unchanged`). `rate_per_minute` and `PER_PASS` hold them back.

### Н1.4 Recording for a time — an order

`rec record` is an order of a recording row: `worker.creates: [{sub: rec, rows: recordings, key: "{cam}"}]` → `rec/recordings/auto-<cam>`
(V/02.1): `cam`, `until` = `occurred + minutes × 60`, `home` = the `archive` of the action (a volume; `must_match` on `home` is removed, V/02.1: an order onto a card of another server
is started, and the card's recorder does not write it — `reason`, V/02.6 Н1.7; there is no volume — the order is started, V/02.1). A second triggering extends it. What stays long is the path to the first frame (the controller's
pass and the start of the recorder) — the camera's pre-recording covers it (V/02).

### Н1.5 Ceilings

The order of checks of a firing: already decided (the name is in the evaluator's memory, in `runs` or `done`) → the pass limit → the checks of Н1.8 → the scenario ceiling → `repeat` (Н1.11). The memory keeps a name
while the questions of chapter 2 Н1.2 read the row; after a move it is gone, a repeat gives the same names. The request `run`, the chain and the door `test` do not go through the limit and the ceiling.

- **The pass limit** `PER_PASS` = 4 firings per scenario decided by this pass, `skip` and suppressed ones too — a queue, not a refusal: the cursor stops before the first unfiled one (chapter 2
  Н1.4), the rest go with the next pass.
- **The ceiling** `rate_per_minute` (V/07.1; `0` — as the default) — per scenario, in the evaluator's memory between passes: what is counted
  is filings with `now − момент подачи` (now minus the moment of filing) < 60 s, not rows of the window and not `occurred`: otherwise an old row of the backward query would not count even after the filing; a `log_only` run is a filing, `result: skip` is not; a firing is counted once: a repeat of an undecided one is the same filing, otherwise half of what was filed would fall under the ceiling; above it — a refusal: the firing is decided,
  there are no actions, the count `suppressed`, one event row `suppressed` (`count`, `from`, `to`) per series. A series is a pass; `from`,
  `to` are the smallest and the largest `occurred`; `t` is the moment of writing. The ceiling is needed: a sensor
  bouncing at 5 Hz would otherwise file three hundred door commands a minute. **Limit:** a suppressed firing leaves no trace
  (`suppressed` does not name firings), the count lives in memory: after a move, firings rejected
  by the former holder and still visible to the backward query (chapter 2 Н1.2) are filed as new ones, but under the ceiling: no more than 2R and one pass a minute (`R` is the ceiling). For requests they are also held back by
  the deadline `valid_for`, for orders by `ORDER_HORIZON`.

### Н1.6 The `auto` events

The worker's writer (P/03.6 Н1.2): the tree `auto`, the unit is the scenario id, the epoch is its epoch; the class `observation`, except `alarm` (Н1.10). The log is the trace of firings, `GET /rules/{id}/fires` is assembled from it (V/11).

| Kind | When | Fields beyond `t`, `occurred` |
|---|---|---|
| `fired` | the run has reached the end or the first wait; with a wait — once more at the end | `firing` (the firing name), `firing_key` (`f` of Н1.1), `by` (`event`, `sched`, `manual`, `test`), `result`, `source` (ids of the cameras of the firing's events, the target `from_event`), `events` (`{sub, kind, match, cam, occurred}` of the firing's events, chapter 2 Н1.3; `match` — the row fields named by the trigger's `match`, for `io.input` always `port`, `value`), `checks` (Н1.8), `actions` (the number of filed actions, all late — 0), `late?` — `true` if at least one request was late, `log_only?`, `waiting?` — `true`, the run is waiting, `chain?` — the source firing and `level` (Н1.13) |
| `step` | a tree step has been executed, one row per target device; a firing with `result: skip` writes no steps | `firing`, `step` (the path), `target` (the device or `null`), `action` (the action as it was in the run; for a wait step `null`), `control` (the kind of the wait step or `null`), `label?` (the caption of the flow step, `{code, params}`), `result` (`ok`, `fail`, `would`, `skip` — the branch did not go, the level was not needed), `reason?` (a code), `note?` (`{code, params}`) |
| `suppressed` | Н1.5 | `count`, `from`, `to`; `occurred` = `to` |

`t` is the moment of writing, `occurred` is that of the cause, for a step — the `occurred` of the step. The `result` of a firing: `skip` — a check did not pass; `watch` — `log_only`, if not all steps are `fail`; otherwise `ok` — all actions went out, `part` — some, `fail` — none; no actions — `ok` (`watch`), the `waiting` row — by the steps before the wait. A flow step does not enter the result. A repeat of a firing and a waiting run give a second row: the reader takes the last by `(t, id)` per `firing` and per `(firing, step, target)`. The `reason` code: `device.offline`, `device.disabled`, `device.ruleOff`, `target.*` (Н1.9), `request.late`, `request.refused`, `order.refused`, `role.denied`, `answer.refused` (chapter 6 Н1.9), `chain.*` (Н1.13). The codes of `label`, `note` and their `params` are the table of flow steps below: this is what the form reads, therefore the named codes have no empty `params`; the translation of `role`, `user`, times — V/11.3 Н1.3. A row with `action: null` always carries `label`: the form draws the step by it.

| Outcome | Step |
|---|---|
| the request is filed, `filings.again` | `ok` |
| the request's deadline has passed | `fail`, `request.late` |
| the request is not by the schema, `filings.refused` | `fail`, `request.refused` |
| the order is `created`, `extended`, `unchanged`; `watched` (Н1.2) | `ok` |
| the order is `taken`, `gone`, `expired`, `refused` (P/03.6 Н1.6) | `fail`, `order.refused` |

| Control step | `step` rows at the end of the wait (`result: ok`, under `log_only` — `would`) |
|---|---|
| `wait` | `label` `ctl.wait` `{sec}` |
| `wait_event` | `label` `ctl.waitEvent` `{event}`, `note` `waitEvent.came` `{sec}` — seconds from the start of the step, or `waitEvent.timeout` `{sec}` — the step's deadline |
| `ask` | `label` `ctl.ask` `{text, role}` — the step's fields; `note` `ask.answer` `{answer, user, at}` — the fields of the effective answer row, or `ask.noAnswer` `{sec, counted}` — the step's deadline and its `default`, with an unreadable answer row `answer.unread` with the same `params` |
| `escalate` | `label` `ctl.escalate` `{roles}` — the `role` of the levels in order. A level is a row of its own, `step` — `<путь>.level.<n>`, `n` from 1, `label` `esc.level` `{n, role, via, needed}`, `note` `clock` `{at}` — the start of the level, written on entering the level, `needed: true`; outside `log_only` a level which was not reached is written at the end of the wait, `result: skip`, `needed: false`, without `note`. The end is a row of its own, `step` — `<путь>.end`, `label` `esc.ack` `{user, at}` — the fields of the effective row `ack:<n>`, `esc.reset` `{at}` — the `occurred` of the event, or `esc.none` `{}` — the deadline of the last level has run out |
| an answer refusal (chapter 6 Н1.9) | a row of the running run: `step` — the step's path, `action` `null`, `control` — the kind of the step, the step's `label`, `ctl.ask` or `ctl.escalate`, with the same `params`, `result: fail`, `reason` `answer.refused`; the end of the wait replaces it, a removed run leaves it |
| a branch that did not go | one `step` row — `<путь>.<ветка>`, `result: skip`, `label` `branch` `{branch, control, test}`: `control` — the kind of the step, `test` — `true` with `by: test`; the branch's steps write no rows |

A step `ok` means the request is filed, the order is accepted, the event row is written: the step does not wait for the holder's outcome. **Limit:** the holder's refusal is visible by the label and its event `command.failed` (V/01.4), it is not in `step`, `retry` does not repeat it. **Limit:** the size of the trace is the limit of the log: the retention of the tree `auto` (the event log contract `retention`) and the cut `truncated` of the answer: the trace of an area of a thousand cameras does not come onto the page whole. The page shows the scenario's firings for a day (`/events?subsystem=auto&unit=auto/<id>`, V/11).

### Н1.7 Heartbeat and series

The form is [P/03.4 Н1.1](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md); the platform puts the capacity, the reserve, `filings`
(P/03.6 Н1.5), `creates`, `made_rows` (P/03.6 Н1.6). `auto` adds: at the top — `pass_seconds`, `queries`, `lag_seconds`
(the cursor's lag), `latency_seconds`, `latency` (a histogram «event → filing», buckets 1, 2, 5, 10, 30, 60, 300 s),
`late`, `suppressed`, `waits`, `woken`, `early_passes`, `wait_errors`, `want_skipped` (chapter 2 Н1.5), `lines_garbled` (the sum of the merged answers of the pass, chapter 2 Н1.2); the scenario element —
`phase` (`running` | `pending` | `refused` | `failed`; the epoch is taken), `reason`, `unfit`, `unchecked` (chapter 3), `holding`,
`decided_without` (chapter 2), `watched` (Н1.2). `pending` — the scenario is disabled. `failed` — a failure of the evaluator on the scenario (the runtime's backoff, P/03.3): a pause inside the unit, not its removal: the memory of the ceiling and of decided firings is intact, the log, the runs and the chain of the scenario do not go during the pause.
`lag_seconds` is the largest `now − through` over the scenarios of the pass, 0 without scenarios; `latency_seconds` is the largest road of the pass,
0 without firings; `latency` is cumulative, the road from `occurred`, the form `{buckets, count, sum}` (P/06.5 Н3). The scenario element also has `answers_refused` (chapter 6 Н1.9), `runs` (the number of elements of the run row), `runs_unreadable` (`true`, Н1.11); the top — `chains_unreadable` (Н1.13). The object `object_mode` of the top — chapter 6. The manifest
series are `scenarios_running` (the series `console.running`: the scenarios in the phase `running` at live evaluators — by the metric's declaration
`from`, `agg`, `equals`, `live`, P/06.5 Н3), `filings_total`, `creates_total`, `made_rows` (by the label `target`:
`rec/recordings`, `det/units`, `detjob/jobs`), `pass_seconds`, `queries_per_pass`, `cursor_lag_seconds`,
`firing_latency_seconds`, `event_to_request_seconds`, `fired_late_total`, `firings_suppressed_total`, `waits_total`,
`woken_total`, `early_passes_total`, `wait_errors_total`, `want_skipped`.

### Н1.8 Checks before actions

A matched firing goes through the checks before the first step; all are counted, a failure does not cut them short. The result is the elements `checks` of the `fired` row, `{code, params, ok, reason}`; `reason` is the contract's `{code, params}` or `null`. At least one `ok: false` — `result: skip`, there are no steps, the firing is decided.

| `code` | The element exists | `params` | `reason` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cond` | per condition; per trigger target which is not read | `{condition}`; `{trigger}` — the trigger's number | `ok: true` — `null`; `cond.time`, `{at}`; `cond.mode`, `{modeId}`; `cond.devices`, `{items, more}`: the first two devices and how many more; `target.*`; `cond.unread` |
| `muted` | muted | `{until, by, reason}` | `skipped` |
| `maintenance` | per window into which the moment fell | `{windowId, name}` | `skipped` |
| `busy` | there is a running run | `{}` | `busy.skip`; `ok: true` — `busy.restart`, `busy.queue` |
| `chain.off` | Н1.13 | `{}` | `chain.off` |
| `testOff`, `event.came`, `event.other`, `event.missing` | the door `test` (Н2) | `{}`; `{trigger, deviceId}`; `{event, deviceId}`; `{trigger}` | `null`; `event.expected`, `{trigger}`; `event.none`, `event.notInWindow` |

| Check | Passes | Input | `run` |
|---|---|---|---|
| the condition `time` | `in_effect(schedule, occurred, TIMEZONE)`; a removed schedule — false | chapter 6 Н1.3 | judges |
| the condition `mode` | `object_mode(occurred, TIMEZONE)` in `modes`; no modes — false | chapter 6 Н1.5 | judges |
| the condition `armed` | `armed` = `value` (no `value` — `true`) at each camera of the target | the row `cam/rule_state/<cam>`: no row — not armed, unreadable — the word is unknown, not «no» (V/01.4 Н1.4д) | judges |
| the condition `online` | the camera is held at each: a fresh heartbeat of the holder (chapter 3 Н1.2), `status[]` carries it in the phase `running`, `idle` or `held` without `device_state`; a child is judged by its camera | `cam/heartbeats/*` | judges |
| the condition `recording` | each camera has a recording which is being written: a heartbeat of the recorder fresh by `LOST_AFTER` holds it in the phase `running`, `written_through` lags behind the `ts` of this heartbeat by no more than 45 s (the threshold of V/02.6 Н1.4, without its memory of observations and graces: there is no history, a just started recording may read as «not written» for the first 45 s) | `rec/recordings/*`, `rec/heartbeats/*` | judges |
| muting | not `muted(сценарий, occurred)` | the row `auto/rule_marks/<scenario>`, chapter 6 Н1.6: no row — not muted | does not judge |
| maintenance window | there is no window with `window_hit(w, сценарий, occurred, TIMEZONE)` | chapter 6 Н1.6 | does not judge |

The conditions `time` and `mode`, muting and windows are computed by `occurred` (ADR-0105 item 3): a repeat decides the same. **Limit:** `online`, `recording`, `armed` are the current state at the moment of the decision, there is no history: a late and a repeated firing is judged by the present, not by `occurred`; the same for manual switching of a mode: `mode_current` keeps no history, one removed after `occurred` is judged by the present row (V/07.6 Н1.5), only mode schedules go by `occurred`. The target of a condition is expanded by Н1.9; empty, `gone` or no area — `ok: false`, `reason` the code `target.*`. The trigger's target is not read — the element `cond`, `reason` `cond.unread`.

Not read is not absent: a row of a chapter 6 table, including `auto/rule_marks/<scenario>`, the row `cam/rule_state/<cam>`, the target folder are unreadable, the moment function is `unknown` (chapter 6 Н1.1) — the check is `ok: false`, `reason` `cond.unread`, the row is in `unchecked` (chapter 3 Н1.3): an action in doubt is not executed, the word is distinguishable from «the condition is false». No fresh heartbeat — this is read: `online` is not satisfied. The store did not answer — the firing is not decided (Н4).

**Log only** (`log_only`) — the run goes by the same course, with waits, but the actions are not executed: `step` with `result: would`, there are no requests, orders and alarms, the chain row goes with `log_only: true`, `result: watch`, `log_only: true`. This also concerns the request `run`. `ask`, `escalate` in such a run ask nobody and wait out their deadline: the rows `answers` are not read, `ask` ends with the branch `default` by `until`, `escalate` — by the deadline of the last level, the levels without rows.

### Н1.9 Target

A step with the field `target` (the form of V/07.1: `include`, `exclude`, `gone`, `from_event`) is a step of its own per target device: a request, an order or a row of its own, a `step` row of its own. The evaluator expands it, reading the rows at the moment of the step: a new device enters by itself, `backend` does not expand, the run row holds no composition: the target has no size ceiling. A repeat expands anew, for the same device the name is the same, there is no second command. **Limit:** a device that entered between the times gets its first request, one that left the target gets no more.

| Element of `include` | Devices |
|---|---|
| `device` | the camera `cam/cameras/<id>` |
| `child` | the child `cam/children/<cam>-<type>-<number>` (V/01.1 Н1.2а): its camera and number |
| `folder` | the member cameras of `platform/folders/<id>` and of the folders under it by `parent`, with children |
| `server` | the cameras whose fresh holder heartbeat (chapter 3 Н1.2) carries this `server`, with children |
| `domain` | all live cameras of the cluster, with children: the `domain` of the contract is its own cluster (V/11.2) |

In a domain the folders are the agent's copies with their own units. `exclude` is subtracted from the result by equality of the key: a camera does not subtract its children. A device of the target's kind enters:

| Target | Enters |
|---|---|
| `output` | a relay: the child `relay`, the output is its number, the step's `port` is not read; a camera as the element `device` with the step's `port` — its relay, the key `<cam>-relay-<port>`: one relay in the target is one request; a camera of an area, a camera without `port` and a camera of `from_event` do not enter |
| other actions, the conditions `armed`, `recording` | a camera |
| the condition `online` | a camera and a child |
| a trigger | a camera — all its events; the child `sensor` — the `io.input` of its camera with `port` equal to the number, together with `match.port`: both conditions; `relay`, `mic`, `spk` do not enter |

The target `from_event` is the run's `source`. `gone` — per element the step `fail`, `target.gone`, the rest is executed.

| Case | Step |
|---|---|
| no device, no folder, a tombstone | `fail`, `target.deviceGone`, `target.areaGone` |
| the area is empty | `fail`, `target.areaEmpty` |
| `from_event`, but the event has no camera (a run without an event) | `fail`, `target.noEventDevice` |

The table is judged top down: a disabled camera is not held, its word is `device.ruleOff`, `device.disabled`.

The target's steps go in one pass, there is no limit on them: the ceiling counts firings. **Cost:** a folder of a thousand cameras is a thousand requests and `step` rows per firing.

**The right** is judged at writing (V/07.1 Н1.4, the limit of the form is there too): a device that entered later does not ask for the right anew. `run_as_role` is different: an action on a device is judged by the role's right, not the author's: the input `may` with `as_role: <роль>`, `cap: edit`, the unit is the one which `names` reads from the step at writing (`cam/<камера>`, for a child its camera; for `output` — all units of the camera's group; for `runrule` — `auto/<rule>`), `grants` — the cluster copy `domain/grants` (the right contract, section «Действие от роли», the vectors `may-role-*`): this is not a substitution of a person, `user` is empty, the role `admin` is a refusal. No field — on behalf of the system, rights are not judged.

### Н1.10 Arming, disabling, alarm

- `cam arm`, `cam disarm` — the request of Н1.1, the body `{unit, action: arm}`, `{unit, action: disarm}`; the row `cam/rule_state/<cam>` is written by the holder (V/01.4 Н1.4д).
- `cam disable` — a request, the body `{unit, action: disable, disabled_by_rule, disabled_until}`: `disabled_by_rule` is the scenario id, `disabled_until` = the step's `occurred` + `minutes` × 60; `valid_until` is not later than `disabled_until`: a late request would otherwise lift someone else's disabling (V/01.4 Н1.4д); the holder applies it. `arm`, `disarm`, `disable` do not call the device: they are filed to a camera that is not connected too.
- `auto alarm` — a row of the alarm log: an event of the kind `alarm`, the class `alarm`, the worker's writer, the tree `auto.alarms` (the event log contract `class`), one per target device; the fields `firing`, `step`, `cam`, `text`, `priority`. No `text` — empty, no `priority` — `normal`. Values of the run are substituted into `text`: `{камера}` — the `name` of the step's camera, `{устройство}` — the device key, `{событие}` — the `kind` of the closing event, a run without an event — empty, `{время}` — the step's `occurred`, `ГГГГ-ММ-ДД ЧЧ:ММ:СС` in the zone `TIMEZONE`, `{правило}` — the scenario's `name`. The member's alarm page and the history take it ([the alarm history contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/alarm-history.md)). **Limit:** a repeat of a firing after a move or a write refusal (Н4) is a second alarm: `same_line` compares `t_src`, the firing name does not enter it. **Limit:** the alarm's unit is the scenario, therefore two alarms of a step with equal `t` are one row in the history: the device does not enter `same_line`.

The contract gives no closing of an alarm: a history row is immutable, `alarmclose` is not declared.

### Н1.11 The run row and waiting

A wait step puts the run onto the row `unit.state.runs`, `auto/state/runs/<scenario>` ([P/03.6 Н1.7](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)): one writer, the holder, under the epoch guard; one row per scenario, no deletion. The value is `{runs: [{fire, step, until, vars, trace}], done: [{fire, t}]}`, `runs` no more than `RUNS_MAX` = 16 elements, `done` no more than `DONE_MAX` = 256 (the schema's `maxItems`; both values have no support, they are checked by measurement).

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `fire` | the firing name: the continuation goes by it, the action names of the steps are the same, `rid` does not change on a repeat |
| `step` | the path of the step on which the run stands |
| `until` | the end of the wait, seconds; `0` — the run is in the queue, not started; on a step without a wait it is not judged |
| `vars` | the step's `occurred`, `by`, `source`, `events`, `checks`, `actions`, `late`, `log_only`, `chain` (the source firing and `level`), the values of the template substitutions: what the `fired` row carries and the step substitutes; the level of `escalate` is derived from the step's `occurred` and `wait_min` |
| `trace` | the result of the steps up to here: `{step, ok, fail}` per step, the count of devices; the run's `result` comes from it |

A run without a wait step writes no row, except `done` below. When it stops, the row is written before the cursor moves (chapter 2 Н1.4, «сначала подать»): not written — the firing is not decided, the cursor stands. `STATE_EVERY` does not hold the row back: it is a rule of the cursor. The holder takes the row at start; a move and a restart lose nothing: the wait goes on from `step`, a step filed before the crash is filed again with the same name — `again`, `unchanged`.

| Step | Fields | End of the wait |
|---|---|---|
| `wait` | `sec` | `until` = the step's `occurred` + `sec` has passed |
| `wait_event` | `event` (the trigger's form), `sec`, the branches `yes`, `no` | the first matched event of the wait query (chapter 2 Н1.2) — the branch `yes`, the end is its `occurred`; a running phase of a built-in detector answers with the nearest `still`: the motion could have started before the step; `through` ≥ `until` without it with a complete answer of the question — the branch `no`, the end is `until` |
| `ask` | `text`, `role`, `sec`, `default`, the branches `yes`, `no` | the first effective row `auto/answers/<scenario>-<fire>-<step>-*` (chapter 6 Н1.9): `answer` `yes`, `no` — its branch, the end is its `at`; `until` without it — the branch `default`, the end is `until`; an unreadable row — as no answer, until the deadline |
| `escalate` | `levels`, `stop` | a level lasts `wait_min`, the start of the first is the step's `occurred`, of each next one — the end of the previous one; `until` is the end of the last one; the end is whichever comes earlier: an effective row `answers` with `ack:<n>` whose `at` is not earlier than the start of level `n` — its `at`; with `stop: reset` an event of the wait query `value: open` of the same `cam`, `port` as the `io.input` in the run's `events` — its `occurred`; none of them — `until`; there is no `io.input` in `events` (`run`, a chain) — `reset` does not come |

A run from the queue of `repeat: queue` begins with the end of the previous run: its `occurred` is this moment, written by the same write that removed the previous one (`until` is no longer 0): otherwise the request's deadline would run out behind the wait, and a repeat would shift it. It passed the checks of Н1.8 when it was queued and does not judge them anew: **limit:** a condition false by the start of the run does not stop it. The end of a wait is the `occurred` of the next step: the deadline of a request and of an order counts from it, not from the firing's event (a firing without a wait lives by `valid_for`, a request — no longer than 600 s, Н1.1). Waiting for `through` in `wait_event` means deciding «no» only after a complete window: with an incomplete one the wait lasts (chapter 2 Н1.4).

**The end of a wait is decided once.** The branch and the end are written into the row before the first step after the wait: `step` — the next step, `vars.occurred` — the end of the wait, the `step` rows of the end of the wait — after this write. **Limit:** a crash between the write and these rows loses them: the run is correct, `GET /fires` has no wait step. The next step is the first step of the branch that went; if the branch is empty or has ended, or the step has no branches — the next step of the list from which the run came out, if there is none — one level up; if the next one is a wait again — the write puts the run onto it, `until` from this end; if there are no steps — the write ends the run. Not written — the step does not go, the row of Н4. A new holder goes from the write and does not judge the wait anew: a repeat gives the same branch, `occurred`, `digest`, scan window. **Limit:** an event or answer row that landed after the decision does not change the branch.

The deadline of a wait ends on the first pass after `until`: the precision is the pass period. The answer and its right — chapter 6 Н1.9. **Limit:** the `via` of a level has no subject, a level is only a `step` row.

**`repeat`** judges a new run while one is running (there is an element of `runs` with another `fire`), by the check `busy`:

| `repeat` | New run |
|---|---|
| `skip` (default) | `busy` `ok: false`, `reason` `busy.skip`, `result: skip` |
| `restart` | `busy` `ok: true`, `busy.restart`; the previous one is removed, its second `fired` row with the result of the steps that managed to go; the new one goes |
| `queue` | `busy` `ok: true`, `busy.queue`; the element goes to the end, `until` 0, goes after the previous one; above `RUNS_MAX` — as `skip` |

**A finished run**, including one removed by `restart`, is removed from `runs`, and its name with the `t` of the event (a run without an event — with `occurred`) goes into `done` by the same write: branches have different names, therefore a repeat by the backward query after a restart would go by another branch. `done` is written for a run that stood on the row and for every run of a scenario with `repeat: queue`: otherwise a run without a wait, repeated while another one is running, would get into the queue with a new deadline and would file the request a second time; **the cost** is a row write per such run. An element is removed when `t` is below the window of the backward query (chapter 2 Н1.2). Above `DONE_MAX` the oldest goes. **Limit:** a `skip` decided while a run is running does not go into the row and may pass on such a repeat.

**A new `revision`** (chapter 2 Н1.4): the row does not act (the row check contract `acts`: reconciliation of `revision`, the only exception is `cursor`). The runs are removed: a tree step of another `revision` means nothing. **Limit:** they have no second `fired` row, the last one is with `waiting`; the contract reads it as a result by steps (V/11.3 Н1.3). Muting and the order do not edit the scenario row (chapter 6 Н1.1): they do not remove the runs.

`refused` (chapter 3): the runs stand, on revival they go on, a request with a passed deadline is `late`. The row `unreadable` (P/03.6 Н1.7) does not act, like an absent one: the runs are lost, `runs_unreadable` in the heartbeat, the next write replaces it. The store did not answer — the step does not go, the row of Н4.

The section `requests` of the `auto` manifest; `backend` files them with a ticket through `POST /auto/requests`, the mechanism of [P/06.4](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md), the holder is the scenario's evaluator, `unit` is the scenario id; `Idempotency-Key` is the contract's `idempotencyKey`. The execution ends when the run has reached the end or the first wait, `reply` is `{firing}`.

| `action` | Body | Refusal, `error` | Run |
|---|---|---|---|
| `run` | `{unit, action: run}` | `off` — disabled; `refused` — the `reason` of chapter 3 | `<сценарий>-run-<rid>`, `occurred` — the stamp `at`, `by: manual`, the checks of Н1.8 without muting and windows, `log_only` acts, `repeat` judges |

A firing without an event: `events` are empty, the target `from_event` — `target.noEventDevice` (`retry`: the `source` of the former firing). `retry` reads the `step` rows of its unit, `keep: newest`: the firing is not in the answer, and the answer is cut or incomplete — `unread`, not `nothing`. ### Н1.13 Chain

`auto runrule` is a run of the scenario `rule` from another one: a row of the worker table `auto/chains/<target>-<f>-<i>` (`worker.writes`, [P/03.4 Н1.6](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)), `target` = `rule`, `f`, `i` — Н1.1: `worker.requests` does not allow a request to its own subsystem (P/02.1). The source's holder writes it, `cas ""`; a repeat of the firing is the same row, the step `ok`.

| Field | Written by | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `target`, `f`, `i` | the source | the key |
| `firing` | the source | the name of the source firing |
| `level` | the source | the chain level: a run not from a chain — 1, a row carries its own + 1 |
| `moment` | the source | the step's `occurred`: the `occurred` of the target's run |
| `started_by` | the source | the source's `by`: `event`, `sched`, `manual` |
| `source` | the source | the firing's cameras: the target `from_event` |
| `log_only` | the source | the source is in `log_only` |
| `taken` | the target's holder | when the target's run has reached the end, a wait or got into the queue; none — not taken yet |

The source's step reads the target's row and refuses before writing: `fail`, `chain.self`, `chain.ruleGone` (no row, a tombstone), `chain.forbidden` (`allow_chain: false`), `chain.ruleOff`, `chain.max` (the level is greater than `CHAIN_MAX` = 5); the target's row is not read — `target.unread`. A step `ok` means the row is written: the target's result is its `fired`.

The writer of the worker table is a role: the source and the target's holder write the row whole with `cas`, a conflict — reread and keep the other's fields (P/03.4 «Гонки»). The target's holder reads the rows of its scenarios without `taken` on every pass. The run name already exists (memory, `runs`, including the queue, `done`) — there is no run, only `taken`: a crash between the run row and `taken`. `moment` earlier than the target's `since` (chapter 2 Н1.4) — `fired` `result: skip`, the check `chain.off`, `taken`: the row was filed by another `revision`, and a repeat by the new tree would give other names. The run is `<сценарий>-chain-<f>-<i>`, `occurred` = `moment`, `by` = `started_by`, `chain` in `fired`; the checks of Н1.8 in full, with muting and windows; the pass limit and the ceiling do not judge, `repeat` judges; `log_only` of the row or of the target — the run is `log_only`. The target is disabled, `refused`, `allow_chain` is removed — `fired` `result: skip`, the check `chain.off`. Then `taken` by a write with cas; a crash before it — a repeat with the same names. A row not by the form — a skip, the count `chains_unreadable`: the source will not learn of it.

**Limit:** the row is removed by the deadline `delete_after` of the table, 86400 s (P/06.1 Н1.6): a target without a holder for longer than that loses the run. **Limit:** the target's steps are in the target's log, they do not go as nested rows into the source's run.

## Н3 Pass

```
for a firing: already decided? → up to PER_PASS → checks Н1.8 (skip — fired, end) → under rate_per_minute → repeat
steps of the tree then from step i, the target is expanded at the moment of the step (Н1.9):
  output | preset | arm | disarm | disable → filing cam/requests/auto.<f>-<i> (f — the firing digest; valid_until = occurred of the step + valid_for); deadline passed — late
  detect → the operator's detector of the pair enabled without made? watched : order det (base, mask_of)
  scan   → recording → order detjob (window floor/ceil ± before/after; equal window — unchanged, fields of the first order)
  record → order rec (until, home)
  alarm  → row auto.alarms; log_only — nothing, step would
  runrule → checks of the target, row auto/chains/<target>-<f>-<i>
  wait | wait_event | ask | escalate → runs row written (Н1.11), fired waiting, the run has stopped
  each step — an event step
end of the tree — fired; cursor — after the filing and the write of runs (chapter 2)
running runs on every pass: end of the wait? → branch and end into the runs row → next step (the branch, then the step one level up); finished — remove the element, the name into done, queue — the next one
request run → a run without the limit and the ceiling; retry → the failed steps of the firing; POST /test → the same code, no actions
rows auto/chains of its scenarios without taken → name already exists? only taken : moment < since? skip : run <сценарий>-chain-<f>-<i> → taken
```

**Races.** Two triggerings of one scenario in one window are different firings (different events), different request names; the detector
order is one row, extended; a scan of the same window is the same job (`unchanged`), including from another scenario with other
`params` — the fields of the first order. A move after the filing, before the cursor moves, is a repeat of the firing: requests — `again`, orders —
`unchanged` or `extended`. A move during a wait — the new holder goes from the row (Н1.11). An edit during a wait and the end of the wait in one pass: the new `revision` removes the run before the step.
