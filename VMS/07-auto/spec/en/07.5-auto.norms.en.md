# V/07.5 — Scenarios between cameras: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`07.5-auto.norms.md`](../07.5-auto.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 5 of lesson V/07 «Сценарии» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../07-auto.md),
[instructions](../07-auto.README.md), manifest [`auto.subsystem.yaml`](../../auto.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

«A car at the gate camera — the yard camera to preset 3», when the cameras are in different members of the domain. Such a scenario is not a unit
of a cluster: the domain holds it whole in the shared settings, the evaluator of the cluster of the trigger camera executes it, and the action goes
as a request to the receiver at which the target camera holds its poll. The chapter sets norms for the document, the executor, the book `asks`, the request and
its outcomes, and the carrying through a relay. The camera's side (the token `ask`, the execution of a request) — [V/01.6](../../../01-cam/spec/01.6-cam.norms.md)
Н1.3, Н1.4; the receiver — [V/02.9](../../../02-rec/spec/02.9-rec.norms.md).

Norm ids are `V/07.5-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `valid_for` is seconds; the deadline of a request counts from `occurred` of the event, the «now» of a call is the evaluator's clock, their divergence from
the clock of the event's writer is an accepted limit; the receiver converts the deadline to its own clock.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — the copy of the shared settings
`domain/shared` ([P/09.1 Н1.10](../../../../Platform/09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md)), the sections of the book `domain/auto/asks/*/<раздел>` by its own request (Н1.3), the copy
of the topology `domain/member-topology`, the rows `domain/member-list/<раздел>` (`Entry.ca` of the receiver's cluster, Н1.4, V/02.9 Н2; there is no foreign section — the peer client requests it itself),
the copy of the book `domain/cam/sources` (the poll home of a target without an assignment, Н1.3), the assignments `domain/placed/rec/<ref>` (the recording cluster
of the target camera is its home, Н1.3; P/08.6), the heartbeats of the recorders `rec/heartbeats/*` (the receiver addresses); writes — the book contribution
`auto/asks/<слот>` ([P/03.7 Н1.2](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md)), the heartbeat.

**Books, topology and document at the cluster that is the domain holder.** At the cluster that holds the domain (the record `domain/holder` of its own store, the role base P/02.5 Н1.4; it did not pass `record` — the flag is not raised, the copies are read) there are no copies (P/09.1 Н1.4 `member_row`): a book is read from the row of its own cluster `domain/<sub>/<книга>/<CLUSTER>`
([P/08.5](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md) Н1.3), the topology — from the row `domain/topology`
([P/08.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md) Н1.2), the document — from the object named by the field `object` of the row
`domain/shared`; at a member — the object `domain/shared` (P/06.7 Н1.14). The `sha256` sum of the row and the signature are verified; a mismatch — the previous
document. So it is in all the rules of the chapter where a «copy» is said.

### Н1.1 The scenarios document

`domain.shared: [{name: scenarios, type: json, schema}]` — the value `settings.shared.auto.scenarios` of the domain's shared settings
([P/06.7 Н1.14](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.7-console.norms.md), [P/08.9](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.9-domain.norms.md)
Н1.2–Н1.3: the signature, the edit `PUT /domain/shared {base_rev?, shared}` with `If-Match`, the schema check). An array of no more than 1000 scenarios:

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | a string, required, unique in the array | the scenario name |
| `when` | `{camera, kind, member?}`, required | the trigger camera (the `ref` of the camera in the domain), the event kind, and `member` — the member whose camera it is: a `ref` is unique only within a cluster (V/01.6 Н1.2). The token for such a scenario is issued only to this member (P/08.7 Н3 `scenario`); no field — the member is not pinned, the scenario goes to the single owner of the `ref` |
| `then` | `oneOf`: `{camera, member?, action: preset, n, valid_for?}` or `{camera, member?, action: output, port, valid_for?}`, a key of the other branch is forbidden | the target camera (`ref`), `member` — the member whose camera it is: a `ref` is unique only within a cluster (ADR-0112 addendum (10) item 2); no field — the single owner of the `ref`; `n`, `port` — an integer ≥ 1 (the numbering of V/01.4 Н1.1, as in a cluster scenario), the validity period of the request `valid_for` — an integer 10…600 (default 30; the lower bound — as in V/07.1) |
| `rate_per_minute` | an integer 0…600 | the ceiling of firings; 0 or no field — default 6 |
| `enabled` | bool | a disabled one is not executed; no field — enabled |

Between cameras — only `preset` and `output`: recording, a detector and a scan are rows of the cluster, not a request through the domain. The deadline of a request is
`valid_for`, by the same name as in a cluster scenario (V/07.1). The form is caught at the domain door; the meaning (what the target can do) — a refusal
of the target; there is no reconciliation of chapter 3 over the document, the refusal of the executor — Н1.2.

### Н1.2 The executor

A scenario of the document is executed by **the evaluator of the cluster of the trigger camera**: the one that has the camera with `ref` = `when.camera` in
`cam/cameras/*`, while `when.member`, if set, names this cluster (a camera-cluster is a cluster to itself, its evaluator is in its process, P/02.4 Н1.10). The execution is as for
a cluster scenario with one trigger `{sub: cam, kind: when.kind, unit: <id камеры>}` (chapter 2): the log, the firing, the cursor
(the cursor of the document is in the evaluator's memory; after a restart — from «now»), `PER_PASS`, `COLD_START`, the ceiling
`rate_per_minute`; the outcome — in the heartbeat (Н1.6). A new scenario watches from the moment it is seen; an edit does not reset the cursor; `reason`
is cleared by the pass. A scenario without a name or with a repeated name is not executed, a refusal in the heartbeat (the field `shared`, Н1.6). Only
the scenarios of its own trigger cameras are executed; the first live evaluator of the cluster by slot name (heard within 30 s by the heartbeat
`auto/heartbeats/*`, the observation contract `fresh`) — one per cluster.

### Н1.3 The book `asks`

`domain.books.asks: {from: auto/asks/, key: target, pick: all, to: "*"}` (P/08.5): **roads to the target** — at which receivers the target
camera holds its poll. The contribution `auto/asks/<слот>` is written by the first live evaluator of the cluster (Н1.2) of each path node — the poll home
of the target camera (its recording cluster or poll home, P/08.7 Н1.1), the centre, the relay of the trigger; one that is not first — its contribution is empty. A node
decides as follows: there is an assignment of the target — its home is `cluster` (in `star` — the centre); there is none — the centre publishes all the targets of the document, the others take the poll
home from the book `domain/cam/sources`; the target is not in this book — the relay (its own cluster in `relays` of the topology) publishes the road up for the triggers of the document; whose road to take is decided by the `aud` of the token (Н1.4):

```yaml
target:   ref of the target camera (the book key)
by:       [refs of the trigger cameras of the document with this target]
acts:     [actions of the document towards this target]
urls:     [ingest of the recorders of its own cluster heard within 30 s, by slot name]
up?:      true — a road into its own relay for the ascent to the centre (the target is behind a foreign relay)
```

An element exists only for targets that have at least one enabled scenario in the document: no scenario — no road. `then.member` names the home of the target: the node judges the assignment and the source book of the camera of this member. **Limit:** the book key is `ref`, targets with one `ref` at two members share an element, and the token names only `ref` (P/08.7 Н3).
`ingest` of a recorder's heartbeat not by the form of the family `rec-heartbeat-words` (V/02) is unread, not «no receiver»: on this
pass the recorder does not enter `urls`, the others enter; there are no readable ones — the contribution holds the `urls` published last: its own
contribution row; there is none — there is no element. The document or the topology does not read — the contribution does not change.

The book is declared to all: `domain.books.asks.to: "*"`, the field `to` of a contribution is not read (P/08.5 Н1.3 `to_all`), the selection is by the `aud` of the token (Н1.4). The sections are `domain/auto/asks/*/<раздел>`, a section is 12 bits of SHA-256 of the key, the key is the same at the holder and at the copy. Without a request the book does not reach a member: the evaluator on any cluster, at the holder too, at its own start as well, puts `domain/book-want/auto/asks/<раздел>` for the targets of the rules of its cluster and renews `at` once per `BOOK_WANT_EVERY` = 86400 s while the rules name the target; it does not read the request rows, therefore at start and at a change of the first evaluator it puts all of them; it removes nothing itself, a request without renewal is removed by the agent after `BOOK_WANT_TTL` = 259200 s, copies without a request — by the agent as well, at the turn of the term the evaluator does nothing (P/09.1 Н1.4 `book_want`); a section has not come — there is no road, the firing ends as without a road.

**Limit:** a section row is no larger than 524288 bytes (P/08.5): with an element of about 1 KiB, an estimate, — about 500 roads per section; beyond that — the previous row, the log `domain.books.too_large` at the holder, new roads of the section do not come. The copy on a camera grows with its rules, not with the domain (ADR-0113 addendum (11) item 1). A new request gives a road not at once: up to the member's report period and the circle of sections, therefore the first rule for a new target may stay silent as long (ADR-0113 addendum (13) item 9).

### Н1.4 The request to the target's receiver

On a firing the executor:

1. takes the token `ask`, a new or a current one, through the agent's local door `POST /tokens/cam/ask` (`ref` — the target, `by` — the trigger; P/03.7 Н1.5; the scope `scenario` — P/08.7 Н3: the token
   is issued only if such a scenario is in the document, its `acts` are the actions of the scenario); a refusal of issue — a firing without
   action, `reason` in the heartbeat;
2. takes the road to the target from the copy of the section of the book `asks`: the elements with the trigger in `by`, the contributor in the `aud` of the token and the same
   `up`, in the order of the copy; the receivers of `urls` in order, the first one that answers;
3. files the call «request» (V/02.9 Н2) — the token, the action (`action`, `n` | `port`), `firing` — the firing key (`f` of V/07.4
   Н1.1: the first 32 hex of `sha256(UTF-8(<сценарий> + "\n" + <id события>))`), the deadline `occurred + valid_for` and its own «now»
   (the receiver converts the deadline to its own clock);
4. waits for the outcome until the deadline + 5 s outside the pass: the request is in memory, the outcome is asked by a repeat of the call on the pass: `performed`,
   `refused: <почему>` (the target cannot do it), `expired` (the deadline has passed), `unknown` (the receiver lost it — a restart — or the outcome of the holder);
   the outcome — in the heartbeat `shared` (Н1.6).

**The receiver's side** (an addition to V/02.9 Н2 «просьба»): it checks the token of the kind `ask` (`ref` — the target of the call, the action ∈ `acts`:
there is an object with the same `action`, `n` or `port`; `camera`, `valid_for` are not compared),
puts the request into the poll answer of the target (it wakes a held poll; a request at a neighbour is a request here: the poll answer; the outcome is answered where the request was filed); a request with the same (`by`, target, action, `firing`) — live or with
an outcome in the memory `REMEMBER` — gets the same number and the same outcome; different `firing` — different requests; no
more than `MAX_LIVE_ASKS` = 16 live requests per requester at the receiver, beyond that — the outcome `refused: <почему>`; outcomes are remembered for `REMEMBER` = 15 min, no more than `OUTCOMES_KEPT` = 64 per
requester at the target; the requester is the `by` of the token; the deadline is finite, later than «now» and no further than `ASK_DEADLINE_MAX` = 600 s, otherwise 400 `bad body`; expired ones are swept by any
call. The target executes the request as a `cam` request (V/01.6 Н1.4, V/01.4) and answers with the outcome in the next poll; the pusher names
the row `rec.ask-<firing>-<i>` (V/02.9 Н1.2; a document scenario has one action, `i` = 0): a repeat through another receiver or
after a restart of the receiver reaches the holder with the same `rid` — `again` or `refused` by `digest`, there is no second execution.

### Н1.5 Through a relay

A target behind its own relay — a direct road (the relay's book). A target behind a foreign one — one road into its own relay with `up`:
its forwarder (V/02.9 Н1.8) sees the request on its receiver, requests its own token `ask` to the centre (`up` — the centre; the same `by`,
`acts`; P/08.7 Н3 `up_claim`), carries the request to the centre with the same `firing` and waits for the outcome at the centre. At the centre the request goes into the poll answer
of the forwarder of the target's home (V/02.9 Н1.7), which puts it on its own receiver, and the target takes it by an ordinary poll; the outcome goes back the same way,
by the field `outcomes`. At each hop the deadline is the same moment converted to the receiver's clock, nothing is stored; a repeat after
a break — until the deadline, after `period × U(0,5; 1,5)`, `period` = 2 s.

### Н1.6 The heartbeat

The field `shared` of the evaluator is `{<имя сценария документа>: {phase: running | refused, reason?, since, asked, outcomes: {<исход>: n}, holding?, decided_without?}}`
(`since` — the moment from which the executor watches the log; `outcomes` — the four outcomes of Н1.4) for the
scenarios of its own trigger cameras; for the book — `asks_published` (the elements of the contribution). It is read by the form (V/11.4 Н1.21).

## Н3 Pass

```
the document from the copy of domain/shared → the scenarios of its own trigger cameras (the first evaluator of the cluster)
firing (chapter 2, one trigger) → token ask → road from the book asks → «request» to the receiver → outcome → heartbeat shared
contribution of the book asks (the first evaluator of the path node): targets of the document → {target, by, acts, urls, up?}
```

**Races.** The document changed in the middle of a request — an already issued token is valid until its deadline (P/03.7 Н1.5), a new one is not issued if
the scenario is removed. Two evaluators of a cluster considered themselves first — both execute and publish: the requests of one firing carry one `firing` —
the same number at the receiver and the same `rid` at the holder; the contributions of one member — `pick: all`, the elements are identical. A lagging
executor filed a firing later than `REMEMBER` (15 min) — the receiver gives a new number, but the row at the holder is the same
(`rec.ask-<firing>-<i>`): `again`. A double execution remains only if the holder's row has already been removed too — an accepted limit. At a
camera-cluster a repeat is cut off only by the receiver's memory `REMEMBER`; after a restart of the receiver a second execution is possible —
an accepted limit.
Events between the stop and the start of the document executor and at a change of the first evaluator of the cluster are not executed: the cursor
of the document is in memory; the field `since` of the element of `shared` (Н1.6) is the moment from which the executor watches.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a scenario without a name, a repeated name | not executed | the heartbeat `shared` | fix the document |
| the token was not issued (no scenario, no home) | a firing without action | the heartbeat | — |
| no road in the book | a firing without action, `reason` | the heartbeat | — |
| no receiver answered, the token was rejected | a firing without action, `reason`, no repeat | the heartbeat | — |
| `ingest` of a recorder out of form | not in `urls` on the pass; no readable ones — the previous `urls` | the heartbeat | fix the recorder |
| the target cannot do it | `refused: <почему>` | the heartbeat `shared` | fix the document |
| the deadline has passed | `expired` | the heartbeat `shared` | a wider `valid_for` |
| the receiver lost the request | `unknown` after the deadline + 5 s | the heartbeat `shared` | — |
| more than 16 live requests per requester | the outcome `refused: <почему>` | the heartbeat `shared` | — |
| the deadline is not finite, has passed or is further than `ASK_DEADLINE_MAX` | 400 `bad body` | the requester | — |
