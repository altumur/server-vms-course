# V/07.2 — The evaluator — a query to the log: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/07.2-auto.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 2 of lesson V/07 «Сценарии» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/07-auto.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](07-auto.README.md), manifest [`auto.subsystem.yaml`](../auto.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the chapter is moved to the log merge contract — `cf4a3ce`).

The evaluator does not keep a state machine in memory: on every pass it asks the cluster event log what happened after
the cursor, and decides a firing by the closing event. The chapter sets norms for what the evaluator watches, how it reads the log, when
a scenario fires, the cursor, the long poll and a start without an event. Matching a scenario to cameras is chapter 3; what is done on a firing is chapter 4.

Norm ids are `V/07.2-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

### Н1.1 What to watch

**The wishes** of the evaluator are the triples `(sub, kind, unit)` from the triggers of its enabled scenarios with `start: event` and `run_mode: always`
and from the events of all `wait_event` steps of the `then` tree of its enabled scenarios (chapter 4 Н1.11), not only of the running runs: after downtime longer than `JOURNAL_BACK` the event would have landed in the window before the run started; **the cost** is reading these triples on every pass. The event could also have landed in the window of the firing itself, before `through`, so every pass of a wait also asks the wait query (Н1.2); **the cost** is a query per run; a trigger without `unit` means any
unit. A disabled scenario watches nothing; a scenario `schedule`, `manual` and one with `run_mode: manual` does not ask the log, except for the wait query (Н1.6). A trigger with `target` (V/07.1) is a triple without `unit`: the composition of the target is judged by Н1.3; **the cost** is reading all units of the kind. The `unit` of a trigger is the id of a unit of the subsystem `sub` (a number — by its decimal
notation); into the query to the log and into the comparison it goes as the reference `<sub>/<unit>` (the resource rejects a bare id, the contract
`events-merge` «Запрос и ответ ресурса»).

**The built-in detector** (ADR-0107 item 8). Its event lies in the tree `cam`, the row `unit` with `det_kind` (V/01.2 Н1.5), so a `det` trigger and a `det` step event without `unit` give a second triple `(cam, unit, —)`, and everywhere below the «triple» of such a trigger means both. Two triggers in the scenario are not needed: they would mean «and» (chapter 3). Selection by `det_kind` is in memory; **the cost** is reading all `unit` rows of the cluster on every pass while there is such a trigger; while there is none, there is no query. A `det` with `unit` names a row `det/units`; there is no second triple.

### Н1.2 Reading the log

The evaluator is a participant of the [log merge contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/events-merge.md) (owner P/06.4):
it merges by itself `GET /events` of the live resources (the door rows `platform/doors/*`, freshness `LOST_AFTER`; P/05 Н1.3, admittance
`autoworker`) by the same rules as the console — duplicates by `id`, the order `cut`, `truncated`, `incomplete` and `complete`;
the console's door is not opened to it. The epochs are the rows `<sub>/epoch/*` of the subsystems declared in `worker.reads`: `cam`, `rec`,
`live`, `det`, `detjob`, `detedge` (the VMS events); a row of a former epoch the evaluator marks `epoch_is` by the policy
`older_epochs` of its manifest (`fenced` | `earlier-run`); a subsystem whose epochs it did not declare gives `epoch_is: unknown`, not
proof (it gives no firing, Н1.3), `complete` does not change; the epochs of the subsystems with trigger events are declared;
`auto` is not among them: the events of `auto` are not triggers (chapter 3: `unfit`), otherwise a firing would wake itself (Kimi's addition
to the analysis of the V/07 review, question 1); `unknown` is only for a foreign one: if a scenario needs its events, its epoch is in `worker.reads`;
a needed epoch is not read — `incomplete[<sub>/epoch]`, the window is incomplete (Н1.4). A row that it considers fenced-off it
reconciles with a fresh read of the epoch (the contract, `epochs_age`). There is a separate query for each wish triple and each class
(`class=alarm`, `class=observation`: inside a class the order `cut` is by `t`), the read window is `[through, now)` by `t`,
`keep: oldest`: a growing window is read from its start, truncation does not lose early rows. **The window end** is `max(through, now −
JOURNAL_SETTLE)` (`JOURNAL_SETTLE` = 5 s: the cursor does not reach «now» — late rows are still being written): a row between the window
end and `now` is decided at once and is read again by the following passes; the repeat is harmless by the firing name (Н1.3, chapter 4).

**The backward query.** A row can land on a resource when the cursor has already passed its `t`. For each triple and class the same pass asks
a second query: the window `[max(through − JOURNAL_BACK, since), through)` by `t`, `keep: newest` (`JOURNAL_BACK` = 300 s, the value of
`COLD_START`). Its rows are candidates on a par with the rows of the read window; its truncation and incompleteness do not hold the cursor, nor do those of the partners of its candidates: the next
pass asks again. **Cost:** a wide trigger pays for `JOURNAL_BACK` on every pass; rows suppressed by the ceiling come again after a restart and are filed under the ceiling (chapter 4 Н1.5). **Limit:** a row that landed on a resource when the cursor had gone past its `t` by more than `JOURNAL_BACK` does not
become completing, there is no count and no event; it stays a partner while the completing one is still in the backward query: the partner query goes by `occurred`.

**Completeness.** The window is complete when every query of the read window and of the partners of its candidates has `complete` and `truncated` = 0 (the `complete` of the platform is not
redefined, the contract `events-merge` «Ответ»). The answer of a read-window query is truncated (`truncated` > 0) — the window of this pass ends at the `t`
of the last row given out, not including this `t`, — by the smallest of the truncated queries, the window end being no further than it (Н1.4); all
rows given out are at one `t` — the cursor stands. One rule — here, in Н1.4 and in Н4. The window is not held by `partial` (a torn last row is read to the end by the backward query) and by `lines_garbled` (a row not by the form is lost: the count is in the heartbeat, chapter 4 Н1.7).

**The partners** — a separate query for the triple of each **other** trigger of the scenario, `by=occurred&late=<с>`, `keep: oldest`, no
class, the window `[occurred(e) − within, occurred(e) + within]` over the candidates `e` of the pass; the `to` of the request is the binary64 value
next after the upper bound: `to` is not in the window (the parameters `by`, `late` of the contract `events-merge`, «Запрос и ответ ресурса»). A query per
candidate or a common one per pass, separately for the read window and for the backward query — the result is the same. `late` is how much a write could lag behind `occurred`: `⌈now − to⌉` of the request window, not
less than the default 3600; a row written later than `to + late` is not visible to the request. An incomplete or truncated partner query of the read window — the cursor stands: its order is by `occurred`, the `t` of the last row gives no window bound; longer than `COLD_START` — Н1.4, the truncation key `truncated:<sub>/<kind>[/<unit>]`.

**The wait query** — per run, while a step `wait_event` (the triple of the step's event) or `escalate` with `stop: reset` (the triple of the scenario's `io.input` trigger) stands: the partner form (`by=occurred&late=<с>`, `keep: oldest`, no class, `to` and `late` — the same way), the window `[occurred шага, until]` (chapter 4 Н1.11). A match is by Н1.3 items 1, 3, the first one being the smallest `(occurred, id)`: it ends the wait even on an incomplete answer. There is no match, and the answer is incomplete or truncated — «no» is not decided, the wait goes on; longer than `COLD_START` from `until` — it is decided by what there is, the key goes into `decided_without`. This query does not hold the cursor.


### Н1.3 Firing

A scenario fires on a **completing** event `e` (the candidates go in the order of `t`, on a tie by `id` bytes, as `cut`), if:

1. `e` matched one of the triggers: the row field `subsystem` = `sub`, `kind` = `kind`, the reference `<sub>/<unit>` of the trigger is the `unit`
   of the row or its `of` (as the `unit` filter of the resource, P/05 Н3 «запрос (ресурс)» step 4: a `det` trigger with `unit: "7-linecross"` and
   a row with `of: cam/7` do not match — the `of` of another subsystem); for a trigger with `target` instead: the `unit` or `of` of the row is `cam/<c>`, for a `rec` row the field `cam` = `c`, `c` is in the target at the moment of the decision (chapter 4 Н1.9: a child of the target is an input, its number equals the `port` of the row); for a `det` trigger without `unit`, with a target or without, a `cam` row of the kind `unit` with `det_kind` = `kind` also passes, except `phase: end`; a row `phase: still` passes only for such triggers, a `cam` trigger of the kind `unit` does not see it; every `match` field equals the row field strictly (`"1"` ≠ `1`;
   the form of the field is set by the lesson of the event's writer — `io.input` of `cam`, `value` only `open`, `closed`, V/01.2 Н1.5; V/07 only compares; a field not by the form
   of the writer is not read, rather than absent: a trigger with `match` on it does not match, the row goes into the window and the cursor as any other);
2. for each **other** trigger — one that `e` did not match — there is a matched partner event `p`, fit by item 3, with `occurred` ≥ `since` (no field — `t`): the relay does not fire on a pair a part of which happened before it (Н1.4); the `occurred`
   differ by no more than `within` in both directions, and `p` is earlier than `e` by the `(t, id)` of the row, as `cut`. The pair is completed by its
   latest row in this order: a pair gives one firing, whichever of its rows landed first and however many times it is counted;
3. `e` is not a suppression summary (a row with `repeats` never fires) and is proof by the epoch: `epoch_is: current` or
   `earlier-run` (a former run is not fencing) — yes; `fenced`, `unknown` (the epoch is not checked — the event could be fenced-off) and
   a row without `epoch_is` (the epoch is not read) — no (the contract `events-merge` «Эпохи»; ADR-0091 addendum (23)–(27)).

A scenario with one trigger has no window (`within` = 0; otherwise — a refusal, chapter 3). **The firing** is `<сценарий>-<id события e>`;
one event — one firing, however many times it is computed. A matched firing goes through the checks of chapter 4 Н1.8 before the actions.

**The events of the firing** are `e` and, for each other trigger, the partner with the greatest `(t, id)` earlier than `e`: a late row changes the choice less often than with the smallest. A row of the built-in detector goes into them as `{sub: det, kind: <det_kind>}`. From them come `events`, `source` (chapter 4 Н1.6). **Limit:** a partner that landed between the decision and the repeat can change the choice: for the same device the name is the same, there is no second command, a new device gets its first request.

**Order.** The scenarios of a pass go by the `order` of the row `auto/rule_marks/<scenario>` (chapter 6 Н1.1; there is no row or it is unreadable — 0), on a tie by id as a number: for one event the smaller `order` is decided first. **Limit:** the order acts among the scenarios of one evaluator; between evaluators there is none.

### Н1.4 Cursor

`unit.state.cursor` is `{through, since, held_from?}`: up to which `t` the log has been read (seconds), from which `t` the scenario watches, and
from which pass the window is incomplete.

- **Submit first, then advance** (chapter 4): the firings up to the window end are filed — the cursor goes to the window end; it is written no more often than
  `STATE_EVERY` = 10 s (P/03.6 Н1.7). After a move the new holder starts from the cursor — a repeat of the same firing gives the same
  action names (chapter 4), it is harmless; a firing suppressed by the ceiling left no name — the limit of chapter 4 Н1.5.
- **Only for a complete window:** the window is incomplete (`complete: false`: a resource did not answer, `silent`, `said`, or an epoch is not read;
  an incomplete or truncated partner query of the read window) — the firings of the rows read are filed at once, only the cursor stands, a repeat gives the same names; the scenario
  phase is `running` with the field `holding` (seconds since `held_from`); the answer is truncated — the cursor goes up to the `t` of the last row given out, not
  including it, and no further than the window end (Н1.2 «Completeness»), all rows are at one `t` — it stands.
- **The pass limit** (chapter 4 Н1.5): the first unsubmitted one is a row of the read window or a moment `run_at`: the cursor goes up to its `t`, `m`; a row of the backward query: the cursor stands, it is monotone, and the window of the next pass contains the row again.
- **`COLD_START`** = 300 s is the lag ceiling: the window is incomplete longer than 300 s from `held_from` — the evaluator decides by what
  there is and advances the cursor; the field `decided_without` (the keys of `incomplete`: which resources and epochs were silent; truncation at one `t` and truncation of the partners —
  the key `truncated:<sub>/<kind>[/<unit>]`, the value is the word `truncated`, `incomplete` has no such key) — once per outage, in
  the heartbeat of one pass. An outage lasts from `held_from` to the first complete window: `held_from` is kept, every pass of the outage decides and
  advances the cursor. Truncation at one `t`: `through` is the binary64 value next after `t`, not the window end.
- The first take: there is no cursor (read; the read is not confirmed — Н4) — `since` = `now`, `through` = `now − JOURNAL_SETTLE` (the scenario does not fire on the past); the cursor
  is written by the very first pass, `held_from` — at the first incomplete window, by the rule `STATE_EVERY`.
- A new `revision` (an edit, enabling): the field `revision` of the cursor row (P/03.6 Н1.7) is not equal to the `revision` of the run; the holder compares them at start: the platform does not reset the cursor by `revision`, and the process memory does not survive a crash. Then, and on revival from `refused` (chapter 3): `since` = `now`, `through` = `max(through, now − JOURNAL_SETTLE)`, the cursor is written by the very first pass, as at the first take: otherwise a break before `STATE_EVERY` would again give a new `revision`, and the moments of downtime would be lost. **Limit:** a break inside this pass before the write: `since` did not land, the start-up takes a new one, the moments from this pass are lost; the window is one pass. A candidate and a partner are only a row with `occurred` ≥ `since` (no field — `t`), in the read window and in the backward query: the relay does not fire on an event earlier than this moment. **Limit:** the firings of the former `when` over the last seconds are lost.
- A `refused` scenario (chapter 3) does not read the log, `through` stands, the pass writes `since` = `now` into the cursor by the rule `STATE_EVERY`: `refused` is not written anywhere else, and after a restart the revival is judged by this `since`. A disabled one does not move the cursor. **Limit:** the lag of `since` is up to 10 s from the last pass that saw `refused`; from the real end of `refused` — 10 s plus the downtime: nobody saw the end of `refused` during the downtime, the events of the downtime are judged as ordinary ones.

### Н1.5 Long poll

With `LONG_POLL` ≠ 0 the evaluator holds `GET /events/wait` on each live resource ([P/05
Н1.6](../../../Platform/05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md)) with `client` — the name of the evaluator's slot — and `want` =
`<sub>/<kind>[/<unit>]` by the wishes (Н1.1); there are no wishes in `want` — there is no request, the periodic pass wakes it.
A wish a part of which is outside the grammar of `want` (ASCII letters, digits, `_`, `-`, `.`, 1…64 characters, not starting with a dot; P/05 Н1.6) does not
go into `want`: such a scenario is woken only by the periodic pass, there is no early one; the other wishes are woken as usual, rather than the
whole request getting 400 (Kimi's addition, question 2). This does not narrow the form of the trigger fields: the unit names of subsystems are wider than `want`.
The answer `changed` — an early pass only over the touched scenarios (`touched`); the scenarios or the resources changed — a new request,
before it — no longer than 30 s; a wait error — a pause of about 2 s (± 50 %), the pass is not woken. The answer `full` is like an error: an immediate new request would spin the loop on a busy resource; `replaced` — its own wait was replaced by its own new request, not an error. The periodic pass of the runtime
stays (P/03.3). The heartbeat — `waits`, `woken`, `early_passes`, `wait_errors`, `want_skipped` (how many wishes are outside `want`
now; chapter 4).

### Н1.6 Start without an event

**On a schedule** — `start: schedule`, `run_mode: always`, `run_at: {days, time}`: the candidates are the moments `m` of the window `[through, конец окна)` of Н1.2, whole minutes, with `m` ≥ `since`: the wall-clock time of `m` in the zone `TIMEZONE` (chapter 6 Н1.7) equals `time`, the weekday is in `days` (1 — Monday); `24:MM` is a minute of the day following a day from `days`. There are no queries to the log — the window is complete; `wait_event` of a running run asks as usual. The same cursor: file first, then advance, the first take and a new `revision` of Н1.4, `PER_PASS` of chapter 4. **The firing** is `<сценарий>-at-<m>`, `occurred` = `m`, `by: sched`: a repeat gives the same names. A moment missed by downtime is a candidate at start-up: `through` stood still; a request with a past deadline is `late` (chapter 4 Н1.1). **Limit:** wall-clock time that a clock change skips does not exist — there is no candidate; a repeated hour gives two candidates. A `TIMEZONE` name outside the zone database (chapter 6 Н1.7): there is no wall-clock time, there are no candidates, the cursor stands, an entry in `unchecked` (chapter 3 Н1.3): the moment is not lost, a corrected zone will raise it as a moment of downtime. **Limit:** there is no catch-up ceiling: a request is held by `late`, an order by `expired`, while an alarm and a chain have no deadline, so a daily alarm after a week of downtime is seven rows at start-up.

**Manually** — `start: manual` or `run_mode: manual`: a pass has no candidates, a run is the request `run` (chapter 4 Н1.12). The cursor goes by Н1.4: there are no wishes — the window is complete. The triple of a `wait_event` step here only fills the wait candidates: its incompleteness does not hold the cursor, and it does not give the branch `no` (chapter 4 Н1.11).

**The recording schedule window** (ADR-0112 addendum (6) item 1) is a row `hidden: record` without a trigger, `conditions: [{kind: time, schedule}]`, V/11.3 Н1.3. `time` here is not a start but a window. On every pass the evaluator judges the condition, the muting and the maintenance windows, as for a firing (chapter 4 Н1.8); there is no firing, no log row and no `repeat`. `in_effect` is true — the steps of `then`, `occurred` is the moment of the pass, the order is extended only when less than half of `minutes × 60` remains until `until`: otherwise the row `rec/recordings` would change on every pass (chapter 4 Н1.4). A definite false outranks an unreadable value: nothing is done, the order lives out its `until`. Muted, in a maintenance window — the same. `unknown`, and also an unreadable muting and maintenance window with a true or unknown `in_effect` — the decision of the previous pass is kept: the window was running — it is extended; there is no previous decision, a restart — it is extended too: an extra recording is cheaper than a loss of archive, the duration of `unknown` is not limited, the reason is visible by `recordReason` `schedule unread` (V/11.3 Н1.3; a decision of «Архитектор» 2026-11-02). The `cond.unread` of such a pass goes only into the heartbeat, not into the log. **Limit:** as with `motion`: recording by schedule depends on a live `auto`; the extension happens at half of the duration, so recording stops when the evaluator is silent for longer than `minutes × 30` s, not `minutes × 60`.

What an enabled scenario of each `start` MUST have is chapter 3 Н1.1.

## Н3 Pass

```
wishes — from the enabled scenarios; long poll by the wishes (early pass by touched)
scenarios — by order, on a tie by id
for a scenario: new revision (cursor row ≠ run) → since = now, through = max(through, now − JOURNAL_SETTLE), cursor by this pass; chapter 3 (fit?); no — refused, the log is not read, the cursor stands
  window [through, now) → a query per triple and class, keep oldest → events
  backward query [max(through − 300, since), through), keep newest → events
  candidates e — the rows of both → partner query by=occurred&late [occurred(e) − within, occurred(e) + within]
  firings: the closing event and the partners earlier than it by (t, id), all occurred ≥ since; only current and earlier-run, without summaries → chapter 4
  schedule: candidates m of the window [through, window end), m ≥ since, firing <сценарий>-at-<m> → chapter 4
  hidden: record with time: in_effect false → nothing; muted, maintenance window → nothing; unreadable and unknown → as the previous pass, none — extend; otherwise extend when less than half remains; no firing
  running runs (row runs) → chapter 4 Н1.11
  window complete → cursor = window end max(through, now − 5) (after filing); truncated → cursor up to t of the last row (not including), no further than the window end; unsubmitted beyond PER_PASS → cursor up to its t, a backward-query row — stands
  incomplete → holding; > 300 s → decided_without, advance
```

**Races.** Substitution of the evaluator: the former one still files until it loses the lease (`unconfirmed_max: off` — it does not file after), the new one
reads the cursor and computes the same firings — the action names are the same (chapter 4), the repeat is harmless. An event written to a resource
later than its `t` (a late row) — `JOURNAL_SETTLE` 5 s covers the usual delay; what is later and with `t` < `through` is a candidate
of the backward query: the pair is completed by its latest row by `(t, id)`, the name is the same, whoever counts it; beyond
`JOURNAL_BACK` — the limit of Н1.2. A move in the middle of this does not change the name: the order is taken from the rows, not from memory.

## Н4 Refusals

| Reason | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the resource is silent | the window is incomplete, `holding`; after 300 s — `decided_without` | heartbeat | — |
| the answer is truncated (`truncated`) | the cursor — up to the `t` of the last row given out (not including), no further than the window end; all rows at one `t` — it stands, after 300 s — `decided_without` `truncated:<sub>/<kind>[/<unit>]`, the cursor past this `t` | heartbeat | — |
| a row landed when the cursor had gone past its `t` by more than `JOURNAL_BACK` | it does not become completing, the limit of Н1.2 | — | — |
| the partner query of the read window is incomplete or truncated | the cursor stands, `holding`; after 300 s — `decided_without` | heartbeat | — |
| the wait query is incomplete or truncated, there is no match | the wait goes on; after 300 s from `until` — `decided_without`, the branch `no` | heartbeat | — |
| the backward query or the partners of its candidates are incomplete | the cursor goes on, the next pass asks again | — | — |
| an event field not by the writer's form (the VMS event families) | a trigger with `match` on it does not match; not a refusal, the cursor goes on | — | the event's writer |
| the wait failed, the answer `full` | a pause of 2 s, `wait_errors` | heartbeat, series | — |
| the cursor read is not confirmed | the unit does not run, phase `failed` with a backoff: not read is not absent | heartbeat | — |
| `TIMEZONE` outside the zone database, `start: schedule` | no candidates, the cursor stands, `unchecked` | heartbeat, log | fix `w2c.env` |
| the folder of the trigger target is unreadable | the check `cond.unread`, `result: skip` (chapter 4 Н1.8), `unchecked` | events, heartbeat | — |
| a moment `run_at` missed by downtime | a candidate at start-up; a request with a past deadline — `late` | events, heartbeat | — |

## Н5 Acceptance

A firing on the stand (an event of the teaching detector → `fired`) — chapter 4; the merge function — the vectors
[`events-merge`](../../../Platform/common/vectors/events-merge/README.md) (a worker participant: a declared, an undeclared and an unread
epoch); the firing decision — by reading and by a test of the implementation (`fires` over events; the cases: partner A before the cursor and the completing B after it;
a move between them; a late partner row; the trigger `unit` `"12"` and `12` against the unit row `12`; an `of` of another
subsystem; a new `revision` while the holder is down; a partner earlier than `since`; `run_at` at the window edge, a moment of downtime).
