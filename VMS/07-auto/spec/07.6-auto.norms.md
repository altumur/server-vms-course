# V/07.6 — Schedules, modes, windows: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/07.6-auto.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 6 of lesson V/07 «Сценарии» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; ADR-0105 items 2, 9). Lecture (`ru/07-auto.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](07-auto.README.md), manifest [`auto.subsystem.yaml`](../auto.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

A condition of a scenario asks about a moment: whether a schedule is in effect, what the object mode is, whether a maintenance window is on. The chapter
sets norms for the tables `auto` that hold the answers, and for the moment function: it is one, and it is computed in the evaluator. `backend` does not compute
the moment.

Norm ids are `V/07.6-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `t` and the time fields of rows are seconds by the canon P/01.1 Н0; a day is counted in minutes 0…1440.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the tables `auto/<т>/<имя>` (P/06.1 Н1.6) and the heartbeat
field `object_mode`; the chapter has no families of its own beyond them. The time zone — `TIMEZONE`, Н1.7.

### Н1.1 Tables

The table doors are P/06.1 Н1.6, the form is the manifest. The evaluator reads the tables on every pass, at its beginning; between passes it does not
keep the values. Rows do not refer to each other with protection: what follows the removal of a row is done by `backend` (V/11).

| Table | Key | Row | Writes | Reads | Unreadable | No row |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `schedules` | `id` | a schedule: `kind` (`always`, `never`, `week`, `cycle`, `dates`, `astro`), `week`, `overrides`, `valid`, `cycle`, `dates`, `astro`, `owner`, `builtin` | `backend` or the console | the evaluator | `unknown` | `false` |
| `day_groups` | `id` | a group of special days: `rules` | `backend` or the console | the evaluator | `unknown` for a schedule that names it | the group does not contain the day |
| `modes` | `all` — one row | the whole list: `items`, `otherwise`; one version for the whole list | `backend` or the console | the evaluator, `backend` | `unknown` | there are no modes: `object_mode` is not told, the condition `mode` is false |
| `mode_current` | `now` — one row | a manual activation: `mode`, `until` (`null` — until lifted), the stamp `at` | `backend` or the console | the evaluator | `unknown` | there is no activation, the mode is by schedules |
| `windows` | `id` | a maintenance window: `schedule`, `rules` (`null` — all scenarios), `enabled` | `backend` or the console | the evaluator | `unknown` | there is no window |
| `versions` | `{scenario}-{number:int}` | a history version, Н1.8 | `backend` | `backend` | `backend` does not take the history as empty: a read refusal | there is no version |
| `rule_marks` | `scenario` | what is said about a scenario and does not edit it: `mute` (`{since, until, by, reason}` or `null`, expiry does not change the row), `order` (`int`, default 0): there is no new `revision`, ongoing runs are not lost (chapter 4 Н1.11) | `backend` or the console | the evaluator, `backend` | the firing does not go: `cond.unread` (chapter 4 Н1.8) | not muted, `order` 0 |
| `answers` | `{scenario}-{fire}-{step}-{user}` | an operator answer, Н1.9 | the table door, `backend` (V/11.4 Н1.10 «Ответ оператора») | the evaluator | there is no answer until the deadline of the step, the trace names the cause | there is no answer |

Not read is not absent: a read of a table that is not confirmed is not «no row».

**`unknown`.** The moment functions are three-valued: `true`, `false`, `unknown`. A row is unreadable — the check of chapter 4 Н1.8 is `ok: false`, `reason`
`cond.unread`, the row goes into `unchecked` (chapter 3 Н1.3): an action in doubt is not performed. The store did not answer — the firing is not decided (chapter 4 Н4).

The name of a row is the field `title`: a table with a key other than `name` does not declare the field `name` (P/06.1 Н1.6). The built-in rows of `schedules` `always`, `never` with `builtin: true` are created and preserved by `backend` (V/11). The evaluator does not read `builtin`.

### Н1.2 Day groups

The row `rules` is a list of rules; the group contains a date `d` if at least one of them contains it. `dow(d)` — 1…7 from Monday.

| `type` | Fields | Contains `d` |
|---|---|---|
| `range` | `from`, `to`, `yearly` | `from` is empty — no. `to` is empty or earlier than `from` — `to` = `from`. Without `yearly`: `from` ≤ `d` ≤ `to`. With `yearly` the `ММ-ДД` (month-day) are compared: when `from` ≤ `to` — inside the segment, otherwise the segment goes through the new year: `d` ≥ `from` or `d` ≤ `to` |
| `ord` | `nth` (1…5 or `last`), `dow`, `month` | `month` is not 0 and not the month of `d` — no. `dow(d)` ≠ `dow` — no. `last`: `день + 7` > the length of the month (`день` is the day of the month of `d`). Otherwise `⌈день / 7⌉` = `nth` |

Dates are compared as strings `ГГГГ-ММ-ДД` (year-month-day) character by character. `nth` occurs both as a string and as a number (`"2"` = `2`).

### Н1.3 The function `in_effect`

```
wall(t, tz) → (d, m)        d — the local date of the moment t in the zone tz, m — the local minute of the day, seconds and fraction dropped

iv([from, to]) → [f, e]     the first two elements are read; f, e — minutes of the day, 24:00 = 1440; e ≤ f → e + 1440

own(S, d) → a list of [a, b]
  S.valid.from is non-empty and d < from, or S.valid.to is non-empty and d > to → []
  the first in order o of S.overrides whose group day_group contains d:
    none → []; full → [[0, 1440]]; same → iv of each of S.week[o.dow − 1] (no dow — 1); custom → iv of each of o.intervals
  there is no such one — by S.kind:
    always → [[0, 1440]]; never → []
    week   → iv of each of S.week[dow(d) − 1]
    cycle  → k := (d − start in days) modulo length, non-negative; iv of each of days[k], no element — []; start is empty or length is 0 — []
    dates  → iv of each of the intervals of the first record with date = d; there is none — []
    astro  → Н1.4

day(S, d) → the union of the segments:
  for each [a, b] of own(S, d): [a, min(b, 1440)];
  for each [a, b] of own(S, d − 1) with b > 1440: [0, min(b − 1440, 1440)]     # the tail across midnight

in_effect(S, t, tz) → there is [a, b] in day(S, d), a < b, a ≤ m < b; (d, m) := wall(t, tz)
```

A field of the row is absent (the door requires only `id`, `title`, `kind`) — the empty form: `overrides`, `valid` are empty, `week`, `cycle`, `dates`, `astro` give `[]`. `24:MM` is 1440 + `MM`, a start after midnight is not shifted as a tail: `["24:10", "24:20"]` gives `[0, 20)` tomorrow. A special day is stronger than the kind: `never` with a special day `full` is in effect. `valid` judges the day of the start of the interval: the tail of the last day of the validity period
comes on the next day, the tail of the day before the validity period does not come. The schedule `S` is not found — `false`; it is unreadable — `unknown`
(Н1.1). A group unreadable for a schedule that names it: a day that the rules before it did not decide is not decided. `in_effect` reads two days, `d` and `d − 1`: an interval of a decided day covers the moment — `true`, an unknown term of the «or» will not change it; otherwise, if at least one day is not decided — `unknown`.

### Н1.4 `astro`

One formula for all implementations; `S.astro`: `window` (`day`, `night`), `offset_start_min`, `offset_end_min`, `lat`, `lon`,
`utc_offset_hours`. The computation is binary64, angles are in degrees, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan` take and give degrees, `fmod` is the remainder with
the sign of the dividend, `⌊⌋` is rounding down.

```
sun(d, lat, lon, off, rise) → minute of the day | day | night
  N  := the number of the day d in the year, 1 January — 1
  lh := lon / 15
  t  := N + ((rise ? 6 : 18) − lh) / 24
  M  := 0.9856 · t − 3.289
  L  := fmod(M + 1.916 · sin(M) + 0.02 · sin(2 · M) + 282.634 + 720, 360)
  RA := fmod(atan(0.91764 · tan(L)) + 720, 360)
  RA := (RA + ⌊L / 90⌋ · 90 − ⌊RA / 90⌋ · 90) / 15
  sd := 0.39782 · sin(L);  cd := cos(asin(sd))
  ch := (cos(90.833) − sd · sin(lat)) / (cd · cos(lat))
  ch > 1 → night;  ch < −1 → day
  H  := (rise ? 360 − acos(ch) : acos(ch)) / 15
  T  := H + RA − 0.06571 · t − 6.622
  x  := fmod(fmod(T − lh + off, 24) + 48, 24)
  → ⌊x · 60 + 0.5⌋

astro(S, d) → a list of [a, b]
  r, s := sun(d, …, true), sun(d, …, false); r′ := sun(d + 1, …, true)
  day:   r and s are minutes → [[r + offset_start_min, s + offset_end_min]]
         otherwise, the first word among r, s is day → [[0, 1440]], otherwise []
  night: s is a minute → [[s + offset_start_min, 1440 + (r′ is a minute ? r′ : 0) + offset_end_min]]
         s = night → [[0, 1440]], otherwise []
```

`utc_offset_hours` is a field of the row, not `TIMEZONE`: the minutes of `sun` stand on this offset, the formula does not know daylight saving time. A segment with
`a` ≥ `b` is empty. A segment with a negative `a` gives no tail into yesterday.

### Н1.5 The object mode

```
manual(t) → the row mode_current exists, its mode is in items, at ≤ t and (until = null or t < until)
object_mode(t, tz):
  manual(t)                    → the mode of the row
  the first in order m of items with a non-empty schedule, in_effect(schedule, t, tz) = true → m.id
  otherwise                    → otherwise
```

`in_effect` = `unknown` for a mode before the first `true` — `object_mode` is `unknown`. The mode changes with the passage of time, while the row does not
change. The evaluator tells the mode in the heartbeat, as a top-level field, on every pass:

```yaml
object_mode: {mode: <id>, manual: <bool>, until: <время> | null}    # object_mode(now); until — only with manual
```

`unknown` or there is no row `modes` — there is no field. All the evaluators of the cluster compute the same. `GET /modes/current` of the contract reads the heartbeat
of the first heard evaluator by slot, `POST` writes `mode_current` (V/11). There is no evaluator — there is nobody to tell the mode: a limit.

`mode_current` keeps no history: a lifted activation is not read for a moment before the lifting, the condition `mode` judges by the current row.

### Н1.6 The maintenance window and muting

```
window_hit(w, c, t, tz) → w.enabled and (w.rules = null or c ∈ w.rules) and in_effect(w.schedule, t, tz)
muted(c, t)             → mute of the row rule_marks of the scenario c exists and since ≤ t < until
```

The window and muting are checks of chapter 4 Н1.8; they do not stop a manual run. `rules` carries the numbers of scenarios
in decimal notation or as a number, the comparison is by number.

### Н1.7 The time zone and transitions

`tz` is `TIMEZONE`, a cluster setting: a line of `w2c.env`, the worker takes it from the runner's environment ([the environment file contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/env-file.md)),
the part of the subsystem (`W2C_SUB_ENV`) does not get it, there is no key in `/etc/auto/auto.env`. Not set — `UTC`. A name outside the evaluator's time zone database —
`in_effect` and `object_mode` are `unknown` on every pass, a log line at start: the console checks only the form of the name; `run_at` — chapter 2 Н1.6.

A change of `TIMEZONE` takes effect from a restart of the evaluator (P/02.4 Н1.7). Two evaluators with different lines of `w2c.env` will diverge: the value is one per
cluster, this is the concern of the installation.

**The daylight saving transition.** `in_effect` judges the wall minute, therefore:

- at a forward shift the minutes of the gap do not exist: an interval inside it does not take effect on that day, an interval across it is shortened;
- at a backward shift the minutes of the gap occur twice: an interval inside it takes effect both times.

The same wall minutes judge the moment of `run_at` (chapter 2 Н1.6): a skipped minute does not occur, a repeated one gives two candidates.

### Н1.8 The version history

A row of `versions` is what there was after an edit of the content of a scenario: `scenario` (the number), `number` (the counter of the scenario's versions from 1), `note` (`{code,
params}` or `null`), `rule` — the JSON text `{content, was}` (V/11.3 Н1.3; a `json` field is capped at 4000 bytes, and the content carries several such fields), the stamps `by`, `at`.
`number` is not `revision`. Muting and order do not write a version: they are not in the scenario row. `backend` writes it in the same batch as the edit (V/11). There is no
expiry: it is removed with the rule.

### Н1.9 The operator answer

A row of `answers` is an answer to `ask` and an acknowledgement of `escalate`: `scenario`, `fire` (the firing name), `step` (the step path: `2.no.1`), `user` (who answers), `answer` (`yes`, `no`,
`ack:<уровень>`), the stamps `by`, `at`. The evaluator reads it at the step (chapter 4 Н1.11).

**Who answers.** The write is `view` on the scenario (`rights.routes.view`, `unit_of`): the one who answers does not edit the rule. The role of the step is judged by the evaluator, and this judgement is the last: `by` has the role of the step (`role` for `ask`, for a level of `escalate`) by the copy of the roles table `domain/roles` of the cluster, expanded as `roles_in` of the gate contract: `{role, until}`, `until` is 0 or later than `at`; the stamp `by` is set by the console. `user` is not equal to `by`, or the role is a foreign one — the row does not take effect, a `step` row with `result: fail`, `answer.refused` (the form — chapter 4 Н1.6), one per answer row, the heartbeat field `answers_refused`; the deadline goes on. There is no copy, it is unreadable or the table says `unreadable` — the row does not take effect for now, the step waits for the next pass; the deadline is stronger: `until` without an effective row — `default`, `note` `answer.unread` (chapter 4 Н1.6).

**One answer.** A person answers once: `backend` creates the row with `cas ""`, therefore a foreign row takes no place. A `POST` of the table door over a row edits it, the answer and `at` are new, while the step is not decided: a decided step is written in the run row and no longer depends on it (chapter 4 Н1.11). The evaluator lists the rows of the step and takes the effective one with the smallest `at`; equal `at` — the smallest `user` by bytes. An answer after the deadline of the step does not take effect, the run has already gone by `default`. A new `revision` of the scenario removes the run, the row stays without a reader and lives for `delete_after`.

The answer door of the contract is `POST /rule-prompts/{promptId}/answer` (V/11.4 Н1.10). `backend` judges the role at the door in order to answer 403, and may not see the role without the grant rows; the evaluator remains the last judge: a row past the door does not take effect by the rule above. The question is closed by the end of the step, not by a row: a rejected row does not move the step and does not close the question for anyone. Without an answer `ask` ends with `default`.

## Н3 Pass

```
start of the evaluator's pass (chapter 2 Н3): read the tables of Н1.1
tz: the environment, once at start
now → object_mode(now, tz) → the heartbeat field
for a firing: t = occurred (chapter 4 Н1.8); the conditions time, mode, windows, muting are computed by t
```

**Races.** The tables are read separately from the event rows: an edit of a schedule between passes takes effect from the next pass, including for
events that happened before it: there is no history of the tables, a limit. Two firings of one moment in different passes of one evaluator may
read different rows; the firing name does not change from this, a repeat is harmless (chapter 4).

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|

| a table row is unreadable | the check `ok: false`, `cond.unread` | `fired`, `unchecked` | fix the row |
