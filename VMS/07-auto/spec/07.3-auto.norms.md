# V/07.3 — Fit and refusals: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/07.3-auto.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 3 of lesson V/07 «Сценарии» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/07-auto.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](07-auto.README.md), manifest [`auto.subsystem.yaml`](../auto.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The door has checked the form of a scenario (chapter 1); the meaning is checked by the evaluator: whether the camera raises such an event, whether it has such an
output and preset, whether there is such a detector, whether anybody records the camera that the scenario wants to scan. The chapter sets norms for
the refusal of two fields, the check against what the devices have said, «I do not know» versus «cannot» and the texts of refusals.

Norm ids are `V/07.3-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

### Н1.1 Refusal of two fields

A meaning that the form of one field does not express is a refusal of the evaluator (a link of two fields is a rule of the evaluator, as in V/05
finding 2). A disabled scenario is not judged: an unfinished row with empty `when`, `then` is stored (V/07.1). An enabled one gets the phase `refused`, `reason` — the words of the first met condition from the top; it does not fire, the request `run` is refused (chapter 4 Н1.12):

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| `start: event`, `when` is empty, except a row `hidden: record` with a condition `time` (chapter 2 Н1.6) | `an event scenario needs a trigger` |
| a condition carries a field not of its `kind` (chapter 1 Н1.2а): `value` at `recording`, `online`; otherwise the rule would act the other way round | `condition <i>: <поле> does not belong to <kind>` |
| `start: schedule`, there is no `run_at` or `days` is empty | `a scheduled scenario needs run_at` |
| there is not a single action in `then`, at any depth | `a scenario needs an action` |
| `start: event`, two triggers or more with `within` = 0 | `two triggers need within: the window they must fall into` |
| `start: event`, one trigger with `within` > 0 | `within is the window between triggers; with one trigger there is nothing to window` |
| the action `det scan` with `before` = `after` = 0 (an empty window) | `a scan window needs before or after` |
| a target `from_event` with `start` other than `event` | `from_event needs an event: this scenario starts without one` |
| a step without a subject: `sub` `ntf`, `net`, `client`, `alarm` | `<action> has nothing to act on here` |
| the action `rec record` with `duration: event`: the log has no end of the event | `duration event needs the end of an event: the journal has none` |
| `escalate` with `stop: reset`, there is no `io.input` among the triggers | `a reset needs io.input among the triggers` |
| `cam output`, `state` absent or `pulse`, `pulse_ms` absent or 0: the holder would refuse (V/01.4 Н1.1) | `pulse needs pulse_ms` |
| a trigger with `target: null` or `from_event: true` | `trigger <i> is not complete` |
| a step not by the schema (a branch, whose form the door does not judge), a step without a required key, `target` `null`, `auto runrule` without `rule`; `ask`, `escalate` with `role: null`; `sec` outside 1…3600, `levels` empty or more than 5, `wait_min` outside 1…120, at the last level outside 0…120; the event of `wait_event` with `target: null` or `from_event: true`; a condition without its field (V/07.1) | `step <path> is not complete`, `condition <i> is not complete` |

The bounds of `sec`, of the levels and of `wait_min` keep the step's deadline inside `delete_after` of the row `answers`. Completeness of a step with `target` — the keys of V/07.1 Н1.3 without `unit`, `cam`; at `output`, `port` is not required either: the number is the child's (chapter 4 Н1.9).

An edit of the row is a check anew (the row as a whole, as it will become).

### Н1.2 Fit to cameras and detectors

On each pass the evaluator reconciles the scenario with what the devices have said (`can` in the holder's heartbeat — `{events, rays, relays,
ptz, presets}`, `rays` and `relays` are lists of the numbers of this camera's children in ascending order; there is no `can` — `rays`, `relays`, `presets` are not said, `unchecked`; the device row `cam/devices/<хост>` gives only `events`, a row without `events` (it happens: `{device, videos}`) does not say them; V/01.4 Н1.4) and with which units exist.
The holder's heartbeat is the one fresh by `LOST_AFTER` whose `status[]` carries the camera; of two — the one with the greater `epoch` of the element; there is no fresh one — there is no
`can`. **The camera exists** — a live row `cam/cameras/<cam>` with an empty `main`; a stream row, a tombstone, `ref:…` — `there is no camera <cam>`
(a recording of a foreign camera is created by the operator, V/02.9 Н1.1):

| What is in the scenario | Reconciliation | Refusal words |
|---|---|---|
| a trigger `cam`, `kind` | the camera exists; its device raises `kind` (`events`) | `camera <cam> does not raise '<kind>' — it raises <…>`, `there is no camera <cam>` |
| a trigger `cam` without `unit` (wide) | all cameras of the cluster have said, and none of them raises `kind` — unfit; there are ones that have not said — `unchecked`; `match.port` of a wide trigger is not reconciled (the input is shown by the event itself) | `no camera raises '<kind>'` |
| a trigger `cam` with `unit`, `kind` = `io.input`, `match.port` | `p` is in `rays`: the numbers run through the whole device and may start not from 1 (V/01.4 Н1.1); `p` is not an integer or `rays` is empty — the same word | `camera <cam> has no input <p>: it has <…>` |
| a trigger `auto` (`sub: auto`) | unfit: events of `auto` are not triggers (chapter 2 Н1.2) | `events of auto are not triggers` |
| a trigger `det` | a detector with such a `unit` exists; for a model of the registry the event kind is the `kind` of the detector row (V/04.2 Н1.2), a different `kind` of the trigger is unfit, the rule would be silent; any other model has no catalogue of kinds, the kind goes to `unchecked` (`detector <unit>: the kinds its model raises are not said`); a wide `det` and a `det` with a target — without reconciliation: the kind is raised by a built-in detector too (chapter 2 Н1.1) | `there is no detector <unit> — a detector is named by its unit: <cam>-<kind>`, `detector <unit> raises '<вид>', not '<kind>'` |
| the actions `cam output`, `cam preset` | the camera exists | `there is no camera <cam>` |
| the action `cam unit` | the row `cam/device_units/<unit>-<n>` exists, its `type` is not `det`; `operation` is reconciled by the holder (V/01.4 Н1.4б); the table is not read — `unchecked` | `there is no unit <unit>-<n>` |
| the action `det detect`, `kind: linecross` | `params` of the step, when absent — of the base (V/07.4 Н1.2), is an object with the key `zone`, `{shape: line}`: the claim replaces the base entirely, and without a line the order is `failed` (V/04.2); the base row is not readable — `unchecked` | `detect linecross on <cam>: no line in params.zone` |
| the conditions `time`, `mode` | the schedule, the mode exists: otherwise the condition is silently false; the table is not read — `unchecked` | `condition <i>: there is no schedule <id>`, `there is no mode <id>` |
| the action `cam output` | `port` is in `relays` | `camera <cam> has no output <p>: it has <…>` |
| the action `cam output`, the output exists | the output is not disabled by the rule of V/01.4 Н1.4а: the setting of the row `<cam>-relay-<port>` and of the rows with the same `device`, `type`, `number`, including `gone`; the row is not readable — `unchecked`, the holder will give the refusal | `camera <cam> output <p> is disabled` |
| the action `cam preset` | `1 ≤ n ≤ presets` | `camera <cam> has <n> preset(s), not <p>` |
| the actions `rec record`, `det detect`, `det scan` | the camera exists; its `type` is `camera` (V/01.1 Н1.1б) | `there is no camera <cam>`, `camera <cam> has no stream` |
| the actions `cam arm`, `cam disarm`, `cam disable` | the camera exists | `there is no camera <cam>` |
| the action `det scan` | the camera has a recording (`rec/recordings/*` with this `cam`; with `rec` — this recording of this camera); a recording is a row without a tombstone, a disabled and an ordered one too; an unreadable one is not chosen: there is no recording, but there is an unreadable row — `unchecked`, the step will give the refusal (chapter 4 Н1.3) | `nothing records camera <cam>: a scan reads the archive`; with `rec` — `there is no recording <rec> of camera <cam>` |

The listing `<…>` is in ascending order, separated by `, `; an empty one is `none`.

- **Unfit** — the phase `refused`, the field `unfit` is a list of words, `reason` — these words separated by `; `; the order is `when`, then `then`,
  a repeated word — once; the scenario does not fire until it fits (the check is on each
  pass: the camera has been moved to a device with outputs — the scenario comes alive).
- **Unknown is not «cannot»:** event kinds are not said (there is no `can` and the device row does not say them), inputs, outputs, presets are not said (there is no `can`), an event of a subsystem that
  the evaluator does not check (other than `cam` and `det`; `auto` is unfit, the table above) — the scenario works, the field
  `unchecked` lists what is unchecked: `camera <cam> has not said what it raises: '<kind>' is not checked`, `camera <cam> has
  not said what it can do: cam <action> is not checked`, `camera <cam> has not said its inputs: port <p> is not checked`, `<sub> does not describe what its units raise: '<kind>' is not checked`.
  A subsystem outside the declared epochs (chapter 2 Н1.2): the scenario works, does not fire, in `unchecked` — `<sub>: its epochs are not
  declared, its events fire nothing`. `can` in the holder's heartbeat exists but is not readable (not by the form of V/01.4 Н1.4) — it is not
  read, rather than absent, and not «cannot»: the same — the scenario works, `unchecked` (not `unfit`).
- The holder checks the same itself at execution (V/01.4 Н4): the evaluator does not promise that the command will be executed.
- **The target `target`** of one device is reconciled as `unit`, `cam` of the same row: the key of a child — by its camera and number (`output` — the number in `relays`, `io.input` — in `rays`), the camera at `output` — with the `port` of the step. At a trigger `det` the element `device` is a camera: the camera exists and it has a detector `<cam>-<kind>` or a built-in one of this kind (V/01.1 Н1.2б), otherwise the same word `there is no detector <cam>-<kind>…`: the rule would be silent. At a trigger and at the event of `wait_event`, a folder that is not in the folder table, a removed one too: when no live areas are left in the target — unfit, `there is no folder <id>`, the rule would be silent; otherwise `unchecked` with the same word, a rule with a mixed target does not go dark (the Architect's decision of 2026-11-02). The table is not readable — `unchecked`. A folder of a step of `then`, a server, a domain, several devices and `from_event` are not reconciled: the composition is known at the run, a device that does not fit is a refusal of the step, not of the scenario (chapter 4 Н1.9); `gone` and an area that does not exist — the same. A step in a branch is reconciled as a top-level step, the event of `wait_event` — as a trigger. `auto runrule` is not reconciled here: the target of the chain may go away after the write, the other steps go on, the step is judged by chapter 4 Н1.13.

### Н1.3 The unreadable is the trouble of one

The scenario row is not readable by its type, or the value of `when` or `then` is not by the schema: one never read gets the platform phase
`unreadable`; a running one goes on with the last one read, `why` (P/03.3 Н3 step 2, P/03.4 Н1.1); the other scenarios go on. A row
of a camera, a device, a detector or a recording is not readable — this unit is unchecked: in `unchecked` — `<единица>: its row cannot be
read, it is not checked`, `<единица>` is `camera <id>`, `detector <id>`, `recording <id>`, for an output setting — `camera <cam> output <p>`,
`schedule <id>`, `modes`, `window <id>`, `folder <id>`; the scenario works, a check on such a row is `cond.unread` (chapter 4 Н1.8). A time zone outside the zone database at a scenario `schedule` — `timezone <имя> is not known: run_at is not checked`. The store did not answer — everything read from it is unchecked: `unchecked`, not `unfit`.

## Н3 Pass

For each scenario, on each pass:

1. If the refusal of two fields applies, the phase is `refused`; stop.
2. Reconcile by Н1.2. If something is unfit, set `unfit`, the phase is `refused`; stop.
3. List what is unknown in `unchecked`.
4. Otherwise the scenario is fit: fit → firings (chapter 2) → actions (chapter 4).

**Races.** The camera has been moved between passes — the scenario is checked by what was read in this pass; the holder refuses
at execution if the device is already a different one (V/01.4).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| two triggers without a window, a window at one trigger, no trigger, no `run_at`, no action | `refused`, `reason` | heartbeat, page | correct the scenario |
| the camera cannot (an event, an input, an output, a preset), a row without frames, the output is disabled, there is no detector, nothing records | `refused`, `unfit` | heartbeat, page | correct the scenario or the camera |
| the device has not said | works, `unchecked` | heartbeat | — |
| `can` in the holder's heartbeat is not readable | works, `unchecked` (not `unfit`) | heartbeat | — |
| a row of a unit, of a table of chapter 6, of a folder is unreadable | works, `unchecked` | heartbeat | — |
