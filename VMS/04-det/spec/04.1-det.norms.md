# V/04.1 — Detector — the unit: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/04.1-det.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 1 of lesson V/04 «Живая детекция» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/04-det.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](04-det.README.md), the manifest [`det.subsystem.yaml`](../det.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the detector stream `stream` — `41d3ec8`; the mask with its own size, `stream` — `b41527f`).

A detector is one model on one camera: «line crossing on camera 7». The chapter sets norms for the detector row, who
creates it, the deadline, the placement and the snapshot. The source and the model — chapter 2; events — chapter 3; the heartbeat — chapter 4.

Norm ids are `V/04.1-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `until` is in Unix seconds.

**Data** (the common families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the row `det/units/<name>` (P/06.1),
the mask body `det/blobs/sha256-<hex>` (P/06.1 Н1.5), the snapshot `det/snapshot/*` (P/04 Н1.3а); the chapter has no families of its own beyond them.

### Н1.1 Fields

`unit: {rows: units, id: name}`; `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` — the events, grants and labels of a detector are those of the camera.

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, required | the name of the pair; the convention is `<cam>-<kind>` (`7-linecross`), not a rule |
| `cam` | `string`, required, `fixed` | the camera whose stream goes into the model: the `<id>` of a camera `cam` of its own cluster (V/01.1) or `ref:<ref>` — a camera of another cluster by its name in the domain, only its main stream (V/04.2 Н1.1); another camera is another detector; `about` |
| `stream` | `string`, `ref: cam/cameras`, `must_match: {main: cam}` | which stream of the camera goes into the model: the id of another stream of this camera; empty — the main one (the id of the camera itself — 409, V/01.1 Н1.1а); the user's choice; editable: a new revision of the row is a restart of the model (P/03.3 Н3 step 2) with the new source, the mask stays (the scaling — below); the source is the worker's, chapter 2 |
| `kind` | `string`, required | the model kind: `motion`, `linecross`, `lpr`, `tick` (the teaching one, chapter 2) — the worker's kind registry |
| `title` | `string` | a name for people, free text; does not change the key; empty or not read — `name` is shown; read by `backend` (V/11.3 Н1.3) and the page, the worker does not read it |
| `params` | `string` | the model settings, opaque to the platform; the limit is the common row limit (413 `toolarge`, [P/06.1 Н1.4](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)) |
| `mask` | `blob` | a per-pixel mask with its own frame size (below «Mask and frame size»): the row holds `sha256-<hex>`, the body is the object `det/blobs/<digest>` (`PUT /det/units/<id>/mask`, [P/06.1 Н1.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)); a change of the mask is a change of the row is a restart of the model (chapter 2) |
| `mask_of` | `string`, `ref: det/units`, `must_match: {cam: cam}` | whose mask: the id of a detector of the same camera (the `auto` order, V/07.4 Н1.2); the body is the mask of that row at the moment the model starts (below «Which mask») |
| `enabled` | `bool`, default `true` | |
| `labels` | `list`, default `["gpu"]` | where it may run: the labels of the worker's server; a model without a GPU removes the label explicitly |
| `alarms` | `list`, default `["linecross", "lpr"]` | which event kinds of the detector are alarms (chapter 3) |
| `until` | `float`, default `0` | when the detector ceases to be; `0` — never (Н1.3) |

**Mask and frame size.** The detector stream is chosen and changed (`stream`, V/01.1 Н1.1а), so the mask stores its own size.
The body is one PGM image ([Netpbm](https://netpbm.sourceforge.net/doc/pgm.html)) in a narrow profile:
- `P5`, the width, the height, `255` — each after **one** whitespace character (space, TAB, CR or LF), decimal numbers without a
  sign and without leading zeros; there are no `#` comments; after `255` — exactly one whitespace character and exactly `w·h` bytes of raster, by
  rows from top to bottom; there is no tail (a second image is also a tail);
- `maxval` is only `255`; the width and the height are 1…1024; the whole body is no more than 1 MiB (1,048,576 bytes, less than the body limit
  of the door P/06.1): a mask 1024×1024 with its header does not fit into it, 1024×1023 does;
- anything else is not PGM: the mask is not read (chapter 2: `failed`); what the value of a point means is the model's word, like `params`.

A frame of the same size — the mask as is. A frame of another size — the mask is mapped onto the frame by integer nearest neighbour on
each axis: the frame point `(x, y)` with the frame `W×H` and the mask `w×h` takes the mask point `(x·w div W, y·h div H)` — integers,
without floating point and without a shift of centres; without gamma or other transformations of values. The scaling assumes one
field of view for the streams of a camera; a stream with a different framing requires a new mask. The rule is one for the detector and the scan job (V/05.3 Н1.3). Vectors — [`vectors/mask`](../vectors/mask/README.md) (the grammar and the scaling).

**Which mask.** The row's own non-empty `mask` comes first (uploading the body is the operator's edit over the base). It is empty and
`mask_of` is set — the mask of the row `det/units/<mask_of>` at the moment the model starts: its `mask` is empty — without a mask; the row does not exist or it is
not read — `failed` «`mask_of <id>: no such detector`» (chapter 2 Н1.2). Neither is present — without a mask. A reference does not hold the body: the body
is held by the `blob` field of the row referred to (P/06.1 Н3 «уборка тел»), — therefore the order refers to the detector row, not
to the body. `must_match` — a detector of the same camera (the row check contract): when the row is written, the detector `mask_of`, if it exists, MUST be of the same
camera, otherwise 409; no detector is not a refusal (`ref` does not require a target, the vector `ref_no_target`), at the start — `failed`, as with a
detector deleted later (the door does not check the referring rows when the target is deleted). The same order is used by the scan job (V/05.3 Н1.3) and by the `auto` order (V/07.4 Н1.2).

**A camera of another cluster.** `cam: ref:<ref>` — there is no target in its own cluster: `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` finds no rights by the camera,
admittance is by a grant on `*` (the gate and pass contract, «unresolved»); `stream` of such a row is empty (the main one):
`must_match: {main: cam}` without a target gives no refusal, another stream of a camera of another cluster the worker does not take (chapter 2 Н1.1).

The fields `cam`, `kind`, `enabled`, `params`, `labels`, `mask` are read by orderers too: the recorder (scan over `det/units/*`, V/02.8
Н1.5), the task by interval (V/05); their names do not change. The row check — the [row check contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md).

### Н1.2 Who creates

- **The operator** — through the console units door: `POST /det/units` (P/06.1). There is no detector form on the VMS page: detectors are visible
  in «Сценарии» (V/11), rows — through the door.
- **A scenario** — by an order: `auto` declares `worker.creates: [{sub: det, rows: units, key: "{cam}-{kind}"}]`
  ([P/03.6 Н1.6](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)): the row `det/units/auto-<cam>-<kind>` with `made`, the deadline
  is `until`; the base of the order (the operator's detector of the same pair, `params` over it) — lesson V/07. `det` has no `requests:` section:
  requests are not submitted to detectors.

### Н1.3 Deadline

`placement.ends: {field: until, never: 0}` ([P/04 Н1.3](../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md)): `until`
has passed — the controller removes the row from placement, the worker stops the model. The order row (`made`) the controller deletes;
the operator's row it does not: the row is removed from placement and not placed (the count `expired_kept` of the pass report), the operator deletes it (for a table with a
`fixed` field and `about` not by id, the deadline deletes only rows with `made`).

### Н1.4 Placement

| Key | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | `{from: capacity, default: 8}` | streams per worker (decoding and running on the GPU) |
| `headroom` | `{from: headroom}` | the headroom is the platform's: capacity − Σ of the assignment weights ([P/03.4 Н1.1](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)) |
| `constraint` | `labels-subset` | the detector labels ⊆ the server labels |
| `requires` | `resource` | events are written into the resource of its own server (chapter 3) |
| `near` | `{sub: cam, by: cam}` | a preference, not a filter: next to the camera holder — the model reads the share `cam.shm` of its own server (chapter 2); `by: cam` — a neighbour by the field `cam`, not by the detector name, which the holder does not know |
| `tie_break` | `most-free-capacity` | |
| `rebalance` | `{dead_band: 0.10}` | |

There are no `unit_mb` and `base_mb` ([P/03.4 Н1.2](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)): the memory of models on the GPU is
the implementation's, the detector has no memory check of ADR-0070; no measurement is made — models have no common magnitude (as with `assist`).

There is no `home`: there is no one to answer the question «on which server does the model live». When the preference is not met, the filters and
`most-free-capacity` spread the detectors over the servers with a GPU, the source is the receiver of the cluster (chapter 2). There is no `grows`: there are no
spares, the operator installs workers (ADR-0084).

### Н1.5 Snapshot

`snapshot: [name, cam, kind, enabled, until]` (P/04 Н1.3а).

## Н3 Pass

The chapter has no pass of its own: placement and the deadline are the controller's pass (P/04 Н3), the order is the pass of `auto` (P/03.6 Н1.6).

**Races.** The operator and a scenario created a detector of one pair: the names differ (`7-linecross` and `auto-7-linecross`) — both run;
a scenario does not create a second detector if there is an operator's detector (the rule of V/07).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| no `cam` or `kind` | 400 | the answer | — |
| `cam` changes | 400 `fixed`, `field: /cam` | the answer | a new detector |
| `stream` is a stream of another camera | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match` (P/06.1 Н4) | the answer | a stream of this camera |
| `mask_of` is a detector of another camera | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match` (the row check contract) | the answer | a detector of this camera |
| `mask_of` — no such detector | not a refusal: the row is accepted, at the start `failed` «`mask_of <id>: no such detector`» | the heartbeat | a detector of this camera |
| `stream` is the id of the camera itself | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match`: the camera's `main` is empty (V/01.1 Н1.1а) | the answer | the main stream — an empty `stream` |
| the name `auto-…` by hand | 400 (the names of the orderer `auto`) | the answer | another name |
| a mask beyond the door's body limit | 413 (P/06.1 Н1.4) | the answer | a polygon — in `params` |
| no server with the detector labels | `unplaceable` | the pass report | the labels of the server or of the detector |

## Correspondence to former lessons

From М10B/14 «Детекторы» — the introduction, step 1 (the unit as a pair, the fields, `about`, the mask, `until`, placement), step 6 (the headroom).
Differences from the witness: `until` → `placement.ends`, the manual row is removed from placement rather than deleted (the witness deletes it through
the console, ADR-0091 items 1, 2); the `requests:` section (`detect`, `scan`) and the executor of detector jobs are removed — a detector on an
event is created by `auto` as a row, a scan is the job `detjob` (the inventory, «опись» § 4); `near` is to the camera holder, not to the recorder (the argument
«delivery is read over RTSP from anywhere» is removed, ADR-0075 window 2); the headroom is by the assignment (the platform), not by the running
models. Ids ([id-map](../../common/id-map.tsv)): М10B/14 step 1 → V/04.1-01…03; М10B/14 step 1, 6 → V/04.1-04.
