# V/04.2 — Source and model: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/04.2-det.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 2 of lesson V/04 «Живая детекция» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/04-det.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](04-det.README.md), the manifest [`det.subsystem.yaml`](../det.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the detector stream — `41d3ec8`; the mask on a change of stream, `cam` is a stream — `b41527f`).

A detector runs when the worker has a model of its kind and there is a stream of its camera. The chapter sets norms for where the worker takes
the stream from, what a model is for the course (the kind registry and the teaching model `tick`), the detector phases and the restart on a change of the row.
The holder's delivery — [V/01.2](../../01-cam/spec/01.2-cam.norms.md) Н1.3; the receiver — [V/02.9](../../02-rec/spec/02.9-rec.norms.md)
Н1.3; the model's events — chapter 3.

Norm ids are `V/04.2-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Timeouts are by the monotonic clock of the process.

**Data** (the common families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — the rows `det/units/*`
of its assignment and the rows of the detectors they refer to by `mask_of` (V/04.1 Н1.1), the mask bodies `det/blobs/*`, the camera rows `cam/cameras/*` (the role base — the `about` rows), the heartbeats of holders
`cam/heartbeats/*` and of recorders `rec/heartbeats/*` (`worker.reads`: the role base does not give other subsystems' heartbeats,
[P/02.5 Н1.4](../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.5-installer.norms.md)); the share `cam.shm` (`worker.uses`); writes — the epoch
`det/epoch/<name>` (P/03.2 Н1.5), the heartbeat.

### Н1.1 Source

On each pass the worker MUST look for the source of each detector of its assignment — by the heartbeats, once per pass for all detectors:

| Camera | Source (`via`) |
|---|---|
| the holder is on **its own** server: the element `status[]` of the detector stream (`stream`, empty — `cam`; V/01.1 Н1.1а) in a heartbeat `cam/heartbeats/*` with `phase: running`, the heartbeat field `server` = its own `SERVER_NAME`, the heartbeat heard within `LOST_AFTER` = 45 s | the share `cam.shm` — `live_shm` (`shm`; the address is exactly the heartbeat string `shm:///…` of the holder's own epoch, not built, V/01.2 Н1.3); no `live_shm` or the share did not open — the loopback RTSP `live_url` (`rtsp`), V/01.2 Н1.3; no `live_url` either — as «the holder is not `running`» (the row below) |
| the holder is on **another** server of the cluster; a camera of another cluster (`cam: ref:<ref>`) | the receiver of its own cluster: the call «подписка» (subscription) with the leaf of the role `detworker` — `cam` and `stream` of the detector, for a camera of another cluster — `ref:<ref>` and only the main one (V/02.9 Н1.3 «Камера и поток», Н2; the address is the field `ingest` of the heartbeat of a recorder heard within `PEER_LOST_AFTER` = 30 s: of its own server, otherwise the first by slot name; the receiver did not answer or refused — the next one, none — `failed`, backoff, Н3) — `ingest` |
| the row is not `camera`: there are no frames and no delivery addresses (V/01.1 Н1.1б) | none — `waiting`, `reason` `camera <cam> has no stream` |
| there is no camera row, there is no holder or it is not `running`, there is no receiver, `cam` is a stream, the stream is not of this camera | none — `waiting`, `reason` — below «No source» |

If the fresh heartbeats contain several elements of the stream with `phase: running`, the holder is the one whose element `epoch` is higher, and with equal ones — the first
by slot name; the table row is chosen by it.

**A camera of another cluster** (`cam: ref:<ref>`) — a branch before the local checks, as in V/03.2 Н1.1: the worker does not read the rows `cam/cameras/*` and the heartbeats
`cam/heartbeats/*` for it; a non-empty `stream` — `waiting`, `reason` `stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>`;
of the conditions of «No source» only the receiver applies.

**No source** — `waiting`, `reason` is the word of the first met condition from the top (the conditions combine: there is no camera row
and no receiver — `no camera <cam>`):

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| the camera or stream row is not read, the store did not answer («Reconciliation» below) | `cam row unreadable: <id>` |
| there is no camera row `cam/cameras/<cam>` (a camera of its own cluster) | `no camera <cam>` |
| the row `cam` is a stream (its `main` is not empty) | `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>` |
| the stream is not of this camera (there is no row `stream` or its `main` ≠ `cam`) | `stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>` |
| the `type` of the camera row is not `camera` | `camera <cam> has no stream` |
| there is no readable element of the stream: the element is out of form or the heartbeat is not read (below) | `cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>`; the whole heartbeat — `cam heartbeat unreadable: heartbeat` |
| there is no element of the stream (id `stream`, empty — `cam`) in any heartbeat heard within `LOST_AFTER` | `camera <cam> is held by nobody` |
| there is a holder, the element of the stream is not `running` | `camera <cam> is not running` |
| the source is the receiver of its own cluster, and there is no receiver in the cluster | `no ingest in this cluster` |

A camera `live: on-demand` is for a detector on the stream as `always`: the detector is a consumer, its row wakes the stream (V/01.2 Н1.2в; ADR-0103 addendum (7) item 3, (8) item 2);
before the first frames — `waiting` `camera <cam> is not running`.

`live_shm` or `live_url` of a heartbeat element is present but not by the form of the family `cam-heartbeat-words` (V/01) — **unread, not
absent**: the worker MUST NOT switch to another kind, MUST keep an open source, and for a source not opened — `waiting`, `reason`
«`cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>`», the next heartbeat — again. These fields are checked at the holder of its own server; both are out of
form — `<поле>` = `live_shm`, the first by the choice of kind. Another violation of the stream element — the same word, `<поле>` is the field of the refusal;
the holder's heartbeat is not read as a whole — `cam heartbeat unreadable: heartbeat`. The recorder heartbeat's `ingest` out of the form of the family
`rec-heartbeat-words` (V/02) is unread, not «there is no receiver»: on the pass this recorder is not a candidate, the next one is;
there are no readable ones — the source kind does not change, for a source not opened — `waiting`, `reason` `rec heartbeat unreadable: ingest`. The subsystem
`rec` is not in the manifest catalogue — 403 of the resource door on `rec/heartbeats/` (P/05 Н1.1) means «there is no receiver», the pass goes on, the
source is by the table above. The RTSP of another server is not a source (loopback, ADR-0075 window 2).
A subscription on the receiver is a live edge, the wish lives while the detector is subscribed, and another `LINGER` = 10 s (V/02.9 Н1.3). The source is opened when the model starts, one per detector.

**Reconciliation** on each pass: the source has changed (the holder moved, another address) or the holder's epoch has, or the source is dead
(8 s without a frame since opening or since the last frame) — the source is opened anew by the table above; the model is not restarted.
The heartbeats are not read (the resource is silent, `X-Missing`) or the store is silent — the last read source is kept; if nothing was read
— `waiting`, `reason` `cam heartbeat unreadable: heartbeat`. The camera or stream row is not read, the store is silent — this is not
«none»: an open source is kept, for one not opened — `waiting`, `reason` `cam row unreadable: <id>`.

### Н1.2 Model

- **The kind registry** is a property of the worker: kind (the `kind` of the row) → model. The model receives the frames of the source and the settings of the row
  (`params`, the mask body) and gives out events `(kind события, поля)` (event kind, fields; chapter 3); it does not give out frames, tensors or devices. The event kind of the model `motion`, `linecross`, `lpr` is the same `kind`: by it `alarms`, suppression and the scenario trigger judge.
  Which GPU models the worker has is decided by the implementation.
- **The teaching model `tick`** MUST be present at every worker of the course: it runs on any stream without a GPU. The pass on which
  the model is started is the zeroth; `pass` is the number of passes of the worker loop after the start; on a `pass` that is a multiple of 3 (3, 6, …), the model
  gives out one event of the kind `tick` with the field `pass`. The pass frequency is the runtime's ([P/03.3](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.3-worker.norms.md)
  Н1.2: `POLL`, long polling — the implementation): `tick` checks the chain «source → model → recording», not time. It does not
  need frames, it does need a source: without a source `tick` is silent, like any model. This is the acceptance of a detector without a GPU (chapter 4).
- **The line `linecross`** — the key `zone` of the object `params`: `{shape: line, points}`, two points in fractions of the frame. The key is absent or out of form — `failed` «`linecross: no line in params.zone`»: a silent detector is not allowed.
- The mask body — by V/04.1 Н1.1 «Какая маска»: its own non-empty `mask`, otherwise the mask of the row `det/units/<mask_of>` at the moment of
  the start; the worker reads it as the object `det/blobs/<digest>` when the model starts. There is no row `mask_of` or it is not read —
  `failed` «`mask_of <id>: no such detector`»; the body is not read or is not by the PGM profile — `failed`. No answer from the store or the resource is not «none»: `failed`, backoff,
  the word `no such detector` is not written. An edit of the mask of the detector
  referred to does not restart a running model of the order: the revision of the order row did not grow (Н1.4); the new mask applies from the
  next start. A source frame of a size other than the mask's — the mask is mapped onto the frame by
  nearest neighbour (V/04.1 Н1.1 «Маска и размер кадра»): a size mismatch is not a refusal.
- A change of `stream` is an edit of the row: a new revision, the model is stopped and started again under the same epoch
  ([P/03.3](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.3-worker.norms.md) Н3 step 2) with the source of the new stream; the count of the model's passes
  is from the new start (`tick`: the first event — on the third pass after it).
- A change of the frame size within the same stream (the holder reopened the device, the device profile changed) is not an edit of the
  row: the model is not stopped, the mask is mapped onto the new size from the next frame, the phase does not change.

### Н1.3 Phases

The worker MUST take the detector epoch when taking the unit ([P/03.3](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.3-worker.norms.md) the start of a
unit; P/03.2 Н1.5) — before the model and the source: one writer of the events `det/<name>/…`. The epoch is not taken — the unit is not taken,
there is no element in the heartbeat (the form of P/03.4 Н1.1), a retry on the next pass. There is no row or it is a tombstone — skip silently (the assignment
race, P/03.3).

| `phase` | When | What the worker does |
|---|---|---|
| `running` | the model is started, the source is open | runs the model, writes events (chapter 3) |
| `pending` | `enabled: false` | the model is stopped, the source is closed; the detector is visible |
| `waiting` | there is no source (Н1.1) | the model is stopped; `reason` — the words of Н1.1 |
| `unsupported` | the kind is not in the worker's registry | there is no model and no source; `reason` `no model <kind> on this worker`; a model delivered to the worker brings the detector to life on the next pass |
| `failed` | the start of the model or its run failed (an exception, the mask body is not read, the source did not open) | the model is stopped; `reason`; a retry by the runtime's backoff (P/03.3, `stalled` of the platform); the other detectors go on — unit isolation |

An unknown kind is a phase, not an error and not a refusal of creation: the console does not know the models of workers. The subsystem's words are in `reason`,
`why` is a field of the platform.

### Н1.4 Restart by the row

Any growth of the row's `revision` (P/03.3 Н3 step 2: stop and start of the unit) — for example `params`, `mask`, `mask_of`, `kind`,
`labels`, `stream`, `alarms`, `until` — the model is stopped and started anew; the list is an example, not an exception. The count of
`tick` is from the new start (Н1.2). `enabled: false` — `pending` (Н1.3).

## Н3 Pass

```
heartbeats cam/heartbeats/*, rec/heartbeats/* — once each
for each detector of the assignment (the epoch is taken when the unit is taken):
  enabled: false → pending; no kind → unsupported; no source → waiting (reason)
  model not started → mask body, source (shm | rtsp | ingest), model → running; did not work → failed
  source reconciliation: changed or dead → open anew; did not work → failed, backoff
  running → run of the model, events — chapter 3
gone from the assignment → the model and the source are stopped (runtime P/03.3)
```

**Races.** The holder moved between passes — until the reconciliation the model gets the frames of the former source or there are none (8 s — dead).
Two models of one detector on two workers (substitution) — the second one has a higher epoch; the first sees the loss of the lease (P/03.2) and
stops (`unconfirmed_max` — the default `off`).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `live_shm` or `live_url` of a `cam` heartbeat is out of form (V/01 `cam-heartbeat-words`) | unread, not absent: the kind does not change, an open source is kept, one not opened waits | the detector heartbeat, `reason` | the next heartbeat of the holder |
| the heartbeat or the camera row is not read | an open source is kept, one not opened — `waiting`, `reason` | the heartbeat | — |
| no camera, the holder is not `running`, the row is not `camera` | `waiting`, `reason` | the heartbeat | — |
| no receiver in the cluster | `waiting` `no ingest in this cluster` | the heartbeat | install a recorder |
| the recorder's `ingest` is out of form | not a candidate on the pass; there are no readable ones — the source kind does not change, one not opened — `waiting` `rec heartbeat unreadable: ingest` | the heartbeat | fix the recorder |
| the share did not open | loopback RTSP | — | — |
| the receiver did not answer or refused | the next receiver; none — `failed`, `reason`, backoff (at the gateway — `waiting`, V/03.2 Н4) | the heartbeat | — |
| the kind is not at the worker | `unsupported` | the heartbeat | deliver the model or the labels |
| the model crashed, the mask is not read | `failed`, `reason`, backoff | the heartbeat, the worker log | — |
| there is no row `mask_of` or it is not read | `failed` «`mask_of <id>: no such detector`», backoff | the heartbeat | create the detector or remove `mask_of` |
| `linecross` has no `params.zone` or it is out of form | `failed` «`linecross: no line in params.zone`», backoff | the heartbeat | set a line |
| the store or the resource did not answer on the row `mask_of` or the body | `failed`, `reason`, backoff; the word `no such detector` is not written | the heartbeat | — |
| the epoch is not taken | no element, retry | the worker log | — |

## Implementation decides

The implementation MUST name in its description: the **model registry** of the worker (which kinds besides `tick`), the stream **decoder** and how
frames are fed to the models, the **vector pack command** `VMS/04-det/mask` (the `vectors` entry of the profile; [`vectors/mask`](../vectors/mask/README.md)).
The text of `reason` of the phase `failed` is the implementation's, non-empty, except `mask_of <id>: no such detector`.
