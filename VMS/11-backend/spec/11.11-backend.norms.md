# V/11.11 — The plan and its check: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`11.11-backend.norms.md`](../11.11-backend.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 11 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора», the assistant (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the subsystem — ADR-0093 items 4, 5, 8, addendum (1)
items 5–8, addendum (2) items 3, 5, addendum (3) items 1, 2, 5, addendum (4) items 3–5). [Lecture](../../11-backend.md), [instructions](../11-backend.README.md).
The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (P/06.1 Н2, Н3 — the unit row; P/02.1 Н1.3а — the secret).

The dangerous error of the model is not an invention but a plausible substitution: the wrong field, the wrong value, the wrong shelf, an extra unit.
It is caught by the plan check — a pure function of `backend` over the plan, the texts of the turn and a fresh read of the console. It does not depend on
the model and is not configurable: neither the manifest nor the model weakens it (ADR-0093 item 5). Not passed — the model gets the reasons and
fixes the plan; passed — the admin sees the plan in the words of `backend` and decides personally whether to apply it. The rules are the pilot's hand-over package
(tools v4.0, ADR-0093 item 8): the floor of ADR-0093 item 5 and the harness additions beyond it (addendum (2) item 3, addendum (3) item 5).
The chapter sets norms for the plan format, word matching, the check of form and rights, of selection and of the request words, of question and refusal, the check
answer and the display of a plan version.

Norm ids are `V/11.11-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

- **Texts of the turn** — the request and the admin's answers of this turn (chapter 9 Н0); the **text** of the check is them joined by a line break.
  "Named" means "named in the text" (ADR-0093 addendum (3) item 1): the admin's answer to a question of the model is their confirmation
  if it itself names the field and the value («да» names nothing); there is no separate checkbox.
- **Plan version** — one call of `propose_plan`. Each version is checked anew against the texts of the turn and a fresh read;
  there is no inheritance between versions.
- The **current value** of a row field is its field in the check's read; if it is not set and the field has `inherit` — the **effective** value
  of `inherit`. In the error words, `<текущее>` is the own value, `null` for an unset one.
- `same(a, b)` — equality of JSON values without mixing `bool` and numbers (chapter 10 Н1.5).

## Н1 Form

**Data.** The chapter has no families of its own. The check reads the console by the same paths as the tools (chapter 10 Н1.2) and writes nothing.

### Н1.1 The plan

The arguments of `propose_plan` are the **version plan** (the format of lesson V/10, a candidate for the common format of mass editing; ADR-0093 addendum (1)
item 5). Selection, edits and reasons are separate:

```yaml
summary:     a string to the admin — what the plan will do, who is left out and why (the model's words)
selection:   how the admin chose the units and what is edited
  named:     [<unit name>…] — names from the request ("<sub>/<id>" or "<id>")
  where:     [{field, op, value}…] — the condition from the request (the condition form — chapter 10 Н1.5 find_units)
  select_sub: the selection subsystem; default — edit_sub
  edit_sub:  the subsystem whose field is edited; default — the subsystem of the edits, if it is the only one
  fields:    {<field>: <admin word>} — optional
edits:       [{unit, field, from?, to, why}…] — a non-empty list
  unit:      "<sub>/<id>" — the unit row
  field:     a field of fields of the manifest
  from:      the current value (optional)
  to:        the new value; for a field *_secret — the secret label {"secret": N} (V/11.12 Н1.3)
  why:       a non-empty string — which field or label brought the unit into the plan
```

A passed plan gives the **checked plan**: `{version, selection, summary, rows}` — `rows` by V/11.12 Н1.1 (`unit`,
`revision` of the check's read, `changes` `{field, from, to}`, `from` — the current value, for a secret — as the console gave it). It
is shown by Н1.7 and applied by V/11.12.

The format promises neither idempotency nor a check without a write — only partial application by rows (ADR-0093 addendum (2)
item 5).

### Н1.2 Word matching

Common to the "named" rules (Н1.4):

```
norm(t)            = t in lower case, «ё» → «е», Unicode spaces (White_Space: U+00A0, U+202F, U+2028, \v and the like),
                     except the line break, — into the space U+0020 (ADR-0093 addendum (29) item 7); the apostrophes ’ (U+2019) and ‘ (U+2018) —
                     into ' («you don’t have the rights» — `rights`; review of the norms of ADR-0093 (28)–(31) М1)
stems(t)           = the words of norm(t) made of the letters «[a-zа-я]+», of length ≥ 3, except the words stop (harness/words.json)
same_word(a, b)    = a — a word of the text, b — a word of the field or of the data:
                     |a| < 4 → b = a or b = a + e, e ∈ endings (a short word — whole or with a plural
                     ending from the locale data: tag — tags, «тег» — «теги»);
                     otherwise n ≥ 3 and n ≥ min(|a|, |b|) − 2, where n is the length of the common prefix of a and b
word_fits(t, w)    = ∃ a ∈ stems(t), b ∈ stems(w): same_word(a, b)
field_words(f)     = label, help, words of the field and enum_labels, enum_words of its options (chapter 10 Н1.4), joined by spaces
name_in(t, x)      = norm(x) is non-empty and occurs in norm(t), and left and right of the occurrence there is no letter, digit, «_» or «-»
                     (a neighbour is a whole code point, not half of a surrogate pair: «𐐀Gate-1𐐀» does not name Gate-1)
```

- **Name in the text** — `name_in(текст, x)`: this is how the names of units, the names of `selection.named`, string values,
  list elements and polarity phrases are matched (Н1.4; ADR-0093 addendum (4) item 5): `Store-2` is not in `Store-20`, `z2` is not in `z21`.
- Words shorter than three letters are not visible (`on`, `off`); a text word shorter than four letters names only itself and itself with an
  ending from `endings` («tag» names «tags», «все» does not name «всегда», nor «при» «приоритет»; the Architect's decision by
  measurement — a refinement of ADR-0093 addendum (4) item 5); the service words `stop` name nothing: the needed words of the operators are in
  `harness/words.json` (`fields`, `options`).
- **Regular expressions** of `harness/words.json` (`rights`, `not_found`, `all`) are applied over `norm(текст)`, without flags: the text is already
  in lower case. They are the same in RE2, JS and Python (ADR-0093 addendum (28), (29) items 1, 2): without `\b`, `\w`, `\W`, `\d`,
  lookbehind and `\p{…}`; a word start is `(?:^|[^0-9a-zа-я])`, a word end — `(?:$|[^0-9a-zа-я])`, a letter of a word — `[0-9a-zа-я]`
  (`norm` has already done «ё → е»), a word between the words of an expression — `\S+`. `name_in` does not change: names have their own neighbour rule. `list_verbs` and the phrases of `polarity` are comma-separated; `list_verbs` —
  `word_fits(текст, list_verbs)`, polarity — `name_in` of each phrase.
- **Numbers of the text** — all occurrences of the names of the units named in the text are cut out of it (`name_in`, over all readable
  subsystems; an unreadable one is skipped — chapter 10 Н1.2);
  in the remainder the numbers are `[0-9]+([.,][0-9]+)?`, with a minus in front only if the minus does not stand after a letter or a digit; a comma is
  a decimal point.
- Words are taken only from the canon: `display` of the manifest (the VMS canon) and `harness/words.json` (the lesson data). The model does not
  add words: `selection.fields` is matched against the text (Н1.4).

### Н1.3 Form and rights

Each edit `edits[i]`, in order; errors accumulate (all rules are the floor of ADR-0093 item 5, except the marked ones):

| Rule | Error words |
|---|---|
| an edit is an object | `edits[i]: an edit is an object {unit, field, from, to, why}` |
| `unit`, `field`, `to`, `why` are present | `edits[i]: missing <ключи>` |
| `unit` — `<sub>/<id>` | `edits[i]: unit is "<sub>/<name>", e.g. cam/<name>` |
| the subsystem exists, its rows are read (chapter 10 Н1.2) | `edits[i]: <ошибка чтения>` |
| the row is visible to the session | `edits[i]: no unit <unit> (or the session cannot see it)` |
| the field is in `fields` of the manifest | `edits[i]: <sub> has no field "<поле>"` |
| not the name field | `edits[i]: <поле> is the unit's name, it is not changed` |
| not `fixed` | `edits[i]: field <поле> is fixed — it cannot be changed` |
| not `blob` | `edits[i]: field <поле> is bytes, a plan does not carry them` |
| a field `*_secret` — `to` is only a label by V/11.12 Н1.3 | `edits[i]: <поле> is a secret: a plan carries only the secret label {"secret": N} the admin entered, not a value` |
| a secret label only whole in `to` of a field `*_secret`, with a number of this turn, one label — one field; nowhere else (`from`, `selection`, `why`, `summary`, inside a string or an array); the pilot's text label as a string — nowhere | `edits[i]: the secret label is allowed only as the whole to of a *_secret field the admin entered` (for a label outside the edits — `plan: …` instead of `edits[i]: …`) |
| the type of `to` is the type of the field: `string`, `url` — a string; `int` — an integer, not `bool`; `float` — a number, not `bool`; `bool`; `list` — a list of strings; `json` — anything; `null` — allowed | `edits[i]: <поле> is of type <тип>, but to = <to><подсказка>` |
| `enum` — `to` is one of the options (or `null`) | `edits[i]: <поле> is one of <варианты>, but to = <to>` |
| `required` — `to` is not `null` | `edits[i]: <поле> is required` |
| `from` (if given) is the current or the effective value; for `list` the empty `""`, `"[]"`, `[""]`, `null` equal the empty one; for a secret — `***`, `null`, `""` | `edits[i]: from = <from>, but now <unit> has <поле> = <текущее>` |
| not an empty edit (empty: the own value is set and `same(to, своё)`, or both are unset; not a secret; ADR-0093 addendum (2) item 3) | `edits[i]: empty edit — <unit> already has <поле> = <текущее>; a unit with nothing to change is left out of the plan (if no unit has anything to change, there is no plan: tell the admin)` |
| `why` is not empty | `edits[i]: why is empty — an edit needs a reason` |
| the right to write the row is the capability of the write door (`need`, chapter 10 Н1.2; ADR-0093 addendum (4) item 4) | `edits[i]: the admin has no right to edit <unit>` (`backend` does not know the person's name: `/session` is held by the module) |
| a field once per row; a row has one `revision` (V/11.12 Н1.1) | `edits[i]: <unit> <поле> is edited twice; one edit per field` |

- **Coercion.** A string `to` or `from` of a non-secret field is coerced to the type if unambiguous, as the form does: `bool` —
  `"true"`, `"false"` (case-insensitive, without spaces at the edges); `int` — `-?\d+`; `float` — `-?\d+(\.\d+)?`. Otherwise it is kept as is.
- **Type hints**: `bool` — ` — write JSON true or false, without quotes`; `int` — ` — write an integer without quotes,
  e.g. 14`; `float` — ` — write a number without quotes, e.g. 1.5`; `list` — ` — write the whole list of strings, e.g.
  ["blue", "green"]`.
- Values in the words are JSON as is; secret values never appear in the words.

### Н1.4 Selection and request words

The check of `selection` against the edits and the text; errors accumulate after Н1.3. Order: items 1, 2; then by field name — for each edit field, in
order, items 4, 5, then item 6; then items 7–10:

1. Neither `named` nor `where` — one error `selection: the admin named neither a unit nor a selection condition — that is
   a question to the admin, not a plan`; nothing further is checked (floor). Each name of `named` (the id is the part after the last
   `/`) MUST satisfy `name_in(текст, id)`; otherwise `selection.named: <имя> is not named in the admin's request` (floor; ADR-0093 addendum (4)
   item 5).
2. `edit_sub`: if it is not given and the edits have one subsystem — that one; otherwise `selection.edit_sub: name the subsystem whose units you
   edit (the one whose field the request changes)`, and nothing further is checked. An edit outside `edit_sub` — `selection: you edit
   <unit>, but the edit subsystem is <edit_sub>` (floor).
3. **`selection.fields`** is read both ways: `{поле: слово}` and `{слово: поле}` — the key or the value that is a field of
   `edit_sub`.
4. **The value is named** — for each edit of a field of `edit_sub`, without exceptions (floor of ADR-0093 item 5, addendum (4) item 3); an edit
   whose `to` does not fit the form of the field (Н1.3: type, secret label, `blob`) is not matched here — its error already exists, the check does not fail:
   - `bool` and clearing (`to` = `null`) — by the polarity words `polarity` (`true`, `false`, `clear`): for `to` = `true` the text
     has the phrase `true` and does not have the phrase `false`; for `false` — the other way round; for `null` — the text has the phrase `clear` (`name_in` of each phrase);
     otherwise (no word, or both polarities) — the error of the rule;
   - `list` — each added and each removed element (the difference of the sets of the current value and `to`) — `name_in(текст, элемент)`;
     otherwise an error listing the ones not found;
   - `to` is a secret label (V/11.12 Н1.3) — named (the number was entered in this turn);
   - **as at a unit** — at a unit of `edit_sub` whose name is in the text, this field equals `to` (numbers — by value);
   - **as at a related one** — at the related unit of this row (by `about`) the field with the same name equals `to` (not `bool`), and
     the text names that unit by its name or by the words of the relation field (`word_fits(текст, field_words(поле связи))`);
   - `int`, `float` — `to` is among the numbers of the text;
   - a string — `name_in(текст, to)`, or a label of an option (`enum_labels`, `enum_words`) fits the text (`word_fits`);
   - otherwise — `<unit>: value <to> of field <поле> is not named in the admin's request — do not choose it yourself; ask
     the admin so that the answer itself names the field and the value (a yes/no answer names nothing)`.
5. **A list edit — only with a verb** (harness v4.0, beyond the floor): for a field `list` the value is named, but `word_fits(текст,
   list_verbs)` does not hold — one error per field `field <поле>: the request does not say to add, remove or replace <слова
   поля> — "<элементы to через ", " или []>" there is probably the selection condition, not an edit; edit only what the
   request changes`.
6. **The field is named** (floor): `field_words` of the field are non-empty and `word_fits(текст, field_words)` does not hold — `field <поле> ("<слова>") is
   not named in the admin's request — edit only what the admin named; if the needed field does not exist, refuse and
   explain; to confirm it, ask the admin so that the answer itself names the field and the value (a yes/no answer names
   nothing)`; the field is named, but a word of `selection.fields` is given for the field and `word_fits(текст, слово)` does not hold — `selection.fields.<поле>:
   the word "<слово>" is not in the admin's request`. For a field without words (`field_words` empty) the rule does not fire.
7. **The condition is named** (harness v4.0, beyond the floor; ADR-0093 addendum (3) item 5): each condition of `where` in `select_sub`
   (`about.<поле>` — a field of the related subsystem) is named if: the condition is `exists` on `id` or on the name field, and the text fits
   `all` (`harness/words.json`); or `word_fits(текст, field_words(поле))`; or the value (an element of a list of values) is a
   number among the numbers of the text, or a string with `name_in(текст, значение)` or `word_fits(текст, значение)`.
   `bool` and `null` do not name. Otherwise `selection.where: condition <условие> is not from the admin's request — neither
   its field nor its value is named there; select by the admin's words (label codes — field_values, the related unit —
   about.<field>)`. There is no semantic matching of the condition against the request (ADR-0093 addendum (2) item 3).
8. **Selected** (floor): `where` — the units of `select_sub` that match the conditions (comparison — chapter 10 Н1.5; a condition error —
   `selection.where: <ошибка>`, and nothing further is checked); `named` — the ids from the names (the part after the last `/`),
   unknown ones — `selection.named: <select_sub> has no <имена>`; both given — selected by `where`, `named` is only matched against
   the text (item 1). **Expected** in `edit_sub`: the same subsystem — the selected ones;
   `edit_sub` is related to `select_sub` (`about.sub` = `select_sub`) — the rows of `edit_sub` whose relation field is among the selected;
   `select_sub` is related to `edit_sub` — the units by the relation field of the selected; otherwise `selection: <select_sub> and <edit_sub> are
   not related (about) — select in the edit subsystem`, and nothing further is checked.
9. **Extra** (floor): rows of the edits not among the expected — `selection: <id…> not selected (named: <id…> | condition in
   <select_sub>); selected: <ожидаемые или none>`.
10. **Missing** (floor): for each field of the edits, when all edits of the field have one `to` (for `list` — one change: the same added and
    removed elements): an expected unit without an edit, for which this change changes something (not `list`: the effective value ≠
    `to`; `list`: not all added ones are already there, or removed ones are there), and the write right exists (chapter 10 Н1.2), is missing: `selection: selected
    but missing from the plan: <id> (<поле> = <текущее>)…`. Different `to` among the edits of a field — the rule is not
    applied for the field.

### Н1.5 Question and refusal

Checks of an answer without a plan (harness v3.7–v4.0, beyond the floor):

| Rule | Where | Error words |
|---|---|---|
| the first `ask_admin` or `refuse` of a turn without a single search (`find_units`, `field_values`, `list_table`) | the loop (chapter 9 Н1.4 item 4) | «сначала данные» ("data first"), once per turn |
| the reason of `refuse` fits `rights` (`norm(причина)`), units were found during the turn and all of them have the right (chapter 10 Н1.2) | `refuse` | `refusal for lack of rights, but the units found (<до 6 через ", ">) do have the edit right — none has can_edit: false; build the plan with propose_plan` |
| the reason fits `rights`, but not a single unit was found during the turn | `refuse` | `refusal for lack of rights, but no unit of the request was found this turn: the right is per unit — find the units with find_units (can_edit shows the right); then a plan for the editable ones, or a refusal if none is editable` |
| the reason fits `not_found`, and the text names existing units (a name in the text, over all readable subsystems; an unreadable one is skipped — chapter 10 Н1.2) | `refuse`, once per turn | `the refusal says there are no such units, but the request names units that exist: <до 6 "<sub>/<id>"> — find them with find_units (where id) and propose the plan; if the request's condition really matches no unit, say which condition` |

On an error, `refuse` is not accepted and the turn goes on (chapter 9 Н1.4).

### Н1.6 The check answer

- `edits` is not a non-empty list — the tool error `edits is a non-empty list of {unit, field, from, to, why}`.
- There are errors of Н1.3, Н1.4 — the result `{"valid": false, "errors": [<слова>…], "hint": "fix the plan and call propose_plan
  again, or ask the admin"}`; the last reason of the turn (chapter 9 Н1.4 item 7) is the first error.
- There are no errors — `{"valid": true, "diff": [<строка>…], "note": "the plan is shown to the admin; the admin applies it with
  the Apply button (written through the console doors with CAS on revision)"}`; a diff line is `<unit>  <поле>[ «<label>»]:
  <from> → <to>   (<why>)`, `from` — the current value (for a secret `***`), the values — JSON. The turn ends: the outcome «план» (chapter 9
  Н1.5).
- A console read error during the check is an error of the edit or of the selection (Н1.3, Н1.4 item 8): a plan without a read does not pass.

### Н1.7 Display of a plan version

The admin sees the checked plan in the words of `backend` (V/11.12 Н1.2):

- **The selection block — above the diff, separately** (ADR-0093 addendum (3) item 2): the selection condition in words — for `named` — the names of the units;
  for `where` (also when both are given) — per condition: the field label (`display.fields`, otherwise the name; `about.<поле>` — «<слово связанной единицы>: <подпись>»),
  the word of `op` (`eq` «=», `ne` «≠», `in` «одно из», `not_in` «ни одно из», `contains` «содержит»,
  `not_contains` «не содержит», `gt` «>», `gte` «≥», `lt` «<», `lte` «≤», `exists` «задано», `missing` «не задано»), the value
  (for an option — the label `display.options`); the selection subsystem and the edit subsystem, if they differ; **the number of units** — selected
  and in the plan.
- **Diff** — the lines of V/11.12 Н1.2; for each unit — the value of the field of the selection condition by which it got into the plan
  (`where`: the value of the condition field at the **witnesses** — the selected units of `select_sub` by which it is in the plan: itself, its
  related unit or, with a reverse `about`, all the units that name it; the check keeps them at the row of the checked plan —
  `because`; `named` — «названа»), in the words of `backend`, not the `why` of the model.
- «Применить» confirms only the shown version: a new version is a new display and a new «Применить» (V/11.12 Н1.4).

### Н1.8 Floor and additions

- **Floor** (ADR-0093 item 5, addendum (2) item 3, addendum (4) items 3, 5): form and rights of Н1.3, the names of `named` of Н1.4 item 1, "the value
  is named" of item 4 (both `bool` and clearing), "the field is named" of item 6, the matching of Н1.2 (name in the text, short words, `stop`), selection and
  completeness of items 1, 2, 8–10, the empty edit, the secret, actions outside the set (chapter 10 Н1.1). They MUST NOT be weakened by
  the manifest, by the model, or by the lesson data: `harness/words.json` adds words but does not lift rules.
- **Harness additions** (addendum (3) item 5): Н1.4 items 5, 7, Н1.5, «сначала данные» (chapter 9). A new rule comes only by the Architect's
  decision with a measurement of the pilot.

## Н2 Doors

The chapter has no doors of its own. Reading — the console doors of chapter 10 Н1.2 by ticket.

## Н3 Pass

```
propose_plan(args) → edits non-empty? otherwise a tool error
read of the rows of the edit subsystems (and related ones) → Н1.3 for each edit
Н1.4: named|where? → edit_sub → fields, values, list verb, conditions → selected → extra → missing
no errors → checked plan (revision of the read) → display Н1.7 → outcome «план» | errors → valid: false, the turn goes on
refuse(reason) → Н1.5 → accepted | error
```

**Races.** A row was changed between the check's read and «Применить» — the `revision` of the checked plan is older, the write gets
412 (V/11.12 Н1.4). Rights changed between versions — each version sees its own.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| errors of Н1.3, Н1.4 | `valid: false` with the reasons | the model | fix the plan, ask the admin or `refuse` |
| `refuse` without grounds (Н1.5) | an error, the refusal is not accepted | the model | find the units, make a plan |
| versions that do not pass, up to `TURN_CALLS` | «ход не завершён» with the first error of the last version | the admin | rephrase the request |

## Н5 Acceptance

Plan check vectors — the driver `backend` (chapter 7 Н5):
"texts of the turn + overview (manifests, rows, grants of the session) + plan → `valid` and the error words" and "texts + what was found during the turn +
`refuse` → accepted or error". The source is the requests and plans of the pilot's suites (stand 3: six suites, 60 requests) with a translation of the
form: the secret label `{"secret": N}`, the admin's answers as texts of the turn (ADR-0093 addendum (3) item 6), and VMS cases by the manifests
`cam`, `rec`. Each rule of Н1.3–Н1.5 has at least one case "violated" and one "not violated"; among them: `named` not named;
`z2` against `z21`; «все» against «всегда»; `bool` without a word and with both polarities; a question with a value and the answer «да» —
