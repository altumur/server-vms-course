# V/11.4 — Translation of reads and writes: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.4-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 4 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0094 A3, A6; addendum (2) items 3, 4). Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](11-backend.README.md), the table [`data/mapping.tsv`](../data/mapping.tsv), the contract
[`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; an increment of rights on top of the norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark «норма платформы B» — where the chapter relies on them).

Chapter 3 says which kind of consolem is answered by which subsystem. This chapter is how `backend` assembles a record of a kind from the rows
of subsystems, how it disassembles an edit back into rows, how it checks the version, what it remembers about repeats and how it translates a refusal
of the console into a refusal of the contract. All of it is done by callbacks with the ticket of the request at the listing door (V/11.1 Н1.5).

Norm ids are `V/11.4-MM`; the map — [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1
Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

- **Part** — a row of a subsystem from which a record of a kind is assembled: for a camera — the camera row `cam/cameras/<id>` (empty
  `main`) and its streams (`cam/cameras` with `main` = id), its recordings `rec/recordings` and detectors `det/units` (chapter 3 Н1.2).
- **Store index** — the version of a store row (P/01.1): it does not repeat (addendum (4) does not promise growth); a tombstone of a row
  has an index of its own (P/06.1; ADR-0094 addendum (2) item 4 «в», addendum (3) item 9; not a tombstone of the body `.gone/` of a resource).
- **Group write** — `POST /devices` (creation), `POST /devices/update` and `POST /objects/delete` of the contract:
  several objects in one request with `idempotencyKey` in the body; a **step** is one element of `items`.
- **Batch** — the batch write of the store B9 through the console door (ADR-0094 addendum (9); platform norm B):
  every row being written has its own `cas`, the read rows that are not written have guards; all or nothing.
- **Hidden member** — a member of a folder that is not in the composition the form sees: a camera stream, the second row `type: ipsrv` of a host, a member outside the reader's listing (Н1.7).
- **Target root** — the row of an operation by which the console judges occupancy (P/06.9 Н1.3): Н1.8.
- **Entry cluster**, **own**, **foreign** — chapter 3 Н1.2а. **Bundle** — one stored edit of several rows, `POST /domain/edits` (P/08.6 Н1.3б): Н1.13.

## Н1 Form

**Time.** The expiry of the repeat memory is `delete_after` of the table `repeats`: the console counts it from the first observation of the row's version,
not by store time, and the platform reaper removes the row (Н1.4); `backend` does not judge the memory by the wall clock (the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 7, question 3).

**Data.** Parts are read and written through the console doors with the ticket (V/11.1 Н1.3). The chapter's own family is the repeat memory of the
group key: the manifest table `backend/repeats/<h>` (Н1.4; the form is the fields of the table `repeats` of the
[manifest](../backend.subsystem.yaml): the platform family of tables — with the schema `manifest`, P/06.1 Н1.6), through the tables door
and the batch door of the console with the ticket (V/11.1 Н1.5).

### Н1.1 Assembling a record (read)

- A camera (`Device`, `type: camera`) is the camera row `cam/cameras/<id>` (empty `main`, `type: camera`), its streams (the rows `cam/cameras`
  with `main` = id), the recordings `rec/recordings` about this camera (`cam` of the recording is its id, V/02.1) and the detectors `det/units` about it
  (`cam`, V/04.1), all through the unit doors of the console (`GET /<sub>/<rows>`), the heartbeat words — `GET /<sub>/<rows>` (`rows`, P/06.1
  Н2). The fields — by the rows of the table of chapter 3; `recordMode` — from the recordings `<cam>`, `<cam>-<том>` (chapter 3 Н1.3). A row
  `cam/cameras` with a non-empty `main` is a stream of its camera and is not given out as a separate device.
- A detector, a volume — one row of its subsystem (chapter 3 Н1.2); a storage — the volume row and the recordings on it (`retentionDays`);
  a rule and a library — Н1.10, Н1.11. The record row of the camera and `cam/rule_state/<id>` are read by the same snapshot (chapter 3 Н1.3).
- The device tree (chapter 3 Н1.3 «Дерево») is read by the same snapshot: the tables `cam/devices`, `cam/children`, `cam/child_settings`, `cam/device_units` — through the tables doors (`GET /cam/devices`, P/06.1 Н1.6), rows with an index. There is no row `cam/devices/<хост>` — `channels`, `videos` are not stated: `ipsrv` exists only by a row `type: ipsrv`, there is no `videoChannel`. The row is unreadable (`garbled`) — the fields are not read, rather than absent: the records of this host (`ipsrv`, channels, cameras) are not given out in `GET /devices`, a read of one of them is 503, like an unreadable folder (Н1.7). A value of `type` outside the list is not given out: the word is not read (V/01.1 Н1.1б). A 403 of the tables door — the part is not visible, there is no refusal, as with tombstones (below): `ipsrv` and `parentId` are derived from the row and the address (chapter 3 Н1.3), `channels`, `subtype`, `videoChannel` are silent, there are no channels, the part `h` is empty.
- The id of a record of a kind — Н1.6.
- A foreign record — the same assemblies from the domain view, without a double read: chapter 3 Н1.2а.
- **A foreign record** is assembled by the same rules from the domain view (chapter 3 Н1.2а). The version is the same concatenation with the `revision` of the row instead of the index: the view has no index and no tombstones, therefore the part of a removed row is empty, and the version of a foreign record may return to a former one. There is no double read. One record through different entries has two versions: `If-Match` is checked against the version of the entry that issued it; after a change of entry — 412 and a re-read.
- The **version** of a record is an opaque string of the contract, compared only for equality (ADR-0094 addendum (4) item 1). For a
  detector, a volume, `channel`, `storage`, a unit — the store index of the row; for a row `eventsrc`, `acfa` — the index of this row. For a camera — the concatenation of the indices of the parts in a fixed order: `c<индексы
  cam>.r<индексы rec>.d<индексы det>.h<индекс cam/devices>.i<индекс строки ipsrv>.s<индекс cam/rule_state>.a<индекс строки записи>`: after the letter of a part — the indices of its rows separated by `-` (for `c` — the camera row, then its
  streams; for the others — in the order of the row key, UTF-8 bytes), for a deleted row `rec`, `det` — the index of its tombstone, for a
  part without rows — empty (`c17.r.d.h.i.s.a`, `c17-18.r21-30.d44.h9.i31.s52.a60`). An order row (`made`) is never a part: the evaluator keeps it (chapter 3 Н1.3). The rows `cam/devices` of the host, `ipsrv` and `cam/rule_state` enter the version (`parentId`, `videoChannel`, `armed` depend on them), but not the guards of the batch: the edit does not write their fields, and the limit of guards is taken by the parts (Н1.2). This is a limit: their change between the snapshot and the batch does not cancel the edit, a write of `events`, `videos` by a neighbour changes the version of the camera without a visible edit, and the removal of the row `ipsrv` returns an empty part, and the version may return to a former one with the same visible record. For `ipsrv` — `h<индекс cam/devices>.c<индекс строки>`, for a child — `k<индекс cam/children>.s<индекс cam/child_settings>`; there is no part — empty. Children are records of their own and do not enter the version of the camera; `subtype` depends on the children but does not take them into the version: a change of the description also writes the row `cam/devices`. For a storage — `v<индекс тома>.r<индексы записей с home =
  том>` (`retentionDays` depends on the recordings; the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 13). The index is the wire of P/01.1 Н2 (decimal
  digits), therefore letters, the dot and `-` do not confuse the concatenation. The rows `rec` and `det` of a part are the rows of this subsystem about the camera
  (`cam` of the rows is the camera id) and the tombstones of such rows; the tombstones of parts are not cleaned up while the camera is alive (addendum (4) item 1
  «в»). Streams and recordings of a storage enter as live rows: the tombstone of a stream is not found (the row `cam` has no `about`),
  the tombstone of a recording is found by the camera, not by the volume. Therefore the version of a camera or a storage may return to a former one if a stream
  or a recording of the volume was created and removed bypassing `backend`; the visible record is then the same — its fields are a function of the same rows of the same
  indices (an open item of the README: the product's strict «does not return»).
- Indices: the index of a row is in the list element of `GET /<rows>` and of tables (platform norm B4; the analysis of
  the V/11 review, question 6); the tombstones of the parts of a camera — `GET /<rows>?deleted=1&about=<cam>` → `{deleted: [{id, index, revision}]}`
  (a tombstone keeps the value `about`; B4, addendum (5) item 1); a 403 of this door — the part is not visible: there are no tombstones of this subsystem in
  the concatenation, there is no refusal; the tombstone index of its own write is the `ETag` of the answer to `DELETE` and
  the feed message. The console has no door of one row: «404 с `ETag` надгробия» (addendum (3) item 9) is replaced by this read. Before
  B4 the console doors do not give out the index, and `backend` cannot issue the version of a record: the chapter is in force after the increment of the
  platform (together with B1, without which there is no pass of `backend`, V/11.1 Н1.3).
- Fields not from the rows of parts (heartbeats, the holder's server) do not enter the version (chapter 3 Н1.3; the analysis of the V/11 review,
  Claude С-1).
- **The version depends on the viewer** (ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 12). Rows of a part that the person does not see do not enter the concatenation:
  the listing gives out only the permitted ones. Two people with different rights get different versions of one camera, and `If-Match` is checked against
  the version of the one who writes. A part without `view` is not guarded by a guard; a row that the writer does not see refuses in the batch with the same word
  as an absent one: outward this is 412.
- **The read is double** (ibid.): the rows of parts have two doors, therefore the snapshot is read twice in a row until the indices of the two reads coincide; they did not
  coincide in three attempts — 503: there is no snapshot fit for a version. For `GET /devices` the snapshot is the whole list, an attempt is a pair of full reads, 503 is the answer as a whole.
- The answer `ETag` of a record of a kind is its version as a string in quotes (`"c17.r21-30.d44.h9.i.s.a"`); `If-Match` is checked by equality of
  the whole string.

### Н1.2 Disassembling an edit (write)

An own record. A foreign one — the same rows by a bundle: Н1.13.

- **Permanent keys of parts** (the analysis of the V/11 review, question 1 «а»): the recordings and detectors that `backend` creates have
  keys from the camera: the primary recording — `rec/recordings/<cam>`, the backup one on the volume `<том>` — `rec/recordings/<cam>-<том>`,
  a detector — `det/units/<cam>-<kind>` (the V/04 pattern), a second one of the same kind — `det/units/<cam>-<kind>-<n>`: `n` is the largest number of the live rows and tombstones of this pair from the snapshot + 1, not less than 2. A simultaneous creation of one part converges on one `cas ""`:
  the second batch is a refusal, nothing is written.
- **The device lock is the camera row** (question 1 «б»). Every batch of a part of a camera **writes** the row `cam/cameras/<id>` —
  `cas` of its index from the snapshot, the value former or new: an edit of the camera, of its recording or detector, creation and deletion
  of a detector, deletion of the camera. Two writes of one device are ordered by the camera row: the second is 412. Deletion of a camera
  and a write of its part do not both pass.
- **A write of a device is one batch** (ADR-0094 addendum (9) item 3). An edit of a camera (`PATCH /devices/{id}`): the camera row
  (the lock), the parts being written — each with `cas` of its index **from the same snapshot** by which the version was checked (Н1.3),
  not re-read; the other parts of the device from the snapshot — the streams, the rows `rec` and `det` of the camera — as **guards** with their
  indices. The batch passes whole or does not pass: if even one row of the snapshot changed — 412 of the console, nothing is
  written. A part that does not exist yet is written in the batch over its tombstone with `cas` of the tombstone's index if the tombstone was in the snapshot,
  otherwise by creation (`cas ""`). An edit of a detector (`Device`, `type: det`) is a batch of the row `det` and the row of its camera
  (the lock); `zone` with a shape — also the door of the mask body (chapter 3 Н1.3 «`zone`»). Rows about the camera created bypassing `backend` (direct console doors) after the snapshot are not guarded by the batch: the
  next read sees them — a new `version`.
- **`recordMode`** is a transition over the recordings `<cam>`, `<cam>-<том>` (chapter 3 Н1.3; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 2), in one batch
  with the rest of the edit:
  - `always` — the primary `<cam>` `enabled: true`, `replica_from` empty; if it does not exist — creation on the volume `archiveId` of the body, and without that field — 422
    `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: archiveId`;
  - `replica` — the primary `<cam>` `enabled: true`, `replica_from` by `replicaFrom`, without the field — `device`, a non-empty one does not change (chapter 3 Н1.3); if it does not exist — creation in the same way;
  - `none` — all recordings `<cam>`, `<cam>-<том>` — `enabled: false`;
  - `motion` (ADR-0111 item 4) — the primary `<cam>` `enabled: false`, if it does not exist — creation on the volume `archiveId` of the body, and without that field — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: archiveId`; the record row of the camera (chapter 3 Н1.3) in the same batch: absent — creation with `auto/next_id`, as for a camera, present — a write with `cas` of the snapshot; `recordDetectorIds` of the body is its target and `enabled`, without the field the target stays the former one;
  - `backup` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: recordMode`: only an element of `extraArchives` (chapter 3 Н1.3);
  - `sched` (chapter 3 Н1.3) — like `motion`, the record row has `when` empty, `conditions: [{kind: time, schedule}]`; `recordScheduleId` absent or the schedule absent — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: recordScheduleId`, a value equal to the current one is not judged: a camera with a removed schedule can be edited; `recordScheduleId` without `sched` is skipped; a built-in one — the write carries its row with `cas ""` if the row does not exist (Н1.11).
  Leaving `motion`, `sched` removes the record row in the same batch; a transition between them rewrites `when`, `conditions`, `enabled` and `minutes` of the row. `archiveId` with `motion`, `sched` is also written into `archive` of the row's action. A batch with `auto/scenarios` is judged by the scenario right for the whole cluster (`cluster_rows`, V/07): without it — 403 of the whole batch, the mode does not change, this is a limit.
  `archiveId` is `home` of the primary `<cam>` (`null` — remove the primary, `DELETE` in the batch; a volume `backup`, `edge` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, a volume with `admits: false` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: archiveId`, chapter 3 Н1.3); `extraArchives` is the set of additional ones:
  an element is a row `<cam>-<том>`, an element of a volume `edge` (chapter 3 Н1.3) is passed through as an echo without a write (`recordMode` of the element `null`, `always` — `enabled: true`, `replica_from` empty, a new row on a volume `backup` also gets `when: offline`; `replica` — `enabled: true`, `replica_from`: `device`, a non-empty one does not change (chapter 3 Н1.3); `none` — `enabled: false`; anything else — 422
  `NOT_SUPPORTED`; `stream` — `stream` of the recording; `when` of an existing row does not change), the rows outside the set are removed; `stream` — `stream` of the primary; there is no primary
  and the body does not create it (including `archiveId: null` in the same `set`) — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: stream` (the analysis of
  the V/11 review, Kimi's addition, No. 8). A new recording — with the storage retention of its volume (chapter 3 Н1.3). A primary created
  by `archiveId` without `recordMode` in the same `set` — `enabled: false`: the mode does not change.
- **Creation of a device row** (`POST /devices`, an element `type: camera`, `ipsrv`, `eventsrc`, `acfa` with `parentId: null`) is one batch; `type` of the body is a field of the row, the address — chapter 3 Н1.3 «Адрес устройства»: the counter `cam/next_id` (`cas` of the value read,
  the value `n + 1`; the value and the index — `GET /cam/next_id`, P/06.1 Н2 «Дверь счётчика»), the row `cam/cameras/<n + 1>` (`cas ""`), the recordings `<n + 1>`, `<n + 1>-<том>` and the detectors
  `<n + 1>-<kind>` from `children` and the recording fields — recordings and detectors only for `camera` (ADR-0111 item 3: the row `camera` and all its recordings get `ref` = `<CLUSTER>-<n + 1>`, a recording created later — the `ref` of the camera row; the primary in the domain is one: V/02.9 Н1.6; platform norm B9, `POST /console/batch`, P/06.1 Н2 «Пакетная дверь»:
  the caller names the number, the counter goes in the same batch; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 9). `n` is the
  counter value read before the batch: the last number issued; the new camera gets the next one (P/06.1 Н1.3). The counter
  was moved — 412 `row moved` with `key: cam/next_id`, the batch is built anew with a new read (Н1.4). **The `identifier` of creation** is a check, not a key: the row `cam/cameras/<identifier>` is alive and was not issued by this group (`steps` of the row `<h>`) — a refusal of the element 409 `IDENTIFIER_TAKEN`, `field: identifier`, before the other checks of the element: the wizard numbers the rows itself, and the counter may be ahead of its tree, the numbers of its own group do not occupy values; otherwise the value is skipped: the counter issues the number, the answer carries the one issued. The wizard sends a number at every CSV import, counting it by its tree, therefore a free number is not a refusal. Creation of a detector is a batch of the row of the key above (`cas ""`, a tombstone in the snapshot — `cas` of its index) and the camera row (the lock); `kind` — `detectorKind`, if absent — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: detectorKind`; `name` of the body is the field `title` (chapter 3 Н1.3). Creation of a storage
  (`POST /archives`) is the row `rec/volumes/<name>` (`cas ""`) from the fields of the storage and `firstVolume` (the volume = the same
  storage: `disk`, `sizeBytes`, the address — the rows of the table `Volume`).
- **`configMode`** (ADR-0107 addendum (1)) is a field of the camera creation request, not a field of the row; there is no lasting link to the source. `current` — nothing. `default` — into `resolution`, `fps` of the new row go the model's values from `device-catalog.json`; the fields `res`, `fps` of the body take precedence. This changes the camera's setting: the holder passes them to the driver the usual way (V/01.2 Н1.2а). There is no model (`rtspUrl`) or it is not in the file — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: configMode`. `template` — in the same batch a one-time copy from the camera `templateId` of the same model, visible to the reader: `resolution`, `fps`, `firmware`, the rows of its other streams with `profile`, `source` — as in «Потоки за камерой» (chapter 3 Н1.3), a stream on another host is not copied, each gets its own counter number; the fields of the body take precedence. The address, login, password, name, recordings, folders, detectors, `norm` of children are not copied. There is no source or `vendor`, `model` differ — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: templateId`. A stream beyond `DEVICE_PARTS_MAX` — as with other parts.
- **A built-in detector** (`Device`, `type: det`, `builtin: true`; chapter 3 Н1.3). Creation — `parentId` is a primary camera with the address `ipint://`; its `vendor`, `model` are not in `device-catalog.json` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: builtin`. `template` of the body is a detector of the model; outside `detectorOptions` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: template`. Not named — the detectors of the model whose kind by `detector-kinds.tsv` equals `detectorKind`: none — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: detectorKind`; one — that one; several — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: template`. The number and the batch — as for a unit, below; `title` — `name`; `folderId` not `null` — 422 (Н1.7): the row is not a unit of rights. **Edit** — `name`, `enabled`, `sensitivity`, the row whole with `cas` of the snapshot, the camera row is a guard; `detectorKind`, `model` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field`. **Removal** — as for a unit. The right — `admin` on the camera row (`rights.unit_of`, V/01.1 Н1.5). `children` of a camera with `builtin: true` — the same steps in the batch of the camera creation.
- **Deletion of a camera** (`DELETE /devices/{id}`) is one batch: removal of the camera row, its streams, recordings, detectors
  and the record row (the contract: «вместе с дочерними»), each with `cas` of its index from the snapshot. **Deletion of a detector** is a batch: removal of the row
  `det`, a write of the camera row (the lock) and of the record row of this camera without it, if it is in the target. Other rules — the cascade of Н1.10.
- **Enabling a camera switched off by a rule** (chapter 3 Н1.3; V/01.4 Н1.4д): `PATCH enabled: true`, the step `set`, an element of `POST /devices/update`, while the switch-off is in force, is a request of `cam` `disable` with a deadline in the past; a camera row with `enabled: true` does not get the field, a row with `enabled: false` gets it by a batch. Other fields of the body — the usual batch, before the request; only `enabled` — there is no batch, the version is checked by the snapshot (Н1.3). `POST /cam/requests` with the ticket (P/06.3 Н1.9): `{unit: <cam>, action: disable, disabled_by_rule: <значение строки>, disabled_until: 0}`, `Idempotency-Key` is the hex SHA-256 of `enable`, a zero byte, `<cam>`, a zero byte and the index of the row `cam/rule_state`: a repeat of the same edit is the same request. The filing is repeated with the same key until 200 with a label arrives, within the timeout of the route `api/devices`. `performed` — the record is read anew. No label by the deadline, `expired`, `gone`, `unknown` — 409 `DEVICE_OFFLINE`, `field: enabled`; `refused`, a refusal of the door — by Н4, `field: enabled`; at the group doors — the element is `failed`, at a step — the result `fail`; the other fields are written in that case. A step of an operation files the request before the batch of the result, with the same key. The request does not go onto the undo stack: there is no row write. **Limit:** the switch-off returns only by a new firing; a revert and `retry` do not bring it back.
- **`ipsrv`** (ADR-0102 addendum (5) item 5, addendum (6) items 6, 7, 10). There is a row `type: ipsrv` — `PATCH`, `POST /devices/update`, the step `set` write it by one batch, `cas` of the snapshot index: `name`, `enabled`, `short`, `lat`, `lon`, `azimuth`, `mac`, `login`, `password`, the address fields; `DELETE` removes it, the cameras of the host stay and lose only the parent if `channels` is not greater than 1. A change of host is a refusal (chapter 3 Н1.3). There is no row (`derived: true`) — `PATCH` and `DELETE` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` of the first field of the body, for `DELETE` — `field: type`.
- **`eventsrc`, `acfa` as a row** — one row: `PATCH`, `POST /devices/update`, the step `set` write it with `cas` of the snapshot index, `DELETE` removes it and the rows `cam/device_units` on it by one batch; the console removes it from folders in the same batch. A camera and an `ipsrv` with units — the same way. The row `cam/unit_next` stays: a row id does not repeat.
- **Units** (`acfa_sensor`, `acfa_lock`, `acfa_fence`, `relay` of a unit, `acfa` with a parent; V/01.1 Н1.2б). Creation — by `parentId`; `acfa` with a parent is a row `type: controller`, with no `ip` address and no `vendor`, the address of the unit is `path`: for a record of a primary row `cam/cameras` (the root) or of a unit, its `camera` is the `camera` of the new one; for a record `ipsrv` — the row `type: ipsrv` of its host, of two the smallest id as a number. Another parent, including `ipsrv` without a row, — `PARENT_MISMATCH`, `field: parentId` (Н1.5). **Template** — by [`unit-templates.tsv`](../../01-cam/data/unit-templates.tsv): the rows with the `vendor` of the address of the row `camera`, `parent` — the template of the parent (`-` at the root), `type` of the body, for `acfa` — `controller`; the address is not `ipint://` — there are no rows. No rows — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`. One — that one. Several — `model` chooses; not named or outside them — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: model`. Intermediate nodes are created one at a time, top down: `backend` does not build the chain itself, a node may need an address of its own; two nodes of one template are distinguished by their id.
  **Number.** Read by a snapshot `cam/unit_next/<строка>` and the rows `cam/device_units` of this row: `n` is the larger of the counter value and the largest number of the live rows; no counter — 0, no live rows — 0: the row could have been created through the tables door bypassing the counter. The counter is not readable — 503: a removed number could return. The batch: `cam/unit_next` with the value `n + 1` and `cas` of the snapshot (`""` if there is no row), `cam/device_units/<строка>-<n + 1>` with `cas ""`, guards of the primary row and of the parent unit. In a group — consecutive numbers. 412 on any of these rows — the batch is built anew with a new read (Н1.4).
  **Edit** — the row whole with `cas` of the snapshot: `name`, `enabled`, `path`; `model`, `parentId` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field`. **Removal** (`DELETE`, an element of `/objects/delete`) — the unit and all nested ones by one batch, each with `cas`; it does not fit into a batch — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`. The right — `admin` on the primary row (`rights.unit_of`, V/01.1 Н1.5); `folderId` — 422 (Н1.7).
- **`channel`, `storage`** have no edits and no `DELETE`: 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`.
- **Children** (`sensor`, `relay`, `mic`, `spk`). There is no creation and no deletion: `POST /devices`, the step `create` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`; `DELETE`, an element of `/objects/delete` — the same. `PATCH` edits `name`, `enabled`, `norm`; the other fields — by `way` of chapter 3. `POST` of the table is a full replacement of the row, therefore `PATCH` merges: it reads the row `cam/child_settings/<id>` by a snapshot, takes its `title`, `enabled`, `norm`, over them — the fields of the body, writes the row whole with `camera`, `type`, `number` from the key, `cas` of the snapshot index or `""`, in one batch with a guard of the row `cam/children/<id>`. The right — `edit` on the owner device (`rights.routes`, V/01.1 Н1.5). A child `gone` or with a dead owner device — 404 `NOT_FOUND`.
- **Cascades of a storage** (the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 14, Claude С-16) — one batch per object:
  - `DELETE /archives/{id}` — «камеры не удаляются»: for a camera whose primary `<cam>` is on this volume, the primary becomes
    the first additional `<cam>-<Y>` by volume name, except volumes `backup` (the primary `<cam>` gets `home: Y` and the fields of that recording; a `replica_from` that named `<cam>` is removed: otherwise the source would be the recording itself; `<cam>-<Y>`
    is removed); if there is no such one — the primary is removed (`recordMode: none`), the recordings on `backup` stay; the additional `<cam>-<том>` on this volume
    are removed; recordings with other names on the volume are removed; the rows of the affected cameras are written (the lock); then the removal of the
    volume row;
  - a batch larger than the limit B9 — a refusal of the console, nothing is written (Н4).
- **Parts limit.** `DEVICE_PARTS_MAX` = 56: streams, recordings and detectors of a device (ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 4). It is not above the limit
  of guards: the guards — the parts, the folder chain up to 7 and the generation guard give 56 + 7 + 1 = 64 (`BATCH_GUARDS`); the writes — the camera, the parts,
  the counter, the row `<h>`, the folder, the membership, the result and the step counters give 1 + 56 + 6 = 63 ≤ 64 (`BATCH_WRITES`), with the row `backend/undo` — 64. The record row of the camera and `auto/next_id` count as two parts. `backend`
  holds it at the creation of a part: a write `POST /devices`, `PATCH /devices` beyond the limit — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field:
  extraArchives` or `parentId`. Parts created bypassing `backend` bypass the limit: such a batch is a 413 of the console, a row of Н4.
- **Folders at deletion.** The console removes a camera and a detector from folders in the same batch (P/06.8 Н3 «удаление единицы дверью»); `backend`
  does not name these rows. The weight is held by Н1.7 `DEVICE_FOLDERS_MAX`.
- **Batch refusal** — of any row: `cas` or a guard (412), rights, the row check, the batch limit — a refusal of the whole batch without
  a write (addendum (9) item 2); the answer is a refusal of the contract (Н4), at 412 — with the current record of the kind. A half-written
  device does not happen.

### Н1.3 Version check

- A write without `If-Match` — 428 `PRECONDITION_REQUIRED` (the contract). The order of checks and the 412 of a foreign record are the same; the snapshot is the domain view (Н1.13).
- After 428 the body is checked against the schema of the contract: an unknown field, a type, `enum` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field`. Then
  the target: absent — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; then the version (below); then the fields by `way` (chapter 3 Н1.1).
- `backend` reads the parts of the record by one snapshot and computes its version (Н1.1); it did not match `If-Match` — 412
  `VERSION_MISMATCH`, `current` is the current record of the kind (the contract). The `If-Match` of a camera is the version of the whole device. It matched —
  the batch (Н1.2): the camera row is written, the parts being written with `cas`, the others as guards, the indices — from the same snapshot (ADR-0094
  addendum (9) item 3). An edit of **any** row of the snapshot, except `cam/devices` and the row `ipsrv` of the host (Н1.1), between the check and the write — 412 of the console on the batch, nothing is written;
  the answer is 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH` with the current record of the kind. There is no skew across parts: the former restriction (addendum (3) item 5)
  is lifted by addendum (9) — `412` means «nothing changed», the related fields of the rows `rec` (`recordMode`, `archiveId`,
  `extraArchives`) do not diverge under simultaneous edits. **Limit:** `412` speaks only of this request. A single
  edit has no key (Н1.4 — groups and steps), a request without a key is not repeated automatically (P/06.1 Н2 «Двери тел и таблиц»): the console's answer is lost —
  502–504, the outcome is unknown, and the client's repeat with the former `If-Match` over its own landed edit gets 412 (`DELETE` — 404,
  `POST /archives` — a taken name). The outcome after a non-answer is learned by a read.

### Н1.4 Repeat memory of the group key — an own table, always

- A group write carries `idempotencyKey` in the body (the contract). `backend` remembers it **always** (not «до B3») and with a memory shared by the
  instances — its own manifest table `backend/repeats/<h>` (the analysis of the V/11 review, question 3; Codex No. 2, 3, 5–7,
  Claude Б-2, С-6–С-9). `h` is the SHA-256 (lowercase hex) of the bytes of `user`, a zero byte and the external `idempotencyKey`
  (`user` is the person of the ticket). The service family `<sub>/idem/` of the console is not it. Reading — the tables door of the console with the ticket
  (V/11.1 Н1.5); a write with `cas`, including `cas ""`, — a table row in `POST /console/batch`: the table `service: true` (P/06.1 Н1.6, ADR-0094 addendum (15) item 3) is read and written only with a live
  ticket from the role leaf `backend`, by the right of consequences; `may` of the person is not checked — the translation of rights gives people no rows on `sub: backend`.
  The row is `{h, user, idem_key, op, body, state, steps}`: `op` — `operationId` of the group (`createDevices`, `updateDevices`, `deleteObjects`, `createOperation`, `updateRules`, `assignServers`), `body` — the SHA-256
  (hex) of the bytes of the request body, `state` — `started` | `done`, `steps` — the results of the steps `[{index, outcome: done | failed, id?,
  code?, field?}]` as JSON text: the field `json` ends at 4000 bytes (P/06.1 Н1.4), a group is longer. The form is the fields of the table `repeats` of the manifest with the field schemas (the platform family of tables — with the schema
  `manifest`, P/06.1 Н1.6); the outcome of an unreadable row — below.
- **Order.** Memory first, then the version:
  1. The row `<h>` is created with `cas ""` — `{state: started, steps: []}` — **before** the snapshot and before the first step.
  2. Creation failed (the row exists) — the row is read. `user` and `idem_key` of the row are not those of the request — 422
     `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: idempotencyKey` (a collision of `h` of different keys); `op` or `body` differ — the same 422
     (the key is taken by another body; the contract has no code «key taken»); `state: done` — the answer from `steps` (below) without calls to
     the parts; `state: started` — the answer upon completion: the repeat continues the same group (below), its answer is from `steps`
     when the row is `done`.
  3. A step: the snapshot and the version check of the element (Н1.3), then the batch of the device (Н1.2), and in **the same batch** — the row `<h>` with
     the result of the step in `steps` (`cas` of its index): the result and the write of the step land together or do not land (platform norm B9: the batch
     carries a row of the table `backend/repeats`). A refusal of the step (nothing is written) — the result `failed` is written separately, with `cas` of the
     row `<h>`.
  4. After the last step — `state: done`, with `cas` of the row.
- **A repeat at `state: started`** — the first request is still running or broke off (`backend` does not see whether it is alive): the repeat goes
  over the same group. Steps with a result in `steps` are not executed again and do not check the version; the others — in order (step 3).
  There may be two executors, and the row `<h>` orders them: every batch of a step carries its `cas`. A creation step (the element has no version)
  answered 412 on a row with a read index — `cam/next_id`, `cam/unit_next`, the folder row and its chain (Н1.7), the camera row of a detector, the primary row and the parent unit of a unit, the row `cam/device_units`, `det/units` with `cas ""` — this is not a result: another request moved the row,
  the step is built anew with a new read of these rows and of the row `<h>` (Н1.2, «Step keys»), and the refusal is then given by the new read: no folder — `NOT_FOUND`. Another 412 — re-read the row `<h>`: the step's result is in it (another executor wrote it) — the step is over, otherwise — the result
  `failed` with a code by Н4. `409 in flight` and `422 key reused` — re-read the row `<h>`; the result is there — the step is over. There is no result: at `422 key reused` the former batch under this key was refused and not written — the result `failed` `VERSION_MISMATCH`; at `409 in flight` another executor took the key — the answer 503 (Н4), `<h>` stays `started`. A done step does not answer 412 to its own write: its result landed with it in one batch.
  A broken-off group thus reaches the end by a repeat.
- **Answer from memory:** `id` of a step is the root record; an element `done` — the record of the kind read now, for `POST /devices`
  `devices` — it and its children; the record is removed or hidden — the element is omitted; for `/objects/delete` — its id; an element `failed` —
  the saved `code` and `field`; the form — `{done, failed}` of the operation.
- **An unreadable row** `<h>` — an element `garbled` or a value not by the fields of the manifest table (required, `enum`,
  `pattern`), as judged by `backend` — an unknown outcome: 503 (the code — chapter 2 Н1.3), neither a repeat nor an execution anew; the row is not
  rewritten.
- **Expiry** — `delete_after` of the table `repeats`, 86400 s, like the repeat memory of the console (P/06.1 Н3): the console counts it from the
  first observation of the row's version, not by store time, therefore the expiry can only lengthen; the reaper removes the row
  (chapter 6.4 Н3 `tables`; ADR-0094 step 3б). No row — the request is the first. The intake of an operation has `OPS_KEEP` (P/06.9 Н1.6) not less than this
  expiry, but the clocks differ, therefore Н1.8 «Memory and the operation» names what happens at the joint.
- **Step keys.** The `Idempotency-Key` of a step's callback is the hex SHA-256 of `h`, a zero byte and the decimal notation of the step number; for a creation step there follow a zero byte and the decimal notation of the read `n` if the batch takes a number, and, each after a zero byte, the index of every read row of the batch outside `<h>` and the counter, in the order of the batch (`cas ""` carries no index) —
  64 characters, one segment, within the console key (200 bytes, P/06.1; the analysis of the V/11 review, Claude С-9). The key is computed from what was read, not from the number of attempts: the `cas` of rows does not enter the fingerprint, a refusal of the door lies in the console memory, only `412 row moved` removes the claim (P/06.1 Н3 «память повторов», «пакет» step 5), therefore a step built anew, and a repeat after the death of the executor with a moved row, go with a new key. A repeat after
  the loss of the answer to the same batch comes with the same key, and the console answers with the saved answer (B3, B9).
- «Без своего состояния» of the worker (ADR-0094 A3) reads thus: without state outside the platform store. The worker process
  keeps no memory of its own between requests, except the cache of snapshots of domain tables (chapter 3 Н1.2а): it only speeds things up; if it is lost — a full read; the outcome of a request does not depend on it.

### Н1.5 Group write — per element

- `POST /devices` (the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 5, Claude С-6): an element of `items` `{device, domainId, parentId, folderId}` is the
  creation of a device with its children by one batch (Н1.2), in the cluster of Н1.13 «The cluster of a new record»; `domainId` — `Domain.id` (Н1.6; another one — `NOT_FOUND`), `parentId` of a camera
  — `null` or the id of the `ipsrv` of its host, of a detector — the camera id, of a unit — a primary row or a unit (Н1.2), of `acfa` — `null` or the same, of the others — `null`; anything else — `failed` `PARENT_MISMATCH`, `field: parentId`; `folderId` not `null` — a folder by Н1.7, in a domain — by a separate step after the device; `candidateId` — the find drops out of the search result (Н1.9); a kind outside
  `deviceTypes` — `failed` `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`; a taken `identifier` — `failed` `IDENTIFIER_TAKEN`, `field: identifier` (Н1.2). The result is `{done: [{index, devices, folders}], failed:
  [{index, code, field?}]}`: `folders` — the folder `folderId` after the write, otherwise `[]`.
- `POST /devices/update`: elements up to `selectMax`, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: items` (ADR-0113 addendum (10) item 1: beyond 500 — by several operations); every element `{deviceId, version, set}` is a separate edit of a record, its own batch per device
  (Н1.2, Н1.3 with `version` of the element instead of `If-Match`; addendum (9) item 3); the result is `{done: [Device…], failed: [{deviceId,
  code, field?}…]}`.
- `POST /objects/delete`: elements up to `selectMax`, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: items`. Every element `{id, version}` is the deletion of a record of its kind with a version check: `Device.version` or `r<revision>` of the row `DeviceRow` of a camera-cluster, both forms; `r<revision>` is checked against the `revision` of the view row and only for a camera-cluster, for the others — `failed` `VERSION_MISMATCH`; removal — Н1.13; the kind — by the
  UUID name (Н1.6: `Archive` and `Volume` of one row are distinguished by id), a rule — Н1.10, the cascade — as for a single `DELETE` (Н1.2), a batch per
  object; a folder — by the folder removal door with `If-Match` (Н1.7) and the step key (Н1.4), the result is stored in `steps` by a separate write, `cas` of the row `<h>`, like `failed`: a break between them — the repeat gets the saved 200, the result `done`; an id of a `Volume` — `failed` `NOT_SUPPORTED`: a volume is not removed separately (chapter 2 No. 36); a detector of a camera removed earlier in the same body — `done`. The result is `{done: [id…], failed: [{id, code}…]}`. Servers and domains are not deleted this way (the contract).
- Elements are executed in the order of the body; a refusal of an element does not stop the following ones; partial success is per element,
  an element in `failed` is written with nothing, except a foreign one with `kept` (Н1.13). Memory — Н1.4.
- A non-answer of a step (502–504, a break) is not a result: `failed` is not written, the group stops, the answer — Н4, `<h>` stays
  `started`; the repeat goes with the same step key (Н1.4).

### Н1.6 Id of a record of a kind

- The ids of contract records are UUIDs. `backend` derives the UUID deterministically — a UUID of version 5 (RFC 9562, SHA-1) over
  the namespace of the lesson; the name is the UTF-8 bytes of the string `<вид>:<кластер>/<место>` (the analysis of the V/11 review, question 10; ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 4): one record through
  any `backend` gets one id, therefore the cluster stands in the name of own records too. `<кластер>` is the `CLUSTER` of the member whose row it is: for an own one — its own, for a foreign one —
  the field `cluster` of the domain view. For a record from a store row `<место>` is the key of the row `<sub>/<rows>/<id>` or `<sub>/<таблица>/<имя>` as it is written, without normalisation
  (case, Unicode, `/` — as is; ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 10 (7)); `<вид>` — `device`, `archive`, `volume`, `candidate`, `rule`, `ruleVersion`; for records
  without a store row — the listing of chapter 3 Н1.2 (`server:`, `event:`, `ruleFire:`). `Archive` and `Volume` of one
  row `rec/volumes/<name>` get different ids.
- Domain records are one per domain, there is no cluster in the name (the listing of chapter 3 Н1.2): `folder:`, `user:`, the keys of roles and groups; likewise the permanent `schedule:`, `mode:`.
- **`Domain`.** The name is `domain:<root>`, `root` of the field `domain` of the answer to `GET /session`: a permanent sign of the domain; the id does not depend on the entry, the view or the connection. No field — the cluster is outside a domain, an own record, `domain:<CLUSTER>`. `null` — the domain is not read: `Domain` is not derived, 503 (chapter 3 Н1.2а). 404 `no domain here` at `GET /domain` with the field `domain` present — the view is not ready yet, only the own, the id is the former one. When the cluster enters a domain, `Domain.id` and `domainId` of all records change once.
- **Part in the id** (ADR-0113 addendum (22)): every record `device:` of every member, `class: camera`, `class: server` and the own cluster, the children of a camera — the bits of its member. The id is a UUID v5 by the rule above, then its first 12 bits are replaced with the first 12 bits of the SHA-256 of the UTF-8 bytes of the member name: the member's part (P/08.3 Н1.1). The UUID form is the same, the version and the variant are in place, the contract does not change. Records of other kinds — without replacement.
- The way back. `device:`: the bits are the part of the own cluster, the record is own — by the cluster doors (a deleted one — by the camera tombstones); otherwise one row of the part and the tables of only this member (chapter 3 Н1.2б «Запись по id»), not a pass over the servers. Other kinds — by the listing of the rows of the kind: own ones — by the cluster doors, foreign ones — by the domain view. A cluster outside a domain — a foreign record is not found; no connection — 503 (chapter 3 Н1.2а).
- References (`parentId`, `archiveId`, `serverId`, `domainId`) — by the same rule: the cluster of a reference is the cluster of the row it points to; a field of a row names a key in the cluster
  of the row itself, unless the kind says otherwise. The namespace is the lesson constant `BACKEND_NS` = `4ffc0b08-e8bd-4a4e-b7fb-d4ecc8a83ecb`.

### Н1.7 Folders

A folder is a domain row; a member has a read-only copy of it (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 3; P/08.5 Н1.8, P/06.8 Н1.2а). Outside a domain (`GET /session` has no field `domain`, Н1.6) it is a record of the cluster console. Membership is the platform's concern: `backend` translates the form and keeps only the rule of hidden members (ADR-0096 §7).

- **Reading outside a domain.** `Folder` is an element of `GET /folders`, `GET /folders/<id>`: `id` — UUID v5 `folder:platform/folders/<id>`, `domainId` — `Domain.id`
  (Н1.6), `parentId` — the id of the parent or `null` at `parent: ""`, `version` — `index` of the row. The record has no composition: `GET /devices?folderId=` (chapter 3 Н1.2б).
  The way back — by the listing `GET /folders`. An element `{id, garbled: true}` is not given out, and a read of this folder is 503.
- **Reading in a domain.** The tree — `GET /domain/folders`; the fields — as above, `parent` and `name` from the tree, `version` — `index` of the tree.
- **Reading without connection** — the domain doors do not answer (502–504, a break): the tree and the composition from the copy, `index` of the tree in `version` is empty. A change of `version` at a break and at the return of connection is a restriction.
- **A visible member** — `cam/cameras/<id>` with empty `main`, `det/units/<id>` from the reader's listing of units; a row `type: ipsrv` is visible as the `ipsrv` of its host, of two on a host — the smallest id. Children, the rows `cam/device_units`, `channel`, `storage`, an `ipsrv` without a row are not units and are never members (P/06.8 Н1.1): such a record in `add`, as the target of a step `folderIds`, in `folderId` of its creation — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` of that field. An `ipsrv` is visible if its row is visible, or a camera of its host, or a child on its row (ADR-0102 addendum (6) item 6); a channel is visible together with it. A member outside the listing the translation counts as hidden: there is no right or it was
  deleted bypassing the door; by a filtered listing the two cannot be told apart, and the contract says not to reveal the invisible (ADR-0096 addendum (1)). The folder door gives out the members as they are; the platform's reading
  does not change.
- **Creation** — `POST /folders`, in a domain `POST /domain/folders`, `{name, parent}`: `domainId` another — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; `name` empty after trimming the edge spaces — 422 `NAME_REQUIRED`. The answer in a domain — by a read of the tree.
- **Edit** (`PATCH`, only `name`; `If-Match` — 428 without it) outside a domain — `PUT /folders/<id>` with `If-Match: "<version>"`, the fields `name`, `members` as read; `412 folder moved` — 412
  `VERSION_MISMATCH`, `current` from `row` of the answer.
- **Edit in a domain.** `name` — `PUT /domain/folders/<id>` `{name}` with `If-Match: "<version>"`; `412 revision moved` — as `folder moved`.
- **Composition — `POST /folders/{id}/devices {add, remove}`** (ADR-0112 addendum (10) item 4): together up to `selectMax`, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: add`; no folder — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; an id outside the listing of the kind — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: add` or `remove`, without a call to the door; a device under a group operation — 409 `BUSY`, `params.targetId`. Outside a domain — `PUT /folders/<id>`, `If-Match` and `members` — a read before the write, `add` at the end, `remove` taken out, hidden ones in their places (below); `412 folder moved` — a read and a repeat, the second time — 409 `BUSY`: the contract door does not know 412. In a domain — for every unit, in the order of the body, `PUT /domain/memberof/<кластер>/<ссылка>` `{folders}`, `If-Match: "<index>"`, `folders` and `index` — from `GET /domain/memberof/<кластер>/<ссылка>`, plus or minus this folder; the cluster — that of the unit's record, a foreign one too (Н1.6). There is no pair «folder + membership» (P/08.5 Н1.8): the write is not one, a refusal stops, what was written stays, the answer is a refusal of Н4; a unit already standing as asked gives no write, therefore a repeat is safe. There is no order in a domain. The answer — `Folder`. It does not go onto the undo stack (Н1.12).
- **Deletion in a domain** — `DELETE /domain/folders/<id>` with `If-Match` by the rule of the edit; the devices stay.
- **The tree is edited by the domain administrator** (P/08.5 Н1.8 `rights`; a grant row of the cluster does not give this): creation, `name`, deletion by a cluster administrator — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `params: {scope: "domain"}`. The composition he does edit: by the right on the unit and on the folder in his cluster. Showing on reading — chapter 8 Н1.10.
- **Without connection** a folder and a membership are not edited (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 3): the domain doors do not answer (502–504, a break) — 409 `SERVER_OFFLINE`, nothing is written.
- **Hidden members** outside a domain are kept: `members` — the visible ones in the order of the composition, and each hidden member stands right after the nearest visible one
  **before it** in the former list that remained, in the former order of the hidden ones; there is no such one — at the beginning of the list. The composition is read from the same
  version as `If-Match`, therefore a member added bypassing `backend` is not lost: the write answers 412. A change of the folder's `version` from an edit
  of a hidden member is a restriction: a new version without a visible change.
- **A chain refusal** — 404 `NOT_FOUND` (ADR-0096 §7): there is no ancestor, a tombstone, a 412 of a chain guard. The contract has no 409 and 412 at `POST /folders`.
- **`folderId` of creation in a domain** (ADR-0111 item 13) — two steps: before the write the folder is looked up in the tree, then the batch of the device without folder rows (`400 badkey`, P/06.8 Н1.2а), then `PUT /domain/memberof/<кластер>/<ссылка>` `{folders: [<папка>]}`, the cluster — that of the device record. The domain doors do not answer before the write — `failed` `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: folderId`, there is no device; no folder — `failed` `NOT_FOUND`, `field: folderId`. A refusal of membership after the batch — the device is created, `done` with `folders: []`, the reason goes into the worker log.
- **The step `folderIds` in a domain** — `PUT /domain/memberof/<кластер цели>/<ссылка>` `{folders}`, the list of the body whole; no read of the former one is needed, therefore it works for a foreign target too; `Idempotency-Key` — `step_key(id, i)`, then the batch of the result (Н1.8). The target is judged by the holder's guard (P/08.5 Н1.8 `member`). The domain doors do not answer — the result `fail` `SERVER_OFFLINE`.
- **`folderId` of creation and the step `folderIds` outside a domain** — a batch B9 with the rows of folders and membership (P/06.8 Н2 «Строки папок в пакетной двери»): the new member
  at the end; `cas` — the index of the folder row, the membership row from `GET /folders?member=` (`member.index`), guards — the chain of ancestors of each added
  folder and the row of the unit if it is not in `writes`. The folder row or the chain was moved — the creation step is built anew (Н1.4). The order of a device's folders is not stored (ADR-0096, «Запрос продукту» item 1).
- **Cascade weight.** `DEVICE_FOLDERS_MAX` = 15: distinct folders of a camera and its detectors together (the decisions of the rights norms, item 18). Deletion of a device
  writes the row of every such folder; `FOLDER_ROW_MAX` × 15 with the keys of the parts fits into the batch body `-max-body` 1 MiB (P/06.8 Н1.3).
  `backend` holds the limit at `folderId`, at the step `folderIds` and at `POST /folders/{id}/devices` by the listing `GET /folders`: beyond it — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`,
  `field: folderId`, `folderIds` or `add`. Two simultaneous writes and writes bypassing `backend` bypass the limit: deletion of such a
  device — 413, a row of Н4, nothing is written; remove it from folders and repeat.

### Н1.8 Group operation

The console runs an operation (P/06.9); `backend` translates and writes a step. The operations table is `service: true`: its doors — only by the ticket; the translation gives people no rows of
`sub: backend`; a foreign operation — 404 `NOT_FOUND` (the decisions of the rights norms, items 21, 22).

- **Id.** The `id` of an operation is 128 random bits, named by `backend`; in the contract — the same bits in the UUID form 8-4-4-4-12. The way back —
  removing the hyphens.
- **Intake** `POST /operations`, in order (ADR-0095 §2):
  1. Memory first: the row `<h>` with `op: createOperation` is created as «started» with `cas ""`, as in Н1.4. The row exists: foreign `user`, key, `op`, `body` —
     422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: idempotencyKey`; `state: done` — 202 with the same operation (`operation` of the row); `state: started` — the intake
     goes anew over the same row; the race is decided by the `cas` of the memory in the intake batch.
  2. The targets are read by a snapshot (Н1.3) and checked against `expectedVersions`: a mismatch of even one — 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`, nothing is created. A step
     `set` or `folderIds` without the target's version in `expectedVersions` — 428 `PRECONDITION_REQUIRED` (the reading of ADR-0095 §2; «Запрос продукту» item 1).
     Before the snapshot of the targets: a step without exactly one of `set`, `folderIds`, `create`, an empty `items` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: items`.
  3. `POST /backend/operations` `{id, items, memory}`: `items[]` — `{body, root, expected, secret?}`; `body` — the element of the request without the secret;
     `root` — the root; `expected` — `{"versions": {<id объекта корня>: <версия>}}` over the targets of this root; `memory` — `{key: backend/repeats/<h>, fields:
     <строка с state: done и operation>, cas}`. The answer — 202 `Operation`, no results: `state` of the steps — by P/06.9 Н2 (the first `run`, the others `wait`).
  4. `409 root busy` — 409 `BUSY`; `412 row moved` with `field: /memory/cas` — another executor accepted it: re-read the row and answer as for `state: done`.
  The target is not found — 404 `NOT_FOUND`, nothing is created: the root cannot be computed without it.
- **Root** (ADR-0095 §1): `<cam>` — a camera and a row `eventsrc`, `acfa`; a camera and its detectors have one root; `v-<name>` — a volume row; `Archive` and `Volume` of one row have one;
  `ipsrv-<хост>` — `ipsrv`, `channel` and `storage`; `child-<id>` — a child; a unit — `<cam>` of its row; for a step over a server and a domain — `<вид>-<id>`. The rows of the root: for `<cam>` — the camera parts, for a row `eventsrc`, `acfa` — the row itself; for `ipsrv-<хост>` — the row `type: ipsrv` of the host, if absent — the map is empty; for `child-<id>` — `cam/children/<id>` and `cam/child_settings/<id>`. Occupancy and the version chain go by the root.
- **A foreign target** — Н1.13 «A step of an operation». The memory, the operation and the undo stack are at the `backend` of the entry.
- **Occupied objects** (ADR-0111 item 20, replaces in this part ADR-0095 «`BUSY` проверяют только приём»). While an operation is not finished, other requests do not change its objects either. The console door does not judge this (P/06.9 Н1.3); `backend` judges it: a batch of `PATCH`, `DELETE` of a device, a storage, a volume, a stream (Н1.15), of an element of `POST /devices/update`, `POST /objects/delete` goes through the batch door and carries for every touched root a guard `{key: backend/opbusy/<корень>, index: ""}`; for a cascade — the roots of all rows being removed, for `DELETE` of a storage — also the roots of the cameras with recordings on it, for `DELETE` of a detector — the roots of the cameras that record by it. `412 row moved` with the key of an occupancy row — 409 `BUSY`, `params: {targetId}` — the object of this root; at the batch doors — the element `failed` with the code `BUSY`, the other elements go on. The batch of a step does not carry this guard: the step goes by its own root. **Limit:** the folder doors (P/06.8) take no guard, therefore the composition and deletion of a folder under an operation do not refuse.
- **Step.** The console calls `POST /opstep` `{id, index, body, secret?, expected, opcount}` with `X-W2C-Pass-Ticket` by the declared route (P/06.9 Н2 «Вызов шага»);
  the console does not read the answer body, therefore the answer is 204 at any outcome: the outcome is the result row.
  1. **Check.** `expected` comes in two forms. `{"versions": …}` — the first step over the root: contract versions, the check as in Н1.3. `{"rows": {<ключ строки>: <revision>}}` —
     the root chain: a step after a successful step; the map of all live rows of the root MUST be equal to the map of the snapshot. A contract version cannot be put into the chain: the index
     of a batch is unknown when the batch is assembled (P/06.1 «пакет»), while the `revision` of a written row is predictable (the decisions file, the analysis of the V/11 review, item 1).
  2. `set` — as an element of `POST /devices/update` (Н1.5): a snapshot, a batch B9 with `cas` of the snapshot and guards of the parts; `create` — as an element of `POST /devices`, the parent is
     `targetId`; `folderIds` — Н1.7: the composition of the target's folders becomes the list of the body, the camera record does not change; outside a domain the row of the target itself goes as a guard (P/06.8 Н3 step 7);
     a batch `folderIds` has no room for guards of the parts (P/06.8 Н1.3).
  3. In the same batch — the row `backend/opdone/<id>/<i>` with `cas ""` and `backend/opcount/<id>` with `cas` — the value `opcount` of the body of the step call (P/06.9 Н2). `versions` of the result is the map `{"rows": …}`
     of the root rows after the step: for a written row `revision` + 1, for a new one 1, over a tombstone — `revision` of the tombstone + 1 (`?deleted=1`), a removed one is absent, the others from the snapshot. `version` of the result is the fingerprint of this map: the SHA-256 hex of the canonical JSON of its value (the index of a batch is unknown when it is assembled, item 1); it does not go outward; `created_id` — the id of the created one.
     `Idempotency-Key` — `step_key(id, i)` (P/06.9 Н2). The `revision` of a table row removed and created anew may return (P/06.1 Н2 «Версии»): a foreign removal and creation
     of the same row inside the chain is not caught by the check; this is a restriction.
  4. A refusal without a write (the version is wrong, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `PERMISSION_DENIED`) — a batch of the result `fail` with the contract code and `field` and of the counters, **without** a key: `step_key` is taken by the answer
     of the write batch, and a double result is cut off by `cas ""` (the decisions file, the analysis of the V/11 review, item 2).
  5. `412 row moved` — re-read the result: present — the step is over, absent — the result `fail`, the code by Н4 (P/06.9 Н2). The step is not built anew: the limit — `create` has no version, and `VERSION_MISMATCH` here means «the row moved, repeat» (`retry-failed`). `412 leader moved` — finish the call, do not write a result: this answer does not go into the memory of
     `step_key`, it is not an outcome of the step (ibid.). The batch landed, and the new call has another `opcount`: `cas` is outside the fingerprint of the batch (P/06.1 Н3 «память повторов»), therefore the same row values give the saved answer, the step landed; other values of `opcount` — `422 key reused`: finish the call, do not write a result, the console reads the result (P/06.9 Н3 «Гонки»), there is no second count. 5xx, a break, 401, `409 in flight` — finish the call, do not write a result (P/06.9 Н3 «шаг» item 8); other 4xx — item 4, the code by Н4.
  6. `set` with `DomainPatch` — the result `fail` `NOT_SUPPORTED`: this edit is `NO_ROUTE` here (chapter 2 No. 6). `set` with `ServerPatch` — Н1.19, the result by a separate write, it does not go onto the stack; a field other than `labels` — the result `fail` `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- **Reading** `GET /operations/{id}`: `GET /backend/operations/<id>`; `createdAt`, `finishedAt` — seconds in RFC 3339 UTC; `retryOf` — `retry_of` in UUID form;
  `revertOf`, `redoOf`, `single`, `revertible`, `redoable` — Н1.12; `items[i]` — `{index: i, targetId, state, code, version?, createdId?, params?}`, `targetId` — from `items[i].body` of the answer (P/06.9 Н2, at
  `service: true`), `code` — `code` of the result or `null`. `version` — only for a finished operation and only for the last step `ok` of its root: `backend` reads the target by a snapshot (Н1.1) and computes the fingerprint of the map of the root rows; equal to `version` of the result — it is the current version of the target, otherwise there is no field: the target changed after the step, and the contract version at the moment of the step is recorded nowhere. `not_run` — `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` (chapter 2 Н1.3). `params` — for `BUSY` of an incompatible detector and for `fail` of a folder revert: a step result carries no detail fields (P/06.9 Н1.2), therefore `field` of the result is `incompatible:<id детектора>` or `folders` (Н1.12), the read gives `params: {reason: incompatible, with}` or `{reason: folders}`; `field` does not go outward.
- **Control:** `cancel`, `retry-failed` — console doors; `pause`, `resume` are not translated (chapter 2 No. 40, 41); `revert`, `redo` — Н1.12; `409 operation done`, `409 operation running`, `409 nothing to retry` — 409 `BUSY`;
  at `retry-failed` `backend` names a new `id`, there is no key memory: the contract has no key (the stand exception — chapter 6 Н1.3). `cancel` is executed by the leader on its tick, and the contract gives out a finished record: after the door `backend` re-reads the operation until `state: done`, within the timeout of the route `api/operations`; the timeout ran out — the answer with the `state` read.
- **Memory and the operation.** The numbers are equal, 86400 s, the clocks differ: `OPS_KEEP` is counted from `finished_at`, the expiry of the row `<h>` — from the first
  observation of its version (Н1.4). The memory can outlive the operation only by the observation delay and the reaper period, if the operation ended
  at once: a repeat of the key then gets 404 `NOT_FOUND` from the operation read, a day later, while the contract promises 10 minutes. The operation can outlive the memory: it runs up to
  `OPS_RUN_MAX` and another `OPS_KEEP`: a repeat of the key after the memory's expiry goes as a new intake and gets 409 `BUSY` or 412 by versions. A step that changes
  a version is not executed twice in either case; an operation of only `folderIds` does not change versions: such a repeat is a new operation with the same result.

### Н1.9 Device search and the model query

The search and the query are executed by the base worker `cam` on every server of the entry cluster ([V/01.8](../../01-cam/spec/01.8-cam.norms.md)): `backend` asks and assembles the result. The domain of the path is `Domain.id` (Н1.6),
another `domainId` — 404 `NOT_FOUND`. The request does not name a server: the search goes on all servers of the entry cluster and sees their subnets; a device on the network of another cluster is found by entering through that cluster (ADR-0111 item 9). One search per entry. Rights — the tables doors (P/06.1 Н1.6): read `view`, write `admin`, refusal — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`.

- **`GET`.** The row `cam/searches/<CLUSTER>` and the table `cam/found` are read:

| Search row | `state` | `progress` | `startedAt` | `finishedAt` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | `idle` | 0 | `null` | `null` |
| `stopped_at` present | `stopped` | the share at `stopped_at` | `started_at` | `stopped_at` |
| now ≥ `until` | `done` | 100 | `started_at` | `until` |
| otherwise | `searching` | the share now | `started_at` | `null` |

  The share is the integer part of 100 · (t − `started_at`) / (`until` − `started_at`), not less than 0 and not more than 99.
- **The search row is unreadable** — at all three doors 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` (chapter 2 Н1.3): this is neither `idle` nor a free search.
- **`candidates`** — the rows `cam/found` with the `search` of the search row, ordered by `found_at`, then `h`. Not given out: a row that is unreadable (a log line); a find whose `ip`
  equals the `ip` of a camera or an `ipsrv` in the reader's `GET /devices`: the device is registered and is not counted as new.
- **Candidate.** `id` — UUID v5 `candidate:<кластер>/cam/found/<h>` (Н1.6): the same from search to search while the address and the MAC are the same. `type` — `camera`: the driver does not tell the kind. `name` — `<vendor> <model>`: the
  driver does not tell a name. `mac` — the field of the row; if it is absent — an empty string. `vendor`, `model`, `ip`, `port`, `support` — fields of the row (V/01.8 Н1.2). `login`, `password` are not given out: the search does not guess them, the operator enters the password, `support: ok` means «a driver exists», not «logged in».
- **`POST`.** The row says `searching` — 409 `BUSY`. Otherwise — one table row in `POST /console/batch`, `cas` of the row read, no row — `cas ""`: a new `search` (32 hex, random),
  `started_at` — now, `until` — plus `SEARCH_TIME` (V/01.8 Н1.1). 412 `row moved` — another search was started earlier: 409 `BUSY`. The answer — 202 `Discovery`: `searching`, `progress` 0, `candidates`
  empty; the former result is replaced, the finds come as a stream after the answer (chapter 5 Н1.2).
- **`DELETE`.** The row says `searching` — the same write with `stopped_at` = now; 412 — re-read and judge anew. Otherwise — without a write. The answer — 200 `Discovery`; what was found stays.
- **`candidateId`** in `POST /devices`: the device is created by the usual batch (Н1.5); with a `serverId` of another cluster — by a bundle; the candidate stays own. After the batch the row `cam/found` of this `id` is removed by `DELETE` with `cas`; no row or a refusal — the result
  of the creation is the same, a log line. The removal does not enter the batch: a worker table in a batch is 403 (P/06.1 Н2).
- **`POST /devices/probe`.** The body by the schema of the contract, otherwise 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field`. `POST /cam/probes`: a new `id`, the fields of the body, `until` — now + `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` (V/01.8 Н1.3). Then polling `GET /cam/probe_replies`:
  the row of this query with `model` — `{model}`; all connected servers of `GET /servers` answered without `model`, or `until` has passed — `{model: null}`. A row that is unreadable does not count as an answer. `login`, `password` are accepted and go no further than the body: the row `cam/probes` is shared, and the search does not keep credentials (V/01.8). The driver gets empty ones, the answer may be `model: null`, the contract allows this. **Limit** of V/01.8 Н1.3. A repeat is a new query.

### Н1.10 Rules

A rule is the unit row `auto/scenarios/<id>` and its marks `auto/rule_marks/<id>` (`mute`, `order`); the number is issued by the counter `auto/next_id` (ADR-0105 item 1). The version check — Н1.3: the version is the concatenation of two indices (chapter 3 Н1.3), `If-Match` is required, 412 — with the current record. A batch writing one of the rows guards the other. `backend` checks a rule at the write; the evaluator judges the same by its pass (V/07.3), and this judgement is not replaced. The write goes by a batch B9 with the ticket: the right — the doors of rows and tables; `names` of the manifest judges everything the rule names (V/07.1 Н1.4); refusal — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `params.target` `{kind, id}` — the unit from `detail` of the door, the id — chapter 3 Н1.2, nothing is written.

**Limit:** at load the form also calls `/rules/{id}/fires`, `/versions` for every rule: N requests in a row; a refusal of the log on a non-empty installation breaks the start. **Reading at start.** `GET /rules`, `/schedules`, `/day-groups`, `/modes`, `/modes/current`, `/maintenance-windows` are declared, and the WebUI calls them at load, a refusal breaks the start, therefore an empty installation answers them with an ordinary 200: no rows — an empty list and the permanent records of chapter 3 Н1.3; they do not need a live evaluator. A refusal `403` of a tables door — the same: the part is not visible, there is no refusal (Н1.1). `404` of the door `auto` — the subsystem is not installed — the same too. Any other refusal — as a read of any part.

- **List** — the rows without `hidden` and expressible in `Rule` (chapter 3 Н1.3), ordered by `order` of the mark, then by id as a number. `garbled` is not given out; a read of such a row — 503, like an unreadable folder (Н1.7).
- **Check** (`RULE_INVALID`). `name` empty after trimming the edge spaces — 422 `NAME_REQUIRED`, `field: name`, at any `enabled`. A disabled rule is stored unfinished; an undeclared step in it is data. For an enabled one — the first error in the order below: 422 `RULE_INVALID`, `params.issue` — `{code, params}`:

| Check | `issue.code`, `params` |
|---|---|
| `start: event`: no triggers; more than one and `windowSec` 0; one and `windowSec` not 0 | `rule.noTrigger`, `rule.noWindow`, `rule.extraWindow` |
| `start: event`: a trigger without a target; a target area without a device of the kind; `fromEvent` on a trigger, on the event of `waitEvent` | `trigger.noTarget`, `trigger.areaEmpty`, `{kind}`, `trigger.fromEvent`, `control.fromEvent` |
| `start: schedule`: no `runAt`, days, time | `rule.noRunAt` |
| a condition `mode` without modes; for a rule of a foreign cluster: the modes of the entry cluster; `output` with `pulse` without `pulseMs` | `cond.noMode`; `cond.modeOther`; `action.noPulse` |
| a condition `time`: no schedule; the schedule is empty | `cond.scheduleGone`; `cond.scheduleEmpty`, `{scheduleId}` |
| a condition with a device: no target; the area is empty | `cond.noTarget`, `cond.areaEmpty`, `{kind}` |
| not a single action in the whole tree | `rule.noAction` |

| `wait`, `waitEvent`, `ask`: `sec` outside 1…3600; `waitEvent` has no target, the area is empty; `ask` has an empty `text`, an unknown substitution, no `roleId` | `control.sec`; `control.noTarget`, `control.areaEmpty`, `{control}`; `control.noText`; `control.unknownVar`, `{name}`; `control.noRole`, `{control}` |

| `runrule`: itself; no such rule; `allowChain: false` | `chain.self`, `chain.ruleGone`, `chain.forbidden`, `{ruleId}` |

| a target with `gone` or with an `id` without a live record | `rule.targetGone` |

  The codes are the same as the WebUI hint. **Catalogue** (chapter 2 Н1.5): an enabled one outside it — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` — the section `start`, `triggers`, `conditions`, `steps`, `params.issue` — `rule.startNotDeclared`, `trigger.notDeclared`, `cond.notDeclared`, `control.notDeclared`, `action.notDeclared`, `action.fieldNotDeclared`, `target.notDeclared`; in `/rules/update` — the element `failed` with this code; a run of such a rule — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`, `params.reason.code` — the code. `runAsRoleId` not a domain role — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: runAsRoleId`; a step to which the role gave no right — `fail`, `role.denied` (V/07.4 Н1.9). `windowSec` greater than 3600 — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: windowSec`: the bound of `within` (V/07.1). - **Rule cluster** (ADR-0111 items 8, 10). - **`PUT /rules/{id}`** — a batch: the row with `cas` of the snapshot, the fields outside the content — from the snapshot, `changed_*` new; the version row: `number` — the largest + 1, `note` — `historyNote`, without `params` — `{}`, absent — `null`. `historyNote.code: undo` with `params.number` equal to the last version: its row is removed in the same batch, `changed_*`, `log_only_since` — from its `was`; the version of the record is new anyway.
- **`GET /rules/{id}/versions`** — the rows `auto/versions` of the scenario by `number`. The rows live as long as the rule lives: there is no expiry. A foreign rule — chapter 3 Н1.2а «Сверх вида».
- **`GET /rules/{id}/fires`** — `GET /events?subsystem=auto&unit=auto/<id>`, newest first (chapter 3 Н1.3). A foreign rule — ibid.
- **`POST /rules/update`** — a group write (Н1.4, Н1.5): an element `{ruleId, version, set, historyNote?}`, a batch per rule. Absent — `NOT_FOUND`, the version — `VERSION_MISMATCH`, enabling with an error — `RULE_INVALID` without `params`: the element has none. `log_only_since` — today by `TIMEZONE` when the rule enters «log only», `null` — when it leaves it. `mute` — `{since: сейчас, until, by, reason}` in the mark row, `null` removes it; the scenario row is not written then. A change of the state word (`off`, `watch`, `on`) and `historyNote` — a version row, `note: {code: state, params: {mode}}`; muting writes no version. `changed_*` is changed only by the state. A value that is already there does not go into the write; there is nothing to write — there is no row, `revision` is the same, the run is not removed.
- **`POST /rules/reorder`** — one batch: only the rows `auto/rule_marks` with `cas` of the snapshot, the scenario rows — guards; a rule already at its `order` is not written, the version of the others changes. Absent — 404; the version is wrong, a 412 of the batch — 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`, `current`, `params.ruleId`, nothing is written. More than `BATCH_WRITES` changed rows or `BATCH_GUARDS` guards — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: items`: all or nothing is stronger than completeness.
- **`POST /rules/{id}/run`, `/fires/{fireId}/retry`** (ADR-0105 item 10) — the requests `run`, `retry` to the scenario evaluator (V/07.4 Н1.12), `POST /auto/requests` with the ticket: filing only; the right — `need` of the requests door, the person of the ticket is judged (P/06.3 Н1.9). No row, `hidden`, not expressible in `Rule` — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; `fireId` not among the firings of this rule's log — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; `enabled: false` — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`, `params.reason: {code: off}`, the request is not filed: the evaluator is not obliged to hold a disabled rule. The body — `{unit, action: run}`, `{unit, action: retry, fire}`, `fire` — `firing`; `Idempotency-Key` — `idempotencyKey` of the body. The door answers 202 while there is no outcome: the filing is repeated with the same key until 200 with a label, within the timeout of the route `api/rules`. The outcome of the label: `performed` — `reply.firing`, the log is read up to the row `fired` of this firing, the answer 201 `RuleFire`; `refused`, `expired`, `gone` — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`, `params.reason.code` — `error` of the label (`off`, `logOnly`, `refused`, `nothing`, `unread`), without it — the outcome word. `unknown`, the timeout ran out without a label or without a log row — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`: the run may have started, on a wide target `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` runs out before the end, the outcome is unknown, a repeat with the same key is the same run. `409 exists`, `409 spent` — the key is taken by another body: 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: idempotencyKey`.
- **Operator's answer** (V/07.6 Н1.9). `GET /rule-prompts` — chapter 3 Н1.3 «`RulePrompt`», reading `GET /auto/scenarios`, `GET /session`. `POST /rule-prompts/{promptId}/answer`, in order: the question among the open ones; if absent — among the steps of the runs `runs` and the names `done` of the scenario: the id is one-way, only this walk names the firing. Found: the log of this firing carries the end of the step by an answer, `note` `ask.answer` or `label` `esc.ack` (V/07.4 Н1.6), — 409 `BUSY`, `params.reason: answered`; does not carry it — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`, `params.reason.code: over`; the log did not answer — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`. Not found — 404 `NOT_FOUND`, the log is not searched: `done` lives one question back; a question that ended later — 404, a limit. A run removed by a new `revision`, by disabling, by deletion — also 404, a limit: `state` is removed, `runs`, `done` are not in force; a removed `restart` lying in `done` — 409. **Limit:** the end row lands after the write of the step (V/07.4 Н1.11), the log answer may be truncated (`truncated`): there an answered question gives `over`. For an open one: no role (`canAnswer`) — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, this is the door; the evaluator judges last; an answer of the wrong kind — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: answer`; `until` has passed — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`, `over`. The write — the row `auto/answers/<scenario>-<fire>-<step>-<user>` in `POST /console/batch`, `cas ""`, `user` — the person of the ticket, and he is the one judged: `answer` — `yes`, `no`, `ack` ↔ `ack:<level вопроса>`. The row is taken — 409 `BUSY`, `params.reason: answered`, including one's own rejected answer: a person answers once (V/07.6 Н1.9). The answer — 202 without a body: the evaluator decides on the credited answer by its pass; the outcome is visible in the firing (V/07.6 Н1.9).
- **`POST /rules/test`** — `POST /auto/test/<id>` with the ticket (ADR-0105 item 7): `ruleId` not `null` — its number, no row — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; `null` — the smallest number of the listing `GET /auto/scenarios` whose row is held by a live worker (V/07.4). The body — `{scenario, rule, events, at, branches, log}`, `rule` — the draft in the fields of the row; the checks of Н1.10 do not run; `log` without `ruleId` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: log`; `bad_branches` of the door — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: branches`. `RuleFire.id` — from `firing` of the door's answer (present at `log: true`), otherwise a random UUID; `ruleId` — as in the body. No holder or no rows — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`: there is nobody to try it. The right — `view` on the row (P/06.3 Н1.9).
- **Deletion** — an element of `POST /objects/delete`: removal of the row, the mark, its rows `auto/versions` and its own schedules (`owner.kind: rule`) if they are not used anywhere else (Н1.11), by one batch; it does not fit — the versions by their own batches, the row last. The run log stays.

**Device cascade** (ADR-0105, the contract). After the batch of removal of a device with its children, including by an element of `/objects/delete`, `backend` reads `GET /auto/scenarios`: an element `include` `device` of the removed record goes into `gone` under its name, the same `exclude` is removed, the row is written with `cas`, a new version of the record, no history row is added. The rows `auto/versions` of this rule are edited the same way, in the same batch; those that did not fit — by the following ones. At the record row of another camera the detector simply leaves the target. `412` — re-read and repeat, not more than three attempts. **Limit:** the cascade goes after the removal, not in one batch: there may be more rules than the batch limit. A refusal, a break or a `403` of the scenario right (`cluster_rows`) do not change the outcome of the removal: a log line; the target stays with a dead `id`, the evaluator judges it `target.deviceGone`, a write of such an enabled rule — `rule.targetGone`.

### Н1.11 Schedules, day groups, modes, windows

The tables `auto` are read by the evaluator; it alone computes the moment (ADR-0105 item 2): `backend` does not compute whether a schedule is in force. The write — tables in a batch B9 with `cas`, the version — the index of the row, Н1.3. These tables are of the entry cluster: they do not go by a stored edit; a foreign write of the library — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`, entry through its cluster.

- **Built-in** «Всегда», «Никогда» — `Schedule` with `kind` `always`, `never`, `builtin: true`, the id — chapter 3 Н1.2, the version `"1"`. `GET` gives them out even without rows. A write referring to a built-in one carries its row with `cas ""` if the row does not exist: the evaluator reads the table. `PUT`, `DELETE` — after the version check 409 `BUILTIN`.
- **`POST`, `PUT /schedules`.** `name` empty — 422 `NAME_REQUIRED`, `field: name`; `owner` pointing to a non-existent rule or window — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: owner`. `POST` — a new UUID, `builtin: false`, 201.
- **Where a schedule is used** — a mode, a condition `time`, a window.
- **`POST`, `PUT /day-groups`** — the same without `owner`. **`DELETE /day-groups/{id}`**: the group leaves `overrides` of all schedules.
- **`GET /modes`** — chapter 3 Н1.3. **`PUT /modes`** — `If-Match`; no row — `cas ""` at the version `"0"`. A system mode disappeared — 409 `BUILTIN`, `params.modeId`; `otherwise` not from `items` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: otherwise`; an empty name — 422 `NAME_REQUIRED`, `field: items`. A removed mode leaves the conditions `mode` of rules (a new version of the record; no history version is written), its manual switch-on is removed.
- **`GET /modes/current`** — `manual`: the row `auto/mode_current/now`, its mode is in the list, `until` is absent or later than now: `{until}`, otherwise `null`. `modeId`: `manual` not `null` — the mode of the row; no row `modes` — `otherwise`; otherwise — the word `object_mode` of the heard evaluator with the smallest slot, from `GET /servers`; there is no live evaluator or it does not carry the field (`unknown`) — `otherwise`: there is no word from it, and the form needs an answer at load; only the evaluator computes the moment, this is a limit. `GET /servers` did not answer, the row is unreadable — 503. There is no version (the contract).
- **`POST /modes/current`** — `modeId` not from the list — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; the value — the row `now` with `cas`, `null` — removal. The answer — as for `GET`.
- **`POST /maintenance-windows`** — one batch: the window and the schedule with `owner` — this window. The answer — 201 `{window, schedule}`. **`PUT`:** no schedule — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: scheduleId`.
- **Deletion of a window** — also of its schedule, if it is not used anywhere else.
- **Deletion of a schedule:** modes — `schedule: null`; conditions `time` and windows — the built-in «Никогда», except a row `hidden: record`: it stays, `time` is false (V/07.4 Н1.8), the word `schedule is gone`; rules get a new version of the record, history does not.
- **Cascade — one batch.** It does not fit into `BATCH_WRITES` — the dependent rows by their own batches, the removal or the row `modes` last: a break leaves the object alive, a repeat finishes it. **Limit:** between the batches the evaluator sees part of the edits. An edit of an affected row in that window — 412 — re-read, not more than three attempts, otherwise 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`.

### Н1.12 Revert

A revert is only of the author's last unreverted operation, like the undo button: the previous one is reverted only after it (ADR-0106 with addenda (1)–(4); ADR-0111 items 18, 19). Neither the platform nor the record changes; everything is the chapter's own table `backend/undo/<id>` (manifest). Users, roles, grants, folders, passwords, rules and the tables `auto` are outside revert, except the row `hidden: record`.

- **What goes on the stack.** A step of an operation, an element of `POST /devices`, `/devices/update`, `/objects/delete`, and also the single `PATCH`, `DELETE /devices/{id}`, `POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /archives`, `PATCH /volumes/{id}`, on the stack with the `createdId` of the created storage, `POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /devices/{id}/streams…`, in the same package as the write. A preset and a command do not go on the stack: the position and the outcome of the device cannot be read back. `id` is the operation's: for a group write — the first 32 characters of `<h>`; for a single one — 128 random bits: an operation of one step. The first write is `cas ""`, the others — the `cas` of the row. Everything except `POST /operations` carries `single: true`.
- **The row.** A revert operation is a row with `revert_of` and empty `steps`, only for reading `revertOf`; it MUST NOT be reverted. A redo operation is a row with `redo_of` and ordinary `steps`; it is reverted like any other. A step: `index`, `target`, `root` (Н1.8), `rows` — one entry per equipment-settings row `{key, op, born?, before, after}`: `cam/cameras`, `cam/child_settings`, `cam/device_units`, `rec/recordings`, `rec/volumes`, `det/units` and the row `hidden: record` of a change of `recordMode`; counters, the lock, `backend/*`, the rows of the operation are not included; `op` is `set`, `create` or `recreate`; `before`, `after` are the changed fields without `*_secret`; for `create` `after` is all the fields and `born`; for `recreate` (a removed row `hidden: record`) `before` is all the fields of the row without `revision`, and there is no `after`. Only steps with a write.
- **The word `refuse` of a step** is set at the write and gives `fail` of the revert: `volume` — at `rec/volumes` the `url`, its parts, `quota_bytes`, `shrink_confirmed` changed: a revert MUST NOT erase the archive; `deleted` — the row was removed: a number is not repeated; the row `hidden: record` gets no word, it has `recreate`; `bound_to` — the field `bound_to` changed: the password is set anew; `secret` — a `*_secret` changed; `folderIds` — folders are outside revert: the step result is `fail` `NOT_SUPPORTED`, the `field` of the result is `folders`, in the form `OperationItem.params: {reason: folders}`; `view` — a foreign step, a field outside the view (Н1.13).
- **Stack limits.** `UNDO_DAYS` = 7 days — `delete_after`; `UNDO_KEEP` = 50: the revert depth; revert operations do not enter the stack. A new row — `GET /backend/undo`: beyond 50 rows without `revert_of` and beyond 100 rows in all, the oldest by `at` are removed by `DELETE` with `cas`; a refusal is a log line. The row does not fit the store ceiling (`413 row too large`) — `overflow: true`, `steps` are empty, it MUST NOT be reverted: the step matters more than the trace.
- **`POST /operations/{id}/revert`**, in order; every refusal is 409 `NOT_REVERTIBLE`, `params.reason`, except those named:
  1. There is no row, the deadline has passed — `expired`; a foreign `user` — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; not readable — 503.
  2. A row with `revert_of` — `isRevert`; all row entries are reverted — `reverted`; `reverted_by` names an unfinished revert operation — `running`; `overflow`, empty `steps` — `empty`; there is a younger row of this `user` without `revert_of` with an open row entry — `notLast`. A revert operation is finished, and open row entries remain — the revert MAY be repeated: a failed attempt does not close the operation.
  3. The console has a record of the operation and `state` is not `done` — `running`. A cancelled finished one is fit: the executed steps are returned. There is no record — the operation is single or older than `OPS_KEEP`: finished.
  4. `POST /backend/operations`: the steps in reverse order, `body` — `{targetId, revert: {of, index}}`, `targetId` — the target of the step, `root` — the step's, `expected` — `{}`; `memory` — the row `<id>` with `reverted_by` — the id of this revert operation, the `cas` of the one read: a double click does not revert twice; only its step makes a row entry reverted. `409 root busy` — 409 `BUSY`, `params.targetId`; `412 row moved` with `field: /memory/cas` — `reverted`. The answer is 202 `Operation`, `revertOf` — `id`. The new row `backend/undo` goes in the package of the first step, `revert_of` — `id`.
- **A revert step** (`/opstep`, Н1.8, the target row from the snapshot, `expected` is not read): `refuse` — result `fail` `NOT_SUPPORTED`, the word is `field`. A field that does not exist goes in `before`, `after` as the value `null`: the check judges it as a field, the write removes it; so `Archive.recordMode` `null` is reverted and redone. The check is by values: the row `key` exists, every field of `after` equals the current one, for `create` also `born`; otherwise `fail` `VERSION_MISMATCH`: an edit past `backend` is not overwritten. Removal of a created row that has already been changed — the `revision` of the row is greater than the one the operation wrote, or a volume that has a recording on it — `fail` `NOT_SUPPORTED`: the values matched, but the row is no longer the same. A row entry with result `ok` gets `reverted: true` in the package of the step; a row entry with `fail` stays open. An edit of another field of the same row does not hinder the revert. `recreate` is judged differently: the camera has a live row `hidden: record` — `fail` `VERSION_MISMATCH`: there MUST NOT be two record rows; otherwise the creation package of Н1.2: `auto/next_id` with the `cas` of the one read, the row `auto/scenarios/<n + 1>` `cas ""` from `before`, the first row `auto/versions`; the number does not go outside. The write is `before` over the snapshot, for `create` — removal; the package, the lock, the result, the counters — as for a step `set`.
- **The redo queue** of this `user` (ADR-0106 addendum (4)) — like an editor's redo, by the rows `backend/undo` from the youngest by `(at, id)`: a row with `redo_of` marks its target as redone; a row with `revert_of` names the target; a row without both fields ends the traversal: a new edit that got onto the stack clears the redo entirely, it needs no record of its own. A server edit does not enter the stack and does not clear the redo. **The top of the queue** is the first named target that is not marked and has a reverted row entry.
- **`POST /operations/{id}/redo`**, in order; every refusal is 409 `NOT_REVERTIBLE`, `params.reason`, except those named:
  1. There is no row, the deadline has passed — `stale`; a foreign `user` — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; not readable — 503.
  2. `reverted_by` names an unfinished revert operation, or the target is in the queue below the top — `notLast`; not the top and not in the queue (was not reverted, cleared, already redone) — `stale`.
  3. `POST /backend/operations`: the steps with reverted row entries in forward order, `body` — `{targetId, redo: {of, index}}`, `root` — the step's, `expected` — `{}`; `memory` — the row `<id>` with `redone_by` — the id of this operation, the `cas` of the one read: a double click does not redo twice. `409 root busy` — 409 `BUSY`, `params.targetId`; `412 row moved` with `field: /memory/cas` — `stale`. The answer is 202 `Operation`, `redoOf` — `id`. The new row `backend/undo` goes in the package of the first step with a write, `redo_of` — `id`.
- **A redo step** (`/opstep`, the target row from the snapshot, `expected` is not read). `set`: every field of `before` equals the current one, otherwise `fail` `VERSION_MISMATCH`: a foreign edit is not overwritten; the write is `after` over the snapshot. `create`: `fail` `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: created`: what was removed is not returned, the number is new; the row `hidden: record` is created anew, as `recreate`, otherwise `recordMode` would diverge from the recordings. `recreate`: the current record row is removed if its fields equal `before`, otherwise `fail` `VERSION_MISMATCH`. The new row carries the same `before`, `after`; the package, the lock, the result, the counters — as for a step `set`.
- **Reading.** `GET /operations/{id}`: the console has no record, there is a row `backend/undo` — `Operation` from it: `state: done`, `createdAt`, `finishedAt` — `at`, steps `ok`. Fields of the row: `revertOf`, `redoOf` — `revert_of`, `redo_of` in UUID form, no row — `null`; for an operation without a written step `redoOf` is `redo.of` of the step body; `single` — the field of the row, no row — `false`; `revertible` — `revert` would pass steps 1–3; `redoable` — the row is the top of the queue and no revert is running.
- **`GET /operations`** — the listing of this `user`, newest on top: the rows of `GET /backend/undo` and the rows `backend/repeats` with `op: createOperation` whose `operation` is not in `backend/undo`; the record of each is as `GET /operations/{id}`; the order is `at`, on a tie — the greater row index; for a memory row — the `created_at` of the console record; `limit` — 1…50, default 50. A record that is not readable is not returned; a log line. **Limit:** a `retry-failed` without a written step does not enter the listing: it has no rows. Revert and redo go as rows of the listing.
- **A foreign step** — Н1.13, item «Revert».

### Н1.13 Writing to another cluster

One's own is written by the doors Н1.2–Н1.12. Another cluster's has no door: the write goes as a stored edit of the domain ([P/08.6](../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.6-domain.norms.md) Н1.3б, Н1.4; ADR-0110 addendum (4) item 1); the member's agent applies it, and the member's console judges it exactly, by the same person (the rights contract «Суд по цели» `write`). The translation of the body, the checks by `way` and the refusal words are the same as for one's own write.

**State is at the entry** (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 5). `backend/repeats`, `backend/undo`, operations live in the entry cluster: consolem is connected to one `backend`, to another only on loss of the entry. At another entry there are no former operations, and the same `idempotencyKey` is executed anew: after a 5xx and a change of entry the record is re-read.

**Entry.** The `need` of the entry is a grant in any cluster of the domain (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 5): the row `domain/anywhere` of the rights contract. One who entered by `anywhere` without rights on their own cluster has no «own» (chapter 3 Н1.2а): a new record without `serverId` goes to the entry cluster and gets the 403 of its door.

- **The cluster of a new record** (ADR-0111 item 8). `POST /devices`, `POST /archives`, `POST /rules`: the cluster is the member of the server `serverId` by the reverse path of Н1.6; no field — the entry cluster; the server is not in the view listing — `NOT_FOUND`, as for `POST /archives`. `parentId` is a record of another cluster: the cluster of the parent; a `serverId` of yet another cluster here — `PARENT_MISMATCH`, `field: parentId`. After creation, for `camera`, `ipsrv`, `acfa`, `det`, `serverId` is an observation (chapter 3 Н1.1). The `serverId` of a camera without an assignment — no field.
- **Rows.** A write of a device is one edit: `POST /domain/edits {cluster, writes}`, `cluster` — the member of the target, `writes` — the same rows as the package of Н1.2 (ADR-0111 item 16). There are no `cas`, guards, the lock `cas`, `backend/opbusy`, rows `<h>` and `backend/undo` in the bundle: `base` and all-or-nothing application are kept by the platform. A unit row is `{op, sub, rows, id, fields}`, `id` — the `key` of the row of the view table snapshot: without `ref` the row is addressed by the pair (`cluster`, `id`), there is no search, `siblings` do not interfere, and `domain.ref` is not needed; so a detector, a rule, `ipsrv`, `eventsrc`, `acfa`, a stream are edited the same way. A `put` of a unit without `create`, `new` — only over a live row, otherwise the outcome is `refused`: a part that is not in the snapshot or that is removed — `create: true`, with `unit.id: numeric` — `new`. A table row is `{op, sub, rows, name, fields}`, `fields` — the whole row: the fields of the snapshot, the body on top. A new table row is the same `put`: `create`, `new` are words of a unit. A part with `about` goes with the camera row: unchanged — `fields: {}`. In the `fields` of a unit — only the changed fields.
- **What the bundle sends.** Units: `cam/cameras` (camera, `ipsrv`, `eventsrc`, `acfa`, stream), `rec/recordings` — by `domain.ref`; `det/units`, `auto/scenarios` — by `domain.edit`, removal — by `domain.edit_delete` of the manifest. Tables of `domain.edit_tables`: `cam/child_settings`, `cam/device_units`, `cam/unit_next`, `cam/preset_labels`, `rec/volumes`, `auto/versions`, `auto/rule_marks`. Fields are the lists of these words. There are no other rows: what is not in them is not written by a foreign edit.
- **`base`.** An edit by id takes the `revision` of the row of the copy shard, otherwise of the table snapshot (P/08.6 Н1.3б). All the rows above are in `domain.view_tables`, except `auto/versions`: a new row carries no `base`, and one removed on `historyNote` and on deletion of a rule overwrites a local edit; this is a limit. The same for a table with the snapshot `refused`.
- **Creation.** The camera row is `{new: true, op: put, sub: cam, rows: cameras, id: "$id", fields}`, `ref` — `<кластер>-$id` (ADR-0111 item 3), the recordings — `{create: true, id: "$id"}`, `{create: true, id: "$id-<том>"}`, the field `cam` — `$id`. The number is issued by the agent; the outcome carries it in the field `made`: from it `backend` builds the id of the record. Other kinds with `unit.id: numeric` — the same; a detector — `{create: true, id: "$id-<kind>"}`, the second of a kind — with a number; a rule — `{new: true, sub: auto, rows: scenarios}`, `auto/rule_marks/$id` and `auto/versions/$id-1` in the same bundle. There is one `new` in a bundle: creation of a camera with `recordMode: motion` is two edits, the record row after `made`; a copy of a `template` with streams — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: templateId`. `identifier` is judged by the snapshot. A volume — by a bundle: a new row of the table `rec/volumes`, `put` by `name` (P/08.6 Н1.3б); a unit — `cam/unit_next` with `n + 1` by the snapshot and `cam/device_units/<строка>-<n + 1>`, in one bundle; the counter was moved — outcome `conflict`.
- **Key.** The `Idempotency-Key` of a single edit is the hex SHA-256 of `edit`, a zero byte, `user`, a zero byte, the id of the record, a zero byte, `If-Match` and a zero byte with the SHA-256 of the body without the fields `edit_secrets`: the key lies in the open in `domain/pending` and `carry`, and nothing derived from a secret MUST be in it. A repeat of the same edit finds the former one (P/08.6 Н1.4 `repeat`). A secret in the body adds 128 more random bits: a repeat with a secret is a second edit with the same value, harmless. For an element of a group write — the step key of Н1.4 without the read part; for a step of an operation — `step_key` (P/06.9 Н2).
- **Secret.** `password`, `secret` of the body go as an open value only in the body of the door, in a field of `edit_secrets` of the manifest (`cred_secret`, `access_secret`): the process `domain` seals it with the member's key (P/08.6 Н1.3а). It is not in `backend/repeats` and `backend/undo`. An address with credentials is refused by the door: the login goes as a field.
- **Refusals of the door** before admission are the rows of Н4; nothing is stored. `409 kept_full` — 409 `BUSY`, `params.reason: kept_full`: the member does not keep up.
- **Answer.** Reading and the version check — as Н1.3. The member of the target is not `ok` in the view — 409 `SERVER_OFFLINE`, `params.targetId`, the edit is not filed: an accepted edit cannot be cancelled, and a silent box would apply it who knows when. For group writes — `failed`, for a step — the result `fail`. The target is a camera-cluster, `class: camera` (ADR-0113 addendum (1) item 3): there is no waiting, the edit arrives with the camera's period, so immediately 409 `SERVER_OFFLINE`, `params: {targetId, kept}`, as when the deadline has passed. This is every edit of a camera from another cluster, the preset label too; contract b09cdf8 calls `SERVER_OFFLINE` with `params: {targetId, kept}` not a refusal: the edit is accepted and will be applied; for group writes the element `failed` carries the same `params` (`DevicesUpdateResult.failed[].params`). A second edit of this camera before the first is applied — 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`: reading returns the former version; a repeat of the same edit does not create a new one. Otherwise filing, `202 {kept}` — waiting for the outcome within the route's deadline: polling `GET domain`, the element `outcomes.<член>` with `id` = `kept`, then reading anew.

| Outcome | Answer |
|---|---|
| `performed` | 200 of the view record, the new version; the view lags — reading is repeated within the same deadline |
| `conflict` | 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`, `current`: the record on the member was changed after admission |
| `refused`, `status` 403 | 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED` |
| `refused`, `status` 404 | 404 `NOT_FOUND` |
| `refused`, other, including `status` 0 | 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` without `field`: the `error` of the outcome is a phrase, a code is not derived from it (Н4) |
| the deadline has passed | 409 `SERVER_OFFLINE`, `params: {targetId, kept}`: the edit is stored and will be applied; it is only not applied yet. A repeat of the same edit does not create a new one and waits for its outcome; a new version gives 412 |

  A stored edit is not cancelled (ADR-0111 item 16): a revert is a new edit. A group write files the elements in order and waits with one deadline for the whole group; an element without an outcome — `failed` `SERVER_OFFLINE`, there is no result in `steps`, `<h>` stays `started`: a repeat of the key completes it (Н1.4; the refusal `SERVER_OFFLINE` before filing is an ordinary result).
- **A step of an operation.** Filing in the step, waiting within `OPS_STEP_WAIT`, the outcome is the result: `performed` — `ok`, `versions` — the map `{"rows": …}` of the `revision` of the view rows, the others — `fail` by the table above. `expected.rows` of a foreign step is not checked: the order is kept by `base` and `same_path` (P/09.1 Н1.5), the view lags. The deadline has passed — end the call, there is no result (Н1.8 step 5); the console will call the step again, with the same key. **Limit:** after `OPS_STEP_TRIES` the result is `fail not_run`, and the edit will still be applied. The secret of a step — as above.
- **Revert.** The row `backend/undo` of a foreign step goes after the outcome `performed` as a separate write: there is no package with the write; a break between them leaves the step outside the stack. `rows` — the fields of the view table snapshot before and after; a field outside `domain.view_tables` — the `refuse` word `view`. A revert step is a new bundle of `before` over the snapshot, a redo step — of `after`; the check by values is by the view, with its delay.

### Н1.22 Timeline and pieces

ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 5. The order — V/11.16 Н1.2, V/11.17 Н1.1; here — what `backend` does. It calls the recorder doors itself, the address and the token — `where` by the person's ticket (V/11.1 Н1.2а); the bytes of the pieces go past it.

- **`GET /devices/{id}/timeline?from&to&limit&cursor`.** The target is not a camera — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: type`. No `from`, `to` — everything: 0 and now.
  1. Recordings: one's own camera — `GET /rec/recordings` with its `cam`; a foreign one — the member's table `GET /domain/rec/tables/recordings` (chapter 3 Н1.2а). Recordings of other clusters — the book section by `ref`: `GET /domain/rec/books/primaries/*/<раздел>`, the section — 12 bits of the SHA-256 of `ref` (V/02.9 Н1.6); the holder answers, no request is needed, the viewer does not wait for a copy; the elements of the camera's `ref` with the role `primary`, `backup` and `running`. The cost is one read of the section row; it does not grow with the number of cameras of the domain. 404 `empty_part` — there are no recordings of other clusters, silence. 503 — the head of the term has not yet come out, for `primaries` up to 20 min after a move: `unavailable` `what: cluster`, `id` — one's own cluster, the recording of other clusters is named unavailable; another refusal of the door — the same.
  2. Per recording: `GET /rec/where/<запись>`, `GET <door>/timeline/<запись>?from&to`.
  3. Per recording and place of the places table of `rec`: `GET /rec/where/<таблица>/<место>?unit=<запись>` and `/timeline` there. A recording of another cluster — through the target's console, the full timeline: `GET /domain/at/<член>/rec/timeline/<запись>?from&to`, for each of its places also `?place=<таблица>/<место>`, the places — `GET /domain/rec/tables/volumes` of the member; `where` is not needed, the answer is as the door's (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 10, P/06.7 Н1.6). An answer larger than 1 MiB — 502 `answer too large`: the window is split in half, the halves are asked anew, no more than `TIMELINE_SPLITS` = 8 calls for each original call — «recording» and each «recording, place» separately: each has its own answer and its own 1 MiB; this is 4–5 parts of the window, a value to be measured (ADR-0112 addendum (14) item 1; decided by «Спецификация VMS» on the review of norms (14)–(17) М1). Another 502 — the member is not heard, the window is not split. Beyond it, or it did not answer — `unavailable` `what: cluster`: the unread rest of the window is named; «whole» means within these limits.
  4. Merging, yielding, the unavailable — V/11.16 Н1.2.
- **Cost:** 2 + R × (1 + 2P) calls per **page**, R — recordings, P — places: about 125 for 3 recordings and 20 places; for a foreign recording 1 + P without `where`; splitting of the window — up to `TIMELINE_SPLITS` calls instead of each original one: for a foreign recording up to 8 × (1 + P) in all, 168 with 20 places; `backend` has no state, so each page assembles the timeline anew. Each `where` is a journal row `door.issued`: R × (1 + P) rows. Accepted: the cost is per viewer, not per domain (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 5). `TIMELINE_PARALLEL` = 8 calls at once, the door's answer — 5 s (V/02.5 Н1.5), `TIMELINE_BUDGET` = 20 s per read: values without a basis, to be measured. What did not make it goes into `unavailable`; there is no error.
- **Span:** `start`, `end` — `start_ms`, `end_ms`; `archiveId` — the storage of the volume: the home of the recording or the place of the door; for a volume `edge` of a collapsed member — the id of its `storage`; `recordingId` — the id of the recording; `epoch` — the epoch, `0` gives no field (a hold copy, the device archive); `source` — no field — `live`, `backfill`, `device`; `backupOnly` — the rest «только в резервном» of V/11.16; `fenced`. The order is `(start, spanId)`; `cursor` is the encoded `(start, spanId)` of the last row; a foreign one — 422 `INVALID_CURSOR`.
- **`spanId`** — base64url without `=` of the UTF-8 JSON `{m, r, e, s, a, b, p?}`: member, recording, epoch, source, bounds in ms, the place of the door. Client input without trust: there is no signature, the right is judged by `where` by the ticket; a recording not of this camera — 404 `NOT_FOUND`.
- **`unavailable`:** `what: server` — `unreachable`, the `where` of a recording that did not answer; `volume` — `unavailable`, a place 404 with `X-Unreachable`, `id` — `<том>@<сервер>`; `cluster` — the member's door, `id` — the member. `reason` — the words of V/11.16. A place 404 without `X-Unreachable` — silence. 403 of a door — `where` anew once.
- **`GET /devices/{id}/playback?at&spanId`.** With `spanId` — one `where`: of the recording, with `p` — of the place `?unit=`, for a foreign member — through `/domain/at`: the address of a foreign cluster's door to the client, the bytes do not go through the console, the limit `FORWARD_MAX` (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 11). A member `m` without `members[].url`: there is no address, `/domain/at` 404 `no console known`, the timeline — `unavailable` `what: cluster`, no spans, `playback` without `spanId` — 404, a `spanId` with such an `m` — 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER` without `params.url` (Н1.13). The member of the recording judges, not the camera: a recording of a w2c camera behind NAT on a server with `url` is accessible. **Limit:** `members[].url` is the address of the console, `door` is the address of the recorder, a leaf from the root of the foreign cluster: a member with `url` behind a forwarded port gives 200 with an address that the browser will not open; the form will show a failure of the piece, `backend` does not see it. A piece is a grid of 60 s from `a`: `from` = `a` + ⌊(`at` − `a`) / 60 s⌋ × 60 s, `to` = min(`from` + 60 s, `b`). `url` — `<door>/segment/<запись>/e<эпоха>/<fromMs>-<toMs>[.backfill | .device].mp4?t=<токен>`, `expiresAt` — `expires`: the token is 120 s, the address is fit for the piece and the start of the next one. ### Н1.23 Holds

`Hold` is the row `rec/keeps/{cam}-{from:int}-{to:int}` (V/02.7 Н1.1, V/11.18 Н1.2). `id` is the key itself: its `StreamMessage.entityId` is not a UUID. `deviceId` — the camera `cam`, `by` — `stamp.by`, `at` — `made_at`, `releasedAt` — `released_at`, `recordingIds` — `recordings`, `note`.

- **`GET /devices/{id}/holds`.** `GET /rec/keeps` and selection by `cam`: the table has no selection by field; the **cost** is the whole table of the cluster per reading of a card. `states` — the field `keeps` by the heartbeat door `GET /rec/heartbeats`: `{archiveId — хранилище volume пульса, state, why?}` (the storage of the heartbeat's `volume`); not named — not said. Pages — the common rule, the order — by key. A foreign camera — the same doors through `/domain/at/<член>`, `id` — `<ключ>@<член>`: a camera number is unique only within a cluster, a bare key would coincide with one's own keep of the same window.
- **`POST /devices/{id}/holds`** `{from, to, note?, recordingIds?}`. Seconds — down for `from`, up for `to`; `to` not later than `from` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: to`; a recording not of this camera — 422, `field: recordingIds`; `recordingIds` absent or empty — all the recordings of the camera now. There is one — the fields of the row read, `recordings` are merged, the note and `released_at` are not erased, a new `note` — only on an empty one, the `cas` of the read, 200. 412 — anew, no more than three times, otherwise 409 `BUSY`. ### Н1.24 Bookmarks

`Bookmark` is a journal row `kind: mark` (P/06.4 Н1.2). There is no edit or deletion; the term is the journal's term: a bookmark leaves with it, there is nothing to protect it with (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 3). A bookmark is a moment or a span (ADR-0117 addendum (1) item 2): `type: span` — the row has `to`, the end of the operator mark-segment `[occurred, to]` (P/06.4 Н1.2 `marks`), `type: moment` — it has none. The `rec` keep gets no end: ADR-0117 addendum (3) item 2 replaced that edit of V/02.7 by the end `to` of the mark, so a span does not hold the archive — a hold is its own row (Н1.23). The export from a bookmark is built by the window: a span — its bounds, a moment — ± `limits.bookmarkPadSec` of `Reference.export` (chapter 20 Н1.8).

- **`GET /devices/{id}/bookmarks?from&to`.** `GET /events?from&to&unit=cam/<id>&kind=mark&by=occurred&late=<с>`: a bookmark about the past lies in the bucket of its `t`, and a row later than `to + late` is not visible to the query, so `late` is the age of the end of the window, `now − to`, an integer rounded up, no less than the default 3600, as V/11.16 Н1.3; no `to` — now, no `from` — from the start of the journal. **Cost:** reading an old window passes the buckets from it until now. `limit` goes into the journal, the page is one, there is no `nextCursor`: the journal has no cursor, a truncated `truncated` is a limit. `id` — the UUID of `event:`, `occurred` — the field of the row, absent — `t`; `at` — `t`; `by` — `user`; `type` — `span` if the row has `to`, otherwise `moment`; `to` — the field `to` of the row. A span is selected by its `occurred`, like a moment: a span that began before `from` is not in the window — a limit. Newest on top.
- **`POST /devices/{id}/bookmarks`** `{note, occurred?, type?, to?}`. `note` is empty after trimming the edge spaces — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: note`. No `type` — `moment`; `moment` with `to`, `span` without `to` or with `to` not later than `occurred` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: to`. A span without `occurred` — `occurred` is the moment of the request by the clock of `backend`, given in the body: the door takes `to` only together with `occurred` (P/06.4 Н1.2). `POST /marks {unit: cam/<id>, note[, occurred][, to]}`, 201. The right — `edit` on the unit of the body (P/06.3 Н1.9 `marks`), 403 — `PERMISSION_DENIED`; `global.bm` is a derivation from it, V/11.8 Н1.2. A foreign camera — 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER`.

### Н1.25 Pin

`POST /devices/{id}/archive-fetch {archiveId, from, to}` — «Закрепить» of V/11.16 Н1.2, the requests of V/02.8 Н1.4. `to` not later than `from` — 422, `field: to`; a foreign camera — 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER`. The primary is the recording of one's own cluster of this camera on the volume `archiveId`, with a home not on a backup volume and without `replica_from`; there is none — 409 `NO_PRIMARY`.

The segment is split by days; per day — `POST /rec/requests {unit: <основная>, from, to}`. `accepted` — how many were filed. `refused` — only about filing: `per_person` — the limit of 7 per person, the rest of the segment with the same reason; another refusal of the door — with its word. The recorder's refusal comes late, in the journal `archive.backfill.refused` (V/02.8 Н1.4): the camera's `GET /journal` shows it, chapter 3 Н1.3. The gluing of minutes is done by the form by the spans `backupOnly`, `source: device`: `backend` requests what was said. **Cost:** a write per day of the segment.
