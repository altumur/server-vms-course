# V/11.5 — Callback and stream: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.5-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 5 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0094 B1, B2). Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](11-backend.README.md), the contract [`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the increment of rights on top of the norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark «норма платформы B» — where the chapter rests on them).

The worker `backend` holds neither the person's credential nor rights of its own: everything it reads and writes it calls at the console with
the ticket of the request it is translating now. The stream `/stream` of the contract is the same tickets, but a long-lived request: the console
passes it as a stream, and `backend` translates the console's change feed into the messages of the contract. The chapter sets norms for the side of `backend`:
when and how it calls the console and what it sends into the stream.

Norm ids are `V/11.5-MM`; the map is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1
Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

- **Ticket** — 128 random bits that the console issued for one open request of a person and passed to the worker (B1).
- **Feed** — `GET /changes` of the console: messages «row X — revision R / deleted», without the value (B2).
- **Device kinds** — the kinds whose `uid` is built from the name `device:…` ([`mapping.tsv`](../data/mapping.tsv), chapter 4 Н1.6): `camera`, `det`, `ipsrv`, `channel`, `sensor`, `relay`, `mic`, `spk`, `storage`, `eventsrc`, `acfa`, `acfa_sensor`, `acfa_lock`, `acfa_fence`, `stream`. **Decided:** these are the records that the former contracts gave as `kind: device`; the contract 0.2.0 names each by its kind (chapter 14 Н1.1).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** The timeouts of the chapter are seconds of the process's monotonic clock.

### Н1.1 Callback by ticket

- The worker MUST call the console only **with the ticket of the request** it is translating now, and only at the doors of the list
  (V/11.1 Н1.5); the ticket MUST NOT be kept after the answer to the request, MUST NOT be passed to other requests and MUST NOT be written into the log.
- The console accepts the ticket while the external request is open, at the callback listener (`CONSOLE_CALL_PORT`, the address is
  the header `X-W2C-Pass-Console`) with the role leaf `backend` of the server of the instance to which it passed the request (without
  a kit — by the role socket; V/11.1 Н1.2, ADR-0094 addendum (6) items 1, 2), at a door of the list, under the same check as a door token (revocation of `jti`, expiry `exp`, key `kid` of the ticket record at the console),
  otherwise — 401, and under a fresh `may` of the person, a refusal — 403 (B1, ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 1; a platform norm B). The worker gets only the 128 bits of the ticket,
  it does not see the fields of the record. For `backend`, a 401 of a callback is the end of the translation: the answer to the request is 401 `UNAUTHORIZED` (chapter
  4 Н4), there is no repeat with the same ticket.
- The answer to the person is closed (the browser has gone) — the ticket is dead: the worker MUST stop the translation and append nothing.
- The pass waiting limit is by route (V/11.1 Н1.5): a group write (`api/units`, `api/objects/delete`)
  manages the packages in order; if it did not manage — the console closes the request, the worker sees a dead ticket (as above), and the steps done
  are already written into the group memory (chapter 4 Н1.4): a repeat will continue it.

### Н1.2 The stream `/stream` from the feed `/changes`

- `GET /stream` (the console address `/backend/api/stream`) is passed as a stream (the route `api/stream`, V/11.1 Н1.5; B1):
  the stream ticket is good only for reads and `/changes`. The feed is a long poll, not SSE (P/06.1 Н2 «Лента изменений», B2):
  `GET /changes?after=<номер>&wait=<с>` → `{position, changes, reset?}`, `wait` not greater than `CHANGES_WAIT`. For a browser
  stream `backend` holds one such poll after another: the next one — with `after` = `position` of the previous answer. To the browser it
  sends `text/event-stream`: per message — the lines `id:` and `data:` (`StreamMessage`), without `event:` (the contract).
- **`stream.hello`** is the first message of every connection, also with `Last-Event-ID`: `clientId` — new random 128 bits in hex, `id` — `<position>`, `at` — the moment of opening. After it — the start of the subscription below.
- **`PUT /stream/{clientId}/watch`** (`StreamWatch`): `ids` up to `watchMax`, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: ids`; it replaces the previous list. The list is held by the stream, in process memory: there is no such stream or it is another person's — 404 `NOT_FOUND`. There is no write: the ticket of this route is good only for reads (the route `api/stream`). With two instances, a `PUT` to the wrong one — also 404: the form reopens the stream `GET /stream?watch=<id,id,…>` and re-reads the watched ones (ADR-0112 addendum (12) item 2).
- **`watch` of the opening request:** ids separated by commas, up to `watchMax`, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: watch` before the stream is opened; empty — as no parameter. The list is in force from the opening, also with `Last-Event-ID`; a `PUT` replaces it.
- **What goes for the watched ones.** The device kinds (Н0) — only a record from `ids` and its children: `entityId` in `ids` or the `parentId` of the record in `ids`; the row read happens anyway (below). A new list gives no messages for the past: the form reads by itself. The list is empty — there are no device events, including for a stream without `watch` until the `PUT` has arrived: the form sends it right after `stream.hello`, and a `PUT` to the wrong instance gives 404 and a new stream with the list. There is no burst per connection: the redelivery by `Last-Event-ID` also goes by the list. The other `kind`, `tree.changed`, operations, rules — to everyone. The cost of a request is one comparison with memory: constant; the walk over pages («Foreign» below) stays.
- **`tree.changed`** (`layout`, `parentIds`) goes after the message of its own kind with the same number `k + 1`, if the record was born, removed, changed its name or its parent: `folders` — `folder`, the parent is a folder; `servers` — `server` and the device kinds, the parent is a server, for a camera-cluster — the domain; `archives` — the storage kinds `archive.*`, `volume`, the parent is the storage, the domain; `detectors` — `det`, the parent is a camera. The message goes to everyone. **Limit:** the feed does not carry the composition of a folder in the domain, `memberof` is not sent: the stream is silent about it, the form sees it on expanding.
- **`kind` of a message** is an open string (the contract; chapter 14 Н1.2): for a record of `/units` — the kind code of the record (`camera`, `archive.local`, `volume`, `folder`, …, chapter 14 Н1.1), for the others — the words of the table below (`rule`, `operation`, `discovery`, `exportJob`, …). `backend` sends no other value.
- The translation of the feed messages:

| Feed of the console (B2) | `StreamMessage` of the contract |
|---|---|
| a row of a kind (chapter 3) created — `revision: 1` (one created over a tombstone — as changed, below) | `entity.created` (`kind`, `entityId`, `version`) |
| a row changed — `revision: R`; `0` — the revision is not readable | `entity.changed` (`kind`, `entityId`, `version`) |
| a row deleted — `deleted` instead of `revision` (the tombstone index, B4, ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 9; `""` — there is no tombstone) | `entity.deleted` (`kind`, `entityId`) |
| an answer with `reset: true` | `stream.reset` |

| the row `backend/opdone/<id>/<i>` | `operation.progress` (`kind: operation`, `entityId`, `payload` — `{item: <OperationItem>, done, total}`, the counters of the row `backend/opcount/<id>`; the keys await reconciliation with consolem) |

| a row `cam/found/<h>` of the current search, while it runs, which is given in `candidates` (chapter 4 Н1.9) | `discovery.progress` (`kind: discovery`, `entityId` — the domain, `payload` — `{progress, candidate}`) |

| the row `detedge/watches/<name>` | `entity.created`, `entity.changed`, `entity.deleted` (`kind: archiveWatch`, `entityId`, `version`); the frontier is not sent |
| the row `auto/scenarios/<id>` without `hidden`, expressible in `Rule` | `entity.created`, `entity.changed`, `entity.deleted` (`kind: rule`, `entityId`, `version`) |
| the row `auto/rule_marks/<id>` | `entity.changed` of the rule (`kind: rule`, the new version) |
| the camera recording row (`hidden: record`), `cam/rule_state/<id>` | only `entity.changed` of the camera (`kind: camera`, the new version — chapter 4 Н1.1) |

| the row `cam/searches/<CLUSTER>` without `stopped_at` | not sent; the stream remembers its `until`: the deadline has passed and the row is the same — `discovery.finished`, `payload` — `{state: done, progress: 100}` |
| a row that has no kind (chapter 3: `none`), `cam/unit_next`, `cam/ptz_moves`, `cam/preset_labels` (the camera version does not change: another session will see the caption at the next read, a limit), `auto/versions`, `auto/answers`, the other rows `backend/repeats`, `undo`, the rows `people`, `history`, `memberof`, other rows of an operation | not sent |

A stream row is a record of the kind `stream` and a part of the camera (chapter 3 Н1.3 "Streams"): **Decided:** `entity.created`, `entity.changed`, `entity.deleted` of the stream (`kind: stream`), then `entity.changed` of the camera — its `version` (derived messages below). The camera row itself is also its main stream (`uid` `…#main`, chapter 3 Н1.2): its change gives `entity.changed` of the camera (`kind: camera`) first, then of the main stream (`kind: stream`). `kind: streamAccounts` is not sent: the accounts are not declared (chapter 4 Н1.18).

- **Derived messages** (the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 18, Claude С-15, question 11). One message of the feed may give
  several `StreamMessage` — by the rows of the table above, in the order: the message of the row's own kind, then of the camera, then of the
  storage (for a volume — the storage kind `archive.<вид>`, then `volume`). Each has its own `type`. A removed detector: `entity.deleted`
  of the detector (its id — from the tombstone key), then `entity.changed` of the camera — its version with the tombstone index
  (`?deleted=1&about=`, chapter 4 Н1.1); the camera is already gone — there is no camera message.

- The `version` of a message is the version of the kind's record by chapter 4 Н1.1 (the store index; B4): the feed carries «row X — revision R»
  without the index, so `backend` reads the row again through a door of the list before sending the message. The follow-up read answered
  404 (the row has already been removed) or 403 — the message is not sent: the deletion will come as its own feed message; another refusal of the follow-up read and
  three mismatches of the double read — the stream is closed, as for a feed refusal (Н4); the `revision` of the row read
  is higher than `R` — the version of the row read is sent (the analysis of the V/11 review, Kimi's addition, No. 6).
- **Body fields** of `StreamMessage` (the contract: `id`, `type`, `at` are required; Kimi's addition, No. 5, question 2): the body `id`
  equals the SSE line `id:`; `at` is the time of the event on the server, not of sending, RFC 3339 UTC: for a feed message — the field `at` of the feed,
  a JSON number, seconds of the console tick that saw the version of the row; it is later than the reception by the store by no more than one tick, and
  the redelivery gives the same value (a platform norm B2); for the events of an assistant
  turn (chapter 7) — the moment when `backend` produced them. The meaning of `at` was named to the product in the third request — to be confirmed.
- **The message `id`** is `<p>-<k>`: `p` is the number after which the feed gives this message (`n` of the previous message
  of the answer, for the first — `after` of the request), `k` is the number of the derived message within it from 0 (question 11). `p` is taken, not `n`:
  the feed gives only the messages **after** the number, and a break between derived messages would otherwise lose the rest. There is no `-` in a feed
  number (P/06.1 Н2). For the client the `id` is opaque (the contract).
- **Start of the subscription** (ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 6): the first request without `after` answers at once with `{position, changes: []}`;
  `backend` sends the browser the SSE line `id: <position>` without `data:` — by SSE it sets the browser's last `id` and gives no
  event, so it has no `StreamMessage` and needs no `type`. The client applies messages, dropping a revision not higher than
  the one read.
- **`Last-Event-ID`** of the browser (the header passes through the pass of the second kind, V/11.1 Н1.5): `<p>` — `after=<p>`; `<p>-<k>` —
  `after=<p>` and skip the first `k + 1` derived messages of the first message; another form — as the start of the subscription, with
  `stream.reset`.
- **`reset`** is the answer `{position, changes: [], reset: true}` (a foreign or evicted number, a restart of the console instance,
  a change of the person's rights or labels, B2): `stream.reset` with the `id` `<position>`, the browser re-reads everything, the poll goes on with
  `after=<position>`.
- **Folder deletion** (ADR-0096 §3): the feed carries only the folder itself, and the subfolders are unreachable from the same index. The follow-up read of a folder row is `GET /folders`
  as a whole: the same read updates the stream's tree. `backend` remembers the tree of the last read of this stream and, after `entity.deleted` of the folder, sends `entity.deleted` to every descendant that was reachable before the deletion, in
  `id` order; these are derived messages of the same feed message. There is no tree in memory (the start of the stream) — only the folder itself; the browser will
  get a 404 on reading a descendant anyway.
- **Operation rows.** By a ticket the console gives the feed only of the author's operations (P/06.9 Н2), so the stream carries no messages of others. The values —
  by a follow-up read of the door `GET /backend/operations/<id>`; the order of steps beyond the feed is not promised, the truth is `GET /operations/{id}`.
- **Rights.** The feed does not carry domain rows: there are no messages `kind: access`, the WebUI re-reads the snapshot by itself. A feed reset on a change of roles is like
  any other `reset`.
- **End of search** by the deadline is not visible to the feed: there is no row, so `discovery.finished` is sent by the stream's timer; the stream remembers `until` also at opening, the `id` is `<position>-<k>` of the last feed answer, `k` the next one.
  A break of the stream does not redeliver such a message: the result is read by `GET` (chapter 4 Н1.9).
- **Event log through the stream** (ADR-0112 addendum (13)). A stream with a non-empty watch list reads `GET /events?from=<t>` of the cluster once per `JOURNAL_POLL` = 3 s and selects in memory; the window moves by `t` with the margin `JOURNAL_SETTLE` = 5 s: the selection of `/events` goes by `t`, a row of another resource comes late, without the margin it would be lost, duplicates are removed by `id`. The selection: a row of a unit from the list, of its parent, or of an `eventsrc` with a `camera` from the list — `journal.event`, `entityId` — the device, `payload` — `JournalEvent` with `cameraId`; `kind: mark` — also `entity.created` `bookmark` to everyone, `entityId` — the id of the mark, `payload: {deviceId}`. Duplicates — by `id`. The feed does not carry the event log, so this is a poll per stream, not a message. **Cost:** one query once per 3 s per stream, the answer grows with the cluster's event log. **Limit:** `truncated` loses rows until the next read by the form; the stream does not read the event log of another cluster; the delay of a caption — up to 3 s and `JOURNAL_SETTLE`.
- **Hold:** the row `rec/keeps/<ключ>` — `entity.created`, `entity.changed`, `entity.deleted`, `kind: hold`, `entityId` — the key, it is not a UUID, `payload: {deviceId}`, to everyone.
- **Export:** `export.progress`, `export.finished`, `kind: exportJob` (the contract: the kind `export` is taken by the object «Агент экспорта»), `entityId` — the order, only to the author (`by` of the parts — the person of the stream): the state of the parts `exp/parts/<name>` is not in the feed, so the stream reads it no more often than `VIEW_POLL` while the author has an unfinished order — chapter 20 Н1.9.
- **Log and heartbeat** (ADR-0105): a rule run is a row of the event log, a mode change is a heartbeat word, and the feed carries neither (P/06.1 Н2 «Лента изменений» «Охват»). So after every feed answer the stream reads `GET /events?subsystem=auto&from=<t>` with the `t` of the last row and `GET /servers`: a new firing `fired` — `entity.created` (`kind: ruleFire`, `entityId`, `payload: {ruleId}`), its second row after `waiting` — `entity.changed` of the same `kind`; another `modeId` of chapter 4 Н1.11 — `entity.changed` (`kind: objectMode`, `entityId` — the mode). The scan result is a state of the unit, the feed does not carry it either (V/05.1): while the stream has a row `scan-…` without a result, no more often than `VIEW_POLL` per stream it reads `GET /detjob/jobs`; the orders `rec-…`, `auto-…` do not concern the form; a result has appeared — `entity.changed` (`kind: archiveScan`, `entityId`, `version`). At the opening of the stream both values are remembered, there are no messages. The `id` is as for the end of search. Prompts — the same queue: no more often than `VIEW_POLL` per stream it reads `GET /auto/scenarios` of its cluster, the cost is constant, and compares the prompts of chapter 3 Н1.3 with the remembered ones: a new one — `prompt.opened` (`kind: rulePrompt`, `entityId`, `payload: {ruleId, fireId, step, roleId, level}`), a vanished one — `prompt.closed` with `payload: {ruleId, fireId, step, reason}`, `reason` by the step-end row in the log of the firing (V/07.4 Н1.6): `note` `ask.answer`, `label` `esc.ack` — `answered`, also `answer` — `yes`, `no`, for `esc.ack` `ack`, and `by` — the `user` of the row; `note` `ask.noAnswer`, `answer.unread`, `label` `esc.none` — `expired`; `label` `esc.reset` — `reset`. The end row lands after the step is written, so the message waits for it one `VIEW_POLL`; still none — `stopped`: a withdrawn run does not write such rows. **Limit:** a row that landed later than this wait, and a crash between the write and the row (V/07.4 Н1.11), are also read as `stopped`: `VIEW_POLL` covers only the ordinary delay of the row. **Limit:** the delay is the feed answer, up to `CHANGES_WAIT`; a break does not redeliver: the truth is `GET /rules/{id}/fires`, `GET /modes/current`, `GET /archive-scans`.
- **Foreign** (ADR-0110 addendum (2); chapter 3 Н1.2а). The feed is per cluster and carries no foreign rows. So after a feed answer, no more often than once per `VIEW_POLL` = 5 s per stream, a value without a basis, the stream reads one page of `GET domain` and the tables only of its members `class: server`, camera-clusters are compared by the `units` of the page, as the listing does, with `If-None-Match` (chapter 3 Н1.2а «Цена») and the stream ticket, the next page — at the next `VIEW_POLL`, in a circle (ADR-0113 addendum (7)), and compares the records of the device kinds, `archive.*`, `volume`, `rule`, `schedule`, `dayGroup`, `maintenanceWindow` with what it remembered about the members of this page; a part from `unread` is not compared; a new id — `entity.created`, another version — `entity.changed`, an id vanished — `entity.deleted`, only for a member of the page walked: the circle does not serve as a proof of completeness; `version` is the version of the foreign record (chapter 3 Н1.3). It sees what the person of the ticket sees. The first pass of a page remembers its values, there are no messages; with one page — the whole domain per `VIEW_POLL`, as before. The `id` is as for the end of search. A member's table is not read — the records of this kind are not compared for it: the vanishing is not proven, there is no `entity.deleted`. A `server` record is not compared, like an own one. **Limit:** the cost of the stream is constant, the freshness ages: the delay is the domain view, the feed answer and the walk, the number of pages × `VIEW_POLL`, at 250,000 members ≈ 21 min; the stream's memory grows with the number of records of the domain; the records of a departed member give no `entity.deleted`, it is not on the page; a break does not redeliver: the truth is `GET`.
- **Knocking servers and a camera after admission.** The same walk remembers the names `knocking` of the first answer of a page: a name has gone — `entity.deleted` (`kind: server`, the `uid` of `server(knocking)`, chapter 3 Н1.3), a new one — `entity.created`. A w2c camera admitted into the domain appears as a row of a kind: `entity.created` (`kind: camera`) comes by the comparison of the page where it landed; the order of the two messages is not promised, the freshness is the circle. A non-viewer has no `knocking`, there are no messages.
- **Scenarios document.** The `rev` of the field `shared` of the first answer of a page `GET domain` (P/08.9 Н1.6) differs from the remembered one — `entity.changed` (`kind: scenarios`, `entityId` — the `uid` of the record of the kind `domain`, `version` — `r<rev>`, V/11.4 Н1.21). **Limit:** `rev` is common to the domain document: an edit of another field sends the same message.
- **Loss of connection with the domain** (Д-11). The read `GET domain` gave neither 200 nor 403 — the comparison stops, the memory is held, there are no messages: the records are alive, an `entity.deleted` would be a lie. The connection is back — the comparison with the same memory. The signal — on a change of the connection in either direction, `entity.changed` (`kind: domain`, `entityId` — the `uid` of the record of the kind `domain`, `version` — its `version`), then `stream.reset`: foreign records vanish from the answers, this is not a deletion (`status.link` of the record of the kind `domain`, chapter 3 Н1.2а). **Limit:** the signal stands behind `VIEW_POLL`; there is no stream — there is no signal.
- `kinds.changed` is not sent (chapter 14 Н1.1): a manifest change restarts the cluster console and breaks the stream, the client gets `stream.reset` and rereads `GET /kinds` with `If-None-Match` (the contract).
- `test.reset` is not sent:
  `/test/reset` loads the suite as ordinary writes, and the stream carries their messages; another instance of
  `backend` could learn of the reset only past the feed (the analysis of the V/11 review, Claude С-19; chapter 6 Н1.3).
- The stream ticket lives while the browser stream is open; the stream is closed — `backend` stops the poll. The console closes an open
  stream by the ticket on a revocation of `jti`, on the person being switched off, at `exp` and when a fresh `may` by the route's door refused (`unavailable` does not close the stream; the gate and pass contract `close`; addendum (3) item 1); streams go under a separate limit
  of the console, callbacks — in their own lane (addendum (3) item 7).

### Н1.3 The feed only

The source of messages about its own rows is only the feed `/changes`: `backend` MUST NOT fake the stream by polling rows, except for the scan result and the state of the export parts (above). A feed answer, except `reset`, does not
carry values: Н1.2 reads them again. `ruleFire`, `objectMode` have no rows, their read is Н1.2 «Log and heartbeat». The feed carries no foreign rows: Н1.2 «Foreign».

## Н2 Doors

The chapter has no door of its own: `/stream` is an operation of the contract behind the console's pass (V/11.1 Н1.5).

## Н3 Pass

```
a contract request with a ticket
  every callback — with this ticket, only a door of the list
  401 of a callback → the answer 401 UNAUTHORIZED, the translation is over
GET /stream (a stream, the stream ticket)
  no Last-Event-ID → GET /changes without after → id: <position> without data:
  Last-Event-ID <p> → after=<p>; <p>-<k> → after=<p>, skip k + 1 derived messages of the first message
  repeat GET /changes?after=<position>&wait=<с>:
    a feed message → kinds (chapter 3) → read the row again → StreamMessage k = 0, 1, … (id <p>-<k>, data)
    reset: true → stream.reset (id <position>)
    a new firing in the event log, another mode → ruleFire, objectMode (id <position>-<k>)
    GET domain, one page: 200 → the tables of its members class server, units of the others, comparison → entity.* of foreign records (id <position>-<k>); not 200, not 403 → no comparison
  the browser stream is closed → the poll is stopped
```

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a callback — 401 (the ticket is dead, a door outside the list, no certificate of the role `backend`, a leaf of another server) | the answer 401 `UNAUTHORIZED` | WebUI | the console login or a repeat of the request |
| the feed answered `reset` | `stream.reset` | WebUI | re-read everything |
| a feed request refused or broke off; the follow-up read refused with other than 404 and 403 or gave three mismatches | the stream is closed | WebUI | reconnect with `Last-Event-ID` |

## Н5 Acceptance

The consolem API tests (the stream — `tests/api-read.js` at the pinned commit) and the platform acceptance B1, B2 (ADR-0094
Confirmation): the ticket is dead after the request is closed, a callback outside the list, from a leaf of another role and from a leaf of another
server — 401 (addendum (6) item 5), the stream closes with the request, the feed `reset`; before the platform increment — by reading.

What they do not see is checked by [`acceptance/4/`](../acceptance/4/README.md) `09-stream` (ADR-0094 addendum (17)): what is hidden in the stream, `stream.reset` on a change of rights, `Last-Event-ID` after a worker restart.
