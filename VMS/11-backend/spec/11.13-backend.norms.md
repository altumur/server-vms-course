# V/11.13 — Rules in words: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.13-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 13 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора», the assistant (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the subsystem — ADR-0093, addendum (6); the words of «Архитектор» 2026-11-02 by the pilot measurement). Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export), [instructions](11-backend.README.md). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The admin writes an automation rule in words: «при движении на Lobby Cam пиши её 5 минут». The scheme is the same as for configuration: the model proposes a plan, code checks it, a person writes it. What is more dangerous here is something else: a rule accepted by the check may do the wrong thing, and the error is not seen at once, only when the event happens or does not happen. Therefore a check of the form is not enough: the plan is reconciled with the words of the request, and a new rule is created in observation, and a person switches it on. The chapter sets norms for the request type, the rules harness, the plan, the check layers, the reconciliation with the words, writing and display.

A separate chapter, not edits to chapters 9–12: those set norms for the plan `{unit, field}` over unit rows. The model's plan is neutral, in the concepts of the contract, and it is judged as a V/07 row (ADR-0093 addendum (10) item 2, (11)). Rules have another plan, other tools and another writing, and the turn goes to `backend` (ADR-0093 addendum (6)); all they share is the model door (V/10.2) and the loop (chapter 9).

Norm ids are `V/11.13-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

- **Request type** — `config`, `rules`, `explain`, `found`; `backend` chooses it by the utterance (V/11.7 Н1.6).
- **Turn texts** — as in chapter 9 Н0: the request and the admin's answers, as the person entered them.
- **False success** — the accepted plan is not equal to the request, or there is a plan where a question or a refusal is needed.
- **Catalogue** — [`data/rule-catalog.json`](../data/rule-catalog.json), Н1.3.
- **Neutral plan** — the form of the model's plan, Н1.4: names instead of ids, the concepts of the contract, without the words of a V/07 row and without UUIDs.
- **Plan row** — the translation of the neutral plan into a V/07 row; the layers of Н1.5 judge by it.

## Н1 Form

**Data.** No families of its own. The turn lives in the memory of `backend` for the time of the request (V/11.7 Н1.1). The rules harness is lesson data, separate from configuration:

The harness is of the neutral form of the measurement `plan-neutral` (`assist-pilot` `REPORT-neutral.md`, 9dc2afc; ADR-0093 addendum (10)), approach 2; `check_plan` instead of the pilot's `dry_check`, `edits_json` as a string, as before. Not in that measurement were: `ask`, `escalate` (ADR-0093 addendum (9) item 1), the kinds `disappeared`, `abandoned`, `crowd` (ADR-0112 addendum (15) item 2), `via` only `client`, «console form» instead of «console page», `refuse` with `other_kind` (V/11.7 Н1.6). The differences from the former measurement of the row form are **edits after the measurement**; they were not in the run: the tool `check_plan`; `active`, `normal` instead of `closed`, `open` at an input; the line about `params` and about the scan window; `actions` `det detect`, `det scan` wider than the measurement: `detection`, «check its archive»; the line about a step with its own `params`: another step is edited, this one is copied as is, only the time may change; the paragraph about `log_only`; a window is created switched off, `update` only for rules; the keys `whole`, `switched_on`, `not_armed`, `conditions` of the dictionary; the keys `passage`, `open_word`, `other_state`, `relay_n`, the stems «пропад», «отключ», «switch … off», «drop off», `lost_bare`, `lost_contact`, `segment_lost_phrase`, `arm_put` on cut(t), `asks_step`, `same_camera`, «kick off», «stops recording», `open` for `cam output`, the dictionary without `\b`, `\w`, `\d`, the number sign at `relay_n`; the forms of starting a rule, `disarm_ru` with `guard`, «отключ» for a relay, «писат», `default_no`, «do raise», «да», «подним», «подня» for `default_yes`, «пишет» for `recording`; «lose, loses, lost, losing … recording» for `segment.lost` («lost recording», «теряет запись» the dictionary caught before as well), «off guard» for `cam disarm`, `not_armed` and `conditions.armed` (ADR-0093 addendum (19)–(34)); the cluster overview as the first `user` message, as in chapter 9. A new edit of the harness is made by a decision of «Архитектор» and a line in this list.

### Н1.1 Two harnesses

- `backend` chooses the type by the step `route` from the utterance (V/11.7 Н1.6, ADR-0093 addendum (13)): one conversation may contain an edit and a rule; an answer to a question goes by the same harness.
- `config` — chapters 9–12 and V/11.7 as is; the files of `harness/` do not change. `rules` — this chapter.
- The loop of `rules` is chapter 9 Н1.2–Н1.5 and V/11.7 Н1.2: `tool_choice: required`, an answer only by a call, the window, the count of calls. Beyond them: `ask_admin` or `refuse` before any read in the turn is rejected once, with the result `error: an answer not checked against the data. First find_devices for the request's words, then a plan, a question or a refusal. If the request names no device at all, call ask_admin again.`

### Н1.2 Tools

The closed set of the type `rules` is exactly [`harness/rules/tools.json`](../harness/rules/tools.json); the rules of the set are chapter 10 Н1.1:

| Tool | What | Turn |
|---|---|---|

| `list_table` | the rows `detectors`, `recordings`, `rules`, `schedules`, `modes`, `windows`, `folders`, `roles` by name; a rule in the form `Rule` of the neutral plan, for `updateRule` | goes on |

- `propose_plan`: `edits_json` is the neutral plan as a JSON string; the translator and the check judge the form. With free objects the model reduced them to `{"item": …}`, and numbers and `true` to strings: 400 rejected plans and 31 failures on 75 requests; the string removed this, 1 failure.

### Н1.3 Catalogue

[`rule-catalog.json`](../data/rule-catalog.json) is V/10 data, not rows of the `Reference` table: `triggers`, `actions`, `unitActions`, `conditions`, `controls`, `starts`, `targets`, `crossDomain`. `actions`, `conditions`, `controls`, `starts`, `targets`, `crossDomain` are the same values as `GET /reference` returns (`ruleActions`, `ruleConditions`, `ruleControls`, `ruleStarts`, `ruleTargets`, `ruleCrossDomainActions`): `mapping.tsv` refers to the file (V/11.3 Н1.1), there is no copy. **Decided:** `actions` are only the actions of the system, `rule runrule` (the contract: `ruleActions` «здесь — действия системы, не объекта»); the actions of an object are `unitActions` `{action, kinds, params}`, the triggers are `triggers` `{event, kinds, match}`. The contract has no reference for these two (`Reference.ruleTriggers` is removed): they are the events and the actions with `ruleAction` of the kind descriptions (`UnitKind.events`, `UnitKind.actions`; [`kinds.json`](../data/kinds.json), chapter 14 Н1.1). The catalogue keeps them in one place for the harness and the translator (Н1.4) — what the evaluator executes — and MUST agree with `kinds.json`: the same codes on the same kinds, `match` — the fields of `EventDescriptor.params`, `params` — the fields of `ActionDescriptor.params`, `optional` — outside `required`, `options` — the `enum`; `kinds` — the kinds whose description has this event or action. The catalogue of the form is wider than the harness: `assist_omit` names the steps that the harness does not propose, and reconciliation refuses them (Н1.5): the form expresses them, the assistant does not; since ADR-0093 addendum (9) it is empty. The camera triggers also include `disappeared`, `abandoned`, `crowd` (ADR-0112 addendum (15) item 2; V/01.2 `det_kind`). The catalogue does not carry `via` of an `escalate` level: `ruleControls` of the contract has no such field; the judgement is Н1.5. The catalogue is in the words of the contract: the input `io.input` is `active`, `normal`; the canon of the V/07 row and of the V/01.2 event remains `closed`, `open`; the translation is V/11.3 Н1.3.

### Н1.4 The plan

`propose_plan {summary, edits_json}`; `edits_json` is the neutral plan as a JSON string, `[edit]` (ADR-0093 addendum (10) item 2, (11)):

```yaml
createRule:     {op, rule: Rule}
updateRule:     {op, rule: <rule name>, set: {changed fields of Rule}}; a list is written whole
createSchedule: {op, ref, name, week: [Mon … Sun, per day [["HH:MM","HH:MM"], …]]}; a reference to it is "@<ref>" instead of a name
createWindow:   {op, name, rules?: [<rule name>], schedule: <schedule name> | "@<ref>"}; no rules — all rules
Rule:     {name, start, runAt, windowSec, repeat, ratePerMinute, enabled, logOnly, triggers, conditions, steps}
target:   "<name>" | ["<name>", …] | {folder: <path>, except?: [<name>]} | "event" — only in steps and conditions
name:     camera — "<camera>"; sensor, relay, unit — "<camera> / <name>"; folder — the path from the root "<folder> / … / <folder>"
trigger:  {kind, target, value?, word?, port?}
step:     {action, target?, params of the action by unitActions} | {action: runrule, rule: <rule name>}
          | {control: wait, sec} | {control: waitEvent, event: <trigger>, sec, yes, no}
          | {control: ask, text, role: <role name>, sec, default, yes, no}
          | {control: escalate, levels: [{role: <role name>, via, waitMin}], stop}
condition: {kind, schedule?: <name> | "@<ref>", modes?: [<name>], target?, value?}
```

Only the concepts of the contract: names as the tools and the overview give them, no ids, no words of a V/07 row (`sub`, `when`, `then`, `unit`, `cam`). Names are compared case-insensitively. A child and a unit are named together with the camera, a folder by the path from the root through ` / `: the driver gives the children the same names at every camera («Вход 1», «Реле 1»), and a folder name repeats under different parents. The form is a string, not an object `{device, child}`: a target stays one kind of value, and the model copies the name whole from `find_devices` and the overview, as it used to copy an id; an object would add to the model a choice of form at every target. **Limit:** the form is not measured; the measurement `plan-neutral` ran on a world with unique child names: a repeated measurement by «Пилот» is needed, since the name form changes the prompt. A name that contains ` / ` is parsed by the last separator for a child and by every separator for a folder: such a folder name cannot be named. A trigger of the contract is `{source: unit, kinds, event, target, match}`: `event` is the `kind` of the neutral trigger, `kinds` — those of its row of `triggers` of the catalogue (**Decided:** the contract: `kinds` «виды объектов, чьё это событие»); the subsystem of the V/07 row is derived from the event by the translation of V/11.3: `motion`, `linecross`, `lpr`, `disappeared`, `abandoned`, `crowd` — `det`, `segment.lost` — `rec`, the others — `cam`. A step whose `action` is in `unitActions` of the catalogue is an action of an object `{type: unitAction, action, target, params}`, otherwise an action of the system `{source, action, …}` by `actions` (Н1.3).

**One translator:** neutral plan → contract body. `createRule` — `{table: scenarios, op: create, body}`, `body` is the fields of `RuleCreate`; `updateRule` — `{table: scenarios, op: update, id: <UUID правила>, body}`, `body` is the changed fields; `createSchedule` — `{table: schedules, op: create, ref, body: {name, kind: week, week}}`; `createWindow` — `{table: windows, op: create, body: {name, ruleIds, enabled: false, schedule}}`; no `rules` — `ruleIds: null`; a ready schedule — a copy of its row; `@<ref>` — as is. A target is `RuleTarget`: a name — `include` `{uid}` of the record (a camera, a child, a unit; V/11.4 Н1.6), a folder — `{area: folder, uid}` and `exclude` by `uid`, `"event"` — `fromEvent: true`; a rule name — `ruleId` of `{source: rule, action: runrule, ruleId}`; roles — `roleId` of the step `ask` and of an `escalate` level; the fields of an action of an object other than `action`, `target` — into `params` as is, except `state` of `output`: `closed` — `true`, `open` — `false`, `pulse` — `true` with `pulseMs` (**Decided:** `kinds.json`: `state` «true — замкнуть, false — разомкнуть», `pulseMs` «вернуть обратно через столько мс»); the other fields of a step and a level — as is; the branches `yes`, `no` — by the same translation; schedules — `scheduleId`, modes — `modeIds`; `value`, `word`, `port` of a trigger — into `match`, `port` as a string. **Decided:** the condition `online` of the catalogue has no `implements`: a sensor and a relay have no profile, and `ruleConditions` names targets only by profiles; a target of another kind is judged by V/07.3. The body is then translated into a V/07 row by the same code as for writing (V/11.3 Н1.3): the layers of Н1.5 judge by the row; `active`, `normal` of an input are translated into `closed`, `open` before the layer `judge`; `words` judges the row as is. The event `plan` carries the body. **Limit:** on another platform a door for probing a plan is needed instead of V/07.3, and there is none: the validity judgement exists only on w2c.

**Translation refusals** come before the layers, with the result `{valid: false, errors}`; the turn goes on:

| What | Word |
|---|---|
| `edits` is not a list or is empty | `edits: a plan needs at least one edit` |
| `op` is not one of the four or lacks its field | `edits[i]: op is createRule {rule}, updateRule {rule, set}, createSchedule, createWindow` |
| there is no device with the name | `<путь>: there is no device named <имя>: use the names from find_devices` |
| the name of a child or unit without the camera | `<путь>: <имя> is named with its camera: <полные имена через запятую, по возрастанию>` |
| a folder not by the path from the root | `<путь>: <имя> is named by its path: <пути через запятую, по возрастанию>` |
| two cameras, two rules, schedules, modes, roles, two folders of one path with one name | `<путь>: several devices are named <имя>: ask_admin which one`; `rules`, `schedules`, `modes`, `roles`, `folders` — likewise |
| there is no folder, schedule, mode, rule, role | `<путь>: there is no folder named <имя>`; `schedule`, `mode`, `rule`, `role` — likewise |
| an `ask` step or an `escalate` level has no `role` | `<путь>: a question needs the role that answers: ask_admin` |
| the target is empty | `<путь>: no target` |
| a trigger, step, condition is not an object | `<путь>: a trigger is an object`; `step`, `condition` — likewise |
| parsing failed | `the plan has a wrong form` |

The path is `edits[i].triggers[k]`, `.steps[k]`, `.conditions[k]`. A coincidence of names is a question, not a choice: the last one found does not win; with `updateRule` someone else's rule would be edited silently. **Limit:** two cameras or two rules of one name cannot be named by the neutral plan: the plan is refused, the assistant asks, the console form remains. No role — the contract allows `roleId: null`, but V/07.3 judges such a step invalid: the translator refuses earlier, with the word about the role. No `via` at a level — `client`: the contract requires the field, and there is no other subject. A window is `MaintenanceWindowCreate`: the door does not take a reference to a ready schedule (the word of «Архитектор» 2026-11-02); the copy is the fields of `ScheduleWrite` without `id`, `builtin`; `owner` is this window (V/11.4 Н1.11). **Limit:** an edit of the original schedule will not reach the window; the window has its own copy. The fields of `Rule` beyond the plan are translation defaults (ADR-0112 addendum (8) item 2 as read by ADR-0093 addendum (11)); a new rule has `logOnly: true`. **Limit:** the translator is checked by vectors only up to the body ([`vectors/rules-neutral`](../vectors/rules-neutral/README.md)), with the contract ids there as symbols; the translation of the body into a row and into `RuleCreate` is not checked by vectors — left for the repeated measurement.

**Cost** is measured by the threshold of Н1.9 on the service model `gpt-oss`, not by a comparison with MiniMax; the threshold does not stop the service: it is a measure, not a condition of the service (ADR-0093 addendum (40) item 2). A comparison with MiniMax is unfit: there is no return to it, the check code is shared, and the comparison would measure the difference between models, not the readiness of the service (ADR-0093 addendum (39) item 1). The form was chosen by the former measurement; it is history and does not serve as a threshold: `plan-neutral`, MiniMax, 115 requests against the v5 row of the service: correct 102 against 100, real false successes 1 and 1, median turn 8.8 s against 7.8 s, p90 24.1 s against 16.5 s, input 12.7 thousand against 14.0 thousand, the prompt 19% shorter. One run does not prove the difference, and the held-out set is not clean: the author knew the outcomes of its requests. The form is taken as the design: the model does not see the words of V/07, UUIDs and the form of targets, and there is one translator.

Numbers, references and rules MUST be taken only from the tools and the overview. The overview is by name, without ids: folders by the path from the root, schedules with the week, modes, windows, roles, rules.

### Н1.5 Check layers

A new row or the result of a change passes the layers in order; when a layer is not passed, the next one does not judge:

| Layer | What | Norm |
|---|---|---|
| `door` | the row form, the target's rights | V/07.1 Н4, chapter 10 Н1.5 `can_edit` |
| `judge` | validity | V/07.1 Н1.3, V/07.3 Н1.1, Н1.2 |
| `extra` | beyond the norms, below | this chapter |
| `words` | reconciliation with the words of the request, mandatory | Н1.6 |

`extra`, the error words:

| What | Word |
|---|---|
| `via` of an `escalate` level is not `client`; no field — the translator sets `client`, Н1.4 | `<путь>: via <v>: there is no email or Telegram: only client` |
| the target of a step, condition, trigger: a folder, camera, child, server that does not exist; the role of an `ask` step, of an `escalate` level | `<путь>: no <kind> <id>` |
| `auto runrule` whose rule does not exist | `<путь>: no rule <id>` |
| `op: update` for `schedules`, `windows` | `edits[i]: a schedule or a window is only created here: refuse` |
| a new schedule that nobody refers to | `edits: the schedule @<ref> is used by no rule or window: the plan has no rule` |

`via` `tg`, `email` have no subject (V/07.1 Н1.3, **Limit**; ADR-0105 item 5): the assistant does not compose them. The path of a level is `<путь шага>.levels[k]`. A step folder that does not exist is stricter at the assistant than in V/07.3 Н1.2: the evaluator's norm does not check it, the composition is known at run time, and the assistant does not accept a plan with such a target.

### Н1.6 Reconciliation with the words of the request

A mandatory layer (the word of «Архитектор»): the form and V/07.3 do not know what the admin wanted. Deterministic, without a model; the dictionary is the data [`harness/rules/words.json`](../harness/rules/words.json). What is reconciled is what the plan writes: for `update`, only the changed fields. **Text** — the turn texts joined by a line feed; before reconciliation the Unicode spaces (`White_Space`: U+00A0, U+202F, U+2028, `\v` and the like), except the line feed, become the space U+0020: after this `\s` is the same in RE2, JS and Python (ADR-0093 addendum (29) item 7); the typographic apostrophes ’ (U+2019) and ‘ (U+2018) become `'`: «don’t raise» is a negation of `default_no` (norms review (28)–(31) М1).

- `tokens(t)` = the words `[0-9a-zа-яё]+` of t in lower case
- `has(t, n)` = every word of the name n outside generic is in tokens(t), and there are such words
- `hit(t, k)` = the regular expression words[k], or kinds[k], actions[k], is found in t brought to lower case
- `numbers(t)` = the numbers `[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?`, the words of numbers, 1 per word of one_of
- `fits(v, S)` = v / s equals a number of the text for some s in S
- `kind_hit(t, k)` = hit kinds[k]; for `silent`, `disappeared` also hit lost_bare («lost» as a word, with boundaries) when there is no hit segment_lost_phrase («los… recording», «recording … lost», «recording … drops», «stop(s) recording» — narrow, without `.*` and «запис»: «If the bag is lost, start recording» is `disappeared`) and no hit lost_contact (ADR-0093 addendum (35) item 2, (36); norms review (37)–(38) С1)
- `said(t, w)` = w in lower case, with the metacharacters of expressions escaped (the dot in door.forced), between a word start and a word end, is found in t in lower case: «card» is not in «discard»
- `numbered(t, r)` = words.relay_n, with N being the `port` of the step in digits, is found in t in lower case: «open relay 3» at `port` 2 is not the number; the number sign `#`, `№`, `no.`, `number`, `номер` — with and without a space: «relay #2», «реле № 2», «реле номер 2»
- `cut(t)` = t in lower case where each contiguous occurrence of the name of a camera, of a child and of the `title` of a unit of the world — with no letter `[0-9a-zа-яё]`, «_» or «-» to the left and to the right, as `name_in` of V/11.11 Н1.2 — is replaced by a space
- `opens(t)` = hit open_word («open», «откр») and no hit other_state, both on cut(t): «open» in the text is about the state of a relay, and not with «turn on», «off», «pulse»
- `passage(r)` = r is a passage relay: if r has no name or the text names the camera of r and not r (`has` of the camera name, no `has` of the name of r) — hit passage in the last word of the camera name; otherwise hit passage in the last word of the name of r

**Word boundary** (ADR-0093 addendum (25)–(27), (29)) — by `tokens`, not by the engine: a word start is `(?:^|[^0-9a-zа-яё])`, a word end is `(?:$|[^0-9a-zа-яё])`, a word letter is `[0-9a-zа-яё]`, a digit is `[0-9]`, a word between the words of an expression is `\S+`; `\b`, `\w`, `\W`, `\d` are not written in the dictionary: in RE2 and JS they are ASCII only, and before Cyrillic `\b` never matches. The stems of `passage` start at a word start: `lock` is not in Block, `ворот` is not in «поворот»; Gate Light is a light, Gate Barrier is a passage; for a camera, too, the last word counts: Front Door Cam is not a passage (ADR-0093 addendum (20) item 3, (21) item 2, (22) item 1, (23), (29) item 5). **State words** `open_word`, `other_state`, `state_on`, `state_off`, `arm_put` are judged on cut(t): `lock`, `on`, `off` in a name («Back Door Lock», «Turn-On Light») are not a state; `passage`, `numbered` and the other keys — on t (ADR-0093 addendum (29) item 3). A name matched by `has` but not contiguous («lock the back door» with Back Door Lock) is not cut out: `lock` remains a verb.

**Reading of words** (ADR-0093 addendum (19)–(24), (27), (29)–(38)). «Give it N seconds to `<event>`; if still …», «дай N секунд …; если всё ещё …» — a step `wait_event` with `sec` = N, «если всё ещё» — the branch `no`; the word «ждать» is not needed. «Отключи», «выключи камеру», «switch, turn, shut, take … off» — `cam disable`; «отключи реле» — `open` of the relay. Starting a rule — `run`, `runs`, `running`, `ran`, `start`, `starts`, `started`, `launch`, `launched` with boundaries on both sides (`ran` is not in grant), «запуск», «запусти» — with a boundary at the start; «писать» — `rec record` (addendum (30) item 1, (31) item 2). «Lose», «loses», «lost», «losing» … «recording», «теряет запись» — `segment.lost`; «drops off», «dropped off» — `silent`; «lost» without «recording» and «contact» («it's lost») — both `silent` and `disappeared`, the model chooses the kind; «kick off» — `auto runrule`; «stops recording», «stopped recording» — `segment.lost` (addendum (37) item 3); «put … on guard» — `cam arm`, on cut(t), like the state words: «put Fence East and Fence West on guard» is one word between after the names are cut out; there is no bare «on guard» for `cam arm` (addendum (35) items 2, 3, (36)). The check does not translate role names and event words: «охрана» is not Guards, «в тревоге» is not `alarm`, «прикладывает карту» is not `card` — a request that named a role or an event by a word of another language goes to a question (addendum (35) item 4); «off guard» is disarming, like «сними с охраны»: `cam disarm` and `value: false` of the condition `armed` (addendum (34)). «Пропадёт», «пропал» — `silent` and `disappeared`; the model chooses the kind by the subject. «Open» for a passage relay is to switch it on; «for N s» — `state: pulse` with `pulse_ms` = N·1000, or `closed`, `wait` N, `open` of the same relay (V/01.4 Н1.1); «open» and «открой» (addendum (24)) for a relay named by its number and for a relay that is not a passage — a question; for a passage relay «открой» is to switch it on. The contact state `open` of an input does not change the word: `value` is judged by the row `value` of the input. The step **end of «на N секунд»** is `open` of a relay before which, in the walk of the same `then` (the writing order, the branches of a step after it), stands `closed` of the same relay (`unit`, `port`), with a step `wait` between them (ADR-0093 addendum (21) item 3, (29) item 4): the plan `open`, `wait`, `closed` does not exempt its own `open` from judgement. The `<имя>` of a relay in error words is its name; with no name — `relay <port> of <имя камеры>`; `: <words>` and `its words are <words>` — the `msg` joined by commas.

**Limits of reconciliation:** the question text of `ask` is not reconciled with the words; a person sees it at display; `default_yes`, `reset` are regular expressions without measurement, their values are for measurement; «пропад» lets through both `silent` and `disappeared`: a substitution between them is caught by the display of Н1.8 and by observation; «охран» is judged anywhere in the text, not next to the verb: «снимает двор, а склад поставь на охрану» lets `cam disarm` through, «сними с охраны» lets `cam arm` through as well — a substitution of arming and disarming is caught by the display of Н1.8 and by observation; «не пишет» lets `conditions.recording` through, the layer does not judge the value (addendum (33) item 3); «off guard» outside guarding («caught off guard») the layer lets through as disarming; it is caught by the display of Н1.8 and by observation (addendum (34)); the check of «lost» is judged over the whole text, like the negation: a request with a loss of recording and «сумка lost» leads the correct `disappeared` into a question (review of addendum (36) М1); a Russian request to a world with English names of roles and events asks once per rule (addendum (35) item 4); the negation of `default_no` is any «не» one word apart: «не забудь поднять» is a negation too, and `default yes` goes to a question; it is judged over the whole text: «don't raise» anywhere else in the request also removes a named `default yes` — the error leads to a question, not to writing; `running`, `start` let `auto runrule` through instead of motion or recording («while the camera is running», ADR-0093 addendum (31) item 3) — caught by the display and by observation; `выключ`, `off` are in both `switched_off` and the relay: «выключи камеру» lets through a plan with `enabled: false`, «turn off the light» is a word of `cam disable` (ADR-0093 addendum (27) item 2): the substitution is caught by the display of Н1.8 and by `minutes`; without a duration — a question; the direct word `actions["det scan"]` is wide (`scan`, `сканир`), and `asks_step` is narrow: «сканирует номер» lets a plan `det scan` through, but «просканируй камеру за час» gives no needless question; the layer does not judge the branch of `wait_event`: a step in `yes` instead of `no` passes; the event word of a unit is not reconciled against the device, the norm has no listing of words (V/01.4 Н1.4): with a unit that does not raise such a word, the rule is silent; with a unit without `title`, the choice of the wrong unit is not visible; «open» of an input in the text («when the door opens») with a relay step without state words gives a question about the relay, and the model asks it; the check does not know the units of a number: «5 часов» accepts 300 s; the hours of a new schedule and `run_at.days` are not reconciled with the words; `then` is written whole; the fields of a step that are not in the samples, `rec` of `det scan`, `archive` of `rec record`, drop out silently; the order of steps is not reconciled; a dropped condition and a dropped step are not visible: what is reconciled is what is in the plan, not what is missing from it — except a step of `asks_step` that the request names (ADR-0093 addendum (37) item 1, (38)). Hence Н1.7.

### Н1.7 Writing

- **The model does not write.** An accepted plan is shown in words, Н1.8, and only a person's click writes it, with the admin's session, through the contract doors: a new rule — `POST /rules`, a change — `PUT /rules/{ruleId}` with `If-Match` and a body from the current read over the changed fields, a schedule — `POST /schedules`, except a row that only a window refers to: it goes in the window body; a window — `POST /maintenance-windows`; in the order of `edits`, with `@<ref>` being the id of the created row. The assistant does not write the row `auto/scenarios` directly. Partial writing is possible: a row already written stays, the outcome names the rest, there is no rollback.
- **Observation.** A new rule is created with `logOnly: true`, `logOnlySince` being the date: the day of writing by `serverTime`; and if the admin asked for it switched off — `enabled: false`, with the same fields of `Rule`. A new window is created with `enabled: false`: it has no observation; once switched on, it at once and silently mutes a working rule; a person switches it on. It is not applied automatically: the check does not see 27 of 235 mutants of the reference plans — these are a dropped condition and step, `enabled`; a dropped step of `asks_step` named in the request is visible since addendum (37), (38) (Н1.6). A person switches it on, after `POST /rules/test`.
- **Questions and escalation.** A new rule with `ask`, `escalate` is created the same way: in observation the questions disturb nobody, `ask` ends by the `default` branch, `escalate` by the timeout of the last level (ADR-0093 addendum (9) item 1; ADR-0112 addendum (9) item 2; V/07.4 Н1.8). A switched-on one is answered in the form: `GET /rule-prompts`, `POST /rule-prompts/{promptId}/answer` (V/11.4 Н1.10).
- A change of an active rule goes by the same road and does not create observation: the person sees the difference, Н1.8. **Limit:** an edit that adds `ask`, `escalate` to a switched-on rule asks operators right after writing (ADR-0112 addendum (8) item 3).
- The plan reaches the form by the event `plan` with `rules: [{table, op, id?, ref?, body}]`, `AssistRuleEdit`: the translation of `row` into `body` is Н1.4. Writing is done by the form, in order; a refusal stands at its own row; there is no rollback.

### Н1.8 Display

The display names a window with a ready schedule in words: «копия расписания `<имя>`, его правка окно не изменит» (a copy of the schedule; editing it will not change the window). The display reads the rule in words **from the plan row**, not from the model's `summary`: «Create two rules» stood with one rule, «Test» with a working one. The trigger, conditions, steps and targets are named by the catalogue, the labels of the events and actions of the kind descriptions (`UnitKind.events`, `UnitKind.actions`) and the names of the records; for `update`, the fields «было → станет» (was → becomes); next to it, that the rule will be created `logOnly`. `summary` goes separately and is marked «слова модели» (the model's words).

### Н1.9 Measurement

**Threshold** (ADR-0093 addendum (39) items 2, 3; refinements — a decision of «Архитектор»; a measure, not a condition of the service — addendum (40) item 2). The service snapshot recorded in addendum (43) uses `Gemma 4 IQ4`; this threshold was set in the `gpt-oss` measurement. A blind set of 30 cases is written by a new agent from the world and the format, like hold3–hold7: real false successes — 0, correct and to the point — not less than 80%, false refusals — not more than 2. Needless questions — not more than 15 over the whole `rules` set, counted in cases, not in turns. One run, at the service temperature; a «real» false success is marked by «Пилот» in the report, the analysis is by «Архитектор». The numbers are those of «Архитектор» and are revised by measurements.

**Regression** — against the previous `gpt-oss` measurement on the same sets: no previously correct plan has become a false success; a regression is a case that failed in two runs out of two.

**MiniMax** — only for analysis: a failed case is run on it to understand whether it is the model or the check code; there is no scheduled control run (addendum (39) item 4).

**Former measurements** are history, not a threshold:

The snapshot of the form is main `28f65c80a`, older than this norm; the stand is a file, not a cluster; the check is a reading of V/07.3 by the pilot's author, not an implementation; writing was never called. On 101 requests, the models `gpt-oss-20b` and MiniMax:

- Without the layer `words` — 24 false successes of 101. With the layer — 8 of 176 in English and 3 of 75 in Russian; of these the real ones are 5 of 251, that is **2%**; the other 6 are a disputable reference.
- The neutral form, `plan-neutral`, MiniMax, 115 requests: correct 102, real false success 1, median 8.8 s; the v5 row — 100, 1, 7.8 s (Н1.4, **Cost**). Without a model, 74 reference plans went round the circle row → body → neutral → body → row and are equal to themselves; spoiling 6000 plans caused no crashes. The input is the snapshot `fdbe5d5fe`; there is no repeated measurement by the norms of this chapter.
- The held-out set, on which the harness was not tuned: false success 7 → 1 of 26. The most honest number, but the dictionary was written when the texts of this set were already known: there is no clean independent set.
- The check does not see 27 of 235 mutants of the reference plans.
- The cost of the layer is needless questions: 4–6 → 7–12 on 75 requests. This is a safe error.
- `det detect`, `det scan`, a separate set of 12 requests, 2 runs of each model, a newer input: correct 31 of 48, wrong plans 0 of 32, false success 1 of 48, and that is an invented geometry of a line: the model itself wrote `params.zone`. Hence the row `params` of Н1.6. This row and the text about the scan window were not in the run.
- Series 4, `t66`, the blind `hold8`, `gpt-oss`, tag 65: false success 1, correct 23 of 30 (77%) — the threshold of addendum (39) is not reached. On the former regression sets 7 previously correct cases gave a false success without the edits firing; this is one run, and the regression by the rule above («two of two») was not judged. The service is accepted as is; the protection is the display of Н1.8 before a person writes, Н1.7 (addendum (40) items 1, 3).
- The pilot's numbers are a guide, not a guarantee: one run per setting; the spread of one setting used to reach 9 points.
