# V/11.6 — Stand and acceptance: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.6-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 6 of lesson V/11 «Configurator backend» (ADR-0094 A5, Confirmation; addendum (2) item 2; ADR-0050).
Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export), [instructions](11-backend.README.md), contract
[`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is pinned by [`VMS/PLATFORM_PIN`](../../PLATFORM_PIN) (ADR-VMS-0002) (the VMS pin; an increment of rights on top of the norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark «platform norm B» — where the chapter relies on them). The stand `backend` is assembled only on an export with them.

The acceptance of `backend` is not written by the course: the readiness criterion is the API tests of the product itself, which its backend team runs against
its own server. Our `backend` passes the same tests against our stand — in the profile of what we have (chapter 2).
What these tests do not see is checked by the lesson's own suite [`acceptance/`](../acceptance/) (ADR-0094 addendum (17)). The chapter sets norms for the stand, the profile, the service
`/test/*`, the boundary of the suite and the place of `?selftest`.

Norm ids are `V/11.6-MM`.

## N1 Form

- The VMS stand ([the common VMS acceptance README](../../common/acceptance/README.md) «Stand VMS») with the manifests `cam`, `rec`,
  `det`, `auto`, `backend` and the evaluator `auto`: the object mode and the rule probe are its words (chapter 4 N1.10, N1.11); the console with the platform
  norms B1 (the pass with a ticket), B2 (the feed), B3–B6, B7 (static files of the subsystem), B9 (batch write); the stand with
  role kits (TLS): the row `backend/instances/main` with the labels of the stand's server and the worker `backend` with the role leaf
  `backend` (V/11.1 N1.2); the WebUI files next to the manifest `backend` (V/11.1 N1.4). A callback from the leaf of a foreign role and from the
  leaf of another server — 401 (ADR-0094 addendum (6) item 5; the platform acceptance B). Without a kit — the role socket on the loopback (addendum
  (6) item 4). Before the platform increment there is no stand — the chapter is checked by reading.
- The tests call the API at the console address `/backend/api/…` (the contract server — V/11.1 N1.5) with the session of the stand's human (a cookie or
  `Authorization: Bearer`).

### N1.2 Capability profile

- The profile is the «translated» operations of chapter 2 N1.2 and the test table (below). Tests that call «`NO_ROUTE`» operations are excluded by the profile; the check of
  `404 NO_ROUTE` itself for an unknown operation is in the profile.
- The profile is the lesson's table [`data/profile.tsv`](../data/profile.tsv): a row per contract operation, `profile` — `in`, `out`
  (`404 NO_ROUTE`) or `stand` (`/test/*`); it is read by the stand driver (ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 10 (6)). It is reconciled at a new
  contract version (chapter 2). The product answered (ADR-0094 addendum (4)): the capability declaration is in `GET /reference`, the set is
  `one-domain`, the clock is `--clock real`. `backend` serves the declaration by chapter 2 N1.5: its `operations` are the `in` rows
  of the table. The declaration and the table are reconciled; a divergence is a fix of the table, not a silent substitution. `api-rules.js` is in the profile, except the `out` rows of the test table.
- **Test table** — [`data/profile-tests.tsv`](../data/profile-tests.tsv) (ADR-0094 addendum (18)): a row per check of the page at the pinned commit, `profile` —
  `in` (MUST pass), `skip` (the page skips it itself), `out` (outside the profile), with a reason: 68 / 87 / 206 of 361 (the fork `cb728a4`, the contract 0.2.0: 357 tests of 16 files and 4 cross-cutting checks). The page skips only by the test's `needs` (operations, `feature:`, `kind:`, `cap:`, `under:`, `class:`, `implements:`, `clock` against `capabilities` and `GET /kinds`), and not every test
  names what it needs: on an undeclared operation a test fails, and `data-apitest` of the page gives `fail` on any `backend`. Therefore **the driver judges**: one full run, then the rows
  of the report are reconciled with the table. An `in` row — only `PASS`, a `skip` row — only `SKIP`, an `out` row is not judged; a name in the report without a table row is a failure. `&only=` does not fit: with it the page does not reach the cross-cutting check `Неподдержанные операции отвечают 404 NO_ROUTE`, and that check is in the profile. The `out` tests are executed in the same run;
  only their row is not judged.
- **Moving a row.** An `in` row goes to `out` only with a norm as the reason: a failure without such a reason is an error of `backend`, not an edit of the table. If a test takes away an operation or a norm, the move
  goes together with a check in `acceptance/` (ADR-0094 addendum (18) item 3). The cross-cutting check `Каждая объявленная операция покрыта тестами` is `out`: `GET /exports`, `POST /exports/{exportId}/cancel`, `retry-failed`, `POST /exports/{exportId}/files/{fileId}/link` are called only by `out` tests that fail earlier (the export does not finish without the holder `exp`, or `fixturePatch` at the reset). The coverage is held by the own
  suite: 61 of the 116 declared operations that no `in` test calls (by reading the test bodies), `@manual` where the stand does not hold it: `GET /me/export`, `POST /operations/{operationId}/cancel`, `retry-failed`, `POST`, `DELETE /domains/{domainId}/discovery`, `GET /rules`, `PUT`, `DELETE /schedules/{scheduleId}`, `GET /licenses`, `GET /journal`, the five operations `/stream-accounts…`, the four `/archive-watches…`, the three `/archive-scans…`, `GET /rule-prompts`, `POST /rule-prompts/{promptId}/answer`, `GET /tree/paths`, the three `/domains/{domainId}/scenarios…`, the two `/domains/{domainId}/relays…`, `DELETE /holds/{holdId}`, `POST /holds/{holdId}/verify`, `POST /kinds/{kind}/actions/{action}`, `POST /units/{uid}/move`; of `/units/{uid}/…` — `live` (`POST`, `PUT`), `talk`, `timeline`, `playback`, `holds` (`GET`, `POST`), `bookmarks` (`GET`, `POST`), `archive-fetch`, `ptz` (`PUT`, `DELETE`), `presets/{n}` (`PUT`, `DELETE`), `recording-target`, `recording-days`, `creatable`, `schema`; `POST /units/validate`; the eight operations `/exports…` (chapter 2 N1.2, No. 115–122).
  For us the product's test base is narrower than the contract: 65 tests and 3 cross-cutting checks of 361.
- **The set.** The driver gives the set in the form of the contract 0.2.0: the objects as `units` (the kind codes, `Unit.folders`), the kind descriptions as `kinds` (those of `GET /kinds`). The sections from which the tests take the expected values (`FX.domains`, `servers`, `devices`, `folders` with `deviceIds`, `archives`, `volumes`) are derived by the page itself from `units` (`legacyFixture` of `api/server.js`, `tests/harness.js`), therefore the stub loads the page's scripts as they are. A test is skipped by `kind:` only when the kind is not declared or has no `create` and the set has no object of it (`tests/harness.js`): the set has no objects of the kinds `sensor`, `relay`, `mic`, `spk`, `storage`, `ipsrv`, `acfa…`, `eventsrc` — they are written by the holders, and the stand has none.
- **The clock.** With `clock=real` the page skips the tests with `needs: clock` (`skip`). The test with a branch on the stand time («Current administrator and server clock») is `in`.
- Tests that rely on the product's reference set (domains, servers, devices of its demo data) run on our stand
  against the stand's own reference set (N1.3); where the product's set is not expressible for us, the test is outside the profile, the reason — in the list.

### N1.3 Service `/test/*`

- Only on the stand (ADR-0050: process configuration and the loopback): the stand sets the key `TEST_SUPPORT` = `1` of the subsystem file, and
  only passes with the loopback mark `X-W2C-Loopback: 1` are executed — the console sets it when its TCP client is a loopback
  (V/11.1 N1.5; the platform norm B1; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 4); otherwise — `404 TEST_SUPPORT_OFF`
  (the contract; ADR-0094 addendum (2) item 2). `backend` does not judge its own TCP peer: it is always the console. The subsystem file
  `/etc/backend/backend.env` is read by the worker by the [environment file contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/env-file.md)
  (`W2C_SUB_ENV`, P/02.4 N1.2; no file — the defaults; not readable or a value not by the grammar — a start refusal, code 2;
  the summary — V/09.2):

| Key | Read by | Grammar | Default | Norm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `TEST_SUPPORT` | the worker `backend` | `0` \| `1` | `0` | N1.3 |

- `POST /test/reset` — the stand brings the cluster to its reference set through the doors of the console and of the domain with the ticket: it cancels the unfinished operations and waits for their `state: done` (the roots are busy until completion, P/06.9 N1.3): the door has no listing of operations, therefore it takes the ids by walking the table `backend/repeats`, the rows `op: createOperation` with the field `operation`; a finished one answers 409 — skip it; `retry-failed` has no memory row, therefore with `TEST_SUPPORT` = `1` it writes such a row itself: `idem_key` — `retry:<new id>`, `user` — the human of the ticket, `h` — as for a memory row (chapter 4 N1.4) from that human and `idem_key`, `body` — SHA-256 of the request body, `state: done`; it removes the records outside the set, writes the rows of `cam`, `rec`, `det`, `auto` and of its tables, the volumes, folders, humans, roles, grants, and removes the rows `history`, `undo`, `repeats`: `GET /operations` reads them. The timeout is the key `api/test` in `wait` of the manifest: the five seconds of `PASS_WAIT` are not enough for the set. The event log grows and is not rolled back by a reset: its tests are `out`.
  A camera number does not repeat, and a folder id is random (P/06.1, P/06.8): a reset does not bring back a removed record of the set with its former id, therefore the tests that remove it are `out`. There is no substitution of ids in the stub.
  The `out` tests are executed, therefore the reset does not write the records they removed and does not fail on them, and the `in` tests do not rely on them: the first camera with a child and the camera of the first archive in the set are different.
  All of this is done by ordinary writes, therefore the stream carries their messages `entity.*`, and `test.reset` is not sent (chapter 5
  N1.2; the analysis of the V/11 review, Claude S-19). Tests that wait for `test.reset` are outside the profile, as a row of the profile list with
  a reason. `POST /test/clock` — a shift of the stand clock if the stand provides it (the platform clock on the stand is a norm of the
  platform stand), otherwise — `404 TEST_SUPPORT_OFF`.
- `POST /test/stream-elsewhere` — 204: the open streams of this instance go on, `PUT /stream/{clientId}/watch` on them — 404 `NOT_FOUND`, as with a foreign instance; a stream opened after the call is an ordinary one. The mark lives in the stream's memory; nothing is written (chapter 5 N1.2).
- `POST /test/events` — `404 NO_ROUTE` (chapter 2 N1.2, No. 55; the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 15): the contract promises that
  the server changes an object by an event from outside (the link `online`, `fault`, an object found or lost, a new parent; an input signal, a log record,
  a scenario outcome, the domain link, a kind event) and sends the change into the stream, while for us these are words of the heartbeat of the holders
  (`cam` — V/01.2) and of the domain; a door cannot fake them. Tests that rely on it are outside the profile.
- `PUT /test/kinds/{kind}` — `404 NO_ROUTE` (chapter 2 N1.2, No. 126): the kind descriptions are built from the manifests (chapter 14 N1.5), a
  description is not replaced on the fly. Tests that rely on it are outside the profile.
- The stand does not execute `fixturePatch`, `actAs` of the body of `POST /test/reset`: 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: fixturePatch` | `actAs`; a set other than `one-domain` in `fixture` — 404 `NOT_FOUND` (the contract: the stand knows the sets that fit its system); `freezeClock: true` or no field `freezeClock` (the contract's default is «stop the clock») — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: freezeClock`: the stand's clock goes (`--clock real`; the contract: a stand that cannot stop the clock accepts only `false`). The reference set is one, and `actAs` of the contract is another administrator, which our own suite
  does not see either: it checks a second human with fewer rights (N5). Their tests are `out`.
- **Optional scale sets.** `large` and `xl` are optional upstream UI measurement sets, outside this stand profile; neither is required, and this stand answers 404 `NOT_FOUND` for them.
- The stand's reference set is its own, from the lesson's data **after the stand B** (ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 10 (4)): the product's set is
  not expressible for us as a whole (domains, servers, roles with untranslatable values). The tests take the expected values from `CONSOLEM_FIXTURES`, therefore the driver gives them this same set, in the form of
  `api/fixture.js`, under the name `one-domain`; there are no catalogues in the set, nor the address `10.9.9.9:80` (the tests' new cameras take it, `unique`); a server `online: false` is there: `api-hardware.js` reads it at load, and the tests with it are `out`.
  The set holds one volume each of `backup`, `edge`, `incidents`: `api-archive.js` reads them at load, otherwise the file does not load and the cross-cutting check fails. The camera «Главный вход» is without the detector `motion`: the test of a child object creates it; at least one camera with the detector `motion` exists: the test `noDetector` expects a selection of not all cameras. Cameras whose server is online — at least 20: the long operation of the occupancy and pause tests runs on them. Top-level cameras are on two servers or more, a record of a server `online: false` will do: the stream selection test takes a camera on another server, and editing the name does not need a link. The set's `kinds` are those of `GET /kinds`: `api-archive.js` reads the enum `detectorKind` of `det` and `x-archive` of `archive.edge` at load. An allocated online server with three free non-system disks is there, and every storage names a server of the set: `api-storage.js` reads them at load. An allocated online server without `archive.edge` with a free non-system disk is there: the `in` test of the storage kinds builds its row on it.
- **Rules of the set.** The set carries rules and schedules with the names that the tests expect (`Охрана входа ночью`, `Тревога на периметре`, `Камера пропала — писать соседнюю`, `Внерабочее время в будни`, `Ночь`, `По средам 02:00–03:00`, `Рабочие дни`, `Праздники РФ`), only with declared steps (chapter 2 N1.5): `ask`, `wall`, `armed` of the product's set are not kept for us in an enabled rule. There is no `recordMode: sched` in the set. `ruleFires` of the set is empty.
- **Search double.** The stand provides no live search, therefore the rows of the base worker `cam` (V/01.8) are written by the stand program through the store door. After each `POST /test/reset` — a finished search: the row
  `cam/searches/<CLUSTER>` with an expired `until` and the rows `cam/found` of the set's findings. To a row `cam/probes` — an answer with `model` by the set's table; a `vendor` outside the table — an answer without `model`. `network` and `discovery` of the set `one-domain` are the same findings,
  `id` — by chapter 4 N1.6; `reference.deviceModels` of the set names the `model` of these answers.
- **Versions of the set.** The store index does not repeat, therefore after a reset the versions are new, while the tests send the versions from `FX`, and the object there is one for the whole run. After each `POST /test/reset` the stub
  rereads the records and updates `version` **in place** in the objects of `FX` (its `units` and the derived sections): the test files are executed as they are, `draft()` copies `FX.access` at the call and gets the fresh value.

### N1.4 `?selftest`

`?selftest` is the self-check of the product's WebUI from inside the page (the interface scenarios `tests/selftest.js` and `tests/ui-*.js`
of the product). It is not part of the WebUI build (V/11.1 N1.4: there is no built-in server, no sets and no self-check in the build), therefore
it is not on our stand. The product checks the build under the console CSP itself: its build check brings up the product stand in the
role of the console (`/backend/` with the header `script-src 'self'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'none'`, the API under
`/backend/api/`) and opens the self-check of the fork (the answer to the request `2026-10-29-w2c-webui-csp-request.md`). The item `?selftest` of ADR-0094 Confirmation is executed by
the fork with this check on every build that we pin; the lesson's acceptance on our stand is the API tests (N1.2).

## N3 Pass

```
VMS stand with role kits + console with B1–B7, B9 + backend by labels (TEST_SUPPORT=1 of the subsystem file)
  tests api-*.js in a full run against /backend/api/… → the driver reconciles the report with data/profile-tests.tsv
  /test/reset, /test/clock, /test/stream-elsewhere with the loopback mark and TEST_SUPPORT=1 → execute (N1.3); otherwise → 404 TEST_SUPPORT_OFF
  fixturePatch, actAs, freezeClock not false in the body of /test/reset → 422 NOT_SUPPORTED; fixture other than one-domain → 404 NOT_FOUND
  /test/events, PUT /test/kinds/{kind} → 404 NO_ROUTE
```

## N5 Acceptance

The consolem API tests in the capability profile — on the stand N1.1 after the platform increment; before it — by reading. `?selftest` is
the build check by the product (N1.4).
The own suite is [`acceptance/4/`](../acceptance/4/README.md), [`acceptance/8/`](../acceptance/8/README.md) (ADR-0094 addenda (17), (18)): only what the consolem tests in the profile do not
see; each check names it. Classes: a refusal to an ordinary human, the translation of rights of chapter 8, worker failures and the memory `backend/repeats`, profile operations without a test, the search on the double. It is executed by the acceptance
runner through the same doors as the stand N1.1; the first run is with the first generation of V/11; before it the scenarios are the text of a norm.

## Implementation decides

The implementation MUST name in its description the command by which the stand runs the product tests.

The way is **without a browser**: the product's test files are executed as they are in Node.js, and the page is replaced by the stand stub: the page's scripts `api/*.js` and `tests/harness.js` are loaded as the page loads them, the stub gives (`location` with `?api=<address>/backend/api&fixture=one-domain&clock=real`,
`document`, `EventSource` on top of `fetch`); `fetch` carries `Authorization: Bearer` of the stand's human. A request without `Origin` is not judged (the gate and pass contract, `csrf`).
A trial on the sample of the product stand (`ef7175b`; it was not repeated on the contract 0.2.0):
84 passed, 0 failed, 9 skipped as what the set cannot do; with our console the way is not checked — the first run will show it.

The other ways are not feasible (ADR-0094 addendum (17) item 6: we do not introduce CORS):

- the page from disk, including starting it with the product's scripts: `Origin: null` with the cookie is foreign, 403, and `api/client.js` has no other login: it sends only
  the cookie;
- the page from the console address: the static door does not serve `html` (P/06.5, the table of extensions), the set `files` of the manifest is only the WebUI files (V/11.1 N1.4), and `tests/api.html` holds
  an inline script.
