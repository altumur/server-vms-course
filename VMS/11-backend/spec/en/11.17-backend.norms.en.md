# V/11.17 — Playback and live: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`11.17-backend.norms.md`](../11.17-backend.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 17 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the former chapter V/11.17 of the lesson «Страница VMS» — ADR-0118). [Lecture](../../11-backend.md),
[instructions](../11-backend.README.md), the contract — [`consolem.openapi.json`](../../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the chapter was written on `8fc3aa2`; stream delivery — `41d3ec8`; the stream at the gateway door —
`b41527f`).

The tab «Просмотр» plays back the recorded material in pieces from the doors of the recorders, live — over WebRTC at the gateway door. The contract gives the addresses and the tokens; the bytes go past `backend`. The chapter sets norms for the client: pieces and the rule «each moment — once», the repeat on a refusal, the order of live, the hang-up, reconnection, the feed, captions, talk, pan-tilt and relays. The doors — V/02.5 Н1.6 (the piece), V/03.1–V/03.5 (the gateway); the translation — V/11.4 Н1.15, Н1.22.

Norm ids are `V/11.17-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Unix seconds; a piece is `PIECE` = 60 s.

### Н1.1 Pieces

- Playback is in pieces of 60 s: the form calls `GET /devices/{id}/playback?at&spanId` and plays the `url` of the answer, `<door>/segment/<запись>/e<эпоха>/<fromMs>-<toMs>[.<источник>].mp4?t=<токен>` at the door
  that named the span (V/02.5 Н1.6; the door serves a piece as a stream without a length and no more than an hour at a time); the video element
  of the tab plays the piece, the next one — when it ends. The address is good for the piece and the start of the next one: the token is 120 s (V/11.4 Н1.22). A piece of a foreign recording — the address at the door of its cluster; behind NAT `direct: false`: it is not served through the console (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 11).
- **Each moment — once:** spans overlap (two epochs, two volumes, a hold copy); the queue is built from the end of what has already been
  queued, not from the start of the next span; a fenced-off span (`fenced`) plays if it is chosen itself (a click on the span)
  or
  if the moment is not covered by a non-fenced-off one (as V/02 question 3: the fenced-off one loses to the automatic choice; its frames are a
  duplicate,
  not corruption). A jump in time (an event, a mark) goes without `spanId`, `backend` chooses the span: a non-fenced-off span over the moment; if there is none — a fenced-off one.
  Case:
  e1 (`fenced`) and e2 on `[100, 200)`, a jump to 150 — e2.
- From the chosen moment — the pieces that end after it, the first one with an offset to the moment.
- The caption — the interval, the epoch, whether from the backup archive, whether catch-up recorded.
- A piece not served: the video element sees neither the code nor the headers (`busy` — 503 for 5 s, V/02.5 Н1.6, — and a token refusal are
  indistinguishable). A caption and one repeat of the same piece after 5 s **with a new address** (`playback` anew: and a new token); if it
  fails —
  the queue goes on. The cost is one `playback` query per failed piece.
- A seam between pieces once a minute is an accepted cost (no splicing in the browser).

### Н1.2 Live

1. **Delivery and address:** `POST /devices/{id}/live {width, height, network?, streamId?}` combines the delivery and `where` (V/11.4 Н1.15, V/03.1); the network is an explicit choice of the viewer on the tab: «из локальной сети» (`local`, the default) or «извне» (`outside`, a gateway with a public address, V/03.1 Н1.2); the choice is not stored. The answer is `{whepUrl, token, expiresAt, gateway?}`. 409 `OTHER_NETWORK`, `params.labels` — the caption «раздачу создали для другой сети», with the right `admin` on the camera — «переразместить»: `PUT /devices/{id}/live {network}`, which breaks the sessions of all viewers (V/03 question 5), then `POST` again. 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER` — the button «Открыть» by `params.url`, a camera behind NAT — in words `direct: false`.
2. **Offer:** WebRTC receive-only, video and audio (V/03.4 Н1.5), the codec H.265 — with the static payload numbers moved into 96–127 (V/03.4 Н1.2); the camera serves H.265 and the browser does not display it — a refusal in words before the offer;
   `POST <whepUrl>` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>` — `application/sdp`;
   `streamId` — the chosen stream; without it — the size of the video window in screen pixels (`clientWidth`,
   `clientHeight` × `devicePixelRatio`, rounded), the gateway chooses the stream (V/03.3 Н1.2); the window is not laid out, size 0 — no parameters,
   the main one. The size is taken once per display attempt: a new window does not change a running session. 404 `not yet` — repeat (the gateway pass sets up the stream subscription, V/03.3 Н1.2); 404 `stream refused` — the words of
   `detail` to the viewer, without a repeat.
3. **Repeats:** 404 (`not yet` — no subscription yet), 503 (`full`, `busy`), 500 (`answer failed`; V/03.3 Н4), 5xx of `POST /live` and
   a network failure (before the first gateway pass there is no door, V/03.3 Н1.1) — again after 1.5 s, in the same count:
   the first offer
   and no more than 20 repeats, 21 offers in total (V/03.1 Н1.1; a stream in `idle` comes up within 22 s, V/01.2 Н1.2в; an offer after 403 counts within the 21), and only while at least `ICE_WAIT` 5 s remains until `expiresAt`; exhausted — `POST /live` once: a new address — a new series,
   the same one — «шлюз не взял поток». The gateway's `Retry-After` is not visible to the form
   (`cors` of the role certificate contract: `Expose-Headers` without it) — the pause is constant; the repeat window is 20 × 1.5
   s =
   30 s plus `ICE_WAIT` of each offer, within `grace` 60 s; with a small `grace`
   there are fewer repeats (the Architect's decision 2026-10-29). 403
   — `POST /live` anew once;
   any answer other than 201 — in the refusal words.
4. **Success** — 201: the SDP answer, the session — `Location` (`/whep/session/<sid>?gateway=<шлюз>`); the caption — through which gateway. If the answer is not
   accepted by the browser — words, a hang-up, the connection closed.
5. **Hang-up** — `DELETE <Location>` with a fresh token on stop, on leaving the camera, on logout from the session
   and on closing the browser tab (V/03.3 Н1.3); a tab of the same camera is not leaving: «Просмотр» and «Видео» are one client and do not close the connection. `Location` is visible: the gateway serves it in `Expose-Headers`. On closing — `keepalive` with the token from memory; if it does not arrive,
   the gateway removes the session (V/03.5).
   Work started for a former choice is not published after leaving it: a late 201 of an offer — an immediate hang-up `DELETE
   <Location>` and closing of the connection; a late answer of the timeline of the former camera is not drawn; the piece repeat timer is cancelled. The way
   (a choice generation, a cancellation) is a choice of the fork, not a norm.
6. **Reconnection:** the connection is lost (`failed`, `closed`; `disconnected` — after 5 s) — live anew from item 1 (the delivery may have been
   deleted, V/03.1 Н3) after a pause of 1 s (5 s for `disconnected`) plus a random up to 3 s: viewers of one camera do not come all at once
   (V/03.2 Н1.3); the limit — item 3.
7. **The event feed** during live: the snapshot `GET /journal?deviceId&from=<начало − 30 с>&limit=30`, then the stream `journal.event` by observation (V/11.5 Н1.2). `deviceId` — also the rows about the camera (`cameraId`). What has been shown is recognised by the `id` of the row: `id` is unique, duplicates of copies are removed by it. Case: two `lpr` of one `t` with different `id` — both. The order and the caption of a row are the event time: `occurred`, no field — `t`, one time in all places, like the timeline, chapter 16 Н1.3. **The cost and the limit** of the stream — V/11.5: a poll of the event log every 3 s per stream with a list, `truncated` loses rows.
8. **Audio:** a track of the same browser stream, the video element plays it. If the browser refused autoplay with sound — the element without sound (`muted`), the viewer turns it on themselves with the element's buttons. A recording piece with audio
   (V/02.5 Н1.6) is played by the same element.
9. **Talk** (V/03.3 Н1.2а): the button «Говорить» on the tab — the first by number live non-disabled `spk` of this camera (V/01.4 Н1.4а), with the operation `startTalk`.
   The order: the browser microphone; `POST /devices/{spk}/talk` → `{url, token, expiresAt}` (V/11.4 Н1.15: the request `cam`, the delivery, the address); a send-only offer to `url`. 404 `not yet`, 500, 503 — again after 1.5 s, 9 offers in total, after 500 — also `POST` again: the gateway hung up; 409 `busy` — «динамик занят»: the door says it on the offer, not the address issuing; any other answer — the refusal words.
   201 — the button «Закончить разговор», the hang-up — item 5; a late 201 after leaving — an immediate hang-up. Leaving the camera, logout and closing the browser tab end the talk: the microphone does not stay open.
10. **Captions** (ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 6). There is one source, the event log: the rows of kind `text` (V/01.2 Н1.5) of the rows `eventsrc` whose `camera` is this camera (V/01.1 Н1.1г). They are not burned into the frame (V/03.4 Н1.3): the form
    draws them over the video, as text, not markup. Live: the rows `journal.event` with `kind: text`, duplicates — as item 7, a row is shown `overlayDelayMs` after arrival, held for `overlayDurationSec`, `0` and no field — until the next one; the `Device` of the source is read once per display.
    Archive (pieces, Н1.1), **not in the form, chapter 15 Н1.1**, on the witness page: the same rows for the time of the piece, by `occurred`, no field — `t`, plus `overlay_delay_ms`; the display is by frame time. The rows of the pieces read stay for the time the camera is displayed: a caption is not cut off at a piece boundary; before a piece one jumped to the rows are not read, the caption appears from the next one: this is a limit. Live removes the archive captions. A `word` of a row — the colours `color`, `bg` of the element of `words` with the same `msg`; if there is none — without colour.
    A row without `lines` or not in form — no caption; the event log shows it as is. No right `view` on the row `eventsrc` — no rows, no captions. `words`, `overlay_*` not read — without colour, without delay, until the next one: the caption matters more than the styling. The caption delay is the polling cycle of the event log, V/11.5; its cost — on measurement (ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 6).
11. **Pan-tilt and presets** (ADR-0112 addendum (4) item 3): pan-tilt is a block of «Просмотр» with `ptz`, presets — the tab «Видео». Movement while the button is pressed: `PUT /devices/{id}/ptz`, released — `DELETE`, the lease — V/11.4 Н1.16. The button «Пресет» creates a number and does not touch the positions; «Запомнить здесь» — `PUT /devices/{id}/presets/{n} {}`, remembers the current position; the name — `PUT {name}`, never touches the positions. The limit is `Device.presetMax`. A camera behind NAT: **limit**, ADR-0111 addendum (1), V/11.4 Н1.16: the device writes the position, it does not pass through the edit door, 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER`.
12. **Relays manually:** `POST /devices/{relay}/operations/output {state, pulseMs?}`, close, open, pulse; without a connection the button is inactive (V/11.4 Н1.17).

While live, talk, negotiation or playback is going on, the tab does not close the connection on switching to a tab of the same camera (item 5).

## Н3 Pass

```
playback: moment or span → playback → piece url → when it ends, the next from the end of the queued; refusal — 5 s, one repeat with a new address
talk: microphone → POST talk → url: 404/500/503 — 1.5 s × 8 repeats; 409 busy — words; 201 — session
live: POST live {network, streamId} → offer whepUrl: 404 not yet/500/503 — 1.5 s × 20 repeats;
404 stream refused — words; 403 — POST live; 201 — session
break — pause + random → live anew; stop — DELETE <Location>
```

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a piece not served | repeat after 5 s with a new address, then onward | caption | — |
| the gateway did not take the stream after 20 repeats | «шлюз не взял поток» | caption | later |
| the delivery was created for another network | 409 `OTHER_NETWORK`, «раздачу создали для другой сети»; with `admin` — «переразместить» | caption, dialog | re-place or wait for the end of `grace` |
| WHEP refused (not 201, not repeatable) | refusal words | caption | — |
| browser without WebRTC | words | caption | — |
| camera H.265, browser without it | words before the offer | caption | another browser |
| talk: microphone not granted, speaker busy, the gateway did not take the talk | words | form message | later |


The refusal words are the `Problem` of the contract, by chapter 16 Н4.
