# V/11.15 — The form and the viewing tab: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`11.15-backend.norms.md`](../11.15-backend.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 15 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the former chapter V/11.15 of the lesson «Страница VMS» — ADR-0118). [Lecture](../../11-backend.md),
[instructions](../11-backend.README.md), the contract — [`consolem.openapi.json`](../../consolem.openapi.json), the root shell — [`cam.shell.html`](../../../01-cam/cam.shell.html), the witness page — [`Source/vms/cam.shell.html`](../../../../Source/vms/cam.shell.html). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the chapter was written on `8fc3aa2`; the console module — `bbd52ce`: `features`, `pc.ready`,
`select` with a tab, `card.closed`; the source `size`, the common stream fields — `b41527f`).

The VMS console is the form of the consolem fork (ADR-0112 items 1, 2): configuration and viewing in one application. Viewing is the tab «Просмотр» (Viewing) in the camera card (the owner's word, ADR-0112 addendum (13)): one camera on the screen, there is no separate section, no grid and no layouts. The norms of V/08 describe the form; the contract and the form are our specification; the former page is removed (ADR-0112 addendum (19)), its witness is `Source/vms/cam.shell.html`. The chapter sets norms for how the form is served and talks to the contract, what is on the tab, what the form does not do, and the root shell. The timeline and events — chapter 16.

Norm ids are `V/11.15-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Data.** The form has no rows of its own: everything is operations of the contract. The memory of the form is the memory of the browser tab.

### Н1.1 Serving

- The form is the fork build at a pinned commit, the shell [`backend.shell.html`](../../../11-backend/backend.shell.html) is its `index.html` byte for byte, the set and the CSP — V/11.1 Н1.4. The console serves it under `/backend/`. The form is not generated, the implementation serves its bytes as is; a new build is a re-pin by V/11.2 Н1.1.
- **The root shell** `cam.shell.html` (ADR-0112 addendum (19)–(22)): the installer puts it into the field `shell` of the row `platform/manifest/cam` ([P/02.3 Н1.5](../../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.3-installer.norms.md)), the console serves it at `/` as the root `CONSOLE_ROOT=cam`; the root remains, the short `/where`, `/metrics`, `/unplaceable` do not change. On it are the link «Консоль VMS» ("VMS console") to `/backend/` and the platform module P/07: `mount(app, {lang: "ru", sections: ["units", "access", "journal"], subsystems})`, where `subsystems` are the subsystems of `/mounts` without the subsystems of the VMS module (`cam`, `rec`, `live`, `det`, `detjob`, `detedge`, `auto`, `assist`, `exp`, `backend`) and without `psim-*` (addendum (22) and the remark «М» of the review of addendum (22)): their rows are written only by `backend`. On the VMS cluster this is `[]` — not a single one: the module shows the servers, `drain`, `unplaceable`, `break-glass`, the policy, the rights and the log (P/07). The module reads the section `unplaceable` over all subsystems of the catalogue, not only over `subsystems`, and only for reading: unplaceable cameras are visible here, V/11 and the fork have no screen for them (ADR-0112 addendum (23) item 1; P/07 is the share of «Спецификация»). `/mounts` could not be read — also `[]`. A module of another version the shell does not load; it says so in words. Markup without handlers in attributes; the shell has no records of its own and no subject form.
- **The former page is removed** (ADR-0112 item 2, addendum (19)): the fork has closed its listing. Its bytes are the witness `Source/vms/cam.shell.html`; the course tests that are green on it are moved to it, the others are removed (addendum (20) item 2). The condition of removal was: the page is removed when the fork has closed its listing. ### Н1.2 Contract

- The form calls only operations of the contract (V/11.2), the client is `api/client.js` of the build. What is available is `capabilities` of `GET /reference` (V/11.2 Н1.5): no operation — no button either; the form starts without the optional ones.
- The form does not repeat a write by itself: no answer or 5xx — the outcome is unknown, it does not assert a refusal, it re-reads. Except `POST /live`, `/talk`: they make no duplicates, repeats — chapter 17 Н1.2 items 3, 9. A hold and a mark do not re-read on 5xx: the words of the code. A refusal — the words of `Problem`.
- The stream is `GET /stream`, the watched ones — chapter 17 Н1.2 item 7, V/11.5 Н1.2: without a list there are no device events.

### Н1.3 What is on the tab

The camera tabs are «Подключение» (Connection), «Просмотр» (Viewing), «Видео» (Video), «Архив» (Archive), «Детекторы» (Detectors), «Периферия» (Peripherals), «Доступ» (Access); «Просмотр» is only for `camera` and with the operation `startLive`. On the tab, in order:

| Block | What | Norm |
|---|---|---|
| «Просмотр» | live, piece, network, stream, captions, talk | chapter 17 Н1.2 |
| «Поворот» (Pan-tilt) | movement with `ptz` | chapter 17 Н1.2 item 11 |
| «События» (Events) | the feed | chapter 17 Н1.2 item 7 |
| relays | buttons | chapter 17 Н1.2 item 12 |
| timeline | windows, spans, events, «Закрепить» ("Pin"), the unavailable | chapter 16 |
| holds | the list, setting, lifting, «Проверить» ("Check") | chapter 18 Н1.2 |
| marks | setting, ticks on the timeline | chapter 16 Н1.3 |

Presets are on the tab «Видео»: chapter 17 Н1.2 item 11.

### Н1.4 What the form does not do

There is no grid, no layouts and no several cameras on the screen: the owner's word. The bytes of live and of pieces go to the browser past the console and past `backend`, by addresses at the doors (ADR-0015, V/11.4 Н1.22). The form does not assemble the timeline: `backend` assembles it, chapter 16 Н1.2. There is no timeline timer: the read is expensive, it happens on opening the tab and by the window button (V/11.4 Н1.22). The choice of network and stream is not stored.

### Н1.5 Camera

**Camera configuration in the form** — V/11.3, V/11.4. Below is the former page, the witness `Source/vms/cam.shell.html` (Н1.1): the description is held by the course tests moved to the witness, and it is removed together with the last of them (a decision of «Спецификация VMS» on addendum (20) item 2). **Limit:** the page reads `/domain/rec/books/primaries`, and the book is now addressed to everyone (V/02.9 Н1.6): the page does not show recordings of other clusters, there is no fix, the page is being removed.

- **Address** — parsing of `source` for a human: `driverpack://<производитель>/<хост>/ch/<канал>`, otherwise the host and the port of the address; editing —
  only with the right `edit` on the camera, the form is sent by the module.
- **Notes:** a device failure (`phase: failed`) — in the holder's words (`reason`, otherwise `why`), also with `device_state: opening`;
  only `device_state: opening` — check the address, the network, the credentials; `phase: idle` — why there is no stream and when it will open (V/01.2 Н1.2в).
- **Events in words:** the VMS kinds that have a producer — `archive.volume.shrunk`, `archive.footage.dropped`,
  `card.prebuffer.short`, `card.failing`, `archive.backfill.refused`, `archive.backfill.unscanned` (V/02.3, V/02.4, V/02.6, V/02.8); the word of a kind is `display.kinds` of its
  subsystem, otherwise of any VMS subsystem, otherwise of the module (`kindWord`); platform events are described by the module.
- **Camera streams** (V/01.1 Н1.1а; the export `41d3ec8`). A stream is a unit `cam` with `main`; the module draws it as a unit (a tree for
  `about` on its own subsystem is a VMS request after stage C, item 2), the page labels it with the badge «<имя потока> ↑ <камера>» ("stream name ↑ camera").
  The tab «Потоки» (Streams) of a camera (and of a stream — that of its camera): the streams with host, phase and `reason`; «Смотреть» ("Watch") — «Видео» of the stream
  (`select(ref, {tab})`); «＋ Поток» ("＋ Stream") — `POST /cam/cameras {main, profile, source[, cred_username, cred_secret]}` (an empty password means
  the camera's password if the host is the same, V/01.2 Н1.2) — and only these fields: the page gives a stream no common fields (V/01.1 Н1.1а).
  The viewing stream is chosen by the gateway by the window size (chapter 17 Н1.2), the page does not store the choice. For the recording stream the page suggests the
  largest frame (chapter 18). The page takes `size` (V/01.2 Н1.4) from the same place as the phase and `reason` of the unit: the element `status[]` of the
  holder's heartbeat comes in the row `rows` of the answer `GET /cam/cameras` ([P/06.1](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) Н2), and the module gives the units of the event
  `refresh` «как они пришли» ("as they came") ([the module contract](../../../../КОНСОЛЬ-МОДУЛЬ-ПЛАТФОРМЫ.md), `refresh`) — this is how
  the page reads the phase too; without `size` there is no hint, the main stream. The common things — the timeline, events, holds, «Архив» — of a stream are
  read from its camera.
- **The card form** — the blocks `display.form` of the manifest `cam`, drawn by the module (P/07 Н1.5), the page adds no fields of its own:
  «Идентификация» (Identification) — `short`, `type` (the words of `options.type`; unavailable when editing: `fixed`), «Подключение» — `mac`, «Потоки» —
  `resolution`, `fps`, «Положение» (Position) — `lat`, `lon`, `azimuth`. The position fields are of type `json`: the module shows the value as text and
  reads it back; an empty field is not sent: a value is cleared by the word `null` in the field.
- **Row kind** (V/01.1 Н1.1б). Frames, recording and detectors are only for `camera`: an `ipsrv`, `eventsrc`, `acfa` row stands in the tree with a device
  icon and a badge with the word of `type`, the tabs «Видео», «Потоки», «Архив» say that there are no frames, the menu items «Смотреть видео» ("Watch video") and «Архив»
  it does not have, it does not go into the choice of the recording camera (chapter 18). A row without the field `type` is `camera`: the default of the door.
- **Device children** (V/01.1 Н1.2а). `addTreeNodes("unit")` draws under the owner (a camera or an `ipsrv` row) the rows of
  `cam/children` without `gone` — `sensor`, `relay`, `mic`, `spk`, by kind and number; the label is `title` of the setting, otherwise the kind word and `number`;
  a disabled one is dimmed by the rule V/01.4 Н1.4а. The page reads both tables on `refresh`; the console has no table — there are no children; the answer
  was not read — what was read stays, the camera note names the reason; a change of person forgets both tables. When the tables changed, the page calls `repaintTree()` of the module (`features`: `tree.repaint`, P/07 Н1.5): its data did not change, so it will not repaint the tree by itself. The card `child:<ключ>`: kind, number, owner, the fields `title`,
  `enabled` (three values: not said, enabled, disabled), for `sensor`, `relay` — `norm`. A write — with the right `edit` on the owner,
  `POST /cam/child_settings` with the whole row (P/06.1 Н1.6); a field not said is not sent. `enabled` for `mic`, `spk` takes effect
  (V/01.4 Н1.4а); on the `spk` card there is the talk button (chapter 17 Н1.2 item 9).
- **Device units** (V/01.1 Н1.2б). `addTreeNodes("unit")` draws under a row its `cam/device_units` by `n`, flat; the label is `title`, otherwise the kind word and `n`; `enabled: false` — dimmed. They are read together with the children tables, by the same rules. The card `dunit:<ключ>`: kind, number, template, row, owner, `title`, `address`, `enabled`; a write — with the right `admin` on the row, `POST /cam/device_units` with the whole row. The word `units_down` (V/01.2 Н1.4) — in words; `device_state: lost` of a row — «нет связи» ("no connection") on it and on its units. With the right `edit` — an operation: `POST /cam/requests {unit, action: "unit", n, operation}` (V/01.4 Н1.1); the page has no listing of operations, the operator types the word: it has no `.rep`. Creating units is done by `backend` (V/11.4 Н1.2), the page has no button for it.

## Н5 Acceptance

The chapter has no acceptance directory: the behaviour of the form is judged by the fork tests on its build (`tests/ui-view.js`, `ui-live.js`, `ui-stream.js`, `ui-archive.js`, `api-*.js`; there is no real video: a stub WHEP and pieces), the side of `backend` — by V/11 with acceptance.
