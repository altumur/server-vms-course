# V/11.8 — Rights: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`11.8-backend.norms.md`](../11.8-backend.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 8 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0097 §7, ADR-0098 §7, ADR-0099 §7, ADR-0100 §4; ADR-0094 addendum (11), (12)
item 11; ADR-0110 addendum (4), (5); ADR-0111 items 12, 16, 17, 21). [Lecture](../../11-backend.md), [instructions](../11-backend.README.md), contract [`consolem.openapi.json`](../../consolem.openapi.json).
The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the rights increment, ADR-0095–ADR-0100).

The rights of consolem are a snapshot as a whole: roles with levels by fields, people, groups, catalogues. The rights of the platform are the roles and groups of the domain and
the grant lines of each cluster (P/08.4 Н1.3а, the contract `may`). There is one snapshot per domain. The translation goes both ways: `GET /access` assembles the snapshot from
the rows by the grants of the caller, `POST /access/apply` parses the draft back: it writes people, groups and roles in one domain batch, the rights of a role — into its domain row, from which they spread to the clusters, the lines of one cluster — in a separate batch. `backend` has no rights of its own:
the domain doors judge the ticket's person themselves (P/06.3 Н1.9 «Двери домена»).

Norm ids are `V/11.8-MM`; the map is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1
Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

- **Snapshot** — the schema `Access` of the contract.
- **Draft** — the snapshot with the `changes` of the body of `POST /access/apply` applied to it (Н1.5 item 1a).
- **Role point** — one place of consolem with one value of a field: `*`, `folder:F`, `unit:cam/<id>` (Н1.3).
- **Level** — a value of the field `cam` or `arch` from `reference.access.levels`.

## Н1 Form

**Time.** The fields `expiresDate`, `roleUntil`, `rolesUntil` are dates in the time zone `timezone` of the answer of `GET /session`. A date `D`
is written as the end of the day: `expires`, `until` are the wall-clock seconds of the midnight of the day following `D`, in this zone; back —
the date of the second `expires − 1`. `until = 0` and `expires = 0` mean no deadline: the field or the key is absent, `null` for `expiresDate`,
`rolesUntil`.

**Data.** The rows of people, groups, roles and grants come through the domain doors by the ticket (V/11.1 Н1.5). There are two families of its own: the manifest tables
`backend/people/<h>` (Н1.2) and `backend/history/<id>` (Н1.7), both `service: true`.

### Н1.1 Snapshot assembly

`GET /access` (**cost:** with a full domain, about 250 read calls, an estimate, there is no limit) reads `GET /domain/users`, `/domain/groups`, `/domain/roles`, `/domain/grants`, `GET /domain/folders`, `GET /domain` with all pages of `cursor`, `GET /cam/cameras`, `GET /live/accounts` (Н1.11), the tables
`people` and `history`; the cameras of other clusters — Н1.10. The domain doors judge the caller by a filter (P/10 Н1.2д; ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 1): the answer holds only the caller's clusters; none — 403, a refusal of the request: the snapshot is not given out in parts. The camera
listing is needed only for the liveness of the lines `unit:cam/<id>` (Н1.4): a 403 of its door means there is no own listing: there are no visible cameras, and there is no refusal — one who entered by a grant of another cluster has no rights on their own cluster. The rights lines lie at the holder. A read answered 403 — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`: the form hides the section, the start goes on (ADR-0112). Another refusal — Н4: the form does not interrupt the start on it either. Without a connection `POST /access/apply` — 409 `SERVER_OFFLINE`.

**Parts** (ADR-0112 addendum (10)). `GET /access` — `roles`, `catalogs`, `streamGrants`, `version`. The listings are the same snapshot, in pages of chapter 3 Н1.2б, the place is the last id: `GET /access/users` (`q` — a substring of `login`, `name`), `/access/groups` (`q` — `name`), `/access/history`, `/access/cell-history` (`q` — `text`, Н1.7), `/access/roles/{id}/objects` (`q` — `objectId`; an element `{objectId, levels}`, by `objectAccess` of Н1.4, no role — 404 `NOT_FOUND`). **Limit** (ADR-0112 addendum (12) item 5): the snapshot is read whole for each request: the version covers everything, the pages save on the answer, not on the reads; splitting the version — after a measurement.

`rolesUntil` back is the earliest date: the snapshot writes one date for all the roles of the group, so on re-saving it only narrows.

**The snapshot version** is `a` and the hex SHA-256 of the lines `<вид> <имя> <индекс>\n` by bytes: the kind is `user`, `group`, `role` (`lines` in the role row), `grants`
(the row `domain` and the row of each snapshot cluster; for a domain viewer, instead of the index of a cluster row — the hex SHA-256 of its lines without the lines of roles with `lines` that are not `unit:`: the holder pass moves the index but does not change the snapshot), `history` (the readable rows), `people` (only of live people: a row without a person of the snapshot does not
change it), `folder`, `camera` (the cameras named by the lines `unit:cam/<id>` of the snapshot clusters, the name is `<кластер>/<id>`). The index is the `index` of the listing element, for grants — `versions` of the door's answer, for
`camera` — `1` if the camera is in the listing, otherwise `0`: an edit of a camera does not change the snapshot, a deletion does. Folders are included: `folderAccess` depends on them. `ETag` is this version in quotes.

The snapshot covers all the people, groups and roles of the domain, and the rights of roles — of the snapshot clusters (Н1.10). Therefore `apply`
changes only the difference and deletes nothing silently (Н1.5).

### Н1.2 Ids of people, groups, roles

- **Role and group.** The key of the domain row is the consolem `id` as is: a new snapshot record is written with `name` = `id` (ADR-0098 §7).
  A row with a different `name` — the built-in `admin` and records created bypassing `backend` — gets a UUID v5 of `role:<имя>`,
  `group:<имя>` (chapter 4 Н1.6).
- **Person.** A person's name is their login, so `id` cannot be the key. `id` is a field of the row `backend/people/<h>` next to the field `login` (the name), `h` is the hex SHA-256
  of the name: the name `.` or `..` cannot be a table key; there is no row — a UUID
  v5 of `user:<имя>`, as `Me.userId` (chapter 3 Н1.2). `GET /me` reads the same row: otherwise `userId` would diverge from the snapshot.
- A new person with a client `id`: the row `people` is written **after** the domain batch, together with the history rows (Н1.7), `cas ""`, over a former one — `cas`
  of its index. The write goes by the right of consequences, so it cannot happen before the domain judgement: a person with only `view` would write the table and
  move the version for everyone. A break between the batch and this write leaves the person with a v5 `id`: nothing writes the row of an existing person afterwards, the window
  is acknowledged, `id` is only a tag, the rights go by name.
- A deleted person: `apply` removes their row `people` in the same batch as the history rows, `cas` — the snapshot index; there is no row — there is no write. The table has no
  `delete_after`; without the removal it would grow with each deleted name. The row of a person deleted bypassing `backend` stays: it goes neither into the snapshot nor into the version
  (Н1.1), and a new person with this name gets its `id` unless `apply` writes its own.
- **`login` is immutable:** the platform has no renaming of a person. A different `login` with the same `id` and the same `login` with a new `id` — 422
  `NOT_SUPPORTED` (Н1.6): deleting and creating one name would give a removal and a write of one key `people` in one batch.

### Н1.3 Role lines from levels

The translation of the level `cam` by the subsystems of the kind is `(cam, live, rec)`, ranks `0` — `none`, `1` — `view`, `2` — `edit`:

| Level | `cam` | `live` | `rec` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FORBID` | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `ONLY_ARCHIVE` | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| `MON` | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| `ARCHIVE` | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| `ARCH_MANAGE` | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| `FULL` | 2 | 1 | 2 |

- **Points.** `*`: the value is `folderAccess[<id домена>].cam` if present, otherwise `global.dCam`. There are no two lines of one tier from the root and
  from the default: tier 4 would take the greatest of them. `folder:F` — a key of `folderAccess` with the field `cam`. If the root has a
  value, each first-level folder without its own value gets the lines of the root: in consolem the root is a link of every chain.
  `unit:cam/<id>` — a key of `objectAccess` of a camera. `dCam`, `dArch` without a value — `FORBID` (the fields are optional in the schema).
- **Where a line lies.** `*` and `folder:F` — the field `lines` of the domain role row (P/08.4 Н1.3а; ADR-0111 item 16): a source for the whole domain, the clusters and a new member get them by the holder pass, there is no separate distribution. `unit:cam/<id>` — only the grant of the cluster of this camera: the `id` of a camera in another cluster is a different unit, the cluster and `id` give the reverse path of chapter 4 Н1.6; `lines` has no such lines, the pass does not touch them.
- **A line per subsystem.** At each point a line `role:<имя>` is written for each of `cam`, `live`, `rec`, `det`, `detjob`, `detedge`, including
  `none`: an empty tier would hand the decision to a more general one, and `detjob` and `detedge` inherit the camera chains (`about`). `det`, `detjob`, `detedge` are `none` until
  a function raises them: which levels show detectors has been asked of the product (ADR-0097, «Запрос продукту» item 3); this is a narrowing.
- **The pair `MON` and `ONLY_ARCHIVE`** (ADR-0112) — `reference.access.joins`: together they give `ARCHIVE`, between folders of one role and between roles. The translation is the same: the ranks combine by subsystem, there is no refusal. `MON_PROT` is not in the pair: the level is not translated (Н1.6).
- **Functions** act only where the level gives something in this subsystem; this is a narrowing until the product answers (ADR-0099 §7, the decisions file, the analysis of the V/11 review, item 3):

| `feat` | Condition at the point | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| `DEVICES_SETUP` | `cam` ≥ 1 | `cam` — `admin` |
| `ARCHIVES_SETUP` | `rec` ≥ 1 | `rec` — `admin`; an edit through `backend` — also `admin` on `cam` (below) |
| `DETECTORS_SETUP` | the level `cam` is not `FORBID` | `det`, `detjob`, `detedge` — `admin`; a deviation with a reason: the rank of these subsystems is always 0, a condition by subsystem would kill the function; an edit through `backend` — also `admin` on `cam` (below) |
| `EDIT_PTZ_PRESETS` | `cam` ≥ 1 | `cam` — not lower than `edit` |

- **An edit of a detector and of an archive** goes by the anchor row `cam/cameras/<id>` (chapter 4 Н1.2) and requires `admin` on `cam`; there is no special right. A role with `DETECTORS_SETUP`, `ARCHIVES_SETUP` without `DEVICES_SETUP` gets 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED` on such an edit.

- **`arch`** (ADR-0099 §7, А4). `arch` exists only at the point `*`: the value is the field `arch` of the root if present, otherwise `dArch`. `FULL` gives `view` on `*` in `rec` only
  if the level `cam` at the point `*` gives `rec` ≥ 1. Otherwise `arch` is not translated, gives no line, and there is no refusal: this is a narrowing, the template
  «Оператор» (`dCam: MON`, `dArch: FULL`) is applied. Therefore `dArch: FULL` does not open the recordings of all cameras with `dCam: MON`.
- **Saving.** A line of the point `folder:F` is not written if it equals the line of the nearest ancestor folder with a set value. Relative to
  `*` a line is not omitted: a chain without a `folder:` line gives nothing to tier 3.
- **The built-in role** — `unr: true` only for the role `admin`; it has no lines in the grants, its assignments are `assigned` (ADR-0100 §4).
- **Manager.** `USERS_RIGHTS_SETUP` with `ur: ALL` — the line `role:<имя> admin *` in `domain/grants/domain`.
- **`sub: platform`** the translation does not write: folder management (ADR-0097, «Запрос продукту» item 1), `<sub>/policy` and settings snapshots (ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 11) stay with the built-in
  role; such doors refuse any other person, a write of a setting — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`. The assistant — `view` on the model row `assist`, only for the built-in role (ibid.).

### Н1.4 Levels from lines

Reading is the reverse of the translation of Н1.3 and goes by the points of the source (Н1.10):

- **The rank of a subsystem at a point.** The saving of Н1.3 drops a line equal to the ancestor's line, so when there is no line of the subsystem in `folder:F`, the rank is taken from
  the nearest ancestor folder with such a line; there is none — rank `0`. A point without a single line has no value.
- The level of a point by the ranks of `live` and `rec`: `(0, 0)` — `FORBID`, `(0, 1+)` — `ONLY_ARCHIVE`, `(1, 0)` — `MON`, `(1, 1)` — `ARCHIVE`,
  `(1, 2)` — `ARCH_MANAGE`, but with `cam` ≥ `edit`, and `(1, admin)` with `cam` ≥ `edit` — `FULL`; `(1, admin)` otherwise — `ARCHIVE`.
- Different combinations of a level and functions with the same lines read as one: `ARCHIVE` and `ARCH_MANAGE` with `ARCHIVES_SETUP`, `ARCH_MANAGE` with
  `EDIT_PTZ_PRESETS` and `FULL`. Their lines are the same, the rights do not change; the screen shows what was read.
- The point `*` — `global.dCam`; reading gives no key of the root in `folderAccess`. `folder:F` and `unit:cam/<id>` — as they are, the copied
  values of the root too: on re-saving they give the same lines.
- `feat`: `DEVICES_SETUP` — there is `admin` in `cam`; `ARCHIVES_SETUP` — `admin` in `rec`; `DETECTORS_SETUP` — `admin` in `det`;
  `EDIT_PTZ_PRESETS` — `cam` = `edit` at a point whose level is not `FULL`; `USERS_RIGHTS_SETUP` and `ur: ALL` — the manager line,
  otherwise `ur: NO`.
- `dArch` — `FULL` if the rank of `rec` on `*` is not less than `view`, otherwise `FORBID`. A value that did not become a line (Н1.3 `arch`)
  does not come back: the template «Оператор» reads with `dArch: FORBID`.
- `dArch` is not reversible: `FORBID` with the rank of `rec` ≥ 1 reads as `FULL`; the value does not depend on it (there are no `arch` lines beyond `cam`).
- The other fields of `global` — values of `reference.access`: `unr` — Н1.3, `map`, `alert`, `dMic`, `dSpk`, `dSens`, `dRelay`, `dPos`, `dMacro`,
  `dAcfa`, `dVw`, `dPlug` — `FORBID`, `dTel` — `NO`, `depth` — `0`, `restr` — `{}`.
- `bm` — `CREATE` if the rank of `cam` on `*` is not less than `edit`, otherwise `NO`: the bookmark right here is `edit` on the camera (P/06.4 Н2, chapter 4 Н1.24), there is no line of its own. Not reversible, like `dArch`: a value that did not become a line does not come back. **Limit:** `view` does not set a bookmark, `PROTECT`, `EDIT` have no subject.
- **A foreign line** does not change the snapshot and is kept as is (Н1.5 «Difference»), including at a changed role: a line that the translation does not write (`labels:`, another
  `sub`, a deadline `until`), and a line with a scope without a live object — a deleted folder (ADR-0097 §5), a deleted camera. Otherwise its key would return to the draft and
  lock `apply`: the object is not on the screen. Liveness is judged by the reads of Н1.1 with the filter `view`: an object the reader does not see is treated like a deleted one:
  there is no key in the snapshot, the line is kept, the rights are not narrowed.

The translation is reversible on lines: parsing by Н1.3 a snapshot assembled by Н1.4 gives the same lines (all ancestor–descendant pairs and sets of functions were checked by enumeration).

### Н1.5 Applying

`POST /access/apply` — in order:

1. A body not by the contract schema — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field` (chapter 4 Н1.3); no `version`, no `changes` — the same: the version goes in the body, the door has no `If-Match`. The snapshot is read whole (Н1.1); `version` is not the current one — 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`, `current`.
1a. **Draft** — the snapshot to which the `changes` are applied in order; from there on it is judged as a whole (ADR-0112 addendum (10)). `kind`: `role`, `user`, `group` — `set` a whole record, new or former, `delete` — remove; for `role`, on `set` `objectAccess` is taken from the snapshot, `objectAccessCount`, `spread` are not judged. `roleObject` — `id` the role, `value {objectId, levels}`: a key of `objectAccess`, `levels: null` removes it. `streamGrants` — `id: grants`, `value {grants}`, the whole list (Н1.11). `catalog` — Н1.6. A `delete` of what does not exist, a `value` of a wrong kind, a `roleObject` of a role that does not exist — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: changes[i]`. What is not in `changes` is neither changed nor judged.
2. **Draft checks** — without touching the records; the first violation answers:
   - only the records created and changed relative to the snapshot are judged: a former record cannot be corrected by an edit, and rows written bypassing `backend` occur both without `title` and with a
     repeat;
   - an empty name — 422 `NAME_REQUIRED`, `field: name`, `login`; a repeat ignoring case and leading and trailing spaces — 409 `NAME_TAKEN` (role,
     group), 409 `LOGIN_TAKEN` (person), the field: judged at a created record and at a record with a new name, otherwise a duplicate written bypassing `backend` would lock an edit
     of a person's role; a repeat of `id` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: id`;
   - a reference to nowhere: `roleIds`, keys of `roleUntil`, `memberIds`, `UserGroup.roleIds`, `supervision.roleId` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, this field;
     and a role that the draft removes while `supervision.roleId` of another role of the draft refers to it — the same;
   - `supervision.export: true` without `roleId` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: supervision.roleId`: only a person of
     the named role confirms (ADR-0119 addendum (3) item 1);
   - for the role `admin` only the assignments change: a different name, `comment`, deletion — 409 `BUILTIN`;
   - the untranslatable — Н1.6;
   - self-lockout — Н1.8.
3. `dryRun: true` — 200 `{applied: false, access: null}`, `ETag` the former one. There is no domain batch: its checks (items 4–5) are not judged by `dryRun`.
4. **Difference.** A draft record is compared with the snapshot of this `version`; `roleIds`, `memberIds`, `UserGroup.roleIds` — as sets, order is not judged, the answer of `apply` gives the snapshot's order; `apply` does not delete silently:
   - a role, group, person that are not in the snapshot — a creation; those that are in the snapshot but not in the draft — an explicit deletion
     (`op: delete` of the snapshot of this version); there cannot be a record outside the snapshot: the snapshot covers the whole domain;
   - the rights of a changed role — new `lines`: the translation of Н1.3 without `unit:` and the foreign lines of the source (Н1.4), except `unit:`, in the form `{cap, sub?, scope, until}`. An edit of a role removes its divergence: `lines` are newer than the cluster lines (P/08.4 Н3 «расхождение роли»);
   - the lines `unit:` — for each snapshot cluster separately: the lines of a role whose shape in the draft did not change, and the foreign lines (Н1.4) — as read in this cluster, without the field `by`: the door sets it;
     the others are replaced by the translation of Н1.3; the result equals what was read — the cluster is not written; the lines of a deleted role, foreign ones too, do not go into the write `grants`: the cascade `op: delete` removes them
     (P/08.4 Н2);
   - for a cluster administrator (Н1.10) there are no `lines`: the whole rights difference of a changed role is the lines of their snapshot clusters, in the same order;
   - `domain/grants/domain` enters the batch only if the manager line has changed.
5. **Domain batch** — `POST /domain/batch`: the difference of people, groups, roles and of the row `domain`; the snapshot as a whole does not go to the door (the decisions file, the analysis of the V/11 review, item 4); the `cas` of each record is the snapshot index;
   `kind: grants`, `name` — only `domain`; for the role `admin`, `fields` carries only `assigned`. The domain computes the result of the batch by its own state and the difference.
   The difference is empty, for `lines` and the clusters too — there are no writes: neither a batch (the door rejects an empty one, `400 bad batch`) nor the rows of Н1.7. The judgement of rights over `apply` is only the write doors (Н1.9): without it a person
   with only `view` would write the history and move the version for everyone. The answer — 200 `{applied: true, access}` by the snapshot of step 1, `ETag` the former one.
   `Idempotency-Key` — the hex SHA-256 of the bytes of `user`, a zero byte, the value of `version`, a zero byte and the SHA-256 of the body: the worker repeats
   the same batch bytes in the same pass after losing the answer. A client repeat does not reach the batch: the first success moved the version, step 1 answers 412 with `current`.
5a. **Rights** (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 2, ADR-0111 items 12, 16). After the domain batch — a group operation: a refusal of one write does not roll back the others and does not stop the next ones. First the cluster rows: one `POST /domain/batch` per cluster with a difference, in the order of names in UTF-8 bytes, one record `kind: grants`, `name` — the cluster, `cas` — the snapshot index, no row — `""`. Then `PUT /domain/roles/<имя>/lines` `{lines}` for each role with a difference, in the order of names, `If-Match: "<индекс снимка>"`; if the role was in the domain batch — without a condition: its `cas` has already checked it. The order matters: writing the cluster lines later than `lines` would give the cluster the word `local`, and the pass would skip it. The result per cluster is computed by the pass, `role_spread` of the view (Н1.10). The timeout of the route `api/access` has run out — the remaining writes are not made, this is their refusal. A write refusal — a history row `actor: система`, `text` — `права роли «<имя>» не применены: <код>` or `права на кластере <кластер> не применены: <код>` (Н1.7). `Idempotency-Key` — as in item 5, with the name of the cluster or the role and a zero byte before the bytes of `user`.
   **Result.** `clusters` — how many clusters the edit touched: there is a write of `lines` — all the snapshot clusters, otherwise the number of cluster batches; there are no writes — `0`. `notApplied` — a refusal of a cluster write: `{cluster, serverId?, code}`, `serverId` — the first by name record `Server` of this cluster, if visible; a refusal of `lines`: the write is one per domain and has no cluster, so `{cluster: <кластер входа>, roleId, code}`. `code` — a word of Н4. Empty — there is no field. The result of the holder pass is not visible here: it comes in `RoleRow.spread` with the next read.
   **`retryClusters`** — a retry of the spread, `changes` may be empty: a name outside the snapshot clusters — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: retryClusters`. For each cluster, in the order of names, the same `POST /domain/batch` with one record `kind: grants`: the lines of roles with `lines` are brought to `lines`, the others and `unit:` as read; it did not diverge — the cluster is not written. A refusal — as above. Only these clusters are sent: a new write of `lines` would also touch those edited in place.
   No write landed and there was no domain batch — the answer is the first refusal by Н4, nothing is written.
6. **Password.** The `password` of the draft is the field `password` of the record `kind: user` of the domain batch, for a new person and on a change; it gets into neither the rows, the history nor the worker logs, and it is not in the answer. `hasPassword`, `passwordByAdmin` of the draft are not taken. Shorter than `PASSWORD_MIN` — 422 `PASSWORD_POLICY`, `field: password`, `params.id` — the person; it is judged before the batch and with `dryRun`; `bad password` of the door — the same. A new person without `password`: `fields` carries a random password of 256 bits hex, the value is neither stored nor given out anywhere, the person cannot log in until a password is set: `hasPassword` is `true` meanwhile, a limit. An assignment of `admin` to a person with a password set by someone else is judged only by the write: `409 password set by a non-admin` — 409 `PASSWORD_NOT_ADMIN`, `field: roleIds`, `params.id` — the person added by this write; `dryRun` does not see this.
   A password through `backend` raises the alarm `domain.rights_via_backend`, as every such edit does (Н1.9).
7. After the batch — the history rows of Н1.7. The answer — 200 `{applied: true, access}`: the snapshot is read anew, `ETag` is its version.

**Supervision** (ADR-0119 addendum (10)). The rows `exp` are written only by `backend` (`writes_via: [backend]` of the manifest `exp`, P/02.1, P/06.1 Н1.1а):
a part cannot be written by the direct row door bypassing the supervision check, the temporary `NOT_SUPPORTED` is lifted. `supervision.export: true` with the `roleId` of a role R — the domain role has `supervision.exp` = the name of R in the same role record of the domain
batch (P/08.4 Н1.3а), the other keys of `supervision` of the role are kept; `export: false` — the key `exp` is removed. A role being its own
supervisor role is allowed (ADR-0119 addendum (8) item 1). `409 role referenced` of the domain (a reference written bypassing `backend`) — the snapshot is outdated,
as below.

`412 conflict` of the domain batch means that the snapshot is outdated: 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH`, `current` — the snapshot read anew (P/08.4 Н2 `repeat`); for a write of item 5a it is a refusal of that write.

### Н1.6 What `apply` does not translate

The refusal is 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` — the path of the field:

| Value | Why |
|---|---|
| `catalogs` not empty, `UserGroup.source` not `null`, `AccessUser.catalogId` not `null` | there are no catalogues (ADR-0098 §8) |
| a new person with `enabled: false` (`field: enabled`) | `POST /domain/users` has no field `disabled` |
| a different `login` with the same `id` (`field: login`), the same `login` with a new `id` (`field: id`) | Н1.2 |
| `loginSchedule` ≠ 0, `supervision.login: true`, `webSessions`, `mobileSessions` ≠ 0 | ADR-0098 §7; login supervision — `NOT_SUPPORTED`, as before (ADR-0119 addendum (3) item 1) |
| the level `MON_PROT` | there is no subject (ADR-0111 item 21) |
| in `global`: `bm` other than the derivation above; `depth`, `restr`, `map`, `alert`, `dMic`, `dSpk`, `dSens`, `dRelay`, `dPos`, `dMacro`, `dAcfa`, `dVw`, `dPlug` not at the default | there is no subject (ADR-0097 §7, ADR-0111 item 21) |
| in `global`: `dTel` not at the default | there is no right of its own: turning and presets are judged by `edit` on the camera (ADR-0107 item 9, V/11.4 Н1.16) |
| `feat` outside the listing of Н1.3 and `USERS_RIGHTS_SETUP`, including `PROGRAMMING_SETUP` | the automation rights are grants of `auto` rows (`cluster_rows`), there is no role function |
| `unr: true` for a role other than `admin`, `unr: false` for `admin` | ADR-0100 §4 |
| `USERS_RIGHTS_SETUP` with `ur` not `ALL`; `ur: ALL` without it; `ur: DEV` | ADR-0100 §4 |
| a field of `folderAccess`, `objectAccess` other than `cam` (for the root — also `arch`) | kinds without a subsystem |
| a new key of `objectAccess` that is not a camera: a storage, a detector, `vw:`, `macro:`, `plug:` | a volume has no unit, А4 |
| a new key of `folderAccess` that is neither a folder nor the domain | 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: folderAccess` |

Limitation: the `cas` of the batch holds only the changed rows, so Н1.6 and Н1.8 judge what was read. The invariant that an administrator remains is held by the platform (Н1.8).

### Н1.7 History

`GET /access/history`, `/access/cell-history` are the rows of the table `backend/history/<id>`; `history`, `cellHistory` of the body of `apply` are new rows, the time and the author are set by `backend`: `kind` — `history` | `cell`, `when`, `entry`. The platform
log is kept in its own course (`domain.*`, `via: backend/<экземпляр>`): this table only shows the history of the screen.

- The rows are written by a batch `POST /console/batch` **after** the domain batch; there was no batch (an empty difference, Н1.5 item 5) — there are no rows: `id` — a random UUID, `when` — the clock of the answer of `GET /session`,
  `actor` — the `user` of the ticket, for a refusal row of item 5a — `система`. A failure of this write does not change the answer: the rights are already written, the log holds the truth, the cause goes to the worker log.
- It is read newest first, `HISTORY_READ_MAX` = 1000 newest rows: otherwise the snapshot would outgrow the answer limit of the pass, 4 MiB; the retention is `delete_after` of the table, 90 days.
- In the same batch, first, go the rows `people` of new and deleted people (Н1.2): each corresponds to a write of the domain batch, of which there are no more than 63, and
  `BATCH_WRITES` fits them. The body has more history records than there is room left — the extra ones are not written, there is no refusal.

### Н1.8 Self-lockout

`SELF_LOCKOUT` is checked by `backend` on top of the platform (ADR-0100 §4), by the final state of the draft. 409 `SELF_LOCKOUT` if:

- the role `admin` has not a single enabled assignment without an expired deadline, direct or through a group;
- the person of the request removes the role `admin` from themselves (directly or through a group), disables or deletes themselves, sets a past `expiresDate` for themselves or a past deadline
  of their own assignment `admin`: the contract demands not to lock you out.

The invariant that an administrator remains is held by the platform: `409 last admin` of the domain batch is also `SELF_LOCKOUT`. The check of `backend` is needed
for `dryRun` and for removing oneself when there is a second administrator, which the platform allows.

### Н1.9 Who applies

The write is judged by the process `domain`, the batch by row (P/08.4 Н2 «Пакетная дверь»): `kind: grants` of a cluster — `admin` on `*` of this cluster; people, groups, roles, `lines`, the row `domain` — a domain administrator or a manager (`batch_diff`, P/08.4 Н1.3в). The refusal `403 admin only` —
403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, nothing is written. Every edit of the class «admin only» through `backend` raises the alarm
`domain.rights_via_backend` on the domain side (ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 10): `backend` does not send it itself.

Entry is through the `backend` of any cluster: the `need` of the pass is a grant in any cluster of the domain, the entry `anywhere` ([gate and pass contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) `need`; ADR-0110 Д-8).
Limit: a manager reaches `backend` by a positive grant line of some cluster, their own or their role's: the manager line lies in `domain/grants/domain`, does not go into `anywhere` and gives no rights in clusters
(P/08.4 Н1.3, Н1.3в). All cluster lines `none` (Н1.3) — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED` of the console before the worker: neither `GET /access` nor `apply` is available to such a person.

Admission and exclusion of a member change the membership of the domain: they are judged by `admin` of the domain, not by a cluster grant (ADR-0110 addendum (6), ADR-0111 item 17); the doors — chapter 4 Н1.14.

### Н1.10 The domain snapshot

- **The snapshot clusters** are the keys of `grants` of the answer of `GET /domain/grants`, except `domain`: one who is not a domain viewer gets only the clusters where they have `admin` on `*` (P/10 Н1.2д); there is no other cluster in the snapshot. There is the key `domain` — also the `members` of `GET /domain` without their own row: a new member, index `""`, no lines; a 403 of this door — only the keys.
- **The source of values.** The snapshot carries one value of a field. For a domain viewer, `global` and `folderAccess` are read from the `lines` of the role: they are newer than the cluster lines, and the answer of `apply` already shows them. A role without `lines` is a cluster role, as for a non-viewer: the lines of the reading cluster; that is the own cluster if it is in the snapshot, otherwise the smallest name in UTF-8 bytes. `objectAccess` — the union over the snapshot clusters: the key is the id of a camera of its cluster (chapter 4 Н1.6). Folders — `GET /domain/folders`, the id is one for all.
- **Cameras of other clusters.** Liveness — `GET /domain/cam/tables/cameras?cluster=<кластер>`, only for clusters with lines `unit:cam/` and `class: server`; for a camera-cluster — `GET /domain/cam/cameras?cluster=<кластер>` (ADR-0113 addendum (8) item 1). 404 `not reported yet`, 503, a forwarding refusal — the cameras of the cluster are not visible, as in Н1.4: there is no key, the line is kept, the rights are not narrowed.
- **The divergence is visible** (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 2). The result per cluster — `role_spread` of `GET /domain` (P/08.4 Н3 «расхождение роли»). Each pair of a role and a cluster is an element of `RoleRow.spread` `{cluster, state, at}`, `at` — the `at` of the role row; a pair `local` whose lines without `unit:` equal `lines` gives no element. For each pair, at the start of the first page of `GET /access/history`, by cluster and role key, a record `{id, at, actor: "система", text, roleId}`: `text` — `права роли «<имя>» на кластере <кластер> ` and `правлены на месте` (`local`), `ещё не дошли` (`pending`), `не применены` (`refused`); `at` — the `at` of the role row, `id` — UUID v5 `accessNote:<кластер>/<ключ роли>`. `local` whose lines without `unit:` equal `lines` gives no record: this is how the cluster marks a write of `unit:`. The record is computed on reading and is not stored: once the lines converge, it is gone. An observation, not a setting: it does not move the snapshot version. A non-viewer has no field and no records: a limit.
- **An edit on a cluster** is judged as usual: the grant door of this cluster, the last word by write time (ADR-0111 item 16): an edit newer than `lines` — the cluster stays as is, the word `local`; `lines` newer — the pass rewrites, the lines `unit:` stay.
- **A cluster administrator** is `admin` on `*` in a cluster without a right to `domain/grants/domain`: there is no key `domain` in their snapshot. They read the snapshot of their clusters. `apply` is split by rows: a difference only in the rights of roles — the lines of their snapshot clusters, a batch per cluster (Н1.5 item 5a), the word `local`; people, groups, names, `assigned`, a new or deleted role — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, nothing is written: the domain batch goes first, and those belong to a domain viewer. They do not edit the folder tree (P/08.5 Н1.8 `rights`), they do edit the composition: in the answer — chapter 4 Н1.7, on reading — a record `система` at the start of `GET /access/history`: `text` — `дерево папок правит администратор домена`, `at` — the `at` of the row of the role `admin`, `id` — UUID v5 `accessNote:folders`.

### Н1.11 Stream accounts

`Access.streamGrants` (ADR-0112) are grant lines with `subject` = the name of the stream account (V/03.6 Н1.3), not of roles: they only add up, there are no exceptions. The form of a line: `{subject, cap: view, sub: live, scope}`, the door does not give wider than `view` (`names.stream-accounts/.grant`, P/08.5 Н1.7).

- **Reading.** For a stream account from `GET /live/accounts` that has lines in the snapshot clusters: `all` — a line `*` in each snapshot cluster `class: server`, `deviceIds` — the lines `unit:cam/<id>`, the id — chapter 4 Н1.6, `folderIds` — the lines `folder:<id>` of live folders (V/03.6 Н1.4). What a write would not accept, reading does not show: a line of a removed folder, of a removed camera, of a camera-cluster is a foreign line (Н1.4); the snapshot neither shows nor removes it, and it gives no access. A stream account without lines is not in the list. The gateway door did not answer — there is no field `streamGrants`: a write without the field does not change the list. The snapshot version takes the grant lines, not the gateway answer: a flickering gateway does not break drafts. The other lines of the account, labels, `*` not in all such clusters, are foreign lines (Н1.4): they do not change the snapshot and are kept.
- **Write.** No field — no change. The field — the whole list. A stream account not from `GET /live/accounts`, a repeat of an account, `deviceIds` that are not a camera, a camera outside the snapshot clusters or of a camera-cluster, where there is no gateway — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: streamGrants`; `folderIds` — an unknown folder, the same refusal; the gateway door is judged first. The difference — the lines of the snapshot clusters: only what the snapshot showed is removed: an account without an element loses `unit:cam/`, `folder:`, and `*` only if the snapshot showed `all: true`: other `*` are foreign lines; `all` — a line `*` in each snapshot cluster `class: server`; `folderIds` — the lines `folder:<id>` in each such cluster. The gateway door did not answer — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`, with `dryRun` too. The write — Н1.5 item 5a, a batch per cluster, `unknown subject` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: streamGrants`.
- **Removal.** The account is removed: there is no element, the lines are removed by the domain reconciliation by subject (V/03.6 Н1.3); a removed camera and folder — foreign lines, above.
- **Limits.** `all`, `folderIds` — lines only in the snapshot clusters `class: server`: gateways exist only there; on all members it is 250,000 writes; a new server gets no lines until the snapshot is applied again (ADR-0112 addendum (7) item 3).

## Н3 Pass

```
GET /access
  domain doors, /domain/folders, /cam/cameras, tables people, history by the ticket; a read refusal → a refusal of the request (403 of cameras — no visible cameras)

  body not by the schema, no version → 422; snapshot; version not the current one → 412, current; draft from changes

  dryRun → 200 applied: false
  difference empty → 200 applied: true, ETag the former one, no writes
  difference of people, groups, roles → POST /domain/batch (cas of the snapshot, Idempotency-Key)
```
