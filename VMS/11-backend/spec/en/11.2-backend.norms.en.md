# V/11.2 — Contract and differences: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`11.2-backend.norms.md`](../11.2-backend.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 2 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0094 A1, A5, A7). [Lecture](../../11-backend.md),
[instructions](../11-backend.README.md), contract [`consolem.openapi.json`](../../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the increment of rights on top of norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark «platform norm B» — where the chapter relies on them).

The door `backend` is the API of the configurator consolem: the contract is our fork, it goes together with the form (ADR-0112), the translation follows it.
The system differs from the contract in two things only: an operation that we do not have gets `404 NO_ROUTE`; what exactly we have is the declaration
of capabilities in `GET /reference` (the field `capabilities` of the contract, Н1.5). The chapter sets norms for pinning the contract, the fate
of each of the 142 operations, the form of a refusal, the service `/test/*` and the declaration.

Norm ids are `V/11.2-MM`; the map is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

### Н1.3 Refusal — the contract's form

- A refusal is `application/problem+json`, the contract schema `Problem`: `code` (the listing `ProblemCode`), `message`, `field`,
  `params`, `current` (at 412). `backend` MUST NOT introduce codes of its own; there is no code outside `ProblemCode` in any branch.
- The code for a console refusal — chapter 4 (the table of correspondence of the console words and codes to the codes of `ProblemCode`; the word `error` and `field`
  of the console — platform norm B5). A 4xx that is not derived unambiguously from the console word is `VALIDATION_FAILED` without
  `field`.
- The refusals of the console itself on the `api` routes (there is no session, the route is outside the listing, the gate rights, the body is too large, there is no worker
  or it did not answer) are also `Problem`: the map «console status → `code`» of the route in the manifest (V/11.1 Н1.5; platform
  norm B1; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 5). 401 — `UNAUTHORIZED`: the WebUI goes to the console login
  (V/11.1 Н1.3).
- **An infrastructure 5xx** — the console or the store is unavailable, the callback broke off, the memory row is not readable
  (chapter 4 Н1.4): the status 502, 503 or 504, `code: UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`. The same code for a step `not_run` (chapter 4 Н1.8). The contract's `Retry-After` is not returned: the header is not in the closed listing towards the client (V/11.1 Н1.5). A write after a 5xx may have gone through: the outcome — by reading (chapter 4 Н1.3).

### Н1.4 Service `/test/*`

- `POST /test/reset`, `/test/clock`, `/test/stream-elsewhere` (the tag `test-support`) exist only on the stand: `backend` executes them only if the stand
  has declared it by an environment variable of the process (`TEST_SUPPORT=1`, chapter 6 Н1.3) **and** the transfer carries the loopback mark
  `X-W2C-Loopback: 1` — the console sets it when its TCP client is the loopback, and strips an incoming one (V/11.1 Н0, Н1.5; platform
  norm B1; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 4; ADR-0050). `backend` does not check its own TCP peer: it is
  always the console. Otherwise — 404 `TEST_SUPPORT_OFF` (the contract code «служебные операции тестового стенда выключены» — the service operations of the test stand are switched off; ADR-0094
  addendum (2) item 2 — A5 is read so: we do not edit the contract).
- `POST /test/events` — 404 `NO_ROUTE` (table Н1.2, No. 86): the connection and the failure of a camera are, here, words of the heartbeat of the `cam` holder.
- What `/test/reset`, `/test/clock`, `/test/stream-elsewhere` do on our stand — chapter 6.

### Н1.5 Declaration of capabilities

What of the contract we have (table Н1.2), the WebUI and the product tests learn from the declaration of capabilities: the field
`capabilities` of the answer of `GET /reference` (the contract schema `Capabilities`; the product's answer to the owner's request, ADR-0094
addendum (4)). What is declared works by the contract in full; the WebUI hides what is not declared, the tests skip it.

- `operations` — the `operationId` of the 124 «translated» operations of table Н1.2 (with `getMe`, `getReference`, `stream`); the service
  `/test/*` are not in the list, the form calls only what is declared. The list coincides with the `in` rows of the lesson profile (chapter 6 Н1.2).
- The reference `recordModes` of the answer of `GET /reference` is `["none", "always", "motion", "sched", "replica", "backup"]`: `sched` — a recording row with the condition `time` (chapter 3 Н1.3); another value — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`,
  `field: recordMode`.
- The reference `archiveKinds` — the five kinds of `rec/volumes.kind` as a word, `cloud` is not declared (ADR-0112 addendum (5) item 2): the value is the row `Reference` of the table [`mapping.tsv`](../../data/mapping.tsv), the meaning of the fields — chapter 3 Н1.3 «`ArchiveKind`».

  `POST /devices` — as an entry in `failed` (the contract).

## Н3 Pass

For a request `/backend/api/<путь>`:

1. If the operation is not in the contract, return 404 `NO_ROUTE`.
2. If the operation is not declared (Н1.2 «`NO_ROUTE`»), return 404 `NO_ROUTE`.
3. If the path is `/test/reset`, `/test/clock` or `/test/stream-elsewhere`: if `TEST_SUPPORT=1` and `X-W2C-Loopback: 1`, execute it;
   otherwise return 404 `TEST_SUPPORT_OFF`.
4. Otherwise, translate (chapters 3–5); a refusal is `Problem` (Н1.3).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Code | `code` | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the operation is not in the contract or it is not declared | 404 | `NO_ROUTE` | — |
| a device kind or a setting is not declared | 422 | `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` | — |
| `/test/reset`, `/test/clock`, `/test/stream-elsewhere` outside the stand or without the loopback mark | 404 | `TEST_SUPPORT_OFF` | — |
| the console, the store or the repeat memory is unavailable | 502, 503, 504 | `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` (Н1.3) | retry later; a write — after reading |
| there is no session | 401 | `UNAUTHORIZED` | the console login |
