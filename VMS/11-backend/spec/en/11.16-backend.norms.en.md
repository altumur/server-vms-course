# V/11.16 — Timeline and events: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`11.16-backend.norms.md`](../11.16-backend.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 16 of lesson V/11 «Бэкенд конфигуратора» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the former chapter V/11.16 of the lesson «Страница VMS» — ADR-0118). [Lecture](../../11-backend.md),
[instructions](../11-backend.README.md), the contract — [`consolem.openapi.json`](../../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the chapter was written on `8fc3aa2`; `where` without `door` — `d3f8e05`).

The timeline of a camera is all its recordings, in this cluster and in others, from all recorder doors that hold anything about them, and its events. The timeline is assembled by `backend` (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 5, V/11.4 Н1.22); the form draws the pages of `GET /devices/{id}/timeline` and calls the event log. The chapter sets norms for the rules: how to hold a door, how to assemble the timeline, how to name what is unavailable, how to place events and marks. Playback — chapter 17.

Norm ids are `V/11.16-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Unix seconds; the spans of a door are in milliseconds (`start_ms`, `end_ms`, V/02.5 Н1.5).

### Н1.1 The holder door

The door rules are carried out by `backend` (V/11.1 Н1.2а; a recording of another cluster — through the console of the target, V/11.4 Н1.22); the browser calls only the addresses the contract returned: the piece, WHEP.

- The address and the token — `GET /<sub>/where/<id>` of the console ([P/06.5](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.5-console.norms.md) Н3) → `door:
  {url, token, expires, routes}`; the door of a place — `GET /rec/where/<таблица>/<место>?unit=<запись>` (`unit` is the recording id, without
  the subsystem prefix; anything else — 400); a recording of another cluster — through the console of this one: `/domain/at/<кластер>/rec/where/…`
  ([P/06.7](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.7-console.norms.md)); the browser calls the piece address itself: the origin of this console is in
  `CONSOLE_ORIGINS` of the door's cluster (the environment file contract; V/09.2 Н1.2).
- The caller holds the door by the address of the query and takes a new one when less than 20 s remain until `expires`; the margin is by the browser clock,
  a clock skew is closed by 403 and one `where`.
- The token — `Authorization: Bearer` for requests, `?t=` for the video element (the door token contract).
- A token refusal is 403 `forbidden` (the door token contract; V/02.5 Н1.5, V/03.3 Н1.1): the caller asks `where` anew **once**
  and repeats. 401 at the door is `no_role` (a route by role), not the token: it is not repeated. The video element does not see the response code —
  its refusal goes by chapter 17 Н1.1 (one repeat of the piece with a new `where`).
- The answer of another cluster is given in words, both about a recording and about a place: 403 — «там вас не знают», 502 — «его консоль не видна по сети», 404 with
  `error` `no such member` or `no console known` (P/06.7 Н4) — «член отсюда не известен», 503 — «список членов здесь не читается»;
  its archive is not shown, it is not lost. Any other 404 is the member's answer: as for an own door.

### Н1.2 The timeline

**Form:** `GET /devices/{id}/timeline?from&to&limit&cursor`, a page of up to 500, on opening the tab and by the window button, no timer; «Показать дальше» — `nextCursor`. A span is drawn with the words `backupOnly`, `source`, `epoch`, `fenced`; `unavailable` is named by a line. A late answer for the former camera or window is not drawn. The assembly below is carried out by `backend`.

Windows — an hour, 6 hours, a day, all. Sources:

1. the recordings of the camera in this cluster (`rec/recordings` with its `cam`) and the recordings of other clusters by the book of primaries
   (the section `ref`, V/11.4 Н1.22, the elements of its `ref` with the role `primary` or `backup` and `running`; V/02.9 Н1.6). A recording in the domain is a pair (cluster, id): an id is unique only within a cluster. "Not own" is decided by member: `member` ≠
   the name
   of the own cluster (`cluster` of the answer `GET /api/held`, P/06.7 Н1.12; not read — the own ones are recognised by the ids of the recordings of this
   cluster); foreign ones are unique by (`member`, `recording`). Case: an own recording `7` and a recording `7` of the member `m2` — both are on the
   timeline;
2. for each — the recording door: `GET <door>/timeline/<запись>?from&to` (V/02.5 Н1.5);
3. then the place doors: for each place of the places table of `rec` (`places.table` of the manifest) — `GET
   /rec/where/<таблица>/<место>?unit=<запись>` and `/timeline` there; for a recording of another cluster — only its home: `home`
   of the book element (V/02.9 Н1.6), the path — `/domain/at/<член>/rec/where/<таблица>/<home>?unit=<запись>`; for an element without
   `home`
   there is no place query; if the cluster refused in the words of Н1.1, the home is not asked: the answer would be the same; otherwise (404 of the member, 200 without
   `door`, a door failure) the recording door did not answer, but `home` exists — the place is asked anyway.

Merging — without twins by (cluster, recording, `start`, `end`, epoch, source), the door of the recording itself first. The spans of a recording on a
backup volume (and of the recordings of another cluster with the backup role) yield to the primary (`yields`): the remainder is «только в резервном» ("only in backup").
The spans of the device archive (`yields`, source `device`) are part of the answer of the recording door without `stream` (V/02.5 Н1.5): the camera archive,
it plays, it is not part of «только в резервном».

**«Закрепить»** — on the minutes «только в резервном» and on the device minutes. The form glues them from the spans and sends `POST /devices/{id}/archive-fetch` per interval (V/11.4 Н1.25); `backend` sends `POST /rec/requests {unit: <запись основной>, from,
to}` (V/02.8 Н1.4; `unit` is the recording id) per each interval of each primary. The primary is a recording of the own cluster of the same camera and
the same `stream` as the recording of the span, with its home not on a backup volume and without `replica_from` (V/02.6 Н1.4); no own one — no button. An interval is minutes,
glued across a gap of up to 3 s, with the boundaries widened outward to whole seconds (the key `{from:int}-{to:int}`). Some of the requests refused — both
the number filed and the refusal words are stated; a late refusal of the recorder comes into the event log as `archive.backfill.refused`.

**The unavailable is named:** the door did not answer or answered not 200; the fields of the body of `/timeline` (V/02.5 Н1.5): `unreachable`
(recorders), `unavailable` (recording volumes without a live holder, `<том>@<сервер>`); a place 404 with `X-Unreachable` — «недоступно: архив не утрачен, лента может быть неполной»; `where` of a recording or a place answered
without `door` and not 404 — 200 from memory (`lapse`: the location is known, there is no admittance, P/06.5 Н3), 503 or no answer —
the recording or the place is named unavailable. A place 404 without `X-Unreachable` («нет такого места») — silence.

### Н1.3 Events on the timeline

**Form:** `GET /journal?deviceId&from&to&limit=200[&source]` (V/11.3), the translation below; the labels, the filter by subsystem and the jump to a moment are here.

- `GET /events?from&to&unit=cam/<id>&by=occurred&late=<с>` of the console ([P/06.4 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md)):
  the window is by event time: `t` of a row is the writer's clock, a finding over an old archive is written now, while `occurred` is about the minutes
  of the window. `late` is the age of the window end (`now − to`, an integer rounded up), not less than the default 3600: a row written later than `to + late`
  is not visible to the query (the merge contract of the event log `by`, C05-QUERY-LATE-01).
  The events of the camera and of all units "about" it, merged; the fields of the answer — by P/06.4 (`events`, `state`, `truncated`, `withheld`),
  `backend` expects no others. `withheld` — the units whose events the rights could not judge: the labels of the unit or the group were not read (P/06.4): they are not dropped silently —
  the unavailable is named, the timeline and the live feed show the line «скрыто правами: события N единиц» (`N` — the number of units in
  `withheld`; Kimi's addition to the analysis of the V/08 review, question 2).
- An event has one time — `occurred` if the field is present (by the presence of the field, not by its value), otherwise `t`: the tick, the edges of the window
  «всё», the jump and the list. An event written 2 s or more later is labelled «записано в …»; a fenced-off epoch (`fenced`)
  is highlighted; `state` ≠ `live` and a truncation of the window (`truncated`) are stated; fields beyond the fields of the event log (`line` of the event log contract
  and the answer fields of P/06.4) — as `ключ=значение`; a filter by subsystem. Case: an event with `t` = now and `occurred` three hours ago in the window «всё».
- The list — up to 200 latest **by this time** (descending, on a tie — `id` by UTF-8 bytes, like `cut` of the contract
  of the [event log merge](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/events-merge.md)), not in the order of the answer: the classes in the
  answer
  go in groups (first `alarm`, then `observation`). Case: 201 rows `observation` — the newest one is visible.
- **Mark:** `POST /devices/{id}/bookmarks {note, occurred?}`, reading `GET /devices/{id}/bookmarks?from[&to]` (V/11.4 Н1.24; the form sends the `from` of the window), the stream `kind: bookmark`; the ticks on the timeline — by `occurred`. A mark about the past lies in the bucket of its `t`, so the reading goes with `late` by the same rule: `now − to`, not less than 3600.

## Н3 Pass

```
form: window → timeline in pages → journal, bookmarks
backend: window → recordings of the camera (own + book of primaries) → /timeline at the door of each recording → at the place doors (?unit=<запись>)
→ merging without twins, own door first → yielding to backup, «только в резервном» → the unavailable named
→ events of the camera (/events by=occurred, late from the window age), ticks by occurred
```

**Races.** A volume changed its holder between `where` and `/timeline` — the door answers 403, one `where`: a new door; if there is none, the place
is named unavailable; 503 of a closed volume — named at once; the next window will ask again. The token expired between queries — 403, one repeat of `where`. The viewer left the camera (or changed the window) while the timeline
was being built — the form does not draw the late answer for the former one (the work is bound to the choice, chapter 17 Н1.2 item 5).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a token refusal | `where` anew, one repeat | — | — |
| the door of a recording or a place is silent | named unavailable | the timeline caption | — |
| `where` without `door` not 404 (`lapse`, 503) | named unavailable | the timeline caption | — (the next window will ask again) |
| recording volumes without a holder | `unavailable` of the body of `/timeline` — named | the timeline caption | bring the recorder up |
| another cluster refused | in words by the code | the timeline caption | — |
| events are not read | «события недоступны: `<код>`» | a caption | — |
| part of the events is hidden by rights (`withheld`) | «скрыто правами: события N единиц» | a line of the timeline and of the feed | a grant on the units |

The refusal words are the `Problem` of the contract, `message` — only for a network error. One rule for all refusals of the form (chapters 16–19).
