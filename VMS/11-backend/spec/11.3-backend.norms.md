# V/11.3 — Mapping kinds: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.3-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 3 of lesson V/11 «Configurator backend» (ADR-0094 A6). Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](11-backend.README.md), the table [`data/mapping.tsv`](../data/mapping.tsv), the contract
[`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is pinned by [`VMS/PLATFORM_PIN`](../../PLATFORM_PIN) (ADR-VMS-0002) (the VMS pin; the increment of rights over the norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark "platform norm B" — where the chapter relies on them).

The configurator consolem knows domains, servers, devices, folders, storages, volumes, rules, the journal: the contract 0.2.0 (ADR-0116 addendum (9)) gives
the first ones out as kinds of `GET /kinds` and records `Unit` of `/units` (chapter 14), the others by their own schemas. We have the same things —
rows of the VMS subsystems and console doors, but cut differently: a consolem camera is one record, ours is a `cam` row, `rec`
recordings about it and `det` detectors, and the device tree is a `cam/devices` row and children. The chapter sets norms for which consolem kind corresponds to which subsystem and which field. How
to assemble a record and parse an edit — chapter 4. A consolem rule is a row `auto/scenarios/<id>`; schedules, day groups, modes and windows are `auto` tables (ADR-0105): the evaluator reads them, `backend` translates.

Norm ids are `V/11.3-MM`.

## N0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Platform» are [P/01.1
N0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

- **Kind** — `kind` of a record `Unit`, a row of the kind table `GET /kinds` (`camera`, `det`, `archive.local`…; chapter 14 N0, N1.1), or a contract
  schema outside the core (`Rule`, `Schedule`, `Operation`…).
- **Assembly** — a record of a kind made of several rows of our subsystems.

## N1 Form

### N1.1 The table — lesson data

- The mapping is lesson data [`data/mapping.tsv`](../data/mapping.tsv), not code: the `backend` implementation reads the table
  (ADR-0094 A6; «CONSOLEM ADAPTER» § 4 — "the table is better kept as data"). The owner of the table is «VMS Specification».
- Format (this norm): UTF-8, LF, fields separated by TAB, lines with `#` are explanations. Fields: `kind` (a kind code of `GET /kinds`, several — by `|`,
  `archive.*` — the five storage kinds `archive.local`, `archive.network`, `archive.backup`, `archive.edge`, `archive.incidents`; a case in brackets —
  `server(knocking)` a knocking one, `det(builtin)`, `storage(edge)`, `server(patch)`, `folder(create)`, `folder(patch)`, `stream(create)`, `camera(probe)`;
  or a contract schema outside the core), `field` (a path in the record `Unit` — `uid`, `name`, `parentId`, `domainId`, `folders`, `version`,
  `status.link`, `secrets.<name>`, `writable`, `properties.<name>`, `status.observed.<name>`; of a kind action — `params.<name>`, `result.<name>`; of the
  kind description — `x-archive`; outside the core — a field of the schema; several separated by commas; `—` — the kind as a whole), `vms` (the subsystem and the place:
  `<sub>/<rows>`, `<sub>/<tables>`, `<sub>/heartbeats` or a console door `GET /…`; `—` — we have none), `vms_field` (the field on
  our side, a rule or a constant value), `way`, `note`. `way`:
  - `read` — only reading from the place on our side; `both` — reading and writing; `write` — only writing (an input secret:
    `properties.password`, `properties.secret`; the answer carries only `secrets.<name>`), the answer carries no value;
  - `const` — a constant value of the answer named in `vms_field`: JSON, the rule "the smallest by the schema" (empty
    lists and objects, `0`, `""` — for every required field, in depth) or a reference `data:<file>#<key>` to the lesson data: the rule catalogue `ruleActions`, `ruleConditions`, `ruleControls` — [`rule-catalog.json`](../data/rule-catalog.json), V/11.13 N1.3 (the triggers are the events of the kind descriptions, not a reference); `none` — we have none: an optional field is absent from the
    answer.
- **Completeness.** Every field of `Unit` and every property of the kind schema (`kinds.json`, chapter 14 N1.1) of every kind that the operations
  «translated» translate (chapter 2 N1.2), every field of the other answer schemas, and every field of their
  write bodies (`UnitCreate`, `UnitPatch` with the properties of the kind schema, `RuleCreate`, `RuleWrite`, `ScheduleWrite`, `DayGroupWrite`, `ModesWrite`, `ModeCurrentWrite`, `MaintenanceWindowWrite`, `MaintenanceWindowCreate`, `PtzMove`, `PresetWrite`) MUST be named in the
  table. A required field of an answer schema (`required`) is always `read`, `both` or `const` with a value that passes
  the schema (`enum`, `pattern`, `format`); "an empty value of the type" is not given out (the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 8, Claude S-3).
- **A field in a write body** (Claude B-4). **Decided:** the bodies of the kinds are `UnitCreate` (`POST /units`: a row with its `children`;
  the step `createUnit` of `/operations`) and `UnitPatch` (`PATCH /units/{uid}`: JSON Merge Patch of `name`, `folders`, `properties`; the step
  `patch`); the other schemas keep their own bodies (the list "Completeness" above). On the core paths `field` is a JSON Pointer into the body, in
  `POST /units` — into the row (chapter 14 N1.2): `/name`, `/parentId`, `/kind`, `/folders/<i>`, `/children/<i>`, `/properties/<p>`; a volume at
  the creation of a storage — `/children/0/properties/<p>`. Outside the core (rules, schedules, access…) `field` is the field name of the schema.
  - `both`, `write` — into the place on our side (chapter 4);
  - **Decided:** a property is `readOnly` in the kind schema only where our `way` is `read`, `const` and the reference set of the contract marks it
    `readOnly` or does not have it (`kinds.json`, chapter 14 N1.1). Such a property in a body, `null` included — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`,
    `field: /properties/<p>`, `params.keyword: readOnly`. A `read`, `const` property that the reference lets the form send (`serverId`,
    `identifier`, `subtype` of `ipsrv`, `builtin`, `template`, `encrypted`, `storage`, `mode`, `dir`, `mac` of a camera…) is writable in the kind
    schema and outside `Unit.writable`: in a create body a value equal to what reading would give (to the value the record will get by the table)
    is skipped, as is the creation list below; another one — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` — the pointer of this property; in `PATCH` — 422
    `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` (outside `Unit.writable`). `mode`, `dir`, `name` of the first volume and `storage` of a storage are skipped at any value
    (ADR-0111 item 2): `field: /properties/storage` at `edge` judges the address, not this property (N1.3 "The storage kind");
  - **Decided:** `Unit.writable` is given out at every record of a lesson kind (the row `* writable` of `mapping.tsv`): `name` when the kind has
    the capability `rename`, plus the properties of its rows `both` and `write`, narrowed by the record where the chapter says what is not editable
    (the w2c card — N1.3 "`storage` of the w2c camera", a camera-cluster — N1.2b, a foreign record without a link — N1.2a); an ordinary
    `storage` — `writable: []`. A property or `name` outside `Unit.writable` of the record — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` — the pointer of this
    property (`/properties/<p>`, `/name`; the contract `Unit.writable`);
  - **Decided:** a property that is not in the kind schema (`additionalProperties: false`) — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/<p>`,
    `params.keyword: additionalProperties` (chapter 4 N1.3), this replaces ADR-0107 addendum (1) item 4;
  - **Decided:** a change of an `x-immutable` property after creation (the kind schemas of `kinds.json`: `mac`, `configMode`, `templateId` of a camera,
    `template` of `det` and the other marked ones) — the property in `PATCH` or in the step `patch` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/<p>`,
    `params.keyword: x-immutable` (the contract `JsonSchema`); for such a property this refusal replaces `NOT_SUPPORTED` of `Unit.writable` and of `none`;
  - `none` — the default value of the contract answer (no field, `null`; for `configMode` not at creation — `current`: "do not touch the device
    settings", the row `configMode` of `mapping.tsv`) is skipped; another one — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` — the pointer of the property (the
    contract, `Feature`: "the field is in the record, but it cannot be changed"); `file` of a volume is skipped at any value (ADR-0111 item 2);
  - creation (`POST /units`, the step `createUnit`): the fields that the WebUI wizard sends on its own while we have no place for them are skipped without writing and without refusal: `serverId` inside the cluster (the server is an observation, the controller places; it chooses the cluster, an unknown server — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/serverId`, `params.keyword: x-ref`, chapter 4 N1.5, N1.13; `eventsrc` — a label, below); `mac` of a camera (the MAC — only from discovery; the kind schemas of `eventsrc`, `acfa` have no `mac`); `subtype` of `ipsrv`; `children` of `ipsrv` in `UnitCreate` (the composition is found by the holder); a free `identifier` (chapter 4 N1.2);
  - `null` at `both`: `login`, `stream`, `extraArchives`, `short`, `lat`, `lon`, `azimuth`, `mac`, `res`, `fps`, `norm`, `path`, `labels`, `alarmKinds`, `params`, `zone` — the empty value
    of the place; `name` — 422 `NAME_REQUIRED`, `field: /name`; `enabled`, `port`, `recordMode`, `vendor`, `model`, `rtspUrl` — 422
    `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/<p>`, as `ip: null` (N1.3); `firmware`, `videoChannel` — remove the segment or the `source` of the address.
- **`status.link`** (**Decided:**). A row of `mapping.tsv` with the field `status.link` gives `online` where its rule says "true", `offline`
  where "false"; a knocking server (`server(knocking)`: we do not judge the link) — `unknown`.

### N1.2a Domain, own and foreign

`backend` stands on every cluster and through any entry shows the domain (ADR-0110 addendum (2), (4)). Own — the rows of the entry cluster, foreign — the rows of the other members of the domain.

- **The domain.** The field `domain` of the answer `GET /session` ([gate and pass contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md), `session`):

| The field `domain` | What it means | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `{name, root}` | the cluster is in a domain | `domain` — the domain record; own and, with a link, foreign |
| no field | the cluster is outside a domain | `domain` — its own record; `GET domain` is not called, there is no foreign |
| `null` | the row is not read: unknown, not "outside a domain" | the `domain` record is not derived: **Decided:** the domain record (`GET /units?kind=domain`, `GET /units/{uid}` of the domain) and every reading of a record with `domainId` — 503 (chapter 2 N1.3) |

- **Own** — by the doors of the cluster console (chapter 4 N1.1), at any link: `GET domain/at` to the own cluster — 404 `not another member`, so the own cluster is not read through the domain. One who entered by `anywhere` on the own cluster has no rights there: a 403 of the doors of units or tables of the own means this person has no own, not a refusal of reading; the records of the kind are assembled from the foreign.
- **Link.** There is a link with the domain while `GET domain` answers 200 or 403. 403 — there is a link, the foreign is not visible to the person. Another answer — there is no link, including 404 `no domain here` with the field `domain`: the view is not ready yet. The `uid` of the `domain` record does not depend on the link. Without a link the holder is silent, while the door has one timeout: the question `GET domain` MUST wait no longer than `DOMAIN_WAIT` = 2 s, less than the route timeout; a timeout — no link, "only own" is given out in the same request. The answer about the link lives in the instance memory for `DOMAIN_SEEN` = 30 s: while it is "no", `GET domain` is not asked, the form does not wait on every request. The values are without a basis, checked by measurement.
- **Without a link only own, the foreign is hidden** (ADR-0110 addendum (2) item 2): an old snapshot would look live, while writing there is not possible. Listings give out the own; one record with an id not among the own — 503: it could be foreign; not read is not absent. Outside a domain such a record — 404 `NOT_FOUND`.
- **The relay limit** (ADR-0110 addendum (4) item 5, ADR-0111 item 16): there is no rule about cameras behind a relay: when the centre is lost, the server does not show its camera behind it, the cluster behind the relay does not see the domain; both have only the own.
- **Foreign** — from the domain view, by the cluster of the target and by the filter of the caller (the rights contract «Target judgement»):

| What | From where |
|---|---|
| members, their servers | `GET domain`, all pages of `cursor`: `members[]`; a part in `unread` — its members are not read, not removed; **cost:** with a full domain about 250 calls per listing, an estimate, there is no limit |
| knocking ones | `GET domain/members`, all pages of `cursor`: `knocking`; a camera-cluster is accepted by the same action, `status.observed.class` of the `server(knocking)` record — the `class` of the knock: the person confirms it by acceptance (P/08.3 N1.1a) |
| `class` of a member | `members[].class` of `GET domain`, no field — `server` (P/08.1 N1.7) |
| a table row of a foreign one | by listing only at `class: server`: `GET domain/<s>/tables/<table>?cluster=<member>`, the tables `domain.view_tables` of the manifests; one member per call; a camera-cluster — the next row |
| camera-clusters | by pages of the view `GET domain/cam/cameras`, the fields of the view unit (`Unit` of the domain view, not the contract `Unit`); details — by the table of this member, only for a whole record (ADR-0113 addendum (8) item 1) |
| the recording server of a camera by `ref` | `GET domain/rec/recordings`, the fields of the view unit |
| relays and who is behind them | `relays` of `GET domain/topology`; `GET domain/relays/<relay>` → `{relay, rev, members}`, up to 5000 names, one row: `Relay.name` — `relay`, `members` as is, `rev` does not go out; 404 of the door — 404 `NOT_FOUND` (`GET /domains/{id}/relays`, `/relays/{name}`; ADR-0113 addendum (8)) |
| beyond the view | N1.2a "Beyond the view" |

  The fields of a record — the same rows of [`mapping.tsv`](../data/mapping.tsv): `fields` of the snapshot row instead of the fields of the row. The sign of a secret (`secrets.<name>`) — the field name in `secrets` of the snapshot row; the heartbeat words — `phase`, `status`; the holder's server — `server`; labels — `labels`; the version — `revision` (N1.3 "Record version"). The caller and the cluster of the reference — chapter 4 N1.6.
- **What is only at the entry.** `Modes`, `ObjectMode`, `ModeCurrent` — per cluster (ADR-0111 item 10), `Discovery`, the action `probe` of `camera` — on the servers of the entry (item 9), `JournalEvent`, `Operation`, `Me` — of the entry. `License` — a domain book, it needs no view.
- **A member not `ok`.** The units and tables of a silent member stay in the view (P/08.1 N1.3, N1.4a), its records are given out: `status.link` — `offline`, of its `server` records too, `status.observed.fault` — no field.
- **A table not read.** `refused` of the object, 404 `not reported yet`, 503, a broken call — this member's table is not read, rather than empty: the records of the kind for which it is a part are not given out in a listing for this member, reading one — 503, `message` names the member. What is hidden by the filter (`X-Withheld`) — the same: the `items` of the contract carry no word about incompleteness, this is a limit; one hidden record — 404 `NOT_FOUND`.
- **Cost.** There is no group door `/domain/at`: a listing over the domain is table snapshots, a call per table and member, the object is limited (P/04 N1.3b). A full record and a `class: server` row — 10 calls per member: `cameras`, `devices`, `children`, `child_settings`, `device_units`, `rule_state`, `recordings`, `volumes`, `det/units`, `auto/scenarios`, only at `class: server`, of which there are no more than `SERVERS_MAX` 500: no more than 5000. **Limit:** a camera-cluster in a listing is poorer: only the fields of the view unit; children, the device, the rule state are absent until the record is read. Pages — N1.2b. The door gives out an `ETag`, `backend` keeps the snapshot and sends `If-None-Match`: 304 — the snapshot is the same, the number of calls is the same, the answers are empty. The cache is the process memory, the key is the person, the table, the member: the filter differs between people; lost — a full reading (chapter 4 N1.4). Not done within the transfer limit of the route — the request is closed (chapter 5 N1.1). This is a limit. There is no double reading at the foreign: there are no indexes, there is nothing to reconcile.
- **Beyond the view** (ADR-0111 item 15). What the view lacks is read at the console of the target's cluster, only for a whole record, not by listing: `GET domain/at/<member>/<s>/<name>` by a ticket. The right is judged by the target's console; 403 — as at an own door. Not heard (502, 503) — there is no data: 503, `message` names the member. A member behind NAT has no address, `/domain/at` — 404 `no console known`, it has only the view. The listing `GET /rules/{ruleId}/versions` of a foreign rule goes this way, the table `auto/versions`: it does not enter the snapshot, the full text would not fit under its ceiling. `GET /rules/{ruleId}/fires` of a foreign rule — the door `GET /domain/at/<member>/events` with the log query, the record `calls` of `GET domain/at/events`, the right is judged by the target's console. The form calls both doors at start for every rule, so for them a member behind NAT and a refusal of forwarding are not 503 but 200 `{items: []}` and a log line: the contract body is closed, the browser would get no header. **Limit:** for a member behind NAT there is no history and no firings of its rules, the form shows them empty.

### N1.2b Pages, row, tree

The form reads on request, the mode is one at any size (ADR-0112; ADR-0113 addendum (8), (10)).

- **The general rule** (**Decided:** the same for `GET /units` and for the listings that remain). The listings `GET /units`, `/journal`, `/operations`, `/rules/{id}/fires`, `/archive-watches`, `/archive-scans`, `candidates`, `/tree/children`, `/access/*`: `limit` 1…`pageMax`, default 200, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: limit`; the answer is `{items, nextCursor?, total?}`; no `nextCursor` — the end, empty `items` with a live `nextCursor` are lawful: the reader continues from that cursor; an empty page alone is not the end. The `cursor` is opaque and keeps the place, not the row number: an insertion does not shift the pages. It lives one circle of the view; an expired, foreign, not our one — 422 `INVALID_CURSOR`, the form reads from the start. A listing that `backend` assembles wholly from its own cluster and `class: server` is cut by it as well: the place is the last `uid` of the order. Outside a domain — the same rule over the tables of the cluster.
- **`GET /units` gives out records `Unit`, not rows** (**Decided:**). `folders` — only in `GET /units/{uid}`: a listing does not carry it, by cost, as before. A record of the own cluster and of `class: server` — the record of N1.3, the tables by N1.2a. A camera-cluster — a poorer `Unit` from the `cam` unit of a page of the view `GET domain/cam/cameras` of a member `class: camera` (`members[]` of the view), as V/01.7 N1.2: a row with a non-empty `main` is a stream, it does not go into the listing as a camera; the units of members `class: server` are skipped: their record is given by the tables; no `view.type` — `camera`, another one — not a camera-cluster. `name`, `properties.enabled`, `kind` — from `view`; `status.link` — `online` at the unit's `phase` `running`, `idle`, `held` with the unit's `state` `live`, otherwise `offline`; `status.observed.licenseExceeded` — `phase: unlicensed`; `status.observed.fault` — from `status.device_state`; `parentId` — `null`, no property `serverId`; `hasChildren` — `true`, which means "may be": expanding reads the tables of this member and may return empty; `version` — `r<revision>`, this is not the version of the record (N1.3 "Record version"). `properties.ip` and the other properties of the record are not in it. **Limit:** the card of a large domain is poorer (ADR-0113 addendum (8) item 1).
- **`UnitBrief`** is the `unit` of a `TreeNode` (**Decided:**): `uid`, `kind`, `name`, `parentId`, `domainId`, `version`, `subtype`, `brief.enabled`, `brief.serverId`, `status.link`, `status.observed.fault`, `status.observed.licenseExceeded` — the fields of the record (the rows `UnitBrief` of `mapping.tsv`); at a camera-cluster — from the view, as above, `brief.serverId` — `null`.
- **The order of `GET /units`** (**Decided:**). Full pages by the index of the kind, in the order of the contract (`listUnits`), — at `implements` (with or without `domainId`) and at `kind` with `domainId` for `domain`, `server`, `folder`, `archive.*`, `volume`: their listings are whole (the session, the server listings, the folder tree, the volume rows of the members). **Limit:** the contract promises full pages by the kind's index for every `kind` with `domainId`; for the device kinds w2c gives the bounded pass of the view, as without `domainId`. The rest is the bounded pass, as the former `GET /devices`: without `serverId`, `parentId`, `archiveId`, `folderId`, `ref` first the records of the own cluster and of `class: server` by `uid` in bytes, then the pages of the view in the door's order; the place — the last `uid` or the door's `X-Cursor`.
- **Filters of `GET /units` by cost** (**Decided:**; the parameters of `listUnits`). A bounded pass, in the order of parts, not alphabetical: `q` — a substring regardless of case; `fields` — where to search it, separated by commas: `name`, `short`, `uid` (only whole: a part of a `uid` is not searched), property names; `match.field` of the answer — the same word; without `fields` — as the former `q`: `name`, `short`, `ip`, `vendor`, `model` of the record; for a camera-cluster only `name`: the rest is not in the view. `kind` — several — "or" (the former `type`); `implements` — several — "or"; `status` — `online`, `offline`, `unknown` of `status.link` and `run`, `nosignal`, `off`, `license`, several — "or"; `domainId`, `noFolder`, `noRecord`, `noDetector`. `scanPartsMax` is the minimum number of view-door pages examined by a bounded-pass request unless the response reaches `limit` earlier: the door does not name parts, a page is its unit. Beyond that minimum the server MAY fill the page up to `limit` within the existing request bounds; filling before returning a cursor is recommended, not required. The existing per-candidate call bound below still applies. A short or empty page with `nextCursor` is lawful; continuation starts at the stopping place, without losses or repeats. The client does not rely on `scanPartsMax`. `noRecord` — for a record of the own cluster and of `class: server` the table `rec/recordings`; for a camera-cluster `GET /domain/placement/rec/<ref>`, a call per candidate: the recording lies as a unit of the receiver member, in its part, it is not on the camera's page; found on the same page — no call. `noFolder` — there is no row `GET /domain/memberof/<member>/cam/cameras/<id>`, a call per candidate. `noDetector=true` — the cameras without detectors, `noDetector=<kind>` (a detector kind code) — the cameras whose `status.observed.detectorKinds` lack this kind (below, "`detectorKinds`"): for the cameras of the own cluster and of `class: server` — the tables `det/units` and `cam/device_units` of this cluster, once per request, a table larger than `LIST_MAX` 5000 — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`, a limit, as for the other readings of whole tables; for a camera-cluster — `GET /domain/det/tables/units` and `GET /domain/cam/tables/device_units` of its member, two calls per candidate; another value — a kind outside the enum of `properties.detectorKind` of `det` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: noDetector`. Calls per candidate — no more than 200 per request, the rest — `nextCursor`; these are virtual groups, the nodes are built by the form, there is no full counter (ADR-0112 addendum (10) item 4). One member, order by name: `serverId`, `parentId`, `archiveId` — the snapshot of this member; `ref` — the records whose `x-ref` property points to this `uid` (as `archiveId`), one member, by the tables of the kinds that have an `x-ref` property; **cost:** a call per such table of that member. By record: `ids`, up to `idsMax`, more — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: ids`; the cost — below. `folderId`: the door `GET /domain/folders/<id>/units?cursor=&limit=` → `{units: [{cluster, sub, rows, id}], cursor?}`, outside a domain — `GET /folders/<id>`; the server's order, a page may be shorter than `limit` and empty, the hidden is not shown, the end — no `cursor`, a bad `cursor` of the door — 422 `INVALID_CURSOR`. The record `domain/folders` of the listing `calls` covers this door. **`detectorKinds`** (`status.observed.detectorKinds`, read only, only at a camera): the kinds of its detectors without repeats, in the order of the enum of `properties.detectorKind` of `det` — of the server ones, the rows `det/units` with its `cam`, the `kind` of the row, outside the enum — `other`, and of the built-in ones, the rows `cam/device_units` `type: det` of this camera, the kind by [`detector-kinds.tsv`](../../01-cam/data/detector-kinds.tsv) (`driver`, `template`), outside the table `other`; a switched-off detector is counted too: this is a contract field, "there are detectors", not "they work"; no detectors — `[]`. It is read by the same reading as the record assembly (chapter 4), there are no extra calls. `total` — only without a filter, an estimate: `count` of `GET domain/members` (`servers` + `cameras`), no field — no `total`.
- **Record by id** (ADR-0113 addendum (22)). The `uid` of a `device:` record carries the member's part, a child has the same bits (chapter 4 N1.6): `GET /units/{uid}`, `ids`, `/tree/*`, `/objects/delete`, the subject paths `/units/{uid}/…` (`recording-target` and the others), `deviceId` of the scenarios document and of the journal read one row of the view `GET domain/cam/cameras?part=<part>`, the part being four decimal digits 0000…4095. Not found — 404 `NOT_FOUND`, no instance memory is set up. **Cost:** N/4096 members per record, about 61 at 250,000, tables per `class: server` member up to 10; the value is for measurement. **Limit:** a member without `cam` units in the row is not a candidate: the `storage(edge)` record of its `edge` volume is not found this way. `ids` — a reading per distinct part, no more than `idsMax`.
- **Other listings.** The storages — `GET /units` with `kind` of the storage kinds (`archive.local`…) or `implements=vms.archive/1`, the filters `q`, `parentId` (the server, the former `serverId`), `domainId`; the volumes — `kind=volume`, `parentId` (the storage, the former `archiveId`); the folders — `kind=folder`, `parentId`, `domainId`, `q`. `/journal`, `/operations`, `/rules/{id}/fires` — `cursor`: the place is the `(t, id)` of the row. - **`GET /tree/children`.** `layout`: `folders`, `servers`, `archives`, `detectors`; without `parentId` — the top level; no node — 404 `NOT_FOUND`. `TreeNode.id` — the `uid` of the record, for the unallocated ones `unalloc:<domainId>` (`kind: unalloc`). **Decided:** `TreeNode.kind` — the kind code of the record (was `device`), `icon`, `implements` — from the kind description; `unit` — the `UnitBrief` of the record. ### N1.3 Fields and enumerations

- Field ↔ field and enumeration ↔ enumeration — by the rows of the table.
- **Secrets.** The answer carries only the sign "set" (`secrets.password`, `secrets.secret` of the record): `backend` MUST NOT read or give out the values of `*_secret`
  (the console mask, P/06.1). An input secret is `way: write` (the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 9): `properties.password` of a camera —
  `cam/cameras.cred_secret`, `properties.secret` of a volume — `rec/volumes.access_secret` (the bucket secret; `login`, `password`, `secrets.password` of a volume — `none`: `file://` carries no key); the field is absent — the secret is not touched; `null` or `""` — the secret is removed (the field is written
  empty); a string — a new secret. A repeat of the same edit does not reveal the secret: the answer does not carry it. **Operation step:** at
  admission `backend` moves `properties.password` of the step `patch` or `createUnit` (and every secret property of this table) out into the value `secret` of the step: the step body is kept without it (P/06.9 N1.2).
- **`recordMode`** of a camera is not a field but the state of its primary `rec` recording (ADR-0107 item 6; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 2).
  The recordings that `backend` keeps have constant keys (chapter 4 N1.2): the primary — `rec/recordings/<cam>`, an additional one
  on the volume `<volume>` — `rec/recordings/<cam>-<volume>`. The mode is the first one fulfilled from the top:

| Condition | `recordMode` |
|---|---|
| the primary `<cam>` exists, `enabled`, `replica_from` is empty | `always` |
| the primary `<cam>` exists, `enabled`, `replica_from` is not empty | `replica` |
| there is a record row of the camera with the condition `time` | `sched` |
| there is a record row of the camera | `motion` |
| otherwise | `none` |

  **Recording row** (ADR-0111 item 4) — a row `auto/scenarios/<n>` with `hidden: record`, one per camera: `name` — `запись <cam>`, `start: event`, `then` — one action `{sub: rec, action: record, cam: <cam>, minutes: 1, archive: <primary volume>}`, `when` — one trigger `{sub: det, kind: <detector kind>, target}` or empty. The target is the cameras of the detectors `recordDetectorIds`, a built-in one too: the trigger `det` judges its event as well (V/07.2 N1.3), `enabled` — the list is not empty, at `sched` true. Every firing extends the order `rec/recordings/auto-<cam>` (V/07.4 N1.4). The reverse path — by the listing `GET /auto/scenarios`, the field `cam` of the action. Such a row is not given out in `Rule`. Several rows — the one with the smallest id acts: they get set up bypassing `backend`, and it does not remove what is foreign.
  `sched` (ADR-0112 addendum (6) item 1) — the same row: `when` is empty, `conditions: [{kind: time, schedule: <id>}]`, `minutes: 6`: extension at half the term, the margin for a substitution of the first evaluator — 3 min, the recording lasts beyond the window up to 6 min; `recordScheduleId` ↔ `schedule`, the start and the window — V/07.2 N1.6. The schedule was removed: the row stays, there is no order, `status.observed.recordState` — `waiting`, `status.observed.recordReason` — `schedule is gone`; the schedule row, the group of its day, the `auto/rule_marks` row or the maintenance window is not read — `schedule unread`, the recording goes on. Both words are computed by `backend` from the `auto` tables: `rec` does not know about the schedule;
  the directory `recordModes` — `["none", "always", "motion", "sched", "replica", "backup"]` (chapter 2 N1.5). Recordings of the camera with other names (set up bypassing
  `backend`) do not change the mode; they enter the camera's version (N1.3 "Record version"); the order row (`made`, P/03.6 N1.6) does not: the evaluator keeps it, it is not an edit. The order detector `det/units/auto-<cam>-<kind>` is never a `det` record. Transitions of the mode — chapter 4 N1.2.
- **The source of `replica`** (`replicaFrom`, ADR-0112 addendum (5)) — at a recording with `replica_from`; the others have no fields `replicaFrom`, `replicaLagSec`. `device` is read as `null`, the name of a recording — the volume (`home`) of that recording; writing finds the recording of the same camera on the volume `replicaFrom` and writes its name. `null` or no field — `device`; an already non-empty value is not changed by a body without the field. The checks of `backend` come earlier than the recorder's and are for convenience, the truth is at the recorder (V/02.6 N1.9): a volume `archiveId` not `local`, `network` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/archiveId`, at an element of `extraArchives` — `field: /properties/extraArchives`; a non-empty `stream` with `device` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /properties/stream`; `device` at a camera without `storage` (N1.3 "`storage`"), a volume without a recording of this camera, the recording's own volume, a kind without `replicaSource`, two recordings at each other — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/replicaFrom`; another `stream` at the source recording — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /properties/stream`. The steps of one operation are judged by the result of all steps (chapter 4 N1.8). `status.observed.replicaLagSec` — `replica_lag_s` of the heartbeat element down to an integer, `status.observed.recordReason` — its `reason`; they do not enter the version. Lost minutes — `replica_lost_s` of the heartbeat and the event `replica.lost` of the log (V/02.6 N1.9); there is no word in `reason`, this is a limit.
- **Backup on failure** (`when: offline`). `recordMode: backup` ↔ `when: offline`, only at an element of `extraArchives` on a volume of a kind with `backup` in `recordModes`. A primary one with `backup` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /properties/recordMode`; a primary one on a `backup` volume — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /properties/archiveId`, at any `recordMode`: it would not be primary, the recording would stop silently (V/02.6 N1.4); an element on a `backup` volume with another mode and `backup` on another volume — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /properties/recordMode`. - **Recording on `edge`** (ADR-0108 addendum (1)) — the row `cam-<cam>-<volume>` of the holder's order (V/01.6 N1.7): an element of `extraArchives` with `readOnly: true`, `recordMode` — `properties.recordMode` of the `archive.edge` record of the volume, no word — `none` at `enabled: false`, otherwise `always`. The volume `edge`, `incidents` at `archiveId` and at an element of `extraArchives`: at `archiveId` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /properties/archiveId` for `edge`, `VALIDATION_FAILED` for `incidents` by `admits`; at an element — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /properties/extraArchives`.   in the order of the volume name by bytes: `{archiveId: <volume storage id>, recordMode: "always" | "replica" | "none" by enabled and replica_from, stream, labels, state, reason}`: `labels` — the field of the recording, `state`, `reason` — `phase`, `reason` of its element `status[]`, read only, no element — no fields;

  (the belonging of the stream, `fixed`; the analysis of the V/11 review, Codex No. 11, Claude B-6).         not in the kind schema → 422 VALIDATION_FAILED, additionalProperties; readOnly of the schema → 422 VALIDATION_FAILED, readOnly
```
        read, const sent by the form: create — equal to the read value or the skip list? skip : 422 NOT_SUPPORTED, field; PATCH — outside Unit.writable → 422 NOT_SUPPORTED, field
        none — the answer default? skip : 422 NOT_SUPPORTED, field
```
