# V/11.1 — Unit, manifest, door: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.1-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 1 of lesson V/11 «Configurator backend» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the subsystem — ADR-0094 A1–A7).
Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export), [instructions](11-backend.README.md), manifest
[`backend.subsystem.yaml`](../backend.subsystem.yaml). The platform is pinned by [`VMS/PLATFORM_PIN`](../../PLATFORM_PIN) (ADR-VMS-0002) (the VMS pin; an increment of rights on top of the norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark «platform norm B» — where the chapter relies on them).

The subsystem `backend` serves the WebUI of the configurator consolem (the product) its own REST API — the contract
[`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json) at the pinned commit (chapter 2). The translation is on the server: the worker
`backend` receives a human's request by a pass of the console and calls the doors of the console back with the ticket of that request. The worker has no rights of its own,
no login of its own and no data of its own — the console decides the rights and the truth. The chapter sets norms for the unit, placement,
the manifest, the pass and how the console serves the WebUI. The translation of kinds — chapters 3–4; the ticket and the stream — chapter 5.

Norm ids are `V/11.1-MM`.

## N0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Platform» are [P/01.1
N0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

- **Contract** — `api/openapi.json` of consolem at the pinned commit; its copy is
  [`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json).
- **WebUI** — the set of interface bytes of consolem that the console serves (N1.4); it is not part of the course.
- **Pass** — `door.pass` of the console (P/06.5 N1.8); **the second kind** — a pass with a ticket (ADR-0094 B1).
- **Ticket** — 128 random bits that the console issues for one open request of a human (B1; chapter 5).
- **Loopback mark** — the closed header `X-W2C-Loopback: 1` that the console adds to a pass of the second kind when
  its TCP client is a loopback; an incoming header of the same name the console always strips (B1; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 4).

## N1 Form

**Time.** The deadlines of the chapter are seconds of the monotonic clock of the process.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the row
`backend/instances/<name>` (P/06.1), the heartbeat `backend/heartbeats/<worker>`, the snapshot `backend/snapshot/*` (P/04 N1.3a);
the repeat memory of the group key — an own table of the manifest `backend/repeats/<h>` (P/06.1 N1.6; the form — the fields of the table,
chapter 4 N1.4); the undo stack — an own table `backend/undo/<id>` (chapter 4 N1.12). The family `<sub>/idem/` is the service memory of `Idempotency-Key` of the console (P/06.1): `backend` does not touch it. The worker does not itself read rows
of other subsystems (`worker` of the manifest without `reads`, `writes`, `requests`, `creates`): everything the translation
needs, and its own repeat memory, it reads and writes through the doors of the console with the ticket of the request (N1.3).

### N1.1 Fields and rights

`unit: {rows: instances, id: name}` — the row `backend/instances/<name>`, `name` is the name of the instance, given by the operator, on a camera — by the firmware
(`main`). The row has no `about`.

| Field | Form | Meaning | Who reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, required, `fixed` | the name of the instance | — |
| `labels` | `list` | where it may stand: server labels, as with the gateways `live` (`public-address` — the WebUI from outside) | placement (N1.2) |
| `enabled` | `bool`, default `true` | `false` — the worker does not publish `url` in the heartbeat while it holds no enabled row: the console answers 503 «no worker of backend with a door is heard» (P/06.5 N1.8) | holder |

- The row is created by the operator, on a camera — by the boot record `backend/instances/main`, fields `{name: main}` (P/02.4 `boot`; ADR-0110 addendum (4) item 3; V/01.6 N1.5). **Limit:** if the row exists, `boot` does not touch it: `enabled: false` switches off the WebUI of the camera, and there is nothing to bring it back with — the contract has no operation, only the table door of the console; a removed row comes back at the next start. **The budget on a camera** — by a measurement on the first candidate, the owner's word «after generation»; measure the flash, the shell and the seven files of the set of about 3683 KiB with the worker, and the RAM not on an empty domain: `GET /servers`, `GET /access` read all pages of the view, the event log is polled as a stream every 3 s. The threshold and the sample are the owner's word; if it does not fit — to him (ADR-0110 D-9). One row is enough: the console passes the request to a live worker `backend` with a door (the rule `door.pass` of the
  gate and pass contract — the smallest name), a second one is a reserve for a move. If there are no rows or no live
  worker holds any of them, the WebUI does not work (`/backend/api/…` — 503 of the pass).
- The rights on the row are the ordinary rights of units (P/06.3): the cluster's `admin` creates and disables it, on a camera — `admin` disables it and the firmware creates it.
- The phase of the element `status[]` of the instance (P/03.4 N1.1): `running` — holds the row and answers the pass; `pending` —
  `enabled: false`. The series `console.running` is `instances_running` (`equals: running`, `live: true`; V/09.4).

### N1.2 Placement and transport: by labels next to `live`, the callback — TLS with the role `backend`

- **Placement** — by labels, as with the gateways `live` (V/03.1 N1.3; ADR-0094 addendum (6) item 3): `placement.capacity` — 1 (a worker
  carries one instance), `constraint: labels-subset` (the labels of the row ⊆ the labels of the server of its worker), `grows: true` — for a set of
  labels with no worker the controller orders one on a server with these labels (P/04 N1.3), `tie_break: most-free-capacity`.
  Strict placement onto its own server (B8) is not needed for `backend` (addendum (6) item 3). **At most one instance per
  server:** workers of a role with a callback on a server — at most one (the platform norm B1;
  the analysis of the V/11 review, question 8): the role leaf is one per server, and a second instance on the same server would share it.
- **Transport of the callback** — TLS with the certificate of the role `backend` right away, without the mTLS window «console ↔ worker» (addendum (6)
  item 1), on a separate listener of the console, port `CONSOLE_CALL_PORT` (ADR-0094 addendum (16)): there the role leaf is required and there are
  only callback routes; the main listener does not change. `backend` does not configure the address: it takes the URL from the
  header `X-W2C-Pass-Console` of the pass and calls it with its leaf. The ticket is bound to the role leaf `backend` of the server of the instance
  to which the console passed the request (the leaf is `CN backendworker.<server>`: the worker role `backendworker` by the common naming
  rule, ADR-0090 addendum (15); the role certificate contract); a callback from another
  leaf — 401 (addendum (6) item 2; question 8: there is no separate binding to a slot); the gate and pass contract,
  «Second-kind forwarding», and the role certificate contract, «Callback leaf».
- **Role socket** — only the profile without a kit (the stand, the loopback; addendum (6) item 4): without a kit the worker stands on the server of the
  console and calls it through the role socket, the identity is the socket. On a camera — this socket: a cluster of one, nobody issues a leaf without an operator, the word of «Architect» (P/02.4 N1.10).

### N1.2a The holder's door

ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 5. `/timeline` and `/keeps/<key>/verify` are doors of the recorder, not of the console. `backend` takes the address and the token `GET /<s>/where…` with the human's ticket and calls the door itself: a new connection worker → door, outside the callback list, only these two routes. The token is the human's, 120 s, into the request memory; it goes into neither the log nor rows; `where` judges the right, the door judges the token. TLS — the address of the door by `door_address` P/03.4 N1.7: with a role kit the leaf of the door's server is verified by the cluster root; without a kit the door is a loopback, from another server it does not exist: such a door goes to `unavailable`. The door of a recording and of places of another cluster is not here: its root is not given to the worker, the domain is one, therefore `backend` goes through the target's console: `GET /domain/at/<cluster>/rec/timeline/<record>?from&to`, for a place also `?place=<table>/<place>`, with the ticket; the address and the token are not needed (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 10, addendum (16), (17), P/06.7 N1.6). The limit of the route: an answer up to 1 MiB, a deadline of 15 s, 20 s on forwarding; more — splitting of the window, chapter 4 N1.22. `backend` does not call the pieces `/segment`: the address with the token goes to the client. **Limit** (ADR-0112 addendum (17) item 2): `verify` of a keep of another cluster — 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER` (chapter 4 N1.23); the recording cluster verifies it at home.

### N1.3 A worker without its own rights and state

- The worker does not receive and does not store the human's credential: the console passes the request **without** the human's `Authorization`, with
  a ticket (ADR-0094 B1; the platform norm B). The kind
  of pass «with a credential» (the present `door.pass`) is not allowed for `backend` (ADR-0093 addendum (1), ADR-0094 «Decision
  Drivers»).
- A callback — only with the ticket and only to the doors of the **closed list** that the manifest declares (N1.5).
- There is no state of its own outside the platform store. The repeat memory of the group `idempotencyKey` is **always** (not «until B3») and
  shared by the instances: the rows of its own table `backend/repeats/<h>` (the body fingerprint, the state, the results of the steps; the deadline —
  `delete_after` of the table, chapter 4 N1.4), through the table door and the batch door of the console with the ticket (the list of N1.5; chapter 4 N1.4; the analysis
  of the V/11 review, question 3). A repeat that arrives at another instance finds the same row.
- There is no login of its own: a pass without a human session answers what the gate of the console answered — 401 `UNAUTHORIZED` in
  the form `Problem` (N1.5 «Refusals of the console»; the WebUI goes to the console login; ADR-0094 A4).

### N1.4 WebUI: an own shell and the static files of the subsystem (B7)

- `backend` does **not** serve the WebUI of consolem: the console serves it (ADR-0094 A3, addendum (1)).
- **The WebUI build** — a build of the fork under the CSP of the console at the commit `cb728a42cf2708bae186c95607d8edfcda6462fc`, without the section «Training» (`tools/build_webui.py --without-learn`; the answer to
  the request `api/requests/2026-10-29-w2c-webui-csp-request.md` in the product repository: «answered»; ADR-0094 addendum (8)):
  reproducible — one commit gives the same bytes. Its `MANIFEST.json` is
  `[{name, type, purpose, size, sha256}]`, `sha256` is hex, the names are stable, `MANIFEST.json` itself is not in the list;
  `integrity` is not needed — the console serves the bytes, the check is at installation (addendum (8) item 5). A new build commit is a repin of the lesson
  (like the contract, V/11.2 N1.1).
- **The entry page** — the shell [`backend.shell.html`](../backend.shell.html) next to the manifest, like the shells of the other
  subsystems (`<sub>.shell.html`, ADR-0090 addendum (6) item 8): the bytes are `index.html` of the build, SHA-256
  `90e9cd59cb70207a785e23da943fb0785c9f80d382afecfae4d94a0bce9a05ff` (addendum (8) item 3; addendum (2) item 5 revised: own
  markup would lag behind the build). It has no inline code and no attributes `on…=` (handlers are `data-on-<event>` and
  a listener of the application); `<meta name="consolem-api" content="api">` — the API relative to the page, `/backend/api/…`;
  `<meta name="consolem-login" content="../">` — the console login on 401 (addendum (8) item 2). The shell itself without a session — 302 to `/?return=/backend/` (P/06.5 «Shell login»); the files of the set, `HEAD` and the API — 401. The implementation MUST NOT generate
  or edit the shell.
- **Where and how the shell is served:** the console serves at `/backend/` the shell of the mounted subsystem `backend.shell.html`
  because the manifest declares it — by the same rules as the root shell: byte for byte, without `on…=`, with the same session
  (ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 8, the extension B7; the platform norm B); as a separate page, not in
  a nested frame of a foreign page and without `sandbox` (addendum (8) item 4). The `Content-Security-Policy` of the shell is **exactly** the minimum of
  P/06.5 N1.1 `page`: the shell has no inline scripts, there are no hashes — `script-src 'self'; object-src 'none';
  base-uri 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'`; the implementation does not tighten it, so inline styles and `data:` images are allowed (addendum (8) item 1).
  Under this header the product checks the build itself (with its own build check: the stand
  of the product in the role of the console, the self-check of the interface, no policy violations).
- **Static files** — the mechanism B7 (ADR-0094 addendum (1); the platform norm B, P/06.5, the grammar of
  P/02.1 — the list `console.files` of the manifest, the SHA-256 of each — in the manifest row, the console checks it when serving): files next to the
  manifest by the list of the manifest, the console address `/backend/<file>`, byte for byte, the types js, css, json, fonts and images
  `png`, `svg` (ADR-0094 addendum (4) item 2: `svg` — with `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox` and `nosniff`, `png` — with `nosniff`;
  html — not); the same cookie, gate, Origin and `may`. A file name of the set does not start with `api/`: that is the space of the API
  (N1.5; addendum (8) item 2). The shell refers to the files as `<file>?v=<12 SHA-256 characters>` (browser cache busting): the console
  serves the file by name and does not judge the query string (the platform norm B7). The list is `console.files` of the manifest; the shell is declared by `console.shell: true`.
- **The set** — the files of `MANIFEST.json` of the build, except `index.html` (the shell) and `assets/stub-piece.mp4`: `.mp4` is outside `console.files` (P/02.1), the stub piece is needed only by the address `stub://`, which `backend` does not issue. Seven files — `app.css`, `app.js`,
  `client.js`, `fonts/InterVariable.woff2`, `i18n-en.js`, `openapi.js`, `whep-stub.js`, each with a SHA-256. The SHA-256 of the set is the SHA-256
  of the lines `<file SHA-256>␣␣<path>\n`, ordered by path bytewise (the output form of `sha256sum`):
  `083b1638b30c759fc67841b56d1691846cd0424a7435c6a0f7ef84350f40ffb1`; line by line — [the lesson README](../README.md).
  The build has no built-in server of the product, no data sets of it and no self-check.
- **Learning is not enabled by this pin.** The current build remains `--without-learn`. The target supply, catalogue, launch and isolation rules are N1.4a; their contract and supply conditions must be met before learning is included.
- The bytes of the set are not part of the course: they are the code of the fork (the repository `github.com/altumur/consolem`, ADR-0112). The deployment package
  builds them with the product build from the pinned commit and puts them next to the manifest `backend` (V/09.3, like `obsd` —
  built from a pinned commit); the console checks the SHA-256 of each by the manifest row (P/06.5 «Static files»). The shell is the canon
  of the lesson: its bytes are in the course.

### N1.4a Learning supply, launch and isolation

These rules describe the target learning support of ADR-VMS-0004, including addenda (1), (2). They do not enable learning in the current build of N1.4.

- **Supply and contract gate.** The logical typed reference `answer.learn` is the target extension for a learning answer and, when a suitable chapter exists, an additional reference in an `explain` answer (chapter 7). It is absent from the closed answer schema of the pinned contract `cb728a4`. The Console must submit the fork and contract change for agreement; only an agreed contract followed by an authorised repin may add this support to the supply. The current OpenAPI, shell, manifest and seven-file set remain unchanged, with `--without-learn`. No new request field, SSE event or public option is defined here. Learning files enter the supply only with their manifest and SHA-256, after the entry, CSP, complete-file-loading and isolation conditions below have been verified on the real console in every supported browser; the outstanding conditions of answers 093202Z and 103525Z remain, with their separate-tab requirement superseded by the same-tab rule below.
- **One catalogue.** The tours and their catalogue have one source: stable topic and chapter IDs, a catalogue version or digest, language, title, goal, training dataset and availability in the build. Texts are not copied into a second editable catalogue. The catalogue and learning files are part of the VMS supply with SHA-256 under the existing static-file mechanism of N1.4; this chapter assigns no catalogue ID, version or new file format. A model selects an ID, never a URL, HTML, JavaScript or a selector. The interface builds the chapter address only from its own supply. Before offering a chapter, `backend` checks the ID and the current catalogue version; before launching it, the interface checks them against its supplied catalogue as well. An unknown ID or a foreign version launches nothing and offers the manual catalogue when available. An unavailable model leaves the supplied catalogue and ordinary search available; a build without training must not promise a lesson. The current tours are Russian: an English interface must name that language truthfully on the chapter offer.
- **Launch and return.** Only a person's button starts the show, in the same tab; an SSE event never starts it automatically. Before the show, the interface warns that working alarms do not arrive in this tab during the show and that the data are training data. A persistent «Training data» band stays visible throughout the show. Exit restores working mode through ordinary authenticated entry without losing the person's entry; there is no anonymous bypass, separate public entry or new authentication mechanism. The shell and its files retain the entry rules of N1.4: without a session the shell gives 302 and the files give 401. Parameters of the working address, including `api`, `assist`, `fixture` and the other parameters, are not inherited by the training launch. No new role restriction is introduced.
- **Isolation and refusal.** The training server and dataset run in the browser on training data. Training creation, deletion, «Apply», `ask` and `escalate`, including a conversation after the person chooses to try it themselves, never call the working API of `backend` or the console. An external assistant is forbidden in training both through parameters and through page metadata, including a default public marker. Working drafts, history and secrets do not enter training context or training storage; reloads, several tabs, a change of person or the end of a session must not transfer them into training. If the training server or any required file cannot load, including a CSP refusal, or browser-storage isolation cannot be established, the interface refuses the show clearly. It makes no working-API request and does not fall back into working mode. A requested exit uses the authenticated return rule above.
- **Assistant examples.** Chapters about the assistant use prepared answers or stubs and explicitly identify them as examples. Training «Apply» is never presented as a change to the working system. Completing a lesson measures neither the live model's quality nor the plan check of chapter 11. The Console owns these chapters and the fork change; this norm adds no tour, dataset, acceptance input or measured-quality threshold.

### N1.5 The pass and the callback list

- **The pass route** — the segment `api`: `door.pass.routes` of the manifest is `[api]` (ADR-0094 addendum (8) item 2); the keys `stream`
  and `wait` under it — by a path prefix of whole segments (`api/stream`, `api/objects/delete`), the longest one wins
  (the platform norm B1; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 7). The browser calls
  the API at the console address `/backend/api/<contract path>` (a mounted subsystem, P/06.5 N1.1), the stream —
  `/backend/api/stream`; the worker receives `/api/<contract path>` without the mount prefix (the gate and
  pass contract, «Forwarding `door.pass`») and answers any path under it: a path not in the contract — 404 `NO_ROUTE` (chapter 2).
  The static files (N1.4) are outside `api/`; the spaces do not intersect.
- **The answer of the pass** — the media types `application/json`, `application/problem+json`, `text/event-stream` (with parameters,
  `charset` and the like) and answers without a body (204, 304) without `Content-Type`; anything else — 502 of the console. The console adds
  `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox` (ADR-0094 A3, addendum (3) item 2; the platform norm B). A deletion answers 204, a version refusal — 412 `application/problem+json`.
- **The headers of the second kind** — a closed list (ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 3): to the worker — `Content-Type`, `Idempotency-Key`,
  `If-Match`, `Last-Event-ID`, the ticket `X-W2C-Pass-Ticket`, the address `X-W2C-Pass-Console` (only with a kit) and the loopback mark
  `X-W2C-Loopback` (N0; question 4); to the client — `Content-Type`, `ETag`, `Cache-Control: no-store` on a body with a password and a live token (chapter 4 N1.15, N1.18).
  The console cuts off credentials (`Authorization`, cookies) and other headers; the worker sees and sends nothing else.
- **Refusals of the console** on the routes `api` — the form of the contract (the platform norm B1; the analysis of the V/11 review, question 5): the manifest
  declares `door.pass.refusals` `{form: problem, codes}` — a closed map «console status → `code`»; the console answers
  `application/problem+json` `{code, message}`; the platform does not know the product's words. The map: 401 — `UNAUTHORIZED`
  (no session), 403 — `PERMISSION_DENIED`, 404 — `NO_ROUTE` (a route outside the list), 413 — `VALIDATION_FAILED`, 502, 503, 504 — `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`
  (chapter 2 N1.3). The gate on `api` requires a proven identity and any grant and does not read `rights.routes`: what a human
  may do is decided by the callback with the ticket (a fresh `may` of every door).
- **The second kind of pass** (ADR-0094 B1; the platform norm B). The ticket record at the console is `{user, session
  kind, jti, kid, exp, open external request, target instance}`; of it only the 128 bits of the ticket go to the worker.
  Every callback passes the same check as a door token (revocation of `jti`, the deadline, the key) and a fresh `may`; an open
  stream by a ticket is closed on revocation of `jti`, on disabling of the human and at `exp` (ADR-0094 addendum (3) item 1; chapter 5).
  Callbacks go through their own bounded lane of the console, passes as a stream — under a separate limit (addendum (3) item 7).
  A callback is over TLS with the role leaf `backend` of the addressee instance (N1.2; addendum (6) items 1, 2). The manifest declares in
  `door.pass`:
  - **`stream`:** the routes `api/stream` and `api/assist/turns` (the assistant turn, chapter 7) — without a buffer and without the limit of 4 MiB;
    a stream ticket is good only for `GET`, `HEAD` and the door route of a subsystem;
  - **`wait`:** the records of the kinds — reads with the domain view, group and compound writes (`api/units`), `api/objects/delete`, operations, rights and automation with its cascades (`api/operations`, `api/access`, `api/rules`, `api/schedules`, `api/day-groups`, `api/modes`, `api/maintenance-windows`) — 30 s instead of `PASS_WAIT`, the kind descriptions with the manifests (`api/kinds`) and stream accounts (`api/stream-accounts`: the gateway door itself waits `PASS_WAIT`) — 15 s, the stand reset `api/test` — 120 s; the assistant
    turn — `ASSIST_TURN_MAX` = 7500 s (chapter 7 N1.5), the ceiling is `PASS_WAIT_MAX`;
  - **`operations`** (ADR-0095, P/06.9 N1.1): the route of the step call `opstep` — not under `routes`, unavailable to the browser, only
    the console calls it; the operations table is a service one, `service: true` (chapter 4 N1.8);
  - **`calls`**, within the closed list of the platform (P/06.3 N1.9): the rows and tables of `cam` (`cameras`, `devices`, `children`, `preset_labels`, `child_settings`, `device_units`, `unit_next`, `searches`, `found`, `probes`, `probe_replies`), the read of `cam/rule_state`, `rec`
    (`recordings`, `volumes`), `det` (`units`), `detjob` (`jobs`), `detedge` (`watches`), `auto` (`scenarios`, `rule_marks`, `schedules`, `day_groups`, `modes`, `mode_current`, `windows`, `versions`, `answers`) and their tombstones (`?deleted=1&about=`, B4); the counters `cam/next_id` (chapter 4
    N1.2), `auto/next_id` (chapter 4 N1.10); the route of the evaluator door `auto/test` (chapter 4 N1.10); the batch door `console/batch` (B9, ADR-0094 addendum (9) item 2) — the device write and cascades (chapter 4 N1.2);
    the common settings of these subsystems `<sub>/policy` (ADR-0094 addendum (2) item 6); filing of requests `auto/requests`, `cam/requests` — write only, the human of the ticket is judged (P/06.3 N1.9; chapter 4 N1.2, N1.10, N1.16, N1.17); the pan-tilt table `cam/ptz_moves` (chapter 4 N1.16); viewing: `live/streams`, `GET rec/where`, `rec/keeps`, `GET rec/heartbeats`, `rec/requests`, `marks`, `GET domain/rec/books/primaries/*`, `GET domain/at/rec/where`, `GET domain/at/rec/keeps`, `GET domain/at/rec/heartbeats`, `GET domain/at/rec/timeline` (chapter 4 N1.15, N1.22–N1.25); the address and the token of the gateway `GET live/where`, the account door of the gateway `live/accounts` (a pass of the first kind, the human of the ticket is judged), the server labels `servers/labels` (chapter 4 N1.15, N1.18, N1.19; ADR-0112 addendum (1)); the licence book `GET domain/cam/books/licence` read only (chapter 3 N1.3); the domain view `GET domain`, `GET domain/rec/recordings`, the tables of the view `GET domain/<s>/tables/<table>` (`cam`: `cameras`, `devices`, `children`, `child_settings`, `device_units`, `rule_state`; `rec`: `recordings`, `volumes`; `det`: `units`; `auto`: `scenarios`, `rule_marks`, `schedules`, `day_groups`, `windows`) and the read at a member `GET domain/at/auto/versions` — the right of the human of the ticket, chapter 3 N1.2a; the feed `changes` (B2); the reads `GET manifest`, `servers`, `events`, `session` (only «who am I» and

## N2 Doors

The subsystem has no door of its own besides the pass of the console: `door.routes` is empty. The API door is the contract (chapter 2) behind the pass of the
console.

## N3 Pass

```
holder of the row backend/instances/<name> on a server by labels (P/03, P/04)
  enabled false → do not publish url in the heartbeat while there is no enabled row (the console — 503)
pass of the console /backend/api/<path> (second kind: ticket)
  contract path → translation (chapters 3–4) by callbacks with the ticket from the list of N1.5
  path not in the contract or «not supported here» → 404 NO_ROUTE (chapter 2)
  answer — JSON or SSE; anything else — 502 of the console
```

## N4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| there are no rows `backend/instances/*`, no live worker holds any, or `enabled: false` | 503 of the console pass | WebUI | create, enable a row; the server labels; on a camera — the table door of the console (N1.1 **Limit**) |
| `GET /backend/`, `/backend/index.html` without a session | 302 to `/?return=/backend/` of the console | browser | log in on the page `/` |
| no human session, other doors | 401 `UNAUTHORIZED` of the console gate (`Problem`, B1) | WebUI | the console login |
| a route under `api` outside the manifest list, the rights of the gate | 404 `NO_ROUTE`, 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED` of the console (`Problem`, B1) | WebUI | — |
| the answer of the pass is not JSON, not `problem+json`, not SSE and not bodiless | 502 of the console | WebUI | — |
| a callback outside the list, after the request is closed, without the role certificate `backend`, from the leaf of another server | 401 of the console (B1, addendum (6)) | the worker, the console log | — |
