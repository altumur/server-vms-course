# V/11.7 — The configuration assistant in `backend`: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.7-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 7 of lesson V/11 «Configurator backend» (ADR-0093 addendum (6); ADR-0094 addendum (10)). Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](11-backend.README.md), the contract [`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json); the assistant is lesson
[V/10](../../10-assist/README.md). The platform is pinned by [`VMS/PLATFORM_PIN`](../../PLATFORM_PIN) (ADR-VMS-0002) (the VMS pin; the increment of rights on top of norms B, ADR-0090 addendum (17); the mark «platform norm B» — where the chapter relies on them).

The configuration assistant (ADR-0093): the administrator writes a request in words, a model inside the cluster proposes a plan,
code checks the plan, only a person writes. On our page the turn runs in the browser (V/11.9). For the WebUI consolem the turn
runs in `backend` (the owner's decision, ADR-0093 addendum (6)): `backend` has no rights of its own, its calls to the console are made with the rights
of the person by ticket while that person's HTTP request is open (ADR-0094 B1). One norm, two implementations: tools, plan check and
vectors are chapters 9–13: the loop is only in `backend` (ADR-0093 addendum (8) item 2), the page panel is removed. The chapter sets norms for the turn operation,
the turn loop in `backend`, the translation of the plan into the words of consolem and the turn limit.

Norm ids are `V/11.7-MM`.

## N0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Platform» are [P/01.1
N0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

- **Turn** — V/11.9 N0: from the request to the outcome; a question does not end the turn.
- **HTTP request** — one `POST /assist/turns` from opening to the end event: `question`, `plan`, `refused`, `error`.
- **Transcript** — the texts of the turn and the messages of the model steps, which the client holds and returns with every HTTP request:
  `backend` has no state of its own (ADR-0094 A3).
- **The consolem plan** — the checked plan (V/11.11 N1.1) translated into `items` `AssistPlanItem` of the contract: `PATCH /units/{uid}` or the steps `patch` of `POST /operations`.

## N1 Form

**Time.** The timeouts of the chapter are seconds of the process's monotonic clock.

### N1.1 The turn operation

- The turn operation is the streaming operation of the contract `POST /assist/turns` (`assistTurn`, chapter 2 No. 51), the capability `assist`, the body `AssistTurnRequest` `{text, context?,
  secrets?}` (`text` — what the person wrote; `context` — an opaque string from the previous answer of the conversation, the client
  returns it as is; `secrets` — an array of strings, secret No. N is position N counting from 1 (V/11.12 N1.3):
  the model gets only «secret No. N entered», the value is substituted by the code of `backend` into `/properties/password` of the plan, N1.3; secrets
  are not put into `context`; Kimi's addition to the analysis of the V/11 review, a remark of «Console»), the answer is an SSE stream `text/event-stream`: per message one line `data:` (`AssistEvent`), without `event:` and `id:` (ADR-0094 addendum (10) item 2).
- `text` is empty — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: text`, answer `application/problem+json`, no stream. There is no turn type in the HTTP request: `backend` chooses it, N1.6; the field `kind` is unknown to the contract (b09cdf8, 0.2.0) — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: kind`, like any extra field. The field `world` belongs only to the demo page of the contract: V/11 does not accept it, 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: world`.
- The pass route is `api/assist/turns`, **streaming** (V/11.1 N1.5; platform norm B1): without the 5 s limit and the 4 MiB
  buffer; a stream ticket is good only for reads and for the route of a subsystem door — this is enough for the tools and the plan check
  (V/11.10, V/11.11 only read), and the model is called by a route. The wait limit of the route is `ASSIST_TURN_MAX` (N1.5).
- A break of the stream before the end event is a cancellation of the turn: the ticket died together with the HTTP request, therefore `backend` cannot send the cancellation `DELETE` of the step and
  sends nothing more; the model door removes an unfinished step by `ABANDON` (V/10.2 N1.3).
  A broken stream is not continued: the client repeats the HTTP request with the same `context`.

### N1.2 The turn loop in `backend`

The loop is V/11.9 N1.2–N1.5 (step messages, window, step, parsing of the model answer, outcomes). Here — how it is bound to the HTTP request of the contract:

- **The model** is the model door V/10.2 through the console: `backend` calls the route `/assist/chat/<name>/…` by ticket (a
  callback from the listing, V/11.1 N1.5; P/06.3 N1.9 «Door route»), the console passes it to the holder with a door token (the right
  `view` on the model row is that of the person of the ticket; ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 7, ADR-0093 addendum (7)). `backend` does not see the address and the token of the door:
  it does not read `/assist/where`. A step is submitted and polled by V/10.2 N1.2, N1.3; the route has the limit `PASS_WAIT`,
  therefore `wait` of a poll is 3 s (V/11.9 N1.3). The model of the turn is the model row named by `context`; without it
  — the first `assist/models` row visible to the person, by name, with the phase `running`; the list
  is `GET /assist/models` by ticket (a callback from the listing, V/11.1 N1.5; Kimi's addition to the analysis of the V/11 review, No. 1).
  The client sees neither the keys nor the address of the model.
- **The transcript** is in `context`: base64url without `=` of UTF-8 JSON `{v: 1, model, turn, texts, messages}` — the model of the turn, `turn`
  of the turn (V/11.9 N1.2), the texts of the turn and the messages of the steps that go into the history (V/11.9 N1.2); `type` — the turn type while a question is open (N1.6), otherwise the field is absent. Every HTTP request with `context`: the new `text`
  is appended to `texts`, as the answer to a question and as a clarification (V/11.9 N1.5); `context` cannot be read or `v` is not `1` — the turn starts anew without history (a new `turn`). There are no secrets in
  `context`: secret labels live only inside one turn (V/11.12 N1.3), and a new turn does not inherit them.
- **`context` is untrusted client input** (Kimi's addition to the analysis of the V/11 review, question 3): it has no signature (MAC) —
  `backend` has neither state nor keys of its own. Its messages are proof of neither rights nor data: the model sees them as
  history, and the harm is bounded by the rights of the person by ticket (every read is made with that person's right), by the plan check by code over a fresh
  read (V/11.11) and by the fact that the route of the model door passes only with the right `view` of the person.
- **The question** `ask_admin` ends the HTTP request (V/11.9 N1.4 item 6): the stream ends with the event `question`, the person's
  answer is a new HTTP request with the `context` of the question (ADR-0093 addendum (6) item 2).
- **The type `rules`** (V/11.13): the harness, tools, plan and layers are those of that chapter, the turn and the window are these. `backend` chooses the type, N1.6. The plan is the event `plan` with `rules`, `AssistRuleEdit`: the body of the neutral plan translator, `id` is a UUID, `@<ref>` as is, `logOnly: true` for a new rule (V/11.13 N1.4). Rules are written on «Apply» by the contract doors `POST /rules`, `PUT /rules/{ruleId}` with the person's session (V/11.13 N1.7), not by the turn operation.
- **The type `export`** (ADR-0093 addendum (47); V/11.20) — N1.9: a request to export; the model names the order, code builds the body `AssistExport` of the contract (`ExportCreate` without `password`, `supervisor`, `idempotencyKey`) and checks it by the world. The turn writes nothing: the plan is the event `plan` with `export`; the window checks the order through `POST /exports/check`, the person enters the password and the supervisor, and «Export» creates the order with `POST /exports` and the person's session (V/11.20 N1.2). The type is off by default, as `config`: the key `ASSIST_EXPORT` of the subsystem file (`off` \| `on`, default `off`; V/09.2).
- **The tools** of the type `config` are the closed set of V/11.10: the same names, arguments, results, errors and words; reads are console doors from
  the listing of V/11.10 N1.2 by the stream ticket. **The scope** is a lesson constant: `cam`, `rec`, `det` (ADR-0093
  addendum (5), (6)); `list_subsystems` and `get_manifest` — by `GET /manifest` filtered by the scope; `/mounts` is not
  read (Kimi's addition to the analysis of the V/11 review, question 4). **`can_edit`** is the same rule `may("admin",
  "unit:<sub>/<id>", "<sub>")` of the module (P/07 N1.5: the grants of the session and the area of P/06.3 N1.3) over `GET /session` of the person of the ticket. Every
  door the tools read is in the callback listing (V/11.1 N1.5). A 403 refusal of a door is the tool error of
  V/11.10 N1.2 «no access» (`GET <path>: 403 <detail>`), the turn goes on; 401 — the ticket is dead: the stream is closed (N4).
- **The plan check** is V/11.11 whole (form and rights, reconciliation of words, selection, question and refusal), over a fresh read of the console
  by ticket; then — the translation into the consolem plan (N1.3). The model does not check the plan: code checks it.
- **The call count** — `TURN_CALLS` = 25 per turn (V/11.9 N1.2), `STEP_TRIES`, `RESTARTS`, `ENQUEUE_WAIT` — by chapter 9
  (V/11.9 N1.3); the outcome beyond the limit is the same: «turn not completed».

### N1.3 Translation of the plan into the words of consolem

- **The scope** (ADR-0093 addendum (6) item 4). The plan check in `backend` lets through only edits that have a place in
  `UnitPatch` of the record (V/11.3 N1.1, the table `mapping.tsv`; the table below): an edit of another field or another unit is a check refusal with
  the words «do it manually» ("do it manually") and `field` — the pointer of the property of the record by `mapping.tsv` (`/properties/<p>`) when the row field has one, otherwise without `field` (**Decided:** the contract: `field` of `refused` is a JSON Pointer of a field of the object, «absent — the refusal is not about a field»); an edit of a unit `auto` is the refusal `not in scope` (N1.7). **Decided:** an edit with a place whose `name` or property is outside `Unit.writable` of the record in the same read (V/11.3 N1.1: a camera-cluster, a foreign record without a link) is the same check refusal «do it manually» with `field` — the pointer (`/name`, `/properties/<p>`), before «Apply»: the write would get 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`. There is no partial plan: one such edit is a refusal of the whole plan.
- **Edits with a place** — by the units of the record (V/11.3 N1.2):

| Plan edit (`unit`, `field`) | Pointer in `UnitPatch` of the record |
|---|---|
| `cam/<id>` (a camera, empty `main`): `name`, `enabled`, `cred_username` | camera: `/name`, `/properties/enabled`, `/properties/login` |
| `cam/<id>`: `source`, only the host and the port change | camera: `/properties/ip`, `/properties/port` |
| `rec/<cam>`: `enabled` | camera: `/properties/recordMode` — on `true` `always`, with a non-empty `replica_from` — `replica`; on `false` — `none` (V/11.3 N1.3) |
| `rec/<cam>`: `home`, `stream` | camera: `/properties/archiveId`, `/properties/stream` |
| `rec/<cam>-<volume>`: `enabled` | camera: `/properties/extraArchives`, the volume element — `recordMode` `always` \| `replica` \| `none`; the list is whole, from the same read (**Decided:** `UnitPatch` is a JSON Merge Patch, an array is replaced whole) |
| `det/<cam>-<kind>`, `det/<cam>-<kind>-<n>`: `enabled` | detector (the kind `det`): `/properties/enabled` |
| `det/<cam>-<kind>`, `det/<cam>-<kind>-<n>`: `params`, only the key `sensitivity` changes | detector: `/properties/sensitivity` (the codec of V/11.3 N1.3) |
| `cam/<id>`: `cred_secret`, `to` is the label `{"secret": N}` (V/11.12 N1.3) | camera: `/properties/password` = `secrets[N − 1]` of the turn HTTP request (`writeOnly` of the kind schema, `way: write` of `mapping.tsv`: the answers carry only `secrets.password`); labels without such a position in `secrets` are a check refusal |

  Anything else is a check refusal (above): the volume secret (`access_secret`), volumes, recordings with other names, streams,
  fields without a row in the table, `source` with another path, `params` with other keys.
- **The consolem plan** is `items` `AssistPlanItem` (`uid`, `version`, `patch`) and, for each record, an
  explanation: `name` and `changes` — the edits `AssistChange` `{field, before?, after, why}`, `field` is the pointer of the table above, as `Problem.field` of `PATCH /units/{uid}` (the contract; V/11.14 N1.2); a secret has no `before`, `after` is only the number: «new (secret No. N)». The edits of a camera row and of its recordings merge into
  the camera element, the edits of a detector into its own element (a detector is its own record of the kind `det`); `patch` is `UnitPatch` with only the edited `name` and `properties`; `version` is
  `Unit.version` of the record (V/11.4 N1.1) by the same read by which the plan check passed; `uid` — V/11.4 N1.6, V/11.14 N1.1.
  The order of the elements is by `uid` in bytes. The result: `{summary, context, items}`.
- **«Apply»** ("Apply") is not the turn operation: the client writes `items` as is (the contract `AssistPlanItem`) — either `PATCH /units/{uid}` with `If-Match: version` and the body `patch` per element
  (chapter 2 No. 95; V/11.4 N1.2, N1.3): a record changed after the plan — 412 `VERSION_MISMATCH` of its `PATCH`, nothing is written in it (a package,
  ADR-0094 addendum (9)), the other elements are written; or one `POST /operations` with the steps `{uid, patch}`, `version` in `expectedVersions` and a new `idempotencyKey` (chapter 2 No. 5; V/11.4 N1.8): a record changed after the plan — 412 of the whole operation, nothing is executed. The contract names both ways; the choice is the client's.

### N1.4 Stream events

| `type` of the message `AssistEvent` | When | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `progress` | a loop step has started: locating the model, a model step, a tool, the plan check — any number of times | `{text}` — a line of work progress (V/11.9 N1.1 «status line»: «model loading», «model busy», «reading `<sub>`», «checking the plan») |
| `question` | `ask_admin` is accepted | `{text, context}` |
| `plan` | the plan passed the check and the translation | `{summary, context, items}` (N1.3); the type `export` — `{summary, context, export}` (`AssistExport`) |
| `refused` | `refuse` is accepted; an edit out of scope; «turn not completed» (V/11.9 N1.5) | `{text, context, field?}` — the words of the reason |
| `answer` | `answer` of the type `explain` is accepted (N1.7) | `{text, context}` — writes nothing |
| `found` | `found` is accepted, the selection is counted (N1.8) | `{text, context, conditions, ids \| filter, total?, truncated?}` |
| `error` | a console door refusal that the turn cannot fix | `{code, message}` — `code` from `ProblemCode` (chapter 2 N1.3) |

- The stream ends with exactly one of `question`, `plan`, `refused`, `answer`, `found`, `error`; then `backend` closes the stream.
- `action` of `found` (the contract 0.2.0: a command over the selection) is not given out: the harness `found` has no argument for it (N1.8).
- Every message carries `at` — the moment `backend` produced it, RFC 3339 UTC (chapter 5 N1.2). A stream without a final message is taken by the form as a break: `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`.
- Turn events have no `id:`: a turn is not resumed by `Last-Event-ID` (a broken one is a repeat of the HTTP request, N1.1).

### N1.5 The turn limit

- `ASSIST_TURN_MAX` = 7500 s is the ceiling of the HTTP request: `TURN_CALLS` (25) × the limit of a model call (`QUEUE_MAX` 120 s +
  `STEP_MAX` 180 s, V/10.2 N1.3) (ADR-0094 addendum (10) item 2). It is the wait limit of the route `api/assist/turns` at the console
  (the number is here, the ceiling `PASS_WAIT_MAX` is platform norm B1) and the turn limit at `backend`: a turn not finished within it from the start of the
  HTTP request ends with `refused` «turn not completed: longer than the turn limit»; the retries `STEP_TRIES`, `RESTARTS` and the wait
  `ENQUEUE_WAIT` run inside it.
- Limitation: the stream ticket ends with `exp` of the token of the HTTP request (the gate and pass contract, `close`), and `TOKEN_LIFETIME` = 900 s
  ([P/08.4 N1.3](../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.4-domain.norms.md)) is less than `ASSIST_TURN_MAX`. An HTTP request not finished by `exp` is a break of the stream (N1.1): there is no outcome event,
  the repeat goes from the previous `context`. An HTTP request longer than the remainder of the token does not complete; the ceiling is not reached with such a token.

### N1.6 The utterance type

ADR-0093 addendum (13), (15) item 1; the fork contract b09cdf8, and 0.2.0 as well: `AssistTurnRequest` has no field `kind`, nor does `AssistEvent`.

- `backend` chooses the type for each utterance; the caller does not name it: one conversation holds an edit, a rule, a question about the system and a list. The chosen type is not given out, the form does not show it (ADR-0093 addendum (12), (13)).
- **Types:** `config` — chapters 9–12; `rules` — V/11.13; `explain` — N1.7; `found` — selection, N1.8; `export` — V/11.20 (ADR-0093 addendum (47)).
- **An answer to a question** is not classified anew: the `context` of the question carries the type in the field `type` of the transcript (N1.2), the answer goes by the same harness. The type is derived for an utterance without an open question: the first one, and one after a plan, a refusal, an answer and a list.
- **The step `route`** is one step of the model door before the loop: `system` — the bytes of [`harness/route/system.md`](../harness/route/system.md), `tools` — [`harness/route/tools.json`](../harness/route/tools.json), `tool_choice: required`, the `user` message — the texts of the turn separated by an empty line. The call `route {kind}`: `config`, `rules`, `explain`, `found`, `export` (N1.9) — the type; `ask` — the event `question` with `question` of the call, `context` without a type: the answer is routed anew. No call or another value — the step is repeated once, then `question` in the language of the utterance (decided: Cyrillic in the texts of the turn — Russian, otherwise English): «Please say what you need: edit a setting, a rule, a question about the system, or a list.», «Please say what you need: a settings change, a rule, a question about the system or a list.». The step `route` is not counted in `TURN_CALLS`; its failure has the outcomes of V/11.9 N1.3, as for a loop step.
- **Type refusal:** `refuse` of any of the four harnesses carries the field `other_kind`; `true` means a request of another type (an edit for `explain`, a question for `config`, a rule for `found`); the rule for setting it is in every `system.md`. Only on it does `backend` repeat `route` once, with the `reason` of the refusal in the `user` message, and go on with the chosen type; such a `refuse` has no refusal check of V/11.11 N1.5: it is not shown; a second such refusal, or the same type chosen, is `refused` with `reason`. `refuse` without `other_kind` is the ordinary outcome «refusal».
- The harness `route` comes from the experiment `plan-neutral` (`assist-pilot` `REPORT-neutral.md`, 9dc2afc), the text of the pilot's tag `bridge-public-24`. **Cost:** a step of about 2 s per utterance. **Limitation:** on disputable requests the choice errs in about a third (3.5 % of the set, the error among them 33 %), the person does not see this: the plan is visible before «Apply», the `explain` answer is labelled, the selection shows the condition in words; a wrong choice ends with a plan, an answer or a list of the wrong type, there is no write without «Apply». The measurement on the norms of V/11 was not made.

### N1.7 An answer to a question about the system

ADR-0093 addendum (10) item 1, (13), (16): the type `explain` only reads.

- The harness is [`harness/explain/system.md`](../harness/explain/system.md), [`harness/explain/tools.json`](../harness/explain/tools.json): the read tools of V/11.10 (`list_subsystems`, `get_manifest`, `find_units`, `field_values`, `list_table`, their reads, errors and words), `rule_state {rule}`, `rule_fires {rule}`, `answer {text}`, `ask_admin`, `refuse`. There is no write tool and no plan check. **The scope** of `explain` is `cam`, `rec`, `det`, `auto` (ADR-0093 addendum (16)): for `auto` — the rows `scenarios` (`find_units`) and the tables `schedules`, `day_groups`, `modes`, `mode_current`, `windows`, `rule_marks` (`list_table`) — what an answer to «why the rule will not fire» needs; they are read by console doors by ticket from the listing of V/11.1 N1.5, the right is `view`; `list_subsystems`, `get_manifest` give `auto` only to this type. The scope of `config` does not change: a plan edit with an `auto` row is the check refusal `<unit>: not in scope: a rule is changed by a rule request or in the console form` (N1.3, ADR-0093 addendum (18)).
- **Rule state** (ADR-0093 addendum (17)): `rule_state` is the element of `status[]` of the row `GET /auto/scenarios/<id>`: phase, `reason`, `unfit`, `unchecked`, `state.runs` (V/07.3, V/07.4 N1.11; the heartbeat `GET auto/heartbeats` — the words of `servers.status`); `rule_fires` is `GET /events?subsystem=auto&unit=auto/<id>&from=<now − 86400>&limit=50`, newest first, like `GET /rules/{id}/fires` of V/11.4 N1.10. **Decided:** the window is a day, `limit` 50, `late` is the default 3600: a firing is written into the bucket of its own `t`, and `t` is close to `occurred`; `truncated` of the log — in words in the result. Another member's rule — the same doors through `/domain/at/<member>/…`. A rule by name: two with one name — the error `several rules are named <name>`, none — `no rule <name>`. The row `hidden: record` is a camera recording and is not called a rule.
- **Rights:** the ticket, like any other read. No right `view` on `auto` (`GET /session` of the person: not a single grant `view` whose area covers `auto`) — the tool error `no rights to read rules`, not an empty list: one cannot say «the rule does not exist». **Limitation:** with part of the grants, rules outside them are not visible; the answer is about the visible ones.
- The loop is V/11.9 N1.2–N1.5: an accepted `answer` ends the turn, the other calls of the answer are `not executed`. The event is `answer {text, context}`: final, writes nothing; the conversation continues with a new HTTP request with this `context`.
- **The field overview** — with labels and options from the manifest: «recording off» is the recording mode, not `enabled` of the camera (`assist-pilot` `REPORT-rules.md`, X13; tag `bridge-public-19`).
- **Limitation:** the measurement reference checked the named facts, not the whole answer: 0 false successes out of 30 means «the required facts are named»; `rule_fires` does not see firings older than a day.

### N1.8 Selection

ADR-0093 addendum (14), (15); the contract 39ba6a3: `AssistEvent` `found`, `AssistFoundFilter`, the filter `noDetector`; on 0.2.0 (ADR-0116 addendum (9)): `ids` — `Unit.uid`, `AssistFoundFilter` `kinds`, `implements`, `fields`, `status` as a list, `noDetector` — `true` or a detector kind, the detector kinds of a camera — `status.observed.detectorKinds`.

- The harness is [`harness/found/system.md`](../harness/found/system.md), [`harness/found/tools.json`](../harness/found/tools.json): `device_values {field, type?}` — the values of a field of the records of the kind `type` in the scope with counts; a scope larger than `LIST_MAX` is the error `<sub>: more than <LIST_MAX> units — too many to check here`, as for `field_values` of V/11.10 N1.2; `found {text, type?, where}`, `ask_admin`, `refuse`. **`type`** is a kind code of `GET /kinds` (`UnitKind.kind`, chapter 14 N1.1); absent — `camera`. The model composes the condition `where` in the words of the contract: the fields of the record `Unit`, operators and the empty list — as the conditions of `find_units` of V/11.10 N1.5, but over the fields of the record (V/11.3), not rows. **Decided:** a field of the condition is a path of `Unit` by `mapping.tsv`, a property named without `properties.`, as `fields` of `GET /units` names it: `name`, `folders`, the properties of the kind schema (`recordMode`, `archiveId`, …), `status.link`, `status.observed.<name>`. A field the kind does not have is the tool error `<field>: <type> has no such field`, the turn goes on. **The field overview** — the fields of the kind with the labels and options of the kind schema (`x-label`, `x-enum-labels`, `kinds.json`), «recording off» is `recordMode` `none`, not `enabled` `false` (X13, `REPORT-rules.md`; there is no question on these words, ADR-0093 addendum (15) item 5).
- **`backend` executes it, not the model:** `ids` — `Unit.uid` of the matching records, counted by code over the record fields derived by V/11.3; otherwise they are invented. Found 0 is also `found`.
- **Two forms, exactly one.** The scope is the rows that must be read to derive the fields of the condition: by the view rows, for the cameras of a domain — the view pages (V/11.3 N1.2a, ADR-0113 addendum (8)).
  - The scope is not larger than `LIST_MAX` = 5000 rows per subsystem (V/11.10 N1.2): `backend` passes it whole and gives `ids` by name, without repeats (the contract), `total` — of all matches. More than 500 matches (`selectMax`) and all conditions are fields of `AssistFoundFilter` — the form `filter` below; not expressible by it — the first 500 `ids` and `truncated: true`.
  - The scope is larger than `LIST_MAX`: all conditions are fields of `AssistFoundFilter`: `q`, `fields`, `kinds`, `implements`, `status`, `domainId`, `noFolder`, `noRecord`, `noDetector` — `filter`, `type` goes as `kinds: [<type>]`: the form pages through it with the same bounded pass as an ordinary filter (V/11.3 N1.2b «GET /units filters by cost»), no `total`, `truncated: true`: there was no full pass. `serverId`, `archiveId`, `folderId`, `parentId`, `ref` of `GET /units` are not in `AssistFoundFilter`: a condition on them goes by the form `ids` or by a refusal. Not expressible — `refused` «more than 5000 devices: this condition cannot be checked here; narrow the selection by folder, server or kind» (ADR-0093 addendum (15) item 4).
- The event is `found {text, context, conditions, ids | filter, total?, truncated?}`: `text` — the words of the model, `conditions` — one line per condition of `where`, translated by `backend`, not by the model (**decided**): «`<caption fields>: <variant>`» by the labels and options of the field overview, the operator in a word: `eq` — «:», `ne` — «: not», `in` — «: one of», `not_in` — «: none of», `contains` — «: contains», `not_contains` — «: does not contain», `exists` — «present», `missing` — «absent»; the form shows them above the tree.
- **Detectors.** The camera field `status.observed.detectorKinds` (V/11.3, `mapping.tsv`): the kinds of all its detectors, disabled ones too — the `det/units` rows with its `cam` and the built-in ones by `detector-kinds.tsv`, outside the table `other`; the filter `noDetector: <kind>` is the same derivation, `noDetector: true` — not a single detector. «A camera without a motion detector» — one without a row of that kind at all. **Limitation:** a disabled detector counts as configured.
- **Cost:** a read of the scope, up to 5000 rows per subsystem, for every selection utterance; a condition outside the filters — a read of the recording per camera. **Limitation:** one or two conditions on the fields of the camera itself are reliable (ADR-0093 addendum (14) item 2); the selection measurement on the norms of V/11 was not made.

### N1.9 The type `export`

ADR-0093 addendum (47) item 6; the contract 754a52e: `AssistExport`, `AssistEvent.export`; on 0.2.0 — a camera by `uid` (`ExportCamera.uid`, ADR-0116 addendum (9)). The content is the package of the pilot `handover/export` (`assist-pilot`, the branch `export`, 0f40447; the model Gemma 4 26B-A4B IQ4 of CT 103, `tool_choice: required`). The type orders an export by V/11.20 and writes nothing (N1.2): the outcome is the body `AssistExport`, which the person checks and sends in the export window.

- **The switch and the declaration.** `ASSIST_EXPORT` (V/09.2): `on` — the type is reached; `off` — a turn of the type `export` (by the selection words or by `route`) ends without a model step with `refused` «The assistant does not prepare exports here yet; order one in the export window.». There is no `Reference.export` (the system did not declare `createExport`, V/11.20 N1.8; the contract: «present if the system declares createExport») — `refused` without a model step «This system has no export: the reference does not declare it (reference.export).».
- **The world** is what `backend` gives the person on the contract reads, by the stream ticket (V/11.3): `GET /me` — `serverTime`, `timezone`; `GET /reference` — `export`; the cameras — the records of `GET /units?implements=vms.camera/1`: `uid`, `name`, `properties.ip`, `properties.archiveId`, `status.link`, `folders`; the names of the folders — the records of the kind `folder` (`GET /units?kind=folder`). «Now» is `serverTime`, the clock of the server; **decided:** it is read once at the start of the HTTP request.
- **The system message** is the bytes of [`harness/export/system.md`](../harness/export/system.md) with the substitutions of the table and, after the text, the cameras block; the `user` message is the request as is (the texts of the turn, N1.2).

More than 300 cameras — the line `## Cameras` and the line `<N> cameras: find them with find_cameras.` The line of a camera begins with its key: the model names the camera by it, the code translates the key into `Unit.uid`. - **The tools** are [`harness/export/tools.json`](../harness/export/tools.json):
  - `find_cameras {text}` reads. The words of `text` in lower case (`ё` → `е`), without `камера`, `камеры`, `камер`, `камеру`, `камерой`, `camera`, `cameras`, `the`, `с`, `со`, `из`; a word matches when one of the words of the name begins with it or it begins with that word; words longer than 4 letters are compared without the last two letters («главного» ≈ «главный»); every word must match. The name and the folder are apart: the result is `{by_name, folders, in_folder, in_folder_count}` — `by_name` the cameras whose name has all the words, `folders` the folders whose name has them (at most 10), `in_folder` all cameras of these folders; the lines as in the block, each list at most 50. «Касса» is not the cameras of the folder «Кассовые зоны», «папка Цех» is not the camera «Цех — проход»; a folder request takes every camera of `in_folder` (hold1 H22). An empty `text` — all cameras.
  - **The exact name** (H19, R04; decided). A name equal to the whole name of a camera is that camera, even when other names begin with it: «Рампа» with «Рампа 1», «Рампа 2» in the world is the camera «Рампа» — the order, the camera named in `text`, the person sees it in the window before «Export»; `ask_admin` — only when no camera has exactly that name and more than one begins with it. The rule is a line of [`harness/export/system.md`](../harness/export/system.md).
