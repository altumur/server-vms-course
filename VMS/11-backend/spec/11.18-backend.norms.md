### N1.1 Camera recordings

The witness page; the form — V/11.3 N1.3.

- A recording is a row `rec/recordings/<id>` with the field `cam` of the camera; a camera may have several of them (V/02.1 N1.1).
- **The state in one word** — by the row and the element of `status[]`: switched off (`enabled: false`); not placed (there is no element);
  the recorder is silent (`stale`); `running` — it records, for `replica` — it pulls from the source; `pending` — it is starting; `standby` — it waits: the primary is running; `waiting` — it waits;
  `failed` — an error; anything else — by the word of the phase. Several elements — the one with `worker_state: live` is read, there is no such one — the last one. The reason is
  the `reason` of the element (the words of the subsystem; V/02.4 N1.8); why there is no sound — `audio_reason` in the same order.
- **An edit is an action:** switch on and off, the volume (`home`), the retention (an integer ≥ 1), the source `replica_from`, the microphone `audio` (an integer ≥ 0, V/02.1 N1.1) — `PUT /rec/recordings/<id>`
  with one field, except a change of home to `backup`; delete — `DELETE` with a confirmation («what was recorded stays in the volume until its retention expires» — what was recorded stays in the volume until its retention expires). Empty in `home` — «any free volume»
  (any free volume).
- **A backup recording** — with its home on a volume `backup` or `edge` (V/02.1 `when`, V/02.6): on the timeline its spans yield to the primary one (chapter 16 N1.2). A recording has no choice of `when`: a new recording on a volume `backup` and a change of home to it are written with `when: offline` (`{home, when}` in one write, V/02.6 N1.4); on `edge` the word belongs to the volume; the recording `cam-<cam>-<volume>` on the card is ordered by the camera holder (V/01.6 N1.7).
- **A recording without a live stream** (V/02.6 N1.9) — the choice «from where» of a recording that is not on `backup` and not on `edge`: the live stream, the device archive (with an empty `stream`), another recording of the same camera and the same `stream`, except recordings on `backup`; a value outside the list is shown as is. The page does not judge a cycle: the words are `reason`. `replica_lag_s`, `replica_lost_s`, `over_quota` — as a caption; a value not in form — there is no caption. Such a recording is not a primary one: «Pin» does not count it (chapter 16).
- **The choice of volume** for a camera recording — the volumes that are switched on, accept recordings (without `admits: false` — `incidents`), without a card
  (`edge`): the page's selection rule; a volume that the row already names is shown. The door does not refuse a recording on a card (`must_match` of
  `home` is removed, V/02.1 N1.1), the guard is the recorder of the card (V/02.6 N1.7).
- **A new recording** — `POST /rec/recordings {name, cam[, stream], retention_days[, home][, when][, ref]}`; the camera and the stream are chosen together
  (cameras — the rows with an empty `main` and `type: camera`, V/01.1 N1.1b; the stream is empty — the main one; the hint — the largest by `size`, V/01.1 N1.1a); the retention — as for
  an edit; `ref` — the `ref` of the camera row, if it has one (V/02.1 N1.1). The name of the recording is
  the first free one in this order: the camera id; `<cam>-<volume>`, if a volume is chosen; `<cam>-s<stream>`, if there is no volume but a stream is chosen
  (`s` — so as not to coincide with a volume name made of digits); that one is taken too or there is nothing to choose — a refusal in words, without a write. A camera that the chosen server (volume) already records, the page
  does not offer.

### N1.2 Holds

- **The form:** `GET /units/{uid}/holds` (`uid` — the camera), the stream `kind: hold` rereads the list; the translation — V/11.4 N1.23.
- A hold is a row `rec/keeps/{cam}-{from:int}-{to:int}` (V/02.1 N1.2, the `rec` canon `keeps.key`; V/02.7 N1.1), `id` is the key itself; for a foreign camera `<key>@<member>`. **«Hold what is shown»** (hold what is shown): `POST /units/{uid}/holds {from, to, note?, recordingIds?}`; the dialog window is in minutes, by default the window shown on the timeline; `recordingIds` — the recordings of the spans of the window at the moment of setting (one deleted later is copied), an unchecked box does not hold; all are unchecked — there is no request: an empty `recordingIds` means «all recordings of the camera», V/11.4 N1.23, the order of the Console; the end is later than the start. The key already exists — the `recordings` are merged, the note and `released_at` are not erased, 200. A foreign camera — 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER`.
- Lift — `DELETE /holds/{id}` with a confirmation («the segment is again subject to retention» — the segment is again subject to the retention). `releasedAt` — «released — no longer holds». **Limit:** a broken keep that deletion does not take is lifted by the table door, bypassing the form (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 9).
- **The state** — `states[]` `{archiveId, state, why?}`: the heartbeat words, only the fields named by the `rec` manifest in `servers.status` with `of: keeps` (V/02.7 N1.5). The recorder of a recording says `here`, `pushing`, `pushed`, `at risk` and `garbled` (with `why`), `lost`; the `incidents` holder — `kept`, `empty` — «there are no frames in the segment — nothing to seal». The list is open: a word without a translation is shown as is. Not named — not said. `incidents_lost`, `incidents_at_risk` the form does not show: chapter 15 N1.1.
- There is no `incidents` volume with a holder, or it is not confirmed (`status.observed.heldBy` of the `volume` record is `null`, N1.3) — the warning «what is held remains held only while the recording archive keeps it» (what is held is held only while the recording archive keeps it). The form reads `GET /units?kind=archive.incidents`, `GET /units?kind=volume`.
- **«Verify»** (verify) — `POST /holds/{id}/verify` (V/11.4 N1.23); `backend`, per recording of the hold, otherwise per recording of the camera: the place door of the first switched-on `incidents` volume with a holder (`/rec/where/<table>/<volume>?unit=<recording>`, chapter 16 N1.1; each volume has its own seal, V/02.7 N1.4; **limit:** another volume is not verified) and `POST /keeps/<key>/verify?recording=<recording>`, if `rec` opens the route `keeps` (`door.routes`). The answer is `results[].result`, one word per recording: `ok` — intact, `damaged` — damaged, `pending`, `unverified` — nothing to compare with, `empty` — «no frames» (no frames; not a refusal); a refusal — in words.

### N1.3 Volumes

The witness page; the form — V/11.3 N1.3, V/11.4. `held_by` for the form is `status.observed.heldBy` of a `volume` record.

- A volume is a row of the table of places of `rec`; a **place**, while a recorder holds it (`held_by`): «served by: `<worker>`» (served by), «no one serves it, or it is not confirmed»
  (no one serves it, or it is not confirmed) (`null` means both «no» and «unknown», P/06.1), «switched off».
- The tree: under a server — its volumes (except `network`); «Network archives» — `network`. The volume card — the declaration and the recordings
  that chose it; the server block «Server archives» (the server's archives) (`covers: rec/volumes`).
- **The directory is gone** — `volume_missing` of the heartbeat of the server's recorders: it is named, with a link to the volume.
- **Declare** — `POST /rec/<table>` with the row **whole** (P/06.1: a full replacement of the fields): the body is assembled from all fields
  of the read row, except the computed ones (`table_platform_fields` of the row check contract: `by`, `at`, `made_at`, `held_by`, `garbled`,
  `revision`, `index`) and the secrets, —
  and the edits of the window are laid over them; what the window does not show is taken from the row as is — by a general rule, not by a listing
  (`admits: false` of an ordinary volume is kept); `when` — only for `edge`, the window's choice at re-declaration; `incidents` — always with `admits: false` (the schema
  of the table, V/02.1 N1.2); `shrink_confirmed` — by the rule below; a secret is empty — the former one (P/06.2), the address has changed — enter it
  anew. A case: `local` with `admits: false` — re-declaration does not remove it. A quota not touched in the window — the bytes of the row; the access
  key — for a kind with an address, except `edge`; the name does not change at re-declaration. There is the word `api.if-match` — `ifMatch`: the `revision`
  of the read row; 412 — words, open anew.
- **Quota reduction** — only with a second word of the operator: a smaller quota, or a quota whose reduction the holder names
  unconfirmed (`shrink_pending` of the heartbeat, V/02.6 N1.2), — the confirmation «the oldest will be erased» and
  `shrink_confirmed` = the new quota; outside a reduction: the same quota — the former value, a different one — no field; a pending reduction down to the
  declared quota — on the volume card.
- **Revoke** — `DELETE /rec/<table>/<name>` with the confirmation: «what was recorded is not deleted; recordings that chose the volume will move away» (what was recorded is not deleted; the recordings that chose the volume will move away).

## N4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a console door refused with 4xx | «Refused: `<words>`» | a pop-up | fix it |
| there is no answer, or 5xx | «outcome unknown», reread (chapter 15 N1.2) | a pop-up | by what is read |
| reading failed | «not read: `<words>`», the former stays in place | the tab, the block | later |
| the name of a new recording is taken | «the recording already exists», without a write | a pop-up | choose a volume |
| a card of a foreign camera | a refusal of the page in words before the write (not of the door) | a pop-up | another volume |
| the `incidents` holder is not confirmed | «nowhere to verify» | a pop-up | bring up a recorder |
| the verification did not confirm | «Not confirmed: …» per recording | a pop-up | — |
| `rec` is not mounted (there is no manifest) | the card «the recording subsystem is not connected» | the tab, the block | — |


The refusal words are the contract's `Problem`, by chapter 16 N4.
