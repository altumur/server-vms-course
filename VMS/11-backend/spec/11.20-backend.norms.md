# V/11.20 — Export: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/11.20-backend.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 20 of lesson V/11 «Configurator backend» (ADR-0117 § 8, addenda (1)–(7); ADR-0119 item 4, addenda (5)–(7), (10)–(12); the Architect's decision of 2026-11-04 on the fork contract `a9a96e2`). Lecture (`ru/11-backend.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](11-backend.README.md), the contract — [`consolem.openapi.json`](../consolem.openapi.json). The platform is pinned by
[`VMS/PLATFORM_PIN`](../../PLATFORM_PIN) (ADR-VMS-0002) (the VMS pin).

The export window of the fork speaks of an order (`Export`): cameras, periods, settings, progress and files. The subsystem `exp` has no order row: its unit is the part — one camera, one recording, its files ([V/08.6](../../08-exp/spec/08.6-exp.norms.md)). `backend` writes the parts of an order in one batch, assembles the order back by reading its parts, files the requests `cancel` and `delete` to their holders, takes the file link from the console and gives the progress to the author's stream; the bytes go past it (ADR-0015). The chapter sets norms for the ten operations of the tag `export`, for `Reference.export` and for the messages `export.progress`, `export.finished`.

Norm ids are `V/11.20-MM`.

## N0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Platform» are [P/01.1 N0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary). The words of the export — order, part, leading part, part holder — are V/08.6 N0.

- **Order id** — the `name` of the leading part, the field `export` of every part of the order.
- **Unfinished order** — an order with at least one part without `result` (V/08.6 N1.3).
- **`may(c, u, s)`** — the rule `may` of the module over the grants of `GET /session` of the person of the ticket (P/07 N1.5), as `can_edit` of chapter 7 N1.2: the right `c` on the unit `u` in the subsystem `s`.

## N1 Form

**Time.** The moments of the contract (`date-time`) ↔ Unix seconds of the part rows (`periods`, `at`, `result.expires`), the milliseconds — a fraction. The days and the time in file names are in the time zone of the system — `timezone` of `GET /session` (norm B6; the Architect's decision of 2026-11-04).

**Data.** The part rows `exp/parts/<name>` with their state `cursor`, `result` (the element `configured` of `GET /exp/parts`, P/06.1 N2 «State in the answer»), the requests `POST /exp/requests`, the link `GET /exp/where/<part>`, the placement decision and the heartbeats of `exp` — all through the console doors by the ticket of the request (V/11.1 N1.5). The chapter has no family of its own; the repeat memory is the table `backend/repeats` (chapter 4 N1.4). The key of the subsystem file `/etc/backend/backend.env` (chapter 6 N1.3):

| Key | Read by | Grammar | Default | Norm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EXPORT_KEEP` | the worker `backend` | an integer ≥ 60, seconds | `86400` | N1.2, N1.8 |

`EXPORT_KEEP` is the system setting of V/08.8 N1.4: the `keep` of every part of a new order and `limits.keepSec`.

### N1.1 Rights: `getExportGrant`

The right to create a part is `edit` on `exp` by the camera; the function `EXPORT` translates into it, `admin` — `EXPORT` with both flags (V/08.6 N1.5, V/11.8). There is no masking yet, and a mandatory mask cannot be executed: the first generation exports only with `admin` on `exp` by every camera of the order (V/08.6 N1.5 «Without a mask»; ADR-0117 addendum (4) item 5).

- **`GET /me/export?ids=`.** `ids` — the `uid` of cameras separated by commas, up to `idsMax`; more, or an id not in UUID form — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: ids`.
- `allowed` — the grants of `GET /session` give the person `edit` or higher on `exp` at some point (a line with `sub: exp` or without `sub`): this is the function `EXPORT`.
- `devices[]` — `{uid, archive, live}` per `uid` of `ids`, in their order: `archive` — `may("admin", "unit:cam/<id>", "exp")`; `live` — `archive` and `may("view", "unit:cam/<id>", "live")`, the right of live viewing (V/08.6 N1.5). A camera of another cluster and an id that is not a camera of the own cluster — both `false`: such an order is refused (N1.2).
- `passwordRequired`, `privacyForced` — `true` if a camera of `ids` has `archive: false`; without `ids` — if `may("admin", "*", "exp")` does not hold. Both flags are one rank, so the two fields are equal (ADR-0117 addendum (6) item 3).
- `refusal` — with `allowed` and `privacyForced`: «masking is required by policy, and the system has none» (masking is required by the policy, and the system has none); otherwise no field (ADR-0117 addendum (2) item 4).
- `supervisorRequired` — the header `X-W2C-Supervision` of the request names `exp` or is `*` (the gate and pass contract «Supervision»; ADR-0119 addendum (7) item 1); there is no header — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`.
- `watermark` — `false`: there is no watermark (ADR-0117 § 4 item 6). `depthHours` — `0`: `depth` of a role is not judged by the export (V/08.6 N1.5), a limit.
- The grant is an answer, not a judgement: an order is judged anew at creation (N1.2).

### N1.2 Order: `createExport`, `checkExport`

The order id is `<t>-<r>`: `t` — the decimal Unix seconds of the clock of `backend` at creation, `r` — 16 random lowercase hex digits. `Export.id` is the UUID v5 of `export:<CLUSTER>/exp/parts/<order id>` (chapter 4 N1.6); `Export.createdAt` is `t`: the part row has no moment of its own.

**Checks** — in this order; the first refusal is the answer, nothing is written:

1. The body is not by the schema `ExportCreate` of the contract — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field` — the JSON Pointer of the place in the body (the Architect's decision of 2026-11-04).
2. The declaration and the reference: the table «Settings»; `format` outside `Reference.export.formats` or with a `kind` other than `mode` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /format`; `singleFile: true` with a format whose `singleFile` is not `tracks` — `field: /singleFile`; `source: live` with `mode: video` — `field: /source`; more than one camera with `mode: frame` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /cameras` (the Architect's decision of 2026-11-04).
3. The combinations of V/08.6 N1.1 — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`: `mode: video` without `periods` — `field: /periods`, with `at` — `field: /at`; `mode: frame` with `periods` — `field: /periods`; `mode: frame`, `source: archive` without `at` — `field: /at`; `source: live` with `at` — `field: /at`, with `cameras[i].archiveId` — `field: /cameras/i/archiveId`. A period with `to` not later than `from` — `field: /periods/i/to`. An area box outside `w > 0`, `h > 0`, `x + w ≤ 1`, `y + h ≤ 1` — `field: /cameras/i/area` (ADR-0117 addendum (7) item 3). A repeat of `uid` in `cameras` — `field: /cameras/i`.
4. The limits of V/08.6 N1.4 — 422 `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`: more cameras than `EXPORT_CAMERAS_MAX` — `field: /cameras`; more periods than `EXPORT_PERIODS_MAX` or their sum longer than `EXPORT_TOTAL_MAX` — `field: /periods`.
5. The cameras: no record with the `uid` — 404 `NOT_FOUND`, `field: /cameras/i`; the kind of the record does not implement `vms.camera/1` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /cameras/i` (**Decided:** by the contract, `createExport` and `ExportCamera.uid`); a camera of another cluster — 409 `OTHER_CLUSTER`, `params.url` (chapter 4 N1.13; V/08.7 N1.1); `archiveId` names no recording of the camera — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /cameras/i/archiveId`; no `archiveId` and no primary recording — 422 `NO_RECORDING`, `field: /cameras/i`.
6. The rights (N1.1): no `edit` on `exp` by the camera — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `field: /cameras/i`; `edit` without `admin` — the same with `params.reason: privacy`: the mask is mandatory and cannot be executed; a live frame without `may("view", "unit:cam/<id>", "live")` — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `field: /cameras/i`.
7. The password: a camera without `admin` and no `password` — 422 `PASSWORD_REQUIRED`, `field: /password`. In the first generation item 6 refuses such an order earlier; the item waits for masking — a limit; the contract allows the system never to give this code (`cb728a4`).
8. Supervision (ADR-0119 item 4, addenda (5)–(7)): there is no header `X-W2C-Supervision` — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`; it names `exp` or is `*`, and there is no `supervisor` — 403 `SUPERVISOR_REQUIRED`, `field: /supervisor` (ADR-0119 addendum (5) item 2; the contract `cb728a4`); `supervisor` is given — «Supervisor» below. Supervision does not depend on the feature `accessSupervision`: its only sign is `supervisorRequired` (N1.1; the contract `cb728a4`). Without supervision a given `supervisor` is accepted and changes nothing: no call, no `supervised_by`.
9. Unfinished orders of the person (`by` — the person) — `EXPORT_ACTIVE_PER_USER` or more — 409 `BUSY` (the contract: `limits.maxActivePerUser`).
10. Before the write: (a) `source: archive` — per camera `GET /rec/where/<rec>` and `GET <door>/timeline/<rec>?from&to` over the window of its periods, for a frame — `[at − FRAME_BACK, at]` (V/08.7 N1.3); a span with `source: device` does not count: the part reads only `/spans` of the recorders (V/08.7 N1.1). No camera has a span in any period — 422 `NO_RECORDING`, `field: /periods`, for a frame — `/at`; a door that did not answer — and a `GET /rec/where` without a holder — gives no refusal, the part judges itself (`no_recording`, `unreachable`); empty periods of a part are not a refusal: the part goes with `partial`. (b) Transcoding, and no server of the cluster has the label `gpu` (`GET /servers`) — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field` — the first of `/quality`, `/titles`, `/cameras/i/area` that requires transcoding (V/08.6 N1.2 `constraint`). (c) A live frame from a camera whose `status.link` (chapter 3) is `offline` — 409 `DEVICE_OFFLINE`; `unknown` — the order goes, the part judges itself (`offline`, V/08.7 N1.2).

**Supervisor.** `supervisor.login` is the author (`by`) — 403 `SUPERVISOR_REJECTED`, `field: /supervisor`, without a call (ADR-0119 item 4). Otherwise `POST /domain/verify {user: <login>, password, need: {cap: admin, sub: exp, units: [cam/<cam>…]}}` by the ticket (P/06.7 N1.15): 200 with `may: allow` for every unit and, when `supervision.required`, with `supervision.ok` — `supervised_by` is `user` of the answer; a unit with `deny`, `ok: false`, 401 — 403 `SUPERVISOR_REJECTED`, `field: /supervisor`; `unavailable`, 503, `409 deposed` — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`; 429 — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` with its `Retry-After`. When the answer and the header differ, the answer judges (ADR-0119 addendum (7) item 2).

**Write.** One batch `POST /console/batch` with the ticket (V/08.6 N1.1 «Who writes»; `writes_via: [backend]`): every part with `cas ""`; with `idempotencyKey` — also the row `backend/repeats/<h>` by chapter 4 N1.4 with `op: createExport` and one step: `steps` — `[{index: 0, outcome: done, id: <Export.id>}]`. A refusal of the batch is translated by the refusing row: 403 — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, `field: /cameras/i` of this part; 400 — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`; 412 on a part (the id is taken) — a new `r`, once; the others — chapter 4 N4. The answer is 202 with `Export` of the written rows (N1.3: all parts `wait`), `Location` — the path of `getExport` of this order.

**Repeat.** The same `idempotencyKey` — chapter 4 N1.4: at `state: done` — the order read now (N1.3), 202; no part is left — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; another body — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: idempotencyKey`. Without a key every request is a new order (V/08.8 N1.5).

**Body digest (`createExport` only).** The field `body` of `backend/repeats` is lowercase hex SHA-256 of UTF-8 JCS of a copy of the parsed request body after removing exactly `password` and `supervisor.password` (ADR-0119 addendum (3) item 3; the Architect's clarification). Remove each field only if it exists; keep every other field, the order of array elements and the `supervisor` object itself. If neither field exists, canonicalize the whole parsed body as is. JCS is [RFC 8785](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785) in full, the same canon as [P/01 JCS](../../../Platform/01-store/vectors/jcs/README.md). The original request body is not changed: its passwords still serve the checks and the export. Password-only changes, object-key order and JSON whitespace do not change this digest; every other field remains in the hash input. The body digests of other operations are unchanged.

**Check** (`checkExport`). Items 1–10 without the write; the supervision of item 8 and «Supervisor» are judged as in the order, with `/domain/verify` (**decided**, the Architect's word 2026-11-05: the check judges the password of the supervisor too, the contract `cb728a4`: the refusals of supervision are in `errors` with the same codes): each call to `/domain/verify` spends one attempt of the limit of the supervisor (P/08.4 N1.10 `verify`) — a cost. A refusal of item 1 is the answer 422 itself; the others make 200 `ExportCheck`:

- `errors` — a `Problem` per refusal of items 2–10, each with its `field`, in the order of the items; after a refusal of a camera (`field` `/cameras/i…`) the later items of the same camera are not judged; the items of the whole order (`/cameras`, `/format`, `/periods`, `/password`, `/supervisor`, `BUSY`) do not stop the items of the cameras. `ok` — `errors` is empty.
- `transcode` — the translated `settings` require transcoding by V/08.7 N1.4.
- `files` — per camera `{uid, name}`: the name by «File name» below with `n` = 1.
- `noRecording` — `{camera, period}`, the numbers in the body of a camera and a period without a span by item 10 (a frame — `period: 0`); a camera whose door did not answer is not named.
- `estimate` — no field: the size is estimated by the holder before the start (V/08.7 N1.4 «Space»), a limit.

**File name.** The name is the holder's (V/08.7 N1.4): `backend` gives `result.files[].name` as it is and builds the same name in `checkExport`. The time in the name is in the time zone of the system (the Architect's decision of 2026-11-04): `backend` writes `settings.tz` of every part — `timezone` of `GET /session`; the holder names by it (V/08.7 N1.4).

### N1.3 Reading: `listExports`, `getExport`

- **Read.** `GET /exp/parts` by the ticket: the rows the person sees (the `view` filter of the door by the camera, P/06.1), with `state` in `configured`. The parts are grouped by `export`; an order is assembled from its visible parts: the leading one may already be gone (V/08.6 N1.1). **Cost:** one read of the whole table per request.
- **`Export`.** `id`, `createdAt` — N1.2; `by`, `supervisedBy` — `by`, `supervised_by` of a part; `retryOf` — the UUID of the order `retry_of` by N1.2; `transcode` — a part has `gpu` in `labels`; `request` — the body assembled back by the tables of N1.2: `cameras[]` in the order of `n` (`uid`, `archiveId` — the storage of the volume `home` of `rec`, `area`), the rest from the leading part, otherwise from the part with the smallest `n`; `supervisor` — `{login}` of `supervised_by` of the leading part when there is one, without `password`: the password is `writeOnly`, required only in the request (the contract `cb728a4`); what no part carries (`name`, `bookmarkId`, `idempotencyKey`, `password`) — no field; `enforced` — no field: the first generation exports only with `admin`, nothing is imposed (N1.1).
- **`ExportPart`** per part in the order of `n` (`n` = 0 — the leading part): `uid` — of the camera `cam`; `from`, `to` — the start of the first and the end of the last segment of `periods`; `at` — `at`; `state` — without `result`: `wait` without `cursor`, `run` with it; with `result.end` `done` — `ok`, `failed` — `fail`, `canceled` — `cancel`, `expired` — `fail`; `percent` — ⌊100 × `cursor.done`⌋, with `result` — 100; `code` — `result.code`, at `expired` — `expired`; `partial` — `result.partial` is not empty.
- **`progress`:** `total` — the number of parts, `done` — those with `result`, `percent` — ⌊100 × Σ s / total⌋, `s` — 1 for a part with `result`, otherwise `cursor.done`, without `cursor` — 0.
- **`state`:** a part without `result` — `running`, and if none of the parts has `cursor` — `queued`; all with `result`: at least one `done` — `done`; otherwise at least one `canceled` — `cancelled`; otherwise all `expired` — `expired`; otherwise `failed`.
- `finishedAt` — when all parts have `result`: the latest `result.expires` − `keep`; `expiresAt` — the latest `result.expires`.
- **`files`** — `result.files` of the parts in the order of `n`: `id` — `<n>-<file id>`, `uid` — of the camera `cam` of the part, `name`, `sizeBytes` — `size`, `sha256`, `mediaType` — by the extension, as `Content-Type` of the file door (V/08.9 N1.2), `expiresAt` — `result.expires` of the part. Another's order — the files are named, the link is refused (N1.6).
- **`GET /exports`.** `mine=true` — only orders with `by` = the person; `state` — values of `Export.state` separated by commas, «or»; another word — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: state`. Newest on top: by `t`, then by `r`, descending; pages — the general rule of chapter 3 N1.2b, the place is the order id.
- **`GET /exports/{id}`** — the order whose `Export.id` is `id` (the way back — by the listing, chapter 4 N1.6); no visible part — 404 `NOT_FOUND`.

### N1.4 Requests: `cancelExport`, `deleteExport`

- The order — N1.3; none — 404 `NOT_FOUND`. The right — per part: the author (`by` — the person) or `may("admin", "unit:cam/<cam>", "exp")` (V/08.8 N1.1); a part without it — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`, nothing is filed.
- **`POST /exports/{id}/cancel`.** All parts have `result` — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`. Otherwise `POST /exp/requests {unit: <part>, action: cancel}` to every part without `result` (V/08.8 N1.2); 200 — the order read anew: the holders execute later, the truth is `GET` and the stream.
- **`DELETE /exports/{id}`.** To every part — `POST /exp/requests {unit: <part>, action: delete}` (V/08.8 N1.3); a part without a holder (V/08.8 N1.4 «A part without a holder»: the decision `exp/placement/<part>` names no worker or there is no decision row, or the heartbeat of the named one is older than `LOST_AFTER`) — `DELETE /exp/parts/<part>` by CAS of the revision read together with the decision; 412 — read anew, once. 204.
- **Filing refusals:** 403 — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`; 429 of `per_person` (V/08.8 N4) — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE` with `Retry-After`; the requests already filed stay, a repeat files the rest — a limit. The mark of the holder (`refused` `not the author`, `done` `nothing to cancel`) comes later; `backend` does not wait for it.
- **Limit:** `cancel` of a part without a holder is executed by no one: the part stays `wait` (N1.3: no `cursor`) until a new holder takes it or until `DELETE`.

### N1.5 Retry: `retryExport`

`POST /exports/{id}/retry-failed {password?, supervisor?}`: the order — N1.3, none — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; no part with `result.end: failed` — 409 `NOT_RUNNABLE`. Otherwise a new order of these parts by N1.2: `cam`, `rec`, `source`, `mode`, `periods`, `at`, `format`, `settings`, `comment` of the part as they are, `password_secret` — `password` of the body, `by` — the person, `retry_of` — the order id (V/08.8 N1.5); the checks — items 6–10 of N1.2 over these parts. The leading part of the new order is the first of them by `n`: «in one file» holds only the repeated parts — a limit. The supervision is judged anew (item 8 and «Supervisor» of N1.2 over the cameras of these parts): the former supervisor does not count, under supervision without `supervisor` — 403 `SUPERVISOR_REQUIRED`, `field: /supervisor`, a wrong one — 403 `SUPERVISOR_REJECTED`; `supervised_by` of the new parts — the answer of `/domain/verify` (the contract `cb728a4`). There is no repeat memory: every call is a new order, as `retry-failed` of operations (chapter 6 N1.3). 202 — the new `Export`.

### N1.6 File link: `getExportFileLink`

`POST /exports/{id}/files/{fileId}/link`: the order — N1.3, none — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; `fileId` — `<n>-<file id>` of N1.3: no such part or no such id in `result.files` — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; the part is `expired` or its `result.expires` has passed — 410 `EXPORT_EXPIRED`; `by` of the part is not the person — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`: only the author (V/08.6 N1.5, V/08.9 N1.2). Then `GET /exp/where/<part>` by the ticket ([V/08.9](../../08-exp/spec/08.9-exp.norms.md) N1.1): `url` — the address of V/08.9 N1.1 with `door` and `token` of the answer and the file id of the part; `expiresAt` — `expires`, the timeout `EXPORT_LINK_TTL`. A refusal of `where`: 403 — 403 `PERMISSION_DENIED`; 404 or no `door` — 503 `UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE`: the holder is not known. The door judges the token anew (V/08.9 N1.2). `stub://` of the fork's test server `backend` never gives.

### N1.7 Recording days: `getUnitRecordingDays`

`GET /units/{uid}/recording-days?from&to`: no record — 404 `NOT_FOUND`; the kind of the record does not implement `vms.camera/1` — 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `field: /kind`. `from`, `to` — dates in the time zone of the system; none — the last 31 days: today and the 30 before; `to` earlier than `from` — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /to`; **Decided:** by the contract, a window longer than a year (more than 366 days) — 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `field: /from`; the `field` is a JSON Pointer to the query parameter (the contract). The window is from 00:00 of `from` to 00:00 of the day after `to`, in this zone.

- **Own camera.** The recordings — `GET /rec/recordings` with its `cam`; per recording `GET /rec/where/<recording>` and `GET <door>/timeline/<recording>?from&to` (chapter 4 N1.22, steps 1 and 2 for the own cluster). The places of the table of places (step 3) are not read, and a span with `source: device` does not count: the part does not read them (V/08.7 N1.1). An answer larger than 1 MiB — the window is split as in chapter 4 N1.22, up to `TIMELINE_SPLITS`.
- `days` — every day of the window in order, `recorded: true` — a span of a recording intersects the day. `unavailable` — as for the timeline (chapter 4 N1.22 «`unavailable`»): a door that did not answer, the unread rest of the window; there may be more days with a recording.
- **A camera of another cluster** — `unavailable` `{what: cluster, id: <member>}`, all days `recorded: false`: the export does not go there (N1.2 item 5).
- **Cost:** 1 + 2R calls, R — the recordings of the camera; the timeline also reads the places. `depth` of a role is not judged — a limit (V/08.6 N1.5).

### N1.9 Stream: `export.progress`, `export.finished`

The state of a part is not carried by the feed, as the scan result is not (V/05.1; chapter 5 N1.3), so the stream reads it:

- While the person of the stream has an unfinished order of their own (`by` — the person of the stream), no more often than `VIEW_POLL` per stream it reads `GET /exp/parts` and compares `cursor` and `result` of the parts of its orders with those remembered. A new order is learned from the feed: a row `exp/parts/<name>` with `revision: 1`, its follow-up read gives `by`. At the opening of the stream — one read, the values are remembered, there are no messages.
- `cursor.done` or `result` of a part changed — `export.progress`: `kind: exportJob` (the contract: the kind `export` is taken by the object «Export agent»), `entityId` — `Export.id`, `payload` — `{progress, part}`: `Export.progress` and `ExportPart` of this part (N1.3). The last part of the order got `result` — after it `export.finished`, `payload` — `{state, files}` of the order (N1.3).
- Only to the author: parts of another `by` give no messages. The `id` of a message is as for the end of search (chapter 5 N1.2); a break does not redeliver: the truth is `GET`.
- **Cost:** a read of the table per `VIEW_POLL` per stream with an unfinished order; without one — no read.
