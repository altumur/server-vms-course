## Н1 Form

**Time.** Unix seconds; `within`, `valid_for` — seconds.

### Н1.1 Scenarios

- The list is `/auto/scenarios` (`configured` and `rows`): a scenario exists while its row exists; the word: off, `running` —
  watching, `refused` — refused, `unreadable` — not readable, otherwise — being placed.
- **The key is a number.** A scenario is `auto/scenarios/<id>`, the `id` is issued by the door (V/07.1); the list goes by number, the address, the selection and the log — by number, people see `name`. A new one is found by the row from the answer of `POST`.
- **Form = schema:** «if» is 1–4 triggers `{sub, kind[, unit][, match]}` (V/07.1 Н1.1 `when`); an enabled scenario without `start` or with `start: event` needs at least one, a disabled one and the others — none; `match` is a JSON object
  as
  is (JSON text, parsing, the refusal «уточнения — объект JSON»): the read–write circle keeps the types, the comparison is strict (V/07
  question 1, `"1"` ≠ `1`; the case: `{"pass": 3}` stays a number after an edit of the refinements); «then» is 1–4 actions, an enabled one needs at least one, each is
  one of the five forms of `then` of the manifest `auto` with its required fields (`cam output`: `unit`, `port`[, `state`, `pulse_ms`];
  `cam preset`: `unit`, `n`; `rec record`: `cam`, `minutes`[, `archive`]; `det detect`: `cam`, `kind`, `minutes`[, `params`];
  `det scan`: `cam`, `kind`[, `before`, `after`, `rec`, `params`]); a step with `target` does not require `unit`, `cam`, and an action outside these five is shown and written as is. The form writes the values **with the type of the schema `then`** (a form into
  someone else's row is not wider than the owner's schema, class V/07 Н-1): `port`, `n` — integers from 1, `pulse_ms` — an integer from 0, `minutes` —
  a number 1…1440, `before`, `after` — numbers 0…3600; the rest — strings. `params` is the model settings string, as at the owner of the
  field
  (`det.units.params`, V/04.1 Н1.1): a text field «as is», without JSON parsing. `det` actions — only when the console
  mounts `det`: `det` is in `/mounts` of the console (P/06.5 `root`), not among the subsystems of the module (the `mount` of the page is `cam`,
  `rec`,
  `live`): they are offered only then, a saved `det` action is shown and written as is. The trigger kinds are suggested by
  the words `display.kinds` of the trigger's subsystem; another kind is typed in.
- **The form's refusals are the door's refusals** (V/07.1 Н4): an empty name (the name is an ordinary field: Cyrillic and spaces are allowed, a match with another scenario is not a refusal); empty where needed, or more than four
  `when`/`then`, an unfilled required field, a value not of its type or outside the bounds of the schema `then`, `match` not an
  object,
  `when`, `then` larger than 4000 bytes of canonical JSON (P/06.1), `within` outside 0…3600, `rate_per_minute` outside 1…600 (0 — the default),
  `valid_for` outside 10…600 (0 — the default), — **and the refusal of the
  two fields of the evaluator** (V/07.3 Н1.1): two triggers or more without `within`, one trigger with `within`, `det scan` with
  `before` = `after` = 0 (an empty window). The page MUST NOT write what
  will be refused, and says so before the write.
- The write is `POST /auto/scenarios {name, …}` or `PUT /auto/scenarios/<id>` (the fields `enabled`, `when`, `then`, `within`,
  `rate_per_minute`, `valid_for`, `name` — if it changed; the rule fields that the form does not show are not written and stay); the removal is `DELETE` with a confirmation («поданные заявки не отменяются»).
- **The evaluator's words** — from the element of `status[]` (several elements — `worker_state: live`, no such one — the last): `refused` with `reason` — «вычислитель отказал: …»; `unfit` — «не годится: …»;
  `unchecked` — «не проверено» (V/07.3 Н1.2).
- **Firings over a day** — `/events?subsystem=auto&unit=auto/<id>` (V/07.4 Н1.6): the events `fired`, the last 10 — the time (`occurred`,
  no field — `t`), the number filed (`actions`), `late` — «срок прошёл, не подано».

### Н1.2 Domain

- The holder's view is `/domain` (through the module, `refresh`): the cameras and recordings of the members in the tree under the member, their cards; the camera
  of a single camera-cluster member stands in place of the member, its recordings — under it. The page judges by the number of units `cam` of the member with an empty `main` —
  the main rows, as V/11.3 Н1.3 and V/01.6 Н1.5: streams do not cancel the collapse (ADR-0110 addendum (23) item 3); there is no `source` in
  `Unit` (V/11.3 Н1.3 judges by the snapshot row).
- **There are no folders and no server groups on the page.** The domain tree goes by members. Domain folders and their editing belong to the module and to the domain door (P/07 Н1.5 `tree`, P/06.8 Н1.2а); the page neither reads nor writes them. In the camera card the field «Сервер» is the server of the unit, none — «—».
- **The recording assignment** (V/02.9 Н1.1; [P/08.6 Н1.1](../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.6-domain.norms.md)) — the page writes
  only it: `PUT /domain/placement/rec/<ref> {cluster, origin, copies}`; `origin` is the camera's cluster; the primary is `cluster`;
  a backup — add the cluster to `copies` (without `cluster`, without repeats, in ascending order); a backup without a primary is a refusal in words.
  There is the word `placement.if-match` (P/07 Н1.5) — `ifMatch`: the `rev` of the assignment read; 412 — words, the assignment is re-read.
- **The page MUST NOT create or remove recording rows through the domain:** this is the bundle `backend` (V/11.4 Н1.13), and the row `rec/recordings` (`cam: ref:<ref>`, `ref`; for a backup — a home on the backup
  volume, `when: offline`) is created by the operator of the recording cluster with the unit form of the module — the page names it to them. A move of the primary: the former recording
  stops and keeps the archive until its retention ends, its row is removed by the operator of its cluster — the page says so.
- The table «Назначения записи» is `GET /domain/placement` (`records` with the prefix `rec/`): the camera, the primary, the backups.

### Н1.3 Stream accounts

- The node «Учётки потоков» is in the rights section; the doors are `/live/accounts` (V/03.6 Н1.2) through the console: the list, create (`POST
  {name}`), a new password (`POST …/password`), enable and disable (`PUT {disabled}`), remove (`DELETE`, with a confirmation —
  «её гранты снимаются»). Writing — only with `admin`; the server checks by itself.
- **The password — once:** from the answer `{account: {name, disabled, at, by}, password}`; it lives only in the value of the output field —
  not in the markup, not in the browser storage, not in the list; it is forgotten by a button, by the selection of another object and by the card going away.
  An answer without a password — «шлюз не вернул пароль; запросите новый».

## Н3 Pass

```
scenario: draft → the door's refusals and the two fields' refusal before the write → POST/PUT → re-read → the words of status[] → firings over a day
domain: GET the assignments → PUT {cluster, origin, copies} → name the recording row to the cluster; do not touch the former one

```

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the scenario form is not by the schema, or the two fields | words before the write | the form | fix it |
| the door refused with 4xx | «Отказано: `<слова>`» | a pop-up | fix it |
| no answer or 5xx | «исход неизвестен», re-read (chapter 15 Н1.2) | a pop-up | by what was read |
| the assignment changed (412) | words, the assignment is re-read | a pop-up | decide anew |
| the evaluator refused, unfit | the words `reason`, `unfit` | the scenario card | fix the scenario or the camera |
| a backup without a primary | «сначала назначьте основную» | a pop-up | assign the primary |
| the assignment is not readable | «назначение недоступно» | a pop-up | later |
| the gateway did not return the password | words | the accounts card | a new password |


The refusal words of a module call — by chapter 16 Н4 (from the answer body, `message` — only for a network error).
