# V/06.4 — Watch everything, keep what is interesting; heartbeat: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/06.4-detedge.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 4 of lesson V/06 Device archive detection (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/06-detedge.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](06-detedge.README.md), manifest [`detedge.subsystem.yaml`](../detedge.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin).

The watch has already spent the network to look at the minutes; if the model liked something, these minutes can be kept without copying
the rest. The chapter sets norms for which segments around findings the watch asks to keep, how it files the catch-up recording request to the recorder,
the phases of the watch and its heartbeat. The execution of the request is [V/02.8](../../02-rec/spec/02.8-rec.norms.md) N1.4.

Norm ids are `V/06.4-MM`.

## N1 Form

**Time.** Media time of the device archive.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): writes — the requests `rec/requests/<rid>`
(`worker.requests: [rec]`, [P/03.6 N1.5](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)), the heartbeat; reads — the row
of the recording `rec/recordings/<rec>` (`worker.reads`; the retention `retention_days`).

### N1.1 Segments around findings

With `keep: hits` and a set `rec` (V/06.1 N1.2), after the frontier has credited the pieces of the pass (chapter 3 N1.2):

1. each finding of these pieces — a written model row (`detedge.overrun`, `detedge.rewound` are not findings; if the row is lost, there is
   no segment) — gives the segment `[occurred − pre, occurred + post)`;
2. the segments are sorted; neighbours with no more than `join` seconds between them are merged — the interval too;
3. the merged segments are intersected with the credited pieces of this pass: `pre` does not reach into a pause of the device recording, at the bound of the
   pass `pre` and `post` are cut.

The watch MUST NOT request anything but what is credited, up to the rounding of N1.2. `keep: hits` without `rec` does not take effect: the field `keep_off` of the heartbeat element is `keep: hits
needs rec`; the watch goes on watching. No target is not a refusal: `rec` is set, but there is no row `rec/recordings/<rec>` or there is a
tombstone — `keep_off` is `no recording <rec>`, no filings, the watch goes on watching (the "visible from" moment is not computed; a request filed
earlier will be closed by the reaper with the outcome `gone`, V/02.8 N1.4). The row exists but cannot be read in its own form, or the store did not
answer — this is not «no recording» (the class «unreadable — not absent», unreadable is not absent): no filings, `keep_off` is `recording <rec>
unreadable`, the watch goes on watching. `retention_days` is not a finite non-negative number — "visible from" = `now` (the fallback
outcome of V/02.5 N1.2): nothing is requested.

Filings come before the cursor: the frontier that credits the pieces MUST be written only when each filing of its segments has got an outcome
(chapter 3 N1.2 «Write order»).

### N1.2 Catch-up recording request

- Filing by the worker (P/03.6 N1.5; V/02.8 N1.4): the row `rec/requests/<rid>`, the body `{unit: <rec>, from, to}` (`unit` is the id of the recording),
  `rid` — by the key of the `rec` requests (`{unit}-{from:int}-{to:int}`; a repeat — the same row), the stamps `by: detedge/<watch>`, `at`,
  `about`; the body MUST be checked against the schema of the `rec` requests before filing; if it does not fit the schema, it is not filed (`filings.refused`). A `rid` not by the
  grammar and an unreadable row under the `rid` — the same, `refused`, the cursor does not stop at them (P/03.6 N1.5, C03-FILE-RID-REFUSED-01);
  a refusal of the filing log does not change the outcome `filed` (C03-FILE-JOURNAL-01).
- The bounds of a segment MUST be rounded to whole seconds before filing (`from` — down, `to` — up): the body and the `rid` are built from the same numbers,
  and a repeat of the same piece after a move gives `again`, not `refused` by `digest` (P/03.6 N1.5).
- The segment is trimmed from below to the "visible from" moment of the recording (`now − retention_days × 86400`, V/02.5 N1.2; "visible from" is rounded up to the
  second) and cut into pieces no longer than a day from the start of the segment (like the read, chapter 2 N1.4); an empty segment and a piece shorter than a
  second are not filed. With `start: earliest` and a backlog, part of the findings is already beyond the retention of the recording — it is not requested.
- The watch does not wait for the outcome of the catch-up recording: the recorder closes the request (`fetched`), and if there is no one to close it — the reaper (V/02.8 N1.4);
  what arrived is the stream `…/backfill` of the recording with its retention, there is no separate evidence archive. The minutes land within the visible coverage
  of `rec`: without it the request will end with `too old` (V/02.8 N1.4). The `per_person` accounting does not apply to filing by the
  worker: it is the accounting of people at the console door (P/06.4 N1.5), and filing by the worker goes past it.

### N1.3 Phases

The element of `status[]` of a watch is under the taken epoch (chapter 3); `reason` is words of the subsystem, `why` — of the platform:

| `phase` | When |
|---|---|
| `running` | the watch reads the archive (chapter 2 N1.4) |
| `caught_up` | the frontier is at the edge (`newest − SETTLE`), there is nothing to read (chapter 2 N1.2) — a healthy state: the watch has caught up with the archive |
| `waiting` | there is no holder or no archive, the holder does not answer, there is no free session (chapter 2; `reason`); a filing was not written, the cursor was not read (chapter 3; `reason`) |
| `pending` | `enabled: false`: the model is stopped, no reading |
| `unsupported` | the worker does not have the model's kind (V/04.2) |
| `failed` | the model or the mask does not start; the model crashed (chapter 3; the runtime backoff) |

The order is as in V/04.2 N3: `pending`, `unsupported`, the mask — `failed`, then chapter 2. `running` lasts from the first read to the end of the
pass.

### N1.4 Heartbeat and series

The form is [P/03.4 N1.1](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md); the platform puts in the capacity, the reserve,
`filings`. `detedge` adds: at the top — `events_written`, `events_refused` (counters of event rows since start; the pair
`events_written` — `events_refused`, the names of heartbeat extensions do not coincide with the platform ones — as in V/04); in the watch element —
`phase`, `reason`, `through` (the frontier in memory; there is no field until the cursor is written), `lag` = `max(0, newest − through)` (how far
the watch lags behind the edge of the archive; only when `newest` and `through` are known), `events` (rows of all kinds since the unit start; over the
life of the watch — `events` of the cursor), `overrun` (seconds erased before the frontier, chapter 3 N1.3; at zero there is no field, like `devices_slow`
V/01.2), `index_garbled` (garbled pieces of the listing, chapter 2 N1.3: since the unit start, a repeat of the window is counted again, zero is written),
`keep_off` (N1.1; only when `keep` does not take effect). No field means the value is unknown or does not apply; zero does not stand for
«unknown». Series — `watches_running` (the reading watches, `phase: running`, `console.running`: the manifest metric named by
`console.running` — by its declaration `from`, `agg`, `equals`, `live`, like any series of `metrics[]`, P/06.5 N3,
C06-CONSOLE-RUNNING-DEF-01), `filings_total` (manifest). In a cluster where all watches have caught up with the archive, `watches_running` is 0: this is
not idleness, the lag is shown by `lag`.

A growing `lag` and `overrun` mean the watch cannot keep up: thirty-two channels need 32× real time, and the GPU gives 4× — a watch
for such an archive goes on a substream and sparse frames.

## N3 Pass

```
after the credited pieces (chapter 3): keep: hits and rec → segments around findings → merge by join → ∩ what is credited in this pass
→ bounds — whole seconds (from down, to up) → trim to "visible from", pieces ≤ a day → filing rec/requests/<rid>
(by: detedge/<watch>) — before the cursor (chapter 3); no rec row — keep_off, no filings
heartbeat: events_written, events_refused; element — phase, reason, through, lag, events, overrun, index_garbled, keep_off
```

**Races.** Rounding outwards gave neighbouring passes a shared second — different `rid`s, the recorder writes the overlap once
(V/02.8 N1.3, N1.4). A move after the filing, before the cursor — the same piece: the same `rid` and body, outcome `again`, the row removed —
`filed`; a different cut — a different `rid`, the recorder discards the overlap.

## N4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `keep: hits` without `rec` | does not take effect, `keep_off` | heartbeat | set `rec` |
| there is no `rec` row (or a tombstone) | no filings, `keep_off` `no recording <rec>` | heartbeat | set a recording |
| the `rec` row cannot be read in its own form, the store did not answer | no filings, `keep_off` `recording <rec> unreadable` | heartbeat | fix the recording row |
| `retention_days` is not a finite non-negative number | "visible from" = `now`: nothing is requested | — | fix the recording row |
| a filing was not written | the cursor stays, the filing goes first in the next pass (chapter 3 N1.2) | heartbeat | the store |
| a finding is beyond the retention of the recording | not requested | — | — |
| the body does not fit the `rec` schema, the `rid` is not by the grammar, the row under the `rid` cannot be read | not filed, `filings.refused` | heartbeat | — |
| the recorder refused the request | the hole remains | the recorder's heartbeat (`requests_refused`) | — |
