# V/06.1 — The watch is a unit without an end: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`06.1-detedge.norms.md`](../06.1-detedge.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 1 of lesson V/06 «Детекция по архиву устройства» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../06-detedge.md),
[instructions](../06-detedge.README.md), manifest [`detedge.subsystem.yaml`](../../detedge.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin; the device archive is at the camera — `41d3ec8`).

A watch is one model watching the device archive of one camera, always. The chapter sets norms for the subsystem name after the transition, the
watch row, who creates it, placement and the snapshot. Reading — chapter 2; the frontier and events — chapter 3; saving hits and the heartbeat —
chapter 4.

Norm ids are `V/06.1-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the row `detedge/watches/<name>` (P/06.1),
the cursor `detedge/state/cursor/<watch>` (P/03.6 Н1.7), the snapshot `detedge/snapshot/*`; the chapter has no families of its own beyond them.

### Н1.1 Name

The subsystem `detedge` (ADR-0090 addendum (2), addendum (11)): the manifest `detedge.subsystem.yaml`, `name: detedge`,
the keys `detedge/…`, the slot `{prefix: e, name_env: DETEDGE_NAME}`, the program `/usr/lib/detedge/worker`, the role `detedgeworker`
(V/01.5 Н2), the series `detedge_*`. The detector family: `det` — the live stream (V/04), `detjob` — an interval of our archive (V/05),
`detedge` — the device archive.


**Rights.** `rights.routes: {edit: [watches]}`: creating requires `edit` on the camera, the `about` of the row; an edit and removal — `admin` on the unit, the gate contract `need`.

### Н1.2 Fields

`unit: {rows: watches, id: name}`; `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` — the events, grants and labels of a watch are those of the camera.

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, required | the name of the pair; the convention is `<cam>-<kind>` |
| `cam` | `string`, required, `fixed` | whose device archive to watch: the camera id (of the main stream; the device archive is at the camera, V/01.5 Н1.1а); the watch has no `stream` field of its own |
| `kind` | `string`, required | the model kind — the detector registry (V/04.2 Н1.2), including the teaching `tick` |
| `params` | `string` | model settings, opaque; the limit is the common row limit (413 `toolarge`, P/06.1 Н1.4) |
| `mask` | `blob` | the mask as a body (P/06.1 Н1.5) |
| `alarms` | `list`, default `[]` | which event kinds are alarms; hits in an old archive are observations, the operator sets an alarm explicitly |
| `start` | `now` \| `earliest`, default `now` | where the watch begins the first time: the edge of the device archive or its beginning (thirty days of backlog is a decision, not a default) |
| `keep` | `none` \| `hits`, default `none` | whether to save the minutes around hits into a recording (chapter 4) |
| `rec` | `string`, `ref: rec/recordings`, `must_match: {cam: cam}` | the recording of this camera where the saved minutes go; required with `keep: hits` (without it `keep` has no effect, the heartbeat field `keep_off`, chapter 4) |
| `pre`, `post` | `float`, default `5`, `schema: {minimum: 0, maximum: 600}` | seconds before and after a hit; not wider than the pass window (`SECONDS_PER_PASS`, chapter 2 Н1.2) |
| `join` | `float`, default `30`, `schema: {minimum: 0, maximum: 600}` | hits closer than this are one segment |
| `enabled` | `bool`, default `true` | |
| `labels` | `list`, default `["gpu"]` | the labels of the worker's server |

The row check, `fixed`, `ref`, `must_match`, enumerations — the [row check contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md).

### Н1.3 Who creates it

Only the operator — through the console units door (`POST /detedge/watches`, P/06.1). A watch has no orders (`worker.creates`): a watch is
the operator's decision, not a reaction to an event. The work does not end: there is no result, no `retire_when` and no `ends`; a watch is removed by switching it off
(`enabled: false`) or by deleting the row.

### Н1.4 Placement

| Key | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | `{from: capacity, default: 2}` | watches per worker — a budget of its own: a watch does not spend the streams of live detection |
| `headroom` | `{from: headroom}` | the headroom — the platform (P/03.4 Н1.1) |
| `constraint` | `labels-subset` | |
| `requires` | `resource` | events go into the resource of its own server |
| `near` | `{sub: cam, by: cam}` | a preference: next to the camera holder — the archive goes over the loopback, not over the network; `by: cam` — by the field, not by the watch name ([P/04 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md)) |
| `tie_break` | `most-free-capacity` | |

There are no `home`, `grows`, `retire_when`, `ends`. The snapshot is `snapshot: [name, cam, kind, enabled]` (P/04 Н1.3а): to the domain — «camera 7
is being traversed».

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| no `cam` or `kind` | 400 | the answer | — |
| `cam` changes | 400 `fixed`, `field: /cam` | the answer | a new watch |
| `start` or `keep` not from the enumeration | 400 | the answer | — |
| `pre`, `post`, `join` outside `[0, 600]` | 400 by the schema | the answer | — |
| `rec` is a recording of another camera | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match` | the answer | a recording of this camera |

## Н5 Acceptance

[Acceptance 1](../../acceptance/1/README.md): a watch with defaults, `start` and `keep` not from the enumeration — 400, `cam` is immutable, `rec` of another
camera — 409 `must_match`. Placement is the mechanism of P/04, by reading.
