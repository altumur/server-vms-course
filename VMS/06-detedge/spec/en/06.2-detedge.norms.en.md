# V/06.2 — Listing and reading the device archive: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`06.2-detedge.norms.md`](../06.2-detedge.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 2 of lesson V/06 «Детекция по архиву устройства» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../06-detedge.md),
[instructions](../06-detedge.README.md), manifest [`detedge.subsystem.yaml`](../../detedge.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the VMS pin).

The watch reads the device archive through the door of the camera holder: the listing — without a playback session, the bytes — with a session,
of which the device has few and which the operator and the recorder need. The chapter sets norms for where the watch takes the holder from, which window
it reads per pass, how it asks for the listing and how it reads while yielding. The holder's doors — [V/01.5](../../../01-cam/spec/01.5-cam.norms.md);
the frontier and the model — chapter 3.

Norm ids are `V/06.2-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Unix seconds of the media time of the device archive; deadlines — the monotonic clock of the process.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — the holders' heartbeats
`cam/heartbeats/*` (`worker.reads`; the base of the role does not give foreign heartbeats, P/02.5 Н1.4), the cursor (chapter 3).

### Н1.1 The holder and the archive edges

The camera holder is the element of `status[]` of the camera in a heartbeat `cam/heartbeats/*` heard within `LOST_AFTER` = 45 s, with `coverage`
(`from`, `to` — numbers), `index_url` and `playback_url` (V/01.5 Н1.1). There are several elements with `coverage` (a move) — the highest
`epoch` of the element; if equal, the first by worker name. `oldest` = `coverage.from`, `newest` = `coverage.to`. There is no such
element — `waiting`, `reason` `nobody holds this camera, or its device has no archive`; there are no edges: there is no `lag` in the heartbeat,
`through` stays (chapter 4 Н1.4). The camera element exists but is not readable (V/01.2 Н1.4): `coverage` is not by the schema of the family or
`from > to`; `index_url`, `playback_url` are missing or not a string; `<поле>` is the first one in this order. Such an element is not read, rather than
absent: the frontier stands, `waiting`, `reason` «`cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>`», `oldest`/`newest` are not reset
(the cursor — chapter 3); the next heartbeat — again.

### Н1.2 The pass window

The frontier `at` is the frontier in the holder's memory; at takeover — the cursor (chapter 3). The edge `edge` = `newest − SETTLE`, `SETTLE` = 30 s: the end of the archive is still being written. `at ≥ edge` —
phase `caught_up`, no reading. Otherwise the pass window is `[at, min(at + SECONDS_PER_PASS, edge))`, `SECONDS_PER_PASS` = 600 s
of media time.

### Н1.3 Listing

`GET <index_url>?from=&to=` over the window (V/01.5 Н1.3; the leaf of the role `detedgeworker`, V/01.5 Н2; the deadline — `DOOR_ASK_WAIT` = 5 s):
the listing does not spend a playback session.

| Answer | What is read |
|---|---|
| `{spans: […]}` | the pieces of the listing in the window; the pauses of the device recording between them — there is nothing to watch. The door does not promise order and non-overlap (V/01.5 Н1.3): before reading, the pieces are sorted by `from`, trimmed to the window, overlapping and adjacent ones are merged; they are read in order |
| `{spans: []}` | nothing: the device does not have these minutes |
| 501 `no index` or a body not in form (not an object, or `spans` is not an array, including a bare `[]`) | the whole window — optimistically (`None` ≠ `{spans: []}`: «cannot list» is not «empty»); the door serves the holes of the window without bytes (Н1.4) |
| a single piece does not parse (the element is not an object with finite `from`, `to`, `Span` OpenAPI) | skipped, the count `index_garbled` in the heartbeat element; the other pieces are read; for the frontier it is a pause (chapter 3 Н1.2). `to ≤ from` is empty after trimming, not counted |
| 404 `no device archive` | nothing; `waiting`, `reason` `the holder has no archive for this camera`; the frontier stands, the holder heartbeat is re-read: the camera has moved to another holder or is outside the licence (V/01.5 Н3 «Гонки», Н4) — this is not «there are no minutes» (`{spans: []}`) |
| 401 `no_role`, 403 `wrong_role`, `bad_chain` | nothing; `waiting`, `reason` `the holder's door refused the role: <слово>`; the frontier stands |
| another error (503, a break, a deadline) | nothing; `waiting`, `reason` `the holder is not answering`; the frontier stands |

### Н1.4 Reading while yielding

- **The door keeps the session reserve.** The last sessions of the device are for the operator and the recorder: the holder's door does not give the role
  `detedgeworker` the reserve `PLAYBACK_SPARE` = 1 (V/01.5 Н1.5) and answers 503 `device busy`, with «the last session is kept for the operator and the
  recorder» in `detail`. All cameras of a device are held by one holder (`group_by` by the host of the canon `cam`), so every session of the cluster to
  the device goes through its door; clients of the device that bypass the cluster are not visible to the door. Before reading, the watch looks at `GET /devices`
  of the holder (V/01.5 Н1.4): `playbacks ≥ max_playbacks − 1` — do not read, `waiting`, `reason` `the device has no spare
  session`; the numbers are not said — read. `/devices` is the same `<схема>://<хост>:<порт>` as `playback_url`, once per pass, the deadline
  `DOOR_ASK_WAIT`; the element is the one whose `known` contains the camera. A refusal, a break, a deadline, the camera is not in `known`, `max_playbacks` is not an integer —
  the numbers are not said. The check does not spend an opening attempt; the guarantee is at the door. `max_playbacks` = 1 — the watch
  never reads this device (`waiting` with these words). How many sessions a device has is said by the device, not by the lesson.
- **There are no pieces** (`{spans: []}`, all garbled) — no `/devices` and no reading, the frontier is the end of the window (chapter 3 Н1.2).
- **No more than one playback session** per device at a worker: watches of one device on a worker read in turn.
  The device of the camera is `device` of the `/devices` element; not found — there is no queue, the guarantee is at the door. A watch in the queue is `running`.
- **Reading.** The budget unit is one read: `GET <playback_url>?from=&to=` not longer than `READ_SECONDS` = 60 s of media
  time. A piece of the listing is cut into such reads from its beginning; `PLAYBACK_PIECE` is the door's portion inside the answer (V/01.5
  Н1.5), it is not visible to the client. Per pass — no more than `STRETCHES_PER_PASS` = 4 reads per watch, that is up to 240 s of media:
  the budget limits the time until the cursor is written and the share of the device. Reading and the model are not on the thread of the runtime loop (as in V/01.4
  Н1.3): the heartbeat and the lease do not wait for the reading. The read deadline is 30 s of idleness. One watch pass at a time; the next one — not earlier than
  the next turn of the loop after the end of the previous one (P/03.3 Н1.2). The first refusal ends the reads of the pass.
- **Any 503** of the door (the sessions have run out, the reserve `PLAYBACK_SPARE`, `device busy`, `device opening`, the door budget) is neither a refusal nor
  a retry within the pass: `waiting`, `reason` — the door's `detail`, if there is none — `error`; the next pass tries again.
- **404 `no device archive`** of a read — the camera has moved to another holder, is outside the licence, or the window was erased before trimming
  (V/01.5 Н3) — `waiting`, `reason` `the holder has no archive for this camera`, the frontier stands, the next pass
  re-reads the heartbeat and the listing (the erased part — chapter 3 Н1.3). 401, 403 — as with the listing (Н1.3).
- **Another error** of a read (an answer without the last chunked piece — a break, the network, a deadline, the handshake, 400, 405, 413) — `waiting`,
  `reason` `the holder's door failed: …`. `…` is the `error` of the body, if there is none — the code or the text of the client error; the contract is the words before
  the colon.
- **200 without bytes** (the answer ended cleanly) — the read is whole: there are no minutes, for the frontier this is a pause (chapter 3 Н1.2; erased by the ring — Н1.3), the model gets no frames.
- To the model — the frames of the piece in media time (chapter 3): the model receives a read whole, a broken read is not fed to it and gives no
  rows; what has been read whole is decided by the frontier (chapter 3 Н1.2).

**The cause of `waiting`** is the first met condition from the top (as in V/04.2 «Нет источника»); the words are a contract:

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| filing the segment did not get written (chapter 3 Н1.2) | `a request to keep minutes was not filed` |
| the cursor is not read: the store did not answer (chapter 3 Н1.1) | `cursor unreadable` |
| there is no camera element with `coverage` in a fresh heartbeat (Н1.1) | `nobody holds this camera, or its device has no archive` |
| the camera element is not readable (Н1.1) | `cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>` |
| the listing or a read — 404 `no device archive` (Н1.3, Н1.4) | `the holder has no archive for this camera` |
| the listing or a read — 401, 403 of the door (Н1.3, Н1.4) | `the holder's door refused the role: <слово>` |
| the listing — another error (Н1.3) | `the holder is not answering` |
| `playbacks ≥ max_playbacks − 1` by `/devices` (Н1.4) | `the device has no spare session` |
| a 503 of the door on a read (Н1.4) | the door's `detail`, if there is none — `error` |
| another read error (Н1.4) | `the holder's door failed: …` |

## Н2 Doors

The chapter has no door of its own: `/devices`, `/recordings`, `/playback` are the holder's door ([OpenAPI V/01](../../../01-cam/spec/01-cam.api.openapi.yaml)),
class `mixed`, leaf `detedgeworker`.

## Н3 Pass

```
1. The holder by the heartbeat (45 s, coverage, index_url, playback_url) → none: waiting.
2. at = the frontier in memory (at takeover — the cursor); edge = newest − 30; at ≥ edge → caught_up.
3. The window [at, min(at + 600, edge)) → the listing (5 s): spans (sort, trim, merge) | {spans: []} | None (the whole window) | an error → waiting.
4. There are no pieces → without /devices and reading, the frontier is the end of the window.
5. /devices (once per pass): playbacks ≥ max − 1 → waiting; otherwise up to 4 reads of /playback of 60 s of media each from the beginning of the pieces, one session per device.
6. The first refusal ends the reads: 503 → waiting (the door's detail, if there is none — error); another error → waiting.
7. What was read → the model (chapter 3); 200 without bytes → a pause.
```

**Races.** The operator opened a playback between `/devices` and `/playback` — the door answers 503, the watch waits for the next pass.
The device ring erased the window between the listing and the read — `/playback` serves what is trimmed by the coverage, the answer is whole; the frontier is checked against what was served, the erased part goes into `overrun` (chapter 3 Н1.3).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| there is no holder, there is no archive | `waiting`, `reason` | the heartbeat | — |
| the camera element in the holder heartbeat is not readable | `waiting`, the frontier stands, `reason` «`cam heartbeat unreadable: <поле>`» | the heartbeat | — |
| cannot list (501) | the whole window, optimistically, the holes — 200 without bytes | — | — |
| the holder does not answer | `waiting`, the frontier stands | the heartbeat | — |
| there is no free session, 503 (including the door's reserve `PLAYBACK_SPARE`) | `waiting` until the next pass | the heartbeat | the operator and the recorder go first |
| `max_playbacks` = 1 | the watch does not read the device, `waiting` | the heartbeat | a device with two sessions, or no watch |
| a read break | `waiting`, `reason` | the heartbeat | — |
| there is no role kit | 401 `no_role` of the door, `waiting` | the heartbeat, the log | the kit `detedgeworker` |
| the role of the leaf is wrong, the signature is outside the list | 403 `wrong_role`, `bad_chain` of the door, `waiting` | the heartbeat, the log | the kit `detedgeworker` |
| the holder has no archive of the camera | 404 `no device archive`, `waiting`, the frontier stands | the heartbeat | re-read the heartbeat (the camera is at another holder) |

## Н5 Acceptance

The chapter has no acceptance directory: the stand has no double of a device with an archive and no TLS stand of VMS (V/01.5 Н5) — by reading and by a test
of the implementation.

## Implementation decides

The implementation MUST name in its description: **the client of the holder's door** (TLS with the role leaf, reads of `/playback`, the queue of
watches of one device, the read thread outside the runtime loop). Cases for the implementation test: listing spans in reverse order,
an overlap, a refusal of a middle read: the later ones are not read.
