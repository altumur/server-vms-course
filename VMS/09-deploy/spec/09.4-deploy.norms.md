`console` of the manifests direct ([P/06.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.5-console.norms.md) N1.2, N3 `/metrics`). The chapter names which
series to read in order to say «VMS is running» and «the archive is growing».

## N1 Form

### N1.1 Whether the subsystem is running

`/<sub>/metrics` of the console gives the series of the subsystem, `/metrics` gives all of them and the platform series `w2c_*` (P/06.5 N2; the short path is chosen by
`CONSOLE_ROOT` — `cam`, for another subsystem — `/<sub>/…`). The series `<sub>_<console.running>` is the manifest metric that
`console.running` names, computed by its declaration (P/06.5 N3, C06-CONSOLE-RUNNING-DEF-01; the addition by Kimi to the
analysis of the V/09 review, question 2): all nine have `equals: running`, `live: true`; next to it is `<sub>_workers_live`.

| Subsystem | The «running» series | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `cam` | `cam_cameras_running` | cameras that the holder holds and delivers |
| `rec` | `rec_recordings_running` | recordings whose pipeline is writing |
| `live` | `live_streams_live` | deliveries with a subscription set up |
| `det` | `det_units_running` | detectors that are watching the stream |
| `detjob` | `detjob_jobs_running` | scans that are being decoded now |
| `detedge` | `detedge_watches_running` | watches that are reading the device archive |
| `auto` | `auto_scenarios_running` | scenarios that the evaluator follows |
| `assist` | `assist_models_running` | models whose process is loaded and accepts steps |
| `backend` — with the V/11 generation | `backend_instances_running` | instances that hold the row and answer the pass |

**The criterion is a failure, not a difference of numbers.** In a healthy cluster the «running» series is less than what is configured, in the normal phases
of the owners. A subsystem is **not healthy** when at least one of these holds: (a) `/<sub>/unplaceable` is not empty (P/04); (b) there is
a unit in a phase of the group «Failure» of the table below, or with the platform backoff `stalled`, the platform phase `sealed`, `unreadable` (P/03.3, P/03.4
N1.1); (c) `<sub>_workers_live` = 0 while there are units to place: a live `configured` row, not `enabled: false`, not removed by
`retire_when`, not past its end. The «running» series and `configured` are diagnostics: where to look.

| Subsystem | Running | Normally stopped | Failure | Owner's phases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cam` | `running`, `idle` (a stream without a consumer, V/01.2 N1.2c) | `pending` (disabled, waiting for start; the cause is `device_state`, `reason`), `held` (`live: on-demand`, the row is not `camera`) | `failed`, `unlicensed` | V/01.2 N1.4 |
| `rec` | `running` | `pending`, `standby` (a backup `offline`), `waiting` | `failed` | V/02.4 N1.8 |
| `live` | `running` | `waiting` (without a viewer or a source) | — | V/03.5 N1.3, N1.4 |
| `det` | `running` | `pending`, `waiting` | `failed`, `unsupported` | V/04.2 N1.3 |
| `detjob` | `running` | `queued`, `following`, `fetching`, `waiting` | `failed`, `unsupported` | V/05.4 N1.1 |
| `detedge` | `running` | `caught_up` (healthy), `pending`, `waiting` | `failed`, `unsupported` | V/06.4 N1.3 |
| `auto` | `running` | `pending` (disabled) | `refused`, `failed` | V/07.4 N1.7 |
| `assist` | `running` | `loading` (the model is loading), `stopped` (`enabled: false`) | `failed` | V/10.1 N1.3, N1.4 |
| `backend` — with the V/11 generation | `running` | `pending` (`enabled: false`) | — | V/11.1 N1.1 |

`waiting` is «normally stopped» with a `reason`: it is a consequence, the cause is visible at the addressee (the camera holder, the recorder, the door).

### N1.2 Whether the archive is growing

There is nothing to look at in the archive files: the video is in the volumes, behind the engine. «Growing» is read from the series of the recorder (V/02.4 N1.8):

- `rec_last_frame_age_seconds` — how many seconds ago the recording last got a frame from the source — a diagnostic, not
  a criterion: the silence of the source is visible higher up the chain (the camera is not `running`, `device_state`, `reason` of the holder, V/01.2 N1.4);
- `rec_writer{state}` — whether what was given to the writer reaches the ring: `ok`, `stalled`, `losing`;
- `rec_archive_failure{kind}`, `rec_archive_away_seconds` — the volume does not take frames, why and for how long;
- `rec_volume_error` — the volume is not taken; the words are kept until the recorder opens the volume.

The archive is growing: `rec_recordings_running` equals the number of recordings that must be writing (enabled, `until` has not passed, a backup
`offline` in `standby` is not counted — V/02.4 N1.8, V/02.9 «`have`»; the count is by `configured` of `GET /rec/recordings`),
`rec_writer{state="ok"}` at the live recorders that hold a volume, `rec_volume_error` = 0, there is no `rec_archive_failure`. This is the second
question, not a criterion of the subsystem: a recording in `waiting` does not grow the archive, and `rec` is healthy by N1.1. A recording of a camera with `live: on-demand` gets no stream (V/02.4) and in this equality
remains a shortfall: set the camera to `always`, or disable the recording. The series about recording are now declared with `live: true` (V/02.4 N1.8): the heartbeat of a recorder
that has left does not stay in them.

## N3 Pass

```
/<sub>/unplaceable is empty; no units in the «Failure» phases (table of N1.1), stalled, sealed, unreadable; <sub>_workers_live > 0 while there are units to place
the archive is growing: recordings_running = must be writing, writer ok at the live ones that hold a volume, volume_error 0, no archive_failure; last_frame_age is a diagnostic
```

## N4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/<sub>/unplaceable` is not empty | not healthy | `/<sub>/unplaceable` | resources, labels, volumes (P/04) |
| a unit in a «Failure» phase, `stalled`, `sealed`, `unreadable` | not healthy | `/<sub>/<rows>` `rows`, `reason` | the lesson that owns the phase (table of N1.1) |
| `<sub>_workers_live` = 0 while there are units to place | not healthy | `/<sub>/metrics` | the worker units (V/09.1) |
| the «running» series is less than what is configured | a hint, not a refusal | `/<sub>/metrics` | look at the phases (normally stopped is the norm) |
| `rec_writer{state}` is not `ok` | the archive is not growing | `/rec/metrics` | the engine and the volume (V/02.4) |
| the frame age of a recording that must be writing is greater than the pause of its source | a hint | `/rec/metrics`, the recording status | look at the camera holder (V/01) |
