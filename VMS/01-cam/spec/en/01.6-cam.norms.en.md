# V/01.6 — The camera on the domain: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`01.6-cam.norms.md`](../01.6-cam.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 6 of lesson V/01 «Камера» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../01-cam.md),
[instructions](../01-cam.README.md), manifest [`cam.subsystem.yaml`](../../cam.subsystem.yaml).
The platform — the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the stream in the domain view, `view.main` — `b41527f`).

The domain knows a subsystem only by the `domain:` section of its manifest ([P/02.1 Н1.2б](../../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md)).
The chapter sets the norms of the contribution of `cam`: what a camera is called across the whole domain, what the domain sees of it and edits, which book its
holder writes, which tokens `cam` declares, how a camera pushes its stream into the receiver of the recording cluster, what `cam`
adds to a cluster of one, and how the holder orders recording to the card of its server. VMS has no separate domain process (ADR-0074): the contribution is written by the ordinary holder
`camworker`, the books are assembled and carried by the platform (P/03.7, P/08.5, P/09.1); `cam` has a worker on the domain `camdomain` only
for the sake of the carried book `licence` (chapter 7). The receiver side — the books `primaries`, `ingest`, `polls`,
`upstream`, catch-up recording, the reserve, the witness — is lesson V/02; scenarios between cameras — V/07; RTSP accounts — V/03 (addendum (4) item 5).

Norm ids are `V/01.6-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `as_of` — Unix seconds (a JSON number); `t` of a frame — the capture time by the camera clock (Н1.4).

**Data** (the common families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv); the chapter has no keys of its own beyond
them; the file of the token `stream` is not a key of the store, Н1.3): the holder writes — the contribution `cam/sources/<слот>` (P/03.7 Н1.2), the order row `rec/recordings/cam-<cam>-<том>` (Н1.7); it reads — `rec/volumes/*` (Н1.7), the sections of the books `domain/rec/primaries/*/<раздел>` and `domain/rec/ingest/*/<раздел>` by its own request (V/02.9 Н1.6), the keys are the same at the holder and at the copy,
`domain/placed/rec/<ref>`, `domain/rec/polls` (copies brought by the agent, P/09.1; at the cluster that is the domain holder there are no
copies, there the row of its own cluster `domain/rec/polls/<CLUSTER>` is read — the rights to it are derived for the
worker by the rights generator from the `CLUSTER` of the installer, P/02.5 Н1.4, C02-OWN-BOOK-HOLDER-01; `worker.reads` of the manifest
names only the copy; the assignment at the holder is the record `domain/placement/rec/<ref>`, P/08.6 Н1.1 `readers`, the rights are given by `worker.reads` `domain/placed/rec/`), the rows `domain/member-list/<раздел>`, `domain/view`, `domain/keys`, the topology (Н1.6) and the licence decision (chapter 7 Н1.3); the heartbeat — the field `taken_by` (Н1.4). The carried book
`domain/cam/licence/<член>` is written by the worker on the domain (chapter 7 Н1.2).

### Н1.2 Contribution to the sources book

The holder writes the row `cam/sources/<свой слот>` (P/03.7 Н1.2: an element is an object with `key` and `to`; the rights are the role base,
a new holder of the slot clears the rows under it): one element per camera of its assignment, the element name is the camera `id`,

- **the camera is placed** with `origin` = its own cluster (the record `domain/placed/rec/<ref>`, at the domain holder — `domain/placement/rec/<ref>`, P/09.1 Н1.5, P/08.6 Н1.1) —
  `{ref, to, live_url?, codec?, server?, as_of, push?}`: `to` — `cluster` + `copies` of the assignment; `live_url` — the RTSP of the delivery
  (V/01.2 Н1.3), while it exists; `server` — the server of the holder (`SERVER_NAME`; not set — no field); `push: true` — the camera
  pushes the stream itself, whatever happens: a camera-cluster (`source: cfw://local`, Н1.5) — always, a server camera — no field
  (the recorder fetches it); into the book `domain/rec/primaries` (`push`, V/02.9) it is carried over by the recorder, the pusher takes
  it from there (Н1.4, Н1.6);
- **nobody records the camera** (there is no assignment `domain/placed/rec/<ref>`, at the domain holder — `domain/placement/rec/<ref>`), but it must be polled — the camera is `enabled`, with
  `ref`, pushes itself (`push`, a camera-cluster): its stream is available to receivers only through the poll (Н1.4, V/02.9 Н1.7) —
  `{ref, to: [дом опроса] (+ центр, если дом ≠ центр), polls: true, server?, as_of}` (`to`: the poll home, plus the centre if the home ≠ the centre); the home — by the copy of the topology, at the domain holder — by `domain/topology` (P/08.7
  Н1.1 `poll_home`); a server camera without an assignment gets no element (its delivery is the RTSP of the holder in its own cluster);
- an `unlicensed` camera (chapter 7 Н1.3) gets no element;
- the record `domain/placed/rec/<ref>` or the topology does not read, the store did not answer — unread, not absent:
  the previous element is kept, log.

The fields «empty, false, none» are absent: a field without a value (`live_url`, `server`, `codec`, `push`) is omitted, `""` and `null`
do not occur (the element form is a data family, class VMS). `as_of` changes only together with the content of the element: the row
lies on a medium (at a camera-cluster — on flash, Н1.5), a rewrite without a change is wear. The decision «fetch or
push», catch-up recording and reachability belong to the receiving recorder (V/02).

`pick: unique` — one contribution per `ref` in the whole domain: two — the key is not in the book, it is in `view.books.conflicts` (P/08.5 Н1.3);
one camera with one `ref` in two clusters — separate them (`ref` is unique within a cluster, not within the domain). An input out of form
removes the whole contribution of the cluster (`bad_contribution`) — the holder writes elements only by the form above.

### Н1.3 Tokens

The form of a kind, the issuance, the verification at the recipient — the platform ([P/08.7](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.7-domain.norms.md) Н1.4,
Н3; [P/03.7 Н1.5](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md)); a token carries no rights (ADR-0031); it lives only in the memory
of the requester, except the kind `stream` (`keep: true`, below); it is requested through the local door of the agent (the contract of the local tokens door; in a camera-cluster — a call).

| Kind | Declaration | Meaning | Who requests |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stream` | `lifetime: 315360000` (10 years), `claims: [aud, ref]`, `grant: [camworker, recworker]`, `placement: {sub: rec, caller: origin, ref_claim: ref, target_fields: [cluster, copies, star_centre], audience_claim: aud}` | the key of the camera stream into the receivers of the recording cluster and of the reserves (+ the star centre); only the cluster of the camera (`origin`) requests it | the pusher: the holder of a camera-cluster or the recorder of the cluster of a server camera |
| `poll` | `lifetime: 86400`, `claims: [aud, ref]`, `grant: [camworker, recworker]`, `unit: {ref_claim: ref, target_fields: [poll_home], audience_claim: aud}` | a long poll of a camera that nobody records, at the receiver of the poll home (the addresses — `domain/rec/polls`) | the same |
| `ask` | `lifetime: 86400`, `claims: [aud, ref, by, acts, up]`, `grant: [autoworker, recworker]`, `scenario: {doc: auto, …, placement: rec, …}` | a scenario request to a target camera; the fields of the scenarios document — lesson V/07 | the `auto` executor (V/07), the relaying recorder; the target camera does not request |

The pusher requests the token again before `exp` and on a change of the assignment (`domain/placed/rec/<ref>`): the old token does not name the
new recording cluster — the receiver will answer `wrong_audience`. The holder is unreachable longer than the `lifetime` of the token `poll` — the token
cannot be extended: a camera without recording stops polling.

**The token `stream` for recording** (ADR-0110 addendum (9)). A silent domain does not stop recording, therefore the term is 10 years; the form of the kind
is the same, `lifetime` is mandatory.

- **Bound.** `aud` and `ref`: the stream of this camera into the clusters of the assignment; the token opens no other camera and no other cluster.
- **Where it lies.** The kind `stream` is declared `keep: true`, the owner of the file is the kind: the file is kept by the worker that requests the token, the camera holder and the recorder, in its state directory, on a box this is flash, by P/03.7 Н1.5. The keyring is the file `SECRETS_KEY`, the workers `cam`, `rec` are its holders: they have secret fields. The token MUST NOT go into a row, a book, a report or a log.
- **Writing the file.** The file is replaced only after the first call accepted with the new token; until then both are in memory: otherwise a reboot with a receiver lacking the new set would leave a single invalid one.
- **Loading.** The file opened and `aud` contains the `cluster` of the assignment copy — the pusher takes the token without a request, the term is judged by the receiver, not by the camera clock: an expired file is replaced after the refusal `expired`; the domain is not needed, the assignment and the sections of the books `primaries`, `ingest` lie on flash as copies. **Limit:** a camera-cluster gets a book section once per `every` of its report, up to 1250 s: the receiver addresses on flash can be older by as much, after a change of the recorder of the assignment the camera pushes into a dead address, going through the list cures this only if a live address is in the old list. The section `ingest` changes only with a change of the set of recorders of the assignment cluster, therefore flash is rewritten rarely; the head of `ingest` is domain-wide, `rev` grows with the life of any server of the domain, the camera writes it to flash no more often than its report. At a star member all recorders of the member are in `stale` — the elements of its cluster are discarded, the camera does not push into the live centre either: the centre without the member does not record its camera. No file, the envelope did not open, the cluster is not in `aud` — there is no token: a request; the domain is silent — do not push, the card records, log. **Limit:** `w2cctl secrets seal` does not go over the files, and `rotate` does not remove the previous `kid`, therefore a change of the keyring does not touch the file; the keyring is lost or replaced — the envelope did not open, with a silent domain pushing stands still.
- **Requests again**, only with a live domain: a change of the assignment; the `kid` of the token header is not `current` of its `domain/keys`; before `exp`; the receiver refused `expired`, `revoked`, `wrong_audience`; after the refusal `revoked` the request carries the word `revoked: "<jti>"` — the `jti` of the refused token, from its `payload`: equal to the `jti` of the row — the previous one goes into `domain/revoked`, a new one is issued; otherwise the request is ordinary, so a second executor does not extinguish the first (P/03.7 Н1.5, P/08.7 Н1.3; ADR-0110 addendum (14) item 1). The word is executed no more often than `REVOKED_WORDS` per day, 4 for a camera, and not when `domain/revoked` is 80 % full: beyond that the previous `jti` is issued. An answer with the same `jti` — by P/03.7 Н3 «Гонки» (ADR-0110 addendum (23) item 2); at the camera the previous token is kept, the receiver refuses, recording goes to the card (ADR-0110 addendum (15)). It holds a new token with `current` — it does not request; `unknown_key` on a token with `current` — it waits: the receiver has not taken the set, a new issuance will not help. The request failed — the previous token is kept.
- **Revocation.** The domain remembers the target: `domain/issued/cam/stream/<sha16(ref)>`, only for `keep` (P/08.2 Н1.6а); the issuance takes the `jti` from this row, therefore the `jti` of a target is constant and a revocation closes all its tokens with this `jti`, the limit is below. The order: first remove the assignment, otherwise the member at once gets a new token; then `DELETE /domain/issued/cam/stream/<ref>`, by the `admin` of the domain: the `jti` with the greatest `exp` goes into `domain/revoked`, the row is removed. The entry stays for the whole term of the token and reaches the receiver with a live domain.
- **Theft limit.** Theft of a camera with the keyring gives recording of the stream of this camera into the clusters of `aud` until the token is revoked. A change of the assignment does not revoke the token: at the previous cluster it is valid until `exp`. Live revocations are about 18 700 per domain: the row `domain/revoked` is full — `DELETE /domain/issued/cam/stream/<ref>` answers 409 `too many revocations`, nothing is written, the target row remains, the token is not revoked; the issuance to the camera does not refuse meanwhile, the word `revoked` beyond 80 % is not executed; log from 80 % (P/08.2 Н1.6а; ADR-0110 addendum (18), (19) item 1). Loss of the target row: a copy older than the first issuance, a transfer "from nothing", an unreadable row — a new issuance gets a new `jti`, the old token is alive, `DELETE` does not reach it, only a change after theft closes it. **Limit** (ADR-0110 addendum (11) item 4).

A change of the tokens key arrives only through the domain; `OLD_KEYS` = 3600 s ([P/08.2 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.2-domain.norms.md)):

| Case | Previous key | Pusher |
|---|---|---|
| planned change, the camera is connected | valid for `keep` until `keep_until` | requests a token by the new set; a receiver without the set — `unknown_key`, the previous token goes |
| planned change, the camera did not take the set within `OLD_KEYS`: no connection to the holder, the domain fell silent | valid for `keep` until `keep_until` (P/08.2 Н1.6) | the previous token goes; the domain returns — it requests |
| a receiver without connection to the domain | valid: its set is the previous one | the previous token goes; a new one — `unknown_key` until delivery |
| a transfer of the holder without `stolen` (P/08.8 Н1.8 step 7) | valid for `keep` until `keep_until`, as with a planned change | the previous token goes; the domain returns — it requests from the new holder |
| a change after theft, a transfer with `stolen` (P/08.2 Н3) | no window: `unknown_key` as soon as the receiver has taken the set | all tokens of the domain at once; it does not push until a new token, the domain is silent — until its return; the card records. **Limit** |
| no domain | no change | goes until `exp` |

There are no more than 8 live `keep_until`: a camera that missed nine changes gets `unknown_key` and stops until a new request, the domain is silent — the card records (ADR-0110 addendum (10) item 2). **Limit.**

### Н1.4 The stream pusher

A camera that pushes itself (`push`, a camera-cluster) holds **one long poll** to the receiver of the recording cluster and
pushes the stream while somebody needs it; the poll protocol and the reception belong to the receiver (V/02). The camera side:

- **when:** the wish comes as an answer of the poll — to a recorder always or for the window of an event, to a viewer — while they watch and another
  `LINGER` = 10 s;
- **where:** the role and `push` — the record of the camera in the section of its `ref` of the book `domain/rec/primaries/*` (the primary one — by the `cluster`
  of the assignment); the receiver addresses — the elements of the key of the assignment cluster in the book `domain/rec/ingest/*`, by server name, without `<кластер>/<сервер>` from `stale` of the head; the list is truncated — a server outside it is tried (V/02.9 Н1.6; ADR-0113 addendum (29), (30)); the requests of both sections are put by the holder at start and once per `BOOK_WANT_EVERY`; going through in order, none answered — do not push; to the reserve (the second server) — only if none
  of the receivers of the primary answered or its receiver said `uncovered`; the key is the token `stream` (a camera without recording — `poll` on
  the `urls` from `domain/rec/polls`); the element of the camera in the book `primaries`, `ingest` or `polls` out of form (the V/02 family `primaries-entry`,
  `ingest-entry`, `polls-entry`) — unread, not absent: the pusher keeps the receivers, `push` and `urls` of the last read
  element; there is none read since start, the section has not come yet — it does not push and does not poll, it waits, log, the card records;
- **frame time:** a frame carries the capture time `t` by the camera clock; one time line is set before any reader and
  survives the process; before NTP the camera refuses a claim earlier than this point (`adrift`); boot frames without set
  clocks do not go into the answer (`unplaced`); the conversion to the cluster clock — the receiver;
- **pre-recording:** while the stream is not needed, the camera keeps the last `ring_seconds` = 60 s — as much as the ring of 32 MiB holds (Н1.5), no more; the first send after «push» starts
  with them, marked; a viewer gets only the live edge;
- **audio** (ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 7): the camera sends frames of the kind `audio` (V/02.9 Н1.3а, Н2) of the numbers from `audio` of the poll answer next to the video, `t` — of the same line; the ring and the card hold both kinds, pre-recording, continuation by `have` and the export of a claim carry audio of the same times; `have`, `hold_seconds`, `UPLINK_FREE` and the cut are judged by video: audio does not hold the window; **limit:** a camera-cluster has no rows of children (V/01.4 Н3), there is nothing to switch its `mic` off with;
- **archive claim:** in the poll answer `{rid, from, to, recording, piece, audio?}` — the camera reads the card and gives it in pieces:
  `PIECE_BYTES` = 1 MiB — the body of the call «выгрузка» (export) whole, with the base64 of the frames (the door limit `BODY`, V/02.9 Н2), with
  a number `seq` and the mark `more`; an empty export without `more` — the answer is whole; an unacknowledged piece goes again under its number; no card or the segment is not read to the end — a refusal, not emptiness; a piece of a claim goes only
  when the stream is at the live edge (`UPLINK_FREE` = 2 s);
- **a break continues:** the poll answer carries `have` — up to where it is recorded; the camera starts with the first key frame after
  `have`: from the ring, then from the card, then live; the state moves only after a piece that the receiver took;
  the continuation goes ahead of the live edge, back no further than `hold_seconds` = 30 s, no more than two pieces per pass;
  a lag greater than `2 × hold_seconds` — a cut to a key frame with a count;
- **debt to the card:** the pusher remembers what the receiver took and what the claim brought, and tells the camera card what the server lacks
  (`owed_spans`); how the card keeps the debt — V/02;
- **`taken_by`** in the element `status[]` of the camera (V/01.2 Н1.4) — the list of names of the clusters that take its stream now;
  the reader reads the list (otherwise «unknown»);
- **a scenario request** comes as an answer of the poll and is executed as a request of `cam` (V/01.4): `performed` or `refused` with
  words;
- **three channels** — all are opened by the camera: the agent pass (seconds to half a minute), the long poll (about a second), the stream
  (continuous).

### Н1.5 The camera as a cluster of one

A cluster of one is a box (a camera) with all processes, the controller and the agent in-process
([P/02.4 Н1.10, Н1.11](../../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md), [P/09.1 Н1.11](../../../../Platform/09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md));
`cam` adds:
- the row `cam/cameras/1` with `source: cfw://local` (the firmware of the camera itself, V/01.1 Н1.1), `ref` and `name` — the serial number,
  deliberately: this is its name for the domain (the rule "the serial number does not leave the cluster" is only about the `identity` of a row of
  `devices`, V/01.1); the firmware puts it into `/etc/w2c/boot-rows.json`, the camera console at start creates it if absent, the stamp `by: boot`, the row exists — it does not touch it; the file out of form — code 2, the reading failed — the start goes on, log, the row at the next boot (P/02.4 Н1.10 `boot`);
- **in the domain** (ADR-0110 addendum (3), (4) item 2): the mark of a w2c camera is the `source` of the row equal to `cfw://local` byte for byte; the field goes into `domain.view_tables` (Н1.1). A member whose only one of the main rows of `cam/cameras` (an empty `main`) is this one is collapsed: a stream of the same camera does not make it a server; there is no server, the camera is a `Device` record, `serverId` — the server of the receiver, no assignment — no field, the group «Без сервера» (No server) (V/11.3 Н1.3). The firmware writes no other rows: an ordinary camera row shows the member as a server;
- **`backend`** — the row `backend/instances/main`, the fields `{name: main}`, in the same `boot-rows.json` (P/02.4 `boot`; ADR-0110 addendum (4) item 3): the WebUI of the camera lives without an operator; the limits and the budget — V/11.1 Н1.1.
- **the card** — a row of the volume `edge` in the same `boot-rows.json` (V/02.6 Н1.7): a collapsed member has no server on which an operator would create the volume; the volume is shown as a `storage` record (V/11.3 Н1.3);
- the holder is in the camera process; its heartbeat — the words of V/01.2, V/01.5 and `taken_by`; the epoch — once per boot (P/03.2 Н1.5);
- the frame memory — 40 MiB per camera: the ring of 32 MiB (a window of 60 s, video and audio) and two pieces of 1 MiB — the camera; the queue to the card of 6 MiB — the card
  (lesson V/02); the pusher keeps no frames of its own — it reads the ring;
- **several cameras:** besides the row `cam/cameras/1` a cluster of one keeps ordinary camera rows of its server: a holder per camera, an epoch and a heartbeat — for each, the volume `edge` is one (Н1.7, V/02.6 Н1.7). The camera `cfw://local` on a box is one; for the others the stream and the answer to a range claim are run by the recorder of the card (V/02.9 Н1.2, V/02.6 Н1.7): it reads the segments itself and exports them by the camera protocol. **Limit:** the frame memory grows with the number of cameras, the card budget does not;
- the row, the epoch, what the agent brought and the token `stream` (Н1.3) — flash; the heartbeat and the snapshot shard — memory (P/02.4 Н1.11): the holder writes the contribution
  row only on change (Н1.2);
- the camera doors before the first publication of the shard answer 503 `not_ready`; the copy of `domain/grants` — in memory, to flash — on a
  change of the content and no more often than once per hour (the decision on the request

### Н1.7 Recording to the card of the server

The decision is ADR-0108 and its addendum (1). The volume `edge` works with all cameras of its server (V/02.6 Н1.7). Without a recording row a camera has no unit, epoch, heartbeat with `coverage`, therefore the row is ordered by the holder: `worker.creates: [{sub: rec, rows: recordings, key: "{cam}-{home}"}]` — `rec/recordings/cam-<cam>-<том>` (P/03.6 Н1.6): one row per card, a camera that changed server may have two.

- **Who and when:** the holder of a camera — of a main row `type: camera`, `live: always`, not `unlicensed` — reads `rec/volumes/*` with the list of rows of `rec` (V/01.2 Н1.2в). There is an enabled volume `edge` with `server` = its own server (`SERVER_NAME`) and there is no row with this home — an order; two volumes — the first by name in bytes. One order per row.
- **Order fields:** `cam`, the `ref` of the camera (none — no field), `home` — the volume, `when` — the `when` of the volume, no field — by the rule of V/02.6 Н1.7 at the moment of the order; there is no `until`, the recording has 0. `t_E` — `now`.
- **No extension.** A repeated order writes the row with `revision + 1` (P/03.6 Н3 step 4), a new `revision` restarts the unit (P/03.3), and the recording ring is lost. Therefore the row has no term; the word `when` the recorder of the card takes from the volume.
- **The camera moved to another server.** The new holder at once orders the row of its card, the epoch — by the ordinary rule. The previous row stands `waiting` (V/02.6 Н1.7), its debt is copied; the `near` of the camera (V/01.1 Н1.3) may return the camera to it while the row is alive. The previous row is removed by the recorder of the card: the holder is not on the server of the volume longer than `EDGE_UNTIL` = 7200 s and there is no debt (V/02.6 Н1.7).
- **Volume:** disabled, removed, absent on the server — no order, the row is removed by the recorder (V/02.6 Н1.7). The row `rec/volumes` or the list is not read — unread, not absent: no new orders, log.
- **The orderer cannot remove the row:** the right of the role on foreign rows is without deletion (P/02.5 Н1.4). The row is removed by the holder of the row by `worker.deletes` of the manifest of `rec`.
- **Outcomes** — `creates.<outcome>` of the heartbeat (P/03.4 Н1.1); no row — an order at the next read.
- **Disabling the card for one camera** is possible only by `enabled: false` in the row through the tables door, not through the form: the form shows it read-only (V/11.3 Н1.3, ADR-0108 addendum (3)); the door does not close the order row (P/06.1 Н1.1), the order does not repeat it. A row removed by the operator the holder orders again.

## Н3 Procedure

### Contribution (the pass of the holder)

```
for each camera of the assignment (not unlicensed, chapter 7): assignment domain/placed/rec/<ref> with origin = own cluster →
  the element «placed» (Н1.2); no assignment, enabled, ref, push → the element «poll»; otherwise — no element;
  the assignment record or the topology is not read → the previous element, log
a field without a value — omit
the assembled list ≠ the row cam/sources/<slot> → write the row whole (as_of — only at the changed elements)
```

### Ordering recording to the card (Н1.7)

```
with the list of rows of rec:
the edge volume of its own server is enabled → no row cam-<cam>-<volume> → order {cam, ref?, home, when}
                                               the row exists → no order
no volume → no order; the volume is not read → no order, log
```

### Push (a camera with push, a camera-cluster)

```
poll of the receiver (the address — the ingest book, the key — stream | poll) → answer: wish, have, claims, requests
there is a wish → the stream from pre-recording or the continuation from have (Н1.4); otherwise — keep the ring
claim → pieces of 1 MiB when the stream is at the live edge; an unacknowledged one — with the same seq
request → V/01.4; the answer in the next poll
no answer from any receiver of the primary → the reserve or do not push; the token expires, the assignment changed, kid is not current → a new token (Н1.3)
```

**Races.** Two clusters declared one camera (`ref`) — the `conflicts` of the book, the key is nowhere; the operator separates them. The assignment
changed while the stream goes — the old token is rejected by the receiver (`wrong_audience`), the pusher requests again. A new holder
of a slot clears the row under the slot — the elements of the previous one go away before the first write of the new one.
