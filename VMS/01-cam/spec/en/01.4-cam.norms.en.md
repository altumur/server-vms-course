# V/01.4 — Commands and questions to the device: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`01.4-cam.norms.md`](../01.4-cam.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 4 of lesson V/01 «Камера» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../01-cam.md),
[instructions](../01-cam.README.md), [acceptance](../../acceptance/4/README.md), manifest [`cam.subsystem.yaml`](../../cam.subsystem.yaml).
Platform: export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The device behind the channels of cameras (V/01.1 Н1.1, group `group_by`) answers **commands** — requests of an operator or of a
scenario to click a relay, to go to a preset — and **questions** of the holder: what it can do, what channels it has, what is in its archive.
The chapter sets the norms for what `cam` knows: the body of a `cam` request and the meaning of its fields, the execution of a verb by the
holder and its refusals, questions to the device without delaying the loop, the row `cam/devices/<хост>`, the rows of children, `can` in
the camera status, arming and temporary disabling. The request family — submission, deadline, the label before the call, outcome, `unknown`,
reaper, one call per group — is the platform norms
[P/06.4](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md) Н1.5, Н3 and [P/03.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)
Н1.5, Н3 «взгляд держателя», «Действие устройства»; the chapter does not repeat them. The device archive is chapter 5.

Norm ids are `V/01.4-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `since`, `said_at` are time in the writer's form ([P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md)).

**Data** (the common families `<sub>/…` of [the platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv); the chapter has no keys
of its own beyond them): requests `cam/requests/<rid>` and labels `cam/commands/<rid>` — the request family of P/06.4, P/03.6 (the contract
[of the request and its label](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/request-mark.md)); the holder writes `cam/devices/<хост>`, `cam/children/<id>` (worker

### Н1.1 The body of a `cam` request

The section `requests:` of the manifest ([P/06.4 Н1.5](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md)); it is submitted by
`POST /cam/requests` with `Idempotency-Key` (the request name — by P/06.4 Н3 «подать просьбу»):

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `unit` | string, required | the id of the camera (of the main stream, V/01.1 Н1.1а) to whose device the command goes; for `output`, `talk` — the owner of the output, of the speaker (Н1.4а); any other stream — a refusal by the holder |
| `action` | `output` \| `preset` \| `preset_save` \| `preset_clear` \| `talk` \| `unit` \| `arm` \| `disarm` \| `disable`, required | click an output (relay); go to a preset; save the current position into a preset; clear a preset; perform an operation of a unit; take the speaker for a talk (Н1.4а); arm, disarm, disable for a time (Н1.4д) |
| `port` | a word of up to 32 characters or an integer ≤ 2^53 − 1 | the `number` of the output (`output`, Н1.4а), a value from `relays` of the owner, or the `number` of the speaker (`talk`); required — without it a refusal by the holder (Н3) |
| `state` | `open` \| `closed` \| `pulse` | what to do with the output: `closed` — close, `open` — open, `pulse` — close and after `pulse_ms` open; without the field — `pulse`. The driver knows only switching on and off (`SetRelayStatus`), the holder conducts the pulse |
| `pulse_ms` | likewise | the pulse length, ms, greater than zero for `pulse`; not read for `open`, `closed` |
| `n` | likewise | the preset number (`preset`, `preset_save`, `preset_clear`), the `n` of a unit (`unit`: the row `<unit>-<n>`, Н1.4б); required — without it a refusal by the holder |
| `operation` | a word of up to 32 characters | the operation of the unit template as is (`unit`); required — without it a refusal by the holder |
| `disabled_by_rule` | a word of up to 32 characters | the number of the scenario that disables (`disable`); required — without it a refusal by the holder |
| `disabled_until` | number | until which moment it is disabled (`disable`, Н1.4д); required — without it a refusal by the holder |
| `valid_until` | number | the deadline of the request (P/06.4) |

**Integer** — a notation in decimal digits, with or without a minus sign: the holder reads a field of the request row as text (the request
contract), `1` and `"1"` are indistinguishable in it. **Numbering:** inputs and outputs — `number` (Н1.4а), running through the whole device
and from 1; a camera's own numbers are the lists `rays`, `relays` (Н1.4 `can`), they may start not from 1 (the field `port` of the event `io.input` is in the same numbering, V/01.2 Н1.5);
presets — from 1 to `presets`.
The holder (Н3): `port` of `output` and `n` of `preset` — only an integer ≥ 1 (0 — `refused` «ports and presets count from 1»), otherwise
`refused`; `pulse_ms` — an integer ≥ 0, anything else (a word, a negative number) —
`refused`; `state` outside the three words — `refused`; `pulse` without `pulse_ms` or with 0 — `refused` (Н3).

`valid_for: 30`, `most_valid: 600` — a command answers what the operator sees now: a door opened an hour later is an
incident, «sometime this week» is not a command. `stamp: [by, at, group]` — `group` is the host of the device on which
the console asked for the rights (`reach.requests`, V/01.1 Н1.5); a camera moved to another device while the request waits
does not get the command (P/03.6 Н3: refusal by `group`).

The revocation limit: the right is asked once, at the door; `by` is a stamp, execution does not judge it. A request submitted before the right was withdrawn is executed
within its deadline: the command window is the revocation window ([P/08.4 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.4-domain.norms.md)) plus `most_valid`:
for the token of a disabled person 960 + 600 = 1560 s from issue, for a grant withdrawn while a member is without connection — `GRANT_LIFETIME` + 600 s from `as_of` of the copy.

### Н1.2 The holder's verbs

The holder executes the request on the device of the camera's group (`perform`, P/03.6 Н3 «Действие устройства»):

| `action` | Action | Answer (`reply` of the outcome) |
|---|---|---|
| `output` | the output `port` of the device into `state` for `pulse_ms` | `{action, port, state}` |
| `preset` | the device goes to preset `n` | `{action, n}` |
| `preset_save`, `preset_clear` | the device saves the current position into preset `n`, clears it (`SetPreset`, `ClearPreset`, the driver counts from zero) | `{action, n}` |
| `talk` | take the speaker `port` and call the device (Н1.4а) | `{action, port}` |
| `unit` | unit `n` performs `operation` (`CreateOperation`, `Execute`, Н1.4б) | `{action, n, operation}` |
| `arm`, `disarm` | `armed` of the row `cam/rule_state/<id>` (Н1.4д), the device is not called | `{action}` |
| `disable` | the disabling fields of the same row (Н1.4д), the device is not called | `{action, disabled_until}` |

The answer on a refusal is the `reply` of the outcome `refused`: what the constant word `error` does not carry (ADR-0094 addendum (13)):

| `error` | `reply` |
|---|---|
| `device refused` | `{action, device}`: `device` — the words of the device |
| `an argument is a number or a word` | `{action, field, length}`: the field and its length in characters |
| `device is not open now` | `{action, host}`: the host of the device |
| `no such output` | `{action, port}` |
| `output is disabled` | `{action, port, off}`: `off` — the key of the disabling row, its own or a kindred one; in doubt (Н1.4а) there is no field |
| `no such speaker`, `busy` | `{action, port}` |
| `speaker is disabled` | `{action, port, off}`: as for `output is disabled` |
| `no such unit`, `unit is off` | `{action, n}` |
| `no such operation` | `{action, n, operation}` |
| other words of Н4 | `{action}` |

The values of `reply` are strings ([the request contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/request-mark.md) «Метка»; numbers in decimal notation), not longer than 200 code points, a longer one is truncated with «…», like the `error` of a label; there are no secret values in it. `reply` is carried by the label and by the event `command.failed`.

The outcome and its fields (`performed` / `refused` / `unknown`, `error`, `late`) are the request contract and P/03.6; `cam` gives the refusal
words (Н4). One call into a group at a time, calls outside the loop thread, «not ready» — `refused` — are the platform (P/03.6 Н3
«Действие устройства»).

### Н1.3 Questions to the device

Everything the holder asks the device other than a command: capabilities and description (every pass), channels and occupancy, the archive
coverage of each camera (every heartbeat; chapter 5), opening and closing the connection. Rules:

- **not on the loop thread:** every call is made outside the loop; the pass and the heartbeat wait for a round of questions together no longer than
  `DEVICE_GRACE` = 0.2 s and no longer than the remainder of `DEVICE_HOLD` = 0.5 s of the pass or the heartbeat; a late answer goes to the
  next round;
- **one line per device:** questions to one device go in turn; a question to a device whose previous call has not
  returned is not asked again (the opening deadline takes the call off the line, below); slowness is counted from the start of the question, one standing in the queue is not slow;
- **a slow device:** it did not answer within `DEVICE_GRACE` — the element `{device: <хост>, state: slow, since}` of the list `devices` of the heartbeat
  (V/01.2 Н1.4), `since` — the start of the unanswered question; it is asked but not waited for; a fast answer removes the label;
  a slow question does not make a command slow and vice versa (`devices_slow` counts the device once);
- **silence is not an answer:** what the device has not said does not exist: the description stays as before (silence is not «no relays»),
  what did not answer is not written, including as zero; coverage said earlier carries `said_at` — when the device said it;
- **an error is a word of the device:** it is not the end of the pass; into the log — once;
- **opening** is a question on the device line, like the others; the opening model is here, V/01.2 Н3 refers to it:
  - **hangs** — it has not returned within `DEVICE_GRACE`: it is not waited for and not asked again until it returns or the deadline passes; the cameras of the device —
    `device_state: opening`, phase `pending`; they get no commands (`refused` «not ready», Н3), the archive is not served
    (503 «device opening», V/01.5); the device is held from the pass on which the opening returned successfully and passed reconciliation;
  - **reconciliation** — an opening that returned successfully is reconciled with the row as of the moment of return: the unit that asked for it no longer exists (removed,
    disabled, out of licence) or the row changed `revision` or `born` — the connection is closed, the device is not held by it,
    the current unit opens its own; this is not a start failure. A refusal of such an opening does not concern the current unit either;
  - **deadline** — `DEVICE_OPEN_WAIT` = 15 s from the start of the call, an edit of the row does not extend it. It passed — a start failure, like a refusal below:
    the call is taken off the line, a late return is closed. Limit: a call that the driver does not release keeps hanging next to a new
    opening, one per each backoff pause;
  - **refused** — the opening returned with a refusal: a temporary failure by V/01.2 Н3 — a start failure of its cameras with the backoff of
    P/03.3 (phase `failed`, `stalled` after `RETRY_MAX`), `device_state: opening`; a new opening — at a new start of the
    unit, not on every pass.

### Н1.4 The device row and `can`

**`cam/devices/<хост>`** (form — V/01.1 Н1.2) is written by the holder of the group, the whole row with cas
([P/03.4 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)):
- the name is the host in one spelling (`group_by`, `cut_at: host`); two sessions on one host — one row (the events of both,
  the larger of the numbers, `ptz` — true if true in any session, `identity` — the first non-empty one; the blocks and `resolutions` — one set, if they diverge — by the session of the smallest id of a main row of the host, not of the owner: the owner depends on the blocks); a source without a host (`driverpack://file/…`) has no row;
- it is written when the device description or `videos` changed **or there is no row**: every pass reconciles them with the read
  row of the store, the memory of the device's answer does not replace checking the row; a deleted row whose host is still in the holder's
  groups is written again by the next pass with the previously said description ([P/03.4 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md));
  a cas conflict or a failed write is not «written»: re-read the row and write again on the next pass; the device did not
  answer — the description is the previous one, but `videos` is its own: only a change of `videos` touches the row;
- `channels` — the video sources that the description named, the same number as in the door record (V/01.5 Н1.4); the description did not name them — there is no field: «0» means «no video» (Н1.4а);
- `videos` — the indexes of the main rows `type: camera` of the group (V/01.1 Н1.2, Н1.1в), including `enabled: false`: the source counts them by the address, the device is not asked. Therefore the field is written on a change of the group's rows and does not wait for the description: there is no row yet, the description is not said, an index exists — a row with `device` and `videos`, without description fields; there are no indexes — the holder does not create such a row. An empty `videos` with a live row is `{}`;
- `resolutions` — the resolutions that the description named, in the spelling `<ширина>x<высота>`; a value that does not reduce to it is not written; the description did not name them — there is no field; a per-stream list (`.rep`) is folded by a union over the streams in the order of the description, without repeats: there is one row per host;
- `events` — `command`, `command.failed`, `silent`, `source-stats` (every camera raises them, Н1.5, V/01.2 Н1.5) ∪ the events that
  the device named, ∪ `io.input` if the host has a live `sensor` row (Н1.4а);
- `preset_names`, `storage` — the form of V/01.1 Н1.2; `storage` is false if the driver answered the archive listing that it does not support it: the reference `embeddedStorageRef` exists, but there is no archive;
- `identity` — the serial number or MAC said by the device (not longer than 200; a longer one is truncated to 200); an empty answer does not cancel what was said.
  A coincidence of `identity` with the row of another host is not a refusal (one device under two names or two devices with one
  number): the row is written, the status of each camera of the device carries a `warning` with words naming both names, the device record
  of the heartbeat carries `same_serial_as: <хост>`, the counter `identity_coincidences`; every pass checks whether the row of the
  other name still stands — it does not, and the warning goes away. One device under two names: the operator moves the cameras to one
  name and deletes the row of the other through the door (`DELETE` with cas → 200, P/06.1 Н1.6); the holder writes the row again only
  if its host is still in its groups. A change of `identity` under the same host — the log and the counter `identity_changes`,
  not a refusal; the status of each camera of the device carries a `warning`: the names of the children might have landed on other hardware. It lives in the memory
  of the holder while the connection with the device is open: a restart and closing remove it, the log and the counter remain.

**`can`** in the element `status[]` of the camera (V/01.2 Н1.4): the description of the device of its group without `identity`, `channels`, `resolutions` —
`{events, rays, relays, ptz, presets, preset_names?}`; the device has not said it — there is no field. The `ptz` of a camera is whether there is a telemetry reference for the index of its channel (V/01.1 Н1.1в; `telemetryRef` of the description, per DriverPack 2026-10-02, to be reconciled with the new version): no reference — false; the `ptz` of the device row is true for any reference. `preset_names` — the answer of `GetPresets`, only for a driver with `ITelemetry2`; there is none — there is no field, the presets are the numbers up to `presets`: this is a limit, not a list of the saved ones. `rays`, `relays` — the lists of `number` of the live
rows `sensor`, `relay` of this camera with the `device` of its host, ascending (disabled ones too: they exist), `io.input` in `events` — only with a non-empty `rays`. A
camera whose inputs belong to another row of the host has an empty list: a scenario judges the row that executes, and `port` is checked by membership in
the list, not by a range: the numbers run through the whole device. In the device record
of the door (V/01.5 Н1.4) `can` is the same over all live children of the host. This is how the description leaves the cluster: the domain and
the scenarios of other clusters read the heartbeat, not the row.

### Н1.4а Children of the device: the holder's side

Form — V/01.1 Н1.2а; decision — ADR-0102 and addenda (1), (4)–(7), (10). The device description names the video sources, the blocks of inputs and
outputs in order (`ioDeviceRef` in the DriverPack description) and the references to microphones and speakers in order (`audioSourceRef`,
`audioDestinationRef`). A block has a count of its inputs and outputs; a block and an audio reference have `relatedChannel` if the field exists.

**Owner** — a live main row `cam/cameras/*` of the group of this host (`main` is empty). `enabled: false` does not prevent a row from being
the owner. The owner is decided per block, a block goes to one owner whole, there is no per-contact binding. An audio reference is a block of one
element of its kind. Only `camera` and `ipsrv` can be owners, `eventsrc` and `acfa` never (V/01.1 Н1.1б).
«The device row» is `type: ipsrv` with the smallest numeric id; «the camera of index `c`» is `type: camera` with index `c`
(V/01.1 Н1.1в), of two with one index — the one with the smallest numeric id.

| `channels` | Owner of the block |
|---|---|
| no field | not determined, do not touch the rows: silence is not loss (Н1.3) |
| 0 | the device row |
| 1 | the `type: camera` row with the smallest numeric id, the index is not asked: the owner needs it only when `channels` > 1 (ADR-0102 addendum (11) item 1); there is none — the device row |
| greater than 1 | the steps below |

When `channels` > 1, where `k` is the number of blocks of this kind (inputs and outputs are one kind, `mic` and `spk` each its own), `i` is the index of the block from 0, `ch` is
the `relatedChannel` of the block, with no field — `i`; the first that fires:

1. `ch` < 0 — the device row;
2. `k` = 1 — the device row, for any `ch`;
3. 0 ≤ `ch` < `channels`:
   - there is a camera of index `ch` — it, whatever other rows there are;
   - there is none, and all main `type: camera` rows of the host have their index said — the block gives no children and is not diverted to the device, without a `warning`:
     otherwise creating the camera would change the owner and the key, and the names would be lost;
   - there is none, and there is a main `type: camera` row without an index — the block is not decided: its rows are not touched, the status of each camera of the host carries
     a `warning` while the condition holds: that row might have been this channel;
4. otherwise — the device row.

The block got the device row, but there is no such row — the block has no children: its live rows go to `gone`; the status of each camera of the host carries
a `warning` that there is no `type: ipsrv` row for them, while the condition holds.

**Number.** `number` runs through the inputs of all blocks in their order, from 1; outputs are counted the same way, separately. The holder
decomposes `number` back into a pair (block, number within the block) and subtracts 1: the driver counts from zero, otherwise the neighbouring
relay would click. Blocks with 2 and 3 inputs: `number` 4 is the second block (index 1), the input with index 1. Back, the input with index `r` of a block
gives `port`: the sum of the inputs of the previous blocks + `r` + 1. The number does not depend on the owner: for the camera of channel 2 the inputs may start from 3, the
numbers themselves are `rays`, `relays` of its `can`. For `mic`, `spk` the `number` is the index of the reference + 1, separately per kind.

**Writing.** When the description is said, the holder reconciles the rows with the expected set: one `sensor`, `relay`, `mic`, `spk` row per each
number of a decided block, `camera` — the owner, `device` — the host, `gone: false`. Every pass it reads the rows of the set by exact keys.
It lists the whole table `cam/children/*` once per `HEARTBEAT_EVERY` (P/03.3), and also on the first pass with a said
description and on the pass where the expected set changed, before the answer to `output`: with thousands of cameras a listing on every
pass is too expensive, a delay of `gone` is harmless, and a sibling row (below) does not tolerate a delay. A mandatory listing failed —
it is mandatory on the next pass as well, until it succeeds: the set is compared with the one at which the listing succeeded.
- an expected row does not exist or is different, including unreadable or deleted by the operator, — write the whole row with cas
  (P/03.4 Н1.6); the `device` of the row changed — the camera status carries a `warning`, as on a change of `identity`: the name and the settings
  moved to other hardware;
- a live row outside the set with the `device` of this host or with a `camera` of its group, whatever `device` it holds, — set
  `gone: true`: the camera does not exist, the camera went to another host, the owner changed, the number no longer exists; this does not concern the numbers of an undecided block;
- the holder does not touch other rows;
- the description is not said or the device did not answer — do not touch the rows: silence is not loss (Н1.3);
- a cas conflict or a failed write — write again on the next pass.

**Disabled.** The latest word decides (ADR-0102 addendum (11) item 2): the `enabled` of the setting of the live row of this input or output and of the settings of sibling rows — any rows
of `cam/children/*` with the same `device`, `type`, `number`, including `gone` ones: after a change of owner an output is not silently enabled, and the holder
cannot write `child_settings`. No words — enabled. The words are the same — they decide. The words differ — the order is given by the stamp `at` of the setting
(V/01.1 Н1.2а): the latest is `true` — enabled, `false` — disabled; the latest `at` are equal, or some word has no `at` or it is unreadable —
disabled. The row index gives no order: it is opaque (P/01.1 Н1). A person with `edit` on the current owner removes the disabled state:
`enabled: true` in the setting of the current row, this is a new latest word. The door judges
a setting by its `camera` (`rights.unit_of`, V/01.1 Н1.5), therefore that person does not edit another's row, and the `gone` row stays as it is and,
while its word is not the latest, does not disable. The holder reads the settings of its own rows and of the sibling rows found by the listing
by exact keys every pass. **In doubt, disabled:** its own setting is unreadable — disabled; the read of the pass failed —
the previous value, there is no previous one — disabled; a kindred setting is unreadable — disabled: its word might have been the latest; an output — also while
the mandatory listing has not succeeded: a sibling row might have remained unfound. The holder does not write the fields `title`, `enabled`, `norm`.
- a disabled `sensor`: `io.input` is not written, `inputs_off` + 1 (V/01.2 Н1.5);
- a disabled `relay`: `refused` «output is disabled» (Н3).

**Normal state.** The `norm` of the setting of a `sensor` row is the normal state of the contact; there is no field or no settings row — `open`.
The driver tells the contact state, the implementation translates its word into `open`, `closed` («Implementation decides»); any other word — the event
is dropped, `events_refused` (V/01.2 Н1.5). The holder compares the state with `norm`: it differs — the start of an alarm, the `value` of the event
`io.input` is `closed`; it matches — the end, `value` is `open` (V/01.2 Н1.5; the words await the answer of consolem). Thus `match.value` of a scenario means one thing on a make
(normally open) and on a break (normally closed) contact. Only its own setting is read: the `norm` of a sibling row is not inherited. The setting is unreadable — the input is
disabled in doubt (above). The `norm` of a `relay` row is stored, it does not act on the output: the effect on an output is not verified.

**Licence.** The owner is `unlicensed` (V/01.7 Н1.3): an input of its block does not go through — the event is dropped, `events_refused` (V/01.2 Н1.5), — and likewise an output
(Н3): the licence closes the device as a whole.

**Audio** (ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 7; per DriverPack 2026-10-02, to be reconciled with the new version). `mic`, `spk` work; the owner and `gone` are by this chapter. The rule
«Disabled» above acts on them as well, including «in doubt, disabled»: a disabled `mic` gives no audio endpoint, an open one is closed (V/01.2 Н1.3), a disabled `spk` — `refused`
«speaker is disabled». Their `norm` is stored and not read. The owner is `unlicensed` — no point, an open one is closed, and no talk: the licence closes the device as a whole.

**Talk.** One talker per `spk`. A request `talk` (Н1.2) is submitted by a person with `edit` on the owner of the `spk`: this is precisely the right to talk, the gateway does not judge rights (V/03.3 Н1.2а).
The holder takes the `spk` for the `by` of the stamp and calls the device (`Call`): an answer (`CallAccepted`) — `performed`; a refusal (`Failed`, `HungUp`) or no answer within `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` — the occupancy is removed, `refused` «device refused»: an answer after the action deadline is not accepted. Taken by another `by` — `refused` «busy»; by the same one,
with the call accepted, — the deadline anew, `performed`, no new `Call`. The gateway brings the audio by the route `talk` of the door (V/01.5 Н1.3), the holder hands the frames to the device (`PushAudioFrame`); the codec of the frames is
the `talk` of the heartbeat (V/01.2 Н1.4), the gateway transcodes. The occupancy lives in the memory of the holder; it is removed, and the device gets `HangUp`, when: the route is not opened within
the `valid_for` of the request, 30 s from `performed`; the route is closed or broken; there is no frame for `TALK_SILENT` (V/01.5 Н1.5); the connection with the device is closed; the owner became `unlicensed`, the `spk` is disabled or `gone`.
The device hung up by itself (`HungUp`) — the occupancy is removed, the route is closed.

**Limits.**
- An input of a channel that is not created is not visible and is not enabled by anyone (step 3).
- `relatedChannel` is per block: there is no binding of an individual contact.
- The description names no blocks — there are no children: a camera created with the model `generic` will not show inputs.
- A restart of the holder breaks a talk: the occupancy is in memory, the gateway sees a break, the new holder does not know it.
- The order of words is the console clock: when the clocks of its instances diverge, `at` may put an earlier edit after a later one (P/06.1 Н1.6). A word is the whole record
  of the settings row: an edit of `title` or `norm` in an old setting with the previous `enabled` makes it the latest.
- Deleting a child row breaks kinship. A `DELETE` through the console door passes (V/01.1 Н1.2а), the manifest does not forbid it (P/06.1 Н1.6), the holder does not write
  a `gone` row again, and sibling rows are found only by the `children` listing: the setting `enabled: false` stays without a row and does not disable, the output
  of the new owner is enabled. The same holds for a live row if the owner changed before the pass. On the `backend` door such a `DELETE` is a refusal (V/01.1 Н1.2а).
- A change of owner loses the names and `norm`: the children of the new row have another key, the previous `child_settings` remain under the old one and come back
  only if the owner comes back; this cannot be cured, the id comes from the camera. The owner changes by creating and removing a row, by replacing the camera of a channel and
  by editing `source`, including when `channels` = 1. A disabled output stays disabled in this case (above).
- The last camera of a host was deleted — there is no holder, the rows remain without `gone`: the reader checks the liveness of the camera itself
  (`backend`, ADR-0102 addendum (1) item 7); a camera id is not repeated, the row will go to nobody.
- All cameras of a host were disabled (`enabled: false`) — the device is not opened, the description is not said, the rows stay live,
  and the camera is alive: the liveness check does not cut them off. A reader for whom it matters checks the `enabled` of the owner.
- Moving a camera to another host while the old host is no longer gone round: the superfluous rows go to `gone` only when the description
  of the new host is said.
- Replacing the device under the same host carries the names over to the new hardware: the `warning` above.
- Moving a camera to another host takes its setting away: the owner on the old host is new, the rows with the previous `device` and a disabled
  setting no longer exist there, and the physical output of the old device is enabled. The `warning` is only at the moved camera.
- The setting of a deleted camera stays in the table: only someone with a grant on `*` can remove the row itself, it has no live unit
  (gate and pass contract). This does not hinder the output: a new word in the setting of the current row overrides it. The key is the field `off` of the answer.
- The `type: ipsrv` row is a unit with an empty `main`, it takes a licence (V/01.7 Н1.2): blocks on the device cost one licence, blocks on
  cameras — not a single extra one.
- The address of the channel camera did not say the index: the blocks of this channel are not decided until the address says it (step 3).

### Н1.4б Units of the device: the holder's side

Form — V/01.1 Н1.2б; decision — ADR-0102 addenda (9), (10). Per DriverPack 2026-10-02, to be reconciled with the new version: `IUnit`, `IOperationProcessor`, `unitRef`, `DeviceInfo.xsd`.

**Reading.** Rows `type: det` are Н1.4в, they do not enter the tree. The holder reads the rows `cam/device_units/<camera>-*` of its main rows of any type. It lists the whole table at the start of the unit and once per `HEARTBEAT_EVERY` (P/03.3), like `children` (Н1.4а): an edit of a
unit takes effect from the next listing, the row `cam/cameras` is not restarted by it. The listing failed — the previous one is in effect: not read, rather than absent, the units are not removed. A row not in form — `refused` by its `n`, if the key has it.

**Tree.** The holder goes from the root: `CreateUnit(handler, owner, template, id)`, where `owner` is the unit `parent`, the root has no owner, `id` is the row key. Start — `Start`, removal — `Stop`, `Destroy` bottom-up.
A unit is not created, the word in `units_down` (V/01.2 Н1.4) is `refused`, if the template is not in the model description, the template is not named in `unitRef` of the owner's template, `maxCount` is exceeded (`0`, no field — no limit), there is no owner or `parent` leads into a cycle, the owner
has a word in `units_down`. `enabled: false` — there is no unit and no word; nested ones are not created either, no word. The description is not said or is `generic` — the units wait, no words: silence is not loss (Н1.3). The holder does not execute `defCount`: the operator creates the rows.

**State.** `Started` — no word; `SignalLost` — `lost`; `Failed` at start or at work — `failed`, a new attempt — on an edit of the row or a new start of the unit `cam/cameras`, not on every pass. `Started` removes the word. The heartbeat grows with the number of non-working units: this is a limit.

**Events.** `IDetector`, `IEventFactory` of a unit — a `unit` row (V/01.2 Н1.5) into the `camera` bucket. The word of the event is as the driver named it, the list is not checked: templates have dozens of them and they differ. A unit outside the tree (removed, disabled) writes no events.

**Licence and holder.** A unit does not take a licence (chapter 7 Н1.2). A row `unlicensed` (chapter 7 Н1.3): no connection, no tree, a command — a refusal of Н3. The holder changed — the new one builds the tree anew, there is no state.

**Limits.** Operations without parameters: the XSD `.rep` does not describe them. On a controller the group is all its doors: an `Execute` that has not returned `Finished` holds the commands of all doors until `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` and after `unknown`, until it returns (P/03.6 «Действие устройства»), while the deadline of the request runs. `output` is not translated into an operation: a door — only through `unit`. Unit properties other than `address` are not passed. A unit with `Failed` on the device is not distinguished from one removed on it: the holder does not learn about a change of the `.rep` composition until `refused`.

### Н1.4в Built-in detectors

Decision — ADR-0107 item 8. Per DriverPack 2026-10-02, to be reconciled with the new version: `CreateVideoAnalytics`, `IVideoAnalytics`, `detectorRef`, `conflictDetectors`.

A row `cam/device_units`, `type: det` (V/01.1 Н1.2б) is created by the operator, the detector works on the device, in the connection of the camera's holder, it has no `det/units` row and no model on the `det` worker. It does not use the stream and is never a consumer (V/01.2 Н1.2в): it is the only live detection of the camera if the camera has only `replica`.

**Reading and creation** are as for a unit (Н1.4б), `parent` is not read: there is no tree. The holder calls `CreateVideoAnalytics(handler, channel, template)`, `channel` is the camera index (V/01.1 Н1.1в), `Start` when `enabled`, removal — `Stop`. `enabled: false` — there is no detector and no word. The holder passes the `zone` of the row to the detector by the driver's `visualElement`; a value not in form — `refused`. In DriverPack 2026-10-02 the way of passing has not been read, to be reconciled with the new version.

**Refusal** — the word in `units_down` is `refused`: the row's `camera` is not `type: camera` or the channel index is not said; the channel has no such detector in the model description; `conflictDetectors`: an incompatible detector of this channel is enabled — the row with the smaller `n` goes, the other one is `refused`. `backend` judges this earlier (V/11.4 Н1.2), the table is a console door, therefore the truth is here. The description is not said — the rows wait, no words: silence is not loss (Н1.3).

**State, events, licence, holder** are as for a unit (Н1.4б): events — a `unit` row into the camera bucket, a move — anew, no state. There is one difference: the row carries `det_kind`, an open phase repeats (V/01.2 Н1.5), therefore the rule «on motion» and recording on motion work also on a camera without a model on the `det` worker, and such rows of `detector-kinds.tsv` number 275. The package `cam` puts the table next to `device-catalog.json`.

**Limits.** Two holders in the seconds of a double assignment both start the detector: the events are separated by the bucket epoch, there is no protection against a repeat. `sensitivity` is stored, but how the value reaches the device the interface `Analytics::IDetector` in DriverPack 2026-10-02 does not say: the holder does not pass it, to be reconciled with the new version. A detector with tracks only (`TargetList`) has no place in the contract. An open phase does not survive a move: the new holder does not know about it, there is no `still` until the next `begin`. An instantaneous word of the detector — a row per word, no suppression: the ceiling is the `rate_per_minute` of the scenario; a two-phase one is smoothed by V/01.2 `DET_STILL`. The `alarms` of a camera judge the `kind` of the row, `unit`: a built-in `motion` does not become an alarm of the camera. A `det` model and a built-in detector of one kind on a camera give two firings for one motion: there is one recording order, the rule sees a doubling. The kind `other` cannot be named by a rule: there is no such kind in the catalogue (V/11.3 Н1.3).

### Н1.4г Turning the camera

Decision — ADR-0107 item 9, addendum (2) item 3. Per DriverPack 2026-10-02, to be reconciled with the new version: `CreateTelemetryChannel`, `ITelemetry`.

PTZ is a property of the camera, not a device: it has no row, name or enabling of its own. A preset is a request (Н1.2). Continuous movement, zoom and focus are a **lease**: a request would stand in the queue of the group (P/03.6 Н3 «Действие устройства»), and the stop of camera 7 would wait for the call of camera 3.

**Row** `cam/ptz_moves/{camera}-{person}` — a console table, written by a person with `edit` on the camera (`rights.routes`), read by the holder of the camera every pass. `camera` is the main row, `person` is who moves: the name of the person (P/08.4) in the spelling of the table key, a comma in the key is forbidden, such a name will not turn the camera: this is a limit; `seq` grows with every extension, `pan`, `tilt`, `zoom`, `focus` are signed speeds, −100…100, a share of the model speed (`panSpeed`, `tiltSpeed`, `zoomSpeed`, `focusSpeed`), 0 — the axis stands. An extension is a write with a new `seq`, a stop is the removal of the row.

**Deadline.** `PTZ_LEASE` = 5 s by the holder's clock: a row whose index has not changed for that long counts as removed. Therefore movement acts only from an index that the holder has seen changing: a row found at the start of the unit does not move. An abandoned row is cleaned up by `delete_after`. The row is unreadable — as if removed: it is not read, and standing still is safer. Two persons on one camera — the last changed row acts.

**Execution.** Telemetry calls go by their own line of the channel, outside the queue of the group and the question line (Н1.3); movement — `MoveContinuous`, `ZoomContinuous`, `FocusContinuous`, only under the strict `may_act`. It is not executed — `ptz_reason` (V/01.2 Н1.4): `no telemetry` (`ptz` false), `device is not open now`, `device refused`; for another stream and for a camera `unlicensed` — likewise `no telemetry`.

**Stop** is zero speed on each axis that moved: the interface has no common stop, pan-tilt, zoom and focus are different calls. It does not wait for an unfinished call of the line, does not wait for the `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` of the previous holder and does not ask `may_act`: an extra stop is harmless. It is sent when the row was removed, when `PTZ_LEASE` passed, in `stop_unit` before closing the connection, on `unlicensed`, on an opening of the device by the same holder (the connection might have broken during a movement) and on taking the epoch: to each camera with `ptz` of its group, if the epoch is not the first, on the first pass with an open device and a said description: the new holder knows nothing about the movement of the previous one. **An unconfirmed stop** — the call refused or did not return — is repeated on every pass until the device answers: the row is already gone, the device has no deadline, there will be no other occasion. A new movement cancels the repeat.

**A deadline for the device** is set if the driver can do it. `ITelemetry` in DriverPack 2026-10-02 has no deadline parameter: a speed acts «until a new one comes», therefore on the current version no model has a deadline.

**Limits.** A stop arrives within a pass: a released button stops the camera after a time of up to `POLL` plus the call. The holder died — the camera moves until the stop of the new holder: the slot fate of about 90 s (P/04 Н3) and the opening of the device up to `DEVICE_OPEN_WAIT` = 15 s, in total up to **105 s**; the device has no deadline, there is nothing to shorten this with. Tours and cruises are out of scope: the contract does not know them.

### Н1.4д Arming and temporary disabling

Decision — ADR-0105 items 6, 8. The consolem contract — `Device.armed`, `Device.disabledByRuleId`, `Device.disabledByRuleAt`.

**Row** `cam/rule_state/<id>` (form — V/01.1 Н1.2в) — what the rules said about the camera. The holder writes it when executing a request, the whole row with cas ([P/03.4 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)). It is not a field of `cam/cameras`: the worker does not write unit rows (P/02.1 Н1.2а), and a new `revision` would restart the camera on every word of a rule (chapter 2 Н1.1). An edit of the camera, a restart and a move do not touch the row.

**Execution.** The device is not called, therefore the word is accepted by a file camera, by an unopened device and by `enabled: false`. The holder reads the row by the exact key, applies the word and writes; a cas conflict — re-read and repeat; the store did not answer within `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` — `unknown` (P/03.6 Н3): a repeat of the same word is harmless.
- `arm`, `disarm` — `armed` `true`, `false`; the disabling fields do not change;
- `disable` with `disabled_until` later than the moment of execution — `disabled_by_rule`, `disabled_until` from the request; `disabled_at` — the previous one if the disabling is still in effect, otherwise the moment of execution; `armed` does not change. The last word decides: a short deadline shortens a long one;
- `disable` with `disabled_until` not later than the moment of execution — the disabling fields are removed: this is how a camera is enabled before the deadline;
- there is no row — write a new one; the row is not in form — write a new one: what the request did not say is not in it.

**In effect.** A camera is disabled when `disabled_by_rule` exists and `disabled_until` is later than «now» by the reader's clock. The deadline passes by itself: nobody removes the row, the `revision` of the camera does not grow.

**A disabled camera** is not held, like `enabled: false` (chapter 2 Н1.1): phase `pending`, `reason` «rule: off», the stream is closed, the closing is not a break, there is no `silent`. The epoch and the assignment remain, requests are read; the connection with the device is closed if no other camera of the group needs it. It remains the owner of children (Н1.4а), the stream is not held: «main: off by rule». The deadline passed — the next pass starts the camera as enabled. Requests `disable`, `arm` and `disarm` are accepted: they change only the row `cam/rule_state/<id>`, the device is not called; other requests — `refused` «camera is off by a rule» (Н3, Н4).

**Reading by the holder.** It lists the whole table at the start of the unit and once per `HEARTBEAT_EVERY` (P/03.3), like `children` (Н1.4а); it remembers its own word from the write, therefore a camera with this holder hears it at once, a stream on another holder — from the listing.
- the listing failed — the previous word: not read, rather than absent; there is no previous one — the camera runs: in doubt recording matters more, the next listing decides;
- the row is not in form — the camera runs, its status carries a `warning` while the row is such; the holder does not fix it, the next request writes it whole;
- the row disappeared, but the word is in memory (removed through the door, P/06.1 Н1.6) — it is written again on the next pass (P/03.4 Н1.6); there is no memory — there is no row.

**Reading by others.** `armed` is read by the evaluator (the condition of a rule, V/07), `armed` and the disabling fields by `backend` (V/11). There is no row — not armed and not disabled. The row is unreadable or the store is silent — the word is unknown, it is neither «no» nor `false`: what the reader says then is set by its lesson.

**Limits.**
- Clocks: `disabled_until` is the submitter's clock, «now» is the reader's clock, `disabled_at` is the holder's; when clocks diverge the camera is enabled earlier or later by the divergence.
- The word waits in the queue of the group like a command (P/03.6 Н3 «Действие устройства»): a call hanging at the driver delays it, the deadline of the request runs.
- A row is disabled, not a device: the neighbouring cameras of the group, the inputs and outputs live on. Recording and the detectors of the camera remain without frames, their rows do not change.
- `armed` acts on nothing in the cluster except the conditions of rules.
- The row of a removed camera remains: the id is not repeated (Н1.1), it will not become someone else's.

### Н1.5 Command events

An executed command is the event `command`, a refusal is `command.failed` in the bucket of the camera (next to its motion: «at 14:02 the door
was opened, and by whom»); it is written by the worker base after the outcome `performed`/`refused` ([P/03.6 Н3](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)

`unknown`, `expired` and `gone` (there is no camera — the reaper closed the command, [P/06.4 Н3](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md) «жнец»)
— without an event (they are visible by the label and the series). The holder does not write these events itself.
The words of these kinds on the page are the console module (P/07), `display.kinds` does not name them.

## Н3 Procedures

### Perform the request (perform)

The holder's look, the label `begun`, the outcome, `unknown`, the refusal by `group` and by deadline — [P/03.6 Н3](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md);
the call itself:

In order, the first row that fires:

```
camera unlicensed (V/01.7 Н1.3)                                  → refused «camera is not licensed»
unit — another stream of the camera (V/01.1 Н1.1а)               → refused «command the camera, not its stream»
arm, disarm, disable: the row is not type: camera                → refused «arm and disable are for a camera»
disable: no disabled_by_rule | disabled_until not a number       → refused «disable needs a rule and a time»
         disabled_by_rule longer than 32 characters              → refused «an argument is a number or a word», reply {action, field, length}
         otherwise — the row of Н1.4д                            → performed, reply {action, disabled_until}
arm, disarm: the row of Н1.4д                                    → performed, reply {action}
camera disabled by a rule (Н1.4д)                                → refused «camera is off by a rule»
the camera has no device (driverpack://file)                     → refused «camera has no device»
the device of the camera's group is not open (no connection)     → refused «device is not open now» («not ready»), reply {action, host}
any of port, state, pulse_ms, n, operation longer than 32 chars  → refused «an argument is a number or a word», reply {action, field, length}
pulse_ms not an integer ≥ 0 (Н1.1)                               → refused «pulse_ms is a whole number of milliseconds»
output: no port | port not an integer ≥ 1 (0 — «ports and presets count from 1») → refused «output needs a port»
        description said, the device has no outputs              → refused «this device has no outputs»
        description not said | no live row <unit>-relay-<port> of this host → refused «no such output», reply {action, port}
        output disabled (Н1.4а)                                  → refused «output is disabled», reply {action, port, off}
        state not open, closed, pulse → refused «state is open, closed or pulse»; pulse and pulse_ms 0 → refused «pulse needs pulse_ms»
        otherwise — output port into state (no field — pulse), the pair by Н1.4а → performed, reply {action, port, state}
talk:   no port | port not an integer ≥ 1 (0 — «ports and presets count from 1») → refused «talk needs a port»
        no live row <unit>-spk-<port> of this host                    → refused «no such speaker», reply {action, port}
        speaker disabled (Н1.4а)                                      → refused «speaker is disabled», reply {action, port, off}
        taken by another by                                           → refused «busy», reply {action, port}
        taken by the same by, call accepted                           → deadline anew, performed, reply {action, port}
        otherwise — take for by, Call (Н1.4а)                         → device answer: performed, reply {action, port}; refusal, no answer within PERFORM_TIMEOUT: occupancy removed, refused «device refused»
preset, preset_save, preset_clear:
        no n | n not an integer ≥ 1 (0 — «ports and presets count from 1») → refused «preset needs n»
        description said, ptz false                               → refused «this device has no telemetry»
        otherwise — preset n: go to, save, clear                  → performed, reply {action, n}
unit:   no n | n not an integer ≥ 1 | no operation                → refused «unit needs n and an operation»
        no live row <unit>-<n> or the unit is not created (Н1.4б) → refused «no such unit», reply {action, n}
        enabled: false                                            → refused «unit is off», reply {action, n}
        no such operation in the template (.rep), CreateOperation returned 0 → refused «no such operation», reply {action, n, operation}
        otherwise — CreateOperation, Execute, wait for Finished   → performed, reply {action, n, operation}
device error                                                     → refused «device refused», the device's words — reply
```

**Cluster camera** (`cfw://local`, V/01.6 Н1.5) is a device itself: `can` is the firmware description, there are no `devices` rows and no children,
`output` and `preset` are judged by `can`. Therefore a camera collapsed into the domain is one `Device` record without children and address (V/11.3 Н1.3).

**Races.** Two holders of one camera in the seconds of a double assignment: the epoch before the first call (P/03.6 Н3) — one of them
clicks. A repeated click is the same request by `Idempotency-Key` (P/06.4). The camera moved to another device between submission and
the call — the refusal by `group` (P/03.6). The group moved while a call of the previous holder hangs at the driver — the new holder waits out its deadline (P/03.6 Н3). Someone else's `begun` without an outcome is `unknown`, not a repeat: for a door «possibly twice» is worse
than «possibly never» — a human decides. The output was disabled, but the pass has not yet read the settings row — the command
is executed: the operator's word takes effect from the next pass.


### Ask the devices (pass, heartbeat)

```
for each device of the group: no question pending → ask outside the loop on its line
wait for the round ≤ DEVICE_GRACE and ≤ the remainder of DEVICE_HOLD
answered → update the description, the coverage, the row (Н1.4, if changed), the child rows (Н1.4а); remove slow
did not answer → slow with since; the description and the coverage are the previous ones (said_at)
error → «not known», log once
```

**Races.** Two writes of a device row or a child row (a double assignment of the group): cas by what was read (P/03.4 Н1.6) — one passes,
the second re-reads on the next pass.

| no `port` for `output` / `n` for `preset`, or not an integer ≥ 1 | `refused` «output needs a port» / «preset needs n»; 0 — «ports and presets count from 1» | label, `command.failed` | give an integer from 1 |
| the description is said: no outputs / telemetry | `refused` «this device has no outputs» / «this device has no telemetry» | label, `command.failed` | — |
| the `unit` has no live output `port`: another owner, the number out of range, the description not said | `refused` «no such output» | label, `command.failed` | submit to the owner with a number from `can` |
| the output is disabled: by the setting of this or of a sibling row, in doubt (Н1.4а) | `refused` «output is disabled» | label, `command.failed` | `enabled: true` in the setting of this row |
| no `n` or `operation` for `unit`, `n` not an integer ≥ 1 | `refused` «unit needs n and an operation» | label, `command.failed` | specify them |
| there is no unit, it is not created | `refused` «no such unit» | label, `command.failed` | create the unit, `units_down` |
| the unit is disabled | `refused` «unit is off» | label, `command.failed` | `enabled: true` |
| the template has no such operation | `refused` «no such operation» | label, `command.failed` | an operation from `.rep` |
| `talk` without `port` | `refused` «talk needs a port» | label, `command.failed` | give the `number` of the speaker |
| the `unit` has no live `spk` of number `port` | `refused` «no such speaker» | label, `command.failed` | submit to the owner |
| the speaker is disabled: by the setting of this or of a sibling row, in doubt (Н1.4а) | `refused` «speaker is disabled» | label, `command.failed` | `enabled: true` in the setting of this row |
| the speaker is taken by another person | `refused` «busy» | label, `command.failed` | wait for the end of the talk |
| a device error | `refused` «device refused», the device's words — the `reply` of the event (Н1.2) | label, `command.failed` | — |
| the request does not match the schema, the deadline is beyond `most_valid` | 400 at the door (P/06.4 Н4) | the answer | — |
