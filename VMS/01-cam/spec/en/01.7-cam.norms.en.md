# V/01.7 — The licence at the consumer: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`01.7-cam.norms.md`](../01.7-cam.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 7 of lesson V/01 «Камера» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090 addendum (2), (4)). [Lecture](../../01-cam.md),
[instructions](../01-cam.README.md), the manifest [`cam.subsystem.yaml`](../../cam.subsystem.yaml). The platform — the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (a stream outside the licence count — `b41527f`).

The licence is a policy of the subsystem; the platform knows nothing about it (ADR-0080 addendum (20), (21); the decision on the request
[`2026-10-23-запрос-vms-лицензия.md`](../../../../decisions/inbox/2026-10-23-запрос-vms-лицензия.md) — a refusal with a workaround).
A camera is registered, but outside the licence it is not served: its row is created and edited like any other, the holder does not open it, the status
says «вне лицензии» (outside the licence). The chapter norms the consumer side on the domain: where the licence document lies, who counts
the licence for the whole domain and how the holder applies it. The issue of the licence by the vendor, what it contains and what it is bound to — М14
(ADR-0090 addendum (4)); protection against one's own operator is not a goal of the chapter (the document is edited by the operator of the domain).

Norm ids are `V/01.7-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the Platform module are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `issued`, `expires`, `as_of`, `until` are Unix seconds (a JSON number); the one who decides compares them with its own clock
(the domain worker — with the holder's clock, the camera holder — with its own).

**Data** (shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv); the chapter has no keys of its own beyond them):
the document — the field `licence` of the shared document of the domain (`domain/shared`, [P/08.9](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.9-domain.norms.md)
Н1.2); the decision — the rows of the carried book `domain/cam/licence/<член>` at the holder (written by the domain worker `camdomain`,
[P/03.7 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md)) and their copy `domain/cam/licence` at the member (carried by the agent,
P/09.1 Н1.4); the circle counter — the row `domain/cam/licence-count` and the state summary `domain/cam/state-summary` at the holder (`domain.kept`, written by `camdomain`, Н1.2); the reader is the camera holder (`worker.reads` of the canon: the copy `domain/cam/licence` and `domain/view` for `holds`; at the domain holder — the row
`domain/cam/licence/<CLUSTER>`, the rights to it are derived by the rights generator, P/02.5 Н1.4 — Н1.3).

### Н1.1 The licence document

The document field `cam.domain.shared: {name: licence, type: json, schema}` (P/02.1 Н1.2б; signing and delivery of the shared
document — P/08.9; editing — the door of the domain console `PUT /domain/shared`, P/10):

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `domain` | string | the domain the licence is issued to — the fingerprint of the domain root (`domain/root`, P/08.2) |
| `units` | integer ≥ 0 | how many cameras the domain MAY serve |
| `issued`, `expires` | number | issued; expires |
| `vendor_sig` | string | the vendor's signature over the other fields (canonical JSON without `vendor_sig`); the vendor key is in the product installation (lesson V/09), the signature form — М14 |

No document — licensing is not enabled: cameras are not limited. Removing the field also removes the `degraded` freeze: this is a limit; protection against one's own operator is not a goal of the chapter.

### Н1.2 The count for the whole domain

The count is made by the subsystem's worker on the domain `camdomain` (P/03.7 Н1.6; the unit `vms-camdomain.service` on every server with
`w2c-domain`, one is active per slot — [P/02.4 Н1.12](../../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md); `cam` has it because the manifest declares
the carried book `domain.books.licence`) by a circle of the view's parts: the head `domain/view` names `parts`, a pass reads the next row `domain/view-rows/<раздел>` and the book rows of its members, the budget is `VIEW_MEMBERS` ([P/08.1 Н1.4б](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.1-domain.norms.md); ADR-0113 addendum (9) item 5):

- **the camera set** — before sorting: one entry per camera `(кластер, id)` of the `cam` units of the view; a unit in the heartbeats of two
  workers of one member — two elements of the view (P/08.1 Н1.4 `order`: both run it, a move) — is one camera; `ref`, `view.main` and `view.enabled`
  come from the snapshot row, one per `(кластер, id)` (P/08.1 Н1.4 `duplicate`: with two rows — the one with the greater `revision`), and not from the
  number of elements;
- **one licence per main row of any kind** (V/01.1 Н1.1б): `camera`, `ipsrv`, `eventsrc`, `acfa` are counted as a camera; the units `cam/device_units`, children, `mic`, `spk` take no licence (ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 3, addendum (10) item 3);
- **who is counted:** a camera of the part's set with a non-empty `ref` (one `ref` at cameras of two members is the view dispute
  `contested`, P/08.1 Н1.4: their units are not in the view, they are not in the count and are not licensed until the `ref` is separated, chapter 6 Н1.2); a camera without `ref` is not
  in the count and is not licensed while licensing is enabled; the licence is per camera, not per stream: a unit with a non-empty `main`
  (the field `view.main` of the view, `domain.view` — chapter 6 Н1.1) is not in the count even if it has a `ref` (the common fields of a stream
  are inert, V/01.1 Н1.1а: the module form gives `ref` to a stream too); a disabled camera (`view.enabled: false`) is not in the count and holds no seat:
  disabled cameras MAY be created beyond the licence, they do not work; one enabled again is like a new one: no free seat — it waits;
- **seats** (`valid`, `grace`): licensed cameras hold a seat.
  **The occupied seats** of a part are the cameras of the set whose `ref` is in `licensed` of the previous decision of their member, and the `ref` of the row of a member `never` (its cameras are not in the view, their seat waits for them).
  The row `domain/cam/licence-count`, form P/03.7 Н1.6 `kept`, value `{used, parts, part, unread, as_of}`: `used` — the occupied seats of the domain, `null` until the first circle is closed; `parts` — `{<раздел>: {as_of, doc, state, used, waiting, total, offline, silent}}`; `doc` — the `rev` of the shared document; `state` — the state of the count when the part was read: per part, because a circle takes several passes; `unread` — the parts of this circle without a count: the view row or a member's decision was not read; `waiting` — the cameras of the part's count without a seat, including those revoked by this pass; `part` — the last part.
  A part absent from the head — its key is removed from `parts`, `used` decreases by its occupied seats, before any revocation: otherwise a departed part would revoke live cameras.
  A pass reads as many parts as the budget `VIEW_MEMBERS` fits for the period of the pass; a circle is members / budget.
  A part with the previous `as_of` of the head and the same `doc`, `state` in its key is not read, its numbers come from `parts`; with `valid`, `grace` this holds only if, in addition, `used` is not greater than `units` and the part has no waiting cameras, or there are no seats: otherwise a free seat would not reach a waiting camera, and a revocation would not happen.
  When the occupied seats of a part change, `used` changes by the difference with `parts[раздел].used`; no key — 0.
  A new camera, while `used` is less than `units`, takes a free seat at once, `used` + 1, otherwise it waits; for a part of the head without a key in `parts`, or with `as_of` `null` at `used` 0, the occupied seats are unknown: there are no new seats until it is passed.
  While `used` is greater than `units` (the licence was reduced), a pass revokes seats of the part, only of cameras of the set, within the part — the greater `ref` by UTF-8 bytes, `used` − 1.
  No counter row, or it is unreadable — `used` is `null`: no new seats and no revocations until the end of the first circle, the previous decisions stand.
  End of the circle, all parts of `parts` of the head passed: `used` is the sum of `parts`.
  The decisions of a part, the removals of book rows and the row go in one batch: a break does not leave a new `as_of` of the key without decisions, nor decisions without the key; a part with the same `as_of` would not be read (ADR-0113 addendum (24)).
  A change of the document changes the decisions of all members of the part; there are more of them than `BATCH_WRITES`: the batch is divided by the number of writes and by the body limit (P/01.1 Н1, Н2).
  The first batch — part of the decisions and the row, the part's key being the result of the pass with `as_of` `null` and the previous `part`; the middle ones — decisions; the last — the remainder and the row with the real `as_of` and `part` set to this part.
  A break between batches: the part is the first in the next pass, the occupied seats are counted from what was written, the difference repairs `used`, decisions that match are not written.
  The row without decisions of the part is written on a change of the result and once every N passes, not on every pass, N is up to the implementation: a break of it is repaired by the next circle.
  **Limit:** a decision is stale by up to a circle; the domain MAY temporarily exceed the licence by the cameras added during a circle;
  a break between batches, until the next pass, keeps the row with the result and only part of the decisions: on an issue `used` is overstated, a camera stands; on a revocation it is understated, a revoked camera works;
  even then the part is not read — the pass goes on: on a revocation, a seat freed during this time can go beyond `units` for a circle; reading the part catches up with the revocation;
  there is no order by `(ref, кластер)`: it requires the whole set;
  a camera moved to another member is counted as new at the new member: with a full licence the recording stands until the part of the old member is passed;
  a removed document removes the restriction only after a circle; the counter row is reset;
  a part with unknown occupied seats — no key, or `used` 0 with an unreadable view row or member decision — stops new seats of the domain until it is read; the decision row itself will not get repaired: this is visible by `unread` of the result;
  `as_of` is a number of seconds; the row at 4096 parts is about 475 KiB, an estimate, below the ceiling of 524288 bytes; if it does not fit, the write is refused, a log entry, the count goes on from memory until the end of the circle, the decisions are written without it: a break then makes them diverge again, and the divergence holds until the change of the part's `as_of`, not until the circle;
  the vectors of the circle — [`licence-circle`](../../vectors/licence-circle/README.md);
- **the state summary** (ADR-0113 addendum (10) item 3): the circle goes on even without a document. At the end of the circle — the row `domain/cam/state-summary` `{total, offline, silent, unread, at}`, `domain.kept`; `unread` — the parts of this circle without a count: the view row or a member's decision was not read. The count is by entries `(кластер, id)`; with two entries — the one with the greater `revision`, with an equal one — `running` before the others, otherwise the first in the order of the view (P/08.1 Н1.4 `order`); no `view.type` — `camera`; streams with a non-empty `main`, disabled cameras and rows not `type: camera` are outside the summary. `total` — all such entries. `silent` — `phase: failed`, the unit's `state` is `live` and there is no field `device_state`: the device answers; the view has no word for a broken link, this is the closest one, a camera without a stream will not say it (chapter 2). `offline` — the unit's `state` is not `live` or `phase` is not `running`, `idle`, `held`, `unlicensed`, except those that fell into `silent`: the numbers do not intersect. `at` — the end of the circle; the lag is a circle, about 20 min at 250 000 members: a limit, the form shows `at`. It is read by `backend`: `domain.kept_read`, `GET /domain/cam/kept/state-summary` → `{items, index}` (P/10 Н1.2а), the translation — with the contract of stage 7;
- **the state** (by the holder's clock):

| `state` | When | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| `valid` | the signature verifies with the vendor key, `domain` is this domain, now ≤ `expires` | occupied seats and free ones |
| `grace` | the same, `expires` < now ≤ `expires` + `GRACE` (30 days) | as `valid` |
| `degraded` | the signature verifies, `domain` is this one, now > `expires` + `GRACE`; or the signature does not verify or `domain` is foreign, with a previous decision | the set of licensed cameras is **frozen** — the member's previous decision: work, but do not add; no previous decision (the first count of a long-expired licence) — `licensed: []`, license no one in advance |
| `invalid` | the signature does not verify or `domain` is foreign, and there is no previous decision | not a single licensed camera |
| `none` | no document | no book: not limited |

**The previous decision** is the book row `domain/cam/licence/<член>` at the holder (durable: it survives a restart of the domain worker
and a slot handover, P/03.7 Н1.6), not process memory; its `licensed` with `state` `valid`, `grace` or `degraded`.
No row (the first count, a new member) — there is no previous decision. The row exists but the decision is unreadable (the element `decision` or
its `value` is not in form, the store did not answer) — the previous decision is **unread, not absent**: the domain worker
does not write this member's row on this pass (the former one stays), writes a log entry and counts it again on the next pass; the pass does not know which seats are occupied, so it does not hand them out to the waiting cameras. The view did not answer or is unreadable — the pass does not count, does not touch the book rows, a log entry; a stale view is counted as read. The first start, a restart and a slot handover count in the same way:
read the document, the view and the book rows, decide by the table; a member of the view `never` (P/08.1 Н1.3: after a move or a handover — "not yet heard", its units are not in the view) with `valid`, `grace` gets no row, the former one stays: an empty set does not mean that it has no cameras. The document is replaced — the count is made anew by the table: `valid`,
`grace` — seats by the new `units`; a new document long expired or not verifying — `degraded` with the previous decision
(or `[]`, `invalid` without it).

- **the book:** the row `domain/cam/licence/<член>` in the form of a platform book row (P/03.7 Н1.6 `carried_book` — delivery and
  form as for a collected book, P/08.5 Н1.3): the element `""` — `{missing: [], missing_count: 0}`, the element `decision` —
  `{entries: [{member: <держатель>, item: "decision", value: <решение>}]}` (`member` is the cluster — the domain holder
  writing the row); the decision is the `value` of the single element:
  `{state, units, as_of, until?, licensed: [<ref>…]}` — only the `ref` of the cameras of this member; `units` — from the document if
  the signature and `domain` matched, otherwise from the previous decision, without it — 0; `until` — the end of `grace`, `expires` + `GRACE`: present
  when the signature and `domain` matched (`valid`, `grace`, `degraded` by time), otherwise the field is absent; `as_of` changes only
  together with the content; the family of the value is [`licence-decision`](../../data/licence-decision.schema.json) (the VMS catalogue, place
  `/*/entries/*/value`). The row is written when the content has changed (comparison without `as_of`, not on every pass): the copy at the
  member is durable (at a camera-cluster — flash, V/01.6 Н1.5).

### Н1.3 Application at the member

**Where the decision is.** If the cluster holds the domain (`holds` of the object `domain/view`, as for the book door P/06.7 Н1.1), the camera holder
reads the row of its own cluster `domain/cam/licence/<CLUSTER>` (the holder's cluster has no copy — P/08.5 Н1.4, P/09.1 Н1.4
`member_row`); otherwise — the copy `domain/cam/licence` (carried by the agent). The keys are in `worker.reads` of the manifest canon. The view did not
answer or is unreadable — the decision is not read (the third item below), the key is not chosen; only a confirmed absence
of the view makes `holds` false.

**Reading — by the [observation contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/observation.md):**
- no row — the store answered, there is no key (outside a domain; licensing is not enabled — the book is deleted at `none`; the book
  has not yet reached a new member; a domain move did not bring the row of the new holder) — not limited;
- the row is read — the holder holds a camera only if its `ref` is in `licensed` (another stream — by the `ref` of its camera,
  the row `main`); otherwise it does not open it: phase `unlicensed`, `reason` «вне лицензии: `<state>`, `<units>` единиц» (V/01.2
  Н1.4; a disabled camera — phase `pending`, V/01.2 Н1.1);
- the decision is expected but not read — the store did not answer (`lapse`) or the row exists but is unreadable (the outcome
  `<s>worker` of the book copy, P/09.1 Н1.4 `member_row`: it does not use the book in this pass) — the holder holds by the last decision read
  since start; if none has been read since start, cameras are not started (`unlicensed`, `reason` «решение лицензии не читается»
  (the licence decision is unreadable)), not "not limited";
- **outside the licence it is not served:** an `unlicensed` camera — commands are `refused` «camera is not licensed» (chapter 4), the holder's door
  answers 404 on its path (chapter 5), it has no element of the book `sources` (chapter 6);
- the connection with the domain is lost — the holder lives by the last copy: licensed cameras go on working, new cameras will not appear in the
  copy — work, but do not add; the holder does not judge the copy's deadline (`until`) itself — the domain worker decides it
  by the next count.

**Creation is not refused:** the unit door accepts a camera beyond the licence (P/06.1 — the licence is unknown to it); the camera
touches no one and is not served until it enters the licence (another one was deleted or disabled, or its `ref` was removed, or the licence was extended).

## Н3 Pass

### Count (the pass of the domain worker)

```
read the document licence (domain/shared), the head domain/view, the row domain/cam/licence-count; parts — the next after part in parts of the head, by the budget VIEW_MEMBERS
no counter | unreadable → used null: no new seats and no revocations, occupied seats are held, log
remove keys of parts that are not from the head, used less by their occupied seats
a part with the previous as_of, the same doc, state of the key; with valid | grace also used ≤ units, no waiting or no seats → numbers from parts of the row, do not read the part
read domain/view-rows/<part> and the book rows of its members (previous decisions)
view did not answer | unreadable, no row of the part → no decisions for the part: do not touch the book rows, log, unread + 1, as_of of the key null; part moves on, the part is read on the next circle; a stale row — as read
camera set of the part: by (cluster, id) — one, with two entries the one with the greater revision, with an equal one running first, otherwise the first; ref, view.main, view.enabled, view.type, phase, state, device_state — from it; a disabled one — outside the count
total, offline, silent of the part — by the set, Н1.2 "state summary"
no document → state none; delete the book rows of the part (book absent), used 0
check vendor_sig with the vendor key, and domain
  does not verify → degraded (the member has a previous decision) | invalid (it has not)
  verifies → by the clock: valid | grace | degraded
valid | grace → occupied of the part: cameras of the set with ref in licensed of the previous decision of their member, ref of the rows of members never; used + (occupied − parts[part].used, no key — 0);
  used > units → revoke the greater ref of the cameras of the part's set, used − 1; all decisions of the part read, used not null, all parts of the head in parts or it is this one → new ones by ref: used < units → seat, used + 1; the others wait
degraded → the previous decision of the member, none → []; invalid → []
parts of the row: as_of, doc, state, used, waiting, total, offline, silent of the part; a member decision unreadable → occupied and waiting as before, no key — 0, as_of null, unread + 1, no new seats: the part is read again
end of the circle → used — sum of parts; domain/cam/state-summary — sums, unread, at; unread of the row 0
for each member of the part: previous decision unreadable → do not write the row (the former stays), log;
  valid | grace, member never → do not write the row (the former stays); otherwise the decision
  {state, units, until?, licensed — its ref} ≠ the previous value without as_of → into the part's batch (element decision, value — the decision with a new as_of)
the part's batch with decisions or removals: decisions and the counter row atomically; does not fit (BATCH_WRITES, body limit) → first: part of the decisions and the row, the part's key — result of the pass, as_of null, part previous; middle: decisions; last: remainder and the row with as_of, part = this part
no decisions and no removals → the row on a change of the result and once every N passes
```

### Application (the pass of the holder)

```
domain/view did not answer | unreadable → as "did not answer" below, the key is not chosen; no view → holds false
decision row: holds (domain/view) → domain/cam/licence/<CLUSTER>, otherwise the copy domain/cam/licence
no row (an answer without the key) → not limited
did not answer | unreadable → the last decision read since start; none → do not hold, unlicensed
otherwise: a camera whose ref ∉ licensed → do not hold (stop_unit, if it was running); phase unlicensed, reason
```

**Races.** A camera added or enabled in a member between counts — until the next count its `ref` is not in the copy: it is not served until
the domain view shows it and the domain worker counts it (seconds to half a minute, the agent's pass). Two domain holders
(a handover): the carried book is written by one domain worker (the slot `domain/cam/worker`, P/03.7 Н1.6); the new holder takes
the previous decisions from the book rows, and a member it has not yet heard keeps its own row: its `ref` occupy seats (Н1.2), the heard members do not get them, and no camera stops in the process. A new member of the domain is not limited until the first copy of the book (no row — Н1.3), seconds to half a minute; likewise a new holder whose row the move did not bring — until the first count after its report.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a camera outside the licence | not served, `phase: unlicensed`, `reason` | heartbeat, `/cameras` | extend the licence, delete or disable another one, remove its `ref` |
| the signature or the domain does not match | `state: degraded` / `invalid` in the book | the domain view (the book), the cameras' heartbeat | a new document from the vendor |
| the licence expired beyond `GRACE` | `state: degraded`: the set is frozen (no previous decision — `licensed: []`) | likewise | renew |
| a member's previous decision is unreadable (at the domain worker) | the member's row is not rewritten on this pass, log | log | repair the row (next pass) |
| the licence decision is unreadable, none read since start | cameras are not started, `unlicensed` | heartbeat | repair the row (the agent will rewrite the copy) |
