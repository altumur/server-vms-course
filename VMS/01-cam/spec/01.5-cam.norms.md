# V/01.5 — The device archive and its door: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/01.5-cam.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 5 of lesson V/01 Camera (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/01-cam.md`, for people, not in the export),
[HTTP contract](01-cam.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](01-cam.README.md), [acceptance](../acceptance/5/README.md).
The platform — the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the archive at the camera, N1.1a — `41d3ec8`).

A camera can keep its video itself — on a card, in a recorder (NVR). The holder — the only process that has the
device open — serves this archive: a coverage summary in the heartbeat and bytes through the door. The holder serves what the
device already has; the recording is created by the one who writes (`rec`, lesson V/02): the holder does not copy the archive, does not write recordings and
does not hold volumes. The consumers of the door are the recorder (catch-up recording, the piece `.device.mp4`; V/02), detection over the device archive
(V/06), a task over an interval (V/05); their behaviour is their lessons (addendum (4) item 2). The page does not go to the holder: the bytes go
through the recording door of `rec` (ADR-0015).

Norm ids are `V/01.5-MM`.

## N0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the Platform module are [P/01.1 N0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## N1 Form

**Time.** `from`, `to`, `since`, `said_at` — Unix seconds (a JSON number); `from` is inclusive, `to` is not.

**Data** (the common families `<sub>/…` of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv); the chapter has no keys of its own beyond
them): writes — the heartbeat `cam/heartbeats/<worker>` (the fields of N1.1, N1.4; P/03.4 N1.1). The archive bytes are at the device, not in the
store and not on the disk of the holder.

### N1.1 Summary in the heartbeat

In the element of `status[]` of the camera (V/01.2 N1.4), when the coverage is known:

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `coverage` | `{from, to, fragments, said_at?}` | the summary of the device archive for this channel: from, to, the number of pieces; `said_at` — when the device said it, if it did not answer in this round (V/01.4 N1.3) |
| `playback_url` | `<scheme>://<host>:<port>/playback/<id>` | bytes (N1.3) |
| `index_url` | `<scheme>://<host>:<port>/recordings/<id>` | the list of pieces (N1.3) |

The coverage is not known (the device did not say, or there is no archive) — none of the three fields. The index is not put into the heartbeat:
the heartbeat is a summary under the object ceiling (P/05 N1.1), the list is through the door. The summary is asked once per heartbeat by the common round of
questions (V/01.4 N1.3); the timeline of the page does not touch the device. A camera `held` (`live: on-demand`) publishes the summary the same way:
the device archive does not depend on the live stream.

### N1.1a The archive is at the camera

The device archive is the archive of the camera (V/01.1 N1.1a): `coverage`, `playback_url`, `index_url` — only in the element of the camera
(of the main stream); a non-main stream does not publish a device archive of its own, the door on its id — 404 (N4). What the device writes
to the archive (the main or another stream of the channel) is its setting, not a row of `cam`; the `detedge` traversal and the page look at the archive
of the camera (V/06.1, V/11.16); the recording door of a non-main stream does not show it (V/02.5 N1.5), catch-up recording of such a recording does not take it (V/02.8 N1.1).

### N1.2 Door address

The rule of the door address of a process is [P/03.4 N1.7](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) (`kit`): with the kit of the role `camworker` — the addresses by `SERVER_NAME`,
`https://<SERVER_NAME>:<port>` in `playback_url` and `index_url`; without a kit — the loopback `http://127.0.0.1:<port>`; another
`PLAYBACK_HOST` (the subsystem file, optional) or `0.0.0.0` — start refusal, code 2. The port is `PLAYBACK_PORT` of the subsystem file (V/01.2 N1.7; not set or 0 —
the OS). All routes of the holder's door are admittance by role (N2): without a kit they answer 401 `no_role` (a log line at
start). A consumer on another server does not take an address on the loopback.

### N1.3 Routes

Only `GET`, for `talk` — `POST`; another method on a route — 405, another path — 404 `{"error": "no such path", "detail"}` (the refusal body — N4).
JSON answers are `application/json`. The request parsing and the order of checks — N2.

| Route | Answer 200 |
|---|---|
| `GET /devices` | a list, one element per device of the holder's group: `{device, state?, since?, channels?, known, unimported?, playbacks?, max_playbacks?, reads_stuck?, can?, same_serial_as?, why?}` (N1.4) |
| `GET /recordings/<id>?from=&to=` | `{"spans": [{"from", "to"}]}` — the pieces of the device archive, trimmed to `[from, to)`; the defaults of `from`, `to` — N2 |
| `GET /playback/<id>?from=&to=` | archive bytes `video/mp4` for `[from, to)` (defaults — N2), trimmed by the coverage (the header `X-Coverage-From` — the lower edge of the coverage by which it is trimmed, in seconds); HTTP/1.1 — `Transfer-Encoding: chunked`, without `Content-Length`; `Range` is not supported — a jump inside the interval is a new request. Trimming is by the edges of the summary (N1.1). A hole inside the edges is not a refusal: there are no bytes for it; not a single byte for the whole interval — 200 without bytes, with the last chunked piece |
| `POST /talk/<id>/<number>?user=<name>` | a conversation (V/01.4 N1.4a): the body is SMPL sound records in a row (the form — the contract `obsd` of V/02: `major = 3`, `sub`, times; `subtype` is not read, the codec — `talk` of the heartbeat, `body` — a frame of this codec, AAC — an ADTS frame), HTTP/1.1 chunked, while the conversation goes on; the 200 headers go at once, as soon as the occupancy is checked; the answer ends with the conversation, there is no body; `<id>` — the owner of `spk`, `user` — who speaks |

The bytes are as the device gives them (the holder does not repackage them). A refusal after the first byte is an answer without the last
chunked piece: the client sees a break, not an end.

### N1.4 Devices of the door

An element of `GET /devices` (it is also the record of the device in `devices` of the heartbeat, V/01.2 N1.4):

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `device` | the host (V/01.4 N1.4) |
| `state` | `slow` \| `failed` \| `opening` — no field if the device answers in time (V/01.4 N1.3) |
| `since` | the start of the unanswered question or of the failed opening |
| `channels`, `playbacks` | the number of channels and of occupied playback sessions; did not answer — no fields («0 channels» would be untrue) |
| `known`, `unimported` | the ids of the assignment rows of this host, streams too; the names of channels in the words of the driver that `source` of none of these rows points to (rows are created by the operator); the parsing of the address — the driver (V/01.4 Implementation decides) |
| `max_playbacks` | the ceiling of playback sessions said by the device |
| `reads_stuck` | cameras whose abandoned read has not returned (N3) |
| `can`, `same_serial_as` | V/01.4 N1.4 |
| `why` | for `failed` — the words of the opening; the opening deadline has passed (V/01.4 N1.3) — «no answer in `<DEVICE_OPEN_WAIT>` s» |

### N1.5 Door limits

| Name | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `PLAYBACK_BUDGET` | 64 MiB | archive bytes in transit for the whole door; waiting for space — up to 5 s |
| `PLAYBACK_PIECE` | ≤ 60 s, ≈ 4 MiB | a piece of one read from the device: as many seconds as 4 MiB at the pace of the previous piece, within [1, 60]; the first — 2 s; to a slow client (it took the previous piece longer than 1 s) — not more than 4 MiB and `max(256 KiB, pace × 10 s)`, the pace — bytes of this piece per second |
| `DOOR_ASK_WAIT` | 5 s | waiting for the opening of a recording and for the answer to a door question |
| `PLAYBACK_STALL` | 10 s | the timeout for a portion of bytes from the device |
| `PLAYBACK_DEVICE_PACE` | 0.5 s of recording per second | slower — the device «does not answer» |
| `DOOR_READS_PER_DEVICE`, `DOOR_READS` | 8, 32 | simultaneous reads per device and per door |
| `DOOR_STUCK_CHANNELS` | 2 | stuck channels — and the device is stuck as a whole |
| `DOOR_ASKS_PER_DEVICE` | 4 | door questions on the line of the device |
| `PLAYBACK_SPARE` | 1 | device sessions that the door does not give to the role `detedgeworker`: free ones (`max_playbacks` minus those opened by the door and the `playbacks` said by the device) not more than `PLAYBACK_SPARE` — `/playback` of this role 503 (N3); the reserve is for the operator and the recorder; clients of the device that bypass the cluster are not visible to the door; `max_playbacks` = 1 — the traversal never reads this device (V/06.2 N1.4); `max_playbacks` not said — no reserve is kept |
| `PLAYBACK_PER_CLIENT` | 2 | simultaneous `/playback` of one camera from one client: one process — one interval; the client is the subject of the role leaf (CN `<role>.<server>`); without a kit (loopback) — all clients are one |
| `TALK_SILENT` | 8 s | the route `talk` without a frame — the receiver is hung up: the gateway server might have vanished without a break; the gateway sends silence frames (V/03.4 N1.5) |

Connections, the deadline of the request line and headers, the client pace and the body limit are the common door limits of a process
[P/03.4 N1.7](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) `door_limits` (32 connections, 8 per address, 5 s, ≥ 64 KiB/s
after 30 s, body 1 MiB; 503 `busy` with `Retry-After: 1`); the holder's door has no other values, except for `talk`: `BODY` and `PACE_FLOOR` do not act on it (`door_limits.other`), the body goes at the pace of speech until the end of the conversation.

The holder's door has no kinds and fields of the door log of its own beyond the common ones (P/03.4 N1.7 `journal`).

## N2 Doors

The holder's door is the HTTP contract [`01-cam.api.openapi.yaml`](01-cam.api.openapi.yaml): `GET /devices`, `/recordings/{id}`,
`/playback/{id}`, `POST /talk/{id}/{number}`. The identity of the client is the role certificate, the server side of the class `mixed` of the
[role certificate contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/role-certificate.md) as is, routes `{name, role}`
(ADR-0075 window 2); there is no separate strict listener. With a kit the holder requests a client certificate; the trust set is the cluster's own `ca.pem`, `ca.next.pem`
(`W2C_TLS_DIR`): the consumers of the door are processes of its own cluster, the CAs of members are not in the set. The admitted roles are
`recworker`, `detjobworker`, `detedgeworker`, for `talk` — only `liveworker`. The outcomes are the words of the class `role` (`refusal`), the body `{error, detail?}`:
- a chain rejected by TLS (a foreign CA, a wrong chain or validity period, a leaf without `clientAuth`) — a handshake refusal, no HTTP;
- no client certificate — 401 `no_role`; not exactly one role in the SAN of the leaf — 403 `no_role`;
- a role not among the admitted ones — 403 `wrong_role`; a signature of the leaf or of the CA outside the list `sig` or a serial in `revoked` — 403 `bad_chain`.

Without a kit — the loopback, the `role` routes are closed: 401 `no_role` (N1.2; ADR-0091 addendum (8)). The console door token (P/03.4 N3
door token at the holder) is not applied at the door of the holder `cam`: `door.routes` of the manifest canon is four routes
(`devices`, `recordings`, `playback`, `talk`) only with roles `role` (as at `rec`), there are no token routes — the page does not come here.

**Request parsing** is the common parsing of [P/01.1 N2.1](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md) (through
[P/03.4 N1.7](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) `door_limits`): query as
`application/x-www-form-urlencoded`, the request line and headers, the body frame, early refusal — as there. The deviations of the holder's
door:
- **the path** is compared with the routes byte for byte, without decoding and normalization: `/recordings/1/`, `//devices`,
  `/devices/` are not routes, 404 `no such path` (not `404 badrequest` of P/01.1); `<id>` is the last segment as written:
  an empty `<id>` is not a route, 404 `no such path`; a non-empty segment not from the assignment, including one that is not digits, — 404 `no device archive`;
- **a method** other than `GET` on a route (`HEAD` included), for `talk` other than `POST` — 405; an unknown path — 404 with any method;
- **`user`** of the route `talk` — absent, empty, repeated, a value not in UTF-8 — 400 `bad query`; `<number>` not an integer ≥ 1 — 404 `no call`;
- **`from`, `to`** — Unix seconds, the notation of a JSON number (RFC 8259 `number`), a finite value: a repeated parameter, an empty
  value (`from=`), another notation (`NaN`, `inf`, `Infinity`, `0x10`) or a non-finite value after parsing (`1e400`) — 400
  `bad range`; `from > to` (both given) — 400 `bad range`; `from == to` — an empty interval (`/recordings` — `{"spans": []}`,
  `/playback` — 404 `no device archive`, empty after trimming); other query parameters and the body of `GET` are not read;
- **defaults** (for both routes): without `from` — the start of the archive, without `to` — the end of the archive (the interval is the whole coverage).

The order of checks: path (404) → method (405) → admittance (handshake; 401, 403) → query (400) → the procedure of N3. Without a kit
an unknown path — 404, a route — 401 `no_role`.

## N3 Procedures

### Serve bytes (`GET /playback/<id>`)

```
the camera is not in the holder's assignment | the camera is unlicensed (V/01.7 N1.3)   → 404 "no device archive"
the camera has no device (driverpack://file) | the id of a non-main stream (N1.1a)   → 404 "no device archive"
the device is not open: it is opening or the opening was refused (device_state opening) → 503 "device opening"
its device has no archive or no coverage                              → 404 "no device archive"
the camera is in reads_stuck | the device is stuck | DOOR_ASKS_PER_DEVICE questions on the line → 503 "device busy"
PLAYBACK_PER_CLIENT exceeded                                          → 503 "busy": one process — one interval
role detedgeworker and free device sessions ≤ PLAYBACK_SPARE         → 503 "device busy", detail "the last session is kept for the operator and the recorder"
trim [from, to) by the edges of the coverage, the edge — in the header X-Coverage-From; empty → 404 "no device archive"
before the first byte: open a device session for the first piece (2 s)
  reads of the device at DOOR_READS_PER_DEVICE or of the door at DOOR_READS → 503 "device busy"
  the device sessions have run out (max_playbacks)                    → 503 "device busy", detail — the words of the device «device <host>: N playback sessions, all in use»
  the recording did not open within DOOR_ASK_WAIT | no portion for PLAYBACK_STALL | pace < PLAYBACK_DEVICE_PACE → 503 "device busy"
  no space in PLAYBACK_BUDGET within 5 s                              → 503 "busy", detail "this door holds … bytes at once — retry"
200 chunked; for each next piece: a session per piece, closed before serving to the client; the budget is taken when
  the device has opened the recording: the planned size of the piece, after the read — a correction to the actual one; any refusal after the first
  byte — a break without the last piece
a piece that the device does not have (the read ended cleanly, without bytes) is not a refusal: the next piece
the read was abandoned and has not returned → the camera is in reads_stuck, the count door_reads_stuck of the heartbeat; two such cameras of a device
  or a question to the whole device longer than DOOR_ASK_WAIT → the device is stuck as a whole (otherwise the neighbouring channels are read)
abandoned — a deadline has passed: DOOR_ASK_WAIT, PLAYBACK_STALL or the pace; the session is closed by the read itself when it returns;
  returned → the camera leaves reads_stuck, there is no timer
```

### Conversation (`POST /talk/<id>/<number>`)

```
the call of the number <number> of the owner <id> is not accepted: no occupancy or no performed yet (V/01.4 N1.4a) → 404 "no call": the request is not performed or the deadline has passed
occupied for another by | the route of this occupancy is already open → 409 "busy"
200 headers; each record of the body → PushAudioFrame; a record not by the form, the end of the body, a break, no frame for TALK_SILENT → HangUp, the occupancy is released
the device has hung up | the occupancy is released otherwise (V/01.4 N1.4a) → the end of the answer
```

### List and devices (`GET /recordings/<id>`, `GET /devices`)

`/recordings` asks the device for the list without a playback session; the coverage and the list are awaited together, within one
deadline `DOOR_ASK_WAIT`. The refusals are as for `/playback` (404, 503 «device opening», «device busy»), no coverage — 404, not 501; an interval outside
the coverage — 200 `{"spans": []}`. After these refusals, before the question to the device: the driver cannot list — 501
`"no index"`: this is not an empty list (`[]` — the device has no pieces). `/devices` always answers, from what the
devices have said (V/01.4 N1.3), without new questions.

**Races.** The camera moved to another holder between the reading of the heartbeat by the consumer and the request — 404 at the former one; the consumer
rereads the heartbeat. Two consumers of one camera — two reads within `DOOR_READS_PER_DEVICE` and the device sessions;
the extra one gets 503 with the words of the device.

## N4 Refusals

The closed list is [`data/refusal-words.tsv`](../data/refusal-words.tsv), the door `holder`. `error` is a constant word, one per class: names, numbers and words of the device are in `detail` (ADR-0094 addendum (13)).

| Cause | Code | `error` | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| no camera, archive, coverage; empty after trimming by the edges (`/playback`); the id of a non-main stream of the camera (N1.1a); the camera is `unlicensed` (V/01.7 N1.3) | 404 | `no device archive` | reread the heartbeat; the archive is at the camera |
| `from`/`to` not by N2: repeated, empty, not a number, `from > to` | 400 | `bad range` | — |
| `user` of the route `talk` not by N2 | 400 | `bad query` | — |
| no conversation: no occupancy, the deadline has passed, `<number>` not by the form | 404 | `no call` | the request `talk` again |
| another one speaks; the route of this conversation is already open | 409 | `busy` | wait for the end of the conversation |
| the device is not open: it is opening or the opening was refused | 503 | `device opening` | later |
| stuck, deadline, pace, the line of questions, reads | 503 | `device busy` (`detail` — what exactly; «ask again») | later |
| the device sessions have run out | 503 | `device busy` (`detail` — the words of the device) | later |
| the door budget | 503 | `busy` (`detail` — how much the door holds, «retry») | later |
| `PLAYBACK_PER_CLIENT` | 503 | `busy` | one interval at a time |
| the session reserve `PLAYBACK_SPARE` (the role `detedgeworker`) | 503 | `device busy` (`detail` — «the last session is kept for the operator and the recorder») | the next pass (V/06.2 N1.4) |
| door limits (P/03.4 N1.7) | 503, `Retry-After: 1` | `busy` | later |
| a body larger than `BODY` (P/03.4 N1.7) | 413 before reading | `toolarge` (`detail` — the limit) | — |
| the driver does not list | 501 | `no index` | take the summary |
| the chain is rejected by TLS (with a kit) | — | handshake refused | — |
| no client certificate; without a kit — any route | 401 | `no_role` | a role kit |
| the role is not admitted; not exactly one role in SAN; a signature outside `sig` or a serial in `revoked` | 403 | `wrong_role`; `no_role`; `bad_chain` | — |
| another method / path | 405 / 404 | `method not allowed` / `no such path` | — |

The refusal body is `{error, detail}`.

## N5 Acceptance

Scenarios — [`../acceptance/5/scenarios.tsv`](../acceptance/5/scenarios.tsv), the stand — [README](../acceptance/5/README.md): the stand
of chapter 2 without certificate kits (the routes of the holder — 401 `no_role`), a file camera without a device archive. The run — after the first generation
of stage C that has passed the acceptance of the platform (ADR-0090 addendum (8), (9)).

Checked by reading: bytes, pieces, budgets, getting stuck, device sessions, `501 no index`, the outcomes of the class `mixed`
(handshake, 401, 403) — a device double with an archive and a TLS stand are needed (the common TLS stand of the platform — for the class `mixed`).

## Implementation decides

The implementation MUST name in its description: **the device archive driver** (summary, list, reading of an interval, playback
sessions; the driver distinguishes «no minutes» from a read refusal; with a list a hole can be skipped without a session) and by what means the holder
serves bytes in parts at the pace of the client.
