### Н1.1 Camera

The unit of the subsystem `cam` is a **stream** of a device channel held by one worker `camworker`: the row `cam/cameras/<id>`,
`unit.id: numeric` — the id is issued by the units door from the counter `cam/next_id` (P/06.1 Н1.3; an id in the `POST` body is a refusal of P/06.1); in a batch `POST /console/batch` the caller names the number together with the counter row (P/06.1 Н2 «Пакетная дверь»; V/11.4 Н1.2).
A **camera** is its main stream (`main` is empty); the other streams of a camera — Н1.1а. A row is not only a camera: what it is
is said by `type` (Н1.1б).

| Field | Type and declaration | Meaning | Who reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, `cam{id}` | the camera name for people; without a name — `cam<id>` | console, snapshot, page |
| `main` | `string`, `fixed`, `ref: cam/cameras`, `must_match: {type: type}` | the id of the camera's main stream; empty — the row is the camera itself (Н1.1а); the `about` of the manifest | holder (chapter 2), grants, log (`of`) |
| `profile` | `string`, `main` | the stream name, any word of the operator | page (V/11), snapshot |
| `source` | `url`, `required`, `unique: canonical`, `schemes`, `credentials`, `secret_in` | the address of the device channel; the device key for `group_by` (Н1.3) | holder (chapter 2), door (P/06.1, P/06.2) |
| `enabled` | `bool`, `true` | the holder holds only an enabled camera | holder |
| `priority` | `int`, `100` | the operator's word on the order of cameras; placement and the holder do not read it | snapshot, console |
| `labels` | `list` | the networks from which the camera address is visible (`vlan:cctv-a`) | placement (`constraint`, Н1.3) |
| `alarms` | `list`, `inherit: []`, `merge: union` | the event kinds that are an alarm on this device | holder: the class of the event row (chapter 2); inheritance and merging — [P/06.7 Н1.14](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.7-console.norms.md) |
| `ref` | `string`, `unique: true` | the camera name for the layer above (the domain); an empty `ref` does not take part in `unique` ([row check contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md) RC-17) | the section `domain:` (chapter 6) |
| `cred_username` | `string` | the device login | holder |
| `cred_secret` | `string`, `bound_to: [source]` | the device password — a secret | holder (P/03.5) |
| `live` | `string`, `enum: [always, on-demand]`, `always` | `always` — the stream on a consumer, without one the phase `idle`; `on-demand` — the stream on a consumer, a viewer too, without one the phase `held`; the difference is only the phase word (Н1.1; ADR-0103 addendum (7) item 3, (8) item 2) | holder (chapter 2) |
| `type` | `string`, `enum: [camera, ipsrv, eventsrc, acfa]`, `camera`, `fixed` | the row kind (Н1.1б); another word is a door refusal by `enum`, an edit — `fixed` (P/06.1 Н4) | holder (chapter 2), `backend` (V/11), page |
| `lat`, `lon`, `azimuth` | `json`, a number or `null` | where it stands and where it looks: at a camera and an `ipsrv` row; `null` — not stated, `0` — a value; the door judges only the form | `backend`, page; the holder does not read |
| `short` | `string` | a number for display in the tree, a word for people | `backend`, page; the holder does not read |
| `mac` | `string` | the MAC address of an `ipsrv` row, the operator's word; it does not replace the device `identity`. A camera's MAC comes only from discovery and is not stored in the row | `backend`; the holder does not read |
| `camera` | `string`, `ref: cam/cameras` | at `eventsrc`: the id of the camera over whose video the lines go (Н1.1г) | holder, page (V/11.17), `backend` |
| `words` | `json`, a list `{msg, color?, bg?}` | at `eventsrc`: the words the holder looks for, and their colours (Н1.1г) | holder, page, `backend` |
| `overlay_delay_ms`, `overlay_duration_sec` | `int` ≥ 0 | at `eventsrc`: the delay before a line is shown, ms, and how long to keep it, s; `0` — until the next line | page, `backend`; the holder does not read |
| `resolution` | `string`, `schema`: empty or `<ширина>x<высота>` | a device setting for this stream: the holder passes it to the driver (chapter 2 Н1.2а); empty — the setting is not touched; the list of options — `resolutions` (Н1.2) | holder, `live` (V/03.3), `backend` |
| `fps` | `int` ≥ 0 | frames per second, a device setting like `resolution`; `0` — not touched | holder, `backend` |

The login of an `ipsrv` row is its `cred_username`. The operator writes these fields through the units door; a value out of form in the store is unread, not
absent: it is not replaced by the default, a reader does not give the field out, the holder — chapter 2 Н1.2а.

A camera `live: always` without a consumer holds the device without a stream (`idle`, chapter 2 Н1.2в): the stream is opened on a consumer. A camera `live: on-demand` without a consumer holds the device without a stream (`held`, chapter 2). The consumers are the same as for `always`: a viewer (V/03.2),
recording (V/02.4), a detector on the stream (V/04.2), another cluster, the card ring (chapter 2 Н1.2в); the stream is opened on the first one and closed `IDLE_AFTER`
after the last one, the phase is `held` again. The decision of «Спецификация VMS» on ADR-0103 addendum (7) item 3, (8) item 2 is one for all consumers: a refusal on save cannot be carried out, `live` is written by the tables door of `cam`, recordings and detectors by their own doors, the consolem contract does not carry the field, and there is no one to judge them together. The price: `on-demand` differs from `always` only by the phase without a consumer, `held` instead of `idle`.

### Н1.1а Camera streams

The owner's decision of 2026-10-26 (backlog item «Камера с профилями», items 1, 3 («Б»)): a camera has any number of streams, each is its own
unit `cam`; this requires no platform mechanisms beyond the declared ones.

- **Camera = main stream.** A row with an empty `main`. What is common to the camera — `name`, `labels`, `alarms`, `ref`,
  `priority`, `lat`, `lon`, `azimuth`, `short`, the device commands (chapter 4), the device events and alarms (chapter 2 Н1.5) — is **only**
  in it: an edit of the common part is one write.
- **The password is at the camera.** `cred_username`, `cred_secret` of the camera row; another stream on the same host takes them from its
  camera (chapter 2 Н1.2). The channels of one device (a 16-channel recorder) are different cameras, each with its own password: a change of the
  device password is a write into every camera. This is the accepted price: the `devices` row is a finding of the holder, the operator does not
  write it (Н1.2), and `cam` has no password «once in the device row».
- **Another stream** is a row with `main: <id камеры>`: its own `source`, `profile`, `enabled`, `live`, `resolution`, `fps`; `type` is always
  `camera` (Н1.1б). A stream on a host other than the
  camera's (a transcoder, another address of the same device) opens with its own `cred_*`, without them it does not: the camera's password
  is not given to a foreign host (chapter 2 Н1.2).
- **The common fields of another stream are inert.** The module form (`display.form`, one per unit `cam`) offers them to a stream too, the door
  accepts them; VMS does not read them at a stream and does not stop the stream because of them:
  - the holder takes the common part from the camera row (chapter 2 Н1.1); the event class, the licence — by the camera;
  - the licence count does not count a row with a non-empty `main` (chapter 7 Н1.2; `main` is in `domain.view`, chapter 6 Н1.1); the contribution to the
    sources book and the push — only cameras (chapter 6 Н1.1);
  - what the platform reads acts as at any unit: the `labels` of a stream are a placement constraint (`labels-subset`,
    Н1.3; the device group is placed whole, P/04 Н1.3 `group_by`); `ref` is the unit name in the domain view
    ([P/08.1](../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.1-domain.norms.md) Н1.2): a stream with `ref` is visible there as a unit, without a licence and
    a book; `name`, `priority` — the snapshot and the module tree, words;
  - the tab «Потоки» does not offer the common fields to a stream (V/11.15 Н1.5).
- **`about: {sub: cam, field: main}`** — a stream is about its camera: the grant `unit:cam/<камера>` and `labels:L` (the camera labels)
  cover all its streams ([rights contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/may.md) steps 7, «7а»; [gate and
  pass contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) `scope`), the event rows of a stream — `of: cam/<камера>`
  ([event log contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/event-journal.md) step 2). The loader admits `about` on its own
  subsystem (P/02.1: `about.sub` is the name grammar; `about` is not an edge of the install order).
- **A camera has no `about`:** `main` is empty — a unit without `about`, judged by itself, the rows of its events — `of: cam/<id>`
  ([event log contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/event-journal.md) step 2: with an empty field value — the unit itself).
- **Recording and detector choose the stream** — the user does, in a form from the camera's streams; the page only
  suggests (the largest for recording: `size` of the holder heartbeat, chapter 2 Н1.4). Live video is chosen by `live` by the viewer's window:
  - the recording `rec` and the detector `det` name the camera (`cam`, their `about`) and the stream — `stream: {ref: cam/cameras, must_match:
    {main: cam}}`: the id of another stream of this camera, empty — the main one; a stream of another camera — 409 `must_match`; the id of the
    camera itself — also 409 (a camera's `main` is empty, and an empty target value equals nothing — [row check
    contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md)): the main stream is named only by an empty `stream`
    (V/02.1, V/04.1);
  - the `live` delivery is a row per camera (`live/streams/<cam>`, V/03.1 Н1.1); the viewer names the stream at the gateway door
    (`?stream=<id>`, V/03.3 Н1.2), the gateway delivers only a stream of this camera; not named — `live` chooses the stream by the size from the
    request (`resolution` of the row, otherwise `size` of the heartbeat; ADR-0104 item 2);
  - the `about` of a consumer is on the camera, not on the stream: the platform `about` is one-level, and an `about` on the stream would not give a grant
    on the camera over the recordings and detectors of another stream.
- **The scan task** (`detjob`) has no choice of its own: its stream is the stream of the recording `rec` that it reads (V/05.1 Н1.1).
- **The device archive traversal** (`detedge`) has no stream: the device archive is at the camera (V/01.5 Н1.1а; V/06.1 Н1.2).
- **The `cam` of a consumer is a camera.** That the row `cam/cameras/<cam>` is a camera (`main` is empty) the door does not check: `must_match`
  requires a field of the target to equal a field of the row ([row check contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md)
  `ref_and_must_match`), and «the target field is empty» the manifest does not express: the CEL relations of the form (P/02.1 Н1.4, `relations.json`) are
  loader rules over the manifest text, not a row check at the door. **Limit:** the door passes such a row; the recorder, the gateway and the detector do not take it: `waiting`, `reason` `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>`
  (V/02.4, V/03.2, V/04.2); the scan task and the traversal follow: the recording of such a camera has no archive (V/05.2), a stream has no device
  archive (V/06.2) — their former `reason`.
- **The stream holder** is, for now, the holder of all channels of the device (`group_by`, Н1.3): one connection to the device.
  A stream holder at its consumer (option «Б» of the backlog item) requires a group over several holders with a limit of device
  sessions — P/04 has no such mechanism: **limit**, one connection per device.
- **The camera streams** are shown by the form: `Device.streams` (V/11.3 Н1.3), the console tree is not needed; the witness page labels a stream with its camera (V/11.15 Н1.5).

**Default name.** `cam{id}`: the door substitutes the issued id at creation, an edit does not recompute the value
([P/02.1 Н1.3](../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md), [row check contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md) «Подготовка полей» step 3).

**Address and credentials.** One channel — one camera: there are no two live cameras with one address in the canonical spelling of
RFC 3986 (`unique: canonical`, the row check contract «Идентификатор и ограничения полей», ADR-0057; the canon does not drop the default
port). Spellings that the canon distinguishes while their channel is one — `rtsp://h/ch/1` and `rtsp://h:554/ch/1` — are
found by the holder: the second one is «device busy» in its heartbeat (chapter 2 Н1.2 «Тот же канал»). Login and password do not live in the address:
a `source` with them is a door refusal, they go into `cred_username` and `cred_secret` (`credentials`); where in a camera address the login and
password are — the rules `schemes` and `secret_in` of the manifest by the [address contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/url-credentials.md) («Схемы», «Адрес: где в нём логин и пароль»; owner —
P/06.2 Н1.1а); the refusal (`bad_url`) and the mask — there too. The schemes of `cam`:

| Scheme | Where the host is | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `driverpack` | the first path segment (`host: path`); the node `file` — without a host (`none`) | a device through DriverPack; `driverpack://file/<имя>` — a file as a camera (chapter 3) |
| `ipint` | by RFC 3986; there is no fragment (`#` is a character) | a product address |
| `rtsp`, `rtsps`, `http`, `https` | by RFC 3986 | a direct device address |
| `cfw` | the node `local` — without a host (`none`); `cfw://local:80` — the host `local` | the firmware of the camera itself (the camera as a cluster of one, chapter 6); only it writes the row; `cfw://local` byte for byte is the mark of a w2c camera in the domain (chapter 6 Н1.5) |

A `none` node is compared as written, with the port and userinfo, the host in it — in lower case (the address contract «Схемы»
`node`): `driverpack://FILE/x` is a file, `driverpack://file:1/x` is not a file; the holder reads the node the same way (chapter 3).

A change of `source` without a new `cred_secret` is a refusal `refused` by `bound_to` (P/06.2 Н1.1): the password belongs to the address.

### Н1.1б Row kind

`type` equals `Device.type` of the consolem contract, there is no translation of the word. It is set at creation and does not change: a row does not become
another thing. Connection and licence — at all four kinds (chapter 2 Н1.1, chapter 7 Н1.2), the rest differs:

| `type` | What it is | Frames, recording, detectors | Owner of children (chapter 4 Н1.4а) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `camera` | a camera, a device channel | yes | yes |
| `ipsrv` | the device as a whole: a recorder, a block of inputs and outputs, a device without a picture | no | yes: the device blocks |
| `eventsrc` | an event source (POS, Н1.1г) | no | no |
| `acfa` | an access control controller, its units — Н1.2б | no | no |

**Another stream is always `camera`.** `must_match: {type: type}` of the field `main` keeps the pair on one word: a row `type` different from the `type`
of the live row `main` — 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match`. Therefore the door does not create a stream, by default `camera`, at a row of another kind, and
does not give a camera a stream of another kind. The condition «a field of this row equals a word» the manifest does not express (P/02.1): a row with `main` on a row of the same
kind other than `camera`, and a row with `main` on a non-existent target pass the door, the holder does not hold them, the word is in the status (chapter 2 Н1.1): this is a limit.

Units `cam/device_units` exist on a main row of any kind (Н1.2б): an intercom with a reader is a `camera` row, one licence.
An `eventsrc`, `acfa` row has no children; at a recorder it does not replace the owner. Events and commands — at all kinds (chapter 2 Н1.5, chapter 4 Н1.4б).

### Н1.1в Video channel index

The video channel index of a row is a word of the source, a function of the address, without a connection to the device: the source does not
open a disabled row, and such a row MUST NOT change the owner of children (chapter 4 Н1.4а). The index counts from 0.

| Address | Index |
|---|---|
| `ipint://…?source=<N>`, `N` — decimal digits | `N` |
| `ipint://…` without `source` | 0: the address default, the index is stated |
| `driverpack://<вендор>/<хост>/ch/<N>`, `N` — decimal digits, value ≥ 1 | `N` − 1; `ch/02` — `N` 2, index 1 |
| `ipint://…`, `source` not digits; `driverpack://<вендор>/<хост>/ch/0`; `ch/` not in digits; another path | not stated |
| `driverpack://file/…`, `rtsp`, `rtsps`, `http`, `https`, `cfw` | not stated |

Not stated means there is no field; it is not index 0. Only the source (the holder) derives the index from the address: the doors, the console and `backend` do not derive it from the address
and take it from `videos` of the device row (Н1.2). Different addresses of one index (`ch/2` and `ch/02`, `source=1&stream=0` and
`source=1&stream=1`) are not a refusal; the owner is chosen by chapter 4 Н1.4а. An edit of `source` changes the index: the owner may change, this is the limit
of chapter 4 Н1.4а.

### Н1.1г Event source (POS)

The norms of the chapter on POS and units follow DriverPack 2026-10-02 and are reconciled against the new version (ADR-0102 addendum (9), (10)). They are not verified by work.

**Address.** `ipint://<адрес>:<порт>/<бренд>/<модель>`. A till driver with the word «listens» below waits for the connection itself: `<адрес>` is the server address or the
till name, `<порт>` is the port it listens on. `0.0.0.0` and `::` are never an address: `group_by` would gather all POS into one device; such an address is a
holder refusal (chapter 2 Н1.2).

| Brand | Connection | Default port |
|---|---|---|
| `PosXml`, `POSLegacy`, `Commax`, `Commax Complex Server` | listens: the till connects to the server | 2555 |
| `Live Eye` | connects to the device itself, like a camera; any other brand — the same | — |

**Server.** A till knows the address of one server, so the row is bound to it by the label `server:<имя сервера>` in `labels` (`labels-subset`, Н1.3). The label is carried by
the site row `LABELS` of this server (V/09.2 Н1.2) or by its labels in the console (P/06.6); the write through consolem is `Device.serverId` (V/11.3 Н1.3). If the server fails, the row does not
move: there is no other server with this label, the unit waits (`unplaceable`, P/04). This is a limit: a till will not tell apart two servers with one label.

**Fields.** The operator writes them through the units door. `camera` is not checked against the target and does not change `about`: the rights on POS lines are the rights on the `eventsrc` row. `words` is not
longer than 4000 bytes of canonical JSON (the limit of a `json` field, P/06.1 Н1.4). A value out of form in the store is unread, not absent:
`words` — the holder does not set `word` and `warning` names the field, `camera` — the page does not draw captions, `overlay_*` — the page shows a line without delay and until the next one.
A field that disappeared is the same as no field: the holder does not set `word`, the page does not draw captions.

### Н1.2 Table `devices`

`tables.devices` — what the device behind the camera channels turned out to be: the row `cam/devices/<хост>`, the key `device` is the host
by which the addresses of its channels name the device (the same as for `group_by`, Н1.3). The holder of the group writes it (`worker.writes:
[devices]`; the writer's side — chapter 4); the console door gives the row out, a write by the operator is a refusal
([P/06.1 Н1.6](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)); a deletion by the operator — `DELETE` with cas → 200, the holder
writes the row again if its host is still in its groups (P/06.1 Н1.6).

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `device` | `string`, required | the device host |
| `events` | `list` | the event kinds that its channels raise |
| `channels` | `int` ≥ 0 | the video sources that the device description named: the same number as `channels` of the door record (V/01.5 Н1.4). `0` — there is no video, no field — not stated, not «none» (chapter 4 Н1.4а) |
| `ptz` | `bool`, `false` | the device turns |
| `videos` | `json`, `{"<id камеры>": <индекс>}` | the video channel indices (Н1.1в) of the main rows `type: camera` of this host whose index is stated; a stream, other kinds and a row without an index are not included. Written on a change of the group rows, does not wait for a description (chapter 4 Н1.4) |
| `resolutions` | `list` | the resolutions `<ширина>x<высота>` that the device description named; no field — not stated: until the first description and at `generic`, manual input (ADR-0104 item 5) |
| `presets` | `int`, `0` | the positions one may ask to move to (`preset`, chapter 4) |
| `preset_names` | `json`, `{"<n>": "<имя>"}` | the preset names, if the driver names them (chapter 4 Н1.4); no field — only the numbers up to `presets` |
| `storage` | `bool`, `false` | the device has its own archive: the description names `embeddedStorageRef` and the device did not answer that it does not support an archive (chapter 4 Н1.4); not a `rec` volume |
| `identity` | `string`, not longer than 200 | serial number, MAC — not a capability; this field does not leave the cluster (not in the snapshot, not in the domain); the `ref` and `name` of a camera-cluster are deliberately its serial number: this is its name for the domain (chapter 6 Н1.5) |

The row is a finding of the holder, not a decision of the operator: it is read by the scenario form (`auto`, lesson V/07), the page and `backend` (V/11).
Inputs and outputs are not numbers of this row but children rows (Н1.2а; ADR-0102).

`videos` is read by `backend` (`Device.videoChannel`), `resolutions` — by the page as a hint for the choice and by `backend` as the limit of the recording `res` (V/11.3 Н1.3). A field that does not
read is unread, not absent: for `videos` the index is not «not stated», for `resolutions` it is manual input; this does not remove the stated value
of a stream row. If a field or the row disappeared, the holder writes them on its next pass (chapter 4 Н1.4).

### Н1.2а Device children: tables `children` and `child_settings`

An input and an output of a device are a row of their own, with a name, enablement and rights. There are two rows, with one key and one
writer each (P/06.1 Н1.1; the pattern is `rec/keeps`): what exists is a finding of the holder, what it is called and whether it is enabled is the operator's
word. No platform edits are needed.

**`tables.children`** — the row `cam/children/<id>`, the key `{camera}-{type}-{number:int}`, for example `7-sensor-1`. A worker
table (`worker.writes`): the holder writes it, the whole row at once (the writer's side — chapter 4 Н1.4а);
the console door gives the row out, a write by the operator is a refusal, a deletion — `DELETE` with cas → 200 (P/06.1 Н1.6), and the holder writes
the row again on its next pass if it is still true: a child cannot be removed this way. The refusal 422 `NOT_SUPPORTED` on a `DELETE` of a child
(ADR-0102 addendum (1) item 7) is the `backend` door, the V/11 translation, not the console. A `gone` row can be removed this way, and it will not come back: what this means for
a disabled output is the limit of chapter 4 Н1.4а.

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `camera` | `string`, required | the owner: the id of the main row of the host, `camera` or `ipsrv` (chapter 4 Н1.4а) |
| `type` | `sensor` \| `relay` \| `mic` \| `spk`, required | input, output, microphone, speaker |
| `number` | `int` ≥ 1, required | a running number over the device, separate for each `type`; at `sensor`, `relay` it is the `port` of the event `io.input` and of the command `output` |
| `device` | `string`, required | the host, the same as the key of `cam/devices` |
| `gone` | `bool`, `false` | the owner or the number are no longer true; the row stays |

**`tables.preset_labels`** — a preset that went through `backend`: `cam/preset_labels/<camera>-<n>`, the fields `camera`, `n`, `label`, `label` may be empty; the operator's label takes precedence over the driver's name. A table, not a field of the row: a field would move `revision` and break the camera connection on every label. `backend` writes it (V/11.4 Н1.16), the label does not go to the device, the position is another thing. The row has no expiry, the rows of a removed camera remain: camera numbers do not repeat, this is a limit.

**`tables.child_settings`** — the row `cam/child_settings/<id>` with the same key. A console table: the operator writes it through the tables
door (`POST`, an edit — `POST` over the same row, P/06.1 Н1.6). The key template names fields of the row, so `camera`,
`type`, `number` exist here too (P/02.1 Н1.4). `stamp: [at]`: the console sets `at` on every write, and this stamp orders the `enabled` words of different
rows (chapter 4 Н1.4а).

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `camera`, `type`, `number` | as at `children`, required | the key |
| `title` | `string` | the name the operator gave. Not `name`: a table has the field `name` only with `key: name` (P/06.1 Н1.6) |
| `enabled` | `bool`, no default | `false` — a disabled input does not write an event, a disabled output and `spk` answer with a refusal, a disabled `mic` gives no sound; `true` — enabled, and a later `true` removes the setting of a sibling row; no field — no word, the sibling rows decide. Who is disabled — chapter 4 Н1.4а. There is no default: a name given to a row does not enable an output |
| `norm` | `open` \| `closed`, no default | the normal state of the contact of `sensor`, `relay`: the start and the end of an alarm count from it (chapter 4 Н1.4а); no field — `open` |

No settings row — no name. A settings row without a child row is not an error: the door does not check that the child
exists, a `gone` row does not lose its name, and a name can be given in advance. `backend` assembles the device from the two rows
(V/11).

Rights follow the owner: both tables are in `rights.unit_of` (Н1.5), a child has no separate rights layer. A folder member is
a unit, not a table row (P/06.8 Н1.1): it is the camera that belongs to a folder, showing its children there is the V/11 translation. An input block goes
to the camera of its video channel or to the device row as a whole; there is no per-contact binding (chapter 4 Н1.4а).

### Н1.2б Device units: tables `device_units` and `unit_next`

A unit is a node of the DriverPack tree (a physical door, a reader, a zone, a fence segment): `.rep` names what can be, but not what is,
so the operator creates the rows. A console table, read by the holder (the holder's side — chapter 4 Н1.4б). A device unit is not a unit of rights:
it is never a folder member, rights follow the `camera` row.

**`tables.device_units`** — the row `cam/device_units/<id>`, the key `{camera}-{n:int}`, for example `7-3`. The operator writes it through the tables door.

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `camera` | `string`, required | a main row of `cam/cameras`, of any kind |
| `n` | `int` ≥ 1, required | the number on this row, does not repeat (below); the command calls the unit by it (chapter 4 Н1.1) |
| `type` | `acfa_sensor` \| `acfa_lock` \| `acfa_fence` \| `relay` \| `controller` \| `det`, required | the contract kind; `controller` is an intermediate tree node, in the contract `Device.type: acfa` with a parent (V/11.3 Н1.2); `det` is a detector that runs on the device (chapter 4 Н1.4в) |
| `template` | `string`, required | the id of the template `unit` in `.rep` of the model, at `det` the detector id; the holder passes it to `CreateUnit`, at `det` to `CreateVideoAnalytics` |
| `address` | `string` | a word of the device, the property `address` of the template, passed as is |
| `parent` | `int` ≥ 1 | the `n` of the owner on the same row; no field — the device root; at `det` there is no field |
| `sensitivity` | `int` | only at `det`: the sensitivity, the range is the model's own (chapter 4 Н1.4в) |
| `zone` | `json` | only at `det`: the zone `{shape, points}` in fractions of the frame, written by `backend` (V/11.3 Н1.3); no field — the whole picture (chapter 4 Н1.4в) |
| `title` | `string` | the name the operator gave |
| `enabled` | `bool`, `true` | `false` — the holder does not create the unit, nor the nested ones: they have no owner |

A `det` row exists only on a main row `type: camera`; contract kind ↔ detector — [`data/detector-kinds.tsv`](../data/detector-kinds.tsv), read by `backend` (V/11.3 Н1.3). Kind ↔ template — [`data/unit-templates.tsv`](../data/unit-templates.tsv): by brand, the owner's template and the template. `backend` reads the table at creation
(V/11.4 Н1.2); the tables door does not check the field `template`, a wrong template is judged by the holder. The row has no unit properties (`guarded`, thresholds) other than `address`: they are not in the contract.

**`tables.unit_next`** — the row `cam/unit_next/<строка>`, the key `camera`, the field `n`: the last issued number. The number of a new unit is the greater of `n` and the greatest number of the live rows, plus 1; the counter row goes
in the same batch with `cas` (V/11.4 Н1.2). Not a field of `cam/cameras`: an edit of that row restarts the holder (chapter 2 Н1.1). The number does not repeat: a request waiting for a removed unit and a log line MUST NOT
land on the new one. No counter row — the last number is 0; the row does not read — the number is not issued, 503: this is not absence (V/11.4 Н1.2). Whoever writes `cam/device_units` bypassing `backend` does not move the counter:
`backend` takes a live row into account, a removed one it does not, and its number may repeat: this is a limit of the tables door.

Rights follow the row (`rights.unit_of`, Н1.5): to read — `view`, to create and remove — `admin` on it. A row whose `cam/cameras/<camera>` does not exist is not read by the holder, and `backend` does not give it out;
a row id does not repeat, so it will not become someone else's. The target of `parent` and the removal of nested units — `backend` (V/11.4 Н1.2); a target removed bypassing it leaves the nested units without an owner: the holder does not create them (chapter 4 Н1.4б).

### Н1.2в Rule words: table `rule_state`

`tables.rule_state` — what the rules said about the camera (ADR-0105 items 6, 8): the row `cam/rule_state/<id>`, the key `camera`. A worker table (`worker.writes`): the camera holder writes it while executing the requests `arm`, `disarm`, `disable` (chapter 4 Н1.4д); the console door gives it out, a write by the operator is a refusal, a row removed by `DELETE` is written again by the holder (P/06.1 Н1.6). Not a field of `cam/cameras`: an edit of that row restarts the holder (chapter 2 Н1.1).

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `camera` | `string`, required | a main row `type: camera` (`rights.unit_of`) |
| `armed` | `bool`, `false` | armed; read by a rule condition (V/07) and `Device.armed` (V/11) |
| `disabled_by_rule` | `string` | the number of the scenario that disabled it; no field — not disabled |
| `disabled_at` | `float` | since what moment, by the holder's clock; `Device.disabledByRuleAt` |
| `disabled_until` | `float` | disabled while «now» is earlier than it (chapter 4 Н1.4д) |

No row — not armed and not disabled. A value out of form in the store is unread, not absent: chapter 4 Н1.4д. Rights follow the row (`rights.unit_of`, Н1.5): to read — `view` on the camera.

### Н1.4 Snapshot

`snapshot: [name, source, enabled, priority, labels, ref, live, main, profile]` — `main` and `profile` tell a stream from a
camera for a snapshot reader (the domain knows a camera by `ref`; a stream's `ref` is usually empty, and it is not in the domain view; a stream with
`ref` — Н1.1а); the form and the address mask —
[P/04 Н1.3а](../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md). Not in the snapshot: `cred_*` (the secret and the login have no snapshot
reader), `alarms`, `type`, `resolution`, `fps` (the holder reads them from the row), `lat`, `lon`, `azimuth`, `short`, `mac` (they have no snapshot reader). The snapshot leaves the cluster
(the domain, P/08.1); the `identity` of the table `devices` is never in it.

### Н1.5 Rights of people

`rights.reach` ([gate and pass contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Права по телу»; owner — [P/06.3 Н1.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.3-console.norms.md)):
- `group: [source, labels]` — an edit of the address or the labels touches all cameras of the device that the camera leaves, and of the one
  it moves to: moving a channel is an action over the device;
- `cluster: [ref]` — the camera name for the domain changes a grant on the cluster;
- creating a stream is as for any `cam` row: `admin` on `*` (a unit row without `rights.routes` is created by a grant on the cluster,
  the gate and pass contract «строки единиц»); an edit — `admin` on the stream or its camera (by `about`), a change of `source`
  or `labels` — also on all cameras of the old and the new device group (`reach.group`); a grant on the camera covers its streams
  by `about` (Н1.1а);
- `requests` — a command to the device requires `edit` on the whole group (chapter 4), and at an output with an owner too:
  the device that executes the command is one for the whole group;
- `requests: [output, preset, unit]` — a command to a unit, like one to an output, is judged over the whole group;
- `preset`, `preset_save`, `preset_clear`, `arm`, `disarm`, `disable` are not in `reach.requests`: the target is a camera channel or the camera itself, not the device, `edit` on the camera suffices; `routes: {edit: [ptz_moves]}` — the same for a turn (chapter 4 Н1.4г);
- `unit_of: {children: camera, child_settings: camera, device_units: camera, unit_next: camera, ptz_moves: camera, rule_state: camera, preset_labels: camera}`, `routes: {edit: [child_settings, ptz_moves, preset_labels]}` — a child row and a unit row belong
  to the owner (Н1.2а): to read — `view` on the owner, to name or disable — `edit` on it ([gate and pass contract](../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md));
  an output disabled by a setting of the former owner is enabled by someone with `edit` on the current one: by a setting of its own row (chapter 4 Н1.4а); a unit is created and removed with `admin` on the row: the table has no `edit` route.

### Н1.6 Console words and event suppression

`display`, `console` — the form of [P/06.5 Н1.2](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.5-console.norms.md): the section
«Оборудование» (equipment), the words «камера / камеры / камер» (camera / cameras, by number); the event kinds `io.input` — «вход устройства» (device input), `silent` — «камера молчит» (camera silent), `text` — «строка кассы» (till line), `unit` — «событие единицы» (unit event); the series `cam_cameras_running` — the cameras in the phase `running` (`console.running`; the phases — chapter 2).
`events.suppress` ([P/03.6 Н1.2](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)): `io.input` — a window of 30 s (a contact
bounces for milliseconds, and a bad one for hours), `silent` and `source-stats` — 60 s; the kinds and their meaning — [chapter 2](01.2-cam.norms.md) Н1.5.
