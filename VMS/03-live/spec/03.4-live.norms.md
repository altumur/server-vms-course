# V/03.4 — The media path: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/03.4-live.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 4 of lesson V/03 «Живое видео» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/03-live.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](03-live.README.md), manifest [`live.subsystem.yaml`](../live.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

One subscription — many viewers: the delivery's source is opened once, each viewer gets a branch of its own. The chapter sets norms for
branching, the RTP payload type from the offer, passing video through without transcoding (H.264 and H.265), the codec note and sound. The source is
chapter 2; the offer and the answer are chapter 3.

Norm ids are `V/03.4-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Frames carry the source's time (the holder, V/01.2 Н1.4; the receiver — on the cluster clock, V/02.9 Н1.3).

**Data.** The chapter has no rows of its own; the heartbeat has the field `codec` (Н1.4).

### Н1.1 Branching

- The source is opened at the **first viewer** of the delivery (chapter 2 Н1.2) and lives until the subscription is removed (grace, reconciliation — chapters 2,
  5); from the source an elementary stream (not RTP) goes into the tee. The source is the holder's loopback RTSP or the receiver (chapter 2 Н1.1);
  the gateway does not take on reading the frame share `live_shm`: the share format is the business of one implementation of `cam`, the joint between subsystems is loopback RTSP
  (the analysis of the V/01 review, question 2 (b)).
- **The codec of the first offer.** The gateway knows the delivery's codec only from the stream parameters of the source. The first offer opens
  the source before the answer and waits for its parameters within the same budget `ICE_WAIT` (chapter 3 Н1.2 step 5); if they have not come — 404 `not yet`
  (chapter 3), the source stays open until `grace`, and the page's retry finds the codec. A receiver source: the codec and the profile are taken from the codec
  parameters of the subscription's key frame (the `obsd` contract, Annex B).
- **A viewer's branch** is a queue of its own and an RTP packetizer of its own; it is added to a running source without stopping the other
  viewers; it goes away with the session.
- **The branch queue is leaky:** no more than 1 s of frames; when it is full the oldest are dropped (a slow viewer loses its own frames;
  the neighbours and the source do not wait). A viewer's break does not affect the tee.
- **A tee without branches does not block** the source: during the grace without viewers the source is read and discarded until the subscription is
  removed.

### Н1.2 Payload type from the offer

The payload type (PT) is assigned by the receiving side: the numbers in a browser's offer differ (Chrome and Firefox give H.264 different numbers).
The gateway takes from the offer the PT of the delivery's codec. The offer order is the list of formats of the first `m=video` section with a port ≠ 0; if there is no such
section — 400 `bad offer`; the codec name is compared case-insensitively; `codec` of the holder's heartbeat (V/01.2 Н1.4) is the same `rtpmap` name, `H264` or `H265`, and stream selection checks the offer by it (chapter 3 Н1.2); the range 96–127 applies on all branches:

- **H.264** — the first, in offer order, PT `H264` in the dynamic range 96–127 with `packetization-mode=1` whose
  `profile-level-id` matches the profile of the source stream (the first byte; the level is not compared); if there is none — the first in order
  with `packetization-mode=1` (whether the codec plays or not is the note of Н1.4); none with `packetization-mode=1` — 400 `bad offer`;
- **H.265** — the first, in offer order, PT `H265` in 96–127 (the page moves the static H.265 numbers and the `apt=` of retransmissions into 96–127 before sending, V/11);
- the delivery's codec is not in the offer (and another codec: a PT cannot be chosen) — 400 `bad offer` (chapter 3 Н1.2 step 6): an answer without video cannot
  be assembled; a profile or codec that the browser does not play is a `codec` note, not a refusal (Н1.3, Н1.4).

The answer answers with the offer's numbers; the branch packetizer sets this PT. A wrong PT breaks nothing visible (bytes flow, frames are not
decoded) — so the rule above is mandatory.

### Н1.3 Without transcoding

The video stream passes without decoding and encoding: H.264 and H.265 — as they come from the source (the decision of plan 3). The gateway does not do video transcoding, fMP4 or
TURN; a codec that the browser does not play is a note, not a refusal (Н1.4). There is one exception, and only for sound — Н1.5. POS captions are not burnt into the frame: the gateway does not see them; the page draws them over the video from the log (V/11.17 Н1.2 item 10; V/01.2 Н1.5, kind `text`).

### Н1.4 The codec note

As soon as the stream parameters are known (chapter 3 Н1.2 step 5, and also on the refusal of step 6), the gateway writes into the heartbeat `codec: {<cam>: {<поток>: <слова>}}` (the main stream — the key `""`); the note lives with the stream's subscription and is removed with it; `<профиль>` is the source's word as is; a stream that is fine —
has no entry:

| Stream | Note |
|---|---|
| H.264 with a profile from `constrained-baseline`, `baseline`, `main`, `high` | — |
| H.264 with another profile (High 4:4:4, 10 bit) | «H.264 profile `<профиль>`: browsers may not decode it» |
| H.265 | «H.265: browsers without H.265 decode will not play it» |
| another codec | «codec `<кодек>`: the gateway does not transcode» |
| sound in another codec (Н1.5) | «audio codec `<кодек>`: no sound», joined by «; » to the stream note |
| no parameters yet | — |

### Н1.5 Sound

ADR-0102 addendum (9) item 7, an exception to Н1.3; the codecs of the descriptions are per DriverPack 2026-10-02 and are checked again against a new version.

- **Where from.** A subscription with a loopback RTSP source opens, together with it, the endpoint `<live_url>/mic` (V/01.2 Н1.3): the codec is taken from `DESCRIBE`. A subscription on the receiver is with `audio: 0` (chapter 2 Н1.1; V/02.9 Н1.3а): the codec is the frame's `codec`.
  The codec is awaited for `AUDIO_WAIT` = 1 s within the offer's `ICE_WAIT` budget (a value without grounding, checked by measurement). A 404, no sound frames by the deadline, or another failure — there is no sound for this answer: the next offer asks again; a running session does not change its sound. A subscription on the receiver remembers that there were no sound frames: the next offer does not wait for the codec; the first sound frame clears this memory. **Limit:** the first viewer of a camera without a recording on this cluster: the pusher is silent until the first Sender Report, usually longer than `AUDIO_WAIT`: the session is without sound; the value is checked by measurement. Sound does not hold video back: neither 404 `not yet` nor 400.
- **The offer.** The first `m=audio` section with a port ≠ 0 and the direction `recvonly` or `sendrecv`; if there is no section — the viewer is without sound, not a refusal. The `rtpmap` name is case-insensitive; the PT is the first, in offer order, with this name.
- **The branch codec:**

| Source | Name in the offer | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| G.711: `PCMU`, `PCMA` | the same | without transcoding, the static 0 and 8 are fit: the range of Н1.2 is about video |
| G.711 without its own name in the offer; G.726: `G726-*`; AAC: `mpeg4-generic`, `MP4A-LATM`; PCM: `L16`; MP2: `MPA` | `opus` | transcoding into Opus |
| one of the named ones | no fitting one | no sound branch, not a refusal |
| another | — | no sound branch, the note of Н1.4 |

- **The branch codec is per offer:** frames of another `codec` do not go to this session; a new codec is taken by the next offer.
- **Branching** is as in Н1.1: a sound tee, a branch queue ≤ 1 s, a branch is added from the next frame; there is one Opus encoder per subscription.
- **The answer.** The video and sound tracks have one `msid`: the browser sees one stream; a section that the gateway did not serve gets port 0.
- **Time.** The tracks of a branch follow one line — the holder's capture time (V/01.2 Н1.3): the Sender Reports of the video and sound branches are counted from it; the tolerance — by measurement after the first generation.
- **Talk** (chapter 3 Н1.2а) is the reverse path: the browser's Opus is decoded and encoded with the codec `spk` from the heartbeat; SMPL records are the stream to the holder; the gateway MUST encode G.711, G.726 and AAC. If there is no browser frame, the gateway sends a silence frame in its place, at the codec's pace: the stream to the holder is not silent (V/01.5 Н1.5 `TALK_SILENT`).

## Н3 Pass

```
first viewer: open the source → wait for the stream parameters (≤ ICE_WAIT; none — 404 not yet) → codec note → tee;
  branch (queue ≤ 1 s, packetizer with the offer's PT)
next viewer: only a branch; sound — Н1.5: the mic endpoint next to the source, a refusal of the endpoint — the viewer is without sound
viewer left: remove the branch; the last one — the source stays until the subscription is removed (chapter 5)
```

**Races.** A branch is added while the source is giving out a frame: the new branch starts from the next key frame, the old branches
get the frame. The source is closed by reconciliation (chapter 2 Н1.3) while a branch is being built — the session is closed, the offer gets 404 (chapter 3 Н1.2 step 8).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the delivery's codec is not in the offer; for H.264 there is no PT with `packetization-mode=1` | 400 `bad offer` | the page | — |
| no stream parameters within `ICE_WAIT` (the first offer) | 404 `not yet`, the source stays open until `grace` | the page | a retry (V/11) |
| the browser does not play the profile or the codec | the stream flows, the `codec` note | the heartbeat | transcoding — by placement on a gateway with such a capability (outside the course) |
| no sound: no endpoint, no sound frames, another codec, an offer without `m=audio` | the viewer is without sound, the stream flows | the viewer; with another codec — the `codec` note | — |
| a slow viewer | its queue loses the oldest frames | the viewer | — |
