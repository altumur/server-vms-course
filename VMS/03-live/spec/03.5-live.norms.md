# V/03.5 — The end of demand, the heartbeat and refusals: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/03.5-live.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 5 of lesson V/03 «Живое видео» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/03-live.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](03-live.README.md), manifest [`live.subsystem.yaml`](../live.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (grace for each subscription, one deletion condition — `b41527f`).

A delivery is created by a viewer and deleted by the gateway — after a grace period following the last one; the gateway sweeps dead sessions itself. The chapter sets norms for
the grace and the deletion of the row, the sweeping of sessions, the gateway heartbeat (the delivery element, capacity, counters), the series `streams_live`,
stopping and the failure arithmetic. The gateway stores nothing: a restart learns everything anew from rows and heartbeats.

Norm ids are `V/03.5-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** The grace and the session timeouts are the process's monotonic clock.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): deletes — the rows `live/streams/*`
of its own assignment (`worker.deletes: [streams]`, [P/02.5 Н1.4](../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.5-installer.norms.md));
writes — the heartbeat `live/heartbeats/<воркер>`, the door log (`live.view.ended`, chapter 3).

### Н1.1 Grace and deletion of the row

**The subscription and its consumers.** The source is owned by the gateway's subscription to a pair (camera, stream) — one per gateway (chapter 2 Н1.2); its
consumers are WHEP sessions (chapter 3) and RTSP clients (chapter 6 Н1.4). While the delivery row of this camera is in the gateway's assignment,
the subscription belongs to the delivery and is closed by the `grace` of the row (below); without a delivery it is a standalone subscription of the RTSP endpoint,
closed `LINGER` after the last client (chapter 6 Н1.4). An RTSP client does not hold the row: the countdown and the deletion
of the row below count only WHEP viewers. The row was deleted while the subscription still has RTSP clients — the subscription becomes
standalone, without closing the source and without breaking the clients; the other way round — the delivery on this gateway takes over the standalone
subscription of the same pair (the source is opened once). «Viewer» below means a WHEP session.

- A subscription is born free (chapter 2 Н1.2): the grace countdown is each subscription's own, from its setting up; the last
  viewer of the stream left — the countdown starts anew.
- The pass: a subscription (the main or any other) has no viewers and the `grace` of the row has passed — the subscription leaves the delivery: without RTSP
  clients the gateway removes it and closes the source, with RTSP clients it becomes standalone (without closing the source and without
  breaking the clients, closed `LINGER` after the last client — chapter 6 Н1.4); the row lives.
- **The row countdown** runs from the latest of: the gateway taking the row, a demand for any stream (chapter 2 Н1.1), the leaving of the last viewer
  of any subscription, the end of a talk session (chapter 3 Н1.2а). A demand here is an offer that has passed the token, also with a subscription already set up, except for a refusal without recording the demand (chapter 3
  Н1.2 step 3). **Deletion of the row — one condition:** no subscription has viewers, there are no talk sessions and the row countdown is older than `grace`
  (default 60 s, V/03.1 Н1.1). Then the gateway, with `may_act` of the delivery, deletes the row by the `cas` of the read version of the row (the store index; `worker.deletes`, P/04 Н1.3 `unplaced`: «свои строки единиц МОЖЕТ удалять и воркер подсистемы»
  — deletion without the write right), then removes the subscriptions without RTSP clients and closes their sources, the other
  subscriptions become standalone. The placement is removed by the controller at its next pass; until then there is no delivery element in the heartbeat,
  the offer gets 404 `not yet`, the demand is not held; once it has left the assignment — 403 (chapter 3 Н1.1), the page creates the delivery anew (V/11.17).
- A delivery without a source (chapter 2 Н1.4) — by the same condition: a demand without a subscription gives no viewers, and `grace` after the
  last demand the row is deleted. The absence of a subscription of one stream — the main one too — does not delete the row.

**Cases** (V/03.5-01):

| What happens to the streams | Subscriptions and `phase` | Row |
|---|---|---|
| the main one is not running, another is running and is watched | the subscription of the other — with viewers; the demand for the main one — 404 `stream refused` with the words; `phase: running` | lives while the other is watched |
| the main one is running and is watched, the other is not running | the subscription of the main one; an offer for the other — 404 `stream refused`, its demand is removed after `grace`; `phase: running` | lives while the main one is watched |
| none is running | no subscriptions; `phase: waiting`, `reason` — the words of the first demand | deleted `grace` after the last demand |
| both are running, nobody watches | each subscription is removed `grace` after its last viewer leaves; without subscriptions — `waiting`, `no stream asked` | deleted `grace` after the last viewer leaves |
- `cas` did not pass (the row was edited) — the row is not deleted, the countdown holds, the next pass tries again. The store is silent —
  the outcome is unknown: the countdown holds, the next pass rereads; there is no row — as after deletion. A viewer came in the window before deletion — the countdown is cancelled, the delivery lives.
- The grace is hysteresis: without it, reopening a tab would cost a new row, placement and subscription.
- A row that nobody holds is deleted by the controller (`unplaced.delete_after` 30, V/03.1 Н1.3); a delivery that the gateway
  holds — only by the gateway.

### Н1.2 Sweeping of sessions

Each pass the gateway closes sessions: the WebRTC connection is gone (`closed` or `failed`) — at once; there has been no connection for longer than
`CONNECT_SECONDS` = 30 s in a row — likewise. The time without a connection accumulates in both waiting phases: after the answer, until the
connection is established (`new`, `connecting`), and after it was established, while it is interrupted (`disconnected`), — and it is
reset only when the connection is `connected`; otherwise an interrupted connection that has not become `failed` would hang without a
timeout. The count `swept`, the log `live.view.ended` with `why: swept`, for a talk — `live.talk.ended`. A talk session (chapter 3 Н1.2а) is a viewer of the delivery: `sessions`, `viewers`, grace and sweeping are the same. Without sweeping the gateway
would fill up with dead sessions and answer 503 `full`. The last one left — the grace countdown (Н1.1).

### Н1.3 Heartbeat

The form is [P/03.4 Н1.1](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md); `live` adds:

| Field | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | integer | viewers per gateway (V/03.1 Н1.3; default 100) |
| `headroom` | integer | `max(0, capacity − sessions − answering)` |
| `sessions` | integer | sessions now — WHEP and RTSP clients (chapter 6 Н1.4) |
| `subscriptions` | integer, counter | openings of the source since start (chapter 2 Н1.2) |
| `resets` | integer, counter | reconciliations that changed the source (chapter 2 Н1.3), also with zero sessions |
| `swept` | integer, counter | sessions swept (Н1.2) |
| `codec` | `{<cam>: {<поток>: слова}}` | codec notes (chapter 4 Н1.4) |
| `rtsp` | string, only with the RTSP door | the address of the RTSP door (chapter 6 Н1.4) |

**The delivery element `status[]`** (beyond the platform fields): `phase` — `running` (at least one subscription is set up) | `waiting` (none;
`reason` — chapter 2 Н1.4); the delivery epoch is always taken; without an epoch — only the platform element `unreadable` (P/03.4 Н1.1;
there is no `sealed`: there are no secrets in the row), without `viewers`, `source`; `why` is a platform field; `viewers` — the WHEP sessions of the delivery across all subscriptions plus the talk sessions (RTSP clients do not count in `viewers` — they are in `sessions`); `source` — `{<поток>: rtsp | ingest}` by subscriptions (`""` —
the main one; chapter 2 Н1.1).

### Н1.4 The series and `console.running`

`metrics: [{name: streams_live, from: status.phase, agg: count, equals: running, live: true}]`, `console.running:
streams_live` ([P/06.5](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.5-console.norms.md) Н3 «/metrics»): the series `live_streams_live` —
deliveries with a subscription up; it is read by the console health check.

### Н1.5 Stop and restart

- The loop, the leases and the heartbeat are the platform runtime ([P/03.3](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.3-worker.norms.md), P/03.4): a failure
  of a pass does not stop the renewal of leases.
- `stop_unit` (an edit of the row, leaving the assignment, loss of the lease) — like removal at SIGTERM: the WHEP and talk sessions are closed, the log
  `live.view.ended`, `live.talk.ended` with `why: stopped`, the source is closed; an edit of the row therefore breaks the viewers; `start` sets up no subscriptions, they
  will come with demand. `why`: `stopped` — any `stop_unit`, `reset` — only the reconciliation, `swept` — sweeping, `hangup` — a hang-up.
- SIGTERM — P/03.4 Н3: each delivery is removed (sessions closed, the log `live.view.ended`, `live.talk.ended` with `why: stopped`, the source closed),
  standalone subscriptions are closed, the log `live.rtsp.ended`; the last heartbeat, the slot returned; the gateway does not delete the delivery rows on
  stopping — another gateway will pick them up. The stop timeout is the default `worker.stop_timeout`.
- Restart: there is no state — subscriptions are set up on demand (chapter 2 Н1.1), browser sessions reconnect (the page, V/11).

### Н1.6 Failure arithmetic

| What fell | What is seen |
|---|---|
| the cluster console | no new viewers (`/live/where` does not answer); running sessions live on — the bytes bypass the console |
| the gateway | the live video of its viewers stopped; recording goes on (V/02); the deliveries are picked up by another gateway (placement), the page reconnects |
| the camera holder | the reconciliation closes the sessions (`resets`), a new source — when the holder moves |
| the receiver | the next receiver of the cluster; none — `waiting` |

## Н3 Pass

```
sweeping of sessions (Н1.2) → grace: without viewers for longer than grace → delete the row by cas → remove the subscription
heartbeat: capacity, headroom, sessions, counters, codec, asked_solo (chapter 2 Н1.5); an element per delivery — phase, reason, viewers, source, asked
```

**Races.** A viewer came while the gateway is deleting the row: the deletion by `cas` passed — the offer gets 404 (no subscription), the page
creates the delivery anew (V/03.1 Н1.2); it did not pass — the delivery lives. Two passes of one gateway do not overlap (the runtime).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the store is silent on deletion | the outcome is unknown, the next pass rereads the row | the heartbeat | — |
| no connection for 30 s in a row (not established after the answer, or interrupted) | the session is swept, `swept` | the log, the heartbeat | the page reconnects |
| the gateway is full | 503 `full`, `headroom` 0 | the page, the series | a retry; placement counts deliveries, not viewers, growth (`grows`) — by waiting deliveries (P/04 Н3 «нехватка»), not by the viewers of a busy one: the viewers of one camera are no more than the capacity of one gateway, a delivery has one holder |

## Н5 Acceptance

The chapter has no acceptance catalogue: the grace and the sweeping require WebRTC viewers (the WHEP stand — later); the deletion of the row by `cas` —
by reading: `live` is the very consumer of `worker.deletes`. Also by reading — the transition of a subscription with RTSP clients into a standalone one
(the stand has no RTSP clients).
