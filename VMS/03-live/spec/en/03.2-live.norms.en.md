# V/03.2 — The source and one subscription: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`03.2-live.norms.md`](../03.2-live.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 2 of lesson V/03 «Живое видео» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../03-live.md),
[instructions](../03-live.README.md), manifest [`live.subsystem.yaml`](../../live.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the subscription by (`cam`, `stream`) — `b41527f`).

The gateway holds one subscription to a camera stream for any number of viewers. The chapter sets norms for where the gateway takes the stream from — the
holder's RTSP on its own server or the receiver of its own cluster — when it opens the source, how it reconciles with the holder and what it
says when there is no source. The holder's delivery — [V/01.2](../../../01-cam/spec/01.2-cam.norms.md) Н1.3; the receiver —
[V/02.9](../../../02-rec/spec/02.9-rec.norms.md) Н1.3; viewers — chapters 3, 4; grace and deletion — chapter 5.

Norm ids are `V/03.2-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

### Н1.1 Where the camera stream is

Each pass the gateway looks for the source of each **subscription** — by the heartbeats, once per pass for all. A subscription is a pair (camera, stream),
one per gateway (Н1.2), and any one is **on demand**: the main one (`stream` empty) the same as any other. Demand is given by an offer for a stream, named (`?stream=`)
or chosen by the gateway (without either — the main one; chapter 3 Н1.2), or by a client of the RTSP endpoint (chapter 6); without demand there are no subscriptions.

**A camera of another cluster** (`cam: ref:<ref>` of the delivery; it is recorded by this cluster, V/02.9 Н1.2) is a branch before the local checks:
the gateway does not read the rows `cam/cameras/*` (the camera is not in its own cluster); the stream is only the main one: a non-empty `stream` of the demand —
`stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>`; the limit of demands is 1; the source is the receiver of its own cluster by `ref` (the table below).
Case: the first offer for such a camera records the demand for the main stream and gets 404 `not yet` (chapter 3 Н1.2 step 3), the next
pass sets up the subscription at the receiver.

For a camera of its own cluster:
The demands of a delivery are no more than the streams of its camera by the last pass (the main one and the rows `cam` with its `main`); an excess
offer gets a refusal at once, without a record (chapter 3 Н1.2). The pass also remembers, per delivery, for the choice of a stream (chapter 3 Н1.2): for each stream
the `resolution` of the row, the found element `status[]` with `phase`, `size`, `codec`; the row or the heartbeat is not read — the stream is not a candidate. The number of streams by the pass is unknown or zero — the limit is 1; a demand rejected
by the pass does not count towards the limit. The element `status[]` with the stream id is searched for (for the main one — the id of the
camera); «camera» in the table is this stream; if two fresh heartbeats name the camera — the element with the greatest epoch, of equal ones —
the first by slot name. Before the search the gateway checks (V/01.1 Н1.1а):
- the row `cam/cameras/<cam>` is a camera (its `main` is empty), otherwise the delivery is `waiting`, `reason` `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>`;
- the stream of the demand is of this camera: the row `cam/cameras/<stream>` names `main: <cam>`; otherwise there is no subscription, the demand is rejected
  with the words `stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>` — the offer gets them (chapter 3 Н1.2):

| Camera | Source |
|---|---|
| the holder on **its own** server: the camera element `status[]` in the heartbeat `cam/heartbeats/*` with `phase: running` and `live_url`, the heartbeat field `server` = its own `SERVER_NAME`, the heartbeat heard within `LOST_AFTER` = 45 s | the RTSP of the holder's delivery — `live_url` (loopback, V/01.2 Н1.3), TCP, a reception latency of 200 ms; `live_shm` is not taken: the joint of subsystems is loopback RTSP, the format of the frame share is internal to one implementation of `cam` (V/01.2 «Решает реализация») |
| the holder on **another** server of the cluster | the receiver of its own cluster: the call «подписка» by a leaf of the role `liveworker` — `cam`, `stream` of the subscription and `audio: 0` (V/02.9 Н2, Н1.3а) |
| a camera of another cluster (`cam: ref:<ref>`, it is recorded by this cluster, V/02.9 Н1.2) | likewise the receiver of its own cluster, by `ref`; only the main stream (a demand for any other is rejected: `stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>`; V/01.6 Н1.1, V/02.9 Н1.3) |
| there is no camera row; the holder is not `running` anywhere; there is no receiver in the cluster | there is no source — `waiting` (Н1.4) |

The RTSP of another server is not a source: the holder's delivery listens only on the loopback (ADR-0075 window 2). **The receiver of its own cluster** is
the address from the field `ingest` of the recorder heartbeat `rec/heartbeats/*` heard within `PEER_LOST_AFTER` = 30 s: the recorder of its own
server, otherwise the first by slot name; the receiver did not answer — the next one; an `ingest` of a heartbeat not in the form of the family
`rec-heartbeat-words` (V/02) is not read, rather than «no receiver»: on this pass this recorder is not a candidate, the next one is;
there are no readable ones — the source does not change, one not yet opened waits (not `no ingest in this cluster`). The receiver's answer is checked when
the source is opened (chapter 3 Н1.2 step 5, the budget `ICE_WAIT`): anything but 200 means it did not answer, the next one; none did — 404 `not yet`,
the reconciliation considers the source dead (Н1.3). The gateway's wish at any receiver is a wish at all of them
(V/02.9 Н1.5): the camera is pushed by its cluster (a camera-cluster — by itself, a server camera — by the recorder of the holder's server, V/02.9 Н1.2) to where
it already pushes, and the receiver passes it on to the neighbour with a subscriber. A subscription is the live edge (the viewer is not given the
pre-recording ring), the wish lives while the gateway is subscribed and for `LINGER` = 10 s more after unsubscribing (V/02.9 Н1.3).

The delivery of a camera of another cluster is a unit with the name `ref:<ref>` (the name allows a colon, the row check contract
«Идентификатор»); `about` does not resolve its camera — the viewer's right requires a grant on `*` (V/03.1 Н1.2 `unresolved`).

### Н1.2 One subscription

- **A subscription** is the gateway's record of a pair (camera, stream), **one per gateway**: the source (address, server), the holder's epoch. It is
  owned by the gateway, its consumers are WHEP sessions (chapter 3) and RTSP clients (chapter 6). It is set up by the pass on demand (Н1.1),
  the main one and any other alike; a standalone one is set up by an RTSP client (chapter 6 Н1.4); it is born free — without consumers,
  with the grace countdown running (chapter 5).
- **Whose subscription.** While the delivery row of the camera is in the gateway's assignment, the subscription belongs to the delivery and is closed by the `grace`
  of the row (chapter 5). Without a delivery it is a standalone subscription of the RTSP endpoint: it is closed `LINGER` = 10 s after the last
  client (chapter 6). An RTSP client does not hold the delivery row: the grace countdown and the deletion of the row (chapter 5 Н1.1) count only
  WHEP sessions. The row was deleted while the subscription has RTSP clients — the subscription becomes standalone without closing the
  source and without breaking the clients; the other way round — the delivery on this gateway takes over the standalone subscription of the same pair (one
  opening of the source). An RTSP client counts in `sessions` and `headroom` of the gateway, not in `viewers` of the delivery element (`viewers`
  — WHEP sessions; chapter 5 Н1.3). A demand for which no subscription is set up (the stream is not of this camera, there is no source) holds the refusal
  words until the next pass and is removed if there was no offer within the `grace` of the delivery. The demand of a set-up subscription lives with it,
  counts towards the limit and is removed together with the subscription, except for a subscription with a dead source (below): the next pass does not set it up.
- **The source is opened at the first viewer** (chapter 4), not when the subscription is set up: an RTSP connection or a subscription at the receiver
  — one per subscription, however many viewers there are; the heartbeat counter `subscriptions` is how many times opening the source was attempted.
  Fifty viewers of one stream — one opening; a large and a small window of one camera on different streams — two (two subscriptions).
- **The delivery epoch** — the gateway takes the epoch of the unit when it takes the unit ([P/03.3](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.3-worker.norms.md)
  unit start; [P/03.2 Н1.5](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md)), before searching for the source: two gateways on
  one camera are visible by the epoch (substitution, move). The epoch could not be taken — the unit is not taken: there is no delivery element in the heartbeat
  (the form of P/03.4 Н1.1: an element without an epoch — only the platform phases `sealed`, `unreadable`), a line of the worker log, a retry
  at the next pass; the other deliveries go on.
- The delivery left the assignment — the subscription is removed, the viewers are disconnected (their sessions closed), the source is closed; a subscription with
  RTSP clients becomes standalone, without closing the source.

### Н1.3 Reconciliation with the holder

Each pass the gateway reconciles the subscription with the holder's heartbeat: the source changed (the holder moved to another server, another
`live_url`) or the holder's epoch changed, or the source is dead (RTSP broken or 8 s without a frame; the receiver does not answer) — the viewer
sessions are closed (the handsets are hung up), the RTSP clients of the subscription are disconnected in the same way (the log `live.rtsp.ended`), the subscription
gets a new source, the source is opened at the first returning viewer; the count `resets`. The source is dead and there is no new one —
the subscription is removed, the demand stays, the words — Н1.4; the source is alive but the holder's heartbeat does not name it — the subscription is held. A subscription
at a receiver is held at it while it answers: the appearance of a receiver higher in the order does not change the source, the order of Н1.1 is for
setting up and replacement. For a camera of another cluster only the liveness of the source is reconciled. The retry and the jitter of reconnection are
the page's (V/11). The store is silent — the last read source is held; the holder's heartbeat is not read — a trouble of this camera,
not of the pass. The row `cam/cameras/*` or the holder's heartbeat is unreadable — a condition of the words table (Н1.4) is not met, no words are
named: the offer gets 404 `not yet`, `reason` — `not yet`.

### Н1.4 There is no source

A subscription without a source is not set up; the words are `no camera <поток>`, `camera <поток> is held by nobody`, `camera <поток> is not
running`, `no ingest in this cluster`, `stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>` (the check of Н1.1). The viewers of this stream get
them in 404 `stream refused` of the offer (chapter 3 Н1.2). The state of the delivery is by all its subscriptions and demand, not by the main stream:
`running` if at least one subscription is set up (the source is found; it is opened at the first viewer — `running` does not mean
«the stream is up»); otherwise `waiting` (chapter 5 Н1.3; the epoch is taken), `reason` — the words of the demand first
in time, `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>` or, without demand, `no stream asked`. The deletion of the row is one rule of chapter 5
Н1.1; the absence of a subscription of one stream does not delete the row.

**Stream words** — the first met condition from the top; the words are a contract:

| Condition | Words |
|---|---|
| the stream of the demand is not of this camera (the check of Н1.1) | `stream <stream> is not of camera <cam>` |
| there is no row `cam/cameras/<поток>` (a camera of its own cluster) | `no camera <поток>` |
| there is no element `status[]` of the stream in any fresh heartbeat `cam/heartbeats/*` | `camera <поток> is held by nobody` |
| the element exists, its `phase` is `idle` (V/01.2 Н1.2в) | no words: the demand is held, the gateway requests the stream (Н1.5), the offer — 404 `not yet` |
| the element exists, its `phase` is not `running` | `camera <поток> is not running` |
| the source is the receiver of its own cluster (Н1.1), there is no recorder with `ingest` | `no ingest in this cluster` |

A camera `live: on-demand` (phase `held`) is treated as `always`: the viewer is a consumer, the stream request wakes it (V/01.2 Н1.2в; ADR-0103 addendum (7) item 3, (8) item 2),
until the first frames — `camera <поток> is not running` (V/01.1 Н1.1). A row that is not `camera` is also phase `held`, with the same words: it has no frames (V/01.1 Н1.1б).

### Н1.5 Stream request

The holder does not read a stream without a consumer (V/01.2 Н1.2в), so the gateway requests it itself: the delivery element of the heartbeat `live/heartbeats/<имя>` carries `asked` — the ids of the streams with a demand (chapter 3 Н1.2, chapter 6 Н1.4) or a subscription; a standalone subscription and a demand of the RTSP endpoint have no delivery element, they are carried by the top of the heartbeat in `asked_solo`; the main stream is the camera id; without duplicates, in byte order; when there are none, there is no field.

- The gateway writes it, the camera holder reads it each pass; the VMS family [`live-heartbeat-words`](../../data/live-heartbeat-words.schema.json). `asked`, `asked_solo` not in form — the holder considers the stream requested (V/01.2 Н1.2в).
- A demand lives for `grace` (chapter 5): for that long the stream is held by the request, and for `IDLE_AFTER` more — by the holder.
- The gateway died — the heartbeat is older than 45 s, there is no request; the subscriptions of other gateways go on by their own heartbeats. A camera of another cluster (`ref:<ref>`) has no request: its stream is held by the assignment (V/01.2 Н1.2в).
- `idle` is not a refusal: the subscription is not set up, the demand is held, the page retries; the element became `running` — an ordinary pass of Н1.1.

**Limit.** The heartbeat goes once per `HEARTBEAT_EVERY`: the first viewer of an `idle` camera waits up to 22 s (V/01.2 Н1.2в).

**The `waiting` reason of a delivery** — the first met condition from the top:

| Condition | `reason` |
|---|---|
| the row `cam/cameras/<cam>` is a stream (its `main` is not empty) | `cam <cam> is a stream of <main>` |
| there is a demand, the first demand has no words (the unreadable does not decide) | `not yet` |
| there is a demand | the stream words of the demand first in time |
| there is no demand | `no stream asked` |

## Н3 Pass

```
sources: heartbeats cam/heartbeats/*, rec/heartbeats/* — once each; for each delivery of the assignment: the cam row is a camera (otherwise
waiting); demand (offers, RTSP points) → the check "stream of this camera", source, subscription — or refusal words; phase — by subscriptions
for new ones: epoch (P/03.2, on taking) → not taken — no element, log; with a source → subscription (free)
gone from the assignment → remove the subscription, close the sessions and the source (with RTSP clients — standalone)
reconciliation (Н1.3): the source or the holder's epoch changed, the source is dead → close the sessions, disconnect the RTSP clients, a new
source (none — remove the subscription), resets + 1
without a source → phase waiting, reason (Н1.4)
```

The store is read outside the lock that the viewer waits for (the offer, chapter 3): under the lock only subscriptions and sessions change.

**Races.** The holder moved between passes — a viewer who came in that window gets the stream of the old source until the reconciliation of the
next pass. Two gateways took one delivery (substitution) — the second one has a higher epoch: the first one at its next heartbeat sees
the loss of the lease (P/03.2) and removes the subscription (`unconfirmed_max: off`, V/03.1 Н1.4).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| there is no camera, the holder is not `running` | `waiting` with a reason; deletion after `grace` | the heartbeat | — |
| there is no receiver in the cluster | `waiting` «no ingest in this cluster» | the heartbeat | install a recorder |
| the recorder's `ingest` is not in form | not a candidate on the pass; there are no readable ones — the source does not change | the heartbeat | fix the recorder |
| the receiver did not answer | the next receiver; none — 404 `not yet`, the reconciliation removes the subscription, `waiting` | the page, the heartbeat | a retry (V/11) |
| the epoch is not taken | the unit is not taken, there is no element, a log line; a retry at the next pass | the worker log | — |
| the source died, the holder moved | the sessions are closed, `resets` + 1, a new source | the heartbeat, the page | the page reconnects |
