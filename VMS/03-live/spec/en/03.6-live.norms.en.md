# V/03.6 — Stream accounts and the RTSP door: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`03.6-live.norms.md`](../03.6-live.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 6 of lesson V/03 «Живое видео» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../03-live.md),
[instructions](../03-live.README.md), [OpenAPI](../03-live.api.openapi.yaml), manifest [`live.subsystem.yaml`](../../live.subsystem.yaml).
The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the RTSP endpoint of a stream — a grant by the camera, `b41527f`).

A device — a video wall, a foreign VMS, analytics — speaks RTSP with Digest and does not use domain tokens. For it the cluster creates
a **stream account**: a name and a password, the rights are grants, as for a person. The accounts are stored and served by the gateway; the page manages them
through the console of its own cluster (`door.pass`); the domain carries them to other members with the book `stream-accounts`, and the RTSP door of the gateway
lets an account in to the cameras on which it has a grant. The form is C5 of ADR-0074.

Norm ids are `V/03.6-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `at` is Unix seconds.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): writes — the rows of the table
`live/accounts/<имя>`, the contribution `live/stream-accounts/<слот>` ([P/03.7
Н1.2](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md)); reads — the copies `domain/live/stream-accounts`,
`domain/grants`, the folders `platform/memberof/`, `platform/folders/` (P/09.1 Н1.4; `worker.reads`, the grants are the role base with `door.routes`, P/02.5
Н1.4); a cluster that is the domain holder has no copy of the book: the gateway reads the row of its own cluster
`domain/live/stream-accounts/<CLUSTER>` — the read and the secret reader of this one row are derived by the rights generator from
the installer's `CLUSTER` (P/02.5 Н1.4, C02-OWN-BOOK-HOLDER-01); the manifest names only the copy, there is no prefix (it would open
to the gateway the deliveries of all members). The cluster holds the domain when the record `domain/holder` of its store (the role base, P/02.5 Н1.4) passes
`record` and names this cluster; it did not pass — the flag is not raised, the copies are read, the cause goes to the log. It writes the door log (`live.rtsp.*`).

### Н1.1 The table `accounts`

`tables: [accounts]` is the list form: a family for the rights, there is no console CRUD ([P/06.1 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)
`list_form`); `worker.writes: [accounts]` — only the gateway writes; the page MUST NOT write accounts bypassing the gateway's door.

```yaml
live/accounts/<name>:  {doc: <JSON text {name, disabled, at, by}>, password_secret, revision}
  name:             the account name — the grammar of a domain person's name (P/08.4 Н1.1 name); one namespace with people (Н1.3)
  disabled:         bool; a disabled account does not play
  at, by:           when and by whom it was last changed (by — the proven name of a person)
  password_secret:  the password — 32 characters of base64url from 24 random bytes; an envelope of the cluster keyring (the sealing contract); the reader is the gateway
  revision:         a platform field, kept by the worker base (P/03.4 Н1.6); a cas conflict — re-read and compute anew
```

The table secret is sealed by the gateway on write and resealed by the gateway itself at start ([P/03.4 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)
`list_secret`); `revision` does not change thereby.

`secrets.readers`: `live/accounts/` — `worker`; `live/stream-accounts/` — `worker`, `domainagent`; the copy
`domain/live/stream-accounts` — `worker`, `domainagent`, `domain`; the holder's rows `domain/live/stream-accounts/` —
`domainagent`, `domain` ([P/02.5 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.5-installer.norms.md)); the row of its own cluster at
the holder — also `worker` (derived by the rights generator, Н1). Deleting an account is deleting the row, without a tombstone: a repeated
`DELETE` — 404.

### Н1.2 Account doors

Accounts are managed by the cluster's `admin` (C5: its own console, `door.pass`): the password, disabling and deletion do not
require domain rights; creation in a domain requires, in addition, `view` on the domain's people — with this right the gateway checks the name (Н1.3). `door: {pass: [accounts]}` — the cluster console passes `/live/accounts…` to a live gateway with the door
(the least name) by the path `/accounts…` without the mount prefix (at any root; the pass contract,
C06-DOOR-PASS-PATH-01), having checked the person's right beforehand: `view` on anything — reading, `admin` on `*` — writing (`admin` on one
camera gives no writing; an emergency session is not passed to the gateway — 403 `denied` at the console; [gate and pass
contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Передача `door.pass`»: body ≤ 1 MiB, headers
`Authorization`, `Content-Type`, `Idempotency-Key`, `X-Operator`; no gateway is heard — 503 «no worker of live with a door
is heard»; the gateway did not answer within `PASS_WAIT` — 502).

**Admittance at the gateway's account door** is a norm of the lesson (C5 addendum 2026-10-14 (2) item 2), not `door.routes`: the names `pass` and `routes` do not
intersect (P/02.1). Under TLS (the kit of the role `liveworker`) — first the leaf of the role `console` by the class `role` of the
[role certificate contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/role-certificate.md) as is (no leaf — 401 `no_role`; not exactly one role in
the SAN — 403 `no_role`; the role is not `console` — 403 `wrong_role`; a signature outside `sig` — 403 `bad_chain`; a chain
rejected by TLS — handshake refusal), then — the person's credential passed by the console: the token in `Authorization: Bearer`
(the console puts the cookie token there too); the gateway checks it again with the function `token` with `expect {kind: person, cluster: null}`; absent or
invalid — 401 `denied`; there is no domain (no `domain/keys`) — there is nothing to check with, admittance is the `console` leaf, `by` is `operator`; the right
`view`/`admin` is decided by the console's gate beforehand ([gate and pass contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Передача `door.pass`»): the gateway has no role table. Without a kit — the rule `no_kit` of [P/03.4 Н1.7](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md):
the route is closed, 401 `no_role`, a log line at start.

**Input parsing** is the shared parsing of [P/01.1 Н2.1](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md) through P/03.4 Н1.7
`door_limits`, with deviations: the path is byte for byte after the pass: an empty segment (`/accounts/`), an extra segment, another
path, `%`-encoding that is not UTF-8 — 404 `no such path`; a name in the path decoded but not by the grammar — 400 `bad name`;
the query is not read; the body is one JSON object, strictly (UTF-8, a repeated key, a non-finite number) and without unknown fields
(`additionalProperties` of the OpenAPI), otherwise 400 `bad body`; `GET`, `DELETE` and `POST …/password` have no body — a non-empty body is not
read. Order: path → method → admittance → body.

| Request | Answer | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /live/accounts` | 200 `{accounts: [{name, disabled, at, by}]}` | the list; no passwords |
| `POST /live/accounts {name}` | 201 `{account, password}` | a new account; the password — **once**, in the answer |
| `POST /live/accounts/<имя>/password` | 200 `{account, password}` | a new password, the previous one no longer works |
| `PUT /live/accounts/<имя> {disabled}` | 200 `{account}` | enable or disable |
| `DELETE /live/accounts/<имя>` | 200 `{deleted}` | delete the account row; the grants are removed by the domain reconciliation (Н1.3); a repeat — 404 |

Refusals are `{error, detail}`; the words of `error` are a closed list: 400 `bad body` — the body does not parse or has an unknown field,
400 `bad name` — no `name`, not a string, or the name (in the path or in the body) is not by the grammar, 400 `bad disabled` — no `disabled` or not
bool; 401/403 — admittance (above, the words of the class `role`), 401 `denied` — the credential is absent or invalid; 403 `denied` — the domain did not let
the name be checked (Н1.3); 404 `no such path` — the path (above), 404 `no such account` — there is no account; 405 `method not allowed`; 409
`name taken` — the name is taken by a domain person, an account or a grant row (Н1.3); 413 `toolarge`; 503 `busy` — door limits; 503 `unavailable`
— the password did not open or the console does not answer. One word per case; the order is as in «Input parsing». `account` is
`{name, disabled, at, by}`.

### Н1.3 Name, grants, book

- **Name.** The order of the check: its own table, the domain, the book copy. Before creation the gateway asks `GET /domain/users` **through its own console** as this person (the processes of a member have one
  road to the domain — its console, which passes the route to the holder): a person with such a name — 409. By the same road — `GET /domain/grants`: a row with
  `subject` = this name in any cluster — 409: these are the grants of another account or of a former one that the reconciliation has not removed yet, and the name is free only after the removal, as at
  the people door (P/08.4 Н3, step 7a). The domain refused (401, 403) —
  403 `{error: "denied", detail: "checking the name needs view of the domain's people"}`, the account is not created; there is no domain
  (no `domain/keys`) — only its own table and the book copy are checked; the console did not answer or answered other than 200, 401,
  403 — 503 `unavailable`. Without the early check
  the exclusion of names is held by the domain itself (`exclusive_with`, `reverse`); the early check is a convenience: it does not hold the exclusion, but its refusal does not create the account. An account of the same name — by
  its own table and its own book copy; the check by the copy is partial: one name at the same time in two clusters is a `unique` dispute
  of the book (neither is delivered), it is visible in the name index `domain/names/live/stream-accounts`
  ([P/08.5 Н1.7](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md)).
- **Grants** are the domain's grant rows with `subject` = the account name (on the cluster, a camera or labels; `sub` absent or `live`, Н1.4; P/08.4 Н3); the people door
  of the domain does not let a grant of an account be wider than `view` (`names.stream-accounts/.grant: view`, P/08.5 Н1.7). **The gateway does not remove
  grants:** `DELETE` deletes the row and answers 200, the element leaves the contribution with the next heartbeat, the grants are removed by the reconciliation of
  the process `domain` — grant rows whose `subject` is not a person and not a key of any name index are removed before
  the name is freed for `POST /domain/users` (P/08.4 Н3, step 7a of the people door, C08-GRANTS-ORPHAN-01). The state «the row
  is gone, the grants are still there» is safe: there is no account, it has nothing to play with. Limit: grants follow the name — the account is removed, but the name remained a key of the index at another
  cluster (a `unique` dispute, Н3) — the reconciliation does not remove the rows, and they pass to that cluster's account; when resolving the dispute the operator checks the grants of this name.
- **Book.** `domain.books.stream-accounts: {from: live/stream-accounts/, key: name, pick: unique, to: to}`,
  `domain.names.stream-accounts/: {exclusive_with: domain/users, grant: view}`. The contribution `live/stream-accounts/<слот>` is derived
  from the table on every heartbeat: it is written by **one gateway of the cluster** — the live one with the least slot name among those heard within
  `PEER_LOST_AFTER` = 30 s, the others write an empty row; an element per account `{name, to, disabled, password_secret}`, `to` is `"servers"`, all members
  `class: server` of the pass (P/08.5 `recipient`): a copy on a camera is not needed, there is no gateway there (ADR-0113 addendum (8) item 4); delivery by grant is cut off by the collector (P/08.5 Н1.3 `grant`). The `password_secret` of the contribution is the password
  of the table row sealed under the contribution key (P/03.7 Н1.2 `at_rest`); the password did not open — there is no element, a log line.
  The row is rewritten only when the open content changes. The book exists only in an installed domain (`domain/keys` exists).

### Н1.4 The RTSP door

- Enabled by the port `RTSP_PORT` of the subsystem file `/etc/live/live.env`; not set — there is no door (a log line at start).
  The address is P/03.4 Н1.7 `door_address`, scheme `rtsp` (with the kit of the role `liveworker` — the addresses of `SERVER_NAME`, without it — loopback); in
  the heartbeat — the field `rtsp` (`rtsp://<SERVER_NAME>:<порт>`, without a kit `rtsp://127.0.0.1:<порт>`; no door — no field). Limits are
  `door_limits`; `door_limits.other`: `PACE_FLOOR` does not apply to a stream, `CONNECTIONS` = `capacity`. RTSP without TLS (`rtsps` is not
  introduced, ADR-0075 window 2); the password is Digest, not Basic.
- **Endpoint** — `/<поток>` (the row id `cam`) for every stream of a camera of the cluster (V/01.1 Н1.1а) whose holder is `running` or `idle` (the heartbeats
  `cam/heartbeats/*`, chapter 2). `DESCRIBE` on an `idle` endpoint is demand (chapter 2 Н1.5): it is held for `LINGER` = 10 s from the last `DESCRIBE`, the answer is RTSP 503 with `Retry-After: 1`: the stream will open, the client will repeat. **The endpoint's camera** is the row `cam/cameras/<поток>` if its `main` is empty, otherwise the row that
  its `main` names; there is no stream row or camera row — there is no endpoint (RTSP 404). The source is by the rule of chapter 2 Н1.1 for the stream of
  the endpoint, one subscription of the gateway per pair (camera, stream) — chapter 5 Н1.1: a delivery subscription of this gateway to the same pair — it is used (the RTSP
  client does not hold a delivery row), otherwise a standalone one: it is created by the client itself from the source of the pass, opened by the first and waits
  for the stream parameters `ICE_WAIT` (they did not come — RTSP 503), it closes `LINGER` = 10 s after
  the last one; transitions between them — without closing the source (chapter 5 Н1.1). An RTSP client is a session (`sessions`), capacity
  (`headroom`), a branch (chapter 4); it does not enter the delivery's `viewers`. `sessions + answering ≥ capacity` — RTSP 503; the place is taken
  on `PLAY`.
- **Who plays:** the accounts of its own table and the accounts of other clusters from the copy of the book `domain/live/stream-accounts` (at the domain
  holder — the row of its own cluster `domain/live/stream-accounts/<CLUSTER>`, Н1), not `disabled`; another cluster means the `member` of the copy
  element ≠ its own, an element of its own cluster is not read: its own account is decided by the table; an account plays an endpoint if the copy `domain/grants` has a valid row
  of its `subject` (`until` has not passed): rank not below `view`, `sub` absent or `live` (as with the book delivery, P/08.5 Н1.3 `grant`), the scope is `*`, the endpoint's camera
  or its labels (by the camera, not by the stream row: a stream has no labels, V/01.1 Н1.1а) or the camera's folder: a row `folder:<id>` opens the endpoints of the cameras of this folder and of nested ones; the gateway reads `platform/memberof/cam/cameras/<cam>` and the chain `platform/folders/`, as for a person, so that the folder of an account does not diverge from the folder of a person (ADR-0112 addendum (7) item 1); not read — a new session is not opened, a running one is judged by the chain read last: as with the grants copy, a store failure does not break the stream, and the same timeout: a chain older than its read + `GRANT_LIFETIME` does not open the row.
  A copy older than `as_of` + `GRANT_LIFETIME` gives no rights ([rights contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/may.md) step 4);
  it is read once per pass: revocation and expiry take effect from the next pass. Otherwise — 401 (Digest
  anew) or 403. The copy is not read (the row exists but is not readable, the store did not answer) — the pass and `PLAY` judge by the one read last since start, the age — by its
  `as_of`: revocation does not come from it, but the timeout runs; none was read — RTSP 503. There is no copy row (there is no domain or it has not arrived yet) — there is no grant: a created account
  plays no endpoint (RTSP 403); it starts playing with a domain grant in the copy. The pass closes the running sessions of accounts that no longer play (disabled,
  deleted, no grant on the endpoint, the copy is stale), log `live.rtsp.ended`; a new password does not close a running session.
- **An unreadable book is not an absent one** (the element form is the family [`stream-accounts-entry`](../../data/stream-accounts-entry.schema.json)):
  an element of the copy (at the holder — of the row of its own cluster) is not by the form or the envelope `password_secret` does not open (the sealing
  contract) — for this account the gateway holds the element read last since start; there is none — the account does not play;
  a log line without values; the other elements go on. There is no element of a foreign account in the read copy, while the `member` of its element read last since start is in
  `missing` of this copy with a cause for which there is no contribution (`silent`, `never`, `no_copy`, `bad_contribution`, `secret_unopened`; `unseen` contributes, and there is no element in its contribution: the account is deleted;
  [P/08.5 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md)): the account is unknown, not deleted — the gateway holds that element no longer than
  `GRANT_LIFETIME` from the first such copy, the timeout of a cluster without connectivity; the grants are judged as usual. Limit: disabling, a new password and deletion on a silent cluster in
  this window do not reach the gateway. The truncation of `missing` does not hinder this judgment: the gateway is installed on clusters `class: server` (ADR-0113 addendum (8) item 4), servers are first in the list, `SERVERS_MAX` 500 equals `MISSING_SHOWN`. Raising `SERVERS_MAX` without `MISSING_SHOWN` breaks this judgment. The whole book is not readable, the store did not answer, or the holder does not have the row of its own cluster yet (the window after a move, P/08.5 Н1.3 `contributes`) — the copy read last
  since start; the already running `GRANT_LIFETIME` timeout of an element of a silent cluster does not stop, the other elements are held by this copy; none was read — only the accounts of its own table play. A row of its own table is unreadable — the account
  holds what was read since start, there is none — it does not play, a log line; `GET` and writes — 503 `unavailable`.
- **Log** — `live.rtsp.play` (`user` — the account, `target` — the endpoint's camera, `of` — `live/<cam>`: the delivery of the endpoint's camera,
  V/03.1 Н1.1, `stream` — the endpoint's stream, empty — the main one, `session`, `addr`) and
  `live.rtsp.ended` (the same and `seconds`).

## Н3 Pass

```
contribution (the first gateway of the cluster): elements from the table accounts → live/stream-accounts/<slot>, on change; the others — an empty row
RTSP door: endpoints — cameras with a running or idle holder; accounts — own table + book copy; grants — the copy domain/grants (once per pass);
close the running sessions of accounts that no longer play
account doors — on request (door.pass): path → method → admittance (console leaf, then the person) → body → operation
```

**Races.** Two clusters created an account of one name at the same time — a `unique` dispute of the book, neither is delivered, the name index
shows both; the operator renames. Two gateways of a cluster considered themselves first, including a new first one while the slot of the previous one
is still held — both write equal elements: equal elements of one member are one entry ([P/08.5](../../../../Platform/08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md)
Н3 step 4a, C08-BOOK-SAME-ITEM-01), the contribution is intact. Limit: an account changed in this window — unequal elements remove the member's contribution
(`bad_contribution`) until the row of the previous slot goes away. The others write an **empty** row as soon as they hear a gateway of a lesser name.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| no gateway | 503 (pass) | the page | bring up a gateway |
| no `admin` on `*` for writing; an emergency session | 403 `denied` (the console's gate) | the page | — |
| the person's credential is absent or invalid | 401 `denied` | the page | log in |
| no `console` leaf under TLS; without a kit | 401 `no_role` (403 `no_role`/`wrong_role`/`bad_chain`) | the console, the log | the role kit |
| the domain did not let the name be checked | 403 `denied` | the page | the right `view` on the domain's people |
| the name is taken by a person, an account or a grant row | 409 `name taken` | the page | another name; the grants of the former subject will be removed by the domain reconciliation |
| the password did not open, the console does not answer | 503 `unavailable` | the page | — |
| the body does not parse or has an unknown field; the name is not by the grammar; no `disabled` or not bool | 400 `bad body`; `bad name`; `bad disabled` | the page | fix the request |
| the path is not the account door; there is no account | 404 `no such path`; `no such account` | the page | — |
| an account without a grant on the camera | 403 RTSP | the device | a grant `view` |
| the account is disabled or the password is wrong | 401 RTSP | the device | — |
| the account no longer plays, the session is running | the session is closed by the pass, `live.rtsp.ended` | the device, the log | enable the account, return the grant |
| the gateway is full; the stream parameters did not come within `ICE_WAIT`; the grants copy is not read, none was read since start | 503 RTSP | the device | repeat |
| an `idle` endpoint: the stream opens on demand | 503 RTSP, `Retry-After: 1` | the device | repeat |
| an element of the account book is not by the form, the envelope did not open | the previous element since start; none — the account does not play | the gateway log | repair the contribution of the owner cluster |
| there is no element of a foreign account, its cluster is in `missing` of the copy not with the word `unseen` | the previous element since start, no longer than `GRANT_LIFETIME`; none — the account does not play | the domain view | restore the connectivity of the owner cluster |

## Н5 Acceptance

Acceptance 6 — accounts through `door.pass` on a stand with the worker `live` with a role kit (the `console` leaf at the console): creation
(the password in the answer once), the list without passwords, a new password, disabling, deletion, repeated deletion — 404, 400 by name,
404; 409 by a person's name and by a grant row, 403 `denied` of the name check and the removal of grants by the domain reconciliation — a stand with a domain; playing over
RTSP, including RTSP 503 without a read grants copy, RTSP 403 without a copy row and the element timeout under `missing` — by reading (the stand has no RTSP client with Digest). Scenarios will appear with the stand of the worker `live`; until then — by reading.

---

## Implementation decides

The implementation MUST name in its description: **the RTSP server with Digest** (the library; how it remembers the verified account for
the client until `PLAY`) and **the HA1 cache**, if there is one. The password in the row and in the book is an openable secret (C5, ADR-0074); HA1 is
only a local cache of the gateway made from the password and its own `realm`; it is not written to the row or to the contribution.
