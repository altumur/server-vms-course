# V/03.1 — Delivery — a unit on demand: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`03.1-live.norms.md`](../03.1-live.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 1 of lesson V/03 «Живое видео» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../03-live.md),
[instructions](../03-live.README.md), manifest [`live.subsystem.yaml`](../../live.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (delivery of a stream — `41d3ec8`, V/01.1 Н1.1а; delivery per camera, the stream — at the gateway door — `b41527f`).

The unit `live` is the delivery of one camera to viewers: «camera 7 is watched through gateway g-2». The first viewer creates the row; the
gateway deletes it after a grace following the last one (chapter 5); the gateway capacity is in viewers, not in cameras. The chapter sets norms for the fields and
creation, placement, lease, snapshot and words. The source is chapter 2; the door is chapter 3.

Norm ids are `V/03.1-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `grace` is in seconds.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the row `live/streams/<cam>`
(P/06.1), the heartbeat `live/heartbeats/<воркер>`, the snapshot `live/snapshot/*` (P/04 Н1.3а); the chapter has no families of its own beyond them.

### Н1.1 Fields

`unit: {rows: streams, id: cam}` — the row `live/streams/<cam>`, its name is the camera id `cam` (V/01.1), without a number: one delivery per
camera; `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` — the events, grants and labels of a delivery are those of the camera. The name and `about` are one field, so
a grant on camera 7 creates only the delivery `7`, and the tombstone of a delivery always coincides with its lawful creation (the [row
check contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md) «Идентификатор»). **Which stream** of the camera to watch (V/01.1
Н1.1а) the row does not say: the viewer names it at the gateway door — `POST /whep/<cam>?stream=<id>` — or names the window size
(`?width=&height=`), and the gateway chooses the stream (chapter 3 Н1.2); each stream is a gateway subscription of its own (chapter 2).

| Field | Form | Meaning | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cam` | `string`, required, `fixed` | whose camera it delivers; equals the row name | the gateway (the source, chapter 2), `about` |
| `labels` | `list` | where the viewers are: labels of the viewers' network (`public-address` — outside the local network), not of the camera's network | placement (`labels-subset`) |
| `grace` | `int`, `minimum: 10`, `maximum: 3600`, default `60` | the seconds for which the gateway keeps the delivery's subscription after the last WHEP viewer leaves (an RTSP client does not keep the row, chapter 2 Н1.2); outside the bounds — 400 of the units door (the schema, P/06.1 Н4) | the gateway (chapter 5) |

The row check, `fixed` and defaults — the [row check contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md). The lower
bound of `grace` is 10 s: no less than the client's reconnect pause (1 s or 5 s and up to 3 s of jitter — up to 8 s, V/11.17), so that
a viewer who reconnects after a break finds the subscription; the upper one is an hour: a delivery without viewers does not hold the source longer. The default is 60 s: it fits the window of the client's
offer retries (the first offer and 20 retries, V/11.17); with a smaller `grace` the client retries while no less than `ICE_WAIT` is left until `expiresAt`.

### Н1.2 Creation and rights

`rights.routes: {view: [streams]}` — to watch a camera means to create its delivery: `POST /live/streams {cam, labels?}` with
the right `view` on the camera (the [gate and pass contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Права по телу»
`create_about`; the camera does not exist or its row is unreadable — a grant on `*` is needed, `unresolved`).

- the first viewer creates the row — 201 ([P/06.1](../../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) Н3 «создать»);
- the right by `cam` of the body is checked before the row is written: without `view` on the camera — 403, and such a viewer does not
  learn whether its delivery already exists (the gate and pass contract: rights checks — before the change);
- a second viewer of the same camera, of any of its streams, — 409 `exists`: it watches the existing delivery; its `labels` do not
  change the row;
- **the delivery's labels are set by the first viewer** (an accepted limitation): a viewer of another network gets 409 and the address of the existing
  delivery (`/live/where/<cam>`, chapter 3); if the gateway is unreachable for it — an edit of `labels` with the right `admin` on the unit (re-placement breaks the sessions,
  the page reconnects, V/11.17) or the end of the grace (chapter 5). Merging the labels by the second viewer would be a write with the right
  `view` (against «an edit is not viewing»), a delivery per (camera, label set) is a different `unit.id`; neither is taken up;
- `PUT /live/streams/<cam>` with `view` alone — 403: an edit of a delivery is not viewing;
- a delivery of a camera that does not exist is created (the door does not check the camera); the gateway does not deliver it and deletes it after `grace`
  (chapter 5);
- the stream is chosen not here but at the gateway door: the token is for the camera's delivery (`/live/where/<cam>`, the right `view` on the camera);
  the gateway does not deliver a stream that is not of this camera — 404 with words (chapter 3 Н1.2): the right `view` on a camera does not open another camera's stream.

Delivery rows are deleted by the gateway (`worker.deletes: [streams]`, chapter 5) and by the controller (`unplaced`, Н1.3); the console does not delete them
when viewers leave. The gateway does not write delivery rows (the loader rejects `unit.rows` in `worker.writes`).

### Н1.3 Placement

| Key | Value | Meaning | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | `{from: capacity, default: 100, unit_mb: 2, base_mb: 48}` | viewers per gateway; memory — 48 MiB + 2 MiB per unit (measurement of ADR-0070: a viewer on a delivery of its own is the worst case); a delivery with several subscriptions (chapter 2) costs the sum — 2 MiB per subscription, while placement counts it as one: a premise of the lesson, a delivery has no more subscriptions than the camera has streams (chapter 2 Н1.1), and the gateway capacity is in viewers | [P/04 Н1.3](../../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md) |
| `headroom` | `{from: headroom}` | how many more viewers the gateway will take: `max(0, capacity − sessions − answering)`, where `answering` is the offers in progress (chapter 3) | P/04 Н1.3 |
| `constraint` | `labels-subset` | the delivery's labels ⊆ the labels of the gateway's server | P/04 Н1.3; [labels contract](../../../../Platform/common/contracts/labels.md) |
| `tie_break` | `most-free-capacity` | | P/04 Н1.3 |
| `grows` | `true` | there are not enough gateways for a label set — the controller orders one more on a server (`<sub>/want/<сервер>`, the series `workers_wanted{server}`); growth is by waiting deliveries, not by viewers of a busy one: a delivery on a full gateway does not wait (P/04 Н3 «нехватка»); there are no spares | P/04 Н3, [P/02.4 Н1.5](../../../../Platform/02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md), ADR-0084 |
| `rebalance` | `{dead_band: 0.10}` | rebalancing on request; a spread under 10 % — do not move | P/04 Н1.3 |
| `unplaced` | `{delete_after: 30}` | a delivery that no one holds for 30 s (there is no gateway with its labels, or no gateways are left) is deleted by the controller; log `unit.unplaced_deleted` | P/04 Н1.3, Н3 step 8; ADR-0067 |

There are no keys `requires: resource` and `servers`: two gateways on one server are allowed with an OS port (a set port — one gateway per server, chapter 2 Н1); the gateway has no resource and no archive.

**The delivery limit** (an accepted limitation): a delivery has one holder, so the viewers of one camera are no more than the capacity of
one gateway; beyond it an offer gets 503 `full` (chapter 3 Н1.2). Placement counts deliveries, not viewers: a gateway full
of viewers of one camera stays free, and a new delivery may land on it and get 503 `full`. Several
holders of one delivery (a different `unit.id`) and knowledge of WHEP in the controller are not taken up.

### Н1.4 Lease, snapshot, words

- `lease: {unconfirmed_max: off}` ([P/03.2 Н1.6](../../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md)): the gateway does not deliver for a single
  moment after the end of the lease — a delivery under an unconfirmed lease would be a second subscription to the camera next to the gateway
  that took it over.
- `snapshot: [cam, labels]` (P/04 Н1.3а).
- `display`: «живой поток», «живые потоки», «живых потоков», the field captions `fields` and `field_help` (words of the console and
  the assistant, V/11.10 Н1.4); the series `streams_live` and `console.running` — chapter 5.

## Н3 Pass

The chapter has no pass of its own: placement is the controller's pass (P/04 Н3), deletion by `unplaced` is its step 8.

**Races.** Two first viewers of one camera: the write with `cas ""` lets one through — the second gets 409 `exists` and watches
the same delivery (P/06.1 «Гонки»). The gateway deleted the row (grace) while a new viewer is creating it: a creation after the deletion is
a new row, and the delivery comes up anew.

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the camera's delivery already exists | 409 `exists` | the response | watch the existing one (`/live/where/<cam>`, chapter 3) |
| an edit with `view` alone | 403 | the response | — |
| `cam` changes | 400 `fixed`, `field: /cam` | the response | — |
| no `cam` | 400 (P/06.1 Н4) | the response | the camera — in the body |
| no `view` on the camera of the body | 403 `denied` — before `exists` (Н1.2) | the response | a grant on the camera |
| `grace` outside 10–3600 | 400 (the schema, P/06.1 Н4) | the response | — |
| more viewers of one camera than the gateway capacity | 503 `full` at the offer (chapter 3) | the page | a retry; the accepted limit of Н1.3 |
| no gateway with the delivery's labels | `unplaceable`; after 30 s the row is deleted by the controller | the pass report, log `unit.unplaced_deleted` | the labels of the server or of the delivery |

## Н5 Acceptance

[Acceptance 1](../../acceptance/1/README.md), two stands. Scenario 01 (a console stand without a domain, one identity with full rights):
creation with the default `grace`, a repeat — 409 `exists`, `cam` is immutable, `grace` outside the bounds — 400. Scenario 02 (a VMS domain
stand, people with `view` on their own camera): another person's camera — 403 before `exists`, one's own — 201, another person's already created one — 403, not 409; `PUT`
with `view` alone — 403. By reading: deletion by the controller (`unplaced`) — acceptance of P/04 (sc. 18); the first viewer's labels and the delivery
limit — there is no gateway on the stand.

## Implementation decides

The chapter has no choice of its own: the implementation MAY override the default capacity (`capacity` 100) by a heartbeat field if its
memory measurement differs (ADR-0070), and then names this in its description.
