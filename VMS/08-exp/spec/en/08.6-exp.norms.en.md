# V/08.6 — The part — the export unit: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`08.6-exp.norms.md`](../08.6-exp.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 6 of lesson V/08 «Выгрузка» (until 2026-11-03 — chapter V/13.1, ADR-0118 addendum (1)) (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090; the export subsystem — ADR-0117 with addendum (1)–(6)).
[Lecture](../../08-exp.md), [instructions](../08-exp.README.md), manifest [`exp.subsystem.yaml`](../../exp.subsystem.yaml). The platform is
the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

An export is a copy of archive segments or of one frame into files with an expiry. An operator's order may name several cameras and
segments, but the unit of the subsystem is the **part**: one camera, one recording (or the live picture of that camera), its segments and its files. A part
stands next to its recording, cuts the video that it reads through the recorder doors, and keeps the files on its volume until the
deadline passes. There is no coordinator: `backend` assembles the progress and the result of an order by reading the parts (V/11). The chapter sets norms for the part row, its
placement, state, limits, rights and log.

Norm ids are `V/08.6-MM`; the correspondence to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#н0-словарь-слов).

- **Order** — one operator request `POST /exports` (V/11); an order has no row of its own (addendum (5) item 1).
- **Part** — the row `exp/parts/<name>`; **the leading part** — the part whose `name` equals `export`: it carries the order.
- **Part holder** — the worker `exp` to which placement gave the part.

## Н1 Form

**Data.** The rows `exp/parts/<name>` (units), the state `exp/state/cursor/<name>`, `exp/state/result/<name>` (P/03.6 Н1.7),
the requests `exp/requests/<rid>`, the part event log `exp/<name>/e<эпоха>/` with `of: cam/<cam>` (P/03.6 Н1.2). Reads: the heartbeats
`rec/heartbeats/*`, the rows `rec/recordings/*`, `rec/volumes/*`, `cam/cameras/*`, the heartbeats `live/heartbeats/*` (`worker.reads`);
creates and extends `live/streams/<cam>` (`worker.creates`, chapter 7 Н1.2); deletes its own rows `exp/parts/*`
(`worker.deletes`, chapter 8).

### Н1.1 The part row

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, required | the part id: for the leading part — the order id, for the others — `<id заказа>-<n>`, `n` from 1 in the order of the order's parts |
| `export` | `string`, required, `fixed` | the order id — the `name` of the leading part |
| `cam` | `string`, required, `fixed` | the camera; `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` — the rights, labels and log of the camera |
| `rec` | `string`, `fixed`, `ref: rec/recordings`, `must_match: {cam: cam}` | the archive recording; a live frame has none |
| `source` | `archive` \| `live`, required, `fixed` | where the frames come from |
| `mode` | `video` \| `frame`, required, `fixed` | video or one frame |
| `periods` | `json`, `fixed`: up to 32 segments `[from, to)`, Unix seconds of media time | what to export; a frame has none |
| `at` | `float`, `fixed` | the moment of an archive frame |
| `format` | `mkv` \| `mp4` \| `avi` \| `jpg` \| `pdf`, required, `fixed` | the container; `jpg`, `pdf` — only `mode: frame` |
| `settings` | `json`, `fixed`, the manifest schema | `quality`, `width`, `height`, `fps_div`, `audio`, `titles`, `font`, `area`, `single_file`, `file_mb`, `chapters` (chapter 7 Н1.4) |
| `password_secret` | `string` | the ZIP AES-256 or PDF password; it leaves together with the row (addendum (4) item 3) |
| `by` | `string`, required, `fixed` | the author: the person of the ticket that created the order; a copy in every part (addendum (6) item 4) |
| `comment` | `string` | the author's words; a copy in every part |
| `supervised_by` | `string`, `fixed` | the name of the person who confirmed the order (ADR-0119 item 5, addendum (1) item 3); a copy in every part; no supervision — no field |
| `retry_of` | `string`, `fixed` | the id of the order whose failed parts this one repeats (V/11) |
| `keep` | `int` ≥ 60, default 86400, `fixed`, `hidden` | how many seconds the files live after the end of the part — `EXPORT_KEEP` (chapter 8 Н1.4); the kind description does not show it, a person sees `result.expires` (М3 of the merge norms review; a decision of «Спецификация VMS») |
| `labels` | `list`, default `[]` | `["gpu"]` for a part that needs transcoding (chapter 7 Н1.4) |

- **Who writes.** The part rows are written **only** by `backend`: in one batch of the console door (`BATCH_WRITES`) with the person's ticket (V/11;
  addendum (4) item 1). The manifest word `writes_via: [backend]` (ADR-0119 addendum (10)) closes the person's session, the emergency login and the other
  tickets — `403`; so `supervised_by` cannot be forged by a session (P/06.1 Н1.1а). A write of the holder by the leaf of its own role (`state`,
  `worker.deletes`) is not judged by the word (addendum (11) item 1). Parts — a part per camera and recording; a live frame — one part without `rec`. The shared fields of the order (`by`, `comment`, `supervised_by`, `format`,
  `settings`, `keep`, `retry_of`) lie in every part identical, except `settings.area` — every part has the area box of its own camera
  (`cameras[i].area` of the order, ADR-0117 addendum (7) item 2), and `labels` — `gpu` only on a part with transcoding: the file door of a non-leading part judges its own `by`, and
  the leading part may be removed earlier than the others (addendum (6) item 4).
- **`fixed` fields** are not changed by an edit (the row check contract); there is no edit of a part row — there are requests (chapter 8).
- `jpg` or `pdf` with `mode: video`, `mkv`, `mp4`, `avi` with `mode: frame`, `periods` for a frame, `at` for a video, `source: live` with
  `rec` or with `at` — a refusal of `backend` at creation (V/11); the holder does not execute such a row: the result `failed`, `why` — the field.

### Н1.2 Placement

| Key | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | `{from: capacity, default: 64}` | parts that the worker **holds**, finished ones too — until the deadline of their files (addendum (4) item 2): this is memory of files, not work (С1 of the norms review; the word of «Архитектор») |
| `headroom` | `{from: headroom}` | how many more the worker will take |
| `constraint` | `labels-subset` | the `labels` of the part ⊆ the labels of the server: transcoding — on a server `gpu`; no server of the cluster has the label `gpu` — `backend` refuses an order with transcoding before the write (V/11; М2 of the norms review) |
| `near` | `{sub: rec, by: rec}` | affinity, not a filter: next to the recorder of the recording (ADR-0117 item 1.2) |
| `requires` | `resource` | the rows of the camera log — into the resource of its own server |
| `tie_break` | `most-free-capacity` | |

- **Work** (С1 of the norms review): no more than `EXP_ACTIVE` parts of a worker transcode at the same time — a key of the subsystem file
  `/etc/exp/exp.env`, default 2; the others wait without progress (`cursor.done` does not grow). Remuxing and frames are not limited by `EXP_ACTIVE`:
  up to `capacity` parts of a worker read `/samples` of the recorders at once, and their doors answer overload with the connection
  limits (`door_limits`, V/02.5). The answer `503 busy` of a recording door counts for the part as a door that did not answer: the segment — from another door
  with this span, otherwise the result `unreachable`, a repeat — `retry-failed` (V/11) — a limit (М1 of the merge norms review; a decision of
  «Спецификация VMS»). Space — `no_space` of the volume (below).
- **Without `ends` and `retire_when`**: a part is removed by its holder by deleting the row at the deadline (chapter 8 Н1.4; addendum (5) item 4, addendum (6)
  item 1). There is no pinning to a server after `done` in P/04: the holder has moved — the files of the part on the former volume are lost, the part is
  `expired` with a reason — a limit (addendum (4) item 2).
- **The volume** — the part `files` of the subsystem with `volumes: true` and the share `files` (`kind: dir`); its worker takes it through `uses: [exp.files]`
  (ADR-0117 addendum (3) «М»; P/02.4 Н1.1). The volume does not touch the archive ring. Space — the size of the volume: a shortage — a new part is refused
  (`no_space`), other files are not evicted (ADR-0117 item 1.7).

### Н1.3 State

- **`cursor`** — `{revision, done, bytes?, at}`: the revision of the row it refers to; the share of done work `0…1`; bytes written; when
  written. While the part runs — no less often than once in 5 s (ADR-0117 § 2). The truth is the `GET` of the row, the `backend` stream is its translation.
- **`result`** — `{end, expires?, code?, partial?, files?, why?}`: `end` — `done` | `failed` | `canceled` | `expired`; `code` — `no_recording`,
  `unreachable`, `no_space`, `limit`, `offline` (§ 2); `partial` — holes `[from, to)`; `files` — `[{id, name, size, sha256}]`,
  `id` — `f<n>`; `expires` — the moment of the end of the part + `keep`: the end of the files and of the row (chapter 8 Н1.4; М1 of the norms review); `why` — words.
  A partial success is the norm: `done` with `partial`.

### Н1.4 Limits

The values are those of «Архитектор», changed by measurement (ADR-0117 § 3); all are given out in `reference.export.limits` (V/11):

| Constant | Value | Who judges |
|---|---|---|
| `EXPORT_TOTAL_MAX` | 24 h of video in an order in total | `backend` at creation; the holder — for its own part |
| `EXPORT_PERIODS_MAX` | 32 | the schema of the field `periods`; `backend` — by the order |
| `EXPORT_CAMERAS_MAX` | 16 | `backend` |
| `EXPORT_ACTIVE_PER_USER` | 4 unfinished orders of a person | `backend` |
| `EXPORT_FILE_MB` | 4000 MB — file pieces, the default; the window changes it in `settings.file_mb` up to the limit | the schema `settings` |
| `EXPORT_LINK_TTL` | the timeout of the platform `where` token — 120 s (addendum (3) «М») | the console |
| requests of a person | `per_person: 64` = `EXPORT_CAMERAS_MAX` × `EXPORT_ACTIVE_PER_USER`, `cancel` and `delete` together («М» of the review of addendum (6)) | the console |
| parts that a worker holds | `capacity` 64 | placement |
| simultaneous transcodings of a worker | `EXP_ACTIVE` = 2 | the worker |

### Н1.5 Rights

- **Creating** a part — `edit` on `exp` by the camera (`rights.routes: {edit: [parts]}`; ADR-0117 item 4.1), the right — by the `about`
  of the camera. The role functions `EXPORT`, `ALLOW_UNPROTECTED_EXPORT`, `ALLOW_SHOW_PRIVACY_VIDEO_IN_ARCHIVE` are translated into the ranks of `exp`
  (V/11.8; addendum (4) item 4, addendum (5) item 3, addendum (6) item 3): `edit` — `EXPORT`; `admin` — `EXPORT` and both flags. A live frame — also
  the right of live viewing of the camera: judged by `backend` at creation (addendum (2) item 3).
- **Without a mask** an export is available only with `admin` on `exp`: there is no masking yet, a mandatory mask cannot be executed (addendum (4)
  item 5); `backend` refuses before the write (addendum (2) item 4).
- **One's own and another's.** The link and the bytes are received only by the author — the person `by` of the part; the right to the log gives only information about another's
  part (addendum (2) item 5). `admin` on `exp` removes also the parts of others for its own cameras (addendum (5) item 3).
- **Supervision** (ADR-0119): whether the author's role requires confirmation and whether the supervisor confirms is judged by the cluster console
  with the check `/domain/verify` with `need {admin, exp, [cam/<id>…]}` by the ticket of `backend`; `backend` writes the parts only after
  `200` with `supervision.ok` and `may: allow` for every camera, with `supervised_by` — the name of the checked person (V/11; addendum (5), (6)). The part
  holder itself does not judge supervision: a row without `supervised_by` when supervision is required is not written by the `backend` door.
- **`depth`** of the role is not judged by the export, as by the archive (V/11.8 — 422): a limit of the first generation (addendum (5) item 4).

### Н1.6 Log

The rows of the part event log with `of: cam/<cam>` are the camera log (`about`); they are written by the part holder (P/03.6 Н1.2), the class is
observation:

| Kind | When | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `export.made` | the holder started the part | `export`, `by`, `source`, `mode`, `periods` or `at`, `format`, `password` — `true` if a password is given (the password itself — no), `mask`, `comment`, `supervised_by` — if present |
| `export.refused` | the holder refused the part before work (`limit` — also an area box smaller than 16 px, `no_space`, a field not in form) | `export`, `by`, `code`, `why` |
| `export.finished` | the result `done` | `export`, `files` (`name`, `size`, `sha256`), `partial` |
| `export.issued` | the file door sees for the first time a token of this person for this part | `export`, `user` |
| `export.read` | one download of a file (chapter 9 Н1.2) | `export`, `user`, `file` |
| `export.deleted` | the files are deleted: request, cancellation, deadline; a part without a holder has no row — a row of the console log about the deletion (chapter 8 Н1.4) | `export`, `by` (who), `why` (`delete` \| `cancel` \| `expired`) |

A refusal of `backend` before the parts are written (403, 422) is its answer and the log of the console door, not the camera log: there are no parts yet.

## Н2 Doors

The rows — the console door (P/06.1), the batch — `BATCH_WRITES` (V/11). The file door — chapter 9.

## Н3 Pass

```
backend: order → check (V/11) → batch of parts → placement (near rec, labels) → holder: export.made → work (chapter 7)
  → result (Н1.3) → files until result.expires (chapter 8) → deletion of the files and the row
```

**Races.** Two parts of one order on different servers run independently; the overall result is the sum of the parts (V/11). A request `cancel`
earlier than the start of a part — the part does not start, the result `canceled` (chapter 8).

---

## Н4 Refusals

| Reason | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| no `edit` on `exp` by the camera | 403 of the console door, the batch is not written | `backend` → the operator | — |
| a field not by the manifest schema | 400 of the console door | `backend` | — |
| the combination of fields is wrong (Н1.1) | `backend` — 422; a row without it — the result `failed` | the operator | — |
| no space on the volume | the result `failed` `no_space`, `export.refused` | the operator | later or smaller |
