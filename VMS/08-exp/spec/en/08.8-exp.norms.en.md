# V/08.8 — Cancellation, deletion, expiry: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`08.8-exp.norms.md`](../08.8-exp.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 8 of lesson V/08 «Выгрузка» (until 2026-11-03 — chapter V/13.3, ADR-0118 addendum (1)) (ADR-0117 § 2, addendum (2) item 1, addendum (4) items 2, 3, addendum (5) items 2–4, addendum (6) items 1, 2).
[Lecture](../../08-exp.md), [instructions](../08-exp.README.md). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The part row is written by `backend`, and it is removed by its holder: the removal of a unit does not delete the row (addendum (6) item 1). Therefore everything that
ends a part — cancellation, deletion, expiry — goes to the holder: by a request or by its own clock. The chapter sets norms for the requests
`cancel` and `delete`, the expiry of the files and of the row, and the loss of the holder.

Norm ids are `V/08.8-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords follow the [course canon](../../../../НОРМАТИВНЫЕ-СЛОВА.md). The lesson's words — chapter 6 Н0.

## Н1 Form

**Data.** Those of chapter 6; the requests `exp/requests/<rid>` `{unit, action}` (P/06.4 Н1.5), the result is a mark.

### Н1.1 Requests

- **Form** — the `requests.schema` of the manifest: `{unit: <name части>, action: cancel | delete}`, `valid_for` 30 s, `most_valid` 600 s,
  `ttl` 600 s, `per_person` 64 (chapter 6 Н1.4), `stamp: [by, at]`. The result is a mark (`outcome: mark`).
- **Right** — `edit` on `exp` by the part's camera, as for creation (addendum (5) item 2). `backend` files a request to each part of the order
  (V/11); the holder MUST execute only the parts whose `by` is the person of the request, or under `admin` on `exp` by the camera (addendum (5) item 3);
  otherwise the mark is `refused` `not the author`.

### Н1.2 Cancellation

`cancel`: a part that is still running MUST finish the current piece and MUST NOT start the next one; the finished files of the part are deleted;
`result.end: canceled`; a row `export.deleted` with `why: cancel`, if there were files (ADR-0117 § 2). A part not yet started is `canceled`
at once. A finished (`done`) part is not touched by cancellation: the mark is `done` `nothing to cancel`. The part row stays until `result.expires` (Н1.4) — the operator
sees the result.

### Н1.3 Deletion

`delete` (`DELETE /exports/{id}` of the author, V/11): the holder MUST delete the part's files, write `export.deleted` with `why: delete` and
delete the part row whole by CAS (`worker.deletes: [parts]`), together with `password_secret` (addendum (6) item 1). A running part
is cancelled first (Н1.2).

### Н1.4 Expiry

- `keep` is set by `backend` at creation: `EXPORT_KEEP` is a system setting, default 24 h (addendum (1) item 1; V/11). The expiry
  counts from the end of the part, not from creation (М1 of the norms review): the holder MUST write `result.expires` = the moment of `end` + `keep` — for
  `done`, `failed`, `canceled` alike; a part that waited long for placement or transcoding does not lose its files.
- At `result.expires` the holder MUST delete the part's files, write `export.deleted` with `why: expired` and delete the row (addendum (6) item 1). The password
  lives as long — up to 24 h, a limit (addendum (4) item 3).
- **The holder is lost.** A new holder does not find the files on its volume: `result.end: expired`, `why` — `files lost with the
  former holder`; it deletes the row at `result.expires` — the moment when it learned this, + `keep` (addendum (2) item 1, addendum (4) item 2 — a limit).
- **A part without a holder** (ADR-0119 addendum (11) item 2, addendum (12)). The holder is lost if the decision `exp/placement/<part>` does not
  name a worker or the heartbeat of the named one is older than `LOST_AFTER` (P/03.4). Such a part is deleted by `backend` by a ticket — on `DELETE
  /exports/{id}` of the author (`by`) or of an administrator, with the same right as for a request: deletion of the row by CAS of the revision read
  together with the placement decision (V/11, with the contract `/exports`); a session gets `403` (`writes_via`). There is no row `export.deleted` for such a part:
  it is written by the holder into the log of its subsystem, and there is no holder; the deletion is visible as the console log row about the deletion of the part
  row with `by` — the person of the ticket (P/06.1), a limit (М3 of the norms review (10)–(12); a decision of «Спецификация VMS»). A returning former
  holder MUST make every write about a part by CAS of the row it read: if there is no row, the write does not pass, and it writes nothing; the files of
  parts without a row on its volume it MUST delete on start-up, before it takes parts (finding «М» of the review of addendum (12)). A holder that returned
  before the deletion does not stop the deletion: its heartbeat and `state` are its own rows, they change neither the revision of the part row nor the placement decision,
  and the CAS of `backend` passes. The deletion was requested; the holder on its first pass sees that there is no row, drops the work and
  cleans up the files (М1 of the norms review (10)–(12)).
- **A restart of the holder** on the same server does not lose files: the volume persists, the part keeps serving.

### Н1.5 Retry

A retry of failed parts is a new order with `retry_of` (V/11; ADR-0117 § 2). A repeat of the same request with the same `idempotencyKey`
gives the same order — the memory of `backend` (V/11.4 Н1.4).

## Н2 Doors

Requests — the console door `POST /exp/requests` (P/06.4 Н1.5); deletion of the row — the row door by CAS, by the holder.

---

## Н3 Pass

```
cancel → holder: running? finish the piece → delete the files → canceled (+ export.deleted)
delete → holder: cancel → delete the files → export.deleted → delete the row
result.expires → holder: delete the files → export.deleted expired → delete the row
```

**Races.** `delete` and `result.expires` at the same time — the first one deletes, the second sees that there is no row: nothing. A new revision of the row
between the read and the deletion — CAS refuses, the holder re-reads.

---

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| a request not of the author without `admin` | the mark `refused` `not the author` | `backend` → operator | — |
| `cancel` of a finished part | the mark `done` `nothing to cancel` | `backend` | — |
| more than 64 requests of a person | 429 of the console door (P/06.4) | `backend` | later |

---

## Н5 Acceptance

[Acceptance 6](../../acceptance/6/README.md): cancellation midway — no files, `canceled`; deletion — no row, `export.deleted`;
expiry — after `result.expires` no files and no row; a restart of the holder — the files are there.

---

## Implementation decides

The holder's clock for `result.expires`, the order of deleting the files.
