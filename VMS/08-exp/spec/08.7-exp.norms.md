# V/08.7 — The source and the assembly of a file: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/08.7-exp.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 7 of lesson V/08 Export (until 2026-11-03 — chapter V/13.2, ADR-0118 addendum (1)) (ADR-0117 items 1.3–1.5, § 7, addendum (1) item 1, addendum (2) item 3, addendum (4) items 1, 5).
Lecture (`ru/08-exp.md`, for people, not in the export), [instructions](08-exp.README.md). The platform is the export `cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The work goes on next to the archive: video does not travel over the network for the sake of transcoding. A part reads its segments through the recorder doors, like
a detection job (V/05), takes a live frame from the gateway, and assembles the files on its volume. The chapter sets norms for the sources, holes, what
a part does with the frames and how the leading part assembles «in one file».

Norm ids are `V/08.7-MM`.

## N0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The words of the lesson — chapter 6 N0.

- **Remuxing** — the H.264 frames of a recording into another container without decoding; **transcoding** — with decoding.

## N1 Form

**Data.** Those of chapter 6; its own files — on the volume of the part (chapter 6 N1.2).

### N1.1 Archive

- **Whom to ask, the plan and the reading** — as a detection job [V/05.2](../../05-detjob/spec/05.2-detjob.norms.md) N1.1–N1.3: the live
  archive doors by the heartbeats of the recorders, `/spans/<record>` at each, the plan by the epoch rule (V/02.5 N1.3), `/samples/<record>?from&to`
  with `stream` at the door that named the span, the lead-in from a key frame. Access — the role `expworker` in `door.routes` of `rec` (V/02.5 N2;
  ADR-0117 item 1.3). Audio — the same segment with `track=audio` (V/02.5 N1.1), if `settings.audio`.
- **Holes** (ADR-0117 item 1.5). A segment that no live door has is a hole: the file goes with the hole, `result.partial`
  names it. There is no catch-up recording and no waiting; the export does not set a hold — a hold has its own button and right. Not a single frame in all
  segments — the result `failed` `no_recording`. Not a single recording door answered — `failed` `unreachable`.
- **Another cluster** — the part is not created: `409 OTHER_CLUSTER` with the link «Open» is given by `backend` (V/11; ADR-0117 item 1.6).

### N1.2 Live frame

- A part `source: live` creates and extends the delivery `live/streams/<cam>` (`worker.creates`, P/03.6 N1.6), as the first viewer:
  the author's right to the live picture was judged by `backend` at creation (ADR-0117 addendum (2) item 3). The extension — while the frame has not been received and
  `FRAME_WAIT` has not passed; then the part does not extend the delivery, and the gateway removes it by `grace` (V/03.5). The outcome of `creates` is `taken` (the row was created by
  a person): the delivery is another's, the part does not extend it and takes the frame while it is alive (M5 of the norms review).
- The gateway — the one whose heartbeat `live/heartbeats/*` holds the delivery of the camera (the element `status`); the frame — `GET /frame/<cam>` of its door by
  the certificate of the role `expworker` (V/03.3 N1.5): `200 image/jpeg`. Unreadable words of the gateway heartbeat — the outcomes
  [`live-heartbeat-words.outcomes.tsv`](../../03-live/data/live-heartbeat-words.outcomes.tsv), the reader `expworker`.
- Not a single gateway gave a frame within `FRAME_WAIT` (V/03.3 N1.5) — the result `failed` `offline`. The frame is decoded and cropped by `settings.area`
  (N1.4 «Area»; ADR-0117 addendum (7) item 1); the file — `jpg`, `pdf` — a page with this frame. A frame of the camera holder and the last block of a recording are never a source (ADR-0117 item 1.4).

### N1.3 Archive frame

`mode: frame`, `source: archive`: the nearest key frame not later than `at` and decoding up to `at` (§ 7); there is no frame within
`FRAME_BACK` = 10 s before `at` — `failed` `no_recording` (a decision of VMS Specification). The frame is cropped by `settings.area` (N1.4
«Area»); the file — `jpg` or `pdf`.

### N1.4 Video

| What | How |
|---|---|
| `quality: original`, without `width`, `height`, `fps_div`, `titles`, `area`, mask | remuxing into `mkv`, `mp4`, `avi` |
| otherwise | transcoding: size, frame rate divisor, compression step, software captions (`camera`, `time`, `date`, `comment`, font `font`), area `area` (shares of the frame `[x, y, w, h]`) |
| `chapters` | chapter bookmarks of the container `{at, title}` (`mkv`, `mp4`) |
| `audio` | a second track (N1.1) |
| `file_mb` | splitting at key frames: a file piece is no larger than `file_mb` MB (default `EXPORT_FILE_MB`) |
| password | `password_secret` — ZIP AES-256 of the part's files; for `pdf` — the password of the PDF itself (addendum (1) item 1) |

- **Area** (ADR-0117 addendum (7); «M» of the review, S2 of Specification). The shares `[x, y, w, h]` of the camera's area box are converted into pixels of the
  stream frame: `x`, `y` — rounded down, `w`, `h` — rounded down to an integer; for video all four are also rounded down to an even number (H.264 with colour 4:2:0), for a frame
  `jpg`, `pdf` — not. The smallest side of the area box in pixels is less than 16 — a refusal of the part before work: the result `failed`, `code: limit`, `why` — `area smaller
  than 16 px`, the row `export.refused` (chapter 6 N1.6; M2 of the norms review (10)–(12); a decision of VMS Specification). The area box by
  shares (`w > 0`, `h > 0`, `x + w ≤ 1`, `y + h ≤ 1`) is judged by `backend` before the write — `422 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `/cameras/i/area`
  (V/11, with the contract `/exports`). The transcoding order: mask → crop → scale (`width`, `height`) → captions: the mask is laid onto
  the source frame, the captions — onto the finished one and are not cropped; there is no mask yet (ADR-0117 addendum (4) item 5).

- **Whether transcoding is needed** is a function of `format` and `settings`: the table `reference.export` (V/11) gives it out by combinations,
  `backend` sets on the part `labels: ["gpu"]` when needed (chapter 6 N1.2).
- **The file name** — `<cam>-<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ start>-<n>.<extension>` (a decision of VMS Specification); the moment of the start is in the zone `settings.tz` — an IANA name that `backend` writes from the time zone of the system (the Architect's decision 2026-11-04), then without `Z`; without `tz` — UTC with `Z`; for ZIP — `.zip`; `n` from 1
  by the splitting.
- **Space.** Before the start the holder estimates the size: remuxing — the sum of the span bytes, transcoding — by the step; the free
  space of the volume is smaller — `failed` `no_space`, `export.refused` (chapter 6 N1.6).
- **Progress** — `cursor.done` by the media time of what is finished, no less often than once in 5 s (chapter 6 N1.3).

### N1.5 «In one file»

`settings.single_file` — only `mkv` (addendum (1) item 1): the tracks of all cameras of the order in one MKV. It is assembled by **the leading part**:
when all the other parts of the order are `done`, it takes their files through the part door `parts` (chapter 9 N1.3) and writes one file on its volume;
the other parts keep their files as usual (addendum (4) item 1). A failed part — without its own track, the `partial` of the leading part
names it. The leading part waits for the others no longer than they themselves run: a part `failed` or `canceled` — there is no track.

## N2 Doors

Reads — the archive doors of `rec` (`/spans`, `/samples`, the role `expworker`), the gateway door of `live` (`/frame`, the role `expworker`), the part
doors of `exp` (`/parts`, chapter 9). The chapter has no doors of its own.

## N3 Pass

```
video: recording doors → plan (V/05.2) → samples → remuxing | transcoding → splitting → password → files → result done
frame archive: key ≤ at → decoding → jpg | pdf
frame live: live/streams/<cam> (creates) → gateway by heartbeat → GET /frame → jpg | pdf
single_file: leading ← files of the other parts (/parts) → one mkv
```

**Races.** The recorder moved during the reading — the next segment by the new heartbeat, as with a job (V/05.2). A cancellation during
a piece — the piece plays to the end, the next one does not start (chapter 8).

## N4 Refusals

| Reason | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| there are no frames in all segments | `failed` `no_recording` | the operator — the form | — |
| not a single recording door answered | `failed` `unreachable` | the operator | a repeat `retry-failed` |
| there is no space | `failed` `no_space` | the operator | later, smaller |
| the gateway frame did not come | `failed` `offline` | the operator | a repeat |
| a segment without frames | `done` with `partial` | the operator | — |
