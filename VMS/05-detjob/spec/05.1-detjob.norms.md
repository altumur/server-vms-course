# V/05.1 — A job is a unit that ends: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/05.1-detjob.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 1 of lesson V/05 «Задача по интервалу архива» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/05-detjob.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](05-detjob.README.md), manifest [`detjob.subsystem.yaml`](../detjob.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the stream of a job is the stream of the recording, `41d3ec8`; a mask with its own size — `b41527f`).

A job is one model on one interval of one recording. Its work ends: the result lies in the state of the unit, a finished
job the platform does not place and deletes after three days. The chapter sets norms for the job row, who creates it, the result, placement and
the snapshot. Reading — chapter 2; work and events — chapter 3; waiting and requests — chapter 4.

Norm ids are `V/05.1-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `from`, `to` are Unix seconds of **media** time (the time of the video), not the moment the job was created.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): the row `detjob/jobs/<name>` (P/06.1),
the state `detjob/state/result/<job>`, `detjob/state/cursor/<job>` ([P/03.6 Н1.7](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)),
the snapshot `detjob/snapshot/*`; the chapter has no families of its own beyond them.

### Н1.1 Fields

`unit: {rows: jobs, id: name}`; `about: {sub: cam, field: cam}` — the events, grants and labels of a job are the camera's.

| Field | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | `string`, required | the name of the run; orders — `rec-…`, `auto-…` (Н1.2) |
| `cam` | `string`, required, `fixed` | the camera whose video is watched; the `of` of every event row; the stream is the one the recording `rec` recorded on the segment (its `stream` at the hour of recording, V/02.1: a change of the recording's `stream` is a new sequence, a segment may carry two streams of different frame size); the job has no `stream` field of its own |
| `rec` | `string`, required, `fixed`, `ref: rec/recordings`, `must_match: {cam: cam}` | a recording of **this** camera, whose streams are read (V/02.1); a recording of another camera — a refusal |
| `kind` | `string`, required | the model kind — the detector registry (V/04.2 Н1.2), including the teaching model `tick` |
| `params` | `string` | the model settings, opaque; the limit is the general row limit (413 `toolarge`, P/06.1 Н1.4) |
| `mask` | `blob` | the mask as a body (P/06.1 Н1.5): a PGM profile with its own size, applied to a frame of another size by nearest neighbour (V/04.1 Н1.1 «Маска и размер кадра») |
| `mask_of` | `string`, `ref: det/units`, `must_match: {cam: cam}` | whose mask: the id of a detector of the same camera (the base of an order, V/02.8 Н1.5); the body — by V/04.1 Н1.1 «Какая маска» (V/05.3 Н1.3): the mask is the **current** one — the one the detector has when the model starts, not the one it had at the moment of the order; there is no detector — the result `failed` |
| `alarms` | `list`, default `["linecross", "lpr"]` | which event kinds are alarms (chapter 3); orders copy it from the detector |
| `from`, `to` | `float`, required | the interval of media time, `[from, to)`; `asked` = `max(0, to − from)`; the manifest does not express a relation between fields — the holder rule below |
| `labels` | `list`, default `["gpu"]` | the labels of the worker's server |

The row has no fields `state` and `ended`: the result is the state of the unit (Н1.3).

**Holder rules** — relations the door does not check (the schema is per field; a `ref` without a target is not a refusal by the contract
of the row check, `ref_and_must_match`):

- `to ≤ from`, equality included, — the model does not start, the result is `{end: failed, covered: 0, asked: 0, events: 0, why:
  "empty interval: to ≤ from"}`;
- there is no row `rec/recordings/<rec>` or it is a tombstone — the result is `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why: "no
  recording <rec>: whose footage it is cannot be checked"}` (`covered`, `events` — from the cursor): the archive door would show the timeline even without the row (V/02.5 Н1.2),
  but `must_match` without a target has not checked the camera; the row exists but is not readable by its form, or the store did not answer — this is not
  «no recording» (the class «unreadable is not absent»; the analysis of the V/06 review, Kimi's addition, question 1): `waiting`, `reason`
  `recording <rec> unreadable`, the waiting accumulates `waited`, at `WAIT_MAX` — the result `failed` «`recording <rec> unreadable`»
  (V/05.4 Н1.2).

Results — V/05.3 Н1.5. The row check, `fixed`, `ref`, `must_match` —
the [contract of the row check](../../../Platform/common/contracts/row-check.md).


**Rights.** `rights.routes: {edit: [jobs]}`: creating takes `edit` on the camera, the `about` of the row; an edit and removal take `admin` on the unit, the gate contract `need`.

### Н1.2 Who creates

- **The operator** — through the units door of the console (`POST /detjob/jobs`, P/06.1) with an interval of the timeline. **A repeated run is a new
  row** (a new name): the result of a finished job stands while the row is not edited (Н1.3).
- **The recorder** — by a scan order of the catch-up recorded minutes (`worker.creates` to `detjob`, the key `{cam}-{rec}-{kind}-{from}-{to}`,
  the row `rec-<key>`; V/02.8 Н1.5): one job per window, as with a scenario; the fields are those of the base (`params`, `labels`, `alarms`), `mask_of` — by V/02.8 Н1.5.
- **A scenario** — by a scan order around an event (`auto-…`; the key is the window `(cam, rec, kind, from, to)`, V/07.4 Н1.3): one job
  per window — an equal window, including one from another scenario with other `params`, is the same row, the order answers `unchanged`, the fields are
  those of the first order; there is no widening of the window, another window is another job.

An order row carries `made` ([P/03.6 Н1.6](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)); the operator MAY edit any of its
fields except the `fixed` fields (`cam`, `rec`); any edit of the row, the mask included, raises `revision` — the result does not stand,
the scan runs anew (Н1.3).

### Н1.3 Result and retirement

- **The result** is `unit.state.result` `{end: done | failed, covered, asked, events, partial?, why?}`: the holder writes it under the guard
  of the epoch (P/03.6 Н1.7); it stands while the job row is live and its `born`, `revision` equal those of the state — it survives a move: a new
  holder does not execute a finished job again. `covered` — seconds viewed, `asked` — seconds of the interval, `events` —
  rows of the segments that went into `done` (V/05.3 Н1.2; `scan.partial` and repeats after the cursor are not counted), `partial` — what was not
  waited for (chapter 4).
- **Retirement** — `retire_when: {state: result, field: end, in: [done, failed], delete_after: 259200}`
  ([P/04 Н1.3](../../../Platform/04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md)): there is a result — the controller does not place the job and
  retires it with the reason (`done` | `failed`); an order row (`made`) it deletes three days after retirement; a manual one it does not:
  in a table with `fixed` fields the timeout deletes only order rows, a manual one is retired (the count `expired_kept`) and the operator deletes it. The fact «done» cannot live in the heartbeat: a retired job would leave the heartbeat,
  it would be placed again and started from the beginning.
- The phases of work (chapter 4) are only in the heartbeat; any edit of a finished job changes `revision` — the result stops
  standing, the job is placed and runs anew.

### Н1.4 Placement

| Key | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `capacity` | `{from: capacity, default: 2}` | jobs per worker — a budget of its own: retro search does not spend the streams of live detection |
| `headroom` | `{from: headroom}` | the headroom — the platform (P/03.4 Н1.1) |
| `constraint` | `labels-subset` | |
| `requires` | `resource` | events go into the resource of its own server |
| `near` | `{sub: rec, by: rec}` | a preference: next to the recorder of the recording the job reads (by the field `rec`, not by the job name) — the video does not cross the network; if that fails — any server with a GPU, reading through the doors over the network |
| `tie_break` | `most-free-capacity` | |

There is no `home`; there is no `grows`. The snapshot is `snapshot: [name, cam, kind, state.result]` (P/04 Н1.3а): to the domain — «camera 7 is being scanned» and
how it ended; the interval and the recording are not needed above the cluster.

## Н2 Doors

The units door of the console — `POST|GET /detjob/jobs`, `PUT|DELETE /detjob/jobs/<id>`, the mask — `PUT /detjob/jobs/<id>/mask`
(P/06.1); `detjob` has no door of its own.

---

## Н3 Pass

The chapter has no pass of its own: retirement and deletion are the pass of the controller (P/04 Н3), orders are the passes of the orderers (P/03.6 Н1.6),
the result is written by the holder (chapter 3).

**Races.** The result is written and the controller has not yet retired the job — the holder reads the standing result and does not execute the job; a new
holder after a move does the same. The operator edits the job while the holder writes the result: the result of the old `revision` does not stand,
the job runs anew.

## Н4 Refusals

| Reason | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rec` is a recording of another camera | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match` | the answer | a recording of this camera |
| `mask_of` is a detector of another camera | 409 `conflict`, `fault: must_match` | the answer | a detector of this camera |
| `mask_of` — there is no detector (at the write, or deleted later) | not a refusal of the door (`ref` does not require a target); at start — `failed` (V/05.3 Н1.3) | the job result | a detector of this camera |
| `cam` or `rec` changes | 400 `fixed`, `field` | the answer | a new job |
| the name `rec-…`, `auto-…` given by hand | 400 (the names of the orderers) | the answer | another name |
| no `from`, `to`, `kind` | 400 | the answer | — |
| `to ≤ from` | accepted; the result `failed` «`empty interval: to ≤ from`» (holder rule) | the job result | a new job |
| there is no recording `rec` (or a tombstone) | accepted; the result `failed` «`no recording <rec>: …`» (holder rule) | the job result | a new job on a live recording |
| the row of the recording `rec` is not readable, the store did not answer | `waiting` `recording <rec> unreadable`; at `WAIT_MAX` — the result `failed` (V/05.4 Н1.2) | the heartbeat, the result | fix the recording row |
