# V/05.3 — Budget, progress, events, result: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/05.3-detjob.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 3 of lesson V/05 «Задача по интервалу архива» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). Lecture (`ru/05-detjob.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](05-detjob.README.md), manifest [`detjob.subsystem.yaml`](../detjob.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75` (the mask to the frame of a segment — `b41527f`).

A job reads hours of video faster than real time — so it works in portions, remembers what has already been read, and writes the result
when everything has been read. The chapter sets norms for the pass budget, the cursor, the model, events and the result. Reading a segment is chapter 2; waiting and
requests are chapter 4.

Norm ids are `V/05.3-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н0 Vocabulary

Normative keywords (MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, MAY by RFC 2119 and RFC 8174) follow the [course canon](../../../Platform/common/NORMATIVE-TERMS.md). The rules of the module «Платформа» are [P/01.1 Н0](../../../Platform/01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#n0-vocabulary).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** `t` of an event row is the writer's clock (the event log contract), the **media** time of the frame is its `occurred`; timeouts are
the monotonic clock of the process.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): writes — the state
`detjob/state/cursor/<job>`, `detjob/state/result/<job>` ([P/03.6 Н1.7](../../../Platform/03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md)),
the epoch `detjob/epoch/<job>` (on taking the unit, P/03.3), events — into the resource log of its own server `detjob/<job>/e<эпоха>/`,
alarms — into `detjob.alarms/<job>/e<эпоха>/` (P/05 Н1.2); reads — the detector rows `det/units/*` (`mask_of`), the mask
bodies `det/blobs/*`, `detjob/blobs/*`.

### Н1.1 Pass budget

- The unit of the budget is one read: a segment of the plan (chapter 2 Н1.2) longer than `READ_SECONDS` = 60 s of media time is read
  in pieces from its own start, `done` grows piece by piece. Per pass the job MUST read no more than `STRETCHES_PER_PASS` = 4 pieces:
  a long pass would leave the worker without a heartbeat, the lease would expire — a second writer. Reading and the model MUST NOT run on the thread of the
  runtime loop (as V/01.4 Н1.3): the heartbeat and the lease do not wait for the read.
- A job of the assignment whose model is still loading is in phase `queued` (chapter 4): placement gives a worker no more than `capacity` jobs
  (P/04); how many models are held at once is «Implementation decides».

### Н1.2 Cursor

- `unit.state.cursor` is `{revision, done: [[from, to], …], events, waited?, fetch_waited?}` (the accumulated waiting
  timeouts — chapter 4 Н1.2, Н1.4): `done` — the read intervals of the recording's media time
  (merged, ascending), `events` — the number of rows of the segments that entered `done` (`scan.partial` and repeats after the cursor do not count),
  `revision` — the revision of the job row the cursor belongs to. It is monotonic within its own `revision`: `done` only grows.
- It is written **after** the events of a segment (first the rows, then the mark), no more often than `STATE_EVERY` = 10 s (P/03.6 Н1.7); while
  `waited`, `fetch_waited` grow, it is written also without a new segment, with the same limit: after
  a move the new holder reads the cursor and continues. All the work after the last written cursor is repeated —
  up to `STATE_EVERY` of the holder's work, more when a cursor write is refused; there is no limit in seconds of video (the scan is faster than
  real time). A repeat loses no findings: under a new epoch its rows are `earlier-run` (Н1.4); under the same one (an edit,
  a restart, a break of a segment) — next to the former ones, duplicates are allowed (the log is «at least once»).
- The cursor acts by `born` (P/03.6 Н1.7); the job MUST NOT apply a cursor with a foreign `revision`: an edit of the job is a scan anew,
  the cursor of the new `revision` starts empty: `done`, `events`, `waited`, `fetch_waited` are reset.
- **Remainder** — the segments of the plan (chapter 2 Н1.2) minus `done`, by **time**, not by key and not by stream: a growing span
  changes the key of the segment, a higher epoch takes the minutes of the overlap — what was read by time is not read again.
- **Hole** — the minutes `[from, to)` without a segment of the plan and outside `done`; it is not remainder, its fate is decided by chapter 4 (Н1.2–Н1.4): a permanent
  hole does not hold the result; that not everything was viewed is said by `covered` < `asked`.

### Н1.3 Model

The kind registry is the same as the detector's (V/04.2 Н1.2): the worker does not have the kind — `unsupported` (chapter 4). The mask — by V/04.1
Н1.1 «Какая маска»: its own non-empty `mask` (the body `detjob/blobs/<digest>`) first, otherwise the mask of the row `det/units/<mask_of>`
at the moment the model starts (the body `det/blobs/<digest>`), neither — without a mask; it is read when the model starts. The row `mask_of`
does not exist or is a tombstone (deleted later, or it did not exist at writing either — the door does not require the target, V/05.1 Н4) — the result `failed`
«`the detector <id> whose mask this order takes is gone`» (Н1.5); the row is unreadable or the store is silent, the body is not
readable or not by the PGM profile — a start failure, a repeat by the runtime backoff (P/03.3). `stalled` is the question `stalled(id)` to
the library (P/03.1 Н2), there is no failure count of its own — the result `failed`. The mask is the current mask of the detector, not the one it had
at the moment of the order (V/05.1 Н1.1). The mask applies to every frame of the segment: a frame of another
size (another stream of the recording, V/02.1) — by nearest neighbour, the profile and the rule of the detector (V/04.1 Н1.1 «Маска и размер кадра»;
vectors [`mask`](../../04-det/vectors/mask/README.md)). The model is given the frames of the segment (`t0 ≤ ts < t1`, chapter 2 Н1.3).

**The teaching `tick` in a job** is in media time, the grid is set by the interval, not by the slicing: look `k` = 0, 1, 2, … at
the moment `t_k = from + k × STEP` (`STEP` = 1 s, binary64); the event `tick` — at `k` a multiple of 3, `k ≥ 3`, and only if
`t_k` lies in the read segment (`t0 ≤ t_k < t1`); `pass` = `k`, `occurred` is `t_k` in the time notation: `r(t_k × 1000) / 1000`
(the rule `r` of the contract `obsd`), the grid is by the unrounded `t_k`. Continuing after the cursor and growth of the span do not shift the grid.
At the detector `tick` counts passes (V/04.2 Н1.2): the scan runs faster than real time.

### Н1.4 Events

- Each event of the model is a log row by the worker's writer (P/03.6 Н1.2): the tree `detjob`, the unit is the name of the job, the epoch is its
  epoch; `of: cam/<cam>` — from `about`: `/events?unit=cam/<cam>` merges the scan findings with live detection (P/06.4 Н1.3), the field
  `subsystem` tells them apart. `t` is the writer's clock, the bucket is that of the moment of writing; `occurred` is the media time of the frame.
- Findings are read by media time with the query `by=occurred&late=<с>` (the merge contract of the event log): `late` is how much
  the write could lag behind `occurred`, it is counted from the age of the old recording: `⌈now − to⌉` of the query window, no less than
  the default 3600; a row written later than `to + late` is not visible to the query.
- Before each write — `may_write` (P/03.2 Н1.6). A write error — the row is lost, the count `events_refused`.
- The class is by the field `alarms` of the job (V/05.1): `alarm` — into `detjob.alarms/…` with `fsync`, otherwise `observation` (the event log contract
  «class»). There is no suppression (`events.suppress` is not declared for `detjob`): the suppression window is by the writer's clock, the scan is
  faster than real time.
- `events.older_epochs: earlier-run` ([P/06.4 Н1.2](../../../Platform/06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md)): the rows of former
  epochs of the job (a move, a substitution) are a former run for comparison, not a fenced-off writer.

### Н1.5 Result

The holder MUST write the result `unit.state.result` (V/05.1 Н1.3) once, by CAS under the epoch guard (P/03.6 Н1.7). The conditions
of `failed` are checked before `done`; among the rows of `failed` — the first condition from the top that holds, with its `why`:

| When | `result` |
|---|---|
| there is no remainder, `missing` is empty, not `following` and not `fetching`, no holes waiting for the device's verdict (chapter 4 Н1.4); permanent holes do not hold the result | `{end: done, covered, asked, events}` (`covered` < `asked` — not everything was viewed) |
| no door is `silent` or `garbled`, at least one answered, the plan is empty, not `following` and not `fetching`, no holes waiting for the device's verdict | `{end: done, covered, asked, events}` |
| no one answered, `WAIT_MAX` by `waited` has run out (chapter 4 Н1.2) | `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why: "no recorder's archive door answered"}` |
| the wait has run out (`WAIT_MAX` by `waited` or `fetch_waited`, chapter 4 Н1.2), there is nothing to read, not `following` | `{end: done, covered, asked, events, partial: […]}` — only what was not waited for |
| `to ≤ from` (V/05.1 Н1.1) | `{end: failed, covered: 0, asked: 0, events: 0, why: "empty interval: to ≤ from"}` |
| the recording row `rec` does not exist or is a tombstone (V/05.1 Н1.1) | `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why: "no recording <rec>: whose footage it is cannot be checked"}` |
| the recording row is unreadable or the store is silent, `WAIT_MAX` has run out (V/05.4 Н1.2) | `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why: "recording <rec> unreadable"}` |
| the detector row `mask_of` does not exist or is a tombstone (Н1.3) | `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why: "the detector <id> whose mask this order takes is gone"}` |
| there is no model of the kind, `WAIT_MAX` has run out (chapter 4 `unsupported`) | `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why: "no model <kind> on this worker"}` |
| the start or the run of the model fails until the runtime's `stalled` (`RETRY_MAX`, P/03.3; `stalled(id)`, P/03.1 Н2) | `{end: failed, covered, asked, events, why}` |

`covered`, `events` of the result come from the cursor; zeros — only at `to ≤ from`.

While a valid result exists, the job MUST NOT be executed (by a new holder either), the controller removes it (`retire_when`, V/05.1).
A read of the result or of the cursor that did not answer is not «no result»: the job MUST NOT be executed on the pass and MUST NOT scan anew, the count
`pass_failures` (P/03.3 Н3 step 2), the next pass reads again.

## Н3 Pass

```
1. A job of the assignment (the epoch is taken on taking): a read of the result or of the cursor did not answer → pass_failures, do not execute.
2. The result is valid → nothing; a cursor of its own revision → done; otherwise done = [].
3. to ≤ from; no recording rec → result failed (Н1.5).
4. The model (registry det; mask — its own mask or det/units/<mask_of>): with its own mask empty, the detector mask_of does not exist or is a tombstone →
   result failed (Н1.5); did not work out: failed (backoff); stalled(id) → result failed.
5. The plan (chapter 2) − done by time → the remainder; up to 4 pieces: read → events (may_write, class) → done += what was read.
6. The cursor — no more often than 10 s, after the rows; while waited, fetch_waited grow — also without a new segment.
7. No remainder and nothing to wait for (chapter 4; permanent holes are not waited for) → result done.
```

**Races.** A substitution in the middle of a segment: the old holder finishes writing rows until it loses the lease, the new one reads the cursor (up to 10 s back)
and writes a repeat under its own epoch — the reader sees the former epoch as `earlier-run`. Both write the result (a late repeat) — CAS
under the epoch guard lets through only the holder of the current epoch. A result with `partial` (either timeout — `waited` or `fetch_waited`): first the row `scan.partial` (chapter 4
Н1.2), then the result by CAS; a break between them — there is no result, the new holder writes the row again under its own epoch (the log is
«at least once», the former one is `earlier-run`).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the mask body or the row `mask_of` is unreadable, the store is silent | `failed`, backoff; `stalled` — result `failed` | heartbeat, result | the detector's mask |
| there is no model of the kind | `unsupported` (chapter 4) | heartbeat | a model onto the worker |
| writing a row failed | the row is lost, `events_refused` | heartbeat | the resource |
| the cursor was not written | after a move, a repeat of the work after the last cursor | — | — |
| the detector row `mask_of` does not exist or is a tombstone | result `failed` | the job's result | a new job with a mask or a detector |
| a read of the result or of the cursor did not answer | the job is not executed on the pass, `pass_failures` | heartbeat | the store |

## Н5 Acceptance

A scan by the teaching model to the result `done` — the scenario will appear with the TLS VMS stand (chapter 4 Н5); until then the chapter, the cursor, the substitution,
`failed` — by reading and by a test of the implementation.

## Implementation decides

The implementation MUST name in its description: **how many models** a worker holds at once and how it feeds the model the frames
of a segment; **the `why` of the result** at `stalled` — the failure text, non-empty; **the command of the vector package** `VMS/04-det/mask` (the entry `vectors` of the profile; the second owner
of the package V/04, [`vectors/mask`](../../04-det/vectors/mask/README.md)).
