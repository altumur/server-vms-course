# V/05.2 — Reading the interval: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian [`05.2-detjob.norms.md`](../05.2-detjob.norms.md) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 2 of lesson V/05 «Задача по интервалу архива» (ADR-0080; the VMS module — ADR-0090). [Lecture](../../05-detjob.md),
[instructions](../05-detjob.README.md), manifest [`detjob.subsystem.yaml`](../../detjob.subsystem.yaml). The platform is the export
`cammeta-specs` `7a875c75`.

The video of a job lies in volumes held by recorders — sometimes in several, in different epochs and on different servers.
The chapter sets norms for whom the job asks what is recorded over the interval, how it tells «nobody answered» from «no recording», how it
builds the plan and reads each segment. The archive doors for processes — [V/02.5](../../../02-rec/spec/02.5-rec.norms.md) Н1.4, Н2;
the epoch rule — V/02.5 Н1.3; the work by the plan — chapter 3.

Norm ids are `V/05.2-MM`; the map to former lessons is [`id-map.tsv`](../../../common/id-map.tsv).

## Н1 Form

**Time.** Unix seconds of media time; the timeouts of questions — the monotonic clock of the process.

**Data** (the shared families of the [platform catalogue](../../../../Platform/common/data/catalog.tsv)): reads — the heartbeats of the recorders
`rec/heartbeats/*`, the table `rec/volumes/*`, the recording row `rec/recordings/<rec>` (`worker.reads`, V/05.1).

### Н1.1 Whom to ask

**A live door** is a recorder whose readable heartbeat has changed within `LOST_AFTER` = 45 s by the job's clock (P/03.4 Н1.1) and whose
top of the heartbeat carries a non-empty `volume` (V/02.4 Н1.8); the address is the field `url` of its heartbeat. A `url` or `volume` not in the form of the family
`rec-heartbeat-words` (V/02) is not read rather than absent: the door is not live on the pass, its volume is `unread` by the condition of the table
below; a broken `volume` or heartbeat does not name the recording — it does not give `unread`. A recorder without a volume
(`servers: distinct`, V/02.1 Н1.3; `volume: ""`) is a regular case, not silence: it is not asked. The job MUST ask
**every** live door: `GET /spans/<rec>?from=<from>&to=<max(to, now) + VISIBLE_LAG_SECONDS>` (V/02.5 Н1.4), with the leaf of the role
`detjobworker` (V/02.5 Н2; without the kit the process routes are closed — 401 `no_role`, the job cannot read), the question timeout
`ASK_WAIT` = 5 s. The answers are combined as a set: one span from two doors is one span.

| Door outcome | Word | What it means for the job |
|---|---|---|
| `{spans: […]}` | answered | its spans go into the plan |
| 503 `no volume open here` | no volume | the door has no volume (V/02.5 Н1.4): not `silent`, it does not go into `missing` and does not count as an answer: all doors 503 — nobody answered; a volume that nobody holds is caught by `unread` |
| no answer within 5 s, a status other than 200, a body that is not `{spans: […]}` | `silent` | its volumes are not read |
| a span not in the form `Spans.spans[]` of the OpenAPI V/02, or `end ≤ start` | `garbled` | the span is skipped, its volume is not read |
| an enabled volume `rec/volumes/*` without a live holder: the `home` of the recording `<rec>` or a volume that has at least one heartbeat row `rec/heartbeats/*` (even a stale one) with `volume` equal to this volume and with an element of the recording `<rec>` in `status[]` | `unread` | the volume is not read; there are no such volumes — not `unread` |

**Limit.** Outside a volume it is not recorded where the minutes of a recording lie (V/02.3 Н1.2). A volume without a live holder that the recording has left and that is not
its `home` is named by nothing if the last heartbeat of its holder no longer carried the recording: its minutes are a hole (chapter 4 Н1.4), possibly a permanent one, and the result is
`done` without `partial`, although the minutes are intact; that not everything was viewed is told by `covered` < `asked`.

**Nobody answered** (`None`) is not «no recording» (`[]`): the answer «nothing is recorded» is given only by a door that answered. Time in
the plan comes from the spans, not from the `current_epoch` of the door: the epoch is in the stream name.

**The recording row.** There is no `rec/recordings/<rec>` or it is a tombstone — the result `failed` (V/05.1 Н1.1 «Правила держателя»):
whose recording it is cannot be checked. The row is not readable by its form or the store did not answer — this is not «no recording»: `waiting`,
`reason` `recording <rec> unreadable`, the timeout — `waited` (chapter 4 Н1.2). A `retention_days` that is not a finite non-negative
number — «visible from» = `now` (the fallback outcome of V/02.5 Н1.2): the holes before it are permanent (chapter 4 Н1.4).

**Listings.** The listing of the heartbeats `rec/heartbeats/*` or of the volumes `rec/volumes/*` is not read — this is not «no volume»: the pass is without a verdict,
the job does not move, the count `pass_failures` (P/03.3 Н3 step 2).

### Н1.2 Plan

From the spans — segments by the rule «one moment — one epoch» (V/02.5 Н1.3 `authoritative`: an elementary interval goes to the senior by
`(epoch, live)`, at an equal epoch the live one wins over the catch-up recorded one, the copy `e0` loses to any epoch; a fenced-off
`fenced` one loses and is taken only where the senior one does not cover it): a segment `{stream, door, t0, t1}` — the stream
(`<epoch>` or `<epoch>/backfill`), the door
that named the span, and `[t0, t1)` — the intersection of the span with `[from, to)`. The segment key is `<stream>@<t0>-<t1>` (three digits after the point).
The plan is built from the spans of the door's answer **as they are**: the door already trims the answer by the visible set (V/02.5 Н1.2:
`[видно с, ∞)` with the keeps), the job does not trim it again. The «visible from» of the recording row the job takes only for holes
(chapter 4 Н1.4).

### Н1.3 Reading a segment

`GET /samples/<rec>?from=<t0>&to=<t1>&stream=<stream>` — at **the** door that named the span (without `stream` the door decides the epoch
only by its own volume and may give another one). The answer is SMPL records in a row (the contract `obsd` «Время и запись SMPL»), with a lead-in
from the key frame up to `t0`; the job passes to the model the frames with `t0 ≤ ts < t1` (`accepts`), the lead-in — only to the decoder;
`ts` is the `begin` of the record, the comparison is in archive milliseconds: `archive_ms(t0) ≤ begin < archive_ms(t1)`, so at the seam of two
segments a frame goes into one of them.
A break or a refusal of the door in the middle of a segment — the segment is not read (chapter 3: the remainder), the door is `silent` until the next question.
A 200 answer that ended before `t1` — only the received part is read, from `t0` to the end of the last frame; an empty answer —
the segment is not read (the timeout — chapter 4 Н1.2).

### Н1.4 How much and up to where

- `covered` — the sum of the seconds read (`done` inside `[from, to)`): «viewed 40 of 60» is not «nothing happened for 20 seconds».
- `written_through` — the end of the last visible span of the recording, no spans — 0: up to where the recording exists now; by it —
  «follow the recording» (chapter 4).
- `asked` — `max(0, to − from)`; at `to ≤ from` the model does not start (V/05.1 Н1.1, the result `failed`).

## Н3 Pass

```
to ≤ from → result failed; no row rec/recordings/<rec> or a tombstone → result failed (V/05.1 Н1.1)
the row is not readable or the store is silent → waiting «recording <rec> unreadable», waited; WAIT_MAX → result failed
the listing of heartbeats or volumes is not read → the pass without a verdict, pass_failures
live doors (heartbeat 45 s, volume non-empty) → /spans at each, 5 s → the set of spans
  503 «no volume» — not silent; silent, garbled; unread — a volume without a holder: the home of the recording or a volume whose last heartbeat row named the recording
nobody answered → no plan (chapter 4: waiting)
plan: the epoch rule → segments {stream, door, t0, t1} ∩ [from, to)
segment (chapter 3, the pass budget): /samples with stream at its own door → frames t0 ≤ ts < t1 → model
```

**Races.** The volume changed its holder between `/spans` and `/samples` — the door that named the span answers 503 `no volume open here`:
the segment is not read, the next pass asks again. The span grew between the questions — the segment key is different, the remainder is counted by
time and recording, not by key (chapter 3).

## Н4 Refusals

| Reason | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| no door answered | no plan, `waiting`; at `WAIT_MAX` — the result `failed` (chapter 4 Н1.2) | the heartbeat, the result | bring the recorders up |
| a door is silent, an answer not in form | `silent`, its volumes are not read | the heartbeat (`missing`, chapter 4) | — |
| a span does not parse | `garbled`, the span is skipped | the heartbeat | — |
| a volume without a live holder: the `home` of the recording, or the last heartbeat row named the recording | `unread` | the heartbeat | bring the recorder up |
| the `url` or `volume` of a recorder's heartbeat not in form | the door is not live, the volume is `unread` by the condition of Н1.1 | the heartbeat | fix the recorder |
| the door has no volume (503 `no volume open here` on `/spans`) | not `silent`, does not go into `missing`, does not count as an answer | — | — |
| the listing of heartbeats or volumes is not read | the pass without a verdict, `pass_failures` | the heartbeat | — |
| there is no row of the recording `rec`, or a tombstone | the result `failed` «`no recording <rec>: …`» | the job result | a new job on a live recording |
| the recording row is not readable, the store did not answer | `waiting` `recording <rec> unreadable`; at `WAIT_MAX` — the result `failed` | the heartbeat, the result | fix the recording row |
| no kit of the role | 401 `no_role` of the door, nothing to read with | the heartbeat, the log | the kit `detjobworker` |
| the volume changed its holder in the middle of reading | 503, the segment is not read | — | a repeat on the next pass |
