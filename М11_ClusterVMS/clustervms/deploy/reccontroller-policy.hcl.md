# reccontroller-policy.hcl — bound to job reccontroller's workload identity

**Role.** Lesson 2. The only writer of the recorder's placement: `rec/workers/*` (assignments), `rec/placement/*` (with a reason), `rec/slots/*` (redistribution of a released slot), and `objects/rec/snapshot/*` — its own snapshot shards, a grant it did not have from the day it was written until М10A Lesson 27, because nothing checked the file against the code; and `objects/rec/controller/pass` — the report its pass writes, where `/metrics` reads `rec_units_unplaced`: missing until the ninth review, a 403 every five seconds, and the check that missed it compared the file with a hand list (`acl_objects_controller()`). `tests/test_policies.py::test_every_write_the_code_makes_is_granted` checks it against the writes the stand's reccontroller actually makes. Reads `objects/*` (the recorders' heartbeats, the resources' heartbeats), `rec/*` (the recording rows the console wrote), `vms/*` (the cameras its recordings follow, `near`) and `platform/*` (`drain`, `schema`). It read `path "*"` until М10's eighth review — `secrets/vms` with it, the key three jobs are meant to read; never `secrets/*`, never `domain/*` now. Never a recording row, never the VMS's.
- `rec/decommissioned/*` (write, read, list, destroy) — its mark that a server's decommission was carried out for the recorders, as the vmscontroller's `vms/decommissioned/*` (`Controller.apply_decommissions`, М10A Lesson 7, step 7). Releasing a recorder's slot does not touch `rec/holds/*`: the volume it held stays held, and the mark names it (`holds`).
