
- `template` → `secrets/vms.key`, `SECRETS_KEY = "/secrets/vms.key"` — the cluster's key (М10A Lesson 18, `sealing.py`), rendered from the Nomad variable `secrets/vms` into the task's own secrets directory. Three jobs render it — the console, the worker and the recorder (`recworker.nomad.hcl`, which opens a network volume's sealed secret): the console seals device passwords on the way into the store, the worker opens them for the pipeline.
- `variable "monitors"` (default `""`) and `CONSOLE_MONITORS = "127.0.0.1,${attr.unique.network.ip-address},${var.monitors}"` — who has the monitors' lane on this console: `CONSOLE_MONITOR_RESERVE` (4) connections of their own past the common slots, answered on `/metrics` and `/healthz` only (`MONITOR_ROUTES`), at most `MONITOR_PER_ADDRESS` (2) to one address (`w2cplatform/console.py`, `monitors_from`, `Bounds`). The ADDRESSES that scrape, not the network they live in: it was `10.0.0.0/8`, and any eight boxes of the cluster's network took the lane and Prometheus was blind (М10's eighth review, a run). Now this node's loopback and its own address — a scraper or `vms-scaler` on the node, Nomad's checks — and what `monitors` names: the Prometheus the autoscaler reads, wherever it runs (`nomad job run -var monitors=10.0.4.9 console.nomad.hcl`). An empty item is no address, so the default leaves the list at the node's two. A listed address is trusted with the lane — list scrapers, not networks.
- `resources { cpu = 300  memory = 256 }` — memory counted, not guessed (М10's sixth review, a run: four `PUT …/mask` of 32 MiB took the task to its old 128 MiB, and it was killed by a token with no grant at all). Rights are asked before a body is read, and `BLOBS_AT_ONCE` (2) blobs are read at a time, each up to `CONSOLE_MAX_BLOB` (32 MiB) while read and again while stored: some 75 MiB of the process's own plus 2 × 2 × 32 MiB is 256. A site whose masks are kilobytes may lower `CONSOLE_MAX_BLOB` and this with it.
