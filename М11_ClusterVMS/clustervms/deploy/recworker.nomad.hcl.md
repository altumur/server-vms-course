# recworker.nomad.hcl — the recorder job: the only one placed on the archive for footage

**Role.** Lesson 2 (the shape) and Lesson 4 (its failover). `service`, `count = N` with the worker's `scaling` block on `avg(rec_worker_load)` plus one of its own on `rec_volumes_unserved`, the archive constraint `meta.archive is_set` — the line that used to be the worker's reason for the archive and is the recorder's now — `spread` over `node.unique.id`, the same `disconnect` numbers and `kill_timeout = 40s` (SIGTERM: the pipelines stop, the writer is closed after its flush — up to thirty seconds by obsd's protocol — and only then is the hold let go). Volumes: the archive — its events, and the path of its server's own volume, which the host's `obsd` opens — `/run/vms` (the workers' shared-memory branches on this server) and `/run/obsd` (the daemon's socket — this job's alone; the task runs as `user = "0:2101"`, the primary group the daemon's `OBSD_CLIENT_GROUP` lets in, and says `OBSD_SOCKET`); the cluster's key rendered from the Nomad variable `secrets/vms` (`SECRETS_KEY`), to open a network volume's sealed secret (the review's fourth pass, blocker 3: none of this was here, and every recorder saw its volume away); no `/data/media`: it never reads a camera. The daemon is the host's `obsd.service` (М10's unit), not a job: host infrastructure, like `podman.socket`. `ARCHIVE_HOST`/`ARCHIVE_PORT` put its archive door on the node's address, where the console and a primary copying from a backup reach it. The rec spec has no `near`: a recorder is pinned to disks and cannot follow anything, so the recording names its server (`home` on its row) and the camera comes to it (`near: rec` in the vms spec). The usual path is therefore `shmsrc`, not RTSP — a preference, never a filter. `network_mode = host`: it subscribes to workers' RTSP fan-outs on any server. `ARCHIVE = ${meta.archive}` is handed to the process as for the worker.

## Notes
- Still root, and so `/run/obsd`'s 0750 does not stop it (М10's fifth review, Т-M2 — open, said in the jobspec's comment). Root in the container is root on the host paths it mounts: the directory's rights keep out the processes that do not mount it (the holder, a vendor's DriverPack), not this one. It cannot drop to a user of its own yet: it writes its events into `/data/archive`, owned as root by the resource and the workers; it reads the workers' shared-memory sockets in `/run/vms`, created as root with no group to join; and the key Nomad renders 0600 is root's. Each is a change on the other side (the archive's and `/run/vms`'s owner group, the template's `uid`); until all three are made, `user = "<uid>:2101"` would only make the job fail.
- Both scaling checks need a Prometheus to read, and this directory does not deploy one (see `autoscaler.nomad.hcl.md`; М13 builds it). Without it the job runs at whatever `count` says and the unserved archives stay visible on the console — the check is automation, not a dependency of the recorder.
- Two scaling checks, because a recorder has two demands and `load` sees only one. A recorder holds ONE archive (`servers: distinct` over `place_by: volume`), so an archive that is declared (`rec/volumes/*`) and held by nobody needs a PROCESS — while every existing recorder may be perfectly loaded. `rec_recorders_needed` — unserved archives minus spares, so a volume nobody CAN take (a disk on a box with no recorder) stops asking after the first free process — is published by the console beside `rec_worker_load`; the Autoscaler takes the larger of the counts the checks compute, and the archive check never asks for fewer than are running, so scale-in stays `load`'s alone.
- `rec_worker_load` deliberately leaves out SPARES — recorders holding no volume. A spare reports zero capacity, which the formula would read as fully loaded, and one spare would then demand the next for ever. They are counted as `rec_spare_workers` instead.
- `rec/policy {servers: distinct}` by default: a rescheduled `r-1` beside `r-2` on one server idles by policy; the rec controller moves a dead server's recordings instead (two silences). The administrator may set `shared` from the page's `/rec/policy`.
- Bound policy: `recworker-policy.hcl` — `rec/epoch/*`, `rec/slots/*`, its heartbeat under `objects/rec/*`; reads `vms/*` and `objects/vms/*` for the workers' `live_url`.
- `UNCONFIRMED_MAX = "90"` — how long the process goes on recording past a lease's end while the store is silent (М10A Lesson 6, `Lease.may_record`). Unset on one box (no ceiling: the store is a local directory); a number here, because the store is on the network and a cut-off worker may still hold the camera's session. Longer than `lost_after` (45 s), so the recording does not stop before the camera has been given to anybody. `off` restores the strict behaviour.
