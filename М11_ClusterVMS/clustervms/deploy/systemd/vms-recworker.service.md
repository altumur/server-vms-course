# vms-recworker.service — the recorder: the recordings of its assignment, into this server's volume

**Role in the module.** Lessons 3 and 6. `w2c-run.sh recorder` as `vms`, joining `vms-recworker` (its store socket) and `vms-obsd` (the group the archive's engine lets in, gid 2101); `OBSD_SOCKET=/run/vms-obsd/obsd.sock`, after `vms-obsd.service` (М10's unit, aliased by `install.sh`); `WORKER_NAME=r-%l-1`; `BOX_ID=%m` (the machine id: a network volume's hold follows the name at once only on the same box). The cluster's key as a credential (a network volume's sealed secret). `TimeoutStopSec=40`: the writer's flush, then the hold. `tests/test_recorder_job.py` holds it against М10's obsd unit. Its settings beyond its own lines come from `/etc/w2c/w2c.env` and `/etc/vms/vms.env`, read by `w2c-run.sh` UNDER what the unit says (no `EnvironmentFile=`: systemd would let a file override `Environment=`). `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=2`: a crash is systemd's to restart under the same name, on this server.

## Notes
- `tests/test_units.py`: each unit names its role's socket (`PLATFORM_STORE=configstore:///run/configstore/<role>.sock`), joins the group the rights file gives that role, runs `w2c-run.sh <verb>`, comes after `configstore.service`, as `w2c` (the platform's) or `vms` (the subsystem's).
