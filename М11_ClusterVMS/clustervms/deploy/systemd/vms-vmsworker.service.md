# vms-vmsworker.service — the worker: holds its cameras, one per server

**Role in the module.** Lessons 2, 4 and 8. `w2c-run.sh worker` as `vms`, joining `vms-vmsworker`; its name from the unit, `WORKER_NAME=w-%l-1` (`w-srv-a-1` on srv-a, product P4) — a restart claims the same name, a second process with it is fenced by the store's CAS. `RTSP_HOST=0.0.0.0`: the fan-out opened for recorders and gateways on other servers. `UNCONFIRMED_MAX=90`: recording goes on past a lease's end while the store does not answer, for longer than the slot and its margin. The cluster's key as a credential. The box's layout's groups (WP-E: `w2c-events`, `w2c-store`, `w2c-secrets`), `UMask=0007`, `ProtectSystem=strict` writing the events archive and the objects, `/run/vms` its own (`RuntimeDirectory=vms`, kept). `WatchdogSec=360`: its loop says `WATCHDOG=1` every turn, and one that has not turned for six minutes is started again (the twelfth review, major 14). Its spare is `vms-vmsworker-spare@.service`: this unit but the name. `TimeoutStopSec=20`: room to release the slot on a stop. Its settings beyond its own lines come from `/etc/w2c/w2c.env` and `/etc/vms/vms.env`, read by `w2c-run.sh` UNDER what the unit says (no `EnvironmentFile=`: systemd would let a file override `Environment=`). `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=2`: a crash is systemd's to restart under the same name, on this server.

## Notes
- `tests/test_units.py`: each unit names its role's socket (`PLATFORM_STORE=configstore:///run/configstore/<role>.sock`), joins the group the rights file gives that role, runs `w2c-run.sh <verb>`, comes after `configstore.service`, as `w2c` (the platform's) or `vms` (the subsystem's).
