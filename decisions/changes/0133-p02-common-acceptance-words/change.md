change: 0133-p02-common-acceptance-words rev 1 · lessons P/02, Platform/common · author run P02.33 (Claude) · state review

Publication parent `a6748e8ef7da5cf16bdf1f2958a66d8a54c3a443`. Task P02.33, part 2в1 of the readiness audit P02.20 (`decisions/inbox/2026-11-10-P02-аудит-готовности.md`), ADR-0133 addendum (45). The forms of P/02 that were executed only by the prose of the lesson READMEs get shared words of the common acceptance (P/AC-R002: data, not lesson code). A new section «Command tables» of the common README (EN/RU) defines three forms, each the engine word of the routing: `verdicts` (`verdicts.tsv` with `classes.tsv` and `diagnostics.tsv`; the class is observed as the word `refused:<class>`; the diagnostics are a hint), `puts` (installation sequences with three, four or five columns, the table declarations `# canonical:` and `# beside:`, the comparison of the code, stderr, `rev`, the mark and the view, the confirmation of `ceiling`) and `variants` (the rights variants `expect.tsv`, `refused`, `check.tsv`). The words of `puts` move into the common README: `@view-delete`, `@view-spoil`, `@rev-delete`, `@rev-spoil`, `@rev-max`, `@shell-put <source>`, `@file-fill <path> <bytes>`; new words: `@row-spoil <field>` (an unreadable row `platform/manifest/<name>`) and the expectation tail `@stderr ~<text>`. The routing word `run` on a first directory without `scenarios.tsv` runs its `seams.tsv` through the seam adapter protocol. P/AC-R003 and R008: the judge reads the command tables by their forms and inventories their rows by file and physical line. P/AC-R030: the conditions of `installer_ceiling_unproven` and `installer_rights_check_missing`.

Left to other parts. 2в2: the rows of P/02 in `targets.tsv` and `targets.routing.tsv`; one engine word per routing row, so the seams of 2.1 (`run` on `acceptance/1`), the seams of 2.4 and the variants of 2.5 (`variants`) need their own rows beside the targets of `runner-targets.tsv`. 2г: the procedures in the READMEs of P/02 are replaced by references to «Command tables»; the refusal rows of `2/puts.tsv` and `2/puts-ceiling.tsv` get `@stderr` with the key of the view or the mark, which the former rule of P/02.2 set implicitly; the tables of 2.2 get `# canonical:`, the table of 2.3 gets `# beside:`. Not part of 2в1: the 403 rule of 2.5 and the sample through `manifest put` of 2.1.
