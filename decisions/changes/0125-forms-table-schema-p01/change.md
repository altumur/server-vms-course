Second group of the standard form checks: Frictionless Table Schema v1 sidecars next to the five remaining forms of P/01 — `daemon-flags.tsv.schema.json`, `refusals.tsv.schema.json`, `runtime-constants.tsv.schema.json`, `write-checks.tsv.schema.json`, `write-checks.probes.tsv.schema.json` (every field a raw string; required, enum, pattern; primaryKey `flag`, `name`, `name`, `id`, none; `n` of write-checks unique). No foreign key: no column of these forms names a row of another table directly (the `n` of the probes is 0 for pass, and the agreement with write-checks, OpenAPI, the prose and the anchors is semantic). `Platform/forms.datapackage.json` is not extended: it keeps its four resources until the Tools line on Code compatibility; the five resources are proposed outside Source. Fix of the first group: Frictionless anchors a pattern as `^{pattern}$` without a group, so a top-level alternation lost an anchor (`depends`, `kit`, `parts` admitted a value beginning with `-`); every pattern of the sidecars is now `(?:…)`, in `stages.tsv.schema.json` and `stages.kit.tsv.schema.json` as in the new ones. Both filters list the five new files outside the model and judge input. No form, grammar or meaning changes; the old checks stay. No requirement unit changes; no form, grammar or meaning changes.
