### Н1.2 Loop timings

<!-- req: P/03.3-R005 -->

```yaml
loop:
  # LEASE_TTL, LEASE_MARGIN, LEASE_EVERY, again — the lease timings, chapter 3.2 Н1.2
  HEARTBEAT_EVERY: 10
  POLL:         ~2                                  # a pause between passes without events; long polling — the implementation decides
  WAKE_GAP:     0.25; PASS_SHARE: 2   # an early pass not earlier than 0.25 s from the start of the previous one and not more often than
                                                    # two pass lengths — for implementations with long polling
watchdog:
  notify:       from the loop, not from the stand-in; WatchdogSec of the unit is the contract of the environment file «Constants and units» («A-16»)
retry:          # the backoff of repeating a failed start of a unit (ADR-0091 item 6; М10B/02:182–264, М10B/04:576–590)
  RETRY_MIN:    1                                   # s; the first pause after a failure
  RETRY_MAX:    60                                  # s; the limit of the pause
  rule:         the pause grows exponentially from RETRY_MIN to RETRY_MAX with mandatory jitter (the form of the exponent and
                the jitter — «Implementation decides» item 3); only failures of the start and of the work of a unit count — a move,
                fencing and a new name do not change the count; the count is reset on any of three: after RETRY_MAX of continuous
                stable work, by the word of the subsystem (its answer «stable»), when the unit is confirmed absent from the desired
                (the assignment) — an unconfirmed read of the assignment is not absence; a move does not count as a failure, and a unit
                that has left the worker resets the count by the third condition (C03-RETRY-RESET-01)
  stalled:      the pause has reached RETRY_MAX — status[].stalled: true in the heartbeat (chapter 3.4 Н1.1) until the count is reset; the subsystem asks with the library word stalled(id) (chapter 3.1 Н2)
```

## Н3 Procedures

### loop() — one turn

<!-- req: P/03.3-R008 -->

```
1. if nobody or not writing_allowed: «воссоединиться()» (chapter 3.2 Н3 «nobody and getting the name back»);
   no name obtained → only the search/the rival's mark and waiting; do not run steps 2–5
2. the reconciliation pass: read the assignment; an assigned unit without a row or with a tombstone is not started until the next
   pass (the placement row is the controller's business); for each assigned unit that is absent: open the secrets
   (chapter 3.5 Н3 «open»: not opened — Sealed, do not take the epoch; reconciliation of python and go М10/6–11, S19) → «take_epoch» →
   check the gate and may_act again immediately before start_unit; a refusal → do not start;
   a led unit absent from the assignment → cancel its unfinished starts, stop_unit;
   revision grew → with the gate open and may_act, accept the restart attempt: stop_unit (the subsystem learns it by the word restarting(id), chapter 3.1 Н2) and start
   under the same epoch/lease through the local transition of chapter 3.2 Н3 «take_epoch», without a new issue and without resetting last;
   otherwise the change of revision waits, the former unit runs only while may_write allows;
   born changed (the row was deleted and created anew — a new life, the row check contract, born; ADR-0091 item 3) → stop_unit, release
   the epoch and take it anew — a new issue, new bindings of (ADR-0013), as for a deleted and created one (sc. 06); the state
   of the unit the new life writes with its own born and revision (chapter 3.6 Н1.7);
   a crashed process of a unit (an answer of the subsystem) → a raise under the same epoch (chapter 3.2 Н3 «take_epoch(unit)», raise)
   after the retry pause of Н1.2;
   the row of an own unit does not read by its type (the row check contract «Unit row») → a started unit runs with the last read row,
   `why` of the heartbeat element — «row unreadable: <reason>»; a unit never read is not started, the epoch is not taken, the heartbeat
   element is {id, phase: "unreadable", the same why, revision?} without epoch and observed_revision (revision — if the revision of the row
   reads; chapter 3.4 Н1.1; the stage C guess W03-G05, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23); each
   such read — `garbled_rows` of the heartbeat + 1 (chapter 3.4 Н1.1); a read of the assignment or of rows broken off by an error —
   `pass_failures` + 1 (the VMS request «пульс и битая строка», the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23);
   a lost lease is stopped by «lease_step»; each unit in its own attempt
3. the pump: events (`append`, the contract of the object store client `ops`), requests (chapter 3.6 Н3 «holder's look»), commands (the marks
   `objects/<sub>/commands/<rid>`, P/06.4 Н1.5 `mark`)
4. if clock − start_of_previous_lease_step ≥ LEASE_EVERY or again: «lease_step()» (chapter 3.2 Н3)
5. if clock − previous_heartbeat ≥ HEARTBEAT_EVERY: the heartbeat (chapter 3.4 Н1.1)
6. watchdog notify; a pause until POLL or until an event (long polling)
each step in its own attempt and under the marker guarded(step name) for the stand-in (chapter 3.4 Н3 «stand_in()»)
```

<!-- req: P/03.3-R009 -->

**Races.** One loop thread, but the stand-in can lose the name/fence the worker while the epoch issue or
start_unit has not returned. A start and fencing agree through the local transition ([chapter 3.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) Н3 «take_epoch»); the CAS of the store
does not protect the state of the process. The stand-in writes the heartbeat, the slot, the leases and the place under the same `cas`.

## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **long polling or a pause** between passes, the command pump and its period;
3. **the form of the backoff** of repeating a start — the exponent and jitter between `RETRY_MIN` and `RETRY_MAX` (the numbers and the counting rule are a norm, Н1.2 `retry`);
2. ~~**how the worker learns `names_given` and the hang limit**~~ — a norm, not a choice of the implementation: the pass report (P/04; the form —
   the catalogue and the codec, Н1; analysis of review of stage A 3–5, № 16) — [chapter 3.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) «Implementation decides» item 3
   (analysis of review of stage B 3–5, № 11).

## Readiness checklist

An implementation conforms to the chapter when every norm holds; the norm id `P/03.3-MM` is what decision deltas,
findings and window records refer to (ADR-0080 addendum 6).

- [ ] **P/03.3-02** the start retry backoff (Н1.2 retry): an exponent with jitter between RETRY_MIN and RETRY_MAX, only failures count, a reset after stable work, by the word of the subsystem and outside the assignment, `stalled` in the heartbeat — checked by reading: the training subsystems do not crash on the stand's order (ADR-0091 item 6)
- [ ] **P/03.3-03** a change of `born` — a new epoch and new bindings, a change of `revision` — a restart under the former one (Н3 step 2) — sc. 06 (deletion and creation — epoch 2), sc. 35 (a new `born` at a unit that stayed in the assignment — epoch 2), sc. 02 (an edit — the same epoch); the new bindings `of` — by reading; a raise of a crashed process under the same epoch — checked by reading (ADR-0091 items 3, 6)
- [ ] **P/03.3-01** the loop of Н3: the order of the step, each step in its own attempt, the lease step from the start of the previous one, `again` — sc. 02; by reading (the lease step — [P/03.2-09](03.2-worker.norms.md), the subsystem calls — [P/03.1-01](03.1-worker.norms.md); the division of М10/5-03 by the `note` of the id-map)
- [ ] **P/03.3-04** an unreadable row of an own unit (Н3 step 2): a started one runs with the last read, `why` «row unreadable», `garbled_rows`; one never read is not started — sc. 25 (the element `unreadable` without an epoch, `garbled_rows` ≥ 1); `pass_failures` — sc. 12, 25 at zero, nonzero — by reading (the VMS request «пульс и битая строка»)
