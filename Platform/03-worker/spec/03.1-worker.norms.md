## N2 The library contract

<!-- req: P/03.1-R004 -->

The worker has no door of its own in this lesson: it writes and reads the store (P/01.1) and objects (P/05). What is visible of it from outside is the heartbeat, the slot, the rival's mark, `/servers` of the console (P/06, chapter 6.5). The lesson has no HTTP contracts of its own. The forms of the read/write answers are [P/01.1](../../01-store/spec/01-store.api.openapi.yaml), of units — [P/06](../../06-console/spec/06-console.api.openapi.yaml) (chapter 6.1), of objects — [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md#n2-doors). The heartbeat is the form of [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N1.1, not an HTTP answer of the worker.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R017 -->

To the subsystem the worker gives six calls whose form is a matter of the language (the implementation decides):

<!-- req: P/03.1-R005 -->

```
start_unit(id, row, epoch)   — start a unit under an epoch
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R018 -->

```
stop_unit(id)                — stop the unit
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R019 -->

```
status() → [status element]  — report for the heartbeat
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R020 -->

```
reconcile_once()             — a step of reconciling the desired with the current (chapter 3.3 N3 «loop()», step 2)
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R021 -->

```
before_stop_all()            — an orderly stop, before stop_unit of all units: the subsystem prepares (flush buffers,
                               finish what was begun); by default nothing; an error is a log line, the stop goes on
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R022 -->

```
after_stop()                 — after the slot and the place are given up: the subsystem closes its writers; by default nothing;
                               an error is a log line, the exit code of the stop by SIGTERM does not change
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R006 -->

The library word `stalled(id)` is not a call of the subsystem but its question to the library, its form a matter of the language: the unit is stuck, the start backoff has reached `RETRY_MAX` while the count is not reset ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N1.2 `stalled`), the same value as `status[].stalled` of the heartbeat. A subsystem that needs `stalled(id)` asks the library from its calls and keeps no failure count of its own: that count would diverge from the backoff.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R007 -->

The library word `restarting(id)` is the same kind of question of the subsystem from `stop_unit`: true when this `stop_unit` is a restart of the same unit on a growth of `revision` ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N3 «loop()», step 2). For every `stop_unit` other than a restart of the same unit on a growth of `revision`, `restarting(id)` is false. Removal, a move and a worker stop are examples of this complement, not its closed list.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R008 -->

The stop calls `before_stop_all` and `after_stop` are made only at an orderly worker stop (SIGTERM, [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N3).

<!-- req: P/03.1-R025 -->

At a worker crash neither `before_stop_all` nor `after_stop` is called; the controller uses their absence to distinguish a crash from an orderly departure (P/04).

<!-- req: P/03.1-R026 -->

The subsystem calls of the worker follow the rule of three places; they are not platform hooks.

## N3 Procedures

<!-- req: P/03.1-R009 -->

The chapter has no procedures of its own.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R027 -->

Who makes the calls of N2 and when: `start_unit` — after the gate «take_unit_epoch» of [chapter 3.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) N3
and at a restart by revision ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N3 «loop()», step 2).

<!-- req: P/03.1-R028 -->

`stop_unit` — the loop, the lease step, fencing and
the stop (chapters 3.2–3.4).

<!-- req: P/03.1-R029 -->

`status` — the heartbeat ([chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N1.1).

<!-- req: P/03.1-R030 -->

`reconcile_once` — the loop, step 2.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R031 -->

`before_stop_all`, `after_stop` — «stop by SIGTERM» of [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N3, steps 2 and 5.

## N5 Acceptance

<!-- req: P/03.1-R033 -->

The chapter has no acceptance catalogue: the scenarios of the stand do not observe the calls of the subsystem, only the rows that the procedures of chapters
3.2–3.4 write.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R034 -->

Checked by reading: the calls `before_stop_all` and
`after_stop` — the subject: N2 and [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N3 «stop» steps 2 and 5 — the order before stop_unit of all units and after the slot and the place are given up,
only at SIGTERM, an error is a log line, the stop goes on.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R035 -->

The `testsub` worker does not override `before_stop_all` or `after_stop`; evidence is a worker test of a subsystem that overrides these calls.

## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **the form of the six calls of the subsystem** (N2), the synchronization of accepting/cancelling a start with fencing
   and the reconciliation helper (bringing the running units to the desired ones with the backoff of chapter 3.3 N1.2 `retry`).
