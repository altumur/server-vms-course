## N2 The library contract

<!-- req: P/03.1-R004 -->

The worker has no door of its own in this lesson: it writes and reads the store (P/01.1) and objects (P/05). What is visible of it from outside is the heartbeat, the slot, the rival's mark, `/servers` of the console (P/06, chapter 6.5). The lesson has no HTTP contracts of its own. The forms of the read/write answers are [P/01.1](../../01-store/spec/01-store.api.openapi.yaml), of units — [P/06](../../06-console/spec/06-console.api.openapi.yaml) (chapter 6.1), of objects — [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md#n2-doors). The heartbeat is the form of [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N1.1, not an HTTP answer of the worker. To the subsystem the worker gives six calls whose form is a matter of the language (the implementation decides):

<!-- req: P/03.1-R005 -->

```
start_unit(id, row, epoch)   — start a unit under an epoch
stop_unit(id)                — stop it
status() → [status element]  — report for the heartbeat
reconcile_once()             — a step of reconciling the desired with the current (chapter 3.3 N3 «loop()», step 2)
before_stop_all()            — an orderly stop, before stop_unit of all units: the subsystem prepares (flush buffers,
                               finish what was begun); by default nothing; an error is a log line, the stop goes on
after_stop()                 — after the slot and the place are given up: the subsystem closes its writers; by default nothing;
                               an error is a log line, the exit code of the stop by SIGTERM does not change
```

<!-- req: P/03.1-R006 -->

The library word `stalled(id)` is not a call of the subsystem but its question to the library, its form a matter of the language: the unit is stuck, the start backoff has reached `RETRY_MAX` while the count is not reset ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N1.2 `stalled`), the same value as `status[].stalled` of the heartbeat. A subsystem that needs it asks from its calls and keeps no failure count of its own: that count would diverge from the backoff.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R007 -->

The library word `restarting(id)` is the same kind of question of the subsystem from `stop_unit`: true when this `stop_unit` is a restart of the same unit on a growth of `revision` ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N3 «loop()», step 2); outside it — at removal, a move and a stop of the worker — false.

<!-- req: P/03.1-R008 -->

Both stop calls are made only at an orderly stop (SIGTERM, [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N3); at a crash there are none, and by this
the controller tells a departure from a crash (P/04). These are calls of the worker by the rule of three places, not hooks of the platform.

## N3 Procedures

<!-- req: P/03.1-R009 -->

The chapter has no procedures of its own. Who makes the calls of N2 and when: `start_unit` — after the gate «take_unit_epoch» of [chapter 3.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) N3
and at a restart by revision ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N3 «loop()», step 2); `stop_unit` — the loop, the lease step, fencing and
the stop (chapters 3.2–3.4); `status` — the heartbeat ([chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N1.1); `reconcile_once` — the loop, step 2;
`before_stop_all`, `after_stop` — «stop by SIGTERM» of [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N3, steps 2 and 5.

## N5 Acceptance

The chapter has no acceptance catalogue: the scenarios of the stand do not observe the calls of the subsystem, only the rows that the procedures of chapters
3.2–3.4 write. Checked by reading: the calls `before_stop_all` and
`after_stop` — the subject: N2 and [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N3 «stop» steps 2 and 5 — the order before stop_unit of all units and after the slot and the place are given up,
only at SIGTERM, an error is a log line, the stop goes on; the `testsub` worker does not override them, the evidence is
a worker test of a subsystem that overrides them.

## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **the form of the six calls of the subsystem** (N2), the synchronization of accepting/cancelling a start with fencing
   and the reconciliation helper (bringing the running units to the desired ones with the backoff of chapter 3.3 N1.2 `retry`).

## Readiness checklist

An implementation conforms to the chapter when every norm holds; the norm id `P/03.1-MM` is what decision deltas,
findings and window records refer to.

- [ ] **P/03.1-01** the loop calls of N2 (the loop — [P/03.3-01](03.3-worker.norms.md)): `start_unit`, `stop_unit`, `status`, `reconcile_once` — six calls whose form is a matter of the language; `reconcile_once` is step 2 of the loop, `status` — the elements of the heartbeat — sc. 02 of [the acceptance of chapter 3.3](../acceptance/3/README.md) (judge-only: the scenarios of chapter 3.3); `stop_unit` and `start_unit` from step 2 of the loop, none under silence — the seams `s1-revision-same-epoch`, `s2-born-new-epoch`, `s3-silent-revision-waits` of [chapter 3.3](../acceptance/3/seams.tsv) (`CALL worker-reconcile`; judge-only: the seams of chapter 3.3); the form of the calls — by reading («Implementation decides» item 1)
- [ ] **P/03.1-02** the call under the slot (the gate — [P/03.2-10](03.2-worker.norms.md)): `start_unit(id, row, epoch)` — a start of a unit under the epoch issued by the gate «take_unit_epoch», after a repeated check of the gate; a restart by revision — the same epoch — the seam `s1-revision-same-epoch` of [chapter 3.3](../acceptance/3/seams.tsv) (`CALL worker-reconcile`; judge-only: the seams of chapter 3.3) and sc. 02, 06 of [the acceptance of chapter 3.3](../acceptance/3/README.md) (judge-only: the scenarios of chapter 3.3); a start accepted before fencing is cancelled and not revived — the seam `s15-gate-cancelled-late-start` of [chapter 3.2](../acceptance/2/seams.tsv) (`CALL worker-start-gate`; judge-only: the seams of chapter 3.2); the synchronization of accepting/cancelling a start with fencing — by reading («Implementation decides» item 1)
- [ ] **P/03.1-03** the stop calls of N2 (the stop — [P/03.4-05](03.4-worker.norms.md)): `before_stop_all` before stop_unit of all units, `after_stop` after the slot and the place are given up, only at SIGTERM, at a crash there are none; an error is a log line, the stop goes on, the exit code does not change — the seams `s1-stop-order` (the order, the errors of both calls, exit 0) and `s2-crash-no-hooks` of [chapter 3.1](../acceptance/1/seams.tsv) (`CALL worker-stop`, the seam adapter over the worker code; judge-only: the seams of chapter 3.1); the log line — by reading (N5)
