## Н2 The library contract

The worker has no door of its own in this lesson: it writes and reads the store (P/01.1) and objects (P/05). What is visible of it from outside is the heartbeat, the slot, the rival's mark, `/servers` of the console (P/06, chapter 6.5). The lesson has no HTTP contracts of its own (ADR-0082 addendum 13). The forms of the read/write answers are [P/01.1](../../01-store/spec/01-store.api.openapi.yaml), of units — [P/06](../../06-console/spec/06-console.api.openapi.yaml) (chapter 6.1), of objects — [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md#н2-двери). The heartbeat is the form of [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) Н1.1, not an HTTP answer of the worker. To the subsystem the worker gives six calls whose form is a matter of the language (the implementation decides):

```
start_unit(id, row, epoch)   — start a unit under an epoch
stop_unit(id)                — stop it
status() → [status element]  — report for the heartbeat
reconcile_once()             — a step of reconciling the desired with the current (chapter 3.3 Н3 «loop()», step 2)
before_stop_all()            — an orderly stop, before stop_unit of all units: the subsystem prepares (flush buffers,
                               finish what was begun); by default nothing; an error is a log line, the stop goes on
after_stop()                 — after the slot and the place are given up: the subsystem closes its writers; by default nothing;
                               an error is a log line, the exit code of the stop by SIGTERM does not change
```

The library word `stalled(id)` is not a call of the subsystem but its question to the library, its form a matter of the language: the unit is stuck, the start backoff has reached `RETRY_MAX` while the count is not reset ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) Н1.2 `stalled`), the same value as `status[].stalled` of the heartbeat. A subsystem that needs it asks from its calls and keeps no failure count of its own: that count would diverge from the backoff (C03-STALLED-WORD-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, analysis of the VMS pass).

The library word `restarting(id)` is the same kind of question of the subsystem from `stop_unit`: true when this `stop_unit` is a restart of the same unit on a growth of `revision` ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) Н3 «loop()», step 2); outside it — at removal, a move and a stop of the worker — false (C03-RESTART-WORD-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, analysis of the VMS pass).

Both stop calls are made only at an orderly stop (SIGTERM, [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) Н3); at a crash there are none, and by this
the controller tells a departure from a crash (P/04). These are calls of the worker by the rule of three places (the boundary §1.1), not hooks of the platform.

## Н3 Procedures

The chapter has no procedures of its own. Who makes the calls of Н2 and when: `start_unit` — after the gate «take_epoch» of [chapter 3.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) Н3
and at a restart by revision ([chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) Н3 «loop()», step 2); `stop_unit` — the loop, the lease step, fencing and
the stop (chapters 3.2–3.4); `status` — the heartbeat ([chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) Н1.1); `reconcile_once` — the loop, step 2;
`before_stop_all`, `after_stop` — «stop by SIGTERM» of [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) Н3, steps 2 and 5.

## Н4 Refusals

The chapter has no Н4 rows of its own: an error of a stop call is a log line, the stop goes on, the exit code does not change
(Н2); the refusals of the procedures that make the calls are Н4 of chapters 3.2–3.4.

## Н5 Acceptance

The chapter has no acceptance catalogue: the scenarios of the stand do not observe the calls of the subsystem, only the rows that the procedures of chapters
3.2–3.4 write. Checked by reading: the calls `before_stop_all` and
`after_stop` — the subject: Н2 and [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) Н3 «остановка» steps 2 and 5 — the order before stop_unit of all units and after the slot and the place are given up,

a worker test of a subsystem that overrides them.
