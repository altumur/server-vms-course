### N1.2 The envelope and the key ring: the worker's side

<!-- req: P/03.5-R005 -->

The worker opens an envelope by the sealing contract — the sections «[Envelope](../../common/contracts/sealing.md#envelope)» and
«[AAD path](../../common/contracts/sealing.md#aad-path)»: with the key `kid` of the envelope, with the single AAD of its place; the ring
it reads from the file `SECRETS_KEY` by the section «[Ring file](../../common/contracts/sealing.md#ring-file)». Who holds the key
(`holders`) and a change of the ring (`rotate`) are [P/06.2 N1.2](../../06-console/spec/06.2-console.norms.md).
An envelope without a key at the worker:

<!-- req: P/03.5-R006 -->

```yaml
no_key:
  open_without: an envelope without a key / with a foreign kid → Sealed «sealed and this process has no key» / «sealed with key <kid>,
                which this process does not hold»; the unit is not started, the cause is in its status
```

<!-- req: P/03.5-R007 -->

In the message of open_without, `<kid>` is replaced by the name of the envelope's key; the key bytes are not output.
