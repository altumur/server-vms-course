# P/03.5 — The key ring and opening: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/03.5-worker.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 3.5 of lesson P/03 «Worker» (the Worker; ADR-0080; module «Platform» — ADR-0083). Lecture (`ru/03-worker.md`, for people, not in the export),
[instructions](03-worker.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md). The lesson has no HTTP contract of its own (`P/03 no-openapi`):
the worker has no doors. The chapter has no acceptance catalogue (N5). The envelope `enc:v2`, the AAD path, the grammar of `kid` and the form of the key ring file are
the common [sealing contract](../../common/contracts/sealing.md) (vectors [`common/vectors/sealing/`](../../common/vectors/sealing/README.md));
the chapter does not repeat it and norms the worker's side: opening by the recipient, a unit without a key stands with a cause, by what the worker
reads the ring (analysis of review of stage A 3–5, № 21). Sealing by the console, the mask, who holds the key, rotation, the keyless mode and
the fingerprint are [chapter P/06.2](../../06-console/spec/06.2-console.norms.md).

The chapter is moved from M10/7 (stage B, ADR-0083 addendum (1): without
a change of behaviour): N3 «open (worker, start_unit)» with «Races», the row `no_key.open_without` of N1.2 and the paragraph on `<kid>` in its
message, the row of N4 «the envelope does not open at the worker», the reading of opening by the worker of N5 (S2, S19), part of item 1 of «Implementation decides».
The numbers of the subsections are the former numbers of M10/7. A reference «lesson N» without a chapter is the lesson
P/NN of the module; «chapter 3.K» is a chapter of this lesson. Norm ids are `P/03.5-MM`; the map to former ids is
`id-map.tsv` (historical record).

### N1.2 The envelope and the key ring: the worker's side

<!-- req: P/03.5-R005 -->

The worker opens an envelope by the sealing contract — the sections «[Envelope](../../common/contracts/sealing.md#envelope)» and
«[AAD path](../../common/contracts/sealing.md#aad-path)»: with the key `kid` of the envelope, with the single AAD of its place; the ring
it reads from the file `SECRETS_KEY` by the section «[Ring file](../../common/contracts/sealing.md#ring-file)». Who holds the key
(`holders`) and a change of the ring (`rotate`) are [P/06.2 N1.2](../../06-console/spec/06.2-console.norms.md).
An envelope without a key at the worker:

<!-- req: P/03.5-R006 -->

```yaml
no_key:
  open_without: an envelope without a key / with a foreign kid → Sealed «sealed and this process has no key» / «sealed with key <kid>,
                which this process does not hold»; the unit is not started, the cause is in its status
```

<!-- req: P/03.5-R007 -->

In the message of open_without, `<kid>` is replaced by the name of the envelope's key; the key bytes are not output.

## N3 Procedures

### open (worker, start_unit)

<!-- req: P/03.5-R009 -->

```
before «take_epoch» (chapter 3.3 N3 «loop», step 2): read the row; for each *_secret at any depth: not an envelope → as
is (a row from before the key); an envelope → open with the key kid with the single AAD of the sealing contract of the row and the full path; do not try old ways
  no key (SECRETS_KEY not set) / a foreign kid / does not open    → Sealed: do not take the epoch, do not start the unit; the
                                                                     status element — {id, phase: "sealed", why, revision}: why "the
                                                                     password does not open: <reason>" (P/06.2 N1.4 heartbeat)
                                                                     (reconciliation of python and go M10/6–11, S19, S2); there are no
                                                                     epoch and observed_revision, revision — of the read row
                                                                     (chapter 3.4 N1.1; the stage C guess W03-G02, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23)
the value — only to the recipient (the driver, the client); in memory, the heartbeat and logs — the envelope
the place (chapter 3.2 N3 «place: take», «hold»): on taking a place and on a change of the row of a held place — open the *_secret
of the row of the place table by the same rule and with the same AAD of the sealing contract (the key of the table row and the full path) as the unit
row; Sealed → the place is not taken (a held one — leaves the place, leave_place of chapter 3.2 N3 «hold»; released is not written, C03-PLACE-SEALED-LEAVE-01), place_error.<place>: sealed in the heartbeat (chapter 3.4 N1.1),
the cause is at the place, not at the unit; the opened value — only in memory and in the subsystem call (ADR-0091 addendum (9) items 5–6, addendum (10))
```

<!-- req: P/03.5-R010 -->

Step 2 of the loop is [chapter 3.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N3 «loop», `take_epoch` — [chapter 3.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) N3; AAD — the
[sealing contract](../../common/contracts/sealing.md) «AAD path»; `why` of the `status` element of the heartbeat — [chapter 3.4](03.4-worker.norms.md)
N1.1 and [P/06.2 N1.4](../../06-console/spec/06.2-console.norms.md) `heartbeat`.

<!-- req: P/03.5-R011 -->

**Races.** None: a read; the ring changed between sealing and opening — the `kid` of the envelope names the key: it opens with it or `Sealed` with a cause.
