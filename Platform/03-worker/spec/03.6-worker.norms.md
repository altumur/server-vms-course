### N1.2 Event: the writer and suppression

<!-- req: P/03.6-R007 -->

The worker is a participant of `append` of the event journal: the event line, its `id`, the bucket by `t`, the class and the alarm tree, the `fsync` of the alarm and of the
journal, the lock of the journal file — it observes them by the file contract of lesson 5 —
the [event journal contract](../../common/contracts/event-journal.md) (`line`, `bucket`, `class`, `journal`, `writer`) and its
section «Writing a line».

<!-- req: P/03.6-R055 -->

Event repeat suppression is step 3 of the event-journal writing procedure in P/03.6-R007; the suppression window and comparison rules are P/03.6-R058, P/03.6-R059, P/03.6-R062, P/03.6-R064, P/03.6-R070, P/03.6-R009.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R008 -->

**Event suppression: `kinds`.** `events.kinds.<kind>: {severity}` (the form — P/02.1) — the severity of a kind for the description of a kind of the VMS backend **Event suppression: `kinds`.** The platform does not judge the severity of `events.kinds.<kind>` and does not write that severity into the event line.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R057 -->

**Event suppression: `suppress`.** `events.suppress.<kind>: {window, by}`  (the form — P/02.1)

<!-- req: P/03.6-R058 -->

**Event suppression: `window`.** seconds > 0

<!-- req: P/03.6-R059 -->

**Event suppression: `by`.** a non-empty list of string names of fields of the line or of the synthetic unit **Event suppression: `by`.** without by — all fields of the line except t/id/repeats/since/until **Event suppression: `by`.** Naming any of `t`, `id`, `repeats`, `since`, `until` in `by`, or giving an empty `by`, is a load refusal.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R062 -->

**Event suppression: `rule`.** the first line of a window — as is **Event suppression: `rule`.** repeats are counted

<!-- req: P/03.6-R064 -->

**Event suppression: `rule`.** when the window closes or on a pass — one line {…the fields of the first, id: new, repeats, since, until}: repeats — the swallowed ones without the first **Event suppression: `rule`.** In the suppression summary, `since` is `t` of the first event line of that window. **Event suppression: `rule`.** In the suppression summary, `until` is `t` of the last swallowed repeat. **Event suppression: `rule`.** In the suppression summary, `t` is the moment when the summary is written. **Event suppression: `rule`.** a window without repeats gives no line **Event suppression: `rule`.** the summary of the previous window is written before the first line of the new one

<!-- req: P/03.6-R070 -->

**Event suppression: `stop`.** when the writer stops, every open window with swallowed repeats is closed by the same summary line, until — the moment of the stop, under may_write and before the last heartbeat **Event suppression: `stop`.** a window without repeats gives no line

<!-- req: P/03.6-R009 -->

Suppression is always separate for (tree, unit of the path, epoch, kind, class), regardless of by. The name unit in by is the unit from the writer's arguments/the bucket path, not a user field of the line of the same name. The other names of by are the top fields of the written line; an unknown/absent field gives a separate value «absent», distinct from null. The comparison is structural JSON: an object without regard to the order of keys, an array with regard to the order, a bool is not a number. The absence of a selected field in a particular event is not a manifest error. t/id and the summary fields do not take part; when by is absent, the other fields, including occurred/of, take part.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R010 -->

The form of `events.suppress` in the manifest and the refusals of its loading are [P/02.1](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md) N1.2, N4.

### N1.5 Request: the worker's side

<!-- req: P/03.6-R011 -->

The form of the row `<sub>/requests/<rid>`, the manifest keys `requests` and the mark `objects/<sub>/commands/<rid>` are the [request and its mark contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md);
the accounting of `asked` is [chapter P/06.4](../../06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md) N1.5.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R076 -->

The worker in this form: it files a row by
`worker.requests` of another manifest — the fields `filed` (its own instance) and `digest` (it computes it itself at the write, the kind `worker`), `rid` by the
template `requests.key` of the target, otherwise by the filer's key `<own subsystem>.<key>` (the key — deterministically from its own data,
for example `<scenario>-<event id>-<i>`) or a random one without a dot — only for a request whose repeat is not needed (the request contract
`rid`).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R077 -->

A repeated filing with the same `rid` is checked by the order of the door
(P/06.4 N3 «file a request»: first the mark, then the row and the window `kept`; at a target with `requests.outcome: fetched` there is no mark step) and compares the `unit` of the row and the `digest` of the kind `worker`: the
same `rid`, the same `unit`, the same `digest` — `again`; anything else — `refused`; how the holder writes the mark — `begun` before the call, `answer` of the one who began, `no_begun` with `ended_by: holder`,
the cleanup after `kept`.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R078 -->

The holder's limits are the runtime constants used in P/03.6-R012; `CLEAR_EVERY`, `SWEEP_EVERY` and `REAP_AFTER` belong to the reaper of P/06.4 N1.5, not to the holder.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R012 -->

Holder settings are [runtime-constants.tsv](../forms/runtime-constants.tsv). PERFORM_GRACE and REQUESTS_HOLD are exact seconds via /1000, not new settings.

```yaml
# PERFORM_TIMEOUT, COMMANDS_PER_LOOK: runtime-constants.tsv
PERFORM_GRACE: PERFORM_GRACE_MS / 1000
REQUESTS_HOLD: REQUESTS_HOLD_MS / 1000
```

<!-- req: P/03.6-R013 -->

Worker request filing follows the target manifest stamps, body validation and publication rules in P/03.6-R079, P/03.6-R080, P/03.6-R082 and P/03.6-R083; unreadable existing requests and invalid request ids are refused by P/03.6-R084 and P/03.6-R085.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R079 -->

The stamps of the row by `requests.stamp` of the target are as at the door (the request contract «Request»):
`by` — `<sub>/<unit>` of the filer (its own subsystem and the unit on whose behalf it files); `at` — its clock; `group` — by
`placement.group_by` of the addressee unit ([group by host contract](../../common/contracts/group-of.md)); the field `about` — the value of the addressee unit (the request contract `stamp`).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R080 -->

The body is checked by `requests.schema` of the target (the manifest catalogue, P/02.1 N3) before filing: not by the schema — it is not filed, the outcome `refused`. `maxLength` of a body field is measured by the written text of the field (a string — as is, another value — canonical JSON) at any type of the value — as at the door (P/06.4 N2 `too_long`, the rule of the table [`requests-body`](../../06-console/vectors/requests-body/README.md)); the other words of the schema — over the JSON value.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R022 -->

For each row of the family with `unit` of my unit (resource requests — rows without `unit` with `{free, volume, server}` — are not considered; the name
`free-*` by itself means nothing; `elsewhere` — not my actions; a row `free-<server>-<volume>` not in the form of the

(chapter 3.4 N1.1).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R145 -->

**Step 2.** If the mark has an outcome, skip it (a foreign one or my previous one).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R146 -->

**Step 3.** If it is my own `begun` without an outcome, do not call again; wait for the action already begun.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R147 -->

**Step 4.** If the deadline is beyond `most_valid`, refuse before the call (the mark `refused` and the event — below); this comes before the closing of a foreign `begun` and before the deadline.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R148 -->

**Step 5.** If it is a foreign `begun` without an outcome: if its `at` + `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` + `PERFORM_GRACE` is behind, write `{outcome: unknown, ended_by: holder,
error: "begun by <instance> and never answered"}` by the read index (the closer writes by the index; on `conflict` —
re-read, and if an outcome has appeared, do not touch it); otherwise (it is being executed) skip it; `begun` is not `expired`, also after the deadline.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R149 -->

**Step 6.** If the deadline has passed, create the mark `expired` once; further on. **Step 6.** The order of the checks: a mark with an outcome, `most_valid`, a foreign `begun`, then the deadline.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R151 -->

**Step 7.** If the stamp `group` of the request is non-empty and not equal to the group of the unit by `placement.group_by` of its read row (the group by host contract; no `group_by` — the group is empty),

**Step 10.** Call the action with the timeout `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` (+ `PERFORM_GRACE`).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R156 -->

**Step 10.1.** On an answer, read the mark and write the outcome by its index; on `conflict`, re-read and repeat only the write, perform is not repeated (the store door has no «transform» — so the write is atomic relative to what was read, and the holder's word still beats the reaper's word: the reaper and a second holder write by the index once); `late: true` is set by the read mark by whose index the write passed. **Step 10.1.** The outcome is `{outcome: performed | refused, action?, reply?, ended_at}`; `reply` is the answer of perform, the same as in the event below ([request contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) «Mark»); `action` is the value of the field `action` of the request body, not the handler name; a body without `action` has no such field ([request contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) «Mark»); `error` MUST be present at `refused` and is absent at `performed`; if the outcome came after `unknown` (of the reaper or of another holder) — `late: true`.
