### N1.2 Event: the writer and suppression

<!-- req: P/03.6-R007 -->

The worker is a participant of `append` of the event journal: the event line, its `id`, the bucket by `t`, the class and the alarm tree, the `fsync` of the alarm and of the
journal, the lock of the journal file — it observes them by the file contract of lesson 5 —
the [event journal contract](../../common/contracts/event-journal.md) (`line`, `bucket`, `class`, `journal`, `writer`) and its
section «Writing a line» (moved there from P/05 N3 «write an event»; review of stage B 3–5, V1). The suppression of repeats is step 3 of this procedure:

<!-- req: P/03.6-R008 -->

```yaml
kinds:                                       # events.kinds.<kind>: {severity} (the form — P/02.1; ADR-0115 addendum (3) item 6) — the severity of a kind
                                             # for the description of a kind of the VMS backend; the platform does not judge it and does not write it into the event line
suppress:                                    # events.suppress.<kind>: {window, by}  (the form — P/02.1)
  window:   seconds > 0
  by:       a non-empty list of string names of fields of the line or of the synthetic unit; without by — all fields of the line except t/id/repeats/since/until; these five names and an empty by — a load refusal
  rule:     the first line of a window — as is; repeats are counted; when the window closes or on a pass — one line
            {…the fields of the first, id: new, repeats, since, until}: repeats — the swallowed ones without the first; since — t of the first; until — t of the last
            swallowed one; t — the moment the summary is written; a window without repeats gives no line; the summary of the previous window is written before the first line
            of the new one (C03-SUPPRESS-SUMMARY-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, review of the VMS pass)
  stop:     when the writer stops, every open window with swallowed repeats is closed by the same summary line, until — the moment
            of the stop, under may_write and before the last heartbeat; a window without repeats gives no line (C03-SUPPRESS-STOP-01, the Architect's
            decision of 2026-10-29, review of V/04 finding 15)
```

<!-- req: P/03.6-R009 -->

Suppression is always separate for (tree, unit of the path, epoch, kind, class), regardless
of by. The name unit in by is the unit from the writer's arguments/the bucket path, not a user field
of the line of the same name. The other names of by are the top fields of the written line;
an unknown/absent field gives a separate value «absent», distinct from null.
The comparison is structural JSON: an object without regard to the order of keys, an array with regard to the order,
a bool is not a number. The absence of a selected field in a particular event is not a manifest error. t/id and the
summary fields do not take part; when by is absent, the other fields, including occurred/of, take part.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R010 -->

The form of `events.suppress` in the manifest and the refusals of its loading are [P/02.1](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md) N1.2, N4.

### N1.5 Request: the worker's side

<!-- req: P/03.6-R011 -->

The form of the row `<sub>/requests/<rid>`, the manifest keys `requests` and the mark `objects/<sub>/commands/<rid>` are the [request and its mark contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md)
(review of stage B 3–5, V1); the accounting of `asked` is [chapter P/06.4](../../06-console/spec/06.4-console.norms.md) N1.5. The worker in this form: it files a row by
`worker.requests` of another manifest — the fields `filed` (its own instance) and `digest` (it computes it itself at the write, the kind `worker`), `rid` by the
template `requests.key` of the target, otherwise by the filer's key `<own subsystem>.<key>` (the key — deterministically from its own data,
for example `<scenario>-<event id>-<i>`) or a random one without a dot — only for a request whose repeat is not needed (the request contract
`rid`; ADR-0091 addendum (20) item 2, addendum (21) item 2, addendum (23) item 2). A repeated filing with the same `rid` is checked by the order of the door
(P/06.4 N3 «file a request»: first the mark, then the row and the window `kept`; at a target with `requests.outcome: fetched` there is no mark step, C03-FILE-FETCHED-NO-MARK-01) and compares the `unit` of the row and the `digest` of the kind `worker`: the
same `rid`, the same `unit`, the same `digest` — `again`; anything else — `refused` (ADR-0091 addendum (21) item 2, addendum (23) item 1); how the holder writes the mark — `begun` before the call, `answer` of the one who began, `no_begun` with `ended_by: holder`,
the cleanup after `kept`. The holder's limits (the row of constants of N1.5; `CLEAR_EVERY`, `SWEEP_EVERY`, `REAP_AFTER` — the reaper, P/06.4 N1.5):

<!-- req: P/03.6-R012 -->

```yaml
constants:
  PERFORM_TIMEOUT: 10; PERFORM_GRACE: 0.2; REQUESTS_HOLD: 0.5; COMMANDS_PER_LOOK: 200
```

<!-- req: P/03.6-R013 -->

**Filing by the worker** (ADR-0091 item 7). The stamps of the row by `requests.stamp` of the target are as at the door (the request contract «Request»):
`by` — `<sub>/<unit>` of the filer (its own subsystem and the unit on whose behalf it files); `at` — its clock; `group` — by
`placement.group_by` of the addressee unit ([group by host contract](../../common/contracts/group-of.md)); the field `about` — the value of the addressee unit (the request contract `stamp`). The body
is checked by `requests.schema` of the target (the manifest catalogue, P/02.1 N3) before filing: not by the schema — it is not filed, the outcome
`refused`. `maxLength` of a body field is measured by the written text of the field (a string — as is, another value — canonical JSON) at
any type of the value — as at the door (P/06.4 N2 `too_long`, the rule of the table [`requests-body`](../../06-console/vectors/requests-body/README.md));
the other words of the schema — over the JSON value (C03-REQ-MAXLENGTH-WRITTEN-G19, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-27). After the request row is written — a journal line, as the door's audit (P/06.4 N3 «file a request» step 6: the kind `requests.journal`
of the target, the tree `audit` of the worker's role): a refusal of the journal does not change the outcome `filed`, a log line; `again` writes no journal (C03-FILE-JOURNAL-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, review of the VMS pass). The count of filing is the filer's heartbeat `filings.<outcome>` (`filed`, `again` — the same request
filed again, `refused`; [P/03.4](03.4-worker.norms.md) N1.1). A row under this `rid`, at any template of `rid`, that the worker meets when filing and that exists but does not read by its form — the worker does not file and does not touch it: the outcome `refused`, a log line without the value (V7 item 3, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-28, P1; row 3 of the list; C03-FILE-RID-REFUSED-01). A `rid` not by the grammar ([request and its mark contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md)), at any template, — the same: it is not filed, `refused`, the filer does not stall on it (C03-FILE-RID-REFUSED-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, review of the VMS pass).

### N1.6 Ordering a row of another subsystem: the customer's side

<!-- req: P/03.6-R014 -->

The owner's decision ADR-0091 item 1, variant «Г»: the customer — a subsystem C with `worker.creates: [{sub: A, rows, key}]`
(the form and the catalogue checks — [P/02.1](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md) N1.2a, N3) — creates and extends
the unit rows of the addressee A itself, from the worker library, under its own epoch. When, about what and with which fields to order is the logic
of subsystem C (its lesson); here — what the library does with each order (N3 «order»).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R015 -->

```yaml
order:       an event E that a unit U of the customer executes under its own epoch (the holder of U, P/03.2): the event time t_E —
             Unix seconds by the clock of the event's writer; the fields F of the row of A — without the id source field of A, without *_secret and blob
id:          <C>-<key(F)>                    # the prefix is not chosen
key:         the template worker.creates[].key — substitutions {field} joined by «-»: {field} — the value F[field] in the notation values_by_type
             (the row check contract: string, url — as is; int — decimal; float — its choice rule; bool — true | false),
             then every UTF-8 byte of this notation outside A–Z a–z 0–9 _ . ~ — %XX, hex digits in upper case (`-` → %2D,
             `%` → %25); between substitutions — the literal «-»; different values of the fields give different ids, so the id determines these
             values: a returned name is the same subject (ADR-0067); vectors creates-key
born:        a new random value at creation (the row check contract, born)
made, ORDER_HORIZON, not_after, tombstone: the row check contract «Строка заказчика: made» — `made` (keep), `ORDER_HORIZON`,
             `not_after` of the order write, the window over the tombstone gone + 60 (moved there without a change; layout audit «A-11», the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25)
fields:      the row is created by the row check contract «Preparation of fields», as at the door: what is not passed — default or the empty form of the type; the id source field —
             with the ready id; *_secret are never written — they stay empty, the secret is set by the operator through the door («G-8», the
             Architect's clarification of 2026-10-22); blob are empty (P/06.1 N1.5)
until:       the deadline field of the addressee — placement.ends.field of the manifest of A (P/04 N1.3), if ends is declared; extension — max(the previous,
             the new); a previous = never (without a deadline) is not touched
ends_past:   a row whose deadline by the field ends in F has already passed (≠ never and ≤ now by the holder's clock) is not created
deletes:     the worker of the addressee deletes a row with made by worker.deletes (the library) — by the rule of the row check contract
             «Строка заказчика: made» `delete` (layout audit «A-11», the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25); a row without made — as before (cas of the read version)
guard:       every write — CAS of its own row and guard {key: C/epoch/U, index: the version of the epoch row confirmed at the taking of the
             epoch} in one operation (P/01.1 guard: index — the version of the row, not the epoch number), and only while may_write
             (P/03.2 N1.6; «S-4»: after the store is restored, the guard version can coincide — the lease checks the floor)
writers:     the order row has two writers (P/06.1 N1.1): the operator — through the door, any fields except the fields of key (P/06.1 N1.3
             made_key); the customer — only rows of its own made (made.by = C); a row of its own made may be extended by any
             unit of C; a row without its own made is taken: C does not write it
check:       the common row check — the row check contract (P1–P5, R1–R3), the same as at the door; its reads are derived from the
             declaration (the rights — P/02.5 N1.4)
outcomes:    created | extended | unchanged | taken | gone | expired | refused   # the count — the heartbeat of C, P/03.4 N1.1 creates
```

<!-- req: P/03.6-R016 -->

Outcomes: `created` — the row is created; `extended` — a row of its own `made` is extended by a write; `unchanged` — the values of a row
of its own `made` do not change, there is no write; `taken` — the row is taken (no own `made`); `gone` — a tombstone stands in the place of the row,
there is no write; `expired` — the event is older than `ORDER_HORIZON`, the deadline of the row has already passed or the store refused with `409 expired`, there is no write; `refused` — the addressee is not in the
catalogue, the id is not by the grammar or does not fit, the common row check refused or the retry limit is exhausted. The cut-off of the
epoch (`412 guard`) does not count as an order outcome: the unit is stopped by P/03.2 N3 «fence».

### N1.7 Unit state

<!-- req: P/03.6-R017 -->

## N3 Procedures

### holder's look (the worker, on a pass; ≤ REQUESTS_HOLD, ≤ COMMANDS_PER_LOOK)

<!-- req: P/03.6-R020 -->

This procedure through begun/the mark is only the ordinary profile. The append is done by its own
data-checking handler, which reports fetched without a mark; elsewhere — by its owner. In the append profile
(`requests.outcome: fetched`) a body with an `action` from `elsewhere` is also an append: the holder checks the data and reports fetched;
`elsewhere` takes a row out of the mark procedure and out of the reaper only in the ordinary profile (the Architect's decision of 2026-10-20, review of stage B 6–7).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R021 -->

The order within a group (`placement.group_by`, «Device action» below): the rows of one group go by `at` of the stamp, equal ones — by `rid` in bytes, a row without `at` or with an unreadable one — after the stamped ones, by `rid`; while a call into the group is busy, all younger ones wait too: the `rid` of the door is random, so by the name a stop filed after a movement would be executed earlier. A limit: `at` is the filer's clock, two consoles with a clock skew do not keep the order, and for `at` of one second the order is given by `rid`; rows of different groups are not ordered (C03-REQUEST-GROUP-ORDER-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, a VMS request).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R022 -->

For each row of the family with `unit` of my unit (resource requests — rows without `unit` with `{free, volume, server}` — are not considered; the name
`free-*` by itself means nothing, ADR-0060; `elsewhere` — not my actions; a row `free-<server>-<volume>` not in the form of the
resource is not a request: skip it, the reason to the journal without the value — V7 item 3, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-28, row 10 of the list):

<!-- req: P/03.6-R023 -->

1. If the request row or its mark does not read by its form (the outcomes — `requests.outcomes.tsv`, `commands.outcomes.tsv` of P/06.4),
   stop the operation over this row: no write, delete or call of the action; a log line, `garbled_rows` of the heartbeat + 1
   (chapter 3.4 N1.1) (stage C guess RM-G01, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23).
2. If the mark has an outcome, skip it (a foreign one or my previous one).
3. If it is my own `begun` without an outcome, do not call again; wait for the action already begun.
4. If the deadline is beyond `most_valid`, refuse before the call (the mark `refused` and the event — below); this comes before the closing of a foreign `begun` and before the deadline.
5. If it is a foreign `begun` without an outcome: if its `at` + `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` + `PERFORM_GRACE` is behind, write `{outcome: unknown, ended_by: holder,
   error: "begun by <instance> and never answered"}` by the read index (ADR-0054: the closer writes by the index; on `conflict` —
   re-read, and if an outcome has appeared, do not touch it); otherwise (it is being executed) skip it; `begun` is not `expired`, also after the deadline.
6. If the deadline has passed, create the mark `expired` once; further on.
   The order of the checks is as in the witness: a mark with an outcome, `most_valid`, a foreign `begun`, then the deadline (C03-REQUEST-CHECK-ORDER-01,
   the Architect's decision of 2026-10-30, norm questions of the Gemini audit, P/03 item 2).
7. If the stamp `group` of the request is non-empty and not equal to the group of the unit by `placement.group_by` of its read row (the group by host contract; no `group_by` — the group is empty),
   the group has moved: refuse before the call, `error: "unit <id> was moved to another group after this request was given: it was not performed — give it again if it is still wanted"`;
   if there is no stamp or it is empty, it is not compared (C03-GROUP-MOVED-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, review of the VMS pass: as the witness).
8. On a refusal before the call (the deadline beyond `most_valid` — above; the group has moved, a foreign epoch), create the mark `refused` once `{…, error: the reason}`;
   and write the event `command.failed {outcome: refused, action?, by, error}` into the bucket of the unit under the holder's epoch, as at a
   refusal after the call, without `reply`; if the holder has no epoch of the unit (not taken), there is no event, the refusal is visible by the mark and the metric series
   (C03-REFUSED-BEFORE-CALL-EVENT-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-30, norm questions of the Gemini audit, P/03 item 3: as the witness).
   The holder checks before the call the deadline (`valid_until` in the past → `expired`; beyond `most_valid` → `refused`), the stamp `group` and its own epoch
   of the unit — it takes only the rows of the units that it leads under its own lease.
9. Create the mark `begun` = `put_new {instance, slot, unit, of?, at, digest, valid_until?}`; on `conflict`, re-read;
   on a refusal or an unknown outcome — no call; only a confirmed success permits the action.
10. Call the action with the timeout `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` (+ `PERFORM_GRACE`).
    1. On an answer, read the mark and write the outcome by its index; on `conflict`, re-read and repeat only the write, perform
       is not repeated (the store door has no «transform» — so the write is atomic relative to what was read, and the holder's word
       still beats the reaper's word, ADR-0054: the reaper and a second holder write by the index once;
       stage C guess W03-E06, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23); `late: true` is set by the read mark
       by whose index the write passed.
       The outcome is `{outcome: performed | refused, action?, reply?, ended_at}`; `reply` is the answer of perform, the same as in the event below ([request contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) «Метка», C06-MARK-REPLY-01); `action` is the value of the field `action` of the request body, not the handler name; a body
       without `action` has no such field ([request contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) «Метка»; review of stage B 6–7, № 16);
       `error` MUST be present at `refused` and is absent at `performed`; if the outcome came after `unknown` (of the reaper or of another holder) — `late: true`.
    2. Then the worker base writes into the bucket of the unit under the holder's epoch (N1.2, the event journal contract) an event of kind
       `command` at `performed`, `command.failed` at `refused`: `{outcome, action?, by, late?, error?, reply}` — `action` and `by`
       of the request body (`by` — the filer's stamp), `late` and `error` — as in the mark, `reply` — the answer of perform; a refusal before the call is also
       `command.failed`, without `reply` (above); at `unknown` and `expired` there is no event (they are visible by the mark and the metric series); the words of module P/07 N1.5 are by these fields (VMS request «команды и строка устройства», the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23).
    3. With no answer, write the full outcome with the saved `begun` fields, `ended_at`, `outcome: unknown`,
       `error: "no answer in <PERFORM_TIMEOUT> s"`; a late actual answer can still replace `unknown` with `late`. This is the protocol of one holder process epoch; elapsed timeout plus grace does not establish completion of an older holder call or cross-holder exclusion (R025).
11. Heartbeat: `fetched += SaidID(rid)` (the form — chapter 3.4 N1.1 `fetched`; `said_id` — the request acknowledgement contract «fetched»; the console will remove the row).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R025 -->

Device action (ADR-0091 item 6; a new norm from М10B/04:223). Within one holder (one process epoch), not more than one unfinished call of an action per group
of the unit (`placement.group_by`, [group by host contract](../../common/contracts/group-of.md); the target of the call is the group, so the targets are placed together, ADR-0011), also after
`unknown`: while a call into the group has not returned, there is no second one into it. The memory of the call is with the process, so a new holder of a group makes its first call into it not earlier than `PERFORM_TIMEOUT` + `PERFORM_GRACE` from the taking of the epoch of its first unit, if this epoch is not the first: a call of the previous holder could still be going; the request waits, its deadline runs (C03-GROUP-TAKEOVER-WAIT-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, review of the VMS invariants). «Device not open» is the subsystem's answer «not ready»
(`refused` with a reason), not a delayed call. The fixed wait is not proof that a previous holder call has physically finished: after a holder change, calls of two holders can overlap, even after unknown. This risk is explicit; no cross-holder exclusion is promised. A subsystem SHOULD provide epoch fencing. If the action receiver accepts an epoch, it MUST reject a call carrying an epoch lower than the last it has seen. Losing an epoch prevents the old holder new admissions and guarded writes; it does not prove cancellation or completion of an already sent call. The two-holder late-return seam records overlap_possible_marked without inventing an IO or cancellation guarantee. Calls go outside the loop thread; leases are held with all targets hung
(ADR-0037). For a unit of assignment without an epoch — first take the epoch ([P/03.2](03.2-worker.norms.md) N3 «take_epoch(unit)»,
М10B/04:257); the start of the unit inherits this epoch without a new issue ([P/03.3](03.3-worker.norms.md) N3 step 2).

### order(E, U) — the customer's library (N1.6)

<!-- req: P/03.6-R026 -->

The holder of U of the customer C under its own epoch, while `may_write`; `t_E` is the event time, F — the row fields, A/rows — from `worker.creates`.

<!-- req: P/03.6-R027 -->

1. If the manifest of A is not in the manifest catalogue, the outcome is `refused`.
2. If now − `t_E` > `ORDER_HORIZON`, or the field ends of A in F ≠ never and ≤ now, the outcome is `expired` (no write).
3. Let id := C + "-" + key(F) (N1.6 key); if id is not by the row check contract «Identifier and field constraints» or the key A/rows/id is not by P/01.1 N1, the outcome is `refused`.
4. Read A/rows/id:
   1. If there is no row: build the row `{id, revision: 1, born: new, made: {by: C, at: t_E, keep: t_E + ORDER_HORIZON + 120}, …}` with
      the fields by N1.6 fields; if the common row check (the row check contract, creation P1–P5) refuses, the outcome is `refused`;
      write with `cas ""`, the guard of N1.6 and `not_after` = `t_E + ORDER_HORIZON + 60`, the outcome is `created`.
   2. If it is a tombstone of its own made with `made.gone` and `t_E > made.gone + 60`: the row as above, the revision of the tombstone + 1, a new born;
      if the common check (creation over a tombstone: the same fixed) refuses, the outcome is `refused`;
      write with `cas` of the version of the tombstone, the guard and `not_after`, the outcome is `created`.
   3. If it is another tombstone (its own without gone, or the event not later than made.gone + 60), the outcome is `gone` (no write).
   4. If there is no made or made.by ≠ C (a live row or a tombstone), the outcome is `taken` (no write).
   5. If it is its own, the new values are: the field ends of A — max(the previous, from F), a previous = never is not touched; made.at — max(the previous, t_E);
      made.keep — max(the previous, made.at + ORDER_HORIZON + 120).
      1. If the values (including made.at, made.keep) do not change, the outcome is `unchanged` (no write).
      2. If the common row check (the row check contract, edit R1–R3 of the changed fields) refuses, the outcome is `refused`.
      3. Write `{…row, …new, revision + 1}` with `cas` of the read version, the guard and `not_after`, the outcome is `extended`.
   6. On `conflict` (cas), re-read from step 4; if the retry limit is exhausted, the outcome is `refused`.
   7. On `409 expired` (the admission time is later than not_after), the outcome is `expired`; do not repeat with the same write.
   8. On `412 guard`, the epoch of U is lost: no write, no order outcome; U — P/03.2 N3 «fence».
   9. On `unavailable` / `ambiguous`, repeat with the same write with the same write id (P/01.1 N4; P/03.4-07).
5. The outcome is counted in the heartbeat of C `creates.<outcome>` (P/03.4 N1.1).

<!-- req: P/03.6-R028 -->

**Races.** Two units of C order one event: `cas ""` lets one through, the second re-reads and finds a row of its own
`made` — `unchanged` or `extended`. An edit by the operator between the read and the write is a conflict, re-read. A delete of the row
(the controller, the worker of the addressee, the operator) between the read and the write is a conflict; a re-read tombstone — `gone`; no row
(physically deleted) — step 4 creates it again only if the write is admitted not later than `not_after`: the physical delete of a
row with `made` is admitted not earlier than its `not_before` = `made.keep` − 60 ≥ `not_after` of any executable replay
(P/04 N3 step 8), and the store's admission time does not decrease — a late replay gets `expired`, however the wall clocks go. The cut-off of the epoch of U and a write in any
order: the write is made before the change of the epoch row or refused with `412 guard` (P/01.1 «Races»).
