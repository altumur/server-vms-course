<!-- req: P/03.2-R009 -->

The age of an observed version is the time on the monotonic clock of a particular judge since the moment it first successfully read the current version of the row. A first look, a new version or changed bytes give age 0; a repeated read of the same state and a non-answer of the store do not reset it. The memory of observation is each judge's own; after a restart the first look is fresh again. Therefore held/lapsed are states relative to the observer, not a comparison of until with its clock. Judges with different histories can diverge for a while.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R104 -->

A write by someone else's clock gives no right to capture: that right is released/a free name or names_given with the exact version, plus the exceptions of N3.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R010 -->

The holder itself, to admit its own writes, remembers the monotonic moment of sending the last confirmed capture (the final write released:false) or slot renewal. The holder's own slot term ends SLOT_TERM after that request-send moment; neither receiving the answer nor rereading the slot extends that term. An answer to the slot capture or renewal request received after that own slot term has ended does not allow writing without a new confirmed renewal. The holder's own slot term is local to its own request; it is separate from the observer's version-age memory of P/03.2-R009.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R108 -->

For a replay of one write with the same id, the moment of its first send is kept; the answer again does not start a new term. A new renewal is a new write, not a replay of the former confirmation.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R011 -->

Confirmed row absence supplies the initial generation operand 0.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R110 -->

An existing `gen` MUST be an exact decimal integer in `1..9007199254740991`, the current capture writer range. Missing, zero, negative, above-bound or otherwise invalid gen selects the existing refusal-catalogue entry `slot_gen_unreadable`: do not rewrite the candidate, even after `TORN_STILL` or controller permission. Preserve its original bytes, without zeroing or rounding. The maximum `9007199254740991` is readable.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R114 -->

Before an allowed capture, check whether the next generation fits: the maximum gives `generation_exhausted` before a write.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R115 -->

Damage to other fields with a readable gen permits the first capture after `TORN_STILL` only with exact gen+1 inside the writer range. NameOnAnotherBox and capture admission still apply.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R117 -->

The machine input `gen_readable` is checked on the original bytes before narrowing; `gen_incrementable` is true for confirmed absence or a readable gen below the maximum. Machine int gen is an exact operand or an unused zero placeholder; a wide damaged gen never becomes zero.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R119 -->

With no preferred name, rejecting one candidate does not prevent checking the next ones.

### N1.4a The contender mark

<!-- req: P/03.2-R012 -->

The local policy selector is [`contender-writer.machine.yaml`](../forms/contender-writer.machine.yaml), with its [traces](../forms/contender-writer.traces.tsv) (judge-only: the policy traces) and [effect boundary](../forms/README.md#worker-policy-machines). Capture, IO and cryptography still follow the ordinary procedures below.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R121 -->

The priority of refused is above the other two.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R123 -->

On a decommissioned server (N3 «start()» step 3) there is no mark: the worker is nobody without a name search and is not a contender.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R124 -->

In a search without a given name `name` is - and `holder` is `""`; with a given name — the sought name and its current holder.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R125 -->

It is rewritten every `CONTEND_EVERY`; the freshness at the reader is the [observation contract](../../common/contracts/observation.md) «Freshness of a contender mark» (`CONTENDER_FRESH`): a mark older than `CONTENDER_FRESH` is not counted; an unreadable mark (not in form) is, for a worker reader, as one older than `CONTENDER_FRESH`, for the console a fresh unreadable one is a conflict with a flag (the outcomes by reader — [`contenders.outcomes.tsv`](../data/contenders.outcomes.tsv)); `since` is kept within one state.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R126 -->

<!-- req: P/03.2-R128 -->

Within one state retain since only from a readable current object or valid local policy memory, never from an unchecked value. A state change supplies a new since.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R130 -->

Freshness remains based on the observed version, not at or since.

### N1.5 The epoch row

<!-- req: P/03.2-R013 -->

**`<sub>/epoch/<id>.epoch`** — `<decimal integer ≥ 1>` — MUST; **`<sub>/epoch/<id>.epoch`** — grows by 1 on every taking of the unit by a worker (a new holder or a return after a loss); **`<sub>/epoch/<id>.epoch`** — a restart of the same unit by the same holder on a revision change does not change the epoch; The row `<sub>/epoch/<id>` is not deleted. **`<sub>/epoch/<id>.epoch`** is at most 2^53 − 1: an issue beyond that bound is refused before the write (N3 «take_epoch(id)»).

<!-- req: P/03.2-R132 -->

### N1.6 The lease

<!-- req: P/03.2-R014 -->

Action window: LEASE_TTL - LEASE_MARGIN from the last confirmed request send.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R133 -->

**`lease.states.confirmed`** — the issue of my epoch is confirmed or a read carries my number → last — the greatest send moment of the confirmed requests

<!-- req: P/03.2-R134 -->

**`lease.states.fenced`** — the row has another number, the row does not read or the worker is fenced → forever for this epoch

<!-- req: P/03.2-R135 -->

**`lease.states.silent`** — the store did not answer while the window was open → unconfirmed is counted from the end of the window

<!-- req: P/03.2-R136 -->

**`lease.states.lost`** — the window ended without a request (overslept), or the initial issue/launch was late, or the epoch is below floor — not silent

<!-- req: P/03.2-R137 -->

**`lease.may_act`** — not fenced and not lost and now − last < (LEASE_TTL - LEASE_MARGIN) and the own slot term of N1.4 has not expired. With lost writes and live reads, the old holder stops acting before the move: its own slot term is SLOT_TERM from the confirmed renewal send. **`lease.may_act`** — movement follows the controller contract P/04

<!-- req: P/03.2-R138 -->

**`lease.may_write`** — may_act or (silent and unconfirmed < unconfirmed_max) — unconfirmed_max: off = 0, forever = ∞

<!-- req: P/03.2-R139 -->

**`lease.unconfirmed_max`** — from manifest.lease (P/02.1): forever | off | `<integer ≥ 1 s>`; **`lease.unconfirmed_max`** — default off

<!-- req: P/03.2-R015 -->

The moment of a request is before the first send of the logical call. Network retries inside it, including a replay of a write with the same id/the answer again, do not move this moment. A new confirmation request has its own t0; last from requests already confirmed does not decrease.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R142 -->

On the first issue of an epoch, last is set by the confirmed write, then N3 «confirm» applies.

## N3 Procedures

### Full keys and store refusal

<!-- req: P/03.2-R017 -->

The worker branches of N3 «Full keys and store refusal» — the choice of a slot, the capture after it has started, the renewal, the write under the slot guard, the issue and confirmation of an epoch; the branch of the units door (`400 badkey`) — [chapter P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) N3 «Full keys and store refusal», the branches of the place hold — N3 «place: take, hold, let go».

<!-- req: P/03.2-R018 -->

In all operations below, the key rules of P/01.1 N1 apply after the full path of P/06.1 N1.1 is assembled, including the prefix, the family and the separators; the guard key is checked the same way. The grammar of UnitId, WorkerName and the 200-byte limit of a component of Idempotency-Key are kept. The schema of a component does not promise the capacity of the full key. Keys are not shortened and not hashed on a refusal.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R019 -->

A definite `badkey` on a read or a write is a refusal of that operation, not the absence of the row, not a CAS conflict and not a non-answer. The applicable key rules can be checked before calling the store; a refusal for exceeding the `KEY_BYTES` limit of P/01.1-R156 is handled the same way.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R020 -->

- In the choice of a slot, badkey means a refusal of the candidate before the first write: a given name leaves
  the process nobody, in a search the next one is checked; a refusal of a new computed name ends
  the search as nobody. After the capture has started, a refusal of an operation aborts the attempt without handing out a live slot;
  writes already confirmed are not rolled back. On a renewal the name is lost; on a write under the guard
  the write is refused, and the error of the target key by itself does not prove the loss of the slot.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R151 -->

- On the issue of an epoch badkey forbids the launch, on a confirmation — fenced without extending the window.
  In no branch is a refusal replaced by a success, an empty read or an endless retry.

### capture(name?) → slot | nobody

<!-- req: P/03.2-R021 -->

A call at the first start of an instance differs from a name search after a loss: the exception for its own box below is available only to the first call from N3 «start()».

<!-- req: P/03.2-R152 -->

A repeated search, a rejoin and continuing after fencing do not restore this right; the way to keep the flag is chosen by the implementation.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R153 -->

A first call none of whose candidate reads confirmed the row or its absence (the store did not answer) does not spend the right: the right passes on to the next search (N3 «nobody and getting the name back», rejoin()) until such a read happens.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R022 -->

**The capture transition table.** The normative machine is [forms/slot-capture.tsv](../forms/slot-capture.tsv). These are local states of an attempt, not fields of the slot row. Its header declares the input and action vocabulary; rows are checked in order, with refusals before permissions. The adapter derives the inputs from the re-read version and the contracts named there. Candidate order and the cleanup procedure remain the pseudo-code below.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R158 -->

A `-` action is an explicitly ungenerated procedure at its requirement anchor, not an empty action or a permission to skip it.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R159 -->

There is one kind of worker: an additional role process on a server is the same unit with its own name and the ordinary capture.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R023 -->

A candidate is not captured — pass to the next one; with a given name or an exhausted list return nobody. The table does not allow passing to another name for a named process.

<!-- req: P/03.2-R024 -->
