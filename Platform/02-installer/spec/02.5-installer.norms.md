### N1.3 Secret rows and readers

<!-- req: P/02.5-R005 -->
```yaml
secrets:                                     # a manifest section (the form — chapter 2.1)
  readers: {<row or prefix>: [<role>…]}     # own secret rows and who reads them
  reads:   [<row or prefix>…]               # foreign rows that the worker of this manifest reads
roles:     [console, controller, worker, domainworker, domain, domainagent, resource, domainconsole]
platform_readers:                            # the vectors platform-lists
  domain/break-glass/: [console, domain, domainagent]
  domain/signer:       [domain]
  domain/signer-pending: [domain]
  domain/book-seal:    [domain]
  domain/users/:       [domain]
  domain/member-key:   [domainagent, domain]
  door/signer:         [console]             # the token signer
rule:                                        # the rules of the declarations — below
```

<!-- req: P/02.5-R007 -->
`secrets.reads` ADDS `<B>worker` to the readers of a named row and takes nothing away. A row is made secret only by `readers` (of its own manifest or of another one) or `platform_readers`; an undeclared family stays with its own roles (N1.4).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R008 -->
The list `roles` is closed. `domainworker`, `domain`, `domainagent`, `domainconsole` are the domain roles (M11). `domainworker` is the platform word for the role `<sub>domain` of the worker on the domain (N3 step 1, at a manifest with `kept` or a transferable book; P/03.7 N1.6); the manifests of subsystems do not include it.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R011 -->
`platform_readers` are the vectors platform-lists; the rows `domain/*` belong to lesson P/08 and M11 (`signer`, `signer-pending`, `book-seal` — P/08.2 N1.3–N1.4). `domain/signer-pending` is an exact name, not covered by `domain/signer`.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R013 -->
- The form of `domain/member-key` is P/09.1 N1.1 (`domain` reads it only for the signature of `GET /api/backup`, P/08.8 N1.8 step 5); the public key is non-secret in `domain/member` and `Entry.key`.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R014 -->
- The backups of P/08.8 N1.5 are objects, not secret rows: they are not in the list.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R015 -->
`door/signer` is the token signer of P/06.5 N3 «`/where/<id>`»; the console always reads it — the issue of the token
  (P/06.5) and the resealing at start (P/06.2) — not depending on `secrets.readers` of the manifest. A declaration of the manifest adds readers of `door/signer`, it does not take the console away.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R018 -->
The declarations `secrets.readers`/`reads` do not contain a literal `*` and do not start with `!` or `delete:`. An unrepresentable declaration is rejected by the loader/generator before the file is issued; it is not escaped and not truncated. The restriction concerns the declarations of rights, not the general store key of P/01.1.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R020 -->
A key with `/` at the end is a prefix, without it — an exact key (`domain/<sub>/<book>` does not close `domain/<sub>/<book>/*` — that is why they are named separately). A domain book at a member is the exact name `domain/<sub>/<book>`, its rows by clusters at the holder are the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` (the manifest names both).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R023 -->
Declarations do not intersect by the rows they close: two prefixes where one is the start of the other (`a/` and `a/b/`) are a refusal
  of the loader. An exact name and a prefix with the same start (`domain/signer` and `domain/signer-pending`,
  `domain/<sub>/<book>` and `domain/<sub>/<book>/`) do not intersect — an exact name closes one row,
  a prefix — the rows under `/`; therefore there is no «longest match»: a row has exactly one declaration.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R025 -->
A row declared secret without a reader is read by nobody except admin (a manifest has no such row: the reader list `secrets.readers` is not empty — the form of P/02.1, `[]` is a load refusal). The rights file is not generated if the readers ≠ those able to read (N3 step 4).

### N1.4 Derivation of rights from the manifest

<!-- req: P/02.5-R026 -->
The positive lists below are the source rights, not a bypass of P/01.2 N1.3: never applies to everyone,
including admin, regardless of the rights file (P/01.2 N1.3 never — exactly three: delete of a row `<x>/epoch/<y>` — one segment `<y>`
after «`<x>/epoch/`», including an empty one, `x` ≠ `platform`; delete under `domain/` not by a domain role — `domain`, `domainagent`, `<sub>domain`;
write and delete of `platform/epoch_floor`). The negations of the secret readers of N1.3 are subtracted from read.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R028 -->
The writers of the families — [P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) and P/03 (unit rows, slots, epochs), [P/04](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md), P/05; [the contract of a request and its mark](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) and P/06.4 N1.5 (`<s>/requests/*`, `<s>/asked/*`); P/06.6 N1.1a, N1.2 (`platform/schema`, `platform/drain`), P/06.1 N1.5, N1.6 (`<s>/sweep`, `<s>/<table>/*`); here is a summary of what the generator derives for each role from the manifest keys.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R029 -->
The read rights are positive lists, derived the same way. Secret rows are read only by the declared readers (N1.3).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R031 -->
The general derivation rules act on every row of the table; a row does not repeat them: the `read` of each role includes `platform/manifest/*` — the manifests of the cluster by which it works, if it is
  not already covered by `platform/*`; `read` includes all positive patterns of its `write`; delete-only patterns (`delete:…`) and the negations of `write` do not enter `read`;

<!-- req: P/02.5-R036 -->
the negations `!<s>/idem/*` and `!<s>/asked/*` (the applications and the accounting of people — only the console) are given only to the roles whose reads
  reach them (a pattern covering `<s>/*`); a declaration that directly names a row or a prefix inside `<s>/idem/` or
  `<s>/asked/` for a role other than the console (`worker.reads`, `secrets.reads`, `secrets.readers` with another reader) is a
  generation refusal before the file, not a subtraction and not an access;

<!-- req: P/02.5-R037 -->
- the rights file for the same inputs is byte-stable;

<!-- req: P/02.5-R038 -->
the words of `secrets.readers`: `controller` and `worker` are the roles of the declaring manifest (`<s>controller`, `<s>worker`),
  the others are the platform roles as they are, `domainworker` — by P/01.2 N1.1; `secrets.reads` of the manifest `B` ADDS `Bworker` to the readers of the named row and takes nothing away; the negations `!<pattern>` to the others the generator issues only for rows
  declared secret — in `secrets.readers` of a manifest of the catalogue or in the vectors [`platform-lists`](../vectors/platform-lists/README.md); a family named in `secrets.reads` but not declared secret (the unit rows of another manifest — `testsub/counters/` at
  `testsub2`) gives only the read of `Bworker`: its owner roles read it by their own rights, as without a declaration;

<!-- req: P/02.5-R042 -->
the object pattern `objects/<s>/commands/*` is issued into the file as it is, as a `write` pattern (and with it, by the general rules, `read`
  and delete) of the role `<s>worker` at a manifest with the section `requests`: the request marks are written, closed and removed by the holder —
  the worker (the contract of a request and its mark, «Mark» mark); the object pattern does not touch store rows, the row evaluator does not meet it.

### N1.5 The worker role socket to the console

## N3 Procedures

### generate_rights(manifest catalogue, deployment) → file

<!-- req: P/02.5-R076 -->
1. Roles: `console`, `resource`, `domain`, `domainconsole`, `domainagent` (M11); for each manifest — `<s>controller`, `<s>worker`
   and `<s>domain` — at a manifest with `domain.kept` or a transferable book (the subsystem worker on the domain, P/03.7 N1.6);
   the groups by P/01.2 N1.1.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R078 -->
2. For each role — write by N1.4 from the manifest keys. Delete = write, minus all rights «write only» of N1.4:
   the command marks at the console, `<s>/controller/placement_turn` and `<s>/controller/placement_intent` at the controller;
   plus all delete-only ones of N1.4: `delete:platform/doors/*` at the console, `delete:<s>/<rows>/*` at the controller with `delete_after` and
   at the worker by `worker.deletes`. The delete list is issued explicitly,
   so that the absence of the field does not bring back the excluded rights through the default of P/01.2 N1.2.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R081 -->
3. Read by N1.4; `worker.reads`: a key with `/` at the end → a prefix. `domain/placed/<sub>/` additionally gives `domain/placement/<sub>/`; the source `domain/placed/<sub>/` is kept.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R082 -->
   The copy of its own book `domain/<sub>/<book>` in `worker.reads` (or `secrets.reads`) additionally gives the exact key `domain/<sub>/<book>/<CLUSTER>` —
   its own book row at the holder cluster, where there is no copy (P/08.5 N1.4); at a member there is no row, the right is empty. The worker is a secret reader
   of this one row if the copy is in its `secrets.reads`, without a `!<pattern>` of the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` (step 4 does not issue it: the worker's reads
   reach only this row). The prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` is neither derived for the worker by the manifest nor widened. A book `to: "*"` is declared by the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` (the head and the sections, P/08.5 N1.4): the rule does not fire, such a book has no row `<CLUSTER>`, and the prefix already covers its own keys — there is no exact key in the file, otherwise the output of two implementations would diverge on `--check`.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R087 -->
4. First check the representability of all declarations of N1.4; on a refusal there is no file. Secrets: a secret row is one declared in `secrets.readers` of a manifest of the catalogue or in the vectors platform-lists; its readers are by
   `secrets.readers`/`reads` (and the platform readers, N1.3). `secrets.reads` on an undeclared row — only +`Bworker`, without `!`. The pattern is the key as declared (N1.3): a prefix with `/` at the end → `<prefix>*`, an exact key → the key itself without `*`
   (`domain/<sub>/<book>` does not close `domain/<sub>/<book>/*`). Declarations do not nest (N1.3) — the negation of one does not
   touch another.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R091 -->
   Across manifests: an element of `secrets.reads` that strictly nests into a declared secret row
   or prefix (`secrets.readers` of any manifest of the catalogue, `platform_readers`) or contains it is unrepresentable — a
   generation refusal (an equal key is an ordinary +`Bworker`; the negation wins everywhere, P/01.2, — a part of a secret cannot be expressed without widening access).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R092 -->
   To a reader — the pattern, if its reads do not reach it.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R093 -->
   To a non-reader whose rights reach it — `!<pattern>`,
   but after subtraction: only if after the negations already issued to it (the role row of N1.4, N1.4a and the general derivation rules)
   its rights still reach the pattern; there are no extra ones — `domainconsole` with `!domain/signer*` has no `!domain/signer` and
   `!domain/signer-pending`.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R094 -->
   If the set of the declared ordinary readers ≠ the set of the ordinary roles able to read, refuse the generation.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R095 -->
   The internal admin is not compared with this set (its exclusion — P/01.2 N1.3 and N1.3).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R097 -->
5. Output JSON with sorted patterns (byte-stable). «generated — do not edit» — the field `comment` or an accompanying text outside JSON. Do not write comments of the syntax `//` or `/* */`. For the same inputs the form and the text of the mark are stable. `--check` compares by meaning (N3 «install»), not bytes.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R102 -->
**Races.** The input is an unchanged catalogue by P/01.2 N1.2. Two generations with the same inputs give the file
byte for byte. Different inputs can give the same rights; the check does not prove that the
source files are unchanged, only that the rights file coincides with the result of generation.

### install(rights file)

<!-- req: P/02.5-R104 -->
1. The rights file: the installed file is `/etc/w2c/configstore-rights.json`, the candidate is `/etc/w2c/configstore-rights.json.new` next to it. The order: `rights` generates the candidate from `MANIFEST_DIR` after the step «manifests» (P/02.4 N1.7 `steps`; a generation refusal —
   no candidate, the preliminary check of P/02.4 N1.7 runs the same generation over the manifests of the installation before changes) →
   `rights --check <candidate>` against `MANIFEST_DIR`; if it diverges, the installation is refused, the candidate is removed, the installed file is intact →
   the atomic rename of the candidate over the installed file. The installed file is not compared with the candidate before the replacement:
   a difference (a new subsystem, a new role, a changed manifest) is the ordinary replacement of old rights by new ones, not a refusal.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R107 -->
   The running daemon takes the new file only at a restart (P/01.2 N1.2; races below).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R108 -->
   The comparison is by meaning: the same set of roles; at each role `group` and the sets of patterns read, write, delete are equal
   (order and repeats in the list do not matter; no delete — an empty set, as at the evaluator of P/01.2 N1.2). `comment`, the order of keys and spaces are not compared. A file not by the form of P/01.2 N1.2 diverges.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R111 -->
2. Units: `SupplementaryGroups=<role group>`; `PLATFORM_STORE=configstore:///run/configstore/<role>.sock`.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R112 -->
3. The daemon: a socket per role, 0660, the role group — `SocketGroup=` of the unit `configstore-<role>.socket` (chapter 2.4 N1.1); `admin.sock` 0600.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R113 -->
**Races.** The installation is performed by one installer on an unchanged catalogue; a parallel
installation and an edit of `MANIFEST_DIR` are excluded from this lesson.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R114 -->
A running daemon keeps the previous
rights until a restart.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R115 -->
A subsystem added to a running cluster gets its rights by the same construction from the manifest file in `MANIFEST_DIR` and by a restart of the member one server at a time (P/02.4 N1.7 `add`, N1.9 `rollout`).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R116 -->
The rollout barrier compares `GET /v1/rights` of the member with the rollout file by the same comparison as `--check` (N3 «install»).

<!-- req: P/02.5-R117 -->
Before a start with a new file the installation performs `--check` of the candidate (N3 «install» item 1); on a
divergence the candidate does not replace the installed file and the daemon is not started with it.

<!-- req: P/02.5-R118 -->
The lesson does not promise a live switch of rights or an atomic
switch of the whole cluster.

## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **the internal API of the generation tool** (the tool itself — N2; public argv — [`operator-commands.tsv`](../forms/operator-commands.tsv)), the default file path (`/etc/w2c/configstore-rights.json`), the path of the sockets on non-Linux;
2. ~~**how the daemon gets into the role groups or does without them by setgid directories**~~ — normed: the group of the role socket is set by `SocketGroup=` of the socket unit, the daemon does not enter the role groups (chapter 2.4 N1.1, P/01.3 N1.3 `sockets`);
3. **the internal API of the admin writes to `platform/mirror`, `platform/space`, `<s>/retention` and `<s>/alarms_retention`** (public `w2cctl mirror|space|retention` argv are fixed by `operator-commands.tsv`, chapter 2.4 N1.9; the resource and the console only read them — N1.4) and **how the console deletes `platform/doors/<s>` at the withdrawal of a server** (`delete:` in N1.4);
4. **the literal text of the mark `comment`** beyond the mandatory content (P/01.2 N1.2); the acceptance does not compare `comment` (P/02.5-R128).
5. **the deterministic ordering rule for sorted patterns**; the choice of comparator is not judged. The observable obligations are byte-identical output for the same inputs (P/02.5-R097, P/02.5-R102) and the rights comparison by meaning at `--check` (P/02.5-R128).
