### N1.3 Secret rows and readers

Moved from M10/7 N1.3 (stage B).

```yaml
secrets:                                     # a manifest section (the form — chapter 2.1)
  readers: {<row or prefix>: [<role>…]} # own secret rows and who reads them
  reads:   [<row or prefix>…]           # foreign rows that the worker of this manifest reads: ADDS <B>worker to the readers,
                                             # takes nothing away; a row is made secret only by readers (of its own manifest or
                                             # of another one) or platform_readers — an undeclared family stays with its own roles (N1.4)
roles:     [console, controller, worker, domainworker, domain, domainagent, resource, domainconsole]   # closed; domainworker, domain, domainagent, domainconsole are the domain roles (M11); domainworker is the platform word for the role <sub>domain of the worker on the domain (N3 step 1, at a manifest with kept or a transferable book; P/03.7 N1.6), the manifests of subsystems do not include it
platform_readers:                            # vectors platform-lists (vectors/platform-lists/cases.tsv); the rows domain/* — lesson P/08 and M11 (signer, signer-pending, book-seal — P/08.2 N1.3–N1.4)
  domain/break-glass/: [console, domain, domainagent]
  domain/signer:       [domain]
  domain/signer-pending: [domain]           # an exact name, not covered by `domain/signer`
  domain/book-seal:    [domain]
  domain/users/:       [domain]
  domain/member-key:   [domainagent, domain]            # the form — P/09.1 N1.1 (domain — only the signature of GET /api/backup, P/08.8 N1.8 step 5); the public key — non-secret in domain/member and Entry.key
                                            # the backups of P/08.8 N1.5 are objects, not secret rows: they are not in the list
  door/signer:         [console]             # added by M10/9 (the token signer of P/06.5 N3 «/where/<id>»); the console always reads it — the issue of the token
                                            # (P/06.5) and the resealing at start (P/06.2) — not depending on secrets.readers of the manifest; a declaration of the manifest
                                            # adds readers, it does not take the console away (analysis of review of stage B 6–7, № 36)
rule:      the declarations secrets.readers/reads do not contain a literal `*` and do not start with `!` or `delete:`;
           an unrepresentable declaration is rejected by the loader/generator before the file is issued, it is not escaped and not truncated.
           The restriction concerns the declarations of rights, not the general store key of P/01.1.
           A key with `/` at the end is a prefix, without it — an exact key (`domain/<sub>/<book>` does not close `domain/<sub>/<book>/*` — that is why they are named separately);
           a domain book at a member is the exact name `domain/<sub>/<book>`, its rows by clusters at the holder are the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` (the manifest names both);
           declarations do not intersect by the rows they close: two prefixes where one is the start of the other (`a/` and `a/b/`) are a refusal
           of the loader; an exact name and a prefix with the same start (`domain/signer` and `domain/signer-pending`,
           `domain/<sub>/<book>` and `domain/<sub>/<book>/`) do not intersect — an exact name closes one row,
           a prefix — the rows under `/`; therefore there is no «longest match»: a row has exactly one declaration; a row declared secret without a reader is read by nobody except admin (a manifest has no such row: the reader list `secrets.readers` is not empty — the form of P/02.1, `[]` is a load refusal; C02-EMPTY-SECRET-READERS-01);
           the rights file is not generated if the readers ≠ those able to read (N3 step 4)
```

### N1.4 Derivation of rights from the manifest

The positive lists below are the source rights, not a bypass of P/01.2 N1.3: never applies to everyone,
including admin, regardless of the rights file (P/01.2 N1.3 never — exactly three: delete of a row `<x>/epoch/<y>` — one segment `<y>`
after «`<x>/epoch/`», including an empty one, `x` ≠ `platform`; delete under `domain/` not by a domain role — `domain`, `domainagent`, `<sub>domain`;
write and delete of `platform/epoch_floor`; C02-NEVER-LIST-01); the negations of the secret readers of N1.3 are subtracted from read.

The writers of the families — [P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) and P/03 (unit rows, slots, epochs), [P/04](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md), P/05; [the contract of a request and its mark](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) and P/06.4 N1.5 (`<s>/requests/*`, `<s>/asked/*`; analysis of review of stage B 6–7, № 24); P/06.6 N1.1a, N1.2 (`platform/schema`, `platform/drain`), P/06.1 N1.5, N1.6 (`<s>/sweep`, `<s>/<table>/*`); here is a summary of what the generator derives for each role from the manifest keys. The read rights are positive lists, derived the same way; secret rows are read only by the declared readers (N1.3).

The general derivation rules act on every row of the table; a row does not repeat them:

- the `read` of each role includes `platform/manifest/*` — the manifests of the cluster by which it works (ADR-0068), if it is
  not already covered by `platform/*`;
- `read` includes all positive patterns of its `write`; delete-only patterns (`delete:…`) and the negations of `write`
  do not enter `read`;
- the negations `!<s>/idem/*` and `!<s>/asked/*` (the applications and the accounting of people — only the console) are given only to the roles whose reads
  reach them (a pattern covering `<s>/*`); a declaration that directly names a row or a prefix inside `<s>/idem/` or
  `<s>/asked/` for a role other than the console (`worker.reads`, `secrets.reads`, `secrets.readers` with another reader) is a
  generation refusal before the file, not a subtraction and not an access (C02-IDEM-ASKED-EXPLICIT-READ-01); the rights file for the same inputs is byte-stable;
- the words of `secrets.readers`: `controller` and `worker` are the roles of the declaring manifest (`<s>controller`, `<s>worker`),
  the others are the platform roles as they are, `domainworker` — by P/01.2 N1.1; `secrets.reads` of the manifest `B` ADDS `Bworker`
  to the readers of the named row and takes nothing away; the negations `!<pattern>` to the others the generator issues only for rows
  declared secret — in `secrets.readers` of a manifest of the catalogue or in the vectors [`platform-lists`](../vectors/platform-lists/README.md) (`../vectors/platform-lists/cases.tsv`; N1.3);
  a family named in `secrets.reads` but not declared secret (the unit rows of another manifest — `testsub/counters/` at
  `testsub2`) gives only the read of `Bworker`: its owner roles read it by their own rights, as without a declaration;
- the object pattern `objects/<s>/commands/*` is issued into the file as it is, as a `write` pattern (and with it, by the general rules, `read`
  and delete) of the role `<s>worker` at a manifest with the section `requests`: the request marks are written, closed and removed by the holder —
  the worker (the contract of a request and its mark, «Mark» mark; analysis of review of stage B 6–7, № 24); it does not touch store rows, the row evaluator does not meet it (log of cammeta-go, lesson 8 № 4).

Declarations translated into patterns (`worker.reads`, `secrets.readers`, `secrets.reads`) do not contain a literal `*` and do not start with `!`/`delete:`; an unrepresentable declaration is a load/generation refusal before the file. This is not a ban of such a key in the store itself. Check not only the readers of the secret itself but also that the rights of the neighbouring ordinary rows are unchanged.

The rules of the generator: a role deletes what it writes (except the command marks at the console and the two kept admission rows at the controller); the pattern `delete:<…>` gives only delete without write (the console — `platform/doors/*` of a withdrawn server and the table rows of `worker.writes`, the controller — the unit rows with `delete_after`, the worker — the rows of its own units with `worker.deletes`, a delete with `cas` by the read version); `worker.writes` MUST name a declared own table; the name `unit.rows` and an undeclared name give the load refusal `refused:reference` (chapter 2.1). The unit rows stay with the console and with the controller's delete by admission; the exclusion of the worker's table gives it no rights to `unit.rows`; the families `<s>/idem/*` and `<s>/asked/*` — the negation `!` to all except the console; secret rows (declared in `secrets.readers` or `platform_lists.tsv`) — to the readers by `secrets.readers` and `secrets.reads`, to the others whose right reaches it even after the negations already issued — `!<pattern>` (N3 step 4; C02-NONREADER-DENY-AFTER-01); `secrets.reads` without such a declaration only adds the read of `Bworker` (N1.4), where the pattern is the key as declared: a prefix with `/` → `<prefix>*`, an exact key — without `*` (N1.3); the generator refuses to issue the file if the set of the declared ordinary readers of a secret is not equal to the set of the ordinary roles that will read it. The internal admin is checked by the special rule of P/01.2 N1.3 and is not included in the declaration of readers.

## N3 Procedures

### generate_rights(manifest catalogue, deployment) → file

1. Roles: `console`, `resource`, `domain`, `domainconsole`, `domainagent` (M11); for each manifest — `<s>controller`, `<s>worker`
   and `<s>domain` — at a manifest with `domain.kept` or a transferable book (the subsystem worker on the domain, P/03.7 N1.6; ADR-0074 C7
   item 2, addendum 5); the groups by P/01.2 N1.1.
2. For each role — write by N1.4 from the manifest keys; delete = write, minus all rights «write only» of N1.4:
   the command marks at the console, `<s>/controller/placement_turn` and `<s>/controller/placement_intent` at the controller;
   plus all delete-only ones of N1.4: `delete:platform/doors/*` at the console, `delete:<s>/<rows>/*` at the controller with `delete_after` and
   at the worker by `worker.deletes`; the delete list is issued explicitly,
   so that the absence of the field does not bring back the excluded rights through the default of P/01.2 N1.2.
3. Read by N1.4; `worker.reads`: a key with `/` at the end → a prefix; `domain/placed/<sub>/` additionally gives `domain/placement/<sub>/`; the source `domain/placed/<sub>/` is kept;
   the copy of its own book `domain/<sub>/<book>` in `worker.reads` (or `secrets.reads`) additionally gives the exact key `domain/<sub>/<book>/<CLUSTER>` —
   its own book row at the holder cluster, where there is no copy (P/08.5 N1.4); at a member there is no row, the right is empty; the worker is a secret reader
   of this one row if the copy is in its `secrets.reads`, without a `!<pattern>` of the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` (step 4 does not issue it: the worker's reads
   reach only this row); the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` is neither derived for the worker by the manifest nor widened (C02-OWN-BOOK-HOLDER-01, the
   Architect's decision of 2026-10-29, analysis of V/03 question 8); a book `to: "*"` is declared by the prefix `domain/<sub>/<book>/` (the head and the sections, P/08.5 N1.4): the rule does not fire, such a book has no row `<CLUSTER>`, and the prefix already covers its own keys — there is no exact key in the file, otherwise the output of two implementations would diverge on `--check`.
4. First check the representability of all declarations of N1.4; on a refusal there is no file.
   Secrets: a secret row is one declared in `secrets.readers` of a manifest of the catalogue or in the vectors platform-lists; its readers are by
   `secrets.readers`/`reads` (and the platform readers, N1.3); `secrets.reads` on an undeclared row — only +`Bworker`, without `!`;
   the pattern is the key as declared (N1.3): a prefix with `/` at the end → `<prefix>*`, an exact key → the key itself without `*`
   (`domain/<sub>/<book>` does not close `domain/<sub>/<book>/*`); declarations do not nest (N1.3) — the negation of one does not
   touch another; and across manifests: an element of `secrets.reads` that strictly nests into a declared secret row
   or prefix (`secrets.readers` of any manifest of the catalogue, `platform_readers`) or contains it is unrepresentable — a
   generation refusal (an equal key is an ordinary +`Bworker`; the negation wins everywhere, P/01.2, — a part of a secret cannot be expressed without widening access;
   C02-SECRETS-READS-INTERSECTION-01); to a reader — the pattern, if its reads do not reach it; to a non-reader whose rights reach it — `!<pattern>`,
   but after subtraction: only if after the negations already issued to it (the role row of N1.4, N1.4a and the general derivation rules)
   its rights still reach the pattern; there are no extra ones — `domainconsole` with `!domain/signer*` has no `!domain/signer` and
   `!domain/signer-pending` (C02-NONREADER-DENY-AFTER-01; the Architect's decision of 2026-10-30, norm questions of the Gemini audit, P/02 item 2).
   If the set of the declared ordinary readers ≠ the set of the ordinary roles able to read, refuse the generation;
   the internal admin is not compared with this set (its exclusion — P/01.2 N1.3 and N1.3).
5. Output JSON with sorted patterns (byte-stable); «generated — do not edit» —
   the field `comment` or an accompanying text outside JSON. Do not write comments of the syntax `//` or `/* */`.
   For the same inputs the form and the text of the mark are stable; `--check` compares by meaning (N3 «install»), not bytes.

**Races.** The input is an unchanged catalogue by P/01.2 N1.2. Two generations with the same inputs give the file
byte for byte. Different inputs can give the same rights; the check does not prove that the
source files are unchanged, only that the rights file coincides with the result of generation.

### install(rights file)

1. The rights file: the installed file is `/etc/w2c/configstore-rights.json`, the candidate is `/etc/w2c/configstore-rights.json.new` next to it.
   The order: `rights` generates the candidate from `MANIFEST_DIR` after the step «manifests» (P/02.4 N1.7 `steps`; a generation refusal —
   no candidate, the preliminary check of P/02.4 N1.7 runs the same generation over the manifests of the installation before changes) →
   `rights --check <candidate>` against `MANIFEST_DIR`; if it diverges, the installation is refused, the candidate is removed, the installed file is intact →
   the atomic rename of the candidate over the installed file. The installed file is not compared with the candidate before the replacement:
   a difference (a new subsystem, a new role, a changed manifest) is the ordinary replacement of old rights by new ones, not a refusal;
   the running daemon takes the new file only at a restart (P/01.2 N1.2; races below; review P/02 S8).
   The comparison is by meaning: the same set of roles; at each role `group` and the sets of patterns read, write, delete are equal
   (order and repeats in the list do not matter; no delete — an empty set, as at the evaluator of P/01.2 N1.2); `comment`, the order
   of keys and spaces are not compared; a file not by the form of P/01.2 N1.2 diverges
   (the store pilot, A5).
2. Units: `SupplementaryGroups=<role group>`; `PLATFORM_STORE=configstore:///run/configstore/<role>.sock`.
3. The daemon: a socket per role, 0660, the role group — `SocketGroup=` of the unit `configstore-<role>.socket` (chapter 2.4 N1.1); `admin.sock` 0600.

---

**Races.** The installation is performed by one installer on an unchanged catalogue; a parallel
installation and an edit of `MANIFEST_DIR` are excluded from this lesson. A running daemon keeps the previous
rights until a restart; a subsystem added to a running cluster gets its rights by the same construction from the manifest file in `MANIFEST_DIR` and by a restart of the member one server at a time (P/02.4 N1.7 `add`, N1.9 `rollout`; ADR-0115 addendum (21)–(24)); the rollout barrier compares `GET /v1/rights` of the member with the rollout file by the same comparison as `--check` (N3 «install»). Before a start with a new file the installation performs `--check` of the candidate (N3 «install» item 1); on a
divergence the candidate does not replace the installed file and the daemon is not started with it. The lesson does not promise a live switch of rights or an atomic
switch of the whole cluster.
