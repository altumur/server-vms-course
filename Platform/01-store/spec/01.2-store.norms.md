### N1.1 Roles

<!-- req: P/01.2-R006 -->
```yaml
roles:
  per_manifest:
    <sub>controller:     the controller of the manifest
    <sub>worker:         the worker of the manifest
  platform:              [resource, console, domain, domainconsole, domainagent]
  store_internal:        [admin, configstore, configstore-door]
  reader_word_only:      [domainworker]
name:        ^[a-z][a-z0-9_-]{0,63}$
group:
  platform role:         w2c-<role>
  subsystem role:        <deployment>-<role>
deployment:  ^[a-z][a-z0-9]*$
identity:
  cluster:
    <role>.sock:         {path: /run/configstore/<role>.sock, mode: "0660", group: the group of the role}
    admin.sock:          {mode: "0600", owner: root}
  box:                   the writer's name
  doors:                 the role certificate
```

See also: P/01.1-R144, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R015, P/01.2-R017, P/01.2-R025, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R006, P/01.3-R009, P/01.3-R011, P/01.3-R016, P/01.3-R032, P/01.3-R033, P/01.3-R035.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R032 -->
The roles `<sub>controller` and `<sub>worker` come from each manifest of the catalogue.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R033 -->
`console` is one per cluster and writes the operator rows of all manifests.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R034 -->
`domain`, `domainconsole` and `domainagent` are the roles of the domain (M11): `domain` is the signer with keys, `domainconsole` has no keys (P/08.2 N1.3).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R035 -->
The roles of `store_internal` are not named in the rights file.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R036 -->
`configstore` is the role of a peer, another daemon of the group; `configstore-door` is the door without a vote (added by P/01.3 N1.3).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R037 -->
`domainworker` (P/03.7 N1.6: a worker of a subsystem on the domain) is a platform word in `secrets.readers` (P/02.5 N1.3): it gives no role in the rights file.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R038 -->
`domainworker` maps to the role `<sub>domain` of a manifest that has `domain.kept` or a portable book (P/03.7 N1.6: a platform capability, not the removed process `domainpart`).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R039 -->
`domainworker` does not enter the comparison «declared = able to read».

<!-- req: P/01.2-R040 -->
A role name matches `name`; `<sub>` follows the grammar of the manifest name (P/02.1).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R041 -->
The group of a platform role — `console`, `resource`, `domain*` (M11) and every `<sub>controller` — is `w2c-<role>`.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R042 -->
The group `<deployment>-<role>` of a subsystem role belongs only to `<sub>worker`: the controller is the platform role `w2c-<sub>controller`; the deployment name matches `deployment`.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R043 -->
On a cluster the identity is the socket of `identity.cluster`; the group of `<role>.sock` is `SocketGroup=` of the socket unit (P/02.4 N1.1).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R044 -->
On a box the identity is the writer's name at a store of files/memory, with the same list of rights; the box door also opens a socket per role.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R045 -->
On a box there are no trusted forwarding senders: if a role header comes on any socket, `admin.sock` included, then the store MUST refuse with `403` naming the role of the socket.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R046 -->
At the doors the identity is the role certificate — P/02.4 N1.6 `doors`; its form is the role certificate contract (`Platform/common/contracts/role-certificate.md`).

### N1.2 Rights file

<!-- req: P/01.2-R007 -->
The file is a JSON object with the required field `roles` and the optional string `comment`. The full static form is `RightsFile` in N2.

See also: P/01.1-R018, P/01.2-R003, P/01.2-R008, P/01.2-R009, P/01.2-R029, P/01.2-R030, P/01.3-R007, P/01.3-R029.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R047 -->
`comment` is the generator's mark; it takes no part in granting rights.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R048 -->
The mark necessarily names that the file is generated, the deployment, the source manifests and «do not edit by hand»; for the same inputs its text is the same (P/02.5-01).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R049 -->
Each role has the required `group` (a non-empty string), `read` and `write` (arrays of strings); `delete` is an optional array of strings.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R050 -->
Unknown fields are rejected.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R051 -->
Role names are checked whole, including the end of the string.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R052 -->
The internal roles of the store are not in the file: the file does not name `admin`, `configstore`, `configstore-door`.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R008 -->
```yaml
configstore-rights.json:
  comment:  <generator mark>
  roles:
    <role>:
      group:  <socket group>
      read:   [<pattern>…]
      write:  [<pattern>…]
      delete: [<pattern>…]
pattern:
  exact:    "testsub/next_id"
  prefix:   "testsub/counters/*"
  deny:     "!domain/signer*"
  literal:  ["", "!", "!*", "p/!x", "a*b"]
```

See also: P/01.1-R018, P/01.2-R003, P/01.2-R007, P/01.2-R009, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R029, P/01.2-R030, P/01.3-R007, P/01.3-R029.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R053 -->
The generator always writes `delete` for every role: it equals `write`, unless the manifest separated them by `delete:`.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R054 -->
For the evaluator an absent `delete` is an empty list: no deletes (minimum of rights).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R055 -->
The pattern `exact` matches the key equal to it.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R056 -->
A `*` as the last character makes the pattern a prefix (`prefix`); a `*` not last is a literal character.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R057 -->
A `!` only as the first character makes the pattern a negation (`deny`); a negation wins everywhere in the list.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R058 -->
The patterns of `literal` are admissible in the file and match by the rules above: `""` — only the empty key, `"!"` — the negation of the empty key, `"!*"` — the negation of everything; a `!` not first and a `*` not last are literal characters (vector Platform/01-store/vectors/rights-evaluator).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R059 -->
If the file is out of form, then the whole file is refused.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R060 -->
The file is generated from the catalogue of manifests (`MANIFEST_DIR`) and the deployment name; it is not edited by hand.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R061 -->
The daemon loads the file at its start (`-rights`); there is no live reload.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R062 -->
Without the file only `admin.sock` opens.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R063 -->
The installer refuses to install a file not equal in meaning to the generated one (`--check`, P/02.5 N3 «install»).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R064 -->
The door shows the file only to admin.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R009 -->
The names of derived roles fit N1.1: the manifest name is limited to 32 characters
(P/02.1 N1.2), the suffix controller adds 10, worker — 6. The name is not truncated.

See also: P/01.1-R018, P/01.2-R003, P/01.2-R007, P/01.2-R008, P/01.2-R029, P/01.2-R030, P/01.3-R007, P/01.3-R029.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R065 -->
The chapter sets no limit on the deployment name: the group `<dep>-<s>worker` may be longer than 32 characters, the generator neither truncates it nor refuses; whether the OS creates it is up to the installer, P/02.4 «Implementation decides» item 1.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R066 -->
The comparison `--check` refers to one unchanging catalogue; publishing manifests during generation and installation is excluded in this chapter by the conditions of service, not detected automatically.

### N1.3 Evaluator

<!-- req: P/01.2-R010 -->
```yaml
allowed(role, action, key):
  action ∈ {read, write, delete}
  role not in the file                                   → no
  any !pattern of the role for action matched            → no
  any pattern of the role for action matched             → yes
  otherwise                                              → no
never:
  - {action: delete, key: "<x>/epoch/<y>"}
  - {action: delete, prefix: domain/, role: not domain_role}
  - {action: [write, delete], key: platform/epoch_floor}
domain_role: [domain, domainagent, <sub>domain]
```

See also: P/01.1-R019, P/01.1-R020, P/01.1-R024, P/01.1-R058, P/01.1-R066, P/01.1-R074, P/01.1-R075, P/01.1-R101, P/01.1-R102, P/01.1-R119, P/01.1-R131, P/01.1-R144, P/01.1-R146, P/01.2-R006, P/01.2-R008, P/01.2-R014, P/01.2-R017, P/01.2-R019, P/01.2-R020, P/01.2-R021, P/01.2-R022, P/01.2-R023, P/01.2-R024, P/01.2-R025, P/01.2-R026, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R006, P/01.3-R009, P/01.3-R011, P/01.3-R016, P/01.3-R021, P/01.3-R026, P/01.3-R027, P/01.3-R029, P/01.3-R032, P/01.3-R033, P/01.3-R035.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R067 -->
never holds for admin too.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R068 -->
The epoch row of never is `<x>/epoch/<y>` with exactly one segment `<y>` after «`<x>/epoch/`», an empty one included (`t/epoch/`), and x ≠ platform (the epoch row of P/03.2 `<sub>/epoch/<id>`).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R069 -->
A domain role is the name `domain`, `domainagent` or `<sub>domain` (`<sub>` — the grammar of the subsystem name of P/02.1, `[a-z][a-z0-9_-]{0,31}`); domain roles delete under `domain/` in their own families by the rights derivation contract of P/02.5 N1.4, N1.4a.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R070 -->
never checks under `domain/` only the domain role; «their own families» are set by the delete patterns of the rights file derived by the generator (P/02.5, the `--check` comparison), the store does not re-check them against the catalogue.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R071 -->
`platform/epoch_floor` is written and deleted only by the restore operation (P/01.3).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R072 -->
A listing gives the names and versions under a prefix to any role; it gives no values.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R073 -->
A write with a guard requires the read right to the guard row.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R074 -->
A guard does not extend rights.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R075 -->
A batch (P/01.1 N3 «write batch») is judged per row: write or delete of every row of writes, read of every guard, never — for admin too.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R076 -->
A refusal of one row of a batch is a refusal of the whole batch.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R077 -->
admin may do everything except never.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R078 -->
A peer (another daemon) has nothing of its own.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R079 -->
`configstore-door` has only its own `platform/configstore-doors/<id>` (P/01.3 N1.3; not `platform/doors/*` of resources).

## N2 Doors

<!-- req: P/01.2-R012 -->
The rights doors are the store doors of chapter 1.1, behind the role socket; request parsing is chapter 1.1 N2.1.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R080 -->
For `GET /v1/rights` the query is not read.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R013 -->
The HTTP contract: [01-store.api.openapi.yaml](01-store.api.openapi.yaml).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R014 -->
`GET /v1/rights` returns the loaded object `RightsFile` whole, without an envelope;
if it has a comment, the field is returned too.

See also: P/01.1-R066, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R015, P/01.2-R019, P/01.2-R026, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R016.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R081 -->
While no file is loaded, the route `/v1/rights` answers `404 badrequest`; an empty object `roles` does not stand in for the absence of the file.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R082 -->
Access to `/v1/rights` is checked by the identity admin, before the file is given out: the row `rc-rights_not_admin` of [`rights-checks.tsv`](../forms/rights-checks.tsv).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R083 -->
A successful `GET /v1/list` uses the `Listing` of chapter 1.1: without the values of rows, with versions and the order of names by UTF-8 bytes.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R084 -->
The presence of the path and the trust of the identity are checked for list too; the absence of the read right does not prevent listing.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R015 -->
The forms of all answers of the two routes are set in OpenAPI. The refusals `400` (`/v1/list` out of the form of chapter 1.1 N2.1), `403`, `404`, `503` are
exactly `{kind, error}` as strings, respectively `badrequest`, `forbidden`, `badrequest`, `unavailable`;
no `detail`/`fault` of the console.

See also: P/01.1-R144, P/01.2-R006, P/01.2-R014, P/01.2-R017, P/01.2-R026, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R006, P/01.3-R016, P/01.3-R033.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R085 -->
A body larger than the limit, whatever the method, is `413 toolarge` (`BodyTooLargeFault`, `body_limit` of chapter 1.1) by the frame of chapter 1.1 R034, before the identity: the row `rc-body_limit` of [`rights-checks.tsv`](../forms/rights-checks.tsv).

<!-- req: P/01.2-R086 -->
For a refusal of an operation on a row, `error` names the role, the action and the key; for a refusal of the identity itself or of `/v1/rights` — the role and the cause/route.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R087 -->
With a role header on a role socket, `error` names the role of the socket, not the role imposed by the header; parsing the key of the operation for diagnostics is not required.

## N3 Procedures

### check(role, action, key) — in every store before an action

<!-- req: P/01.2-R088 -->
The order of the checks of the identity and the rights of a request is the table [`rights-checks.tsv`](../forms/rights-checks.tsv): the rows in order, the first violated row gives the code, the kind and the fields of the refusal; none — the request goes on to its operation. The table reads the request object `{method, path, query, size, role_header}` and the role of the socket or of the transport; the steps below name the rows of the table and keep as prose what the table does not read.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R017 -->
1. Let role be the role from the socket (a cluster) or from the writer's name (a box). A role header on a role socket is the row `rc-role_header`:
   after the frame (`rc-body_limit`), before the body is parsed (a body out of form with such a header is also `403`).

See also: P/01.1-R044, P/01.1-R055, P/01.1-R058, P/01.1-R066, P/01.1-R074, P/01.1-R075, P/01.1-R088, P/01.1-R101, P/01.1-R119, P/01.1-R131, P/01.1-R144, P/01.2-R006, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R015, P/01.2-R018, P/01.2-R021, P/01.2-R022, P/01.2-R023, P/01.2-R024, P/01.2-R025, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R006, P/01.3-R016, P/01.3-R017, P/01.3-R033.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R018 -->
2. If the role is forwarded, first check the trust in the group daemon (the row `rc-role_header`) and the ban on a forwarded admin (step 9), before the other rows of the rights.

See also: P/01.1-R044, P/01.1-R055, P/01.1-R088, P/01.1-R131, P/01.1-R144, P/01.2-R017, P/01.2-R022, P/01.2-R024, P/01.2-R025, P/01.2-R026, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R006, P/01.3-R016, P/01.3-R017, P/01.3-R033.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R019 -->
3. If the operation is list, after the identity check return the names and versions without checking the read right: the table has no row of the read right for `/v1/list`.

See also: P/01.1-R066, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R014, P/01.2-R026.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R020 -->
4. never (N1.3) is a part of `allowed` in the rows of the rights; it refuses with `403 forbidden`.

See also: P/01.1-R101, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R021, P/01.2-R029.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R021 -->
5. For admin `allowed` permits the operation after never; for the internal roles — only by the exceptions of N1.3.

See also: P/01.1-R058, P/01.1-R066, P/01.1-R074, P/01.1-R075, P/01.1-R101, P/01.1-R119, P/01.2-R010, P/01.2-R017, P/01.2-R020, P/01.2-R022, P/01.2-R023, P/01.2-R024, P/01.2-R025, P/01.2-R029, P/01.3-R006, P/01.3-R016, P/01.3-R033.

<!-- req: P/01.2-R022 -->
6. For the other roles `allowed(role, action, key)` of N1.3 decides; a refusal is `403 forbidden`, before the record to the log. The read of `/v1/get` is the row `rc-read_forbidden`;
   a write and a delete are the rows `wc-rights` and `wc-write_rights` of [`write-checks.tsv`](../forms/write-checks.tsv).
