## Cores and machines

[The requirements of the course to code](../common/acceptance/PROFILE.md#requirements-of-the-course-to-code) apply to the names in [`forms/code-rules.tsv`](forms/code-rules.tsv):

- cores: `write_model` (the checks of the write command), `jcs`, `store_core` (admission, `cas`, `guard`, `id` memory, journal), `rights_core`, `copy_core`;
- machines: `admission_step` and `journal_step` in `store_core`, `copy_step` in `copy_core`.
