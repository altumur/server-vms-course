# P/09.2 — Relay mode: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 9.2 of lesson P/09 «The agent» (the agent; ADR-0080; module «Platform» — ADR-0083). Lecture (`ru/09-agent.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](09-agent.api.openapi.yaml) (the tag `relay`), [instructions](09-agent.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),
[acceptance](../acceptance/2/README.md). Here — the relay mode of the agent (`RELAY=1`): the same process `domainagent`, the
same role and the same member key. A relay is a member of the domain, not the author of what is foreign and not more than one hop between a member and
the holder; its door for the members behind it (`POST /api/member/<member>`, `GET /api/carry/<member>`, `POST /api/tokens/<member>/…`)
answers from memory with `domain_age`; the batch `relayed` to the holder with the knocks of strangers, `BATCH_LIMIT`, `KNOCKS`; the
relay pass and a break; a token through a relay byte for byte; a named limit — the member's signature is not carried through a
relay; the server side of the relay door is the class `signed`.

The member side behind a relay is [9.1](09.1-agent.norms.md); the holder, topology and two roads are [P/08.3](../../08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md).

The common algorithms are the contracts
of [report reception](../../common/contracts/accept.md) (owner P/08.3; this chapter is a participant), [the agent
signature](../../common/contracts/agent-signature.md) (owner — chapter 9.1; this chapter checks), [the domain
envelopes](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) (this chapter carries the envelope `m1` without opening it), [the domain key
set](../../common/contracts/keys.md) (this chapter reads `keys` of the batch answer) and [forwarding](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md)

### N1.1 The relay

```yaml
relay:        a member of the domain through whose agent other members report to the holder — those that the holder does not
              reach (a camera in the office network); admitted as any member (P/08.3), with its own key and its own report
door:         the relay's agent opens a door for the members behind it: RELAY=1 (exactly «1» — there is a door; the variable is absent or
              empty — there is no door; another value — the agent does not start, exit code 2 — a refusal of the setting, P/02.4 N1.1
              common; the Architect's decision, analysis of review of stage B 8–10, № 17, ADR-0083 addendum (15)), RELAY_HOST (default 0.0.0.0),
              RELAY_PORT (default 8446); the door is opened only by the active agent of the relay cluster (P/09.1 N1.13: an agent on
              every server, one is active by the turn domain/agent-turn; a standby one does not open the door); a member behind a relay
              gets RELAY_URL instead of DOMAIN_URL — the addresses of the servers of the relay cluster with RELAY=1 separated by commas, in
              order: a connection refusal or 503 standby — the next one; RELAY_MEMBER — one, the name of the relay member
              (DOM-AGENT-TURN-01, the owner's consent of 2026-10-26)
not_author:   the relay is not the author of what is foreign: it carries the members' reports to the holder and the holder's answers to the members, the secret part of the answer
              (the envelope m1, P/08.3 N1.5) it cannot open; it carries the token asks byte for byte (P/08.7)
one_hop:      between a member and the holder — at most one relay; the holder is nobody's relay
```

### N1.4 The relay batch

```yaml
batch:         the body of POST /api/relay/<relay> — a JSON object {relayed, knocks?, relay_rev?}, the signature of the relay's agent (the agent signature contract)
  relayed:     {<member>: <a publication of chapter 9.1 (M11/3 N1.2)>} — a whole or a change against the rev that the holder gave this road
  knocks?:     {<name>: {publication: <the text of the body>, signature: <X-Agent-Signature as it came>}} — the knocks of strangers
               at the relay door; the holder checks the signature by the key that the body presented (chapter 9.1, M11/3 N1.2 key), over
               POST /api/member/<name> and the body; a valid one — a knock (P/08.3 N1.1 knock), an invalid one or a member, except an entry without key with revoked_keys (P/08.3 N3 «admit a member» step 1), — a skip
answer:        {relay_for, relayed, refused, keys, collecting?}
  collecting:  true while the gathering «from nothing» goes on (P/08.8 N1.8a); the relay keeps the word until an answer without it: the door answers a stranger with a valid signature 403 with send_row: true, a body with row goes as a knock; otherwise a member behind the relay would not return the row and the gathering would forget it silently
  relay_for:   [<member>…] — whom the topology leads through this relay, by name
  relayed:     {<member>: <carry P/08.3 N1.5>} — the holder's answer to the members with a body in this batch (ADR-0113 addendum (3) item 5); rev — for the road of this relay
  relay_rev:   rev of the row domain/relay-members/<relay>; relay_for and keys go only if relay_rev of the batch lags behind
  refused:     {<member>: <reason> | {reason: busy, retry_in}} — what of relayed of the batch is not accepted (the topology, the form, 409 of the report reception contract); {reason: send_row} — the gathering goes on, and the member is not in the list or has another key: the door answers it 403 with send_row: true; knocks — not here
  keys:        {<member>: <hex of the key>} — the accepted keys of the members of relay_for; a revoked one — an empty string
BATCH_LIMIT, KNOCKS: the report reception contract «The relay batch: limits» (moved there without a change; C08-G05, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25)
hold:          the relay door holds the POST of an accepted member until the answer of its batch, not longer than a pass plus FORWARD_WAIT: the member reads its own answer, not the former one; it did not make it — an answer from memory; there is no answer to this member in memory — 503 «the relay has nothing from the domain for <member> yet»: N2, N3 — the second case of this text, an accepted member with a new body before its first answer
rows:          GET /api/members/<member>/<part>: forwarding to the holder with the signature as it came, the row is signed and is put into memory; the holder did not answer — the row from memory; there is none — the same 503
domain_age:    age of this particular cached carry: monotonic seconds since that carry was received from the holder; a new carry for another member or an empty batch does not reset it
```

**Cached-carry origin (P09 review C1).** Store each cached carry together with its own receipt origin. Every response of that cached carry computes `domain_age` from that origin, including GET and the POST hold fallback. A successful empty batch, a carry for another member, retries and `heard` do not refresh it. A new carry of the same member replaces both its content and origin, even if its content compares equal; it is a genuinely new confirmed holder response. The relay cache, origins and `heard` are process memory: after restart there is no cached carry or inherited age until a new holder response supplies it. The receiving member retains its existing rule `as_of = receipt - domain_age`, with its freshness/write throttles; an old cache cannot renew expired grants. Global `heard` remains a separate liveness observation, never the origin of all members' carries.

The row `domain/reached` (an object at the member: the moment of the answer minus `domain_age`) is chapter 9.1 (M11/4 N1.4; P/09.1-05). The schemas
of the batch and of the answer are `Batch`, `RelayAnswer` of the [OpenAPI of the process `domain`](../../08-domain/spec/08-domain.api.openapi.yaml);
the holder on the batch is [P/08.3](../../08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md) N3 «the holder on `POST /api/relay/<relay>`».

The six forwarded GET success forms and parameters are the existing owner references in [Forwarded owner forms](../forms/README.md#forwarded-owner-forms). `BackupPartRevision` remains a required query `rev` on GET `/api/backup-part/{member}/{part}` as at the owner; no revision or default is inferred.

| POST `/api/tokens/<member>/…` | the relay door | [P/08.7](../../08-domain/spec/08.7-domain.norms.md) | P/08.7; the holder did not answer — 502 `did not answer`, as forwarding ([forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md) «Forwarding is one function» `answer`; the Architect's decision on the reconciliation of M11, Y8) |

The body of a refusal of the relay door is `{error: "refused", detail, reason?, send_row?}` (the schema `RelayRefusal` of the lesson contract, one closed branch per status): normative are
the status and `reason`, `detail` is required and is the words of the implementation, except two texts, 403 «… does not reach the domain through this relay …» and
503 «the relay has nothing from the domain for \<member> yet», which the norm names. `reason` is, for the outcomes of «accept», a word
of the report reception contract (`not_a_publication`, `same_sent_at`, `rebase`, `not_ready`, `whole_too_large`, `rev_exhausted`), for
401 — a word of the agent signature contract; for the early 400 of a chunked body and the early 413 of the body (both before «accept»), for 405
and for both 403 there is none. A wrong method on these paths — 405; the door
does not set the header `Retry-After` (the reference of the witnesses and the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23, items 1, 4–6).

`same_sent_at` at the relay door is by the last write of its mark of the member: the mark gets the hash of the body (`request_sha256`) at
every write — both of a generation and of `heard` (the report reception contract, steps 2 and 6); an exact repeat of the last body by the same road is
`same`, the answer is a copy (item 3).

The relay door accepts a member by the same function «accept» ([report reception contract](../../common/contracts/accept.md);
owner P/08.3) over its own memory: the relay has its own whole copy of each member and its own `rev` for its deltas. The holder
sees only what the relay raised by a batch.

The token door returns the holder's codes (a hop of the holder) as they came: they are carried by the OpenAPI of the process `domain` (the operation
`holderToken`), the OpenAPI of the lesson carries only the own codes of the relay door (analysis of review of stage A 8–10, decision 3).

## N3 Procedures

<!-- req: P/09.2-R008 -->
### the relay pass (in every pass of its agent, after its own report)

```
1. take from memory the publications of the members accepted since the former pass (per member — the last one) and the knocks
2. for each member: the holder's rev for this road is known (up[member]) → a change against it (chapter 9.1, M11/3 N1.2 change),
   otherwise a whole; the base of the delta is the one confirmed by this road (chapter 9.1, M11/3 N1.2 change); all the fields of the publication outside the delta (`stale`,
   `key`, `keys_rev`, `pinned` and the fields of later lessons: `outcomes`, `tls_ca`, `tls_ca_certs`, `members_rev`, `sign_failures`, any
   following ones) — from the last publication of the member as they are: a rule, not a list
3. split into batches of at most BATCH_LIMIT in the order of names; the knocks — in the first one
4. POST /api/relay/<me> each batch with relay_rev; an answer → up[member] = relayed[member].rev for the accepted ones; refused with reason: busy — the publication stays in memory, up is not forgotten, a repeat after retry_in, a new arrival replaces it; forget up[member] for the other refused and
   unanswered ones (next time — a whole); relay_for, keys — replace, if they came, relay_for at a change — the row domain/relay-for {rev, members}: the relay's workers see whom it leads; the answers of the members — into memory (members not from relay_for — forget);
   heard = now for relay-to-holder liveness only; replace a member cached carry and its monotonic origin only when this answer actually contains a new carry for that member
5. no answer → return the publications not taken into memory, if nothing newer has come since; the knocks — drop; do not change heard;
   an answer of the holder larger than BATCH_LIMIT — as «no answer», a journal entry without the value (C09-ANSWER-LIMIT-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29)
```

The relay pass goes even if its own report to the holder did not pass (chapter 9.1 N3 «the agent pass» step 5;
M11/3 N3). A batch goes out every pass, an empty one too (`{relayed: {}}`): this is how a relay without members in memory learns
`relay_for` and `keys`. While `relay_for` does not name a member, its report at the relay door is the report of a stranger: a valid
signature by its own key — a knock in memory and 403 (N2), an invalid one — 401. Before the first answer of the holder the relay answers a member
503 (N2), and the member does not write `domain/reached`; to a stranger before the first answer — 503 too, without a knock (the Architect's decision on
the reconciliation of M11, `Y2-a`). The first answer is the first batch of the holder, an empty one too: after it a 503 «no answer» to a stranger does not happen, a stranger gets
the signature, the knock and 403 (C09-RELAY-FIRST-ANSWER-01); to an accepted member POST — the case `hold` of N1.4, and GET carry — the separate missing-cache case of N2 after the member/key/signature checks (P09 review M1).

The holder on the batch is [P/08.3](../../08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md) N3 «the holder on `POST /api/relay/<relay>`»;
the member's agent behind a relay is chapter 9.1 (M11/4 N3 «the member's agent behind a relay»).

**Races.** The relay writes its memory by one process; a late answer of the holder to an old batch does not overwrite a newer
publication of the member (step 5). A member moved by the topology to another relay stops getting the answers of the former one at its
next pass (`relay_for`). Two roads of one member — [P/08.3](../../08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md) N1.3a, N3.
