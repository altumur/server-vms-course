# P/09.2 — Relay mode: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand.

The canon of chapter 9.2 of lesson P/09 «The agent» (the agent; module «Platform»). Lecture (`ru/09-agent.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](09-agent.api.openapi.yaml) (the tag `relay`), [instructions](09-agent.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),
[acceptance](../acceptance/2/README.md). Here — the relay mode of the agent (`RELAY=1`): the same process `domainagent`, the
same role and the same member key. A relay is a member of the domain, not the author of what is foreign and not more than one hop between a member and
the holder; its door for the members behind it (`POST /api/member/<member>`, `GET /api/carry/<member>`, `POST /api/tokens/<member>/…`)
answers from memory with `domain_age`; the batch `relayed` to the holder with the knocks of strangers, `BATCH_LIMIT`, `KNOCKS`; the
relay pass and a break; a token through a relay byte for byte; a named limit — the member's signature is not carried through a
relay; the server side of the relay door is the class `signed`.

The member side behind a relay is [9.1](09.1-agent.norms.md); the holder, topology and two roads are [P/08.3](../../08-domain/spec/08.3-domain.norms.md).

The common algorithms are the contracts
of [report reception](../../common/contracts/accept.md) (owner P/08.3; this chapter is a participant), [the agent
signature](../../common/contracts/agent-signature.md) (owner — chapter 9.1; this chapter checks), [the domain
envelopes](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) (this chapter carries the envelope `m1` without opening it), [the domain key
set](../../common/contracts/keys.md) (this chapter reads `keys` of the batch answer) and [forwarding](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md)
(502 `did not answer` of the token door).

### N1.1 The relay

```yaml
relay:        a member of the domain (rules below)
door:         RELAY, RELAY_HOST, RELAY_PORT; RELAY_URL, RELAY_MEMBER (rules below)
not_author:   (rules below)
one_hop:      (rules below)
```

<!-- req: P/09.2-R029 -->
`relay` — a relay is a member of the domain through whose agent other members report to the holder — those that the holder does not reach (a camera in the office network). `relay` — a relay is admitted as any member (P/08.3), with its own key and its own report.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R031 -->
`door` — the relay's agent opens a door for the members behind it: with `RELAY=1` (exactly «1») there is a door. `door` — the variable `RELAY` is absent or empty — there is no door. `door` — `RELAY` has another value — the agent does not start, exit code 2 — a refusal of the setting, P/02.4 N1.1 common. `door` — the relay's agent opens the door with `RELAY_HOST` (default 0.0.0.0) and `RELAY_PORT` (default 8446).

<!-- req: P/09.2-R035 -->
`door` — the door is opened only by the active agent of the relay cluster (P/09.1 N1.13: an agent on
every server, one is active by the turn `domain/agent-turn`; a standby one does not open the door).

<!-- req: P/09.2-R036 -->
`door` — a member behind a relay gets `RELAY_URL` instead of `DOMAIN_URL` — the addresses of the servers of the relay cluster with `RELAY=1` separated by commas. `door` — a member behind a relay uses the addresses of `RELAY_URL` in order: a connection refusal or 503 standby — the next one. `door` — a member behind a relay gets `RELAY_MEMBER` — one, the name of the relay member.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R039 -->
`not_author` — the relay is not the author of what is foreign: it carries the members' reports to the holder and the holder's answers to the members. `not_author` — the secret part of the holder's answer (the envelope `m1`, P/08.3 N1.5) the relay cannot open.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R041 -->
`not_author` — the relay carries the token asks byte for byte (P/08.7).

<!-- req: P/09.2-R042 -->
`one_hop` — between a member and the holder there is at most one relay. `one_hop` — the holder is nobody's relay.

### N1.4 The relay batch

```yaml
batch:         the body of POST /api/relay/<relay> — a JSON object {relayed, knocks?, relay_rev?} (rules below)
  relayed:     {<member>: <a publication of chapter 9.1 N1.2>} (rules below)
  knocks?:     {<name>: {publication: <the text of the body>, signature: <X-Agent-Signature as it came>}} (rules below)
answer:        {relay_for, relayed, refused, keys, collecting?}
  collecting:  true (rules below)
  relay_for:   [<member>…] (rules below)
  relayed:     {<member>: <carry P/08.3 N1.5>} (rules below)
  relay_rev:   rev of the row domain/relay-members/<relay> (rules below)
  refused:     {<member>: <reason> | {reason: busy, retry_in}} (rules below)
  keys:        {<member>: <hex of the key>} (rules below)
BATCH_LIMIT, KNOCKS: the report reception contract «The relay batch: limits»
hold:          (rules below)
rows:          GET /api/members/<member>/<part> (rules below)
domain_age:    (rules below)
```

<!-- req: P/09.2-R046 -->
`batch` — the batch carries the signature of the relay's agent (the agent signature contract).

<!-- req: P/09.2-R047 -->
`relayed` of the batch — the publication of a member is a whole or a change against the rev that the holder gave this road.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R048 -->
`knocks` of the batch are the knocks of strangers at the relay door.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R050 -->
`knocks` — the holder checks the signature of a knock by the key that the body presented (chapter 9.1 N1.2 key), over `POST /api/member/<name>` and the body. `knocks` — a knock with a valid signature is a knock (P/08.3 N1.1 knock). `knocks` — a knock with an invalid signature or of a member, except an entry without key with revoked_keys (P/08.3 N3 «admit a member» step 1), is a skip.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R052 -->
`collecting` of the answer is true while the gathering «from nothing» goes on (P/08.8 N1.8a).

<!-- req: P/09.2-R053 -->
`collecting` — the relay keeps the word `collecting` until an answer without it; otherwise a member behind the relay would not return the row and the gathering would forget it silently. `collecting` — while the relay keeps the word `collecting`, the relay door answers a stranger with a valid signature 403 with `send_row: true`. `collecting` — while the relay keeps the word `collecting`, a body with `row` goes as a knock.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R056 -->
`relay_for` of the answer is whom the topology leads through this relay, by name.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R057 -->
`relayed` of the answer is the holder's answer to the members with a body in this batch. `relayed` of the answer — `rev` is for the road of this relay.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R059 -->
`relay_rev` — `relay_for` and `keys` go in the answer only if `relay_rev` of the batch lags behind.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R060 -->
`refused` of the answer is what of `relayed` of the batch is not accepted (the topology, the form, 409 of the report reception contract). `refused` — `{reason: send_row}` — the gathering goes on, and the member is not in the list or has another key. `refused` — on `{reason: send_row}` the relay door answers the member 403 with `send_row: true`. `refused` — knocks are not here, in `refused`.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R064 -->
`keys` of the answer are the accepted keys of the members of `relay_for`. `keys` — a revoked key of a member of `relay_for` is an empty string.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R066 -->
`hold` — the relay door holds the POST of an accepted member until the answer of its batch, not longer than a pass plus FORWARD_WAIT: the member reads its own answer, not the former one. `hold` — the answer of the batch did not make it within a pass plus FORWARD_WAIT — an answer from memory to the POST of the accepted member. `hold` — the answer of the batch did not make it and there is no answer to this member in memory — 503 «`the relay has nothing from the domain for <member> yet`»: N2, N3 — the second case of this text, an accepted member with a new body before its first answer.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R069 -->
`rows` — `GET /api/members/<member>/<part>` goes by forwarding to the holder with the signature as it came. `rows` — the row of the holder's answer to `GET /api/members/<member>/<part>` is signed and is put into memory. `rows` — the holder did not answer `GET /api/members/<member>/<part>` — the row from memory. `rows` — the holder did not answer and there is no row in memory — the same 503 as in `hold`.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R073 -->
`domain_age` is the age of this particular cached carry: monotonic seconds since that carry was received from the holder. `domain_age` — a new carry for another member or an empty batch does not reset the `domain_age` of a cached carry.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R075 -->
**Cached-carry origin.** Store each cached carry together with its own receipt origin. Every response of a cached carry computes `domain_age` from the origin of that cached carry, including GET and the POST hold fallback. A successful empty batch, a carry for another member, retries and `heard` do not refresh the origin of a cached carry. A new carry of the same member replaces both the content and the origin of its cached carry, even if its content compares equal; it is a genuinely new confirmed holder response.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R079 -->
The relay cache, origins and `heard` are process memory: after restart there is no cached carry or inherited age until a new holder response supplies it.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R080 -->
The receiving member retains its existing rule `as_of = receipt - domain_age`, with its freshness/write throttles. An old cache cannot renew expired grants.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R082 -->
Global `heard` remains a separate liveness observation, never the origin of all members' carries.

<!-- req: P/09.2-R083 -->
The row `domain/reached` (an object at the member: the moment of the answer minus `domain_age`) is chapter 9.1 (N1.7; P/09.1-05: P/09.1-R429, P/09.1-R430, P/09.1-R432, P/09.1-R435, P/09.1-R437, P/09.1-R441, P/09.1-R443, P/09.1-R677).
