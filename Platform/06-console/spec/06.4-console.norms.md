(the chapter is the owner of the contract).

contract](../../common/contracts/event-journal.md), its owner is [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md); the doors
of the resource that the console goes to at the merge are P/05 N2. The holder's side (the mark `begun` and the outcome, the cleanup of the mark, the append)
and the suppression of repeats are [chapter 3.6](../../03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md); the worker heartbeat and `fetched` are
[chapter 3.4](../../03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) N1.1. The keys `requests.*` in the loader are [P/02.1](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md)
(the form and the load refusals). The action of a request is N1.5 `action`; the freshness bound of a live resource of the merge is `≤ LOST_AFTER` by the [observation contract](../../common/contracts/observation.md), N3 «merge».

Step 1, deadline. valid_until is written by the time writer of P/01.1 N0: truncation down to milliseconds (towards −∞), a negative one — 0 (1000.1234 → 1000.123, 1e-12 → 0, -1 → 0). Step 1, deadline. A valid_until of 0 and the past are accepted, «as is» means the deadline is not shifted forward, not the bytes (the deadline has passed; the digest does not include the deadline).

### reaper (the console): every CLEAR_EVERY — remove the answered; every SWEEP_EVERY — close the abandoned

## Implementation decides

The implementation names the following choices and fixed boundaries in its description:

1. **the free CAS retry limit of request accounting**. Its exact permitted value is not judged; exhaustion keeps the existing unavailable outcome, re-reads and preservation of a reservation that a concurrent repeat may have used. `FETCHED_COUNT`/`FETCHED_BYTES` and `MARK_SWEEP` belong to the [request mark contract](../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) «fetched» `limits`; their values belong to [chapter 3.6](../../03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md) «Implementation decides» item 1, not to another console setting.
2. **merge observations** are not a free deadline or lane choice: the resource answer deadline is 3 s and there are 8 lanes by the [event journal merge contract](../../common/contracts/events-merge.md). An unanswered live resource is `incomplete`; its late answer does not change that response. N3 «merge» determines live resources by their door rows. The internal scheduling is not judged if these outcomes remain unchanged.
3. **the permitted POST body limit and applicable declared budgets** belong to [chapter 6.3](06.3-console.norms.md) N1.3 `body`. A free limit's exact permitted value is not judged; the general body limit remains ≥ 1 MiB, and framing/slow/excess keep 400/408/413 with their full refusal forms. BODY_WAIT and its header-based start are not a blanket deadline for every response. Streams and mirror reception belong to [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md) «Implementation decides» item 6.

P/05 «Implementation decides» item 3 refers here for the fixed merge lanes and deadline.
