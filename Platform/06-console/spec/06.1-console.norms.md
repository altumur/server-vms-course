## Н1 Form

<!-- req: P/06.1-R002 -->

**Time.** The time fields of the rows and objects of the chapter are time by [the canon of Н0 of P/01.1](../../01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#time-in-a-row): `<sub>/sweep` — `at`; the stamps of table rows — `at`, `made_at`; the tombstone `.gone/<ключ>` — `written` ([P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md) Н1.1 «Tombstones»). The unit row, the counter `next_id` and the claim `idem` have no row-time fields (the time of a claim is a form of the implementation, «Implementation decides» item 3; the slot and the epoch floor — [P/03.2 Н1](../../03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md) «Time»).

**Data** ([catalogue](../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):
- owns: `<sub>/<manifest:unit.rows>/<id>`, `<sub>/next_id`, `<sub>/idem/<key>`, `<sub>/blobs/sha256-<hex>`, `<sub>/sweep`, `<sub>/<manifest:tables>/<имя>`;
- writes: `.gone/<ключ>` (put), `<корень событий>/audit/<роль>/e1/<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ>.events.jsonl` (append), `<корень объектов>/<каталог>/~lock` (create);
- reads: `platform/manifest/<sub>`, `platform/manifest/<sub>/rev`, `<sub>/placement/<id>`, `<sub>/heartbeats/<имя>`, `<sub>/holds/<место>`, `<sub>/state/<имя>/<id>`.

The `audit` journal of the chapter — the kinds `unit.*`, `<t>.written`, `<t>.deleted`, `next_id.recovered`; `<sub>/holds/<место>` is read for `held_by`
of the places table (review of stage B 6–7, № 41). The batch door (Н2 «Batch door») writes the same rows of the same families.

The controller deletes a unit row by `placement.unplaced.delete_after`, `placement.retire_when.delete_after` and
`placement.ends` ([P/04](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md) Н1.3), the worker — by `worker.deletes` (participants `delete`);
it is also created and prolonged by the customer — a worker of another subsystem by its `worker.creates`, only rows of its own `made`
([P/03.6](../../03-worker/spec/03.6-worker.norms.md) Н1.6): an order row has two writers — the operator through the door, the customer through
the library (decision ADR-0091 item 1, an exception from «one writer» below). The unit state `<sub>/state/<имя>/<id>` is written by
the holder of the unit (P/03.6 Н1.7) and deleted by the controller (P/04 Н3); the console reads it for the representation of the unit (Н2);
a table from `worker.writes` is written by its worker (participant `cas`, [P/03.4](../../03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md) Н1.6);
the body copy is put and removed by the resource (participant `put`, `delete`, P/05 Н1.1 «Bodies at the resource»). The body tombstone `.gone/<ключ>`
is an object row of the resource (owner P/05; review of stage A 6–7, № 17): the console writes it (`put`) at the completion of the sweep
window (Н3 «body sweep»). The heartbeats `<sub>/heartbeats/<имя>` the console reads for `rows` in `GET /{rows}` (Н2; form —
P/03.4 Н1.1).

### Н1.1 Key layout of a subsystem

<!-- req: P/06.1-R003 -->

`unit`, `unit.rows` and `unit.id` are mandatory without defaults (P/02.1 Н1.2). `unit.rows` is a name by the grammar and not from the list of forbidden names of the summary table P/02.1 Н1.2а (the service families
of this layout, the first segments of the platform doors, the object families, and also cluster, worker, ts from the shell of the
snapshot [P/04 Н1.3а](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md)). An unrepresentable name is a load refusal, not an unreachable door.

All keys of a subsystem begin with its name (P/02.1). Each row has one writer — except the order row: it is written by the operator through the door and by the customer through the library (P/03.6 Н1.6; decision ADR-0091 item 1); a family may have several when the owner lesson divides it by the form of the row (requests — the row `<sub>/requests/<rid>` of [chapter 6.4](06.4-console.norms.md) Н1.5, writers by `worker.requests`; tables — `worker.writes` Н1.6); rights — the roles and patterns of P/02.5 Н1.4 (`console`, `<s>controller`, `<s>worker`).

### Н1.1а Writing only by a ticket: `writes_via`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R004 -->

ADR-0119 addendum (10), (11). The manifest word `unit.writes_via: [<sub>…]` and `tables.<t>.writes_via` (form — P/02.1): the rows are written
only by the named subsystems, by a ticket. So the holder of the part `exp` gets rows only from `backend`, which judges the oversight, and
a field of the part that a session would forge, a session cannot write (ADR-0119 addendum (10)).

```yaml
writes_via:
  covers:   unit.writes_via — the rows of a unit <s>/<rows>/<id>: create, edit, delete (Н3), the doors of bodies and bytes (Н2 «Doors of bodies and
            tables»), the row <s>/next_id (written only together with a creation, Н2 «Batch door» «Counter») and these rows in a batch;
            tables.<t>.writes_via — POST and DELETE of the table <t> and its rows in a batch. The word of the unit does not close tables, the word of a table does not close
            the unit (the Specification's decision, a refinement of ADR-0119 addendum (10))
  pass:     a callback by a ticket (the gate and pass contract, «Pass of the second kind» `ticket`) whose addressee's sub is in the list, and whose
            transport is the role of this addressee; a door.pass ticket of both kinds. The right of the ticket's person is judged as before (need and may
            of the door): the word narrows the path, it does not replace the rights
  refuse:   a person's session, the emergency entry (break-glass), a debug session and a ticket of another subsystem — 403 {"error": "writes via"},
            without field (field — only at 400, 409, 412, Н2 «Refusal»); detail names the subsystems of the list; in a batch — a refusal of the whole
            batch, nothing written, detail names the number of the row; journal — access.denied {sub, reason: "writes via"} (the gate
            contract «Subsystem of the check»)
  order:    the gate, right after parsing the key (subsystem, unit or table), before need and may and before the body: a person without rights also
            gets writes via — the manifest is public, there is no leak; for a batch — by step 3 of «batch» on each row of writes
  outside:  a write by the right of consequences: the holder by the leaf of its role (state, worker.deletes, the table worker.writes), the controller
            (placement.unplaced.delete_after, retire_when.delete_after, ends), the customer by worker.creates, the cascades of the console itself
            (removal from folders on deletion, tombstones, the delete_after reaper) — the word does not judge (ADR-0119 addendum (11) item 1)
  same:     reading, the change feed, the guards of a batch, the rows of folders and membership (chapter 6.8) and requests (chapter 6.4: cancel, delete — not
            a write of rows) — as before
  catalog:  a subsystem of the list outside the catalogue is not a load refusal and not a start refusal: while it is absent, nobody writes the rows for people, and the order
            of installation is free; the loader judges only the form (P/02.1; the Specification's decision)
```

### Н1.3 Identifier

<!-- req: P/06.1-R006 -->

```yaml
id:
  numeric:                                  # unit.id: numeric
    rule:    a decimal integer ≥ 1 from the counter <sub>/next_id: n — the last issued number, the next — n + 1 (review of stage B 6–7,
             № 38); no row — recover below and the creation cas "" with n = the number being issued: without units and claims it is 1, with already
             existing units — the greatest + 1, not 1 (issuing 1 — a collision with a live unit; (the Architect's decision of 2026-10-20, review of stage B 6–7)); the first — 1; a number is never used again,
             in the path — a decimal notation without a sign, spaces and leading zeros (`01`, `+1` — 400 `badkey`);

The counter does not read or there is no row → the greatest number among the rows <rows>/ (with tombstones) and the numbers in live claims + 1 — the issued
             number; the counter is rewritten whole with n = this number (the last issued, № 38); the numbers of refused attempts leave no traces and after a recovery MAY come out again —
             the only exception from «never», journal `next_id.recovered {from, to}`
  named:                                    # unit.id: <поле> — [the row check contract](../../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Identifier and field constraints»
  made_prefix:                              # ADR-0091 item 1
    rule:    a creation through the door (POST) of an id that begins with «<заказчик>-» of the subsystem that declared worker.creates to this
             table (the manifest catalogue, P/02.1 Н3), — 400 «names starting with "<заказчик>-" are made by <заказчик>
             (worker.creates)», also over a tombstone: under this prefix live only the customer's rows; a row with such a
             name, created before the customer was declared, stays and is taken — without its own made the customer does not write it
  made_key:                                 # ADR-0091 item 1
    rule:    in a row with made the fields that the customer's key names (worker.creates[].key, P/03.6 Н1.6) are immutable also
             for the door, like fixed: an edit with another value — 400 «<поле> is fixed»; the other fields the operator edits through the door
# tombstone, unique, canonical_url, ref_and_must_match — [the row check contract](../../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Identifier and field constraints»
```

The recovery profile numeric is not compatible with physical cleanup: unit.id: numeric together with placement.unplaced.delete_after is a load refusal of P/02.1. Successful numbers are represented by saved rows/tombstones; the cleanup of done claims does not cancel that. An arbitrary loss of these rows is not covered by the recover formula.

Any name that the grammar of the id and of the slot and the full key limit of P/01.1 permit is stored by any implementation: file names
(bucket directories — the event journal contract «File names in shared trees») the implementation encodes itself; there is no narrower file profile — otherwise parts of different
implementations over the same data are incompatible (reconciliation of python and go М10/3–5, U20).

**Term of a done claim.** The console MUST keep a completed saved answer of a claim with an HTTP code <500 for at least
86400 seconds from the moment it was saved. Cleanup uses a local monotonic age;
after the memory is recovered a new observer counts it as fresh and does not trust foreign wall clocks.
This does not extend done to 5xx or pending: their fate stays by Н3.

### Н1.5 Bodies

<!-- req: P/06.1-R008 -->

A blob is declared only in unit.fields; a blob in a table is outside the profile and is rejected by the loader.
The name of a blob field is 1–64 ASCII: the first A-Z/a-z/_, the rest A-Z/a-z/0-9/_.
An empty name, a path separator, a dot, a percent, a space, a control or non-ASCII character
do not represent the blob door of this lesson: a load refusal `refused:value` with the path of the field.
Other fields are not narrowed automatically by this restriction.

A manifest with any blob field of a unit does not declare unit.fields.bytes: a load refusal
`refused:value` by P/02.1. Without blob the field bytes is permitted. This restriction keeps
the full Unit in the answer of the upload; a renaming of the counter or a new shell is not introduced.

```yaml
field:    type: blob; in the row — "sha256-<64 hex>"; bytes in the body of the row → 400 «takes a digest, not the bytes (N of them):
          PUT the bytes to /<rows>/<id>/<поле> and the row gets the digest back»; in snapshot — the manifest does not load
object:   <sub>/blobs/sha256-<hex>; the name — the digest of the bytes; the reader verifies (BlobMismatch — «the object was replaced»)
write:    the object durably (fsync) → the row with the digest (revision + 1) → journal unit.changed {fields, revision, digest}
mirror:   the body copy to a neighbour and its door — P/05 Н1.1 «Bodies at the resource» (stage B; the decision on В2 of the move)
delete:   the tombstone and the convergence of body copies — the tombstones contract common/contracts/tombstone.md (owner P/05; C06-BLOB-GC-33-01)
sweep:    <sub>/sweep {at, digests}  — writer the console; once in 60 s
          # the bytes of the row: at — the wall-clock seconds of the first mark of the batch, text by values_by_type Н1.2 (float);
          # digests — a list of "sha256-<hex>" by the rule list Н1.2 (comma-separated); an empty batch — digests: ""
          # or no row, at then is not read; example: {at: "1760700000.5", digests: "sha256-ab…,sha256-cd…"}
  mark:   only with an empty previous batch, the digests without references from rows (≤ SWEEP_LIMIT 64) → write with cas; do not refresh a non-empty one; delete nothing;
          a reference — a blob field of the rows of its own subsystem; a row of another subsystem (its string field with <sub>/blobs/<digest>) does not hold the body:
          whoever needs the body longer copies it into its own blob field (C06-SWEEP-FOREIGN-REF-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29, review V/04)
  wait:   SWEEP_GRACE 300 s
  sweep:  deletion only in the exceptional maintenance window of Н3; an ordinary background pass only marks/waits.
          After the writers are stopped, check the references again; remove the list with cas (a conflict — delete nothing); delete the objects.
          put_blob removes the digest with cas; this cancellation is a help to the marks, not a protection against a parallel deletion
  metrics: <sub>_blobs_total, <sub>_blobs_marked — at manifests with a blob field
```

The body copy at the resource (`mirror`) — [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md) Н1.1 «Bodies at the resource» (stage B); tombstones (`delete`) — [the tombstones contract](../../common/contracts/tombstone.md) (C06-BLOB-GC-33-01).

### Refusal: `error` and `field`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R011 -->

The refusal of the doors of chapters 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 6.5, 6.6 is `{detail, error, fault?, field?}` (ADR-0094 B5, addendum (13)); there is no second form. The client chooses an action by the word and
puts the error at a field by `field`, so its code does not parse the text for a person. The words of chapter 6.7 are
its Н4; the gate — [the gate and pass contract](../../common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md).

- `error` is a word of the closed list [`refusal-words.tsv`](../data/refusal-words.tsv): a constant string without names and values. A door speaks
  only the words of its own rows of the list and of the `*` rows with the same code.
- `detail` is a phrase for a person: names, the cause, the limit. The exact text is the implementation's choice, except the words that Н4 names;
  it has no values of secrets (chapter 6.2).
- `fault` is a word of the closed vocabulary, the rules as before. At 400 with `fault` the word `error` equals `fault`; at 409 with `fault` — `conflict`.
- Where the norm named a phrase with names, the word is the class of the row of [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Refusals»;
  no class — `refused`. The phrase stays in `detail`. A constant phrase of the norm (`If-Match is a unit revision`) is itself the word.

**The format of `field`** (P/07 and the doors of lessons P/08, P/10 refer to it). `field` is a JSON Pointer string ([RFC 6901](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6901)):
the path of the field from the root of the JSON body of the request, the form `^(/([^~/]|~[01])*)+$`.

- Tokens are separated by `/`; `~` in a field name is written `~0`, `/` — `~1`; a list element is a number from zero: `/start`, `/labels/2`,
  `/feed_secret`, `/config/feed_secret`, `/a~1b`.
- `field` is present only at 400, 409 and 412 and only for the rows of the list where the column `field` is not `-`. A refusal of the whole body goes without `field`; there is no empty
  pointer.
- The value is one. A refusal names several fields — `field` is the first in the order of the check of [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md),
  at `unknown field` — the smallest name by UTF-8 bytes; the other names go to `detail`. The column `field` of the vectors
  [`row-check`](../../common/vectors/row-check/README.md) is the same field, by name.
- A violation in a header, a query parameter or a path segment goes without `field`: the pointer addresses the body.
- At `412 revision moved` the value is constant — `/revision`: the field of the row version that diverged from `If-Match`; the request body does not have it.
- `field` carries only names and numbers; there are no field values in it.

**`rows` in `GET /{rows}`.** Each element of `status` of the last heartbeat of each worker of the subsystem, of any age
([P/03.4 Н1.1](../../03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)), supplemented with the fields: `worker` — the name of the worker's slot; `server` — the field `server` of the heartbeat, and if there is none — `?`;
`age` — the seconds for which this console sees the version of the heartbeat unchanged, by its monotonic clock (the first look — 0),
with one digit after the point; `worker_state` — `live` at `age` ≤ 45 s (LOST_AFTER — [the observation contract](../../common/contracts/observation.md) «Свежесть»; review of stage A 6–7, № 1), otherwise `stale`; it is judged by the exact age, not by the given one with one digit (the vector `fresh-age`; review of stage B 6–7, № 35). An element
out of form is skipped; the list «out of form» is one, the row `/status` of [the outcomes of the heartbeat family](../../03-worker/data/heartbeats.outcomes.tsv) (the form of `status.items` — [the family](../../03-worker/data/heartbeats.schema.json); review of stage B 6–7, № 15; a non-integer `observed_revision` — a behaviour correction of review of stage A 6–7, № 13); `worker` is the slot name from the key
of the heartbeat `<sub>/heartbeats/<слот>`, not a field of the heartbeat. Skipping by element is only in this output; a heartbeat unreadable as a whole

### Versions: `revision`, index, `ETag`, `If-Match`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R012 -->

A row has two versions of different meaning (ADR-0094 B4); both are compared only for equality.

- **`revision`** — the edit counter: an integer, 1 at creation, +1 per edit (Н1.2, Н1.6). `DELETE` of a unit does not move it, a removed table row counts anew, so a former value can come back.
- **Index** — the version of the store row (P/01.1 `index`): it changes on each write of the row, including by a tombstone and by sealing, and does not repeat (P/01.1 Н1). It is the condition of `cas` and of a guard of the
  batch door and the only version that a tombstone does not reset.

The index is given by:

| Row | Where |
|---|---|
| a live unit row | the field `index` of an element of `configured`, of the answer of `POST`, `PUT`, of the bytes door; the row `row` of a 412 refusal |
| a table row | the field `index` of `TableRow` |
| a unit tombstone | `ETag` of the answer of `DELETE`; `GET /{rows}?deleted=1&about=<значение>` |
| the counter `<sub>/next_id` | `GET /{sub}/next_id` |
| a folder row, a membership row | the field `index` of `Folder`, `member.index` ([chapter 6.8](06.8-console.norms.md) Н2) |

The values of a row and its index are taken from one read (P/01.1 Н3 «read»), not from a listing: otherwise the pair would describe different versions.

**`ETag`** — the index in double quotes, a strong tag [RFC 9110 §8.8.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110#section-8.8.3): `ETag: "17"`. It is carried by the success answers that name one row: `GET` of a table row; `POST`, `PUT` of a unit and
the bytes door; `DELETE` of a unit — the tombstone index; `POST` of a table; the doors of [chapter 6.6](06.6-console.norms.md) Н2.1. Without `ETag`: `GET /{rows}` and `GET /{table}` (many rows), `DELETE` of a table row (no tombstone), the batch door (`{written}`), refusals.
`ETag` enters the saved answer of a claim: a repeat gives it as is.

**`If-Match`** is optional for any caller. It is read by `PUT` and `DELETE` of a unit, `POST /{rows}` with `unit.id` by a name (Н3 «create» step 5а), `POST` and `DELETE` of a table, the write doors of chapter 6.6 Н2.1.
The bytes door, the batch door (the condition of a row is `cas`) and the requests door do not read it. At `POST /{rows}` with `unit.id: numeric` the header is not read: the console issues the number, the condition has nothing to judge.

| Value | Compared with | Who sends |
|---|---|---|
| `<revision>` — a decimal integer ≥ 1 without a sign and leading zeros | `revision` of the row | the domain agent (P/08.6 Н1.4 `base`: the domain saw the revision, it has no index), the console module (P/07 Н1.5 `api`) |
| `"<индекс>"` — the index in double quotes, as `ETag` gave it | the index of the row, for a tombstone — the tombstone index | whoever read `ETag` or `index`; more reliable than `revision`: a former value will not come back |

Another value — `*`, a list, `W/"…"`, empty — 400 `If-Match is a unit revision` before reading the row: the word is one on all doors. The value does not enter the fingerprint of a claim (Н3 «repeat memory»). The condition is judged after the claim and on each
re-read of the procedure.

**`412 revision moved`** — the condition is not met, nothing written: `{detail, error: "revision moved", field: "/revision", revision, row?}`.

- `revision` — the current one; 0 — there is no row or its `revision` does not read (Н1.6).
- `row` — the current row in the read form of this door: an element of `configured` for a unit, `TableRow` for a table, the form of chapter 6.6 Н2.1 for its doors. Secrets — by the mask (chapter 6.2). The field is absent if there is no row, if it is a tombstone
  (`POST` over a tombstone) and if the caller would not pass `need` of reading this row (the gate and pass contract): a refusal does not give values past `view`.
- With a key the answer is remembered like the others (Н3 «repeat memory»). A done claim with `row` heavier than the store ceiling — the answer and the write go without `row`: the refusal is more important than a copy of the row, the client re-reads it itself.

### Tombstones by `about`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R013 -->

`GET /{rows}?deleted=1&about=<значение>` → `200 {deleted: [{id, index, revision}]}` (ADR-0094 addendum (5) item 1): the tombstones of the family `<rows>` of a manifest with `about: {sub, field}` whose field `field` equals the value. A tombstone keeps all fields
of the row (Н3 «delete» step 2), hence also the field `about`; there is no separate row. So a reader finds the index of a removed part when its name is nowhere any more.

- Read only: `index` — the tombstone index, `id` — as a string, `revision` — `revision` of the tombstone, an integer, 0 — does not read: a creation over it writes `revision` + 1 (Н3 «create» step 8), a chain of steps counts the next version from here (C06-DELETED-REVISION-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01); the list in ascending order of `id` (UTF-8 bytes); no such tombstones — `{deleted: []}`.
- The right — `view` on the unit `<about.sub>/<значение>` (the gate and pass contract `need`, `scope`); none — 403 `denied`, there is no answer without the right.
- Both parameters are mandatory and go once each, `deleted` — exactly `1`, `about` non-empty; otherwise, and for a manifest without `about`, — 400 `refused`, without `field`: a parameter is not addressed by a pointer. The list is not confirmed — 503 `store unavailable`.
- The value is compared byte for byte after percent-decoding; `about` of a unit is a field with `fixed: true` (P/02.1), so the value of the tombstone coincides with the value of the live row.

The platform does not remove unit tombstones itself, except the declared `placement.unplaced.delete_after`, `placement.retire_when.delete_after`, `placement.ends` ([P/04](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md) Н1.3). A subsystem whose reader relies on the tombstone index of a part does not declare these keys for the
part (ADR-0094 addendum (2) item 4 (c)): a removed tombstone would bring back a former version.

### Counter door

<!-- req: P/06.1-R014 -->

`GET /{sub}/next_id` → `200 {n, index}` (ADR-0094 B4; the Architect's decision of 2026-10-31): a read of the counter for a caller that itself names the number of a new unit in a batch (Н2 «Batch door»). The subsystem is always named in the path, also the root one: `/testsub/next_id`.

- `n` — the last issued number (Н1.3), the next is `n + 1`. `index` — the index of the counter row.
- No row: `n` — by recover of Н1.3, `index` — `""`. The number that recover issues, minus one: the greatest number among the rows `<rows>/` with tombstones and the numbers of live claims; nothing — 0. `n + 1` stays the next number, as with a row.
- The row does not read: `n` the same, `index` — its version (P/01.1 Н3 «read»): a batch write with this `cas` replaces it.
- recover here only counts: the list is not confirmed — 503 `unavailable` (P/06.5 Н4), as in the outcomes of the family `<sub>/next_id`. The door does not write the counter; there is no journal.
- The right — as for creating rows of `<s>` (the gate and pass contract `need`). There is no body, so the check is the one before reading the body at `POST /{rows}` (`create_about`).
- `unit.id` not `numeric` — 404 `no such path`. Another method — 405; `HEAD` — like `GET`.

### Change feed

<!-- req: P/06.1-R015 -->

`GET /changes` is a feed of rows by long polling (the client asks, and the door holds the answer until there is something to say): which rows changed after the named number (ADR-0094 B2, addendum (3) item 6). The browser and the worker
learn of an edit within a tick and do not read all rows to find it. The door is common, like `/drain`: it gets no subsystem prefix, names the rows by full keys, rows of different subsystems go together.

A message names an edit and carries no value: the client reads the value through the ordinary door, where masks and rights act. This is a recorded deviation from ADR-0038 («ответ не несёт события») by ADR-0094 B2; elsewhere ADR-0038 does not change.
ADR-0094 permits SSE (`text/event-stream`) or long polling; the norm takes long polling: each request passes the gate anew, so the rights are checked without a separate closing of a stream, and the answer is ordinary JSON.

```yaml
request:
  after:  <номер>            # optional: from which number to continue; none — the start of a subscription
  wait:   <целое>            # optional; 0 … CHANGES_WAIT, default 0: how many seconds to hold the answer if there is nothing to say
answer 200:
  position: <номер>          # how far the output went: the client continues from it
  changes:  [<сообщение>…]   # in the order of numbers; nothing to say — []
  reset:    true             # only at a reset, changes then []; otherwise no field
message:
  n:        <номер>          # the number of this message; good for after
  key:      <s>/<rows>/<id> | <s>/<t>/<имя> | platform/folders/<id> | <s>/operations/<id> | <s>/opdone/<id>/<i>   # the full key of the row
  revision: <integer ≥ 0>    # the row is live: its revision (Н1.2, Н1.6); 0 — revision does not read or the row has none (a folder, an operation)
  deleted:  <индекс> | ""    # instead of revision: the row is deleted; the tombstone index of a unit or a folder, "" — no tombstone
  at:       <время>          # a JSON number, the rule below
```

- **Scope.** The rows of units and of declared tables of the catalogue's subsystems, folder rows ([chapter 6.8](06.8-console.norms.md)), the record and the outcomes of operations of the declaring subsystem ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md)). A table `service: true` and operations with `service: true` enter only the feed by a ticket (Н1.6, «Filter» below); their messages do not come to people without a ticket. Membership rows (their change is carried by the folder row), the form `tables: [<t>]` (it has no doors), events, heartbeats and metrics do not enter: they have their own doors.
- **Number.** A string of ASCII letters and digits, `.` and `_`, at most 128 bytes; it has no sign `-`, because the worker appends its counter to the number through `-` (V/11.5). The number is compared only for equality; the client does not compute by it.
  The number acts only at the console instance that issued it, and only for a person with the rights with which it was issued («Implementation decides» item 10).
- **Start of a subscription.** Without `after` the door answers at once `{position, changes: []}` with the current number; `wait` is not read. The client takes the number, reads the rows through the doors and asks `after=<номер>`.
  A message with `revision: 0` it verifies by reading the row. Another message with `revision` not higher than the one read it drops: so an edit in the gap of the load is not lost and does not roll back what was read. A message `deleted` it verifies by reading this row, because it has no `revision`, and the row could have been created anew before the read.
- **Output.** With `after` the door gives at once all messages after it that the caller has the right to see. If there are none and `wait` is greater than 0, the door holds the answer until a tick with a message for it, at most `wait` seconds; the term ran out — `changes: []`.
  `position` — how far the output went, including through messages that the caller does not see: the next request does not look through them. There were no new messages — `position` equals `after`.
- **Reset** — `{position, changes: [], reset: true}`: the client reads all rows anew and continues from `position`, as at the start of a subscription. The door answers with a reset when:
  1. `after` is not a number of this instance: a foreign instance, a restart, a value out of form;
  2. the number is evicted from the history (`CHANGES_HISTORY`, Н3 «feed»): part of the messages after it the history no longer keeps. A subscriber has no queue, so an overflow of a subscriber is this same case: it lagged behind by more than the history;
  3. the number was issued with other rights: another person, another set of acting grant rows and role assignments (issue, removal, expiry of `until`, an edit of the roles table, ADR-0098 §4, ADR-0099 §3), another `sub` of the ticket's addressee or one of two requests without a ticket: the composition of messages differs for them, while a new ticket of the same person and the same subsystem does not reset the number;
  4. after the number the field `labels` of a unit changed, and among the caller's acting rows there is a scope `labels:`: the unit could enter its `view` or leave it;
  5. after the number the composition of a folder changed or the folder was deleted, and among the caller's acting rows, direct or in roles, there is a scope `folder:`: membership is an input of the right, the unit could enter `view` or leave it, the subtree of a deleted folder is unreachable at once (ADR-0097 §3, ADR-0099 §3). The simple rule of ADR-0097 is taken: a reset to everyone with a row `folder:`, without redelivery.
- **Filter.** A message is seen by whoever the ordinary door would give this row to. The check is the function `may` with `sub` of the subsystem of the row (a folder row — without a unit, `sub` empty; the gate and pass contract «Subsystem of the check»): role groups and tiers — as at the door; `folder:` reads membership once per request; an unknown input of a group — the row is not given, like a grant that did not cover. A unit row — `view` by `scope` of the gate and pass contract: `*` and `unit:` of the unit itself are compared by the name from the key, `labels:` and `unit:` of an about-unit — by the values that the tick read
  (for a tombstone — by its values); there are no values (the row is removed or does not read) — this grant does not cover. An operation row — `view` on the operations table ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) Н2), the subsystem is its own. A table row — the right `view` on the table: `need` of the door `GET /<table>`, and with `rights.unit_of` also `view` on the unit from the field of the row. The client does not see the values in the check.
  A row of a table `service: true` — only to a request by a ticket whose addressee is the subsystem of this table, and only if the field `user` of the row equals `user` of the ticket; for rows of an operation with `service: true`, instead of `user`, the `by` of the record of this operation is judged (ADR-0094 addendum (15), the edit of 2026-10-31): the worker learns of its own rows, and foreign ones do not come, so `view` is not judged here. The value — from the read of the tick, for a removed row — from the memory of the tick; no field, the row does not read or there is no memory — the message does not come. To a request without a ticket these messages never come.
- **`at`** — time by the writer's record of Н0 P/01.1 «Time in a row»: the wall-clock seconds of the instance in the tick that saw this version of the row. The store admission time is not given by the store door (P/01.1 Н3 «Admission time», «read», «list»), so `at` is later than it by at most a tick with its listing. Redelivery gives the same `at`: the value is kept by the history, not by the clock of the redelivery.
- The right — any grant (the gate and pass contract `need`): what is visible is decided by the filter. Another method — 405; `HEAD` — like `GET`; the request takes the feed lane (chapter 6.5 Н2), not the common one, so the waiting subscribers do not hinder ordinary management.

### Batch door

<!-- req: P/06.1-R016 -->

`POST /console/batch` — a write of the rows of a unit and of its parts by one store operation: all or nothing (ADR-0094 B9, addendum (9), (12)). The door
is common, like `/drain`: it gets no subsystem prefix, names the rows by full keys, rows of different subsystems go together.
The door does not read `If-Match`: the condition of each row is its `cas`.

```yaml
body:
  writes:                    # 1 … BATCH_WRITES (P/01.1 Н1); the order matters; a key occurs once
    - op:     put | delete
      key:    <s>/<rows>/<id> | <s>/<t>/<имя> | <s>/next_id   # a unit row, a row of a declared table, the counter; <s> — a subsystem of the catalogue
      fields: {…}            # mandatory at put: for a unit — the body of POST or PUT of this row, for a table — the body of POST, for the counter — {n: integer ≥ 1}; at delete there is no field
      cas:    <индекс> | ""  # mandatory: the store index of the read row (P/01.1 index; where from — Н2 «Versions»), for a tombstone — its index; "" — no row; without the quotes of ETag
      revision: <integer ≥ 0>  # optional: the version of the row that a stored edit expects (P/08.6 Н1.3б), 0 — no live row; diverged — 412 revision moved, step 5 of the procedure
  guards:                    # optional; 0 … BATCH_GUARDS; the order matters; a key once and not in writes
    - {key, index}           # a row that is read but not written; index "" — no row; form — P/01.1 Н1 guard
answer 200: {written: <the number of rows of writes>}
```

- The body and the elements are closed: there is no other field. `put` on a read row: no row or a tombstone — a creation (Н3 «create»), a live row — an edit (Н3 «edit»); for a table —
  «table — write a row». `delete`: for a unit — a tombstone (Н3 «delete»), for a table — the removal of the row; the counter is not removed: `delete` on `<s>/next_id` — 400 `bad batch`.
- Another family — `<sub>/policy`, `<sub>/idem/*`, `platform/*` except the rows of folders and membership ([chapter 6.8](06.8-console.norms.md) Н2 «Folder rows in the batch door»), the rows of an operation except the outcome and the counters by a step ticket ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) Н2 «Пакет шага»), a subsystem outside the catalogue — 400 `badkey`, `field` — `/writes/<номер>/key`. A table from `worker.writes`
  is not written by the door — 403; its row can be removed, like `DELETE` (Н1.6 `rows`).
- **Anchor row** (addendum (12) item 3). A unit of a subsystem with `about: {sub, field}` whose value of the field `field` is non-empty is a part of the unit
  `<sub>/<rows>/<значение>`; at `delete` the value is taken from the read row. This unit MUST be a row of `writes` of the same batch: otherwise 400 `no anchor`,
  `field` — `/writes/<номер>/fields/<field>`. So two batches over one unit always conflict, and a new part does not slip past; the `revision` of the unit grows from an edit of the part.
  The rule is held only by the batch door: a part created by a single `POST` does not move the anchor.
- **Counter** (ADR-0094 B9, also (6)). The number of a unit with `unit.id: numeric` in a batch is named by the caller itself, so its creation and the row `<s>/next_id` go only together: one without the other — 400 `bad_id`,
  `field` — `/writes/<номер>/key`. `n` is the greatest number of the rows being created, and all of them are greater than the read `n` (Н1.3: `n` — the last issued, a number is not used again); otherwise 400 `bad_id`, `field` — `/writes/<номер>/fields/n`.
- Limits — Н1.4. The form of the body or of an element is wrong — 400 `bad batch`, `field` — the path of the violating field; violations are judged in the order of the body: `writes` in order, then `guards`, then key repeats.
  The input of a row is judged as the body of its door; `field` of a row refusal is `/writes/<номер>/fields/<поле>` (the number from zero).
- `412 row moved {detail, error, field, key, index}`: the first diverged row in the store order — the guards in order, then `cas` in order. `field` — `/guards/<номер>/index` or
  `/writes/<номер>/cas`; `key` — its key; `index` — its index now, `""` — no row. A guard without `view` on the row — the same refusal as for an absent row: `index: ""` (addendum (12) item 12). A row of a table `service: true` under a ticket of this subsystem does not ask `view` (Н1.6 `service`): the guard is judged by the real index, `index: ""` passes while there is no row. The same for the guard `<s>/opbusy/<корень>` ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) Н1): a family of the door only as a guard and only under a ticket of the subsystem `<s>` with the record `calls` `<s>/operations`; it is not declared in `tables` or as a separate record `calls`; in `writes` — 400 `badkey`, for a person without a ticket — 400 `badkey` (ADR-0111 item 20).

In OpenAPI these are the standard servers + paths. /schema and /drain stay common ([chapter 6.6](06.6-console.norms.md)),

The bytes door does not read `If-Match` and `Idempotency-Key` (reconciliation of python and go М10/6–11, coincided without a norm). No unit — 404 `error` «no such unit», no blob field — 404 `error` «no such blob field» (reconciliation of python and go М10/6–11, T26). Route: any PUT `/[<sub>/]<rows>/<id>/<x>` is the bytes door: the limit of 32 MiB and one of the two places (Н1.4) are taken before looking up the unit and the field; no unit or blob field `<x>` — 404 after reading the body |
| GET `/<table>` | `{<table>: [TableRow…]}`; the name of the property equals the name of the table. GET of one row — `TableRow` and `ETag`; 404 `{detail, error}` when it is absent |

This is a full replacement of fields by the manifest, not a patch; `Idempotency-Key` is optional, with it — Н3 «repeat memory» |
| DELETE `/<table>/<name>` | 200 `{deleted: имя}`; 404 when absent, including with `If-Match`; 412 `revision moved`; 409 — the CAS repeats are exhausted. The door of the body copy at the resource `PUT /v1/objects/<sub>/blobs/<digest>` — [P/05](../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md) Н2 (stage B).

`TableRow` contains the mandatory `name`, `revision`, `index`, the declared fields and stamp; `held_by` is added only to the places table, the value is the name of a worker or null. Secret keys with the suffix `_secret` are excluded recursively, addresses are masked by chapter 6.2. The input of POST is its own TableInput, not the ClientFields of a unit. A field not given in POST and without `default` is absent in the row and in TableRow: the empty form of Н1.2 does not apply to tables; `default` is substituted (the same — [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Preparation of fields», «Table row»). The computed `held_by`, `garbled`, `revision`, `index` and the stamps `by`, `at`, `made_at` are not set by the client; a given stamp — 400 `unknown field`, even if it is not included in the stamp of the table; `revision` and `index` — the same. A manifest declaring them as editable table fields is outside the profile of this lesson and is rejected at loading. The forms `SnapshotUnit`/`SnapshotShard` describe the snapshot object ([P/04 Н1.3а](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md)), not a new HTTP route.

JSON bodies are read with the common limit ≥1 MiB, blob with 32 MiB, the resource copy with 64 MiB (P/05). The header does not replace counting the actual bytes. Bad framing/a short body — 400, a timeout — 408, an excess — 413, chunked of the console — 400. Refusals of reading/trust/sealing give 503 with the full form `{detail, error}` by the owner. The success of publishing an object does not prove the success of the next write of the row; with an unknown outcome the client first reads the row or repeats the request with the same `Idempotency-Key` (Н3 «repeat memory»); a request without a key is not repeated automatically.

### Sweep window command

<!-- req: P/06.1-R018 -->

The exceptional sweep window (Н3 «body sweep») is opened by the operator with the implementation's command `sweep` (its name — «Implementation
decides» item 9) with the flag `--writers-stopped`: with it the operator confirms the row «deletion admission» of Н3. The command
goes over all subsystems of the catalogue with a blob field, itself waits out SWEEP_GRACE as a new observer of the version of the list,
then performs the phases sweep and «completion» of Н3.

| Outcome | Exit code | What is done |
|---|---|---|
| the window is done, including when there is nothing to delete; something was deleted in the window, even if batches of other subsystems were dropped — a dropped batch is not a failure: no data is lost, the next mark repeats | 0 | doomed deleted, tombstones written; dropped batches — by output lines «`<sub>`: batch dropped: mark time unknown (`<n>`)» |
| nothing deleted in the whole window: no `--writers-stopped`; a failure reading the references or the list; a CAS conflict of the list; all non-empty batches dropped — `at` absent or does not read (Н3 «body sweep» sweep); the listing of doors or of the output for `peers` of a tombstone is not confirmed ([the tombstones contract](../../common/contracts/tombstone.md) «Neighbours») | 2 | nothing deleted |
| a failure after deletions began: a tombstone or a deletion outcome is not confirmed | 3 | do not open the writers and copying (Н3 «completion») |

The window code is by the whole window, not by subsystem: 2 — only if nothing was deleted in the whole window, otherwise 0 (C06-BLOB-CUTOFF-33-01, the Architect's answer of 2026-10-25).

### Full keys and store refusal

<!-- req: P/06.1-R020 -->

In all operations below the key rules of P/01.1 Н1 are applied after the full path of Н1.1 is assembled,
including the prefix, the family and the separators; the guard key is checked the same way. The grammar of UnitId,
WorkerName and the 200-byte limit of an Idempotency-Key component are kept. The schema of a component
does not promise the capacity of the full key. Keys are not shortened or hashed on a refusal; the digest in the claim key (Н3 «repeat memory») is a constant rule, not an answer to a refusal.

A definite `badkey` at a read or a write is a refusal of this operation, not an absence of the row,
not a CAS conflict and not a non-answer. The applicable key rules can be checked before calling the store;
a refusal of a smaller declared limit of a file implementation is handled the same way.

- The units door answers `400 {detail, error: "badkey"}`, without fault; detail names the family of the
  key and the cause without the contents of secrets. At PUT/DELETE the row and revision do not change.
  POST before getting its pending answers without saving; after getting it — completes the
  refusal by step 9. The former reserve of a number is not returned. A refusal to save the answer itself
  at step 9 still gives 503, not an unconfirmed 400. The rules of the loss of a claim/guard
  keep their former priority; a foreign claim is not changed.
- The worker branches (the choice of a slot, the capture, the renewal, the write under the guard, the issue and confirmation of an epoch) are moved to

### repeat memory — the write doors of people

<!-- req: P/06.1-R022 -->

The repeat memory (ADR-0094 B3) is the claim row `<sub>/idem/<key>`, one mechanism for all write doors of people: steps 1, 2 and 9 of the procedure «create»,
«Claim ownership» and the term of [Н1.3](#н13-identifier) «Term of a done claim». At `POST /{rows}` the key is mandatory. At `PUT` and `DELETE` of a unit, `POST` and
`DELETE` of a table and the write doors of [chapter 6.6](06.6-console.norms.md) Н2.1 the header `Idempotency-Key` is optional: without it the procedure of the door is
as before and a repeat is a new write; with it — the door goes under a claim. At `POST /console/batch` — the same (Н3 «batch»). The bytes door and the requests door (chapter 6.4) have no repeat memory.

```yaml
key:        (user, method, path, key)
<key>:      hex sha256(UTF-8(user) ‖ 0x00 ‖ method ‖ 0x00 ‖ UTF-8(path) ‖ 0x00 ‖ UTF-8(key)); 64 lowercase characters, one segment;
            user — the proven name of the caller (chapter 6.3 Н1.3); method — in uppercase;
            path — without the subsystem prefix, the segments percent-decoded: `/testsub/counters/a%20b` and `/counters/a b` — one path
fingerprint: the caller, method, path, the body as a JSON value (step 2 of «create»); the door input in query is part of the body; no body — like `{}`;
            If-Match does not enter the fingerprint: a repeat after a lost answer carries the former condition, it is not judged (the order below); for a batch, for the same reason, the `cas` of the rows does not enter:
            it is read anew at each repeat
order:      step 1 (the form of the request and of the key, the body, the ring; If-Match — only the form) → the claim step 2 → If-Match → the door procedure →
            the target write under the guard → the answer into the claim step 9
other fingerprint: 422 `key reused` on all memory doors (ADR-0094 addendum (14))
words:      the header value — Н2 (without OWS, twice — 400); a key out of form — 400 `Idempotency-Key required`; a foreign pending younger than 30 s — 409 `in flight`;
            503 — `store unavailable` for units, `unavailable` for tables and chapter 6.6
```

The gate and request parsing go before the claim: their refusals do not enter the memory. An answer `< 500`, including a door refusal and `412 revision moved`, is remembered and
given to a repeat as is, by code and body: having decided anew, the client takes a new key. A repeat from memory writes nothing and writes no journal.

**Target write** of a door — its former `cas` together with the guard `{key: <sub>/idem/<key>, index: моя версия заявки}` (index: my version of the claim); a write door without `cas` — with the guard.
A prepared done claim is checked by weight before the write, like step 8: over the store ceiling — 413 `row too large`, the write is not performed, to step 9.
A guard refusal — «Claim ownership». The fate of one's own pending at step 9 is the same: remove it only if the target write is reliably not performed.

**Continuation.** A takeover of a claim after 30 s does not cancel what the former executor wrote. The one who took over first looks at the target, then goes through the door procedure:

- `PUT` of a unit: step 5а of «edit» keeps the intended `revision` in the claim; the row has this `revision` and the intended `born` → written by this claim, step 9 with this row;
- a write of a table row: step 2а of «table» keeps the intended `revision`; the row has this `revision` → written by this claim, step 9 with this row. Without this the procedure anew would raise `revision` a second time and would refuse its own write with `412`;
- `DELETE` of a unit: the row is already a tombstone → step 9 with `{deleted}`; `DELETE` of a table: the row is already gone → the same;
- a claim without an intended `revision` — the write has not yet begun; the procedure goes anew.

A foreign write of the same `revision` or a foreign removal within the same 30 s are indistinguishable and are accepted, as at creation (Н3 «create», «Races»). The target is looked at before `If-Match`: the condition judges foreign edits, not one's own write.

**Claim ownership.** The version of the claim row is the condition of each change: the writes/removals of the claim itself
go with `cas` of my version, the reserve of the counter and the creation of the unit — with the guard of this version together with `cas`
of the target row. Checking the claim by a separate read before an unconditional write is not enough.
After a successful change of the claim the executor remembers the new version; a takeover always changes it.
After `412 guard` or a `cas` conflict of the claim the former executor stops the changes and re-reads
the claim, without assigning its new version to itself: the same fingerprint and `done` → the saved answer whole;
the same fingerprint and `pending` → `409 in flight`; another fingerprint → `422 key reused`;
no claim or the store did not answer → `503 store unavailable`, the client repeats with the same key.
It does not save this refusal and does not remove a foreign claim. After an unknown outcome of a write it also
does not continue with the old assumption of success: until it is clarified pending is kept, the client gets 503.

A weight field absent after materialization (for example, an ungiven inherit) does not substitute a refusal of
the value: for computing the weight of such a row placement.weight.default is used. A present
0, a negative or a non-numeric value are not replaced by this default. The empty form of int equals 0,
so a manifest with a weight field without a suitable default may require an explicit positive value
at POST; the materialization rules of Н1.2 stay common.

**Races.** Two consoles with one key: the claim with `cas ""` lets one pass; the second sees `pending` and answers `409 in flight`, later — the former answer. Two consoles with different keys and one name: the write of the row with `cas ""` lets one pass; the second gets `409 exists`. A console died after the reserve of the number: the claim after 30 s goes to another console, and that one continues from step 4 — the checks of the form, the mandatory fields and the references are repeated — and writes at step 8 with the number from the claim (numeric): a row with it already exists → the same create, 201 the same row; none → write it with this number. Named: a row with this name exists, `revision: 1`, and there is no answer in the claim → written by this claim, 201 (a foreign creation of the same name in the same 30 s window is indistinguishable and is accepted). A console died between the reserve of the number (cas of the counter) and the write of the number into the claim: a claim without a number — reserve anew from step 3, the former number is burnt (two rows, two operations — there is no atomicity between them). A number written into the claim before the death is not burnt; the number of a refused attempt is burnt (steps 5–7 returned a refusal).

The former console A can revive after the takeover by console B. If A reserved 1 but did not save
it in the claim, its late `cas` of the claim is refused; A does not reach the write of unit 1. B reserves 2
and creates only it. If number 1 is already saved, B keeps it at the takeover: the late write of A
under the old guard is refused, and B continues with number 1. If A wrote the unit before the takeover,
B finds this same row and saves the answer; the late `cas` of the answer of A does not replace the result of B.
The same protection acts on a refusal and a removal of pending: A does not delete the claim of B. A lost answer about the creation
does not give the right to remove pending — the saved number is needed for the continuation. These outcomes rely on the atomic
`cas`/`guard` of P/01.1, not on the assumption that the former executor died.

Exceeding the ceiling of a service write before step 8 also gives 413 row too large. If pending is not yet
created, there is nowhere to save the answer; if the claim is one's own — the refusal completes through step 9. The reserved
number is not returned. A refusal to save the result at step 9 — 503: if the unit is already written or
the outcome is unknown, pending stays for recovery; a foreign claim is not changed.

### body sweep — marks in the background, deletion in a maintenance window

<!-- req: P/06.1-R028 -->

| Phase | Conditions and action | On failure |
|---|---|---|
| mark | Every 60 s a full successful pass; if the current batch of digests is non-empty — do not rewrite at/the version and do not add new ones. If there is no list/it is empty — CAS of a new batch ≤64 without references with at of the first mark | An unknown pass — mark/delete nothing |
| grace | Wait 300 s of observation of an unchanged version of the list by the local monotonic clock. The first look/a new version start from 0; put_blob can remove a mark | Repeat the read later |
| deletion admission | One operator closes the new writes of units/tables with blob, blob PUT, import/restore and incoming mirror copies; waits for the started operations. One sweeper | An unknown outcome of a writer or an unconfirmed quiet — no deletion |
| sweep | In this same window re-read all references and the list and compute the `peers` of the tombstones ([the tombstones contract](../../common/contracts/tombstone.md) «Neighbours»: the listing of doors or of the output is not confirmed — no deletion). doomed = the marked ones without references; remove the list by CAS; only then get_at and delete_at of each doomed one older than the mark — the boundary «older than the mark» only by a readable `at`. `at` absent or does not read with a non-empty batch — delete nothing from it in the window: remove the batch by CAS by the read version, the output line «`<sub>`: batch dropped: mark time unknown (`<n>`)», `<n>` — the number of digests; a dropped batch is not a failure: the window code is by the whole window (Н2 «Sweep window command»); the next mark writes a new batch with `at` (C06-BLOB-CUTOFF-33-01, the Architect's decision and answer of 2026-10-25) | A read error/a conflict — skip the deletion, do not count the absence as proven |
| completion | Durably write tombstones to the deleted ones (the form and `peers` — the tombstones contract); on a partial failure keep the ban on writers until the tombstones are restored/the outcomes are determined | Do not open copying that could resurrect the deleted |
| exit | After the completion of the window return the writers/copying | The next sweep begins a new pass |

mark/grace/sweep of one sweeper do not overlap; in the exceptional window the other sweepers, including mark, are stopped.

at is the diagnostic time of the first mark of the batch, not a sliding term. A decrease of the list does not add a new digest and starts a new conservative window of observing the version; a new observer always waits 300 s. The passes in T0+60…540 do not update a non-empty unchanged batch, so by T0+600 the grace is already waited out. New candidates get only into the next batch after the former one is emptied.

In the ordinary mode only mark/grace are executed, once a minute. Deletion is not an automatic action of a live installation. `delete_at` and the CAS of the list prevent part of the conflicts, but by themselves do not protect from a new reference after the check: only the window without writers ensures that. The results of a pass obtained before the stop are not used for sweep.

```
tick:
1. the prefixes — <sub>/<rows>/ and <sub>/<t>/ of each table of the scope (Н2 «Change feed») of each manifest of the catalogue, platform/folders/, the operation rows of the manifests with a declaration.
2. list(prefix) (P/01.1), each separately. Not confirmed → the memory of this prefix as before, this does not count as a removal; the next tick will see it again.
3. a key whose index is not equal to the memory (a new key is not there): read(key).
   the read is not confirmed → the memory of the key as before;
   no row → step 4;
   a tombstone (of units or of folders) → message {deleted: index};
   the row does not read or revision does not read (Н1.6) → message {revision: 0};
   live → message {revision}. The memory remembers a greater revision: the row was removed and created anew between ticks → before it the message {deleted: ""} of this key.
   The values and the index are taken from one read; the memory gets the index of the read.
4. a key of the memory that is not in the confirmed listing is removed from the memory; the memory does not remember tombstones → message {deleted: ""}.
5. the messages of the tick get numbers in the order of the keys (UTF-8 bytes) and the at of this tick; the history evicts the oldest; the waiting requests wake up.
```

The first tick of an instance only fills the memory: there are no messages, a new history begins with a reset anyway (Н2, reset 1). About a key of the first tick the memory knows only the index. At its next change `revision` is not compared (without `{deleted: ""}`), while `labels` and `members` count as changed: an extra reset is safe.

A message about a live unit whose `labels` differ from the memory is marked by the history: so the door finds reset 4 without re-reading the rows. A message about a folder row whose `members` differ from the memory, and its tombstone, the history marks for reset 5. A new key does not count as a change: the unit enters `view` by its own message. The history does not give values: it keeps them only for the filter.

```
give(after, wait):
0. after repeated → 400 {detail: after, error: refused}
1. there was no first tick → 503 unavailable
2. no after → {position: current, changes: []}; wait is not read
2а. wait repeated, not by the rule «Numeric query parameters» or outside 0 … CHANGES_WAIT → 400 {detail: wait, error: refused}
3. reset 1, 2, 3 → {position: current, changes: [], reset: true}
4. the messages after after: a mark of a change of labels and the scope labels: at the caller → reset 4; a mark of a change of the composition of a folder and the scope folder: at the caller → reset 5; otherwise select by the filter of Н2
5. there are visible ones → the answer; none and wait > 0 → wait for a tick with messages, at most wait; after the tick — step 4 with the former after
6. position — the last looked-through number
```

**Races.** Two edits of one row between ticks give one message with the last `revision`: the client reads the value itself anyway. Sealing changes the index but not `revision` (chapter 6.2): the message repeats `revision`, the client drops it. A table row removed and created anew between ticks, written as many times as it had been: `revision` equals the memory, the message is one, and the client that read the former row will drop it; this requires as many writes within one tick. Two consoles each keep their own history: a move of the client to another one — reset 1. A break of the answer loses only the answer: the client repeats the request with the former `after`.

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **the split of the families** into rows and objects beyond Н1.1, if it is different, and where the objects lie;
2. **the door body limit** (`413`); the form of `*_secret` in a row is a norm of chapter 6.2 Н1.2 (the envelope), not a choice;
