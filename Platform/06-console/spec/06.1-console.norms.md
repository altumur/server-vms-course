### N1.3 Identifier

<!-- req: P/06.1-R006 -->

`id.numeric` (`unit.id: numeric`): a decimal integer ≥ 1 from the counter `<sub>/next_id`: `n` — the last issued number, the next — `n + 1`. `id.numeric`: no row — `recover` below and the creation `cas ""` with `n` = the number being issued: without units and claims it is 1, with already existing units — the greatest + 1, not 1 (issuing 1 — a collision with a live unit); the first — 1. `id.numeric`: a number is never used again; tombstones count; a number issued to a refused attempt is not returned.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R101 -->
- `id.numeric`: in the path — a decimal notation without a sign, spaces and leading zeros (`01`, `+1` — 400 `badkey`).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R102 -->

`id.numeric.recover`: only with a full confirmed list of rows/tombstones and live claims; a loss of history or a read error does not count as emptiness and does not permit an automatic recovery. `id.numeric.recover`: the counter does not read or there is no row → the greatest number among the rows `<rows>/` (with tombstones) and the numbers in live claims + 1 — the issued number; the counter is rewritten whole with `n` = this number (the last issued). `id.numeric.recover`: the numbers of refused attempts leave no traces and after a recovery MAY come out again — the only exception from «never», journal `next_id.recovered {from, to}`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R105 -->
- `id.named` (`unit.id: <field>`): [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Identifier and field constraints».

<!-- req: P/06.1-R106 -->

`id.made_prefix`: a creation through the door (POST) of an id that begins with «`<customer>-`» of the subsystem that declared `worker.creates` to this table (the manifest catalogue, P/02.1 N3), — 400 `names starting with "<customer>-" are made by <customer> (worker.creates)`, also over a tombstone: under this prefix live only the customer's rows. `id.made_prefix`: a row with a name under this prefix, created before the customer was declared, stays and is taken — without its own `made` the customer does not write it.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R108 -->

`id.made_key`: in a row with `made` the fields that the customer's key names (`worker.creates[].key`, P/03.6 N1.6) are immutable also for the door, like `fixed`: an edit with another value — 400 `<field> is fixed`. `id.made_key`: in a row with `made` the other fields the operator edits through the door.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R110 -->
- `tombstone`, `unique`, `canonical_url`, `ref_and_must_match` — [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Identifier and field constraints».

<!-- req: P/06.1-R111 -->

The recovery profile numeric is not compatible with physical cleanup: unit.id: numeric together with placement.unplaced.delete_after is a load refusal of P/02.1. Successful numbers are represented by saved rows/tombstones; the cleanup of done claims does not cancel that. An arbitrary loss of these rows is not covered by the recover formula.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R114 -->
Any name that the grammar of the id and of the slot and the full key limit of P/01.1 permit is stored by any implementation: file names
(bucket directories — the event journal contract «File names in shared trees») the implementation encodes itself; there is no narrower file profile — otherwise parts of different
implementations over the same data are incompatible.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R115 -->

**Term of a done claim.** The console MUST keep a completed saved answer of a claim with an HTTP code <500 for at least DONE_ANSWER_KEEP from the moment it was saved. Cleanup of a done claim uses a local monotonic age; after the memory is recovered a new observer counts the done claim as fresh and does not trust foreign wall clocks. The term of a done claim does not extend done to 5xx or pending: their fate stays by N3.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R118 -->
DONE_ANSWER_KEEP, CLAIM_TAKEOVER_AFTER, SWEEP_LIMIT, SWEEP_GRACE and SWEEP_MARK_EVERY are defined only in [runtime-constants.tsv](../forms/runtime-constants.tsv); their formulas and conditions remain owned by these procedures.

### N1.5 Bodies

<!-- req: P/06.1-R008 -->

A blob is declared only in unit.fields; a blob in a table is outside the profile and is rejected by the loader.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R122 -->

The name of a blob field is 1–64 ASCII: the first A-Z/a-z/_, the rest A-Z/a-z/0-9/_. An empty name, a path separator, a dot, a percent, a space, a control or non-ASCII character do not represent the blob door of this lesson: a load refusal `refused:value` with the path of the field. Other fields are not narrowed automatically by the restriction on the name of a blob field. A manifest with any blob field of a unit does not declare unit.fields.bytes: a load refusal `refused:value` by P/02.1. Without blob the field bytes is permitted. This restriction keeps the full Unit in the answer of the upload; a renaming of the counter or a new shell is not introduced.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R133 -->

<!-- req: P/06.1-R134 -->

### Refusal: `error` and `field`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R011 -->

The refusal of the doors of chapters 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 6.5, 6.6 is `{detail, error, fault?, field?}`; there is no second form. The client chooses an action by the word and puts the error at a field by `field`, so its code does not parse the text for a person.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R210 -->
The words of chapter 6.7 are its N4; the gate — [the gate and pass contract](../../common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R211 -->

`error` is a word of the closed list [`refusal-words.tsv`](../data/refusal-words.tsv): a constant string without names and values. `error`: a door speaks only the words of its own rows of the list and of the `*` rows with the same code.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R212 -->

`detail` is a phrase for a person: names, the cause, the limit. The exact text is the implementation's choice, except the words that N4 names. `detail` has no values of secrets (chapter 6.2).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R213 -->

`fault` is a word of the closed vocabulary. `fault`: at 400 with `fault` the word `error` equals `fault`; at 409 with `fault` — `conflict`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R214 -->

Where the norm named a phrase with names, the word is the class of the row of [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md) «Refusals»; no class — `refused`. The phrase stays in `detail`. A constant phrase of the norm (`If-Match is a unit revision`) is itself the word.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R215 -->

**The format of `field`** (P/07 and the doors of lessons P/08, P/10 refer to it). `field` is a JSON Pointer string ([RFC 6901](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6901)): the path of the field from the root of the JSON body of the request, the form `^(/([^~/]|~[01])*)+$`. Tokens are separated by `/`; `~` in a field name is written `~0`, `/` — `~1`; a list element is a number from zero: `/start`, `/labels/2`, `/feed_secret`, `/config/feed_secret`, `/a~1b`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R218 -->

`field` is present only at 400, 409 and 412 and only for the rows of the list where the column `field` is not `-`. A refusal of the whole body goes without `field`; there is no empty pointer. The value is one. A refusal names several fields — `field` is the first in the order of the check of [the row check contract](../../common/contracts/row-check.md), at `unknown field` — the smallest name by UTF-8 bytes; the other names go to `detail`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R563 -->
- The column `field` of the vectors `row-check` is the same field, by name; for `unknown_field` it lists all unknown names in ascending byte order separated by commas, and the `field` of a door is the first of them.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R220 -->
- A violation in a header, a query parameter or a path segment goes without `field`: the pointer addresses the body.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R221 -->

At `412 revision moved` the value is constant — `/revision`: the field of the row version that diverged from `If-Match`; the request body does not have it. `field` carries only names and numbers; there are no field values in it.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R223 -->

**`rows` in `GET /{rows}`.** Each element of `status` of the last heartbeat of each worker of the subsystem, of any age ([P/03.4 N1.1](../../03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)), supplemented with the fields: `worker` — the name of the worker's slot; `server` — the field `server` of the heartbeat, and if there is none — `?`; `age` — the seconds for which this console sees the version of the heartbeat unchanged, by its monotonic clock (the first look — 0), with one digit after the point; `worker_state` — `live` at `age` ≤ 45 s (LOST_AFTER — [the observation contract](../../common/contracts/observation.md) «Freshness»), otherwise `stale`; it is judged by the exact age, not by the given one with one digit (the vector `fresh-age`). An element out of form is skipped; the list «out of form» is one, the row `/status` of [the outcomes of the heartbeat family](../../03-worker/data/heartbeats.outcomes.tsv) (the form of `status.items` — [the family](../../03-worker/data/heartbeats.schema.json)). In `rows`, `worker` is the slot name from the key of the heartbeat `<sub>/heartbeats/<slot>`, not a field of the heartbeat. Skipping by element is only in this output; a heartbeat unreadable as a whole (the path `""` of [the outcomes of the heartbeat family](../../03-worker/data/heartbeats.outcomes.tsv): out of form outside `status[]`, `worker` ≠ the name in the key) acts earlier than an element — none of its elements enters `rows`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R226 -->
The list `rows` is ordered by unit id (UTF-8 bytes), with equal ones — by the name of the worker's slot (UTF-8 bytes).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R227 -->
The filter `view` of `rows` — chapter 6.3 N1.3 `need`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R228 -->
No answer when reading the heartbeats (and their listing) is `lapse` of [the observation contract](../../common/contracts/observation.md): `rows` from the memory of the last read versions with their age; the heartbeats were never read since the start — 503 `unavailable` (not `[]`).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R565 -->
The unit rows (`configured`) are a mandatory read of `GET /{rows}`, no answer — 503.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R229 -->

**State in the answer**. An element of `configured` of a unit row gets `state` — an object, the name of `unit.state` → the value of the acting state: of the row `<sub>/state/<name>/<id>` that acts by the row check contract «Unit state» acts (its `born` and `revision` equal `born` and `revision` of this live row, for `cursor` — only `born`; the epoch is not compared); the value is `value` of the state row without `born`, `revision`, `epoch`. No acting one — no name; not a single one — no `state`. A state row that does not read by its type (not by the form of P/03.6 N1.7 or `value` not by the schema `unit.state.<name>`) does not act — no name.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R233 -->
Read only: `state` in the body of POST and PUT is a platform name, 400 (N3 step 4).

### Versions: `revision`, index, `ETag`, `If-Match`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R012 -->

A row has two versions of different meaning; both are compared only for equality.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R234 -->
- **`revision`** — the edit counter: an integer, 1 at creation, +1 per edit (N1.2, N1.6). `DELETE` of a unit does not move it, a removed table row counts anew, so a former value can come back.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R235 -->

**Index** — the version of the store row (P/01.1 `index`): it changes on each write of the row, including by a tombstone and by sealing, and does not repeat (P/01.1 N1). **Index**: it is the condition of `cas` and of a guard of the batch door and the only version that a tombstone does not reset.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R236 -->
The index is given by:

| Row | Where |
|---|---|
| a live unit row | the field `index` of an element of `configured`, of the answer of `POST`, `PUT`, of the bytes door; the row `row` of a 412 refusal |
| a table row | the field `index` of `TableRow` |
| a unit tombstone | `ETag` of the answer of `DELETE`; `GET /{rows}?deleted=1&about=<value>` |
| the counter `<sub>/next_id` | `GET /{sub}/next_id` |
| a folder row, a membership row | the field `index` of `Folder`, `member.index` ([chapter 6.8](06.8-console.norms.md) N2) |

<!-- req: P/06.1-R238 -->
The values of a row and its index are taken from one read (P/01.1 N3 «read»), not from a listing: otherwise the pair would describe different versions.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R239 -->

**`ETag`** — the index in double quotes, a strong tag [RFC 9110 §8.8.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110#section-8.8.3): `ETag: "17"`. `ETag` is carried by the success answers that name one row: `GET` of a table row; `POST`, `PUT` of a unit and the bytes door; `DELETE` of a unit — the tombstone index; `POST` of a table; the doors of [chapter 6.6](06.6-console.norms.md) N2.1. Without `ETag`: `GET /{rows}` and `GET /{table}` (many rows), `DELETE` of a table row (no tombstone), the batch door (`{written}`), refusals. `ETag` enters the saved answer of a claim: a repeat gives it as is.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R243 -->

**`If-Match`** is optional for any caller. `If-Match` is read by `PUT` and `DELETE` of a unit, `POST /{rows}` with `unit.id` by a name (N3 «create» step 5a), `POST` and `DELETE` of a table, the write doors of chapter 6.6 N2.1. The bytes door, the batch door (the condition of a row is `cas`) and the requests door do not read `If-Match`. At `POST /{rows}` with `unit.id: numeric` the header is not read: the console issues the number, the condition has nothing to judge.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R247 -->
| Value | Compared with | Who sends |
|---|---|---|
| `<revision>` — a decimal integer ≥ 1 without a sign and leading zeros | `revision` of the row | the domain agent (P/08.6 N1.4 `base`: the domain saw the revision, it has no index), the console module (P/07 N1.5 `api`) |
| `"<index>"` — the index in double quotes, as `ETag` gave it | the index of the row, for a tombstone — the tombstone index | whoever read `ETag` or `index`; more reliable than `revision`: a former value will not come back |

<!-- req: P/06.1-R248 -->

Another value of `If-Match` — `*`, a list, `W/"…"`, empty — 400 `If-Match is a unit revision` before reading the row: the word is one on all doors. The value of `If-Match` does not enter the fingerprint of a claim (N3 «repeat memory»). The `If-Match` condition is judged after the claim and on each re-read of the procedure.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R251 -->
**`412 revision moved`** — the condition is not met, nothing written: `{detail, error: "revision moved", field: "/revision", revision, row?}`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R252 -->
- `revision` — the current one; 0 — there is no row or its `revision` does not read (N1.6).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R253 -->

`row` — the current row in the read form of this door: an element of `configured` for a unit, `TableRow` for a table, the form of chapter 6.6 N2.1 for its doors. `row`: secrets — by the mask (chapter 6.2). `row`: the field is absent if there is no row, if it is a tombstone (`POST` over a tombstone) and if the caller would not pass `need` of reading this row (the gate and pass contract): a refusal does not give values past `view`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R254 -->

With a key the answer is remembered like the others (N3 «repeat memory»). A done claim with `row` heavier than the store ceiling — the answer and the write go without `row`: the refusal is more important than a copy of the row, the client re-reads it itself.

### Tombstones by `about`

<!-- req: P/06.1-R013 -->

`GET /{rows}?deleted=1&about=<value>` → `200 {deleted: [{id, index, revision}]}`: the tombstones of the family `<rows>` of a manifest with `about: {sub, field}` whose field `field` equals the value. A tombstone keeps all fields of the row (N3 «delete» step 2), hence also the field `about`; there is no separate row. So a reader finds the index of a removed part when its name is nowhere any more. Read only: `index` — the tombstone index, `id` — as a string, `revision` — `revision` of the tombstone, an integer, 0 — does not read: a creation over it writes `revision` + 1 (N3 «create» step 8), a chain of steps counts the next version from here; the list in ascending order of `id` (UTF-8 bytes); no such tombstones — `{deleted: []}`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R258 -->
- The right — `view` on the unit `<about.sub>/<value>` (the gate and pass contract `need`, `scope`); none — 403 `denied`, there is no answer without the right.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R259 -->
- Both parameters are mandatory and go once each, `deleted` — exactly `1`, `about` non-empty; otherwise, and for a manifest without `about`, — 400 `refused`, without `field`: a parameter is not addressed by a pointer.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R570 -->
- The list of tombstones is not confirmed — 503 `store unavailable`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R260 -->
- The value is compared byte for byte after percent-decoding; `about` of a unit is a field with `fixed: true` (P/02.1), so the value of the tombstone coincides with the value of the live row.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R261 -->

The platform does not remove unit tombstones itself, except the declared `placement.unplaced.delete_after`, `placement.retire_when.delete_after`, `placement.ends` ([P/04](../../04-controller/spec/04-controller.norms.md) N1.3). A subsystem whose reader relies on the tombstone index of a part does not declare `placement.unplaced.delete_after`, `placement.retire_when.delete_after`, `placement.ends` for the part: a removed tombstone would bring back a former version.

### Counter door

<!-- req: P/06.1-R014 -->

`GET /{sub}/next_id` → `200 {n, index}`: a read of the counter for a caller that itself names the number of a new unit in a batch (N2 «Batch door»). The subsystem is always named in the path, also the root one: `/testsub/next_id`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R264 -->

`n` — the last issued number (N1.3), the next is `n + 1`. `index` — the index of the counter row. No row: `n` — by recover of N1.3, `index` — `""`. The number that recover issues, minus one: the greatest number among the rows `<rows>/` with tombstones and the numbers of live claims; nothing — 0. `n + 1` stays the next number, as with a row. The row does not read: `n` the same, `index` — its version (P/01.1 N3 «read»): a batch write with this `cas` replaces it.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R267 -->

recover here only counts: the list is not confirmed — 503 `unavailable` (P/06.5 N4), as in the outcomes of the family `<sub>/next_id`. The counter door does not write the counter; there is no journal.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R268 -->
- The right — as for creating rows of `<s>` (the gate and pass contract `need`). There is no body, so the check is the one before reading the body at `POST /{rows}` (`create_about`).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R269 -->

`unit.id` not `numeric` — 404 `no such path`. Another method at the counter door — 405; `HEAD` — like `GET`.

### Change feed

<!-- req: P/06.1-R015 -->

`GET /changes` is a feed of rows by long polling (the client asks, and the door holds the answer until there is something to say): which rows changed after the named number. The browser and the worker learn of an edit within a tick and do not read all rows to find it. The door is common, like `/drain`: it gets no subsystem prefix, names the rows by full keys, rows of different subsystems go together.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R272 -->

A message names an edit and carries no value: the client reads the value through the ordinary door, where masks and rights act. The feed uses long polling: each request passes the gate anew, so the rights are checked without a separate closing of a stream, and the answer is ordinary JSON.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R274 -->
```yaml
request:
  after:  <number>
  wait:   <integer>
answer 200:
  position: <number>
  changes:  [<message>…]
  reset:    true
message:
  n:        <number>
  key:      <s>/<rows>/<id> | <s>/<t>/<name> | platform/folders/<id> | <s>/operations/<id> | <s>/opdone/<id>/<i>
  revision: <integer ≥ 0>
  deleted:  <index> | ""
  at:       <time>
```

<!-- req: P/06.1-R275 -->
- `request.after`: optional: from which number to continue; none — the start of a subscription.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R276 -->
- `request.wait`: optional; 0 … `CHANGES_WAIT`, default 0: how many seconds to hold the answer if there is nothing to say.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R277 -->
- `answer.position`: how far the output went: the client continues from it.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R278 -->
- `answer.changes`: in the order of numbers; nothing to say — `[]`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R279 -->
- `answer.reset`: only at a reset, `changes` then `[]`; otherwise no field.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R280 -->
- `message.n`: the number of this message; good for `after`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R281 -->
- `message.key`: the full key of the row.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R282 -->
- `message.revision`: the row is live: its `revision` (N1.2, N1.6); 0 — `revision` does not read or the row has none (a folder, an operation).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R283 -->
- `message.deleted`: instead of `revision`: the row is deleted; the tombstone index of a unit or a folder, `""` — no tombstone.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R284 -->
- `message.at`: a JSON number, the rule below.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R285 -->

**Scope.** The rows of units and of declared tables of the catalogue's subsystems, folder rows ([chapter 6.8](06.8-console.norms.md)), the record and the outcomes of operations of the declaring subsystem ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md)). **Scope.** A table `service: true` and operations with `service: true` enter only the feed by a ticket (N1.6, «Filter» below); their messages do not come to people without a ticket. **Scope.** Membership rows (their change is carried by the folder row), the form `tables: [<t>]` (it has no doors), events, heartbeats and metrics do not enter: they have their own doors.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R286 -->

**Number.** A string of ASCII letters and digits, `.` and `_`, at most 128 bytes; it has no sign `-`, because the worker appends its counter to the number through `-` (V/11.5). **Number.** The number is compared only for equality; the client does not compute by it. **Number.** The number acts only at the console instance that issued it, and only for a person with the rights with which it was issued («Implementation decides» item 10).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R287 -->
- **Start of a subscription.** Without `after` the door answers at once `{position, changes: []}` with the current number; `wait` is not read.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R577 -->

**Start of a subscription.** The client takes the number, reads the rows through the doors and asks `after=<number>`. **Start of a subscription.** A message with `revision: 0` the client verifies by reading the row. Another message with `revision` not higher than the one read it drops: so an edit in the gap of the load is not lost and does not roll back what was read. **Start of a subscription.** A message `deleted` the client verifies by reading this row, because it has no `revision`, and the row could have been created anew before the read.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R288 -->

**Output.** With `after` the door gives at once all messages after it that the caller has the right to see. If there are none and `wait` is greater than 0, the door holds the answer until a tick with a message for it, at most `wait` seconds; the term ran out — `changes: []`. **Output.** `position` — how far the output went, including through messages that the caller does not see: the next request does not look through them. There were no new messages — `position` equals `after`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R289 -->
- **Reset** — `{position, changes: [], reset: true}`: the client reads all rows anew and continues from `position`, as at the start of a subscription. The door answers with a reset when:
  1. `after` is not a number of this instance: a foreign instance, a restart, a value out of form;
  2. the number is evicted from the history (`CHANGES_HISTORY`, N3 «feed»): part of the messages after it the history no longer keeps. A subscriber has no queue, so an overflow of a subscriber is this same case: it lagged behind by more than the history;
  3. the number was issued with other rights: another person, another set of acting grant rows and role assignments (issue, removal, expiry of `until`, an edit of the roles table), another `sub` of the ticket's addressee or one of two requests without a ticket: the composition of messages differs for them, while a new ticket of the same person and the same subsystem does not reset the number;
  4. after the number the field `labels` of a unit changed, and among the caller's acting rows there is a scope `labels:`: the unit could enter its `view` or leave it;
  5. after the number the composition of a folder changed or the folder was deleted, and among the caller's acting rows, direct or in roles, there is a scope `folder:`: membership is an input of the right, the unit could enter `view` or leave it, the subtree of a deleted folder is unreachable at once. Reset the feed for everyone with a row `folder:`, without redelivery.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R290 -->
- **Filter.** A message is seen by whoever the ordinary door would give this row to. The check is the function `may` with `sub` of the subsystem of the row (a folder row — without a unit, `sub` empty; the gate and pass contract «Subsystem of the check»): role groups and tiers — as at the door; `folder:` reads membership once per request; an unknown input of a group — the row is not given, like a grant that did not cover. A unit row — `view` by `scope` of the gate and pass contract: `*` and `unit:` of the unit itself are compared by the name from the key, `labels:` and `unit:` of an about-unit — by the values that the tick read
  (for a tombstone — by its values); there are no values (the row is removed or does not read) — this grant does not cover. An operation row — `view` on the operations table ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) N2), the subsystem is its own. A table row — the right `view` on the table: `need` of the door `GET /<table>`, and with `rights.unit_of` also `view` on the unit from the field of the row. The client does not see the values in the check.
  A row of a table `service: true` — only to a request by a ticket whose addressee is the subsystem of this table, and only if the field `user` of the row equals `user` of the ticket; for rows of an operation with `service: true`, instead of `user`, the `by` of the record of this operation is judged: the worker learns of its own rows, and foreign ones do not come, so `view` is not judged here. The value — from the read of the tick, for a removed row — from the memory of the tick; no field, the row does not read or there is no memory — the message does not come. To a request without a ticket these messages never come.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R291 -->

**`at`** — time by the writer's record of N0 P/01.1 «Time in a row»: the wall-clock seconds of the instance in the first successful tick that observed this version of the row. Unconfirmed listings do not create a new observation or advance its time. The store admission time is not given by the store door (P/01.1 N3 «Admission time», «read», «list»); failed ticks and different process clocks give no unconditional upper bound on that difference. **`at`**: redelivery gives the same `at`: the value is kept by the history, not by the clock of the redelivery.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R292 -->
- The right — any grant (the gate and pass contract `need`): what is visible is decided by the filter.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R582 -->
- Another method at the feed door — 405; `HEAD` — like `GET`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R583 -->
- The request of the feed takes the feed lane (chapter 6.5 N2), not the common one, so the waiting subscribers do not hinder ordinary management.

### Batch door

<!-- req: P/06.1-R016 -->

`POST /console/batch` — a write of the rows of a unit and of its parts by one store operation: all or nothing. The door is common, like `/drain`: it gets no subsystem prefix, names the rows by full keys, rows of different subsystems go together. The door does not read `If-Match`: the condition of each row is its `cas`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R295 -->
```yaml
body:
  writes:
    - op:     put | delete
      key:    <s>/<rows>/<id> | <s>/<t>/<name> | <s>/next_id
      fields: {…}
      cas:    <index> | ""
      revision: <integer ≥ 0>
  guards:
    - {key, index}
answer 200: {written: <n>}
```

<!-- req: P/06.1-R296 -->
- `body.writes`: 1 … `BATCH_WRITES` (P/01.1 N1); the order matters; a key occurs once.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R297 -->
- `writes[].key`: a unit row, a row of a declared table, the counter; `<s>` — a subsystem of the catalogue.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R298 -->
- `writes[].fields`: mandatory at `put`: for a unit — the body of POST or PUT of this row, for a table — the body of POST, for the counter — `{n: integer ≥ 1}`; at `delete` there is no field.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R299 -->
- `writes[].cas`: mandatory: the store index of the read row (P/01.1 `index`; where from — N2 «Versions»), for a tombstone — its index; `""` — no row; without the quotes of `ETag`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R300 -->
- `writes[].revision`: optional: the version of the row that a stored edit expects (P/08.6 N1.3b), 0 — no live row; diverged — 412 `revision moved`, step 5 of the procedure.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R301 -->

`body.guards`: optional; 0 … `BATCH_GUARDS`; the order matters; a key once and not in `writes`. `guards[]`: a row that is read but not written; `index` `""` — no row; form — P/01.1 N1 guard.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R303 -->
- `answer 200`: `written` — the number of rows of `writes`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R304 -->
- The body and the elements are closed: there is no other field.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R584 -->
- `put` on a read row: no row or a tombstone — a creation (N3 «create»), a live row — an edit (N3 «edit»); for a table — «table — write a row».

<!-- req: P/06.1-R585 -->

`delete`: for a unit — a tombstone (N3 «delete»), for a table — the removal of the row. The counter is not removed: `delete` on `<s>/next_id` — 400 `bad batch`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R305 -->
- Another family — `<sub>/policy`, `<sub>/idem/*`, `platform/*` except the rows of folders and membership ([chapter 6.8](06.8-console.norms.md) N2 «Folder rows in the batch door»), the rows of an operation except the outcome and the counters by a step ticket ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) N2 «Step batch»), a subsystem outside the catalogue — 400 `badkey`, `field` — `/writes/<number>/key`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R587 -->
- A table from `worker.writes` is not written by the batch door — 403; its row can be removed, like `DELETE` (N1.6 `rows`).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R306 -->

**Anchor row**. A unit of a subsystem with `about: {sub, field}` whose value of the field `field` is non-empty is a part of the unit `<sub>/<rows>/<value>`; at `delete` the value is taken from the read row. This unit MUST be a row of `writes` of the same batch: otherwise 400 `no anchor`, `field` — `/writes/<number>/fields/<field>`. So two batches over one unit always conflict, and a new part does not slip past; the `revision` of the unit grows from an edit of the part. **Anchor row**. The rule is held only by the batch door: a part created by a single `POST` does not move the anchor.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R307 -->

**Counter**. The number of a unit with `unit.id: numeric` in a batch is named by the caller itself, so its creation and the row `<s>/next_id` go only together: one without the other — 400 `bad_id`, `field` — `/writes/<number>/key`. **Counter**. `n` is the greatest number of the rows being created, and all of them are greater than the read `n` (N1.3: `n` — the last issued, a number is not used again); otherwise 400 `bad_id`, `field` — `/writes/<number>/fields/n`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R308 -->
- Limits of the batch body — N1.4.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R590 -->
- The form of the body or of an element is wrong — 400 `bad batch`, `field` — the path of the violating field; violations are judged in the order of the body: `writes` in order, then `guards`, then key repeats.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R591 -->
- The input of a row is judged as the body of its door; `field` of a row refusal is `/writes/<number>/fields/<field>` (the number from zero).

<!-- req: P/06.1-R309 -->

`412 row moved {detail, error, field, key, index}`: the first diverged row in the store order — the guards in order, then `cas` in order. `field` — `/guards/<number>/index` or `/writes/<number>/cas`; `key` — its key; `index` — its index now, `""` — no row. `412 row moved`: a guard without `view` on the row — the same refusal as for an absent row: `index: ""`.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R593 -->

`412 row moved`: a row of a table `service: true` under a ticket of this subsystem does not ask `view` (N1.6 `service`): the guard is judged by the real index, `index: ""` passes while there is no row. `412 row moved`: the guard `<s>/opbusy/<root>` ([chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) N1) is judged the same way, by the real index: a family of the door only as a guard and only under a ticket of the subsystem `<s>` with the record `calls` `<s>/operations`; it is not declared in `tables` or as a separate record `calls`; in `writes` — 400 `badkey`, for a person without a ticket — 400 `badkey`.

### repeat memory — the write doors of people

<!-- req: P/06.1-R022 -->

The repeat memory is the claim row `<sub>/idem/<key>`, one mechanism for all write doors of people: steps 1, 2 and 9 of the procedure «create», «Claim ownership» and the term of [N1.3](#n13-identifier) «Term of a done claim». <!-- req: P/06.1-R355 -->

The gate and request parsing go before the claim: their refusals do not enter the memory.

### body sweep — marks in the background, deletion in a maintenance window

<!-- req: P/06.1-R028 -->

| Phase | Conditions and action | On failure |
|---|---|---|
| mark | Every SWEEP_MARK_EVERY a full successful pass; if the current batch of digests is non-empty — do not rewrite at/the version and do not add new ones. If there is no list/it is empty — CAS of a new batch ≤SWEEP_LIMIT without references with at of the first mark | An unknown pass — mark/delete nothing |
| grace | Wait 300 s of observation of an unchanged version of the list by the local monotonic clock. The first look/a new version start from 0; put_blob can remove a mark | Repeat the read later |
| deletion admission | One operator closes the new writes of units/tables with blob, blob PUT, import/restore and incoming mirror copies; waits for the started operations. One sweeper | An unknown outcome of a writer or an unconfirmed quiet — no deletion |
| sweep | In this same window re-read all references and the list and compute the `peers` of the tombstones ([the tombstones contract](../../common/contracts/tombstone.md) «Neighbours»: the listing of doors or of the output is not confirmed — no deletion). doomed = the marked ones without references; remove the list by CAS; only then get_at and delete_at of each doomed one older than the mark — the boundary «older than the mark» only by a readable `at`. `at` absent or does not read with a non-empty batch — delete nothing from it in the window: remove the batch by CAS by the read version, the output line «`<sub>`: batch dropped: mark time unknown (`<n>`)», `<n>` — the number of digests; a dropped batch is not a failure: the window code is by the whole window (N2 «Sweep window command»); the next mark writes a new batch with `at`  | A read error/a conflict — skip the deletion, do not count the absence as proven |
| completion | Retain the `written` of each exact body version admitted to `delete_at`; before deletion compute its tombstone stamp `max(now, removed_written + 0.001)` in seconds, with exact millisecond arithmetic (the tombstones contract). Durably write those tombstones to the deleted ones (the form and `peers` — the same contract); on a partial failure keep the ban on writers until the tombstones are restored/the outcomes are determined | Do not open copying that could resurrect the deleted |
| exit | After the completion of the window return the writers/copying | The next sweep begins a new pass |

<!-- req: P/06.1-R470 -->

mark/grace/sweep of one sweeper do not overlap. In the exceptional window the other sweepers, including mark, are stopped.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R471 -->
at is the diagnostic time of the first mark of the batch, not a sliding term.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R472 -->

A decrease of the list does not add a new digest and starts a new conservative window of observing the version. A new observer of the list always waits 300 s.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R473 -->

The passes in T0+60…540 do not update a non-empty unchanged batch, so by T0+600 the grace is already waited out. New candidates get only into the next batch after the former one is emptied.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R475 -->

In the ordinary mode only mark/grace are executed, once a minute. Deletion is not an automatic action of a live installation.

<!-- req: P/06.1-R477 -->

`delete_at` and the CAS of the list prevent part of the conflicts, but by themselves do not protect from a new reference after the check: only the window without writers ensures that. The results of a pass obtained before the stop are not used for sweep.

## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **the split of the families** into rows and objects beyond N1.1, if it is different, and where the objects lie;
2. **the door body limit** (`413`); the form of `*_secret` in a row is a norm of chapter 6.2 N1.2 (the envelope), not a choice;
3. **the form of the claim row** `<sub>/idem/<key>` (the key of the row and what it remembers are a norm of N3 «repeat memory»), the storage term of done claims (not less than DONE_ANSWER_KEEP — a norm) and the way of their cleanup;
4. **the exact text** of `detail` beyond the words of N4;
5. **the periods**: the repeat limit on `conflict` at `next_id`, an edit, the deletion mark (the outcome of exhaustion is a norm of N3: 503 store unavailable at the door);
   the renewal step and the repeats of the capture and of the epoch — [P/03.2](../../03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md) «Implementation decides» item 2;
6. **the launch of the stand** for the acceptance — by the section «Stand» of the common README of the acceptance;
7. **the console body limit** (≥ 1 MiB); simultaneous bodies — exactly two (N1.4), there is no choice;
8. **the object store and its ceiling** (`OBJECTS` URI, `max_bytes`), where the tombstones lie;
9. **the name of the sweep window command** (its flag and codes — N2) and how the operator excludes all writers before it and completes the tombstones after code 3; the state beyond `{at, digests}`;
10. **the form of the feed number** (N2 «Change feed»): how it names the instance, the place in the history, the person and the set of their grant rows and role assignments. Without this the door will not distinguish reset 1 and 3: a subscriber does not live on the server between requests.
