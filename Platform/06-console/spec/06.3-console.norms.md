# P/06.3 — The gate and people: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/06.3-console.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand.

The canon of chapter 6.3 of lesson P/06 «Cluster console» (the Cluster console; module «Platform»). Lecture (`ru/06-console.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](06-console.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](06-console.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),
[acceptance](../acceptance/3/README.md). Here — who calls the console and what it may do: the gate (the states `on`, `open`,
`shut`, the identity, what each door requires, the grants, Origin, the body), the session doors `/session`, the keys `rights:` of the manifest as
the boundary of the rights of people; the late layer — the traces of the domain at the gate and «loopback — operator» only in `open` (introduced by lesson P/08).

The order of checks on each request is [chapter 6.5](06.5-console.norms.md)
N3 «request → answer (console)» (steps 3, 4, 6–8 are performed by the gate of this chapter); request parsing and the common codes are chapter 6.5
N2. The session with the grants of the domain, the entry of people and the break-glass entry by meaning are lesson P/08 ([P/08.4](../../08-domain/spec/08.4-domain.norms.md) N1.2, N1.4, N1.6, N1.10; the gate with
grants and the break-glass entry at the console are [chapter 6.7](06.7-console.norms.md) N1.9, N1.10). N1.3 `session` defines the word `via` of the `/session` answer and its values. A reference «P/0N[.K]»
is a lesson or a chapter of the module; «chapter 6.K» is a chapter of this lesson. Norm ids are `P/06.3-MM`.
