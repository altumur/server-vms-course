[contract](06-console.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](06-console.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),

the boundary of the rights of people; the late layer — the traces of the domain at the gate and «loopback — operator» only in `open` (introduced by lesson P/08).

**Batch rows.** The ticket does not widen the list through the batch door: each row of `writes` is an entry without the prefix `GET`. In a batch by a ticket, a guard of `guards` is an entry with the prefix or without it. In a batch by a ticket, the guard `<s>/opbusy/<root>` is the entry `<s>/operations`.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R050 -->

In a batch by a ticket, the rows `platform/folders/<id>` and `platform/memberof/…` are covered by the entry `folders`. In a batch by a ticket, the result and the counters of an operation are covered by the ticket of the step (chapter 6.9 N2 «Step batch»). In a batch by a ticket, a row outside the list is 401 of the whole batch, nothing is written.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R054 -->

**Door route.** The manifest `<s>` declares the door of the unit holder, `door.routes`. A request `<method> /<s>/<route>/<id>[/…]` the console passes to the live holder of the unit `<s>/<id>`: the holder and the address are as for `GET /where/<id>` ([chapter 6.5](06.5-console.norms.md) N3). The right of a door route is `view` on the unit. The journal entry of a door route is `door.issued`.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R057 -->

On a door route the worker receives the path `/<route>/<id>[/…]`, the query, the body ≤ 1 MiB, `Content-Type` and `Authorization: Bearer <door token>` for this person. The answer of a door route is the status, `Content-Type` and the body ≤ 4 MiB, as they came from the worker. The limit of a door route is `PASS_WAIT`. A door route without a live holder — 503 `{detail: "no worker of <s> with a door is heard", error: denied}`.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R061 -->
The door `/where` itself is not in the list: the address and the token stay at the console, and the ticket dies together with the request.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R062 -->
A stream ticket goes to a door route by any method: only so does a stream route call the model, and there is no write in this call.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R063 -->
**Domain doors**. An entry of `calls` opens exactly its own door.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R064 -->

The ticket goes no further than the cluster console: the console forwards the request to the domain console by the [forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md) with the original token of the external request. On a domain door by a ticket the cluster console keeps the original token of the external request only in memory, next to the ticket. On a domain door by a ticket the worker does not see the original token of the external request.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R065 -->
On a domain door by a ticket the kind of the session is `person`.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R066 -->

`GET domain`, `GET domain/<s>/<rows>`, `GET domain/<s>/tables/<table>`, `GET domain/<s>/kept/<name>`, `GET domain/relays`, `GET domain/topology` and `GET` of the entry `domain/shared` the domain console answers itself. On the domain doors that the domain console answers itself the right is [P/10](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md) N1.2a.

<!-- req: P/06.3-R067 -->
On a domain door by a ticket the process `domain` judges the right of the person as for the page ([P/08.4](../../08-domain/spec/08.4-domain.norms.md) N1.5).

<!-- req: P/06.3-R098 -->

On a domain door by a ticket `by` is the name of the person. On a domain door by a ticket the journal carries `via: <sub>/<instance>`. The cluster console sets on the forwarding of a domain door by a ticket the header `X-W2C-Via-Worker: <sub>/<instance>` ([forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md)).

**Not included:** the writes of `/session`, `/session/break-glass`, the grants of the cluster and people by other doors, keys, `/drain`, the write of `/schema`, `/mounts`, the families `<s>/idem/*`, `<s>/asked/*`, the other `platform/*` and `domain/*`.
