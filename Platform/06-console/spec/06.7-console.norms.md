### N1.1 The book door of this member

<!-- req: P/06.7-R003 -->

The door `GET /domain/<sub>/books/<book>` is at the cluster console, of a member and of the holder (N2). The door reads the book of this member:
if the cluster holds the domain (N1.4 `holds`) — the row `domain/<sub>/<book>/<CLUSTER>`, otherwise — the copy `domain/<sub>/<book>`. If the
member has no row of the book (no domain, the book has not arrived yet) — 404 `{error: "no such book shown", detail: "not delivered
yet"}`: an empty book is `""` with an empty `missing`, not an absence of the row. Fields outside `show` and secrets are never in the answer.
The segment `<book>` is compared with the book name of the manifest byte for byte: `%72olls` is not `rolls`, 404.

The door of this member does not hand out a section of the book with `to: "*"`: the copy comes on request, in a pass. `GET /domain/<sub>/books/<book>/*/<part>` is a row of the table «Domain doors», the cluster console forwards it to the holder ([P/08.5](../../08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md) N2).

The declaration of a book and `show` — [P/02.1 N1.2b](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md); the assembly at the holder and the row
`domain/<sub>/<book>/<member>` — [P/08.5](../../08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md) N1.3; the row of the book at a member —
[P/09.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.4 `member_row`. A book is observation, not authority (P/08.5 N1).
