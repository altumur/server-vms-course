the chapters of lesson [P/08](../../08-domain/README.md) (the process `domain`), the agent [P/09](../../09-agent/README.md) and the domain console
[P/10](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md). Lecture (`ru/06-console.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](06-console.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](06-console.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),
[acceptance](../acceptance/7/README.md). Here is the cluster console as the cluster's door to the domain: the book door of this member;
forwarding of `/domain/*` to the domain console (hops 1–2 of the [forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md)) and `where`
of a member; the console's TLS client to another cluster (`peer`); forwarding to the signer of the move doors; a session with grants (`may` by
the copy of the cluster's grants) and the break-glass entry; the door of key families `GET /domain/keys`; `GET /api/held` and `GET /api/backup`;
the value of a shared field at read time (`resolve`, `GET /domain/shared/<sub>`).

`identity` — [chapter 6.3](06.3-console.norms.md) N1.3 and the [gate-and-pass contract](../../common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md)
(P/06.3-03); here — `may` by grants at the guard, the order of the break-glass entry door, `GLASS_SESSIONS_MAX` and the limit of its
attempts. The forwarding table is the [forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md) «Domain doors». What the domain console does with forwarding (reception, the guard by
`domain/grants/domain`, handing to the process) is lesson [P/10](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md); what the
process `domain` executes is the chapters of lesson P/08. A reference «P/0N[.K]» is a lesson or a chapter of the module; «chapter 6.K» is a chapter of this lesson.

### N1.1 The book door of this member

<!-- req: P/06.7-R003 -->
