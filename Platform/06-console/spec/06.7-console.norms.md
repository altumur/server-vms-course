## Н1 Form

**Time.** The chapter writes no rows or objects with time. The deadline of a break-glass session `until` (Н1.10) is time by the
[canon of Н0 of P/01.1](../../../01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#time-in-a-row); a journal line (`domain.forwarded`,
`access.break_glass.*`, `domain.shared.refused`) carries `t` by the [event log contract](../../../common/contracts/event-journal.md).
The documents that the doors hand out as read (`/api/held`, `/api/backup`) carry the time of their owners.

**Data** ([catalogue](../../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):
- writes: `domain/member-want/<раздел>` (create), `<корень событий>/audit/<роль>/e1/<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ>.events.jsonl` (append);
- reads: `<sub>/heartbeats/<имя>`, `domain/<sub>/<manifest:domain.books>/*`, `domain/<sub>/<manifest:domain.books>/*/<раздел>`, `domain/root`, `domain/anywhere`, `platform/manifest/<sub>`, `domain/view`, `domain/<sub>/<manifest:domain.books>/<член>`, `domain/<sub>/<manifest:domain.books>`, `domain/member-list/<раздел>`, `domain/member`, `domain/keys`, `domain/revoked`, `domain/grants`, `domain/roles`, `domain/disabled`, `domain/break-glass/<кластер>`, `domain/holder`, `domain/backup-taken`, `domain/backup`, `domain/shared`, `domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>`.

The chapter has no rows of its own. The book door reads the holder's row `domain/<sub>/<книга>/<CLUSTER>` at the cluster that holds the

Н1.6, Н1.7). The gate with grants reads the copy of `domain/grants` and `domain/disabled`, the read condition is the may contract (Н1.9; the token — `domain/keys`,

### Н1.1 The book door of this member (М11/5 Н2)

The door `GET /domain/<sub>/books/<книга>` is at the cluster console, of a member and of the holder (Н2). The door reads the book of this member:
if the cluster holds the domain (Н1.4 `holds`) — the row `domain/<sub>/<книга>/<CLUSTER>`, otherwise — the copy `domain/<sub>/<книга>`. If the
member has no row of the book (no domain, the book has not arrived yet) — 404 `{error: "no such book shown", detail: "not delivered
yet"}`: an empty book is `""` with an empty `missing`, not an absence of the row. Fields outside `show` and secrets are never in the answer.
The segment `<книга>` is compared with the book name of the manifest byte for byte: `%72olls` is not `rolls`, 404 (added by the reconciliation of М11, B6).

The door of this member does not hand out a section of the book with `to: "*"`: the copy comes on request, in a pass. `GET /domain/<sub>/books/<книга>/*/<раздел>` is a row of the table «Domain doors», the cluster console forwards it to the holder ([P/08.5](../../../08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md) Н2; ADR-0113 addendum (26) item 2).

The declaration of a book and `show` — [P/02.1 Н1.2б](../../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md); the assembly at the holder and the row
`domain/<sub>/<книга>/<член>` — [P/08.5](../../../08-domain/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md) Н1.3; the row of the book at a member —
[P/09.1](../../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.4 `member_row`. A book is observation, not authority (P/08.5 Н1).

### Н1.2 The cluster console is the cluster's door to the domain (М11/8 Н1.1)

`domain` (lesson P/08). The chapter says how they are reached from any cluster (ADR-0061, ADR-0066, ADR-0075, ADR-0074 C5 and
the Architect's decision on the questions of М11/8).

           the worker are clients of their cluster: URL — own_url, the server check — own_client of the role certificate contract,
