### Н1.9 Callback by ticket: the closed list of doors

A worker with `door.pass` of the second kind calls the console back by the ticket of the request ([gate and pass contract](../../../common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «Передача второго вида»; ADR-0094 B1, ADR-0091 addendum (30)).
This is a separate path of identity: neither a cookie nor a token, so the gate judges it by its own order `ticket`, and with a kit it goes to a separate listener of the console with a required role leaf (the role certificate contract «Лист обратного вызова»; ADR-0094 addendum (16)), without a kit — to the socket of the role ([P/02.5](../../../02-installer/spec/02.5-installer.norms.md) Н1.5). The doors of the list are the same as at the main listener: only the identity is different. The manifest names the doors in `calls`; the check is the intersection with the closed list below. The loader rejects
`calls` outside the list ([P/02.1](../../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md) Н1.4), a door outside `calls` on a request is 401 (`ticket` step 4). Thus a captured worker does only what a translator inside the console could do, and only for people whose requests go through it now.

The prefix `GET ` at an entry `<s>/…` leaves only `GET` and `HEAD`. The path is compared without the mount prefix: with `CONSOLE_ROOT=<s>` the entry `<s>/<rows>` also covers `/<rows>`. `<s>/<rows>` at a subsystem that is not in the catalogue is not judged: there are no doors, the request is 404.

**Batch rows.** The ticket does not widen the list through the batch door: each row of `writes` is an entry without the prefix `GET`, a guard of `guards` is an entry with the prefix or without it, the guard `<s>/opbusy/<корень>` is the entry `<s>/operations`; the counter `<s>/next_id` is covered by the entry `<s>/<rows>`, because it goes in the batch when a row is created; the rows `platform/folders/<id>` and `platform/memberof/…` — by the entry `folders`; the result and the counters of an operation — by the ticket of the step (chapter 6.9 Н2 «Пакет шага»). A row outside the list is 401 of the whole batch, nothing is written.

**Door route.** The manifest `<s>` declares the door of the unit holder, `door.routes`. A request `<метод> /<s>/<маршрут>/<id>[/…]` the console passes to the live holder of the unit `<s>/<id>`: the holder and the address are as for `GET /where/<id>` ([chapter 6.5](../06.5-console.norms.md) Н3), the right is `view` on the unit, the journal entry is `door.issued`. The worker receives the path `/<маршрут>/<id>[/…]`, the query, the body ≤ 1 MiB, `Content-Type` and `Authorization: Bearer <токен двери>` for this person; the answer is the status, `Content-Type` and the body ≤ 4 MiB, as they came. The limit is `PASS_WAIT`. No holder — 503 `{detail: "no worker of <s> with a door is heard", error: denied}`. The door `/where` itself is not in the list: the address and the token stay at the console, and the ticket dies together with the request (ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 7). A stream ticket goes to this route by any method: only so does a stream route call the model, and there is no write in this call.

**Domain doors** (ADR-0098 §6, ADR-0094 addendum (11), addendum (12) items 9, 10). An entry of `calls` opens exactly its own door. The ticket goes no further than the cluster console: the console forwards the request to the domain console by the [forwarding contract](../../../common/contracts/forwarding.md) with the original token of the external request, which it keeps only in memory, next to the ticket; the worker does not see it. The kind of the session is `person`. `GET domain`, `GET domain/<s>/<rows>`, `GET domain/<s>/tables/<таблица>`, `GET domain/<s>/kept/<имя>`, `GET domain/relays`, `GET domain/topology` and `GET` of the entry `domain/shared` the domain console answers itself, the right is [P/10](../../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md) Н1.2а. The process `domain` judges the right of the person as for the page ([P/08.4](../../../08-domain/spec/08.4-domain.norms.md) Н1.5), `by` is the name of the person, the journal carries `via: <sub>/<экземпляр>`. The cluster console sets on this forwarding the header `X-W2C-Via-Worker: <sub>/<экземпляр>` ([forwarding contract](../../../common/contracts/forwarding.md)).

**Not included:** the writes of `/session`, `/session/break-glass`, the grants of the cluster and people by other doors, keys, `/drain`, the write of `/schema`, `/mounts`, the families `<s>/idem/*`, `<s>/asked/*`, the other `platform/*` and `domain/*`. The reason: doors beyond the translation of rows would give a captured worker consequences that outlive the request (ADR-0094 B1); the consequence of writing rights is accepted by the owner with the alarm `domain.rights_via_backend` (ADR-0094 addendum (12) item 10); the accounts of the first kind and the server labels outlive the request: accepted by ADR-0112 addendum (1) item 1; the mark `marks` is only a journal: accepted by ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 8.

## Н2 Doors

The doors of the chapter are the session doors: `GET`, `POST`, `DELETE /session` and `POST /session/break-glass` (Н1.3 `session`);
the forms of answers and refusals are the [lesson OpenAPI](../06-console.api.openapi.yaml). The break-glass entry in order (the door, the password, the session
term, the limit at the console) is [chapter 6.7](../06.7-console.norms.md) Н1.10; the row `domain/break-glass/<кластер>`, the hash form and the limit are lesson P/08
([P/08.4](../../../08-domain/spec/08.4-domain.norms.md) Н1.6, Н1.10) (analysis of review of stage A 8–10, № 22). The other
doors of the console ask the gate of this chapter before their own execution: what a door requires is Н1.3 `need`; the common codes, the lanes,
`HEAD` and request parsing are [chapter 6.5](../06.5-console.norms.md) Н2.

`GET /session` in every form of the answer carries the fields `server_time` and `timezone` ([gate and pass contract](../../../common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md)
`session`; ADR-0094 B6): `server_time` is the time of the clock of this console by [P/01.1](../../../01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md) Н0 «Time in a row»;
`timezone` is the name of an IANA time zone, a setting of the cluster, not of the server OS (`TIMEZONE`, [environment file contract](../../../common/contracts/env-file.md)
«Переменные окружения»). The client counts terms from the clock of the console and shows time in the zone of the cluster, so it needs neither its own clock nor
the zone of the browser. `TIMEZONE` empty or out of form is a start refusal, code 2 ([chapter 6.5](../06.5-console.norms.md) Н4).

## Н3 Procedures

The gate is executed on every request in the order of [chapter 6.5](../06.5-console.norms.md) Н3 «request → answer (console)»:
the trust `gate` — by reading on every request, without a cache, before parsing the path and the body (step 3); `/session*` — the procedure of Н1.3 after
the identity is established, with the Origin check before a change of the session and the cookies (step 4); the identity by Н1.3 `identity` (step 6); Origin
by cookie, the units from the path and the required capability — a grant or 403 with the journal (step 7); the units of the body by Н1.5 (`unit_of`,
`reach`, `names`) after the bounded read of the body, not a single change before all checks are completed (step 8). The chapter has no procedure
of its own besides this order.
