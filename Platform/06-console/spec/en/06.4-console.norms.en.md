**Data** ([catalogue](../../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):
- owns: `<sub>/requests/<rid>`, `<sub>/asked/<sha16(имя)>`, `objects/<sub>/commands/<rid>`;
- writes: `<корень событий>/console/<экземпляр>/e1/<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ>.events.jsonl` (append), `<корень событий>/audit/<роль>/e1/<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ>.events.jsonl` (append), `<sub>/requests/free-<server>-<volume>` (delete), `<sub>/<manifest:tables>/<имя>` (delete), `<корень событий или объектов>/<путь>/%h<sha256>.name` (create);

**Cursor of `GET /events`** — the parameter `from` (ADR-0094 B2): the door has no separate cursor, the client continues reading with `from` equal to `t` of the last read line, and drops the duplicates at the boundary by `id`.

**Who sees a line of `GET /events`** (C06-EVENTS-VIEW-33-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25): a line belongs to the unit of its `unit` and
to the unit `of`; a grant `view` on either of the two shows the line; a grant by labels is checked against the labels of the unit `of` if there is `of`,
otherwise of `unit`: the labels are the field `labels` of the unit row declared in `unit.fields` as a list (the same field as the input `unit.labels`
of the function `may`); no field — no labels (`[]`); the unit row is unreadable or its read is not confirmed — the labels are unknown:
the line is seen by a grant on the unit itself (or `of`) and on `*`, not by a grant by labels, and this is not a 503 of the door; to a caller with a grant
by labels such units are given in `withheld` of the answer (`ConsoleEvents`; without repeats, by bytes; none — no field)
(C06-EVENTS-LABEL-SOURCE-138-07, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-26; the merge contract step 5а); a line without a unit of a subsystem and without `of` (the journal `audit`, a console mark without `of`) — only `view` on `*`. The filter is
at the console after the merge (duplicates, epochs) and before the common cut `cut`/`limit`: with a grant narrower than `view` on `*` the console asks
the resources with `limit` = the limit 10000, the common `limit` — after the filter; `limit=1` gives the first visible line by `cut` in the window of the resource's
limit (fewer visible lines than `limit` and `truncated` > 0 — there may be more); with `view` on `*` — the same query as before;
`truncated` is the sum of the answering resources plus the visible lines beyond `limit`; the filter does not change `complete`/`incomplete` ([event journal merge
contract](../../../common/contracts/events-merge.md) «Merge» step 5а). Example: with `view unit:testsub/c1` an alarm `of=testsub/c2`
is not visible, an observation `of=testsub/c1` and a console mark `unit=console/<экземпляр>`, `of=testsub/c1` are visible.

Subsystem and groups (ADR-0097 §3, ADR-0099 §3, §5). `GET /events` filters by `sub` of each event: the subsystem of the journal tree, that
is, of the writer of the line, not the subsystem of `about`; the trees `console` and `audit` are `platform`. Both sides of the line, `unit` and `of`, are checked against
this `sub`: the alarm `testsub2/t1` with `of testsub/c1` is seen by `view sub: testsub2 unit:testsub/c1` and not seen by `view sub: testsub unit:testsub/c1`.
The field `sub` of an `audit` line and any application field `sub` of an event do not affect visibility. A direct group — as above: the line is shown by
a grant `view` on either of the two sides. In a role group `of` decides if there is one (as with labels): `view sub: testsub2 *` + `none sub: testsub2
unit:testsub/c1` does not show the event `testsub2/t1` with `of testsub/c1`. Between groups — «or». A group «нечитаема» (the rights contract) —
such units are in `withheld`, as with unreadable labels. Step 4а sees everything. The fast path «a grant narrower than `view` on `*`» is only the line `view *`
without `sub` of a direct group, a role group that has the line `view *` without `sub` and neither lines narrower than `*` in any subsystem nor lines
`none`, and step 4а; the line `view sub: testsub *` is a filter.

Refusals of the console doors of this chapter have the form of [chapter 6.1](../06.1-console.norms.md) Н2 «Refusal: `error` and `field`» (ConsoleRefusal), the words are the list [`refusal-words.tsv`](../../data/refusal-words.tsv).
RequestError is the same form at `400` of the request door: it additionally allows `fault:"too_long"` (then also `error: too_long`) only when the length
of a field by requests.schema is exceeded (`maxLength` — by the written text of the field at any type of the value: the rule of the table
[`requests-body`](../../vectors/requests-body/README.md); C03-REQ-MAXLENGTH-WRITTEN-G19); other causes get no fault. The schema is applied before
the check of the existence of the unit.

**Request parsing.** The console doors of this chapter — [chapter 6.5](../06.5-console.norms.md) Н2 «Разбор запроса консоли». The resource door — [P/05 Н2](../../../05-resource/spec/05-resource.norms.md)
«Query parsing».

The common refusals of the console by chapter 6.5: 400/401/403/404/405/408/413/429/503;
bodies ConsoleRefusal (at POST requests 400 — RequestError), at 401/403 error=denied.
The console's body limit is declared by the deployment and is not less than 1 MiB; chunked/a short body
gives 400, an excess of the size — 413, of the read time — 408. At 429 the input limit acts.
The read time must be declared before the start, not fitted to the request. A TLS refusal before HTTP
gets no invented HTTP body. Every applicable common answer is put in the OpenAPI.
At busy — Retry-After: 1. On an unknown path — 404; on an unsupported method of a known
path — 405; at the console ConsoleRefusal. This common rule is enough:
it does not create a separate OpenAPI method for each impossible operation.

`POST /marks`: unit is required and has the form of a full reference; an absent note is an empty string,
a passed note is a string. occurred is optional: the moment the operator mark is about, a number ≥ 0, with at most 3 fraction digits (P/01.1 Н0 «Time in a row»); it goes to the field occurred of the line, absent — the line has no such field (ADR-0112 addendum (13) item 7). to is optional: the end of a segment, a number of the same form; only together with occurred and not less than it, otherwise 400 refused, nothing is written; it goes to the field to of the line, absent — no field (ADR-0117 addendum (3) item 2: a VMS segment bookmark is an operator mark with to, otherwise the extra field would be lost silently). The line lies in the bucket of its t: `GET /events` with by=occurred sees an operator mark about the past at late not less than its age ([event journal merge contract](../../../common/contracts/events-merge.md) by). The form of the reference is checked, not the presence of a live unit row; the absence of the row introduces no separate 404/400 of this door. Extra fields are accepted but do not get into the event. The door requires
a grant edit on the addressee by [chapter 6.3](../06.3-console.norms.md) Н1.3. `Idempotency-Key` here does not promise a memory of repeats.
For POST requests the header is required, the safe form/size — as at the units door of [chapter 6.1](../06.1-console.norms.md);
the fingerprint, rid and the memory are the own rules of Н1.5/Н3, not a copy of pending/done of units.
The server fields rid/digest/filed/by/at/group in the client body are rejected; the only
allowed service field is valid_until. The body fields are additionally checked by
requests.schema and the stamp rules of the selected manifest, the existence — after the form.

## Н3 Procedures

### merge (the console)

the races are the [event journal merge contract](../../../common/contracts/events-merge.md) (moved there without a change of the console's
behaviour; the chapter is its owner; ADR-0091 addendum (21) item 1, addendum (23) item 4, addendum (27) item 2). The console is the participant `console`: it reads
the epoch rows of all subsystems of the catalogue, so `epoch_is: unknown` never happens at it. The answer body is `ConsoleEvents` (Н2).

### States of an executed request and its mark

The state table of filing, of the holder and of the reaper, the confirmation of the outcome, `settle` and the class of the row (an ordinary request, an append,
resource rows, elsewhere) are the [request and its mark contract](../../../common/contracts/request-mark.md) «States of an executed request and its mark» (moved there whole;
review of stage B 3–5, В1). The rows «подача …» of the table are executed by the procedure «file a request» below, «жнец …» and «clear …» —
by the procedure «reaper».

### file a request (the console, `POST /<sub>/requests`)

The body check, digest/rid, the creation of the row and the person's limit are common. The steps of reading
the mark 2/5 apply only to the ordinary profile of the state table (the request and its mark contract; review of stage B 3–5, В1). The append has none of them:
a matching or created row confirms 202 queued, a mark is not required,
the release of the accounting is by fetched/the exception rule or by the reaper's mark (`gone`, `expired`), not by an absent mark.

0. If the manifest has no requests (or the subsystem takes no requests), return 404 ConsoleRefusal; if there is no unit `unit` (no row, or it is deleted) —
   checked after the schema, step 1 — return 404 ConsoleRefusal (Н4 «request: no requests / no unit»).
1. Check Idempotency-Key (absent → 400, Н4); the body is an object with `unit`; the schema. Let digest = `sha256(JCS(the body without valid_until — the only server field that the client may send))` — kind client; at a target with requests.key — kind worker: `sha256(JCS(the fields of the row body as the door will write them — as text, without unit, valid_until and the stamps))`, as at the worker, and a repeat compares `unit` and the digest of this kind (the request contract «Просьба» digest; ADR-0091 addendum (24) item 3, addendum (25) item 3). Let rid be: by requests.key — the template `{field}` from the body (`{field:int}` — the integer part of a number, P/02.1 Н1.4; not a number → 400; deterministic: the same
   fields — one rid, create-once); otherwise `r-<sha16(Idempotency-Key)>` — the header is required at the door, there is no other branch (a random rid is at the worker that writes the row itself).
   If valid_for is set, valid_for ≤ most_valid (checked by the loader) and valid_until = now + valid_for; without valid_for the row has no deadline, ttl acts. valid_until from the client body with most_valid set is
   not later than now + most_valid, otherwise 400 too far (by the value as sent), and then it is the deadline; valid_until is any finite
   JSON number: null, a string, bool, not a number — 400; it is written by the time writer of P/01.1 Н0: truncation down to milliseconds (towards −∞),
   a negative one — 0 (1000.1234 → 1000.123, 1e-12 → 0, -1 → 0); 0 and the past are accepted, «as is» means the deadline is not shifted
   forward, not the bytes (the deadline has passed; the digest does not include the deadline; C06-DEADLINE-PRECISION-33-01); at a manifest without most_valid
   valid_until in the body — 400 «this subsystem takes no deadline» (reconciliation of python and go М10/6–11, E50, E52, E54). All refusals of step 1
   (the form, the deadline, the template) come before the refusal «no unit» of step 0 (E51); a body that is not JSON — 400 RequestError without fault (E49);
   Idempotency-Key has the form of the units door of chapter 6.1: `.` and `a,b` are allowed (E48); `{field:int}` is the canonical decimal notation:
   −0 → 0 (E53).
2. If the mark `objects/<sub>/commands/<rid>` already exists (the row could have gone by clear): the same `unit` and digest and the mark has an outcome (answer or
   no_begun) → 200 answered; the same `unit` and digest, but only begun without an outcome (being executed) → 202 `{queued: rid}`; do not touch the accounting and the row;
   another `unit` or digest → 409 spent; the mark is unreadable by its form (commands.outcomes.tsv) → 409 spent, do not touch the accounting, the row and the mark
   (stage C guess RM-G01, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-23).
3. per_person: read the accounting: an unavailable/unconfirmed read → 503; a damaged form is read
   (rids is not a list of allowed rid, wrong by/at) → 429, without a change; rid is already in rids → do not change the accounting and go on with step 4, there is no answer here;
   otherwise len(rids) ≥ per_person → 429; otherwise add rid by cas and go on with step 4; a conflict → re-read the accounting.
4. Write the row `cas ""`; a confirmed creation → step 5; it already exists: read the row, the same `unit` and digest → step 5;
   another `unit` or digest → 409 exists (do not roll back the reservation); it exists but is unreadable by its form (requests.outcomes.tsv) → 409 exists,
   do not touch the row, do not roll back the reservation (RM-G01); it vanished at the read → repeat step 4.
   A refusal or an unknown outcome of the write/read → 503; a reservation alone does not confirm the queuing of the request.
5. Re-read the mark: it appeared after step 2 with the same `unit`/digest and **with an outcome** (the holder executed it) →
   remove rid from the accounting, the answer 200 answered; leave the row to the reaper with respect to settle; the same `unit` and digest, but only begun without an outcome (the holder took my row) →
   touch nothing, 202 `{queued: rid}`; another `unit` or digest → delete only my own row by cas, keep the reservation, 409 spent;
   the mark is unreadable by its form → 409 spent, do not delete my own row (the unreadable does not permit deletion), keep the reservation (RM-G01).
6. journal → a journal line (the tree audit; it has no of — the unit of the request is in the fields sub and target, ADR-0069, so
   `/events?unit=` does not return it; review of stage B 6–7, № 35); 202 `{queued: rid}`.

Read/write errors → 503, not a success by assumption; a successfully read but damaged
accounting is a separate 429 of step 3. At any refusal after the reservation (including a definite refusal
of the creation and 409 exists) the reservation is not rolled back: a parallel repeat could have used it.
A repeat with the same rid reads the row and the mark anew; a break after the reservation before the creation is healed by
the transition from step 3 to step 4, where the absent row is created. The answer 202 is permitted only
after the confirmation of the row in step 4 and the check of the mark in step 5, or by begun of step 2. The release is only by a confirmed
outcome with the same `unit`/digest or by the maintenance below. This is a conservative limit, not
a transaction of the accounting, the row, the mark and the journal; an extra reservation can temporarily reduce availability.

**Races.** Two identical filings with one key: one rid, the creation of the row lets one through;
the second gets queued or the already known answered by Н3. With a different body
unit/digest are compared, a foreign version is not replaced. Different rid of one unit are different requests;
this lesson does not promise their global order. Filing against the reaper checks the mark again
after the creation of the row; the freshness of the row by itself does not replace this check.

### reaper (the console): every 2 s — remove the answered; every 30 s — close the abandoned

Two more jobs of the reaper with their own periods: [chapter 6.8](../06.8-console.norms.md) Н3 «folder reaper», [chapter 6.9](../06.9-console.norms.md) Н3 «operations reaper». Neither depends on the maintenance window.

- **clear:** an ordinary request: a confirmed mark with an outcome for the same `unit`/digest;
  fetched is a hint to re-read the mark, the absence/displacement of rid from the heartbeat does not cancel a confirmed outcome;
  rid in fetched is the name rid or SaidID(rid) among the comma-separated names (P/03.4 Н1.1 `said_id`);
  the ADR-0054 append: a confirmed fetched of the holder, do not read/create a mark;
  wait for the age ≥ settle by the rule of the request and its mark contract («Состояния…»; В1), confirm the current row and the ground;
  delete by cas; remove rid from the person's accounting (rids − rid, cas); unknown → leave it.
- **gone:** before the deadline branch, every 30 s: a request (an ordinary one and an append, except resource rows) whose addressee unit is absent —
  a confirmed read: there is no row `<sub>/<rows>/<unit>` or it is a tombstone — the mark gone create-once
  `{unit, digest, outcome: gone, ended_by: reaper, ended_at}` (the request and its mark contract no_begun; at an append — its
  only mark), then delete the row by cas after the age ≥ settle and remove rid from the person's accounting by cas —
  the reservation per_person is released; an unknown read — leave it (ADR-0091 addendum (11) item 4: otherwise an append to a deleted unit would
  never close); at an append settle runs from the first confirmed read of the mark gone (the request contract «Состояния…»;
  the vectors reap; C06-GONE-FETCHED-33-01).
- **expired:** after gone, every 30 s: an append (except resource rows; a body with an action from elsewhere is also an append) without a confirmed
  fetched and without a mark, the unit confirmedly exists, ttl is declared and not 0, the age of the row by the ttl rule (the request contract,
  the manifest section) ≥ ttl + REAP_AFTER — the mark expired create-once `{unit, digest, outcome: expired, ended_by: reaper,
  ended_at}` (no_begun; the second mark of an append after gone, a row has one of the two): no one serves such a request, a live
  holder closes it earlier. Further as gone: settle runs from the first confirmed read of the mark, the row is removed by
  cas, rid leaves the person's accounting by cas — the reservation per_person is released; a conflict of the mark → re-read; an unknown
  read — leave it. A new request with the same rid after the mark is accepted, and the former mark closes it through settle, not through ttl (the repeat window is shortened; the mark is not removed, the request contract «Состояния…»). A late fetched does not change the mark and does not shift the start of settle: the outcome is one, the first confirmed one
  (the request contract «Состояния…»; VMS request «срок просьбы», the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29; the vectors reap).
- **sweep:** the marks branch is only the ordinary profile; an append, elsewhere and resource rows get no marks of this procedure.
  An ordinary row with valid_until + REAP_AFTER < now or older than ttl (without a deadline):
  - no mark → the mark expired create-once `{ended_by: reaper, unit, digest from the row}` (the request and its mark contract `no_begun`; В1);
  - the mark begun without an outcome → `{outcome: unknown, ended_by: reaper, error: "begun and never answered"}` by the index;

  delete the row by cas only after a confirmed mark with an outcome and the age ≥ settle;
  a conflict of the mark → re-read; an unknown read/write → leave the row;
  remove rid from the person's accounting by cas; resource requests (the family of the key `requests/free-<server>-<volume>`, P/05 Н1.4; review of stage B 6–7, № 37) without an owner — by ttl.
  An append without fetched is not closed by a mark and not removed by age alone; its handler finishes it. The exceptions are
  the branches gone and expired above (age ≥ ttl + REAP_AFTER).
- **tables:** every 30 s: for each table of the manifest with delete_after (P/02.1 Н1.2а; ADR-0094 step 3б; the tables holds.table and placement.places.table declare no term) — list `<sub>/<t>/` (P/01.1 Н3
  «list»: key → version). The age of a row is the local monotonic age of the first observation of this version by the console:
  the store does not give the admission time of a write (P/01.1 Н3 «Admission time»), the version is opaque, and the writer's clocks do not judge the term —
  the stamp at is not read here. A new version starts the age anew. A restart of the console does too: an unknown age is not
  considered expired, the term only grows longer. Age ≥ delete_after — delete the row by cas of the observed version; the body
  of the row is not read, so an unreadable row is removed the same way. conflict → a new version, the age anew; the listing
  or the deletion is not confirmed → leave it. There is no tombstone; the journal `<t>.deleted` is written only by the door (it has the user and addr
  of the request, P/06.1 Н1.6). The reaper does not touch the rows of tables without delete_after.
- **asked:** an age ttl/settle alone does not permit removing a non-empty accounting. In the background remove a single rid by CAS
  only after a confirmed mark with an outcome of an ordinary request, or a confirmation of fetched of an append
  by the exception rule, or its confirmed reaper's mark (gone, expired); a repeated CAS re-reads the whole list,
  not losing new rid. An empty accounting row may be deleted by CAS after ttl/settle.
  A reservation without a proven outcome, including the absence of the row, is not released automatically: a late
  filing can still create the row. The administrative DELETE asked is called by the operator only in a window where all filings of this person are stopped
  and finished and their remaining requests are closed. Unconfirmed quiet — the operator
  does not call DELETE; this is an allowance of the served profile, an automatic detection of the window is not promised.

**Races.** Marks against holders (two holders, the reaper against the one who began, a repeat of filing with the same rid) — the [request and its mark contract](../../../common/contracts/request-mark.md)
«Гонки меток» (moved there; review of stage B 3–5, В1).
