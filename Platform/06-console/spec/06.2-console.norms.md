### N1.1 What a secret is

<!-- req: P/06.2-R003 -->

<!-- req: P/06.2-R024 -->

<!-- req: P/06.2-R028 -->

<!-- req: P/06.2-R029 -->
- `mask_in`: recognition of the mask at the input is the log mask contract `mask_in`; it gives a refusal.

### N1.2 The envelope and the key ring

<!-- req: P/06.2-R005 -->

The envelope `enc:v2`, the AAD path, the grammar of `kid` and the form of the key ring file (`line`, `mode`) are the common
[sealing contract](../../common/contracts/sealing.md) (vectors — `sealing`).
Here — who holds the key, rotation, the keyless mode and the fingerprint.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R033 -->
- `ring_file`: the file form is the sealing contract «Ring file».

<!-- req: P/06.2-R034 -->

`ring_file.holders`: the group `w2c-secrets`: the console, the workers of subsystems whose manifest has secret fields or `secrets.readers` with the role worker, the domain signer, the domain agent. `ring_file.holders`: the controller, the resource, the domain console — without a key.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R036 -->

`rotate`: a new line on top → a restart of the holders (the worker at start reseals its list table, P/03.4 N1.6 `list_secret`) → the console at start reseals its rows under the current kid (cas, revision does not grow) → `secrets seal` seals the rest → `status`. `rotate`: all old keys are kept.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R038 -->

`no_key.unset`: `SECRETS_KEY` is not set → development mode. `no_key.unset`: the development mode comes with a loud log line. `no_key.unset`: reading/PUT of open values are kept. `no_key.unset`: PUT with a non-empty `*_secret` writes the value open.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R040 -->

`no_key.unset`: POST of creation with a non-empty `*_secret` passed at any depth is prohibited before the claim (400, N4). `no_key.unset`: PUT and a table write with `Idempotency-Key` with a non-empty `*_secret` passed at any depth are prohibited before the claim the same way (400, N4): the fingerprint of such a body needs the ring (`print` below).

<!-- req: P/06.2-R042 -->
- `no_key.unset`: `SECRETS_KEY=""` (set empty) is not development mode, but `unreadable` below.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R043 -->

`no_key.unreadable`: set, but does not read → all POST/PUT of units are refused with 503 `unavailable` (N4), including a body without a secret field: the ring is also needed for the HMAC of the replay memory. `no_key.unreadable`: a table write with `Idempotency-Key` is refused with 503 `unavailable` the same way. `no_key.unreadable`: a set but unreadable ring is not the keyless mode. `no_key.unreadable`: GET/DELETE are not blocked by the refusal of an unreadable ring.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R046 -->
- `no_key.open_without`: P/03.5 N1.2 (the worker's side).

<!-- req: P/06.2-R047 -->
- `no_key.row_check`: the same three states at the common row check — the row check contract «Preparation of fields», «Ring boundary» (the ring state is an option of the caller).

<!-- req: P/06.2-R048 -->

`print`: the idempotency fingerprint of a body with a secret is the HMAC of the original request under a key derived from the ring (`hmac:<kid>:<hex>`). `print`: on a replay — the kid of the saved claim, not the current key.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R050 -->

`print`: without a ring a write with a claim and a non-empty secret is outside the profile. `print`: the mask and a bare hash of a secret body do not replace the HMAC.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R052 -->
The message of open_without is [P/03.5 N1.2](../../03-worker/spec/03.5-worker.norms.md).

<!-- req: P/06.2-R053 -->

Rotation is run by one operator. During rotation ordinary writes of secrets are stopped for the time of the ring change, the resealing and the final check `status`. Concurrent `rotate` and a manual edit of the ring are excluded by the maintenance conditions.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R055 -->
Removal of former keys is wholly outside the profile of the
chapter: zero old envelopes in status does not allow removing a key. It can be needed by
a live pending/done claim with `hmac:<kid>:…` and by a copy that status does not list.
This is a narrowing of the scope of the command, not a promise of a transactional switch of all processes.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R056 -->

A transition to an unset ring with saved HMAC claims is also outside the profile. The development mode without a ring is begun with a new empty claim memory.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R057 -->

Rotation does not change already saved fingerprints. Rotation does not try to restore the original request from a changed unit.

## N3 Procedures

<!-- req: P/06.2-R008 -->

### create/edit a unit row, secrets included (console; a procedure of chapter 6.1)

<!-- req: P/06.2-R009 -->

Before step 2 of creation of chapter 6.1, after the check of the header and of the JSON object, check the profile: with an unset ring and a non-empty `*_secret` passed at any depth, POST gives 400 without creating/removing a claim, reserving a number or writing the unit. With an unset ring and a non-empty `*_secret` passed at any depth, `PUT` and a table write with `Idempotency-Key` give 400 the same way. The check of an unset ring is a check of the input body, not of the masked answer.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R068 -->

With a ring present, the order of the form, the HMAC and the claim ownership of chapter 6.1 remain. With a ring present, replays are checked under the kid of the saved fingerprint.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R069 -->

A set but unreadable ring is checked after parsing the JSON object and before the HMAC/claim/write for any POST/PUT of a unit; the result is 503 of N4. This is an earlier check of the same condition than the sealing step below; there may be no secrets in the body. Bad framing/a non-object before the check of an unreadable ring gives its own 400.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R071 -->

1. After the bounded read and parsing of the JSON object: the key is set but does not read → 503 unavailable (N4), before the HMAC, the claim and the field checks; a PUT without `Idempotency-Key` has no claim — before the field checks and the write. In step 1 the state of its own claim follows the 5xx rules of chapter 6.1 N3. In step 1 a foreign claim is not removed.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R072 -->

2. Refusals of the form (chapter 6.1 N3, step 4) come before refusals of the secret value: first the type and the url by the field rule → bad_url, then an envelope value → 400, then a mask value → 400; the first refusal in this order names the cause. In step 2 a nested `*_secret` is a string or null; anything else (a number, bool, an object, an array) → 400 before the write.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R074 -->

3. Edit: an empty, null or absent top-level `*_secret` → the former value. In step 3 a field from bound_to changed with an empty secret → 400 «must be given anew» — whether the stored row has a secret or not. In step 3 the former secret never goes to a new address (checked inside cas against the row as stored). In step 3 the rule is one for the unit door and the table door (chapter 6.1 N3 «table — write a row»: POST over a row with the same name is an edit).

<!-- req: P/06.2-R077 -->

In step 3 a json field with nested `*_secret` is replaced whole by an edit: a nested secret of the incoming value is its own, there is no keeping «by index» or by key from the stored value. In step 3 a nested `*_secret` that came empty, null or without the key is without a secret and is stored as it came (the answer — "", null or without the key), but never the secret of a neighbouring or former element; the answer to a nested null is null.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R079 -->

4. Walk the non-empty `*_secret` with the full path from the root of the row, including inside the value of a json field at any depth; seal with the single AAD of N1.2 (the answer masks them at the same depth, the worker opens by the same path). In step 4 an envelope already — as is, do not move it to another path. In step 4 the full path of a secret inside arrays includes the index.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R081 -->

5. Write the row. In step 5 the answer is with the mask. In step 5 the copy for a replay is with the mask.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R082 -->
**Races.** Two edits of one row: `cas` (chapter 6.1); `bound_to` is checked inside the mutator against the row as stored — a change of the field and a change of the password in two edits at once: one passes, the second re-reads, sees the new field and without a secret gets 400 «must be given anew».

<!-- req: P/06.2-R083 -->
Opening of the envelope by the worker (`start_unit`) is [P/03.5 N3 «open»](../../03-worker/spec/03.5-worker.norms.md).

### reseal at start (console)

<!-- req: P/06.2-R010 -->

What is done with one value (the walk of fields, the current and the former kid, an open value, enc:v1, not by the grammar, an unknown kid,
a tag that does not open) is the [sealing contract](../../common/contracts/sealing.md) «Reseal a value» (vectors `reseal.tsv`); here — which rows, `cas`, races.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R084 -->

For each row of units and tables of all manifests of the catalogue where there are fields `*_secret` (these are all the rows that the console writes: the worker table from worker.writes declares no `*_secret` fields — P/02.1 N1.2a; the list form is resealed by the worker itself — P/03.4 N1.6 list_secret): `*_secret` at any depth, including inside the value of a json field, open or under an old kid → seal with the current one, cas by the one read. In the rows of units and tables resealed at start, do not change revision. In the rows of units and tables an enc:v1 value — do not touch and count as «?» (a dead format).

<!-- req: P/06.2-R086 -->
- The row door/signer (chapter 6.5 N3 «`/where/<id>`»), if it exists: seed_secret open or under an old kid → seal with
  the current one, the same cas by the version read.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R087 -->

A row that does not read (the value of a json field is not JSON, the row is out of form) → skip: the row does not change; the others are resealed. A row that does not read at resealing: a log line with the key and the cause. A row that does not read at resealing does not interrupt the console start.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R088 -->
- After the pass — its own signer kid in door/keys: chapter 6.5 N3 «`/where/<id>`».

<!-- req: P/06.2-R089 -->
The walk also passes the rows of operation steps `<sub>/opsteps/*`: [chapter 6.9](06.9-console.norms.md) N3 «resealing».

<!-- req: P/06.2-R090 -->

**Races.** Two consoles reseal the same thing at start: `cas` by row — the second sees the new `kid` and writes nothing. **Races.** Resealing against an operator's edit — `cas`, the loser re-reads.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R136 -->
**Races.** Resealing of `door/signer` against its creation at the first issue — `cas`, the loser re-reads.

### Ring change: maintenance states

<!-- req: P/06.2-R011 -->

The admission conditions are N1.2: one operator, ordinary writes of secrets are stopped. In a ring change the CAS of resealing stays required. The table of maintenance states below introduces no new fields on disk.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R092 -->
| State | Action | Result and refusal |
|---|---|---|
| The former ring is available, maintenance is admitted | Check the ring; rotate adds a new kN on top, keeps the old keys | A check error leaves the file as before; former envelopes read with the old keys |
| The new file is installed, holders may have an old copy | Restart the holders with the new ring; then the console | Do not start resealing before the holders are ready; a restart error leaves the maintenance unfinished |
| The holders know both keys | The console at start and seal walk the rows with CAS by N3 | A conflict — re-read; a read/write error or an unknown outcome is not equal to completion |
| The walk is finished | status checks the same coverage | Keep all former keys, also for pending/done HMAC and copies; zero envelopes does not allow removal |

<!-- req: P/06.2-R093 -->

After a break the operator continues from the confirmed state, keeping the old keys. Automatic recovery of the maintenance process is not part of the chapter.

### read_address(value, field rule, catalogue) → display | refusal(field)

<!-- req: P/06.2-R012 -->

**Races.** Two edits of one row: `cas` of chapter 6.1; the binding check is inside `cas` against the row as stored, so «the former password stayed with the new address» is impossible even in a race.

<!-- req: P/06.2-R095 -->
A key change and the holders: the holders are restarted before the console, otherwise a worker with the old ring will not open what was resealed.

## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **who else holds the key** beyond N1.2 and how the key gets into the console process (`LoadCredential`, a mount, a variable); into the worker process — [P/03.5](../../03-worker/spec/03.5-worker.norms.md) «Implementation decides» item 1;
2. **the derived key of the fingerprint** of idempotency and the string under the HMAC; the original request includes the identity/method/path/body by chapter 6.1, the mask does not substitute the body. The fingerprint lives only in the claim row of its own console and MUST be stable across its restart and across a change of kid: a replay uses the kid of the saved claim. A claim with a fingerprint whose form the console does not recognize (a claim of a console of another implementation) it neither removes nor compares: a replay under the same key gets 503 by the rules of a foreign claim of chapter 6.1 N3 for as long as the claim is alive; two consoles of different implementations on one store are outside the profile while the form of the fingerprint is not common;
3. **how the console opens the envelope `door/signer`**; the form of `door/signer` and `door/keys` and the bytes of the door token are [chapter 6.5](06.5-console.norms.md) N3 «`/where/<id>`»;
4. **the log mask**: which other places of text are masked beyond N1.4;
5. **on platforms without groups** (macOS) — what replaces `w2c-secrets` and `InaccessiblePaths`.

The behaviour of `secrets status`/`seal` without manifests in the store and the form of their output are [P/02.4](../../02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md)
«Implementation decides» items 5, 6.
