## Implementation decides

The implementation is to name in its description:

1. **who else holds the key** beyond N1.2 and how the key gets into the console process (`LoadCredential`, a mount, a variable); into the worker process — [P/03.5](../../03-worker/spec/03.5-worker.norms.md) «Implementation decides» item 1;
2. **the derived key of the fingerprint** of idempotency and the string under the HMAC; the original request includes the identity/method/path/body by chapter 6.1, the mask does not substitute the body. The fingerprint lives only in the claim row of its own console and MUST be stable across its restart and across a change of kid: a replay uses the kid of the saved claim. A claim with a fingerprint whose form the console does not recognize (a claim of a console of another implementation) it neither removes nor compares: a replay under the same key gets 503 by the rules of a foreign claim of chapter 6.1 N3 for as long as the claim is alive; two consoles of different implementations on one store are outside the profile while the form of the fingerprint is not common;
3. **how the console opens the envelope `door/signer`**; the form of `door/signer` and `door/keys` and the bytes of the door token are [chapter 6.5](06.5-console.norms.md) N3 «`/where/<id>`»;
4. **the log mask**: which other places of text are masked beyond N1.4;
5. **on platforms without groups** (macOS) — what replaces `w2c-secrets` and `InaccessiblePaths`.

The behaviour of `secrets status`/`seal` without manifests in the store and the form of their output are [P/02.4](../../02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md)
«Implementation decides» items 5, 6.
