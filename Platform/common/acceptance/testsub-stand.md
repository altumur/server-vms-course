# Testsub process stand

A topic of the [common acceptance README](README.md): the directives and doors are its [dictionary](README.md#dictionary-of-directives-and-doors).

The stand of role processes on one server over the stand manifests `testsub` and `testsub2`: one store, one console and the controllers, workers and resources that the steps start. A lesson README that uses this stand references this section and writes only its own preparation, roles and exceptions. The section introduces no norm of a product.

- **Each scenario on a fresh stand.** Before the first step of a scenario the judge stops every process of the previous scenario and waits for the exit of each, including an earlier copy of a name and a process that was stopped and started again; only then does it remove their state. The scenario gets a new data directory `{data}` and new ports, so no rows, objects, events, presence locks or caches of the previous scenario remain: a new store with an empty id memory, a new console with no POST claims, no bound names `$name` and no replay keys of the judge. The judge starts the store, installs the stand manifests with their service marks by the command `manifest-put` in the order of «Manifests» of the [stand](stand.md#layout-by-processes-stage-c), then starts the console; their doors are ready before the first step. The preparation is not a transfer of the state of the previous scenario and not a check of the installer. A lesson README that keeps the store between the scenarios of one table says so in its own boundaries (P/06.2).
- **No foreign processes.** There are no controllers, workers, resources, spare processes or start timers that the steps of the scenario do not start; a role started before the first step is named by the lesson README (the resource of P/03.6).
- **One box.** Every process has the stand box `BOX_ID` and `SERVER_NAME=srv-a`, except by the words `on <server>` and `<NAME>=<value>` of `@start`; each start is a new instance. The parameters and deadlines of the stand manifests are the values of the manifests: the judge does not accelerate them for the test.
- **Stop.** `@sigterm` and `@sigkill` signal the process group of the named live process of the scenario and match `ok` only after the whole group has exited, by N3.2 of the [norms](spec/acceptance.norms.md) (R017; judge-only: the norms of the judge). A stop whose term expires, or a name that names no live process of the scenario, is a mismatch of the step, not `ok`.
- **Time of a scenario.** A lesson README sets no budget of a scenario: the budget is the sum of the waits of its steps plus the reserve of the judge (N3.3, R036), and the judge does not end a scenario before the waits of its steps run out.
- **Parallel scenarios.** Scenarios that run at the same time each use their own data directories and ports.
