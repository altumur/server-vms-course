`domain/groups/<имя>` — `at`, the values of `joined`; `domain/roles/<имя>` — `at`, `assigned[].until`, `assigned[].at`.

**Data** ([catalogue](../../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):

- reads: `domain/keys`, `domain/revoked`, `domain/signer`, `domain/signer-turn`, `domain/names/<sub>/<книга>`.

The revocation window at a member without connection is two limits, separately (the Architect's decision on R41-13 of run 41):
- **an already issued token** of a disabled person or a revoked one — `TOKEN_LIFETIME + TOKEN_SKEW` = 960 s from issue;
- **a grant** removed at the holder, at a member without connection — `GRANT_LIFETIME` (a day) from `as_of` of the copy: a person who was not

still let him in. The process `domain` at start and at every pass of chapter 8.1 (Н3 «проход держателя») reconciles: `domain/disabled`
— by the flags of the person rows; for each mark `domain/deleting/<имя>` — it removes the grant lines with subject = `<имя>`, then
deletes the mark; it does not wait for the name indexes; the divergence lives no longer than a pass. The reconciliation does not touch the person row: a mark with
a still readable `domain/users/<имя>` (a break of step 9 before its deletion, the mark written directly — sc. 09) — only the grants and
the mark are removed, the person stays without grants, the deletion is finished by a repeated `DELETE` (C08-DELETING-USER-ROW-01). It does not look for «people who do not exist»
by the absence of a row: a subject of a name index is not a person of the domain either (log of cammeta-go, М11-09 № 1; the Architect's decision).

A subject without a name (C08-GRANTS-ORPHAN-01; the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29 on the request of VMS, analysis of V/03, question 2). By the same pass, after the name indexes of the pass (chapter 8.5 Н3
«сборка книги»), the process `domain` removes the grant lines in all `domain/grants/*` whose `subject` is not a person of the domain (there is no row
`domain/users/<subject>`) and not a key of any name index — only when all indexes are known (chapter 8.5 Н1.7; a catalogue without
books with `domain.names` — all are known and empty) and the list of people is read: the indexes of this pass are not built (a failure of a part of the pass, chapter 8.1 «Гонки»; the window after a taken term, chapter 8.5 Н1.3), an index is not known, the list of people or an index row is not
read (the store did not answer) — the reconciliation of this pass does not look for subjects without a name (П1, C08-PASS-PART-02). The person row exists but
is unreadable — the person exists: his grants are not removed. A grants row does not read — it is skipped, the reason goes into the journal. The removal is
CAS of the grants row by the read version; a conflict — reread and repeat at most three times, the rest — by the next
pass; the journal without values. While a line of such a subject stands, the name is taken for `POST /domain/users` (step 7а of the people door):
a new person with this name does not get the grants of the former subject. Subjects that the norms remove themselves (the mark `domain/deleting/`) — as before.

New kinds (ADR-0098 §5). By the same pass the process removes the grant lines `role:R` if there is no row `domain/roles/R`; the elements of `members` and
`assigned` with a person who does not exist, and of `assigned` with a group that does not exist. The rule of the pass is the former one: the lists of people, groups and roles
are read whole and successfully — otherwise this pass looks for nothing (П1); a row exists but is unreadable — «exists», nothing is removed; CAS,
three repeats, the rest — by the next pass; the journal without values. **While a restore or a move of the domain is not finished, the reconciliation of the new
kinds removes nothing**: an empty list after an incomplete restore is not a ground.

**Expiry** (ADR-0098 §3, ADR-0100 §2). At every pass the process writes into `domain/disabled` the names with an expired `expires`, except the case: after that
not a single proven administrator would remain (Н1.3) or the proof is unknown (unreadable rows). Then the person stays
enabled, the journal `domain.user.expiry_kept {name}` once per pass. A proven administrator is unlimited, so in the normal state
the rule does not fire: it is a protection against an anomaly.

**Reconciliation of the `admin` role** (ADR-0100 §1). The pass writes `domain/roles/admin` only at a confirmed absence of the row and a non-empty `domain/users` (before `first-admin` it does not write the row); it does not
touch an unreadable row, the journal once per pass. Creating it, it assigns the role once (`until: 0`) to each person with an effective unlimited direct line;
it leaves the line to him. No proven administrators — the journal `domain.no_admin` once per pass.
