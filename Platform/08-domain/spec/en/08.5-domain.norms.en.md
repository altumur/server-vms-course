### Н1.4 Delivery to a member

```yaml
carry:        the answer of the holder (P/08.3 Н1.5) carries the books in sealed (the envelope m1) in the field books — the form: the domain
              envelopes contract «Содержимое sealed» (analysis of review of stage A 8–10, № 12)
```

A book `to: "*"` goes not into `sealed` and does not reach a member without a request (ADR-0113 addendum (11) item 1). A reader declares `domain/<sub>/<книга>/` in `worker.reads` and puts the request `domain/book-want/<sub>/<книга>/<раздел>`; the agent holds copies of these parts under the same key `domain/<sub>/<книга>/*/<раздел>`, the copy is grown by the rules of the member, not by the domain. The publication names `book_parts` — the versions of what it holds, and `null` for a request without a copy, `carry.book_stale` — what is stale or has appeared: the comparison is as for `members_rev`, a version for the whole book would raise all readers on every change. A wide member carries a window of `BOOK_PARTS_SHOWN` 64 parts by round, only they are judged: the cost is constant, the lag of a wide member is minutes; after a takeover all parts are stale, 4096 with `BOOK_PARTS_PASS` 8 per pass — about 43 min: a limit. A request lives by a term: the reader renews `at` once per `BOOK_WANT_EVERY` 86400 s while the part is needed, the agent considers it removed at `now − at > BOOK_WANT_TTL` 259200 s and removes the row itself: readers share the row of the part, so it is not for any of them to remove it. The agent takes it by the door `GET /api/book/<член>/<sub>/<книга>[/<раздел>]` ([chapter 8.3](../08.3-domain.norms.md) Н2), without a part — the head; there are no secrets in them. A reader puts a request on any cluster; the agent of the holder's cluster does not execute it: the keys are the same, the holder's row is read. A change of the term requires nothing from the reader: the rows of the former holder become copies, are judged through `book_parts`, rows without a request are removed by the agent. A reader puts requests at its start too: agent clocks that jumped forward remove requests at once, this is a limit.

The book row at a member (`member_row`: the elements as received, the secrets are envelopes of the member's ring; the writer is the agent; the cluster
of the holder has no copy: the holder does not send books to itself in `sealed` and reads its own book from the row
`domain/<sub>/<книга>/<себя>`, ADR-0074) — [chapter 9.1](../../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) (Н1.4
`member_row`). The envelope `m1` — the [domain envelopes contract](../../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) «Конверт `m1`».

```yaml
             absent or damaged»;
           a new grant row whose subject is not a person of the domain, the indexes are known, and there is no such key in any index →
             400 unknown subject; grant rows that were in the former row are not checked (log of cammeta-go, М11-09 № 1; a decision of the Architect); a grant row of a person of the domain (and root) does not wait for the index: the names exclude each other
             (exclusive_with), a person is never a key of an index
           a catalogue without books with domain.names — there are no indexes: all are known and empty; a new grant row whose subject is not a
             person of the domain — 400 unknown subject, not 503 (a decision of the Architect on cammeta-python М11-09 № 21)
           removal of a person and removal of rows — never wait
reverse:   an element of a book with names whose key is the name of a person is delivered to nobody: the field of the view view.books.<sub>/<книга>.person_names
           [<key>…] ascending (Н1.5, added by М11/9); it is absent from the report of the function assemble — its input is without people;
           a person here is a name from the list domain/users, an unreadable row of a person is a person too; the list is not read — a book with names in
           this pass is not assembled, its rows and person_names are the former ones, the log without the value: the name of a person does not go to members (C08-REVERSE-UNREADABLE-01)
delivery:  Н1.3 grant — the recipient has a grant row with subject = key, rank(cap) ≥ rank(grant), valid (the right contract, step 5, sub is the subsystem
           of the book, from domain/names/<sub>/<книга>; only direct rows); grant too wide compares only the rank: a row with sub is narrower than a row without it (ADR-0097 §4)
```

### Н1.8 Shared folders (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 3, ADR-0111 item 13)

A folder is one per domain, the grants `folder:<id>` live at all clusters. A unit row stands on a member, so there is no single batch for the pair «folder + membership»: the folder row and the membership row are at the holder, the target of membership is judged by the guard, the copy at the member is read-only. Without a link to the domain neither a folder nor membership is edited: a refusal, not a divergence that would later have to be merged.

## Н3 Procedures

### assembly of a book (the holder, in the pass of chapter 8.1 Н3)

```
1. for each assembled book of the catalogue: the members of the list (P/08.3 Н1.1) with their view state and copy
2. a member silent | never | without a copy → missing (why — the state or no_copy), no contribution; the mark exists but its generation is not
   read (P/08.3 Н3 «прочитать копию») — no_copy, although the view calls such a member silent: the reason is closer — there is no copy
   (the function assemble: copies[member] = null — no_copy, the vector assemble-08-states; C08-MARK-NO-COPY-01)
3. a member ok | unseen: the objects of the copy by from of the book; unseen → into missing (why: unseen)
4. each input: check the form of the input (the domain envelopes contract «Вклад» entry; an object in a report — P/09.1 Н1.2 in_report) for all
   elements of the contribution of the member; an error → bad_contribution: the whole contribution of the member is removed;
   then the secrets — open (the domain envelopes contract «Конверт x25519:v1», seed — domain/book-seal), did not open →
   secret_unopened: the whole contribution is removed
   (the form before the secrets: an element with a secret that did not open and an element not by the form — bad_contribution; added by the
   reconciliation of М11, B2)
4а. under unique — the elements of one key at one member: equality by JCS of the value with the secrets opened (envelopes of one value differ): equal ones — one input, the item smallest in bytes of UTF-8; unequal → bad_contribution: the whole contribution is removed. One key under two slots is an ordinary thing: a new first worker writes while the slot of the former one is still held, and there is no reason to remove the contribution for that; a limit: the value changed in this window — the contribution is removed until the row of the former slot goes away (C08-BOOK-SAME-ITEM-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, Quint V/03.6 В1)
5. keys: unique — one contribution per key, otherwise a conflict (no key); all — all
6. for each member of the list: the elements where it is in to; grants (Н1.3 grant, Н1.7); the row domain/<sub>/<книга>/<член> by Н1.3 result
7. write the changed rows (secrets — with the ring of the holder), remove the rows of former members; view.books — Н1.5, too_large — by the outcome of this step
```

After the assembly of the books of the pass — the name index of each book with `domain.names` (Н1.7 `domain/names/<sub>/<книга>`).

### delivery (the holder, in the answer to the agent of chapter 8.3)

```
holder:    read domain/<sub>/<книга>/<член> of each book of the catalogue, open the secrets with the ring → sealed.books (m1);
           a secret did not open — as an unreadable row: do not carry this book to this member in this pass, the reason to the log without the value, the other books go (C08-BOOK-SECRET-UNOPENED-01)
```

The record at a member (the agent: open `m1`, each book — into `domain/<sub>/<книга>` by CAS, a book that is not in the answer is not
touched) — [chapter 9.1](../../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) (Н3 «the write at the member»).

**Races.** The assembly reads the copies at different moments (chapter 8.1): a book is an observation. The capture of a slot and the write of a worker under the guard —
[P/03.2](../../../03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md) Н3: a late write of a former holder is fenced off by the guard, a surviving row — by the cleanup at capture and by the liveness filter
of the agent. Two assemblies of one book do not intersect: there is one holder ([P/08.8](../08.8-domain.norms.md)). A break at step 7 of the assembly — part of the rows of the book of the new
pass, part of the former one, `view.books` the former: the next pass reassembles from the copies and writes the differences (a write only
on a change); the delivery before that carries the row as is — the member has the book of one of the two passes, not a mix inside a row.
