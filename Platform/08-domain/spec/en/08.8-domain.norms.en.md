### ask-sig — the ask signature

The messages `backup` and `prepare`, the form of `X-W2C-Time`, `X-W2C-Signature` and the order of the check (`missing`, `bad_time`, `skew`,
`bad_signature`) — the [ask signature contract](../../../common/contracts/ask-sig.md) (owner — this chapter; moved there
without a change; review of stage A 8–10, № 13 / decision 2). Vectors — [`common/vectors/ask-sig`](../../../common/vectors/ask-sig/README.md).
The process `domain` signs `backup` at a move (Н1.8 step 5) and `prepare` at a handover (Н1.7 step 4) and checks
`prepare` at the target (Н2 `/api/prepare`).

The agent's following of the holder (Н3 «follow» of the source, the function `follow`) and the check of the backup copy by the keeper —
[P/09.1](../../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н3 «follow», «check the backup copy».

**Races.** Two records of one term at two holders happen not only at simultaneous moves, but also when the holder
of this term did not answer the move (Н1.8 step 5): the members follow the one whose issuer is in their follow set (the set of the
later move is newer and does not know the former issuer), and the holder in whose store a record of its own term
by another holder has appeared yields (Н1.6 yields); step 3 «никуда» (P/09.1 Н3 «follow») — only when both records pass record by one set,
the operator sorts it out (added by the Quint model of М11, G4). A move onto a cut-off member with a smaller term and a new set
does not take the members back (SET_GUARD, the key set contract «Набор следования»): it stays a lonely holder of a smaller term (G7) and does not yield by itself: its set has revoked the issuer of the chain of handovers, a record of a larger term does not pass record at it, and the neighbour's set is older (Quint М12 Н1). Both sides raise the alarm `domain.term_diverged` (P/09.1 Н3 «follow» step 2а), the way out — a move with why onto a member that does not hold the term: the term and the set are above both (P/09.1 Н4). Handover and
reports: the holder in the freeze accepts reports; the target takes only the backup copy named by the grant. A move in two places at once — each
has its own new term; the members follow the larger one, and the smaller one yields at the first meeting of its record if the set of the larger one is newer; otherwise — as above, a lonely holder and an alarm. A late answer
of `/api/held` of the old holder the agent does not take (`term` smaller than its own). A break of a handover: the holder fell before step 5 of Н1.7 —
there is no handover; the freeze is held by the process, and it leaves with it; the target's prepared key serves once and is not good for the next
handover. After step 5, before step 6 — the target has a record of a larger term: the members follow it (P/09.1 Н3 «follow» step 3),
the former one yields at the first meeting of its record (Н1.6). A break of a move before `claim` (Н1.8 step 8) — there is no term, no new set and no new signer keys: `domain/keys` and `domain/signer` are written in a batch with claim, the target stays a member and applies the answers of the live holder; the holder of the exception of step 3 stays the holder of its term with the former set, keys, seed and pointer of the shared document, the rest of its state is already the copy's; a retry of the move begins with step 1, the restore writes over (C08-KEYS-CLAIM-BATCH-01, C08-SEAL-COPY-BATCH-01).
