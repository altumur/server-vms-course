## N1 Form

<!-- req: P/08.10-R002 -->

**Time.** The row of the chapter has no time fields: the age of the turn is the monotonic clock of the process over the version of the row
`domain/signer-turn` (N3 «the turn of the domain process» step 3).

**Data** ([catalogue](../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):
- owns: `domain/signer-turn`;
- writes: `domain/member-rows/<part>` (cas), `domain/view` (cas), `domain/members/<member>/reported` (cas), `domain/members/<member>/tables/<sub>/<table>` (cas), `domain/alarm-history/<member>` (cas);
- reads: `domain/signer`.

The turn row is written only by the `domain` process (N1.7 `rights`). The ceilings of N1.5 are checked by every write of `domain/member-rows/<part>`
(the owner of the row is [chapter 8.3](08.3-domain.norms.md)); the objects `domain/view` ([chapter 8.1](08.1-domain.norms.md)), the mark
`reported` (chapter 8.3) and `domain/alarm-history/<member>` ([chapter 8.9](08.9-domain.norms.md)) the process writes by the rule N1.7
`objects`; `domain/signer` (the holder keys, [chapter 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md)) is re-read by a process that became active before
every signature (N1.7 `active`).

A member reports by itself ([chapter 8.3](08.3-domain.norms.md)): the domain opens no connections to it, silence is the age
of the mark by the holder's clock. The chapter introduces no new form of a member. It names what the domain costs on hundreds of small members,
sets the ceilings and fixes one `domain` process at the holder.

### N1.7 One `domain` process at the holder

<!-- req: P/08.10-R004 -->

```yaml

            common/contracts/turn.md (TURN_TERM, TURN_RENEW); own — CAS of the read version (N3 step 2), a foreign one unchanged
            ≥ TURN_TERM — take;
            holder of the row — the process instance <box>:<pid>:<6 hex> (the instance form — the instance contract, common/contracts/instance.md)
release:    on SIGTERM the active process gives up the turn — holder "" by CAS of its own version, as the controller at the end of a pass
            (the turn contract step 6); a standby one takes it at the next TURN_RENEW. A killed one (SIGKILL) does not give up the turn — it is taken
            by TURN_TERM
guard:      every write of the domain process to a row of the holder's store — of the pass, of a report admission, of an edit through the domain console
            (chapter 8.0 N1.8), of any chapter — goes with the guard {key: domain/signer-turn, index: my version}; the guard refused —
            the process stops being active. The domain console does not write the holder rows, it does not need the turn row
objects:    the objects of P/05 have no guard, and none is introduced:
            - an object with a pointer row is written without a guard, and the pointer, a row under the guard, makes it effective:
              domain/shared-docs/rev-* — domain/shared (chapter 8.9), the backup copies domain/backups/rev-* — domain/keepers/* (chapter 8.8); rev-* of a copy is written by put_new: a deposed one does not overwrite the object of the new active one, the object exists — a conflict
            - a mutable object without a pointer row — domain/view, domain/view-rows/*, domain/shadow and domain/members/<member>/tables/* (chapter 8.1), the mark reported with the generations under it (chapter 8.3),
              domain/alarm-history/* (chapter 8.9) — every write of such an object, both of the pass and of the admission door (generation, delta, mark — each): the process confirmed
              the turn no earlier than TURN_RENEW ago (`fresh_turn`) and writes put_at by the index of the object (the object store client contract «Operations» ops) in the order: get_at of the object →
              its own CAS confirming the turn for this write, freshness does not replace it → put_at by the index read BEFORE the confirmation; the new active one wrote the object
              after our confirmation — our put_at gets conflict: do not write, re-read the turn; before our
              confirmation — the confirmation itself does not pass (get_at after the confirmation
              would leave a window — a pause between them would give the index of a write already made by the new active one, and put_at would overwrite it)
window:     the residual window: an object that the new active one has not touched yet, the put_at of the deposed process lets through — such a
            write is the last one anyway, the next pass of the turn holder corrects it (the view — by recomputation, the mark — by the next
            admission, the history — by appending); put_at does not write over a write of the new active one
standby:    a process without the turn is standby: it runs no pass, each of its doors answers
            503 {error: "standby", detail: "the domain process of this cluster is <instance>"} — before reading the body, then
            closes the connection; <instance> — its own, of the standby one that answered (the instance contract): the turn holder in detail

no_signer:  the turn is for the whole process and without a condition on domain/signer: the process takes and confirms domain/signer-turn even without

            domain/signer (absent or unreadable) it does not take the holder role (chapter 8.2 «the process domain does not found the domain»,

fresh_turn: a door of the domain process answers on the merits only if the process confirmed the turn (N3 step 2 or 3) no earlier than
            TURN_RENEW ago; otherwise — confirm it now (N3 step 2) or answer 503 standby; so a process
            frozen longer than TURN_TERM (SIGSTOP) and woken up does not answer 200 next to the new active one.
            The limit: a process stopped between the check and the answer can answer 200 after the loss
            of the turn — the answer window of a door is not bounded; the keys are the same, the harm is «two answer»
```

### N1.9 Cost

<!-- req: P/08.10-R005 -->

A descriptive norm by the constants of chapters 8.1, 8.3, 8.8, 8.9 and of lesson P/09; it has no new rules. The site — 300 members,
the agent pass `REPORT_EVERY` 5 s.

| What | How much | Where |
|---|---|---|
| holder admissions | 300 / 5 = 60 per second: a signature, a CAS of the mark or of a generation, an answer | the `domain` process |
| writes of a quiet site | no generations (only `ts` changes); the mark — 300 / 22.5 ≈ 13 object writes per second | the holder's object store |
| a change of a member | a generation: 3 objects (`reports/<rev>`, `.delta`, `reported`) and 2 deletions | the same place |
| the holder's answer | units of KB; the list of members (~300 B per member, 90 KB) — only to one that fell behind (chapter 8.3, P/08.3-10) | the channel to the members |
| view | the head `domain/view` once per `VIEW_EVERY` s, the rows `domain/view-rows/<part>` in a round of `VIEW_MEMBERS` | the holder |
| the backup copy of chapter 8.8 | once per `BACKUP_EVERY` s, on a change, at `BACKUP_HOLDERS` keepers | the holder, the keepers |
| the alarm front of chapter 8.9 | one read of the index row per second | at each member, not at the holder |
| home writes at a member | only on a difference ([P/09.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.4 `home_write`, `fresh`); freshness marks (`as_of` of grants, `domain/reached`) — once per `FRESH_EVERY` s, about 1,440 of each per day | the member's store |

The form of the rows and objects that the chapter owns by the [catalog](../../common/data/catalog.tsv) is the JSON Schema 2020-12 of the family (ADR-0092). An instance of a row is `{key, row}`, of an object `{key, object}`; the key is the grammar of the column `key` of the catalog. ## N3 Procedures

<!-- req: P/08.10-R007 -->

### the turn of the domain process (at start and every TURN_RENEW)

<!-- req: P/08.10-R008 -->

1. Read `domain/signer-turn`. If it is unreadable, become standby and write the log.
2. If `holder` is this instance, rewrite the row by CAS of the read version (only the instance itself writes under its name); on a conflict, become standby.
3. If there is no row, CAS "" with `holder` = me; if `holder` is "" (given up, N1.7 release), CAS of the read version with `holder` = me;
   if `holder` is foreign and its version has not changed for ≥ TURN_TERM by my monotonic clock, CAS of the read version with `holder` = me.
   On success, become active: the holder pass (chapter 8.1 N3 and the following ones) with the guard of N1.7 guard; otherwise, become standby.
4. If `holder` is foreign and fresh, become standby.
5. On SIGTERM at the active process, write `holder` "" by CAS of its own version (N1.7 release), then exit.

An active process whose write guard refused becomes standby at once and does not repeat the write. A write with a guard without
an answer (an unknown outcome) is like the barrier of [the writer turn contract](../../common/contracts/turn.md) «Barrier»: it does not count as a success, the pass is interrupted; the process re-reads
`domain/signer-turn` and decides anew by steps 1–4 — the turn is its own (holder is this instance) → CAS of the read version
(step 2), success → active, the interrupted work is done by the next pass by what is read anew; otherwise → standby. An unknown outcome
of a write of the turn row itself (a take or a renewal applied, the answer lost) is the same: the row names this instance with
a version it did not know, and step 2 by the read version confirms it; the only process does not stay standby.

**Races.** Two processes take the turn after it is given up by CAS of one read version: one passes, the second is standby.
SIGTERM of the active one between a renewal and a write with the guard: the write carries the guard of its version and is either accepted before the giving up or
refused after it (the version changed) — there are no two writers. A standby one became active while a write of the former one is in flight: it carries
the guard of the former version and is refused (N1.7 guard). Objects without a guard are written only after the turn is confirmed (N1.7).
