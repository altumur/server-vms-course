### N1.5 Ceilings

<!-- req: P/08.10-R003 -->
```yaml
ENTRY_MAX: forms/runtime-constants.tsv; bytes with class, url ≤ 256, via, batch;   # bytes of JCS Entry
MEMBERS_MAX:   <the ceiling of a section row>
revoked_keys:  <the keys of Entry.revoked_keys>
every_entry:   <all Entry of the written row>
whole:         <the whole generation> ≤ BODY_LIMIT (16 MiB)
objects:       <the object ceiling of P/05>
idem:          domain/idem/<key>
  IDEM_KEEP:   forms/runtime-constants.tsv      # s
  IDEM_MAX:    forms/runtime-constants.tsv      # rows
```

<!-- req: P/08.10-R025 -->
`ENTRY_MAX` — bytes of JCS Entry — the worst case of the profiles: by — the name of a person, 64 characters of 4 bytes (256); key 64; ca 2 × 64; revoked_keys 4 × 64; how ≤ 43 («held the domain until term» and 16 digits); since ≤ 24 characters of a JSON number; keys and JCS markup. The bytes are with class, url ≤ 256, via, batch. `ENTRY_MAX` — a write after which the Entry is larger than `ENTRY_MAX` is not written: 409 «the entry of `<member>` would exceed ENTRY_MAX bytes». `ENTRY_MAX` — a row of 524288 bytes holds ≈ 420 Entry of the worst size; the average at 250,000 members — 61.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R029 -->
`MEMBERS_MAX` — a limit of a section row, not of the domain: the ceiling of P/01.1 N1; the domain — ≈ 1.4 million at an average of 350 per row. `MEMBERS_MAX` — `SERVERS_MAX` is counted by a separate count `domain/member-count`; the holder is in the count (P/08.3 N1.1a). `MEMBERS_MAX` — keep `SERVERS_MAX` equal to `MISSING_SHOWN` (P/08.5 N1.3 result): raised alone, it would take silent servers beyond the cut list, while the readers judge servers.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R031 -->
`revoked_keys` — at most the 4 latest; the oldest — the first in the array — is pushed out (chapter 8.3 N1.1). `revoked_keys` — a report with a pushed-out key: on a write with key — 403 «the entry has no key or the key does not match» (chapter 8.3 N2); on a write without key — a knock, the admission refuses by revoked_members of the set (chapter 8.3 N3 step 4).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R033 -->
`every_entry` — any write of `domain/member-rows/<part>` (admission, leaving, the holder in the list, claim, Entry.ca, an administrator's edit) checks all Entry of this row, not only the changed one: revoked_keys of each — at most the 4 latest, then an Entry larger than ENTRY_MAX — 409 «the entry of `<member>` would exceed ENTRY_MAX bytes», nothing is written (a large Entry seeded past the admission would otherwise stay in the list). `every_entry` — for Entry.ca after an accepted report (chapter 8.3 N1.6 on_accept), an Entry larger than ENTRY_MAX is not 409: the report is accepted, nothing changes, the log.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R035 -->
- `whole` — the whole generation (chapter 8.3 N1.3 whole') ≤ BODY_LIMIT (16 MiB): larger — 413 {error: "whole too large", detail}, the mark is not touched (the report acceptance contract «accept» step 4a).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R036 -->
- `objects` — the view (chapter 8.1) and the alarm history (chapter 8.9) are objects: the object ceiling of P/05, not rows.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R037 -->
- `objects` — the books (chapter 8.5) are rows under the row ceiling of 524288 bytes (P/01.1 N1); the outcome of assembling a book larger than the ceiling — chapter 8.5 N1.3 `write`.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R038 -->
- `idem` — the repeat memory of the edit doors (chapter 8.0 N1.9): the rows `domain/idem/<key>` are under the two numbers `idem.IDEM_KEEP` and `idem.IDEM_MAX`.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R039 -->
`idem.IDEM_KEEP` — s: the row lives at least this long, as P/06.1 N1.3 «Term of a done claim»: a new edit takes a new key. `idem.IDEM_KEEP` — the row is removed by the holder pass (chapter 8.1 N3) with the turn guard, by the local monotonic age from the first observation of the row version. `idem.IDEM_KEEP` — a new process counts the age anew: the term is only longer.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R042 -->
- `idem.IDEM_KEEP` — an unreadable row `domain/idem/<key>` is removed the same way as a readable one: by the holder pass with the turn guard, by the monotonic age.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R045 -->
`idem.IDEM_MAX` — rows: an edit every 21 s around the clock. `idem.IDEM_MAX` — the count is the listing of the pass plus own writes after it. `idem.IDEM_MAX` — at the ceiling a new key — 503 {error: "unavailable", detail} before the pass of the door, nothing is written. `idem.IDEM_MAX` — at the ceiling a key with a ready row answers from the memory, a door without a key works: the ceiling protects the store, not the edits.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R047 -->
The lines `body` and `SAY_EVERY` (the agent: a delta at `base > 0`, does not send a whole larger than `BODY_LIMIT`, no repeat of the whole after 413)
— the agent side, [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md)
N1.2 `body`.

### N1.7 One `domain` process at the holder

<!-- req: P/08.10-R004 -->
```yaml
turn:       domain/signer-turn
release:    SIGTERM → holder ""
guard:      {key: domain/signer-turn, index: <my version>}
objects:    <a pointer row under the guard | put_at after the turn confirmation>
window:     <the residual window of put_at>
standby:    503 {error: "standby", detail: "the domain process of this cluster is <instance>"}
no_signer:  <the turn without a condition on domain/signer>
fresh_turn: <the turn confirmed no earlier than TURN_RENEW ago>
active:     <domain/signer re-read before every signature>
url:        <an address that reaches any node of the cluster>
term:       <one turn, not one server>
rights:     <the role domain>
```

<!-- req: P/08.10-R048 -->
`turn` — domain/signer-turn is the turn row of the domain process at the holder's cluster; the algorithm — the writer turn contract common/contracts/turn.md (TURN_TERM, TURN_RENEW). `turn` — own — CAS of the read version (N3 step 2), a foreign one unchanged ≥ TURN_TERM — take. `turn` — holder of the row — the process instance `<box>:<pid>:<6 hex>` (the instance form — the instance contract, common/contracts/instance.md).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R051 -->
- `release` — on SIGTERM the active process gives up the turn — holder "" by CAS of its own version, as the controller at the end of a pass
  (the turn contract step 6).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R052 -->
- `release` — a standby process takes the given-up turn at the next TURN_RENEW.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R053 -->
- `release` — a killed process (SIGKILL) does not give up the turn — it is taken by TURN_TERM.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R054 -->
`guard` — every write of the domain process to a row of the holder's store — of the pass, of a report admission, of an edit through the domain console (chapter 8.0 N1.8), of any chapter — goes with the guard {key: domain/signer-turn, index: my version}. `guard` — the guard refused — the process stops being active.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R056 -->
- `guard` — the domain console does not write the holder rows, it does not need the turn row.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R057 -->
- `objects` — the objects of P/05 have no guard, and none is introduced.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R058 -->
`objects` — an object with a pointer row is written without a guard, and the pointer, a row under the guard, makes it effective: domain/shared-docs/rev-* — domain/shared (chapter 8.9), the backup copies domain/backups/rev-* — domain/keepers/* (chapter 8.8). `objects` — rev-* of a backup copy is written by put_new: a deposed one does not overwrite the object of the new active one, the object exists — a conflict.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R060 -->
`objects` — a mutable object without a pointer row — domain/view, domain/view-rows/*, domain/shadow and `domain/members/<member>/tables/*` (chapter 8.1), the mark reported with the generations under it (chapter 8.3), domain/alarm-history/* (chapter 8.9): at every write of such an object, both of the pass and of the admission door (generation, delta, mark — each), the process confirmed the turn no earlier than TURN_RENEW ago (`fresh_turn`) and writes put_at by the index of the object (the object store client contract «Operations» ops) in the order: get_at of the object → its own CAS confirming the turn for this write, freshness does not replace it → put_at by the index read BEFORE the confirmation. `objects` — the new active one wrote the mutable object after our confirmation — our put_at gets conflict: do not write, re-read the turn. `objects` — the new active one wrote the mutable object before our confirmation — the confirmation itself does not pass (get_at after the confirmation would leave a window — a pause between them would give the index of a write already made by the new active one, and put_at would overwrite it).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R064 -->
`window` — the residual window: an object that the new active one has not touched yet, the put_at of the deposed process lets through — such a write is the last one anyway, the next pass of the turn holder corrects it (the view — by recomputation, the mark — by the next admission, the history — by appending). `window` — put_at does not write over a write of the new active one.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R066 -->
`standby` — a process without the turn is standby: it runs no pass. `standby` — each door of a standby process answers 503 `{error: "standby", detail: "the domain process of this cluster is <instance>"}` — before reading the body, then closes the connection. `standby` — `<instance>` in detail — its own, of the standby one that answered (the instance contract): the turn holder in detail is not named — with an unreadable or given-up turn it is unknown.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R069 -->
`no_signer` — the turn is for the whole process and without a condition on domain/signer: the process takes and confirms domain/signer-turn even without the signer row (the target of a handover and of a move is a cluster without the holder keys, chapter 8.8). `no_signer` — without a readable domain/signer (absent or unreadable) the process does not take the holder role (chapter 8.2 «the process domain does not found the domain», the outcomes of domain/signer): there is no holder pass. `no_signer` — without a readable domain/signer, POST /api/prepare, /api/take and /domain/move answer on the merits in their own order (chapter 8.8 N1.7, N1.8: root, set, signature, record — checks of the doors themselves), their writes — under the turn guard (guard). `no_signer` — without a readable domain/signer, the other doors of the process — 503 standby, as at a standby one.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R072 -->
`fresh_turn` — a door of the domain process answers on the merits only if the process confirmed the turn (N3 step 2 or 3) no earlier than TURN_RENEW ago; otherwise — confirm it now (N3 step 2) or answer 503 standby; so a process frozen longer than TURN_TERM (SIGSTOP) and woken up does not answer 200 next to the new active one. `fresh_turn` — the limit: a process stopped between the check and the answer can answer 200 after the loss of the turn — the answer window of a door is not bounded; the keys are the same, the harm is «two answer».

<!-- req: P/08.10-R074 -->
- `active` — a standby one that became active re-reads domain/signer (the holder keys, chapter 8.2 N1.3; forget of chapter 8.8 N1.6) before
  every signature — on every pass and every request; it does not keep what it read at start.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R075 -->
- `url` — the url of the record domain/holder (chapter 8.8 N1.1) at a holder of several nodes — an address that reaches any node
  of the cluster, not one server.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R076 -->
- `term` — a holder on one node is «one turn», not «one server».

<!-- req: P/08.10-R077 -->
- `term` — a change of node inside the cluster is not a move: the term does not change.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R078 -->
- `rights` — domain/signer-turn is written only by the role domain (its family domain/*).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R079 -->
The line `agent` (503 `standby` at the agent — «the holder is unavailable for this pass», at the next pass) — the agent side,
P/09.1-19 ([chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.12 `agent`).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R080 -->
The answer of a standby process at each door of the process is the summary table of [chapter 8.0](08.0-domain.norms.md) N2.

## N3 Procedures

### the turn of the domain process (at start and every TURN_RENEW)

<!-- req: P/08.10-R008 -->
1. Read `domain/signer-turn`. If it is unreadable, become standby and write the log.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R083 -->
2. If `holder` is this instance, rewrite the row by CAS of the read version (only the instance itself writes under its name); on a conflict, become standby.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R084 -->
3. If there is no row, CAS "" with `holder` = me; if `holder` is "" (given up, N1.7 release), CAS of the read version with `holder` = me;
   if `holder` is foreign and its version has not changed for ≥ TURN_TERM by my monotonic clock, CAS of the read version with `holder` = me.
   On success, become active: the holder pass (chapter 8.1 N3 and the following ones) with the guard of N1.7 guard; otherwise, become standby.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R085 -->
4. If `holder` is foreign and fresh, become standby.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R086 -->
5. On SIGTERM at the active process, write `holder` "" by CAS of its own version (N1.7 release), then exit.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R087 -->
An active process whose write guard refused becomes standby at once and does not repeat the write.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R088 -->
A write with a guard without
an answer (an unknown outcome) is like the barrier of [the writer turn contract](../../common/contracts/turn.md) «Barrier»: it does not count as a success, the pass is interrupted; the process re-reads
`domain/signer-turn` and decides anew by steps 1–4 — the turn is its own (holder is this instance) → CAS of the read version
(step 2), success → active, the interrupted work is done by the next pass by what is read anew; otherwise → standby.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R089 -->
An unknown outcome
of a write of the turn row itself (a take or a renewal applied, the answer lost) is handled the same way as a write with a guard without an answer: the row names this instance with
a version it did not know, and step 2 by the read version confirms it; the only process does not stay standby.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R007 -->
**Races.** Two processes take the turn after it is given up by CAS of one read version: one passes, the second is standby.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R090 -->
SIGTERM of the active one between a renewal and a write with the guard: the write carries the guard of its version and is either accepted before the giving up or
refused after it (the version changed) — there are no two writers.

<!-- req: P/08.10-R091 -->
A standby one became active while a write of the former one is in flight: that write carries
the guard of the former version and is refused (N1.7 guard).

<!-- req: P/08.10-R092 -->
Objects without a guard are written only after the turn is confirmed (N1.7).
