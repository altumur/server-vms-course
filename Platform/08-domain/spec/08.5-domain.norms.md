# P/08.5 — Books and the name index: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/08.5-domain.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 8.5 of lesson P/08 «Process domain» (the process `domain`; ADR-0080; module «Platform» — ADR-0083). Lecture (`ru/08-domain.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](08-domain.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),

`domain/names/<sub>/<book>`, its checks at the doors of people and grants and the delivery of a key by a grant.

### N1.4 Delivery to a member

<!-- req: P/08.5-R004 -->

```yaml
carry:        the answer of the holder (P/08.3 N1.5) carries the books in sealed (the envelope m1) in the field books — the form: the domain
              envelopes contract «Content of sealed» (analysis of review of stage A 8–10, № 12)
mask:         the value of a book secret gets neither into a door (the book door of the cluster console, the OpenAPI of lesson 6), nor into the log, nor into the view
              (P/06.2 N1.4)
```

A book `to: "*"` goes not into `sealed` and does not reach a member without a request (ADR-0113 addendum (11) item 1). A reader declares `domain/<sub>/<book>/` in `worker.reads` and puts the request `domain/book-want/<sub>/<book>/<part>`; the agent holds copies of these parts under the same key `domain/<sub>/<book>/*/<part>`, the copy is grown by the rules of the member, not by the domain. The publication names `book_parts` — the versions of what it holds, and `null` for a request without a copy, `carry.book_stale` — what is stale or has appeared: the comparison is as for `members_rev`, a version for the whole book would raise all readers on every change. A wide member carries a window of `BOOK_PARTS_SHOWN` 64 parts by round, only they are judged: the cost is constant, the lag of a wide member is minutes; after a takeover all parts are stale, 4096 with `BOOK_PARTS_PASS` 8 per pass — about 43 min: a limit. A request lives by a term: the reader renews `at` once per `BOOK_WANT_EVERY` 86400 s while the part is needed, the agent considers it removed at `now − at > BOOK_WANT_TTL` 259200 s and removes the row itself: readers share the row of the part, so it is not for any of them to remove it. The agent takes it by the door `GET /api/book/<member>/<sub>/<book>[/<part>]` ([chapter 8.3](08.3-domain.norms.md) N2), without a part — the head; there are no secrets in them. A reader puts a request on any cluster; the agent of the holder's cluster does not execute it: the keys are the same, the holder's row is read. A change of the term requires nothing from the reader: the rows of the former holder become copies, are judged through `book_parts`, rows without a request are removed by the agent. A reader puts requests at its start too: agent clocks that jumped forward remove requests at once, this is a limit.

The book row at a member (`member_row`: the elements as received, the secrets are envelopes of the member's ring; the writer is the agent; the cluster
of the holder has no copy: the holder does not send books to itself in `sealed` and reads its own book from the row
`domain/<sub>/<book>/<self>`, ADR-0074) — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) (N1.4
`member_row`). The envelope `m1` — the [domain envelopes contract](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) «Envelope `m1`».

### N1.8 Shared folders (ADR-0110 addendum (5) item 3, ADR-0111 item 13)

<!-- req: P/08.5-R007 -->

A folder is one per domain, the grants `folder:<id>` live at all clusters. A unit row stands on a member, so there is no single batch for the pair «folder + membership»: the folder row and the membership row are at the holder, the target of membership is judged by the guard, the copy at the member is read-only. Without a link to the domain neither a folder nor membership is edited: a refusal, not a divergence that would later have to be merged.

The form of the rows and objects that the chapter owns by the [catalog](../../common/data/catalog.tsv) is the JSON Schema 2020-12 of the family (ADR-0092). ## N3 Procedures

### assembly of a book (the holder, in the pass of chapter 8.1 N3)

<!-- req: P/08.5-R010 -->

```
1. for each assembled book of the catalogue: the members of the list (P/08.3 N1.1) with their view state and copy
2. a member silent | never | without a copy → missing (why — the state or no_copy), no contribution; the mark exists but its generation is not
   read (P/08.3 N3 «read the copy») — no_copy, although the view calls such a member silent: the reason is closer — there is no copy
   (the function assemble: copies[member] = null — no_copy, the vector assemble-08-states; C08-MARK-NO-COPY-01)
3. a member ok | unseen: the objects of the copy by from of the book; unseen → into missing (why: unseen)
4. each input: check the form of the input (the domain envelopes contract «Contribution» entry; an object in a report — P/09.1 N1.2 in_report) for all
   elements of the contribution of the member; an error → bad_contribution: the whole contribution of the member is removed;
   then the secrets — open (the domain envelopes contract «Envelope x25519:v1», seed — domain/book-seal), did not open →
   secret_unopened: the whole contribution is removed
   (the form before the secrets: an element with a secret that did not open and an element not by the form — bad_contribution; added by the
   reconciliation of M11, B2)
4a. under unique — the elements of one key at one member: equality by JCS of the value with the secrets opened (envelopes of one value differ): equal ones — one input, the item smallest in bytes of UTF-8; unequal → bad_contribution: the whole contribution is removed. One key under two slots is an ordinary thing: a new first worker writes while the slot of the former one is still held, and there is no reason to remove the contribution for that; a limit: the value changed in this window — the contribution is removed until the row of the former slot goes away (C08-BOOK-SAME-ITEM-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, Quint V/03.6 V1)
5. keys: unique — one contribution per key, otherwise a conflict (no key); all — all
6. for each member of the list: the elements where it is in to; grants (N1.3 grant, N1.7); the row domain/<sub>/<book>/<member> by N1.3 result
7. write the changed rows (secrets — with the ring of the holder), remove the rows of former members; view.books — N1.5, too_large — by the outcome of this step
```

After the assembly of the books of the pass — the name index of each book with `domain.names` (N1.7 `domain/names/<sub>/<book>`).

```
           a secret did not open — as an unreadable row: do not carry this book to this member in this pass, the reason to the log without the value, the other books go (C08-BOOK-SECRET-UNOPENED-01)
```

pass, part of the former one, `view.books` the former: the next pass reassembles from the copies and writes the differences (a write only
