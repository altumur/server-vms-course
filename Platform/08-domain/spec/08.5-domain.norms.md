### N1.3 Assembly at the holder

<!-- req: P/08.5-R003 -->
```yaml
inputs:           <the copy of each member of the list>, <its view state>
contributes:      ok | unseen
missing:          [{member, why}]
  why:            unseen | silent | never | no_copy | bad_contribution | secret_unopened
bad_contribution: → missing
secret_unopened:  → missing
pick:
  unique:         one contribution per key
  all:            all contributions
result:           domain/<sub>/<book>/<recipient>
  "":             JSON text {missing, missing_count}
  <key>:          JSON text {entries: [{member, item, value}]}
    member:       <member>
    item:         <slot>/<item> | <item>
    value:        <the input object>
  order:          entries by (member, item)
  at_rest:        enc:v2
  write:          rewrite | remove
recipient:        <member> | "servers"
grant:            domain.names.<book>/.grant
cadence:          <each pass of the holder>
to_all:           domain.books.<book>.to = "*"
  parts:          domain/<sub>/<book>/*/<part>
  head:           domain/<sub>/<book>/*
  stale:          [<member>/<server>…], stale_count
  version:        (term, rev)
  round:          <the round of the view parts>
  book-keys:      domain/book-keys/<sub>/<book>/<member part>: {"": {term}, <member>: [<key>…]}
  unique:         pick: unique → a catalogue refusal
  first:          503 unavailable
  first_pass:     <from scratch>
  order:          <the rows of the parts>, <book-keys>
  ceiling:        524288 bytes; NEAR_CEILING
```

<!-- req: P/08.5-R031 -->
`inputs`: the copy of each member of the list (P/08.3 N3 «read the copy»), including the holder (its signed
report), and its view state (chapter 8.1 N1.3).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R032 -->
`contributes`: a member ok or unseen (there is a copy) contributes; silent, never, no copy — does not contribute. `contributes`: for the first LOST_AFTER from `at` of the term record taken by takeover or handover (term ≥ 2; now − at ≤ LOST_AFTER by the wall clock of the process, `at` is not changed by a restart, chapter 8.8 N1.1 `claim`), there is no assembly: book rows and the name index of N1.7 are not written, `view.books` is empty; never means «not heard yet», and a book without contributions would replace the full one at the readers.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R034 -->
`missing`: the rule in one phrase: **what is missing from the book because of a member — that member is in
missing**.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R035 -->
`missing`: class server first, inside by member.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R036 -->
`missing`: one per book.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R037 -->
`missing.why`: a closed list of words. `missing.why`: unseen both contributes and is in missing: the answer is incomplete until the change is seen (chapter 8.1). `missing.why`: a member has one missing record: unseen with a removed contribution — why is the reason of the removal (bad_contribution, secret_unopened), not unseen.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R040 -->
`bad_contribution`: the input is not an object or not JSON, there is no key or it is not a non-empty string, to is not a name, not a list of names and not "servers", for a book to: "*" — a field *_secret at any depth, unequal elements
of one key at one member under unique (equal ones are one input, N3 step 4a) → the whole contribution of the member in this book is removed, the member is in missing.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R041 -->
`secret_unopened`: a secret of the contribution did not open (the domain envelopes contract «Envelope x25519:v1»; including a ring envelope that the member did not open, — not_v1) → the whole contribution is removed, the member is in missing. `secret_unopened`: the value is not revealed.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R043 -->
`pick.unique`: exactly one contribution per key in the whole domain; two or more — the key is absent from the book (a conflict, diagnostics N1.5).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R044 -->
`result`: a row at the holder domain/\<sub>/\<book>/\<recipient> for each member of the list (including the holder).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R045 -->
`result.""`: missing — the first MISSING_SHOWN: class server first (Entry, no field — server), inside by member, missing_count — all: readers judge servers, cameras are cut. `result.""`: keep MISSING_SHOWN equal to SERVERS_MAX (P/08.10 N1.5), otherwise a silent server would go beyond the list. `result.""`: missing — only here and with truncation: on each key and without truncation it multiplied the row by the number of keys, and with ~11,000 silent ones a single "" would be larger than the ceiling at all members. `result.""`: reader: missing_count greater than the length of missing — it does not know about a member outside the list.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R049 -->
`result.<key>`: only the elements where the recipient is in to.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R050 -->
`result.<key>.member`: the contributing member (from the origin of the report, not from the input).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R051 -->
`result.<key>.item`: \<slot>/\<item> for a family, \<item> for a single row.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R052 -->
`result.<key>.value`: the input object as is (secrets — opened and sealed with the ring of the holder, below).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R053 -->
`result.order`: entries — by (member, item) in bytes of UTF-8; keys of the elements — arbitrary (a store row).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R054 -->
`result.at_rest`: the secrets of value — envelopes of the ring of the holder enc:v2 (the sealing contract), AAD: the row key 0x00 «/\<row item>» +
the JSON Pointer of the secret in the JSON text of the element.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R055 -->
`result.write`: rewrite a row only if its open content changed. `result.write`: the row of a former member — remove. `result.write`: an assembled row larger than the row ceiling of 524288 bytes (P/01.1 N1) is not written: the former one stays, the log domain.books.too_large {sub, book, member} once per pass and view.books.too_large (N1.5), the other rows, books and the pass go on.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R058 -->
`result.write`: the book in the door path and in the row key — byte for byte, without percent decoding.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R059 -->
`recipient`: an element is delivered to each member from to that is in the member list. `recipient`: "servers" — to the members of the list of this pass whose Entry is class server (no field — server), a camera receives nothing and does not go into undelivered. `recipient`: a name outside the list receives nothing in any form — it gets only into the diagnostics of N1.5 (undelivered).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R062 -->
`grant`: a book with domain.names.\<book>/.grant (N1.7) — a key is delivered to a recipient only if it has a valid grant row with subject = key and a rank not lower than grant (the right contract, step 5, sub is the subsystem of the book: a row admin sub: testsub does not deliver a key of the book testsub2; only direct rows: rows role: and the role table do not take part, the key role:ops is delivered to nobody; the input of assemble does not receive the table). `grant`: for a book with domain.names.\<book>/.grant, without a grant the recipient sees only "" (missing). `grant`: for a book with domain.names.\<book>/.grant, a key that is the name of a person is delivered to nobody (N1.7 reverse).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R065 -->
`cadence`: the assembly — in each pass of the holder (chapter 8.1 N3), after the copies are read.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R067 -->
`to_all`: the manifest declares the book to all: domain.books.\<book>.to = "*" (P/02.1 N1.2b). `to_all`: the field to of the input is not read.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R068 -->
`to_all.parts`: a row per part of the key: domain/\<sub>/\<book>/*/\<part>, the part is 12 bits of SHA-256(UTF-8 key), as for members (P/08.3 N1.1); \<key> — {entries}, "" — {missing: [], missing_count: 0, term, rev}. `to_all.parts`: ≈ 500 keys per part: a limit, above the row ceiling — write above. `to_all.parts`: there is nothing per recipient in the row, so a book to: "*" has no secret and no grant.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R071 -->
`to_all.head`: the row domain/\<sub>/\<book>/* — only "": {missing, missing_count, term, rev, stale?, stale_count?}, the completeness of the book. `to_all.head`: missing and missing_count — as in result: otherwise silent cameras would overflow the row.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R073 -->
`to_all.head`: the head is written at the end of a round, on a difference, and on a change of the class server set in missing — by the pass of the holder, without waiting for the end of the round: at the end of a round a fallen cluster is seen by the spare after 175–300 s with 250,000 cameras against the ring of 66 s, by the pass — after about 53 s with any domain. `to_all.head`: the first head of a term — at the end of a round begun not earlier than at of the term record + every of the term + TURN_TERM: a camera reports once per every, the round is shorter, and a member not heard yet would deprive the readers of live routes, and a part walked before the term would lose the reports after it.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R075 -->
`to_all.head`: TURN_TERM 45 s (the turn contract) — the readiness window: a slot in the window 503 standby does not lose its route. The limit: this margin is shared by the window 503 and the lag of the copy count, after the copy about 9000 cameras eat it, and their routes are lost for a round, about 2.5 %.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R076 -->
`to_all.head`: every of the term is counted once on taking the term, by the formula P/08.3 N1.1a from domain/member-count taken by the takeover; while the collection «from nothing» is not closed and without a count — 3600: the count grows together with the reports, and the term at the moment of the round would hold nothing; a restart counts by the same rule.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R077 -->
`to_all.head`: the term, not completeness: a silent member does not hold the head, copies are held by first.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R078 -->
`to_all.head`: a camera with retry_in greater than every, a lost report and a phase change of up to 1.5 every may not make it by the term: with 250,000 cameras about 1 % of routes per round, a limit.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R079 -->
`to_all.head`: rev grows only on a change «missing empty / not empty», on a change of the class server set in missing and, for a book with stale, on a change of the stale set (stale below): the set of silent cameras would raise all readers on every round, and without this a fallen server is not seen in the head, missing in a live domain is almost always not empty; servers up to 500, they fall rarely.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R080 -->
`to_all.head`: the limit: the fall of one record in a live wide member is noticed within 175–450 s with 250,000 cameras, with the round and the report; 45 s is a walk of 576 parts, a member is counted wide from 65 parts.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R081 -->
`to_all.stale`: a book with domain.books.\<book>.stale (P/02.1 N1.2b): the head carries stale — \<member>/\<server> by the keys \<sub>/heartbeats/\<slot> in stale of the publication of the member (P/09.1 N1.2; pulse.\<sub>.stale of the view is set the same way, P/08.1 N1.4), \<sub> is the subsystem of the book, for members ok and unseen. `to_all.stale`: \<server> is the field server of the element of the contribution of this slot in the copy (for rec — rec/receivers/\<slot>): for a slot without a heartbeat the server name is not in the heartbeat. `to_all.stale`: an element without a string server does not go into stale, the log without the value. `to_all.stale`: the first MISSING_SHOWN by bytes of UTF-8, stale_count — all.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R085 -->
`to_all.stale`: by subsystem, not by server: on a server a live cam with a dead rec; a server is dead together with the agent — the member is silent, it is in missing.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R086 -->
`to_all.stale`: rev of the head grows on a change of the set, and the head is written at once on the change, outside the round, not at its end: otherwise with 250,000 cameras about 3 min against the ring of 66 s; before the first head of the term — by the rule first.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R087 -->
`to_all.stale`: the limit: a death is seen with the delay of the member report and LOST_AFTER of the agent; a heartbeat that did not reach the holder makes the worker silent even when it is alive.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R088 -->
`to_all.version`: (term, rev) at each row: term — the term of the write, rev + 1 on a write, for the head — head; a pair, not a number: after a takeover rows are assembled anew.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R089 -->
`to_all.round`: the assembly goes by the round of the view parts (P/08.1 N1.4b), not by the pass: for each member of a part it removes the keys from domain/book-keys and puts the keys of the new copy, in the rows of their parts. `to_all.round`: a silent member is removed by the round the same way (P/08.5-R089). `to_all.round`: a departed member — the round also goes over the parts of the family book-keys: a name that is not in the row of the member part is removed. `to_all.round`: rows are stale by a round: a limit.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R093 -->
`to_all.book-keys`: which keys the member gave, term — the term of the write of the row; the part of a key cannot be found by the member name, and the memory of the process does not survive a start. `to_all.book-keys`: outside the copy, rebuilt, like the rows.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R095 -->
`to_all.unique`: a book to: "*" with pick: unique — a catalogue refusal (P/02.1 N1.4): the row does not keep the losing contribution, the key would blink round by round.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R096 -->
`to_all.first`: while the head /* of the current term is not written, book_stale does not name the part, and the door answers 503 unavailable: the copy holds; otherwise a new holder would remove the copies of all readers, a book without contributions would again replace the full one. `to_all.first`: the head, not complete: a silent member would keep the door closed forever, and a small domain would close the round before the first assembly. `to_all.first`: the limit: while there is no head, a new reader without a copy gets 503, a new camera has no routes; this is every, the readiness window and up to two rounds, with 250,000 cameras up to 1545 s, after the collection «from nothing» about an hour.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R099 -->
`to_all.first_pass`: while the head of the current term is not written, the round builds from scratch: book-keys with a term less than the current one is not read, that of its own term is read, the book row is written entirely. `to_all.first_pass`: with the write of the head the rows with a term less than the current one are removed: the new holder has copies under the same keys, and there is nothing to remove their keys with. `to_all.first_pass`: the limit: the readers of the cluster of the new holder see incomplete rows until the head is written.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R102 -->
`to_all.order`: first the rows of the parts, book-keys last: a fall between them repeats the removal idly, and the reverse order would lose it. `to_all.order`: while the head of the current term is not written, the other way round, book-keys before the rows: there is nothing to remove there, and a key of a row without book-keys cannot be removed until the next takeover, one term is not enough.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R104 -->
`to_all.ceiling`: a book-keys row larger than the ceiling of 524288 bytes is not written as such: the ceiling is per member part, about 10 thousand keys of all its members together. `to_all.ceiling`: contributions are removed from the end, by member name in bytes of UTF-8 from the greatest, until the row fits, usually one: the removed one is in missing with the word bad_contribution, the log domain.books.too_large {sub, book, member} for each removed one, only its cluster writes the spares. `to_all.ceiling`: the order is by name, not by size: the same member is removed on every round.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R107 -->
`to_all.ceiling`: threshold: a book-keys row larger than NEAR_CEILING bytes (80 % of the ceiling) — the log domain.books.near_ceiling {sub, book, part, bytes} once per pass and view.books near_ceiling (N1.5).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R108 -->
`to_all.ceiling`: the admission of a server into a part whose book-keys row is larger than NEAR_CEILING — 409 (chapter 8.3 N3 «admit a member» step 5a). `to_all.ceiling`: the door /domain/placement does not stop the growth of an already admitted cluster by assignments: a refusal of an assignment would leave cameras without recording; an overflow is a limit.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R110 -->
The sealing contract is [`Platform/common/contracts/sealing.md`](../../common/contracts/sealing.md) (the envelope `enc:v2`, the AAD path, the grammar of `kid`).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R111 -->
Opening a secret of a contribution — the [domain envelopes contract](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) «Envelope `x25519:v1`»
(the book acceptance key — `domain/book-seal`, chapter 8.2).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R112 -->
A valid grant row and the rank — the [right contract](../../common/contracts/may.md)
step 5 and the grant row of [P/08.4 N1.3](08.4-domain.norms.md).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R113 -->
The form of the input (`entry`: `key`, `to`, the secrets `*_secret`) — the [domain envelopes contract](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) «Contribution». The input in a report is an object (`in_report`, P/09.1 N1.2).

### N1.4 Delivery to a member

<!-- req: P/08.5-R004 -->
```yaml
carry:        sealed.books (m1)
mask:         the value of a book secret
```

<!-- req: P/08.5-R115 -->
`carry`: the answer of the holder (P/08.3 N1.5) carries the books in sealed (the envelope m1) in the field books — the form: the domain
envelopes contract «Content of sealed».

<!-- req: P/08.5-R116 -->
`mask`: the value of a book secret gets neither into a door (the book door of the cluster console, the OpenAPI of lesson 6), nor into the log, nor into the view
(P/06.2 N1.4).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R117 -->
A book `to: "*"` goes not into `sealed` and does not reach a member without a request.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R118 -->
A reader of a book `to: "*"` declares `domain/<sub>/<book>/` in `worker.reads` and puts the request `domain/book-want/<sub>/<book>/<part>`.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R119 -->
The agent holds copies of the parts of the requests `domain/book-want/<sub>/<book>/<part>` under the same key `domain/<sub>/<book>/*/<part>`, the copy is grown by the rules of the member, not by the domain.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R120 -->
The publication names `book_parts` — the versions of what the agent holds, and `null` for a request without a copy, `carry.book_stale` — what is stale or has appeared: the comparison is as for `members_rev`, a version for the whole book would raise all readers on every change.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R121 -->
A wide member carries a window of `BOOK_PARTS_SHOWN` parts by round, only they are judged: the cost is constant, the lag of a wide member is minutes. After a takeover all parts are stale, 4096 with `BOOK_PARTS_PASS` per pass — about 43 min: a limit.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R123 -->
A request lives by a term: the reader renews `at` once per `BOOK_WANT_EVERY` s while the part is needed.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R124 -->
The agent considers a request removed at `now − at > BOOK_WANT_TTL` s and removes the row itself: readers share the row of the part, so it is not for any of them to remove it.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R125 -->
The agent takes the parts by the door `GET /api/book/<member>/<sub>/<book>[/<part>]` ([chapter 8.3](08.3-domain.norms.md) N2), without a part — the head; there are no secrets in them.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R126 -->
The owner HTTP forms `HolderBookHeadBody` and `HolderBookPartBody` of the door `GET /api/book` carry raw KV strings under items, without decoding or normalizing their internal JSON; the family key and content rules stay unchanged.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R127 -->
A reader of a book `to: "*"` puts a request on any cluster.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R128 -->
The agent of the holder's cluster does not execute a request: the keys are the same, the holder's row is read.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R129 -->
A change of the term requires nothing from the reader: the rows of the former holder become copies, are judged through `book_parts`, rows without a request are removed by the agent.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R130 -->
A reader puts requests at its start too: agent clocks that jumped forward remove requests at once, this is a limit.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R131 -->
The book row at a member (`member_row`: the elements as received, the secrets are envelopes of the member's ring; the writer is the agent) — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) (N1.4
`member_row`).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R132 -->
The cluster of the holder has no book copy (`member_row`): the holder does not send books to itself in `sealed` and reads its own book from the row
`domain/<sub>/<book>/<self>`.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R133 -->
The envelope `m1` — the [domain envelopes contract](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md) «Envelope `m1`».

### N1.5 View: the field `books`

<!-- req: P/08.5-R005 -->
```yaml
domain/view.books:   {"<sub>/<book>": {conflicts, dropped, undelivered}}
  conflicts:   [<key>…]
  dropped:     {<member>: bad_contribution | secret_unopened}
  undelivered: {<contributing member>: [<name outside the member list>…]}
  person_names: [<key>…]
  too_large:   [<member>…]
  too_large_count: <count>
  near_ceiling: [<part>…]
  near_ceiling_count: <count>
```

<!-- req: P/08.5-R134 -->
`domain/view.books`: each assembled book of the catalogue. `domain/view.books`: too_large, too_large_count, near_ceiling, near_ceiling_count — only when non-empty.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R136 -->
`view.books.<sub>/<book>.conflicts`: keys of unique with two contributions or more, ascending.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R137 -->
`view.books.<sub>/<book>.dropped`: removed contributions (without values and secrets).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R138 -->
`view.books.<sub>/<book>.undelivered`: recipients of to who are not in the list, ascending.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R139 -->

## N3 Procedures

### assembly of a book (the holder, in the pass of chapter 8.1 N3)

<!-- req: P/08.5-R010 -->
1. For each assembled book of the catalogue: the members of the list (P/08.3 N1.1) with their view state and copy.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R263 -->
2. A member silent | never | without a copy → missing (why — the state or no_copy), no contribution. In step 2, the mark exists but its generation is not read (P/08.3 N3 «read the copy») — no_copy, although the view calls such a member silent: the reason is closer — there is no copy (the function assemble: copies[member] = null — no_copy, the vector assemble-08-states (judge-only: a row of the vector table `vectors/assemble/cases.tsv`; it checks P/08.5-R263, P/08.5-R265 and P/08.5-R037)).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R265 -->
3. A member ok | unseen: the objects of the copy by from of the book. In step 3, unseen → into missing (why: unseen).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R267 -->
4. Each input: check the form of the input (the domain envelopes contract «Contribution» entry; an object in a report — P/09.1 N1.2 in_report) for all
   elements of the contribution of the member; an error → bad_contribution: the whole contribution of the member is removed.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R268 -->
   In step 4, after the form of the input is checked, the secrets — open (the domain envelopes contract «Envelope x25519:v1», seed — domain/book-seal), did not open →
   secret_unopened: the whole contribution is removed.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R269 -->
   In step 4, the form before the secrets: an element with a secret that did not open and an element not by the form — bad_contribution.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R270 -->
4a. Under unique — the elements of one key at one member: equality by JCS of the value with the secrets opened (envelopes of one value differ): equal ones — one input, the item smallest in bytes of UTF-8; unequal → bad_contribution: the whole contribution is removed. In step 4a, one key under two slots is an ordinary thing: a new first worker writes while the slot of the former one is still held, and there is no reason to remove the contribution for that; a limit: the value changed in this window — the contribution is removed until the row of the former slot goes away.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R272 -->
5. Keys: unique — one contribution per key, otherwise a conflict (no key); all — all.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R273 -->
6. For each member of the list: the elements where it is in to; grants (N1.3 grant, N1.7); the row domain/\<sub>/\<book>/\<member> by N1.3 result.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R274 -->
7. Write the changed rows (secrets — with the ring of the holder), remove the rows of former members. In step 7, view.books — N1.5, too_large — by the outcome of this step.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R276 -->
After the assembly of the books of the pass — the name index of each book with `domain.names` (N1.7 `domain/names/<sub>/<book>`).

### delivery (the holder, in the answer to the agent of chapter 8.3)

<!-- req: P/08.5-R011 -->
The holder: read domain/\<sub>/\<book>/\<member> of each book of the catalogue, open the secrets with the ring → sealed.books (m1). The holder: a secret of the row domain/\<sub>/\<book>/\<member> did not open — as an unreadable row: do not carry this book to this member in this pass, the reason to the log without the value, the other books go.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R278 -->
The record at a member (the agent: open `m1`, each book — into `domain/<sub>/<book>` by CAS, a book that is not in the answer is not
touched) — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) (N3 «the write at the member»).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R009 -->
**Races.** The assembly reads the copies at different moments (chapter 8.1): a book is an observation.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R279 -->
The capture of a slot and the write of a worker under the guard —
[P/03.2](../../03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md) N3: a late write of a former holder is fenced off by the guard, a surviving row — by the cleanup at capture and by the liveness filter
of the agent.

<!-- req: P/08.5-R280 -->
Two assemblies of one book do not intersect: there is one holder ([P/08.8](08.8-domain.norms.md)).

<!-- req: P/08.5-R281 -->
A break at step 7 of the assembly — part of the rows of the book of the new
pass, part of the former one, `view.books` the former: the next pass reassembles from the copies and writes the differences (a write only
on a change); the delivery before that carries the row as is — the member has the book of one of the two passes, not a mix inside a row.
