# P/08.0 — The process doors: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/08.0-domain.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand.

The canon of chapter 8.0 of lesson P/08 «Process domain» (module «Platform»). Lecture (`ru/08-domain.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](08-domain.README.md), [lifecycle](../lifecycle.md). The chapter has
no acceptance catalogue (N5). Here is the door of the `domain` process (`SIGNER_URL`) as a whole: who writes the rows of the holder, how the process
accepts the edits that the domain console passes on (`X-W2C-Client` — only on a connection of the role `domainconsole`), the answers
of the process `deposed`, `frozen`, `standby`, which nobody on the way recomputes, the server side of the doors of class
`signed`, the summary table of the process doors, the versions and the repeat memory of the edit doors.

The domain console side of forwarding is [P/10 N1.3](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md). The process uses the [role certificate contract](../../common/contracts/role-certificate.md) for `server` and `signed`, and the [forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md) for the domain door list.

### N1.8 Accepting the edits of the domain console — the `domain` process

```yaml

pass:      the doors /domain/* that the domain console passes on, POST /api/login, POST /api/verify → the domain process
answers:   409 deposed, 503 frozen, 503 standby
```

<!-- req: P/08.0-R032 -->
`writer`: the rows of the holder are written only by the `domain` process (chapter 8.2 N1.3): edits of people, admission of reports, the pass.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R033 -->
`writer`: the exception is the rows of the worker on the domain (P/03.7 N1.6: kept and the movable books — it is their writer).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R034 -->
`pass`, the process side: the doors `/domain/*` that the domain console passes on (the list of domain doors — the forwarding contract
«Domain doors»; the passing — lesson
P/10, P/10-02), and `POST /api/login`, `POST /api/verify` (chapter 8.4 N1.2, N1.2a) come to the domain process (`SIGNER_URL` of its own box).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R035 -->
`pass`: the method, the path
with query, the body, `Authorization`, `Idempotency-Key`, `If-Match` come to the process as they came.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R036 -->
`pass`: `X-W2C-Client` is the address of the client's TCP peer:
the domain console sets it; when, whom the process trusts and what is stripped outside — the forwarding contract
Platform/common/contracts/forwarding.md «Client address».

<!-- req: P/08.0-R037 -->

<!-- req: P/08.0-R038 -->

#### Versions

<!-- req: P/08.0-R006 -->
- **`revision`** — the edit counter, as P/06.1 N2 «Versions». The field `revision` of the rows `domain/users/<name>`, `domain/grants/<cluster>`, `domain/break-glass/<cluster>`, `domain/groups/<name>`, `domain/roles/<name>` and `domain/folders/<id>`: 1 at creation, +1 for every write of the `domain` process,
  whoever caused it: a door, the removal of rows when a person is deleted, the reconciliation of the pass, `first-admin`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R046 -->
- An existing row without the field `revision` or with an unreadable field is unreadable by its current family; it is not revision 0 and a write does not normalize it to 1.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R047 -->
- For the field `revision`, confirmed absence and creation at revision 1, explicit corruption deletion and restoration from a current readable copy keep their existing rules.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R048 -->
- The copy at a member does not carry the field `revision`: the agent gets the `doc` of the grant and the fields `break_glass`, as before.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R049 -->
- **`ETag`** — the index of the row in double quotes (P/06.1 N2 «Versions»).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R050 -->
- `ETag` is carried by the answers of success per the table and by the read doors of the domain console ([P/10](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md) N1.2).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R051 -->
- The listings `GET /domain/users`, `GET /domain/groups`, `GET /domain/roles`, `GET /domain/break-glass`, `GET /domain/placement`, `GET /domain/grants` do not carry the header `ETag`: they have many rows, the index and `revision` stand at each row.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R052 -->
- Apart from the explicit folder and membership success bodies of P/08.5 N1.8, write success bodies do not carry the index: it becomes known only after the batch (below).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R053 -->
- **`If-Match`** is optional; the form of the value — the two forms of P/06.1 N2 «Versions»: `<revision>` as a number or the index in quotes.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R054 -->
- Another value of `If-Match` — 400 `If-Match is a unit revision` before the row is read.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R055 -->
- The doors `POST /domain/users`, `/domain/groups`, `/domain/roles` and `/domain/folders` do not read `If-Match`: there is no row yet, the condition has nothing to judge.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R056 -->
- The door `POST /domain/batch` does not read `If-Match`: the condition of a row is its `cas`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R057 -->
- Zero is not a form of `If-Match`: the condition names a row that exists, so the first write of the topology and of the shared document is carried by `base_rev: 0` (below).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R058 -->
- **The condition** is judged at the first read of the door's row: after the memory and the body form, before the content of the edit.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R059 -->
- After a `cas` conflict the condition is judged again, by the re-read row.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R060 -->
- A door that needs the row (`PUT`, `DELETE` of a person, a group, a role, `DELETE` of a placement, `DELETE` of a member)
  answers its own 404 before the condition; a door that creates the row — 412 with `revision: 0`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R061 -->
- The row exists but its `revision` does not read: use the current whole-row unreadable outcome of its family before judging the condition.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R062 -->
- Neither a numeric condition nor a matching store index turns a row whose `revision` does not read into a readable row.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R063 -->
- An explicit corruption-deletion path still follows its own stated procedure.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R064 -->
- **`412 revision moved`** — the condition is not met, nothing is written: `{detail, error: "revision moved", field: "/revision", revision, row?}`, the form of P/06.1 N2 «Versions».

<!-- req: P/08.0-R065 -->
- The doors of the table require a right above `view`, so in `412 revision moved` `row` is present if there is a row and it reads.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R066 -->
- `row` carries no secrets:

| Row | `row` |
|---|---|
| `domain/member-rows/<part>` | `{rev, term?, members}` |
| `domain/topology` | the document `doc` |
| `domain/users/<name>` | an element of `GET /domain/users`; there is no `pwhash_secret` in it |
| `domain/grants/<cluster>` | `{lines, revision, index}` |
| `domain/break-glass/<cluster>` | `{set_at, revision, index}` |
| `domain/groups/<name>` | an element of `GET /domain/groups` |
| `domain/roles/<name>` | an element of `GET /domain/roles` |
| `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>` | `Placement` with `index` |
| `domain/shared` | the pointer `{term, rev, sha256, object}` |
| `domain/folders/<id>` | the live `DomainFolderView` of P/08.5 N1.8; no internal counts |

<!-- req: P/08.0-R067 -->
**Membership is index-only.** `domain/memberof/<member>/<sub>/<rows>/<id>` contains only `folders`; it has no revision counter.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R068 -->
The `optional-index` condition of membership accepts an absent If-Match or a double-quoted store index, including `""` for confirmed absence.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R069 -->
For membership, a bare number or another spelling of If-Match is 400 `If-Match is a store index`, before reading the row.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R070 -->
Judge the membership condition after repeat memory and the body form, and again after a CAS conflict.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R071 -->
For membership, unknown reading is 503 unavailable, never index `""`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R072 -->
On a membership mismatch return 412 `{error: "revision moved", field: "/index", index, row?}` without a revision counter; `row` is the current `{folders,index}` when readable and present.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R073 -->
For membership, the ordinary right check and response ceiling still apply.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R074 -->
Successful membership GET and PUT return `{folders,index}` without ETag.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R075 -->
An empty membership PUT deletes the row and returns `{folders: [], index: ""}`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R076 -->
A lost-answer replay of a membership edit returns the saved response before rejudging its old condition.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R077 -->
**`base_rev`** of `PUT /domain/topology` and `PUT /domain/shared` is the former input. `If-Match` is the main one.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R078 -->
The door reads `base_rev` only if there is no header `If-Match`, and then the refusal is the former one: 409 of the revision.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R079 -->
Both `If-Match` and `base_rev` are present: `<revision>` equals `base_rev` — the door judges `If-Match`; not equal, or an index stands next to `base_rev` — 400 `refused`, `field: /base_rev`: a counter and an index cannot be compared without the row, and the door does not guess an edit with two different conditions.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R080 -->
Neither `If-Match` nor `base_rev` is present — the former 400: at these doors an omitted field stays as it was, so an edit without a condition is not written.

#### Repeat memory

<!-- req: P/08.0-R007 -->
```yaml
header:    Idempotency-Key
key:       (user, method, path, key) → the row domain/idem/<key>
form:      the value of the header Idempotency-Key
print:     who, method, path, body
row:       domain/idem/<key>: {print, code, body, index?, at}
  code:    the answer code
  body:    the answer body as text
  index:   the ETag index of the answer
  at:      the wall-clock seconds of the write
order:     the sequence of a door under the memory
memory:    the claim row of the key
in_flight: the same <key> in work
write:     success — one batch
refusal:   an answer < 500 without a target write
replay:    the former answer
weight:    the claim row and the store ceiling
```

<!-- req: P/08.0-R081 -->
`header`: `Idempotency-Key` is optional: absent — the pass of the door is the former one, a repeat is a new write; present — the door goes under the memory.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R082 -->
`key`: (user, method, path, key) → the row `domain/idem/<key>`; `<key>` — hex sha256 by P/06.1 N3 «repeat memory», 64 lowercase characters.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R083 -->
`key`: user — the name proven by the token (chapter 8.4 N1.5); path — the whole path of the door, `/domain/…`, the segments percent-decoded.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R084 -->
`form`: the header value — P/06.1 N2 (without OWS, twice — 400); a key not in form — 400 `Idempotency-Key required`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R085 -->
`print`: the print — who, method, path, body as a JSON value; no body — as `{}`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R086 -->
`print`: `If-Match` is not part of the print: a repeat after a lost answer
carries the former condition, it is not judged.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R087 -->
`print`: the value — the HMAC print of chapter P/06.2 N1 under the holder's ring: the bodies of the people doors carry a password,
a bare hash would open a brute force past PBKDF2.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R088 -->
`print`: the ring does not read — 503 `unavailable`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R089 -->
`row.code`: the answer code is below 500.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R090 -->
`row.index`: the ETag index of the answer, if this write did not write the target (the same content, a refusal does not carry it); no field — the index of the row itself.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R091 -->
`order`: the refusals of N3 (`standby`, `deposed`, `frozen`) and identity with the grant — they are not remembered → the body is a JSON object, the key form, the `If-Match` form →
memory → the pass of the door: body form, condition, content → write → answer.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R092 -->
`memory`: no row — the pass of the door; the same print — the former answer; another print — 422 `key reused`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R093 -->
`memory`: the row does not read or the read
is not confirmed — 503 `unavailable`, the row is not touched.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R094 -->
`in_flight`: the same `<key>` already in work in this process — 409 `in flight`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R095 -->
`in_flight`: a change of process leaves no work: the target and the answer land in one batch.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R096 -->
`write`: success — in one batch of P/01.1 (`op: batch`): the target write of the door with its condition and the claim row with `cas ""`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R097 -->
`write`: guards — `domain/signer-turn`
of my version and the guards of the door (of references, of the administrator's proof, of the target status; chapter 8.4 N1.3, N1.3c, N3) in the same batch.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R098 -->
`write`: what is written before the target (a document object, the ca-certs row, a deletion mark) repeats without harm — N3 of the door.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R099 -->
`refusal`: an answer < 500 without a target write, including 400, 404, 409, 412, — the claim row alone, with `cas ""` and the turn guard.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R100 -->
`refusal`: 5xx is not remembered.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R101 -->
`replay`: code and body byte for byte.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R102 -->
`replay`: `ETag` — by the table: the field `index`, otherwise the index of the claim row itself: all rows of a batch get one version (P/01.1 N1).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R103 -->
`replay`: a repeat writes nothing, there is no log.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R104 -->
`weight`: a claim row heavier than the store ceiling — 413 `row too large`, the target is not written, the refusal is not remembered.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R105 -->
`weight`: a 412 with `row` — first without `row` (P/06.1 N2 «Versions»).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R106 -->
The claim of the cluster console needs `pending`, a takeover and a guard of its own version, because there are several consoles, and the target and the answer are written by different operations. Here the writer is one, and the target and the answer land in one batch: the batch landed whole or it did not happen, so there is nothing to continue.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R107 -->
A break before the batch leaves only writes that repeat without harm.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R108 -->
A repeat goes through the pass anew and judges by the state: `DELETE /domain/users/<name>`, whose former pass removed the person's row, answers 404, and the grant rows are finished by the reconciliation (chapter 8.4 N3).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R109 -->
An unknown outcome of the batch is a barrier (chapter 8.10 N1.7): the answer is 503, a repeat finds the claim row or goes through the pass.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R110 -->
The term and the ceiling of the repeat memory — chapter 8.10 N1.5 `idem`.

<!-- req: P/08.0-R111 -->
A move of the holder does not carry the memory: `domain/idem/*` is not in the `state` of the backup copy (chapter 8.8 N1.5).

<!-- req: P/08.0-R112 -->
The new holder meets the key as a new one, while the `If-Match` condition stays.

### Data families (built from the standard files)

The form of the rows and objects that the chapter owns by the [catalog](../../common/data/catalog.tsv) is the JSON Schema 2020-12 of the family. An instance of a row is `{key, row}`, of an object `{key, object}`; the key is the grammar of the column `key` of the catalog. The fields of `row` are the values of `items` after the [codec](../../common/data/codec.tsv): the writer checks the schema and its `$defs.writer`, the reader the schema (layer 3 — the words `x-*` of the schema). Unreadable — the row did not decode or did not pass the schema in the reader's mode; the outcome is by the table of outcomes of the family: the path is a prefix of the path of the refusal (empty — the whole row); with several paths of refusal the outcome of the least priority applies.

#### `domain/idem/<key>` — row

Schema — [`idem.schema.json`](../data/idem.schema.json), outcomes — [`idem.outcomes.tsv`](../data/idem.outcomes.tsv); form version 1; norm — P/08.0 N1.9; P/08.10 N1.5 idem.

| Key | Type and bounds | Requirement | Lesson | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `print` | string | required |  | request fingerprint: who, method, path, body — the HMAC print of chapter P/06.2 N1 under the holder's ring; form and kid — the implementation's choice (P/08.0 N1.9 «Repeat memory») |
| `code` | integer; ≥ 200; ≤ 499; the whole sign for the reader too (x-whole) | required |  | HTTP code of the stored answer, below 500: the memory does not keep 5xx |
| `body` | string | required |  | body of the stored answer as text; a repeat returns it byte for byte |
| `index` | string | optional |  | index ETag of the answer if the target was not written by this batch; no field — the ETag carries the index of the claim row itself (P/01.1: one version per operation) |
| `at` | time (P/01.1 N0 «Time in a row») | required |  | wall-clock seconds of the write of the claim: for people and the log, the term is counted by the monotonic age (P/08.0 N1.9) |
