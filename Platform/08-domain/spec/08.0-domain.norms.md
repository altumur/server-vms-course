### Н1.6 The server side of the doors of class `signed`

The server side of the listener of the `domain` process (`SIGNER_URL`) is class `signed`, row `signer`: [the role
certificate contract](../../common/contracts/role-certificate.md) «Серверная сторона: класс `signed`» (moved there from М11/8 Н1.6 `server`, `signed` without change; one place for
P/08.0-02 and the relay [P/09.2](../../09-agent/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md) Н1.6; the Architect's decision of 2026-10-20, ADR-0083 addendum (14) item 6; review of stage A
8–10, № 21). The role set of the process is `domain` ([P/02.4 Н1.6](../../02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md); the roles
of processes — P/10 Н1.4 `roles`). Class `role` (the door of the domain console) is [the role certificate contract](../../common/contracts/role-certificate.md)
«The server side: the class `role`». The class is divided by listener, not by route: all routes of the summary table Н2 — the holder doors for
the agents of [chapter 8.3](08.3-domain.norms.md), the relay batch, the move doors of chapter 8.8 and the doors of people that
the domain console passes on (Н1.8) — are one listener; identity is by the norm of the route (signature, token, password). Under TLS the listener
requests a client certificate optionally and checks a presented one in the handshake: the leaf chains to its own `ca.pem` or
`ca.next.pem`, the validity period holds, there is no extendedKeyUsage without `clientAuth`; otherwise — an alert, no HTTP; not presented —
the handshake is `ok`. The role `domainconsole` is only for `X-W2C-Client` (Н1.8; [the forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md)
«Client address»; [P/02.4](../../02-installer/spec/02.4-installer.norms.md) Н1.6 `client_cert`); clients from other
clusters do not present a leaf (the role certificate contract `clients`; ADR-0083 addendum (20), addendum (21)).

#### Versions

- **`revision`** — the edit counter, as P/06.1 Н2 «Versions». The field `revision` of the rows `domain/users/<имя>`, `domain/grants/<кластер>`, `domain/break-glass/<кластер>`, `domain/groups/<имя>`, `domain/roles/<имя>`: 1 at creation, +1 for every write of the `domain` process,
  whoever caused it: a door, the removal of rows when a person is deleted, the reconciliation of the pass, `first-admin`. A row without the field or with an unreadable field — `revision` 0, the next write writes 1: rows of former installations read (the outcomes of the families).
  The copy at a member does not carry the field: the agent gets the `doc` of the grant and the fields `break_glass`, as before.
- **`ETag`** — the index of the row in double quotes (P/06.1 Н2 «Versions»). It is carried by the answers of success per the table and by the read doors of the domain console ([P/10](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md) Н1.2).
  The listings `GET /domain/users`, `GET /domain/groups`, `GET /domain/roles`, `GET /domain/break-glass`, `GET /domain/placement`, `GET /domain/grants` do not carry the header: they have many rows, the index and `revision` stand at each row. The bodies of the write doors do not carry the index: it becomes known only after the batch (below).
- **`If-Match`** is optional; the form of the value — the two forms of P/06.1 Н2 «Versions»: `<revision>` as a number or the index in quotes. Another value — 400 `If-Match is a unit revision` before the row is read. The doors `POST /domain/users`, `/domain/groups`, `/domain/roles` do not read it: there is no row yet, the condition has nothing to judge. The door `POST /domain/batch` does not read it: the condition of a row is its `cas`.
  Zero is not a form: the condition names a row that exists, so the first write of the topology and of the shared document is carried by `base_rev: 0` (below).
- **The condition** is judged at the first read of the door's row: after the memory and the body form, before the content of the edit. After a `cas` conflict — again, by the re-read row. A door that needs the row (`PUT`, `DELETE` of a person, a group, a role, `DELETE` of a placement, `DELETE` of a member)
  answers its own 404 before the condition; a door that creates the row — 412 with `revision: 0`. The row exists but its `revision` does not read: `<revision>` matches nothing, the index is compared.
- **`412 revision moved`** — the condition is not met, nothing is written: `{detail, error: "revision moved", field: "/revision", revision, row?}`, the form of P/06.1 Н2 «Versions». The doors of the table require a right above `view`, so `row` is present if there is a row and it reads. `row` carries no secrets:

| Row | `row` |
|---|---|
| `domain/member-rows/<раздел>` | `{rev, term?, members}` |
| `domain/topology` | the document `doc` |
| `domain/users/<имя>` | an element of `GET /domain/users`; there is no `pwhash_secret` in it |
| `domain/grants/<кластер>` | `{lines, revision, index}` |
| `domain/break-glass/<кластер>` | `{set_at, revision, index}` |
| `domain/groups/<имя>` | an element of `GET /domain/groups` |
| `domain/roles/<имя>` | an element of `GET /domain/roles` |
| `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>` | `Placement` with `index` |
| `domain/shared` | the pointer `{term, rev, sha256, object}` |

**`base_rev`** of `PUT /domain/topology` and `PUT /domain/shared` is the former input (the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29 on the plan of the domain share, В3). `If-Match` is the main one. The door reads `base_rev` only if there is no header, and then the refusal is the former one: 409 of the revision.
Both are present: `<revision>` equals `base_rev` — the door judges `If-Match`; not equal, or an index stands next to `base_rev` — 400 `refused`, `field: /base_rev`: a counter and an index cannot be compared without the row, and the door does not guess an edit with two different conditions.
Neither is present — the former 400: at these doors an omitted field stays as it was, so an edit without a condition is not written.

#### Repeat memory

```yaml
header:    Idempotency-Key is optional: absent — the pass of the door is the former one, a repeat is a new write; present — the door goes under the memory
key:       (user, method, path, key) → the row domain/idem/<key>; <key> — hex sha256 by P/06.1 Н3 «repeat memory», 64 lowercase characters;
           user — the name proven by the token (chapter 8.4 Н1.5); path — the whole path of the door, /domain/…, the segments percent-decoded
form:      the header value — P/06.1 Н2 (without OWS, twice — 400); a key not in form — 400 Idempotency-Key required
print:     the print — who, method, path, body as a JSON value; no body — as {}; If-Match is not part of it: a repeat after a lost answer
           carries the former condition, it is not judged; the value — the HMAC print of chapter P/06.2 Н1 under the holder's ring: the bodies of the people doors carry a password,
           a bare hash would open a brute force past PBKDF2; the ring does not read — 503 unavailable
row:       domain/idem/<key>: {print, code, body, index?, at}
  code:    the answer code, below 500; body — the answer body as text; at — the wall-clock seconds of the write
  index:   the ETag index of the answer, if this write did not write the target (the same content, a refusal does not carry it); no field — the index of the row itself
order:     the refusals of Н3 (standby, deposed, frozen) and identity with the grant — they are not remembered → the body is a JSON object, the key form, the If-Match form →
           memory → the pass of the door: body form, condition, content → write → answer
memory:    no row — the pass of the door; the same print — the former answer; another print — 422 key reused; the row does not read or the read
           is not confirmed — 503 unavailable, the row is not touched
in_flight: the same <key> already in work in this process — 409 in flight; a change of process leaves no work: the target and the answer land in one batch
write:     success — in one batch of P/01.1 (op: batch): the target write of the door with its condition and the claim row with cas ""; guards — domain/signer-turn
           of my version and the guards of the door (of references, of the administrator's proof, of the target status; chapter 8.4 Н1.3, Н1.3в, Н3) in the same batch; what is written before the target (a document object, the ca-certs row, a deletion mark) repeats without harm — Н3 of the door
refusal:   an answer < 500 without a target write, including 400, 404, 409, 412, — the claim row alone, with cas "" and the turn guard; 5xx is not remembered
replay:    code and body byte for byte; ETag — by the table: the field index, otherwise the index of the claim row itself: all rows of a batch get one version (P/01.1 Н1);
           a repeat writes nothing, there is no log
weight:    a claim row heavier than the store ceiling — 413 row too large, the target is not written, the refusal is not remembered; 412 with row — first without row (P/06.1 Н2 «Versions»)
```

The claim of the cluster console needs `pending`, a takeover and a guard of its own version, because there are several consoles, and the target and the answer are written by different operations. Here the writer is one, and the target and the answer land in one batch: the batch landed whole or it did not happen, so there is nothing to continue.
A break before the batch leaves only writes that repeat without harm. A repeat goes through the pass anew and judges by the state: `DELETE /domain/users/<имя>`, whose former pass removed the person's row, answers 404, and the grant rows are finished by the reconciliation (chapter 8.4 Н3). An unknown outcome of the batch is a barrier (chapter 8.10 Н1.7): the answer is 503, a repeat finds the claim row or goes through the pass.

The term and the ceiling — chapter 8.10 Н1.5 `idem`. A move of the holder does not carry the memory: `domain/idem/*` is not in the `state` of the backup copy (chapter 8.8 Н1.5). The new holder meets the key as a new one, while the `If-Match` condition stays.

## Н3 Procedures

The chapter has no procedure of its own. The order at each door is Н3 of the door in its chapter; a standby process answers 503 `standby` before reading the body
([chapter 8.10](08.10-domain.norms.md) Н1.7 `standby`); a deposed one — 409 `deposed` ([chapter 8.8](08.8-domain.norms.md) Н1.6);
the freeze of a planned handover — 503 `frozen` at the edits of people and of the signer (chapter 8.8 Н1.7 `order` step 1).

## Н4 Refusals

| Cause | Outcome | Who sees it and how | What the caller does |
|---|---|---|---|
| the process is deposed | 409 `deposed` with `holder` — the record of the new holder (chapter 8.8 Н1.6): edits and reports | the agent; a person — through the console as it came | go to the new holder (chapter 8.8) |
| the `domain` process is standby | 503 `standby` (chapter 8.10 Н1.7) | the agent; a person — through the console as it came | the agent — the next pass (P/09.1-19); a person — a repeat |
| a planned handover is in progress | 503 `frozen` (chapter 8.8 Н1.7) | a person — through the console as it came | — |
| an edit by ref at two or more executors without `?cluster=` | 409 `executors` with `clusters` (chapter 8.6 Н3 «принять правку» step 2а; chapter 8.1 Н4) | a person — through the console as it came | repeat with `?cluster=<исполнитель>` (ADR-0091 addendum (15) item 4) |
| door Н1.9: `If-Match` not in form; key not in form | 400 `If-Match is a unit revision`, `Idempotency-Key required` | a person | fix the header |
| door Н1.9: `If-Match` is not equal to the row version | 412 `revision moved` with `revision`, `row?` | a person | re-read and decide anew |
| door Н1.9: the same key in work; another body under the same key | 409 `in flight`, 422 `key reused` | a person | wait; a new edit — a new key |
| door Н1.9: the claim row is heavier than the store ceiling | 413 `row too large` | a person | repeat without a key or make the edit smaller |
| door Н1.9: the claim does not read, the ring does not read, the read is not confirmed, the ceiling `IDEM_MAX` (chapter 8.10 Н1.5) | 503 `unavailable` | a person | repeat; at the ceiling — repeat without a key |

The body of these refusals is their word in `error`, not `refused`: 409 `deposed` with `holder` (chapter 8.8 Н1.6), 503 `frozen` (chapter 8.8
Н1.7), 503 `standby` (chapter 8.10 Н1.7), 409 `executors` — `{error, detail, clusters}`, `clusters` in ascending order of bytes
(chapter 8.6 Н1.3); the forms — [the OpenAPI of the lesson](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml).

A refusal of the process doors is `{error, detail?, reason?, field?}` (ADR-0094 B5). The client chooses the action by the word and puts the error at a field
by `field`, without parsing the text for people.

- `error` — a word of the closed list [`refusal-words.tsv`](../data/refusal-words.tsv): a constant string without names and values; a door speaks
  only the words of its own rows and of the rows `*` with the same code. The words of the present Н4 stay, there is no renaming.
- `detail` — a phrase for a person. The phrases in quotes of the Н4 tables are the `detail` of the word `refused`, unless the row names them as the `error` word.
- `field` — the path of a body field, the format — [P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) Н2 «Refusal: `error` and `field`». It is present only at 400, 409 and 412 and
  only at rows of the list where the column `field` is not `-`; a refusal not about a field is without it. Code 412 is given only by the doors of Н1.9; the outcome
  `conflict` (412) of applying a stored edit is seen in `outcomes` (chapter 8.6 Н4).
