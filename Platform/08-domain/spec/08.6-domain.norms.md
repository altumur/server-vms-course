## Н1 Form

**Time.** The time fields of the rows of the chapter are time by the [canon Н0 of P/01.1](../../01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#time-in-a-row): `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>` — `at`; an edit in `domain/pending/<член>` — `at`; an outcome in `domain/outcomes/<член>` — `at`, `kept_at`.

**Data** ([catalogue](../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):
- owns: `domain/placement`, `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>`, `domain/pending/<член>`, `domain/outcomes/<член>`;
- writes: `domain/view` (cas);
- reads: `domain/member-rows/<раздел>`, `<sub>/<manifest:unit.rows>/<id>`.

The fields `pending` and `outcomes` of the object `domain/view` (the owner is [chapter 8.1](08.1-domain.norms.md)) are written by the process in
the view pass (Н1.6). The copies of assignments `domain/placed`, `domain/placed/<sub>/<ref>` and the outcome row of a member
`domain/outcomes` are written by the member's agent — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md)
Н1.5. The record forms of an assignment and of an outcome are common to the holder and the agent — the definitions `common.domain_placement` and
`common.domain_outcome` of the [common family definitions](../../common/data/common.schema.json) (the Architect's decision on the move plans
of 8–10, item 3).

The common part of [chapters 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md) and [8.3](08.3-domain.norms.md) Н1 applies. An assignment is written only by the operator:
placement of units by the domain by reachability is a debt in `ХОД-Спецификация.md` (the answer to the note of М11 item 3). The domain writes no
rows into members: an edit of a member is applied by the member's agent ([chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.5) through the units door of its own
console ([P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)).

### Н1.2 The holder's answer: the member's assignments

```yaml
carry.placement:  {rev, records: {"<sub>/<ref>": {cluster, origin, copies: [<член>…], reason, at, rev}}}   # chapter 8.3 Н1.5
  records:   only the assignments that name this member: it is cluster, origin or in copies (minimum of knowledge;
             the cost of the answer with hundreds of members — chapter 8.10 Н1.9)
  rev:       the counter domain/placement of the holder
  absent:    no field while there is no counter (rev 0)
```

The answer is built under the same mutex as the write of an assignment, and after the raise of the counter at start and at a new taking of the
queue (Н3 «write an assignment»; C08-PLACEMENT-BUMP-TURN-01). The copy at a member (`domain/placed/<sub>/<ref>`, `domain/placed`) — what the agent writes by this field, without rollback, and
its readers — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.5 `member`,
`readers`; the holder writes no copies: its readers take the record (Н1.1 readers).

### Н1.4 The kept edit

```yaml

  edit:     {id, seq, what, method, path, body, key, at, base?, by?, iat?, asked}
    id:     "e-" + the first 12 characters of hex sha256(UTF-8(member) ‖ 0x00 ‖ UTF-8(Idempotency-Key))
    seq:    the number of reception — the value of the element seq of this row after +1 on reception, under the same CAS; seq sets the order of the waiting
            edits of a member: by it base is assigned (below) and edits are issued (carry); at is only time for people and outcomes
            (the Architect's decision on R41-09 of run 41)
    what:   the names of the body fields ascending through «,», at POST — with the field domain.ref, without cluster (DELETE — ""); for a bundle — Н1.3б edit
    keys:   the rows that the edit touches: <sub>/<rows>/<id>, <sub>/<таблица>/<имя> (the row name of a POST of a table — by tables.<t>.key), for a bundle — every key of its writes; there is no field, this is derived from path and body.
            The rules below (base, a waiting DELETE, same_path of the agent) go by keys, not by path: two doors of one table row and a bundle see each other
    method: POST | PUT | DELETE;  path — the path of the door of the member's console: /<sub>/<rows>, /<sub>/<rows>/<id>, /<sub>/<таблица>, /<sub>/<таблица>/<имя>

    by, iat: the person and the iat of their token, if the door let them pass by a row of the target's cluster, and not by domain/grants/domain (the right contract `write`):
            the member's console judges them by its own grants on application (P/09.1 Н1.5 apply). A domain viewer has no fields: the domain's judgement is final
    key:    Idempotency-Key of the request
    asked:  {method, path, body} — the request of the domain door, as the process accepted it: the path of Н2 with ref and ?cluster=<член> at PUT and
            DELETE, the body with cluster at POST; by it the edit is repeated at a new holder (chapter 8.8 Н1.6 apply);
            path is normalized: at PUT and DELETE — /domain/<sub>/<rows>/<ref>?cluster=<member found>, even if the request
            did not carry ?cluster=, with ?id= — also &id=<id>; at POST — /domain/<sub>/<rows> without query; any other query of the request does not go into path
            (added by the reconciliation of М11, A3)

            executed, an unreadable row does not confirm absence; the outcomes of domain/outcomes; C08-KEPT-EDIT-BOUNDS-01)
            the wall-clock seconds of the process domain; if an outcome is already written for this id (the row domain/outcomes/<член> of the holder,

            number (nextafter): a new reception of the same key is distinguishable from the former one; an outcome evicted from the last 50 gives no boundary:
            the reception goes with the wall-clock seconds, it can coincide with the former kept_at only on a rollback of the clocks, this is a limit (C08-KEPT-AT-EVICTED-01);
            in the writer's form (a fraction not longer than three digits) this is not less than kept_at rounded to thousandths + 0.001:
            the number next after kept_at would round back into kept_at and coincide with it (C09-KEPT-AT-PRECISION-01)
            the boundary is computed exactly in decimal notation; if the binary64 of the value round(kept_at) + 0.001 is not greater than the binary64
            round(kept_at) (the binary64 step there is coarser than 0.001), at is not less than the smallest value of the writer's form (a fraction not
            longer than three digits) whose binary64 is greater than the binary64 round(kept_at): at 4503599627370496 — 4503599627370497;
            there is no such finite one (kept_at is the largest finite binary64) — reception 503 {error: "unavailable"}, nothing is
            written. Without an outcome by id the boundary does not apply: at — the wall-clock seconds in the writer's form, converting the clocks to three
            digits (truncation or rounding) — up to the implementation (C08-KEPT-AT-BOUND-01)
    base?:  the revision of the unit in the member's copy at the holder at the moment of reception (PUT, DELETE), for a table row — from the object domain/members/<член>/tables, if the table is in domain.view_tables; at POST there is no unit; no shard row with
            the revision of this unit in the copy — no base, the edit goes without If-Match; there is a waiting write of the same row by keys

            was used for another edit"}; an unreadable former record of this id — 422 (the key is not reused)
```

## Н3 Procedures

### write an assignment (the process domain, PUT and DELETE `/domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>`)

```
0. headers and the repeat memory (chapter 8.0 Н1.9)                             → 400 / 409 / 422 / the former answer
1. form, manifest, names — in the order of Н2                                   → 400 / 404 / 409
1а. If-Match is not equal to the row version: at PUT there is no row — 412 with revision 0, at DELETE — 404 of Н2  → 412 revision moved
2. PUT: the same content as in the row                                          → 200 without a new rev
3. r = domain/placement.rev + 1; PUT — write the row domain/placement/<sub>/<ref> with rev = r and at; DELETE — delete
   the row (there is none — 404)
4. domain/placement := {rev: r} by CAS (a conflict — reread and repeat steps 3–4, up to 8 attempts, then 503)
5. 200
```

With a key, steps 3–4 are one target write: the row, the counter and the claim row lie in one batch (chapter 8.0 Н1.9 `write`), a counter conflict — the same 8 attempts from step 1а. So a repeat does not find its own write with the former counter and does not get a false 412.
Step 2 writes nothing, so the claim row carries the `index` of the read record.

The answer to the agent (Н1.2) is built by the same process under the same mutex as the write: the answer does not see an intermediate state.
A break between steps 3 and 4 — the row is new, the counter is the former one: the process `domain` at start and at each new taking of the queue
`domain/signer-turn` (a waiting one became active, [chapter 8.10](08.10-domain.norms.md) Н1.7) — before the first answer to the agent in this
ownership — raises the counter: another process that owned the queue might have broken off as well (C08-PLACEMENT-BUMP-TURN-01). The same at taking the term by a move or a transfer. The raise: on term 1 — `rev + 1`; otherwise max(rev + 1, whole wall-clock seconds + 60), no row — rev 0: the counter came from a copy or there is none, the members hold `domain/placed.rev` of the former holder, and the agent does not go back (C08-PLACEMENT-AFTER-MOVE-01).

The limit of term 1: a rollback of the holder's store on this term (`restore`, `import`, `recover`, P/01.3) is not cured: the counter goes away together with the rest of the state, `rev + 1` stays below `placed.rev` of the members. The way out is a transfer: term 2 will raise the counter by max. The price of closing this path is bound values instead of exact `rev` in the whole acceptance of term 1; it outweighs the rarity of the path.

The limit of the other terms: the raise is above the former values while the former counter is less than the whole wall-clock seconds of the new holder + 60. The counter has no set invariant «`rev` does not outrun the wall clock», so the margin from the first such raise is 0 s: the counter goes 60 above the wall clock. A passed second adds one to the margin; each write and each raise by the branch `rev + 1` take one away; a lag of the new holder's clocks takes away as much. A margin equal to zero is already a refusal: the agent needs strictly more. The margin is exhausted — the member gets no edits until the counter outruns its `placed.rev`.

After the raise the members reread the records and converge (copies are written only on a difference —
[chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.5).

The raise of this ownership is not confirmed — the CAS of the counter did not pass in 8 attempts, the write was rejected or there is no answer
(an unknown outcome): the answer to the agent is without `rev`, as with an unreadable counter (the outcomes of `domain/placement`): the field
`carry.placement` is omitted entirely, as Н1.2 `absent` (the form `CarryPlacement` requires `rev` and `records`); the former `rev` is not
issued, the agent door does not answer 503 — the other fields of carry go. Before each next answer to the agent in this ownership
the raise is repeated. An unknown outcome is as the barrier of the [writer turn contract](../../common/contracts/turn.md) «Barrier»
(chapter 8.10 Н1.7): it is not counted as success, the process rereads the queue and the counter and raises it by the read version.
A confirmed CAS of the counter by this ownership in the write of an assignment (step 4) is a raise too (C08-PLACEMENT-BUMP-FAIL-01).

A repeat of steps 3–4 after a CAS conflict: a row that this pass has already written or deleted is its own; DELETE does not answer 404
because of its own delete (404 — only when there is no row before the first step 3), the CAS of the counter is repeated by the reread `rev`; PUT
rewrites the row with a new r. Eight conflicts — 503, the edit remains, and the counter is in the raise debt of this ownership, as
the unconfirmed raise above: the answer to the agent is without `rev` until the raise is confirmed (C08-PLACEMENT-EDIT-RETRY-01).

### outcome (the process domain, in the pass of chapter 8.1)

```
for each member: the outcomes of its copy (chapter 8.3 Н3 «прочитать копию», the field outcomes of the publication) → settle Н1.6:
write domain/outcomes/<член> (trim to 50), then remove the edit from domain/pending/<член> by CAS
```

**Races.** Reception and removal are in one process `domain`, the CAS of the row `domain/pending/<член>`; the removal of one id — only of
an unchanged edit. A local edit of a unit on the member between reception and application — `If-Match` gives 412, the outcome `conflict`: it is not
overwritten silently. An agent that got an edit twice (a repeat of the answer) — the `Idempotency-Key` of the units door ([P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md)) and the rule
`kept_at`. An assignment and a unit are different rows: deleting an assignment does not touch the unit. A break of the agent between application
and the write of the outcome into `domain/outcomes`: the edit still waits and is applied again — `POST`, `PUT` and `DELETE` give the former answer by `Idempotency-Key`
([P/06.1](../../06-console/spec/06.1-console.norms.md) Н3 «repeat memory»; ADR-0094 B3). A new edit takes a new key (at — Н1.4; ADR-0094 addendum (14)): the member's door remembers a key for 86400 s, the former key there gives the former answer, and with another body — 422 `key reused` and the outcome `refused`. The base of a new edit is taken
from the member's copy, and the snapshot shard may lag behind the value already applied by the former edit, so a 412 and the outcome
`conflict` are possible — acceptable, the operator decides anew (it agreed at the implementations, recorded by the reconciliation of М11). Application by the agent —
[chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.5.
