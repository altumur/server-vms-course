### N1.1 Assignment at the holder

it (origin = cluster — an executor as cluster).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R029 -->

executors).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R030 -->
The role of an executor (primary or reserve) is the subsystem's concern.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R031 -->

### N1.4 The kept edit

<!-- req: P/08.6-R008 -->
```yaml
domain/pending/<member>:   a holder row; the element <id> is the JSON text of an edit
  edit:     {id, seq, what, method, path, body, key, at, base?, by?, iat?, asked}
    id:     "e-" + the first 12 characters of hex sha256(UTF-8(member) ‖ 0x00 ‖ UTF-8(Idempotency-Key))
    seq:    the number of reception
    what:   the names of the body fields ascending through «,»
    keys:   the rows that the edit touches
    method: POST | PUT | DELETE
    path:   the path of the door of the member's console
    body:   the object of fields
    by, iat: the person and the iat of their token
    key:    Idempotency-Key of the request
    asked:  {method, path, body}
    at:     the wall-clock seconds of the process domain
    base?:  the revision of the unit in the member's copy at the holder at the moment of reception
writer:     the process domain
repeat:     the same id and the same asked
cancel:     there is no cancel
KEPT_LIMIT: forms/runtime-constants.tsv          # waiting edits per member
row_full:   the row ceiling of 524288 bytes
reply:      202 {kept: <id>, cluster: <member>, what, note: "kept; <member> applies it when it next reports"}
carry:      pending: [<edit>…]
```

<!-- req: P/08.6-R231 -->
`domain/pending/<member>` is a holder row; the element `<id>` is the JSON text of an edit; `edit.id` is "e-" + the first 12 characters of hex sha256(UTF-8(member) ‖ 0x00 ‖ UTF-8(Idempotency-Key)).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R117 -->
`edit.seq`: the number of reception — the value of the element seq of this row after +1 on reception, under the same CAS. seq sets the order of the waiting edits of a member: by it base is assigned (below) and edits are issued (carry); at is only time for people and outcomes.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R119 -->
`edit.what`: the names of the body fields ascending through «,», at POST — with the field domain.ref, without cluster (DELETE — ""); for a bundle — N1.3b edit.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R120 -->
`edit.keys`: the rows that the edit touches: `<sub>/<rows>/<id>`, `<sub>/<table>/<name>` (the row name of a POST of a table — by `tables.<t>.key`), for a bundle — every key of its writes; there is no field, this is derived from path and body. The rules below (base, a waiting DELETE, same_path of the agent) go by keys, not by path: two doors of one table row and a bundle see each other.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R122 -->
`edit.method`: POST | PUT | DELETE.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R232 -->
`edit.path`: the path of the door of the member's console: `/<sub>/<rows>`, `/<sub>/<rows>/<id>`, `/<sub>/<table>`, `/<sub>/<table>/<name>`
or /console/batch (N1.3b) (id — the local id of the unit from the member's copy).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R233 -->
`edit.body`: the object of fields (DELETE — {}).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R123 -->
`edit.by`, `edit.iat`: the person and the iat of their token, if the door let them pass by a row of the target's cluster, and not by domain/grants/domain (the right contract `write`).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R124 -->
The member's console judges `edit.by` and `edit.iat` by its own grants on application (P/09.1 N1.5 apply).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R125 -->
A domain viewer has no fields by, iat: the domain's judgement is final.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R126 -->
`edit.asked`: {method, path, body} — the request of the domain door, as the process accepted it: the path of N2 with ref and `?cluster=<member>` at PUT and DELETE, the body with cluster at POST. By `edit.asked` the edit is repeated at a new holder (chapter 8.8 N1.6 apply).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R128 -->
`edit.asked.path` is normalized: at PUT and DELETE — `/domain/<sub>/<rows>/<ref>?cluster=<member found>`, even if the request
did not carry ?cluster=, with ?id= — also `&id=<id>`; at POST — `/domain/<sub>/<rows>` without query; any other query of the request does not go into path.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R129 -->
The row `domain/outcomes/<member>` wholly unreadable — any reception at this member is 503 unavailable: the id might have been
executed, an unreadable row does not confirm absence (the outcomes of domain/outcomes).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R130 -->
`edit.at`: the wall-clock seconds of the process domain; if an outcome is already written for this id (the row `domain/outcomes/<member>` of the holder, N1.6) — not less than the next after its kept_at number (nextafter): a new reception of the same key is distinguishable from the former one. An outcome evicted from the last 50 gives no boundary to `edit.at`: the reception goes with the wall-clock seconds, it can coincide with the former kept_at only on a rollback of the clocks, this is a limit. In the writer's form (a fraction not longer than three digits) the boundary of `edit.at` is not less than kept_at rounded to thousandths + 0.001: the number next after kept_at would round back into kept_at and coincide with it. The boundary is computed exactly in decimal notation; if the binary64 of the value round(kept_at) + 0.001 is not greater than the binary64 round(kept_at) (the binary64 step there is coarser than 0.001), at is not less than the smallest value of the writer's form (a fraction not longer than three digits) whose binary64 is greater than the binary64 round(kept_at): at 4503599627370496 — 4503599627370497.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R134 -->
If there is no such finite value of the writer's form (kept_at is the largest finite binary64), reception — 503 {error: "unavailable"}, nothing is
written.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R135 -->
Without an outcome by id the boundary does not apply: at — the wall-clock seconds in the writer's form, converting the clocks to three
digits (truncation or rounding) — up to the implementation.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R136 -->
`edit.base`: the revision of the unit in the member's copy at the holder at the moment of reception (PUT, DELETE), for a table row — from the object `domain/members/<member>/tables`, if the table is in domain.view_tables; at POST there is no unit. No shard row with the revision of this unit in the copy — no base, the edit goes without If-Match.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R138 -->
For `edit.base`: there is a waiting write of the same row by keys
(PUT, POST of a table, put of a bundle) — base = base of the last of them by seq + 1 (so its result is applied, not the former value), if the
last has no base — the new one has no base either.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R139 -->
A waiting removal of the same row (DELETE, delete of a bundle) — a new edit of this row is refused 409 {error: "unit is being deleted"}. A waiting edit was not applied (conflict) — the following ones of the same path will give conflict too: the operator decides anew.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R141 -->
`writer`: the process domain — reception (N3, the door of the domain console — chapter 8.0 N1.8) and removal of an executed one (N3 «outcome»); CAS of the row.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R142 -->
`repeat`: the same id and the same asked (method, path, body, without the fields of N1.3a) — 202 with the former record, the former at; otherwise — 422 {error: "this Idempotency-Key was used for another edit"}. An unreadable former record of this id — 422 (the key is not reused).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R144 -->
`cancel`: there is no cancel: an accepted edit is applied or gets an outcome; a rollback is a new edit.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R145 -->
`KEPT_LIMIT`: waiting edits per member; the 51st — 409 `{error: "kept_full", detail: "<member> has 50 edits waiting"}`.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R146 -->
`row_full`: the row `domain/pending/<member>` with this edit does not fit into the row ceiling of 524288 bytes (P/01.1 N1): a row of seq and this one edit in the record form greater than the ceiling — 413 {error: "too large"}. When the row with this edit does not fit, but a row of seq and this one edit is not greater than the ceiling — 409 `{error: "kept_full", detail: "<member>'s waiting edits fill the row"}`: wait until the member applies.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R148 -->
`carry`: the holder's answer (chapter 8.3 N1.5) carries `pending: [<edit>…]` of this member by seq; the element seq of the row is a counter, not an edit (the edits are the elements e-…; KEPT_LIMIT counts them). pending is always present when the row `domain/pending/<member>` is read; no row or no edits in it — pending: []. There is no field pending only with an unreadable row (the outcomes of domain/pending): «unknown», not «empty».

## N3 Procedures

### write an assignment (the process domain, PUT and DELETE `/domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>`)

<!-- req: P/08.6-R012 -->
0. Headers and the repeat memory (chapter 8.0 N1.9) → 400 / 409 / 422 / the former answer.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R175 -->
1. Form, manifest, names — in the order of N2 → 400 / 404 / 409.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R176 -->
1a. If-Match is not equal to the row version: at PUT there is no row — 412 with revision 0, at DELETE — 404 of N2 → 412 revision moved.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R177 -->
2. PUT: the same content as in the row → 200 without a new rev.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R178 -->
3. r = domain/placement.rev + 1; PUT — write the row `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>` with rev = r and at; DELETE — delete
   the row (there is none — 404).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R179 -->
4. domain/placement := {rev: r} by CAS (a conflict — reread and repeat steps 3–4, up to 8 attempts, then 503).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R180 -->
5. Answer 200.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R181 -->
With a key, steps 3–4 are one target write: the row, the counter and the claim row lie in one batch (chapter 8.0 N1.9 `write`), a counter conflict — the same 8 attempts from step 1a. So a repeat does not find its own write with the former counter and does not get a false 412. With a key, step 2 writes nothing, so the claim row carries the `index` of the read record.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R183 -->
The answer to the agent (N1.2) is built by the same process under the same mutex as the write: the answer does not see an intermediate state.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R184 -->
A break between steps 3 and 4 — the row is new, the counter is the former one: the process `domain` at start and at each new taking of the queue `domain/signer-turn` (a waiting one became active, [chapter 8.10](08.10-domain.norms.md) N1.7) — before the first answer to the agent in this ownership — raises the counter: another process that owned the queue might have broken off as well. The process `domain` raises the counter the same way, before the first answer to the agent in this ownership, also at taking the term by a move or a transfer. The raise: on term 1 — `rev + 1`; otherwise max(rev + 1, whole wall-clock seconds + 60), no row — rev 0: the counter came from a copy or there is none, the members hold `domain/placed.rev` of the former holder, and the agent does not go back.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R187 -->
The limit of term 1: a rollback of the holder's store on this term (`restore`, `import`, `recover`, P/01.3) is not cured: the counter goes away together with the rest of the state, `rev + 1` stays below `placed.rev` of the members. The way out of the limit of term 1 is a transfer: term 2 will raise the counter by max. The price of closing this path is bound values instead of exact `rev` in the whole acceptance of term 1; it outweighs the rarity of the path.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R189 -->
The limit of the other terms: the raise is above the former values while the former counter is less than the whole wall-clock seconds of the new holder + 60. The counter has no set invariant «`rev` does not outrun the wall clock», so the margin from the first such raise is 0 s: the counter goes 60 above the wall clock. A passed second adds one to the margin; each write and each raise by the branch `rev + 1` take one away; a lag of the new holder's clocks takes away as much. A margin equal to zero is already a refusal: the agent needs strictly more. The margin is exhausted — the member gets no edits until the counter outruns its `placed.rev`.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R193 -->
After the raise the members reread the records and converge (copies are written only on a difference —
[chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.5).

<!-- req: P/08.6-R194 -->
The raise of this ownership is not confirmed — the CAS of the counter did not pass in 8 attempts, the write was rejected or there is no answer (an unknown outcome): the answer to the agent is without `rev`, as with an unreadable counter (the outcomes of `domain/placement`): the field `carry.placement` is omitted entirely, as N1.2 `absent` (the form `CarryPlacement` requires `rev` and `records`); the former `rev` is not issued, the agent door does not answer 503 — the other fields of carry go. Before each next answer to the agent in this ownership the unconfirmed raise is repeated.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R196 -->
An unknown outcome of the raise is as the barrier of the [writer turn contract](../../common/contracts/turn.md) «Barrier»
(chapter 8.10 N1.7): it is not counted as success, the process rereads the queue and the counter and raises it by the read version.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R197 -->
A confirmed CAS of the counter by this ownership in the write of an assignment (step 4) is a raise too.

<!-- req: P/08.6-R199 -->
A repeat of steps 3–4 after a CAS conflict: a row that this pass has already written or deleted is its own; DELETE does not answer 404 because of its own delete (404 — only when there is no row before the first step 3), the CAS of the counter is repeated by the reread `rev`; PUT rewrites the row with a new r. Eight conflicts — 503, the edit remains, and the counter is in the raise debt of this ownership, as the unconfirmed raise above: the answer to the agent is without `rev` until the raise is confirmed.
