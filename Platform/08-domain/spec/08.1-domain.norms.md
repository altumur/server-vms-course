[contract](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](08-domain.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),

<!-- req: P/08.1-R035 -->
The list of members of `member` is `domain/member-rows/<part>` at the holder — chapter 8.3 N1.1, `domain/member-list/<part>` at the member — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.1.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R036 -->
The name of a member is `CLUSTER` of its installation (P/04 N1.3a `cluster`), 1–64 characters [a-z0-9-], not empty.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R037 -->
The holder is a role of a member, not a kind of cluster. Which member is the holder is a decision of deployment (chapter 8.8), not the order of installation.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R039 -->
The copy of a member at the holder is what the holder last accepted from the member (chapter 8.3 N1.3: a report by generation and the mark `reported`). The domain reads a member only by its copy, it does not go to the member itself.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R041 -->
**What a cluster does not know.** A cluster answers about its units and only about them.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R042 -->
Three questions are of the level of the domain: where a unit is,

<!-- req: P/08.1-R043 -->

<!-- req: P/08.1-R044 -->

### N1.3 Freshness of a member and of a unit

<!-- req: P/08.1-R005 -->
```yaml

member_state: the observation contract «Freshness of a domain member»

  live:
  stale:
  configured:
  silent:
```

<!-- req: P/08.1-R058 -->
LOST_AFTER is the same term as for a worker's heartbeat.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R059 -->
`unit_state` is the state of a unit from a member's copy.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R060 -->
`unit_state.live`: the unit is in `status[]` of a worker's heartbeat (P/03.4 N1.1), and the heartbeat is not in `stale` of the member's mark (publication — P/09.1 N1.2; mark — chapter 8.3 N1.3).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R061 -->
`unit_state.stale`: the unit is in a heartbeat, and the heartbeat is in `stale` of the mark: the member's agent has not seen it change for longer than the subsystem's threshold
(P/09.1 N1.2 stale: heartbeat.stale_after, no key — LOST_AFTER) by the member's monotonic clock.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R062 -->
The liveness of a worker is a fact of the cluster, P/04; the domain observes it and does not infer it from the arrival of a copy.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R063 -->
`unit_state.configured`: the unit is only in a shard of the snapshot (P/04 N1.3a), no heartbeat carries it.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R064 -->
`unit_state.silent`: the member is not `ok` (`unseen`, `silent`): the units of its last copy remain in the view with this word, they are not removed.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R065 -->
A change of a heartbeat is a change of its JCS (chapter 8.2 N1): `1` and `1.0` in any field are not a change.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R066 -->
`unit_state` live and stale — only for a member that is ok. This process's first look at a heartbeat is a change in any state of the member (the age of the heartbeat counts from it).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R068 -->
For `unit_state.silent` — phase, worker, server and age by the unit's origin: from a heartbeat — its phase and age,
from a shard — "unobserved" and the age of the mark.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R069 -->
A member `unseen`, `silent` or `never` is «not confirmed», not «there is nothing»: an answer that needs it is incomplete (N1.5).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R070 -->
**The window after a start and after a transfer of the holder.** The holder's monotonic clock starts anew at each start of its process. After a restart of the same holder the marks of the members remain in its store, but the first look at a mark is not a change: a member with a mark is `unseen`, the view is incomplete, until the process sees a change. A live member updates its mark at least every LOST_AFTER/2 (chapter 8.3 N1.3) and comes to `ok` no later than this term. A member that died before the restart becomes `silent` after LOST_AFTER. So it does not look alive after the restart, and an edit of chapter 8.6 on its unit answers 503 «not heard», not 404 «nowhere».

<!-- req: P/08.1-R074 -->
After a transfer of the holder (chapter 8.8) the new holder has no marks: the generations of reports do not travel with the transfer (chapter 8.8), each member is `never` until its first report to the new holder, the view is incomplete. There is no new window «from old memory».

<!-- req: P/08.1-R184 -->
`kinds.stalled`: a claim with revision > observed_revision, the count of SHADOW_STALL has run out.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R185 -->
`kinds.orphaned`: there is a placement, and there is no acting claim of ref from its cluster for longer than SHADOW_STALL — since the placement appeared or since the last acting claim vanished. Before SHADOW_STALL has run out for `orphaned` there is no finding (the member's controller has not placed yet).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R187 -->
`kinds.unmanaged`: an acting claim of ref without a placement.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R188 -->
`kinds.conflict`: acting claims of ref at two members, not all of which are executors of the placement of ref; or at two
workers of one member with an equal epoch on one unit (the same id; N1.2 siblings); or at a member that is not an executor of the placement.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R189 -->
`kinds.stale_epoch`: a claim from a live heartbeat with an epoch below the greatest epoch of this unit (ref and id) among the claims from live heartbeats of the same member in the state ok; such a claim is not acting. An equal greatest epoch at two workers on one unit (the same id) — conflict; different ids of one ref are different units (N1.2 siblings), not a dispute.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R191 -->
`kinds.unknown`: a placement on a member that is not ok or on a name outside the list of members (detail names the state or «not a member»).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R192 -->
`facts`: the assigned one does not claim (orphaned) and an unassigned one claims (conflict) — two different facts: a placement c → m2
with an acting claim only at m1 gives both findings.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R193 -->
`grain`: lagging, stalled, unmanaged, stale_epoch — a finding per claim (cluster, worker); conflict — one per ref (cluster — the extra member, if it is the only one; for two workers of one member with an equal epoch — this member); orphaned, unknown — per placement (cluster of the placement). The findings lagging and stalled — only for acting claims.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R195 -->
`orphaned_from`: the first pass without an acting claim of the assigned ref or the first look at the placement. A placement is the triple (sub, ref, cluster): a change of the target and a return to the former one — a new placement, the count anew.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R197 -->
The count of `orphaned_from` goes on also while the member is not `ok`: the finding is `unknown` for now, the member is `ok` again
without a claim and the term has run out — `orphaned` in the same pass, the claim has come back — the count is removed.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R198 -->
`unread`: the placements of the pass are not read (N3 step 3a) — the report of this pass is not built: domain/shadow is not rewritten
(the former one remains), clean_streak neither grows nor is reset, the counts orphaned_from and SHADOW_STALL neither start
nor are reset.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R199 -->
`findings_order`: by the order of the kinds in counts, then (sub, ref, cluster, worker) in UTF-8 bytes.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R201 -->
SHADOW_STALL for class camera — max(60, 3 × every of the mark) (P/08.3 N1.1a). SHADOW_STALL is counted by the holder's monotonic clock: the time of lag without a change of observed_revision of this pair (member, worker, ref) — from the latest of: the last change of observed_revision, the start of the lag (a pass with revision > observed_revision after a pass where the claim of the pair did not lag; a unit that stood long without lag, with a new revision — lagging, not stalled at once); and the time without an acting claim of the assigned ref. Both counts of SHADOW_STALL — from this process's first look at the pair or the placement, in any state of the member. The count has run out — the time is strictly greater than SHADOW_STALL: at exactly SHADOW_STALL it has not (both counts).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R204 -->
`silent_member`: the claims of a member that is not `ok` (`unseen`, `silent`, `never`) are not acting. A placement on a member that is not `ok` is not orphaned but unknown (`unknown` in findings, it does not enter the count of failures).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R276 -->
`exit`: may_write ⟺ unmanaged == 0 ∧ no failures (stalled, orphaned, conflict, stale_epoch) ∧ clean_streak ≥ SHADOW_CLEAN.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R206 -->
`SHADOW_CLEAN`: passes in a row without findings; any finding or unknown resets the count.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R207 -->
`may_write` is the condition under which the domain will be allowed to place units by itself. In the module the domain does not place by itself (the placement is written by the operator, chapter 8.6).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R209 -->
The report is a measurement of the model: each
`unmanaged` is a unit that the model of the domain does not describe.

## N2 Doors

<!-- req: P/08.1-R014 -->
The chapter has no doors of its own.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R226 -->
The view and the shadow report are given by the domain console through the doors `GET /domain`, `GET /domain/<sub>/<rows>`, `GET /domain/shadow` — lesson P/10 (P/10-01; [N1.2a](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md)): the object `domain/view` as is plus `age`, the units of the subsystem from the view, the report of the last pass. The content of the doors `GET /domain`, `GET /domain/<sub>/<rows>`, `GET /domain/shadow` is N1.4, N1.6 of this chapter.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R230 -->
The command `view` of an implementation («Implementation decides» item 1): the input is a file of passes (N5), the output is the JSON `{view, shadow}` of the last pass. In the output of the command `view`, `shadow` is the report of the last pass that built it, N1.6 `unread`; none built it — `null`. The exit code of the command `view`: 0 — computed, 2 — the input is not in the form of N5. The command `view` is the same function as the holder's pass, without the store.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R233 -->
Native camera input. In `ViewInput`, an explicitly present `silent_after` selects camera freshness for that pass; without it the input models a server. This is the native command’s abstraction of `Entry.class` and the mark’s `received_at`, which its closed input does not carry. At the first look at an existing camera mark, the command seeds its receipt time with that pass’s monotonic `at`: the member is `ok` while the mark exists and the age since that receipt or the last observed mark change is at most `silent_after`, and `silent` when it is greater. In the camera input of the command, a mark never seen is `never`; a vanished mark is `silent`, as in N1.3. In the input of the command, a server keeps the ordinary first-look `unseen` rule until an observed mark change.

## N3 Procedures

### the holder's pass — the view and the shadow report (the head once per VIEW_EVERY, the parts by the round of N1.4b)

<!-- req: P/08.1-R015 -->
The steps go by the parts of the round over their members. Step 7 writes the row of a part on a difference, the head once per VIEW_EVERY; step 5 — `complete` of N1.4b. Steps 3a, 6 — once per round (N1.4b `shadow`).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R240 -->
1. Read the list of members (chapter 8.3) and the mark of each member. In step 1, a member without a mark is never if this process has not seen its mark, otherwise silent with the former copy (N1.3). In step 1, one that has left the list — forget it (N1.3 leave). In step 1, a change of the mark in sight — the age is reset.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R243 -->
   In step 1, the process's first look at a mark is not a change (unseen, N1.3; a camera — N1.4b stale).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R244 -->
   In step 1, `domain/member-rows/<part>`, `domain/keys` or `domain/topology` exists but
   is not readable — there is no pass: domain/view and domain/shadow are not rewritten, clean_streak and the counts — as for `unread` (N1.6),
   a log entry without the value: the unreadable is not replaced by an empty list.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R245 -->
2. For each member that is not never: the copy is the report of the mark's generation (chapter 8.3 N3 «read the copy»), for a vanished mark — the former copy of the process. In step 2, a copy that is not readable — the member is silent. In step 2, the mark is readable, there is no generation or the sha256 is not the right one: silent also at the process's first look, not unseen. In step 2, for a member silent with a readable mark, reports — the fields of this mark, as for any member with a mark, N1.7.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R249 -->
3. For each manifest of the directory with domain.ref: units from the heartbeats and the shards of each copy (N1.4). In step 3, an id already from a heartbeat — the shard does not duplicate it. In step 3, a row without a value of domain.ref is not a unit (N1.2 no_value).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R252 -->
3b. For each manifest with domain.view_tables and each copy: the generation of the copy has changed since the last pass or the process has not yet parsed it — for each table take the object `<sub>/snapshot-tables/<table>`. In step 3b, an object `<sub>/snapshot-tables/<table>` that is not in the copy, or not by the family, or whose cluster is not the member's name — do not touch it, a log entry without the value. In step 3b, for an object `<sub>/snapshot-tables/<table>` that is in the copy, by the family and whose cluster is the member's name: if rows or refused differs from what is written → write `domain/members/<member>/tables/<sub>/<table>` (chapter 8.10 N1.7 objects).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R255 -->
   In step 3b, a write error or the queue is not confirmed — the copy is not counted as parsed, the next pass repeats
   regardless of the generation.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R256 -->
   In step 3b, the view is written independently (N1.4a).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R257 -->
3a. Read the placements `domain/placement/<sub>/*` — once per pass: this read serves step 4 and the shadow report (step 6). In step 3a, the placements are not read — there are no placements of this pass.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R260 -->
4. A ref given by two members or more, except a ref all members of which are executors of its placement of step 3a (N1.4 executors), → contested, remove the units of this ref from units. In step 4, a ref given by two members or more, all members of which are executors of its placement of step 3a (N1.4 executors), is not contested: its units remain in units.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R261 -->
5. complete — N1.4b: the round is closed, all the parts are complete (member ok, contested empty).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R263 -->
6. The shadow report by N1.6: the placements of step 3a; the claims; the kinds; clean_streak and may_write. In step 6, the placements are not read — the report of this pass is not built (N1.6 unread).

<!-- req: P/08.1-R264 -->
7. Write domain/view (put of the object); the report — into the memory of the pass and into the object domain/shadow in the same pass. In step 7, the report is not built — domain/shadow and the memory of the report are the former ones.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R266 -->
**Races.** The copies of the members are read at different moments: the view is an observation, not a snapshot.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R267 -->
A member can change its mark between steps 1
and 2: the pass takes the generation of the mark it read, the next pass — the new one.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R268 -->
Two passes of the holder (there is one holder, chapter 8.8)
do not overlap.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R269 -->
After a restart of the holder the members are `unseen` until the first seen change, `clean_streak` is 0.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R270 -->
A write error
of `domain/view` does not change the former object; the door gives the former view with its `as_of`.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R271 -->
A break between the write of the view and of the shadow
report — the view of the new pass, the report of the former one: the next pass writes both; a divergence of one pass is admissible.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R272 -->
A failure of a read or of a write of a part of the pass
interrupts this pass: what is written remains, the rest is done by the next one.

<!-- req: P/08.1-R273 -->
At a failure of a part of the pass, the reconciliation of people ([chapter 8.4](08.4-domain.norms.md) N3: `domain/disabled`, deletion marks, the term, new kinds, the role `admin`, each with its own condition) and the backup are works of their own, they go on.
