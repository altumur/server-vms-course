## N1 Form

<!-- req: P/08.1-R002 -->

**Time.** The time fields of the rows and objects of the chapter are time by the [canon of N0](../../01-store/spec/01.1-store.norms.md#time-in-a-row): the object `domain/view` — `as_of`.

**Data** ([catalogue](../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):

- reads: `domain/members/<member>/reported`, `domain/members/<member>/reports/<rev>`, `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>`, `domain/member-rows/<part>`, `domain/topology`, `domain/keys`.

The view and the shadow report are written only by the process `domain` in the holder's pass (N3); objects — by the queue rule of
[chapter 8.10](08.10-domain.norms.md) N1.7 `objects`. The fields of the view of other chapters (`books`, `pending`, `outcomes`, `term`,

reads `domain/keys` for the field `trust` (N1.2a; the owner of the row is [chapter 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md)), `domain/topology` — for the field
`topology` (N1.6a; owner — chapter 8.3), `domain/member-rows/<part>` — the list of members (owner — chapter
8.3), the copies of the members — by the mark `reported` and the generation ([P/08.3 N1.3](08.3-domain.norms.md) and N3 «read the copy»), the placements — for
the shadow report (N1.6; owner — chapter 8.6).

### N1.1 Domain, member, holder

<!-- req: P/08.1-R003 -->

```yaml
domain:      a set of member clusters under one root of trust (chapter 8.2); no raft spans two members:
             the members have no common row, slot, epoch or object
member:      a cluster of M10 (one row store, its own raft, its own processes) accepted into the list of members (`domain/member-rows/<part>` at the holder — chapter 8.3 N1.1, `domain/member-list/<part>` at the member — [chapter 9.1](../../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.1);
             the name of a member is `CLUSTER` of its installation (P/04 N1.3a `cluster`), 1–64 characters [a-z0-9-], not empty
holder:      exactly one member on which the services of the domain run; the holder is a role of a member, not a kind of cluster;
             which member is the holder is a decision of deployment (chapter 8.8), not the order of installation
copy:        the copy of a member at the holder is what the holder last accepted from the member (chapter 8.3 N1.3: a report by generation and the mark
             `reported`); the domain reads a member only by its copy, it does not go to the member itself
```

**What a cluster does not know.** A cluster answers about its units and only about them. Three questions are of the level of the domain: where a unit is,
if this cluster has not heard of it («not here» ≠ «nowhere»); into which cluster to put a new unit (placement — chapter 8.6);
whether the answer is complete. Search inside a cluster, placement on a worker, transfer between workers are M10; the domain does not repeat them, and
a worker does not cross the border of a cluster. The epoch of a unit is monotonic in its cluster ([P/03.2 N1.5](../../03-worker/spec/03.2-worker.norms.md)); the domain needs no issuer of epochs.

### N1.2 The name of a unit in the domain

<!-- req: P/08.1-R004 -->

```yaml
ref:         the value of the field `domain.ref` of the unit's row (summary table P/02.1 N1.2a; for `testsub` and `testsub2` — `name`);
             the number of a unit (`id`) belongs to the cluster and is not searched in the domain: for a manifest with `unit.id: numeric` id 1 exists in every
             member (the vectors of N5 are such); for `testsub` the id is the name (P/06.1 N1.3), the search is by ref all the same
siblings:    at one member several units can carry one ref: the main one and the other records of a camera (ADR-0111 item 3). This is no dispute: ref is a name for people and for placement, a row is addressed by the pair (cluster, id).
             Which of them is the main one the domain does not judge: that is a word of the subsystem; an edit chooses the unit explicitly, chapter 8.6 N1.3 find
no_ref:      a manifest without `domain.ref` takes no part in the domain's directory: its units are not in the view, the door `/domain/<sub>/<rows>` — 404
no_value:    a unit row without a value of the field `domain.ref` (no field or "") is not a unit of the domain: it is not in `units`, it gives no claims
             of the shadow report (neither `unmanaged` nor `conflict`), it takes nothing from the completeness of the view, `may_write` does not
             change because of it; the heartbeat of such a unit goes into the member's report as before (ADR-0091 addendum (12) item 2, addendum (13) item 4)
```

### N1.3 Freshness of a member and of a unit

<!-- req: P/08.1-R005 -->

```yaml
LOST_AFTER:  the observation contract «Freshness» (the value is there)   # the same term as for a worker's heartbeat (layout audit, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25, A-01)
member_state: the observation contract «Freshness of a domain member» (moved there without a change; layout audit, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25, A-15)
unit_state:                                      # a unit from a member's copy
  live:      is in `status[]` of a worker's heartbeat (P/03.4 N1.1), and the heartbeat is not in `stale` of the member's mark (publication — P/09.1 N1.2; mark — chapter 8.3 N1.3)
  stale:     is in a heartbeat, and the heartbeat is in `stale` of the mark: the member's agent has not seen it change for longer than the subsystem's threshold
             (P/09.1 N1.2 stale: heartbeat.stale_after, no key — LOST_AFTER; ADR-0113 addendum (47) item 4) by the member's monotonic clock (the liveness of a worker is a fact of the cluster, P/04; the domain observes it and does not infer it from the arrival of a copy)
  configured: is only in a shard of the snapshot (P/04 N1.3a), no heartbeat carries it
  silent:    the member is not `ok` (`unseen`, `silent`): the units of its last copy remain in the view with this word, they are not removed
  # a change of a heartbeat is a change of its JCS (chapter 8.2 N1): `1` and `1.0` in any field are not a change (added by the M11 reconciliation, V6)
  # live and stale — only for a member that is ok; this process's first look at a heartbeat is a change in any state of the member (the age of the
  # heartbeat counts from it); for silent — phase, worker, server and age by the unit's origin: from a heartbeat — its phase and age,
  # from a shard — "unobserved" and the age of the mark (cammeta-python M11-01 № 2, 3)
```

A member `unseen`, `silent` or `never` is «not confirmed», not «there is nothing»: an answer that needs it is incomplete (N1.5).

**The window after a start and after a transfer of the holder.** The holder's monotonic clock starts anew at each start of its process.
After a restart of the same holder the marks of the members remain in its store, but the first look at a mark is not a change: a member with
a mark is `unseen`, the view is incomplete, until the process sees a change. A live member updates its mark at least every LOST_AFTER/2 (chapter 8.3 N1.3)
and comes to `ok` no later than this term; a member that died before the restart becomes `silent` after LOST_AFTER. So it does not look
alive after the restart, and an edit of chapter 8.6 on its unit answers 503 «not heard», not 404 «nowhere». After a transfer of the holder (chapter 8.8) the new holder has no marks: the generations of reports do not travel with the transfer
(chapter 8.8), each member is `never` until its first report to the new holder, the view is incomplete. There is no new window «from old memory».

### N1.4 The domain view

<!-- req: P/08.1-R006 -->

```yaml
domain/view:                     # the form of the whole view: the command view computes it; the layout on objects — N1.4b, the head once per VIEW_EVERY s
  as_of:     <the holder's wall seconds at the end of the pass>
  holder:    <the holder's name>
  complete:  true | false        # the command view: true ⟺ every member is `ok` and `contested` is empty; for the pass — the round, N1.4b complete
  members:   [{name, state: ok | unseen | silent | never, age, class?, url?, servers?}]   # age — seconds since the last change of the mark that
                                                         # this process saw, without a change — since the first look; one digit after
                                                         # the point (N1.4 round); for `never` — null; the order — by name
                                                         # url — the member's entry address from the object domain/servers of the copy: the holder takes it only if it is https with a host, without credentials and without a fragment, otherwise there is no field, a log entry:
                                                         # the value goes to the browser, and the box stands in the street; none — the member is behind NAT, no link is built (ADR-0111 item 11);
                                                         # servers — [{name, online?, labels?, pulse?}] by name in UTF-8 bytes: the names from the object
                                                         # domain/servers of the copy, the field server of its heartbeats and of the rows of shards; online — there is a heartbeat
                                                         # of this server: true if the member is ok and the heartbeat is fresh (N1.3), otherwise false; no heartbeats —
                                                         # no field: nothing is known of a server without a worker; labels — from domain/servers;
                                                         # pulse — {<sub>: {<field>: <value>}}: the fields domain.view_pulse of the manifest from the heartbeat
                                                         # of a worker of this server, from the first by key in UTF-8 bytes, *_secret cut off;
                                                         # pulse.<sub>.stale: true — the key of this heartbeat is in stale of the member's last publication
                                                         # (P/09.1 N1.2: unchanged for longer than LOST_AFTER by the agent's clock), per subsystem; none — no field (ADR-0113 addendum (33));
                                                         # a word of the server, not of the unit (ADR-0111 item 2);
                                                         # no copy or not a single name — no field
  units:     {<sub>: [Unit]}     # every manifest of the directory with domain.ref (N1.2), the key exists for an empty one too
  backup_short?: {have, need}    # there are cameras, and fewer took a copy than BACKUP_HOLDERS: have — those who took it, need — BACKUP_HOLDERS (chapter 8.8 N1.5 new_camera; ADR-0113 addendum (4) item 4)
  contested: {<sub>: {<ref>: [<member>…]}}   # a ref claimed by two members or more, except the ref of executors (executors
                                         # below); its units are not in units;
                                         # the key exists for each manifest with domain.ref, without disputed ones — {}
  <fields of other chapters>:    # trust — N1.2a; knocking — N1.7; topology, relay_refused — N1.6a;
                                 # books — chapter 8.5; pending, outcomes — chapter 8.6; url — the forwarding contract; term — chapter 8.8;
                                 # shared — chapter 8.9; reports — N1.7; role_spread — chapter 8.4
Unit:        {sub, id, ref, cluster, revision, labels?, about?, worker?, server?, phase, age, state, view?, status?}
  id:        the id of the unit in its cluster (as a string); ref — N1.2; cluster — the member
  revision:  the version of the unit's row from the row of the shard, the one taken by the rule duplicate; opaque to the reader; for a foreign record
             it is the version for an edit (ADR-0110)
  worker, server: from the heartbeat (`worker`, the field `server` of the heartbeat) or from the row of the snapshot shard; none — no field; worker of a heartbeat
             without the field worker — the name from the heartbeat's key `<sub>/heartbeats/<name>` (added by the M11 reconciliation, V9)
  phase:     `status[].phase` of the heartbeat (P/03.4 N1.1); for `configured` — "unobserved"
  age:       seconds: for live/stale — since the last change of the heartbeat, for configured — since the last change of the mark; one digit after the point
round:       «one digit after the point» — rounding of the exact decimal value of the seconds (not of a binary fraction) to 0.1,
             half — away from zero: 0.25 → 0.3, 60.05 → 60.1 (added by the M11 reconciliation, V4)
  labels, about: the field labels and the value of the field about.field of the manifest from the row of the shard; no field in the snapshot or the value is empty — no field;
             they serve the judgement by target (the rights contract), so that the view is filtered no coarser than the console
  view:      the fields named by `domain.view` of the manifest (P/02.1 N1.2a), from the row of the shard; there are no secret fields (P/06.2 N1.4)
  status:    the fields named by `domain.view_status` of the manifest, from the element of status[] of this unit's heartbeat, the keys *_secret inside cut off
             at any depth (P/06.2 N1.4); there is no element
             (`configured`), no list or not a single field — there is no field status; the heartbeat is unreadable — as if there is no element
order:       units in each subsystem — by (cluster, id, worker) in the order of UTF-8 bytes; a unit in the heartbeats of two workers
             of one member — two elements (both carry it; the shadow report sorts out which of them acts)
duplicate:   an id in two shards of the snapshot of one member — the row with the greater revision; at an equal one — from the first shard by key
             in the order of UTF-8 bytes (added by the M11 reconciliation, V5)
no_ref:      an element of a heartbeat whose id has no row in the shard of its member does not enter units (the Unit has no ref) and does not claim;
             likewise a row of a shard without a value of domain.ref (N1.2 no_value)
executors:   a ref all members of which are executors of its placement ({cluster} ∪ copies, chapter 8.6 N1.1; origin of a member neither adds to this
             set nor excludes from it) is not contested: the units of all executors are in units; the role of an executor
             (main or reserve) is the concern of the subsystem, the view does not show it; a member outside the set that gave the same ref is
             contested; the placements of the pass are not read (N3 step 3a) — there is no exception: a ref at two members or more is contested;
             the completeness of the view — by N3 step 5 (ADR-0091 addendum (12) item 1, addendum (13) items 1–2, addendum (15) items 1–2)
as_of:       for the command view — the wall seconds of its process at the end of the pass of the input; acceptance does not compare it
```

The outcomes of the family of heartbeats (`heartbeats`, [P/03.4](../../03-worker/spec/03.4-worker.norms.md)) are applied by a live reader of the object from
the store — the holder's pass reading the objects of a member's copy: a heartbeat unreadable as a whole does not get into the view. The pure function
of the view (the command `view`, the vector [`view`](../vectors/view/README.md)) receives what has already been read: the form of its input is `ViewInput`
(`worker` may come from the key, `instance` is not mandatory), not the family; the claims of the shadow report are by the strict `status.items`
(N1.6 `claim`) (the Architect's decision of 2026-10-20 (Go M11-01 № 7; the family heartbeats, review of stage B 6–7, № 14, № 15)).

### N1.4a Tables of the view

<!-- req: P/08.1-R007 -->

Another cluster needs not only the units with `ref`: the console sees the member's settings and volumes, the manifests without `domain.ref` and the units without a value of `ref`, and the rules and rights stand on tables (ADR-0110, review: «the domain snapshot is short»). Tables do not enter `domain/view`: the view is written every 3 s, and tables change rarely. A dispute of `ref` (`contested`) does not remove the rows of tables: a row is addressed by the cluster and the key.

```yaml
domain/members/<member>/tables/<sub>/<table>:   # an object of the holder; the writer is the holder's pass, N3 step 3b
  {cluster, ts, refused?, rows}                 # the snapshot of P/04 N1.3b from the member's copy as is; cluster equals the member's name
manifest:  domain.view_tables: {<table>: [<field>…]} — a declared table, not service: true, not from domain.kept, or unit.rows; the fields — from its fields
           or unit.fields, not *_secret, not blob, held_by — only for the table of places (P/02.1, relations 137–143); domain.view_status — N1.4 Unit status
refused:   the table did not enter, there are no rows; the reader shows that there is no data, not that the table is empty
stale:     the object is an observation: ts is the member's clock; a silent member — the former object, the reader takes liveness from members[].state of the view,
           after a change of the holder — likewise: the member is unseen until a report has come
leave:     the pass, on seeing a member leave (N3 step 1), removes its objects; an error of the removal — the next pass; a member that returns with the same name
           does not see foreign rows: the door answers by the list of members, the objects are removed before the acceptance
versions:  the manifest of the member has no key, while the manifest of the holder has it — there is no object: the door 404 not reported yet, the same as when the report has not come yet;
           there is no need to distinguish them, there is no data in both cases
move:      the objects do not enter the backup of the domain: the new holder writes them by the same step from the accepted copies
```

### N1.4b The view by parts (ADR-0113 item 4)

<!-- req: P/08.1-R008 -->

The form of the fields of N1.4 is the same, the command `view` computes the whole view; what changes is the layout: the view is not written as one object, and the cost of a pass does not grow with the number of members. What grows is the age.

```yaml
domain/view:              the head: everything that is not per member — as_of, holder, url, term, complete, topology, trust, knocking, knocking_count, relay_refused, shared, books, role_spread, counts, parts;
                          once per VIEW_EVERY; holds and view.url of transition 2 read only it
domain/view-rows/<part>: {part, as_of, members, units, contested, reports, pending, outcomes} — an object of P/05 per non-empty part
names:    domain/view.<field> of chapters 8.5, 8.6, 8.1 N1.7 about per-member fields — a field of the row of the part of this member; their reader reads the row, not the head. There is no row: the round has not built it yet — the answer is incomplete, as complete: false
knocking: the head carries the knocks of the memory KNOCKS; knocking_count — how many lie in domain/knocks/*, they themselves — the door P/10 N1.2b page by page
reader:   a worker on the domain takes units and members as the rows of the parts from parts of the head, by the right worker.reads domain/view-rows/ (V/01.7): the head does not carry them; a row is written only on a difference, so it reads the part that changed as_of
round:    VIEW_MEMBERS members per second, measurement status: unmeasured; a part is a unit of work, the list of non-empty ones is parts of the head, round = members / budget; a row is written only on a difference
complete: the last round is closed and all its parts are complete; after a change of the holder and a start of the process — false until the round is closed
contested: domain/ref-claims/<sub>/<sha16 ref>: {<member>: <as_of>}; the round writes a member's claim and removes the one the member no longer gives; a row with two members is a dispute. The edit door judges ref by a fresh read of this row, not by the view;
          the executors — the placement read with the claim. Limit: a camera has moved — a false dispute until its report; a dispute is seen with a shift of up to a round
member_list: removed from the view: the list is the rows of the part, the door P/10 N1.2b
stale:    a member of a camera comes out of unseen after a restart of the holder by received_at of the mark, not by the next report (N1.3)
shadow:   the shadow report and clean_streak — by a closed round, the count of SHADOW_STALL — on the pass of the part of the pair; domain/shadow is written once per round; with a small domain the round is shorter than VIEW_EVERY and all is as before
```

### N1.5 Completeness of an answer

<!-- req: P/08.1-R009 -->

The domain's answer about units is a summary by copies: partial and stale by a bounded amount. The answer MUST carry its
incompleteness as a field, not as an absence: `complete: false`, a member `unseen`/`silent`/`never`, `contested`. A short list MUST NOT
be passed off as a complete one. «Not found» in a complete answer is a fact about the domain; in an incomplete one — a fact about the network: the refusal names who
was not heard (N4). Every derivative of the copies (the view, the books of chapter 8.5, the names of chapter 8.5, the alarms of lesson P/10) is an observation, not
power: it gives no right of writing in a member and carries its own list of the absent.

### N1.6 The shadow report

<!-- req: P/08.1-R010 -->

```yaml
shadow:                            # computed by the holder's pass together with the view, writes nothing into the members
  inputs:    the placements domain/placement/<sub>/<ref> {cluster, copies} (chapter 8.6 N1.1), read by the pass once (N3 step 3a):
             the executors of ref — {cluster} ∪ copies; origin does not change the set (origin = cluster — an executor as cluster;
             ADR-0091 addendum (15) item 1); the copies of the members (N1.3)
  claim:     an element of status[] of a worker's heartbeat in a member's copy → (sub, ref, id, member, worker, epoch, revision, observed_revision);
             ref — by the id of the unit from the snapshot shard of this member; an element without ref (no row in the shard) — no claim;
             an element not in the form of P/03.4 N1.1 (epoch, revision, observed_revision are not integers, etc.) — no claim
             (added by the M11 reconciliation, V14); an element phase: sealed (the epoch is not taken, P/03.4 N1.1, P/03.5 N3) — no claim
             (W03-G02); an element phase: unreadable (the epoch is not taken, P/03.3 N3 step 2) — no claim (W03-G05); a unit without
             a value of domain.ref (N1.2 no_value) — no claim: neither unmanaged nor conflict (ADR-0091 addendum (13) item 4);
             an acting claim — only from the heartbeat of a unit in the state live (N1.3): the last heartbeat of a dead worker
             goes stale and stops claiming by itself
  kinds:
    lagging:     a claim with revision > observed_revision, the count of SHADOW_STALL has not run out                         # normal
    stalled:     a claim with revision > observed_revision, the count of SHADOW_STALL has run out                            # failure
    orphaned:    there is a placement, and there is no acting claim of ref from its cluster for longer than SHADOW_STALL — since the placement appeared or since
                 the last acting claim vanished; earlier there is no finding (the member's controller has not placed yet)  # failure
    unmanaged:   an acting claim of ref without a placement                                                       # measurement
    conflict:    acting claims of ref at two members, not all of which are executors of the placement of ref; or at two
                 workers of one member with an equal epoch on one unit (the same id; N1.2 siblings); or at a member that is not an executor of the placement        # failure
    stale_epoch: a claim from a live heartbeat with an epoch below the greatest epoch of this unit (ref and id) among the claims from live heartbeats of the same
                 member in the state ok; such a claim is not acting; an equal greatest one at two workers on one unit (the same id) — conflict; different ids of one ref are different units (N1.2 siblings), not a dispute  # failure
    unknown:     a placement on a member that is not ok or on a name outside the list of members (detail names the state or «not a member»)
  facts:     the assigned one does not claim (orphaned) and an unassigned one claims (conflict) — two different facts: a placement c → m2
             with an acting claim only at m1 gives both findings
  grain:     lagging, stalled, unmanaged, stale_epoch — a finding per claim (cluster, worker); conflict — one per ref
             (cluster — the extra member, if it is the only one; for two workers of one member with an equal epoch — this member, added
             by the M11 reconciliation, V11); orphaned, unknown — per placement (cluster of the placement);
             lagging and stalled — only for acting claims
  orphaned_from: the first pass without an acting claim of the assigned ref or the first look at the placement; a placement is
                 the triple (sub, ref, cluster): a change of the target and a return to the former one — a new placement, the count anew
                 (the Architect's decision on the M11 reconciliation, V10); the count goes on also while the member is not `ok`: the finding is `unknown` for now, the member is `ok` again
                 without a claim and the term has run out — `orphaned` in the same pass, the claim has come back — the count is removed (C08-ORPHAN-SILENT-01)
  unread:    the placements of the pass are not read (N3 step 3a) — the report of this pass is not built: domain/shadow is not rewritten
             (the former one remains), clean_streak neither grows nor is reset, the counts orphaned_from and SHADOW_STALL neither start
             nor are reset (ADR-0091 addendum (15) item 3, addendum (17) item 2)
  findings_order: by the order of the kinds in counts, then (sub, ref, cluster, worker) in UTF-8 bytes
  SHADOW_STALL:  forms/runtime-constants.tsv       # s, for class camera — max(60, 3 × every of the mark) (P/08.3 N1.1a), by the holder's monotonic clock: the time of lag without a change of observed_revision of this pair
                          # (member, worker, ref) — from the latest of: the last change of observed_revision, the start of the lag
                          # (a pass with revision > observed_revision after a pass where the claim of the pair did not lag; a unit
                          # that stood long without lag, with a new revision — lagging, not stalled at once, C08-SHADOW-STALL-LAG-01);
                          # and the time without an acting claim of the assigned ref; both counts — from this process's first look
                          # at the pair or the placement, in any state of the member
  silent_member: the claims of a member that is not `ok` (`unseen`, `silent`, `never`) are not acting; a placement on such a member is not orphaned but unknown
                 (`unknown` in findings, it does not enter the count of failures)
  exit:          may_write ⟺ unmanaged == 0 ∧ no failures (stalled, orphaned, conflict, stale_epoch) ∧ clean_streak ≥ SHADOW_CLEAN
  SHADOW_CLEAN:  forms/runtime-constants.tsv        # passes in a row without findings; any finding or unknown resets the count
  report:        {counts: {lagging, stalled, orphaned, unmanaged, conflict, stale_epoch, unknown},
                  findings: [{kind, sub, ref, cluster?, worker?, detail}], clean_streak, may_write, why}
```

`may_write` is the condition under which the domain will be allowed to place units by itself; in the module the domain does not place by itself
(the placement is written by the operator, chapter 8.6; placement by the domain is a debt in the Specification work log (for people)). The report is a measurement of the model: each
`unmanaged` is a unit that the model of the domain does not describe.

The form of the rows and objects that the chapter owns by the [catalog](../../common/data/catalog.tsv) is the JSON Schema 2020-12 of the family (ADR-0092). ## N2 Doors

<!-- req: P/08.1-R014 -->

The chapter has no doors of its own. The view and the shadow report are given by the domain console through the doors `GET /domain`, `GET /domain/<sub>/<rows>`,
`GET /domain/shadow` — lesson P/10 (P/10-01; [N1.2a](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md)): the object `domain/view` as is plus `age`, the units of the subsystem from the view, the report of the last pass; the content is N1.4,
N1.6 of this chapter.

The command `view` of an implementation («Implementation decides» item 1): the input is a file of passes (N5), the output is the JSON `{view, shadow}` of the last
pass (`shadow` is the report of the last pass that built it, N1.6 `unread`; none built it — `null`); code 0 —
computed, 2 — the input is not in the form of N5. It is the same function as the holder's pass, without the store.

Native camera input (G08-VIEW-CAMERA-INPUT-01). In `ViewInput`, an explicitly present `silent_after` selects camera freshness for that pass; without it the input models a server. This is the native command’s abstraction of `Entry.class` and the mark’s `received_at`, which its closed input does not carry. At the first look at an existing camera mark, the command seeds its receipt time with that pass’s monotonic `at`: the member is `ok` while the mark exists and the age since that receipt or the last observed mark change is at most `silent_after`, and `silent` when it is greater. A mark never seen is `never`; a vanished mark is `silent`, as in N1.3. A server keeps the ordinary first-look `unseen` rule until an observed mark change. This abstraction does not change the running holder: it obtains the class from the accepted entry and the receipt time from the actual mark, never from a heartbeat timestamp. No class or receipt field is inferred from the copy’s objects.

### the holder's pass — the view and the shadow report (the head once per VIEW_EVERY, the parts by the round of N1.4b)

<!-- req: P/08.1-R016 -->

The steps go by the parts of the round over their members: step 7 writes the row of a part on a difference, the head once per VIEW_EVERY, step 5 — `complete` of N1.4b; steps 3a, 6 — once per round (N1.4b `shadow`).

```
1. read the list of members (chapter 8.3) and the mark of each member; a member without a mark is never if this process has not seen its mark,
   otherwise silent with the former copy (N1.3); one that has left the list — forget it (N1.3 leave); a change of the mark in sight — the age is reset;
   the process's first look at a mark is not a change (unseen, N1.3; a camera — N1.4b stale); `domain/member-rows/<part>`, `domain/keys` or `domain/topology` exists but
   is not readable — there is no pass: domain/view and domain/shadow are not rewritten, clean_streak and the counts — as for `unread` (N1.6),
   a log entry without the value: the unreadable is not replaced by an empty list (C08-PASS-UNREADABLE-01)
2. for each member that is not never: the copy is the report of the mark's generation (chapter 8.3 N3 «read the copy»), for a vanished mark — the former
   copy of the process; not readable — the member is silent
   (the mark is readable, there is no generation or the sha256 is not the right one: silent also at the process's first look, not unseen; reports — the fields
   of this mark, as for any member with a mark, N1.7; C08-MARK-NO-COPY-01)
3. for each manifest of the directory with domain.ref: units from the heartbeats and the shards of each copy (N1.4); an id already from a heartbeat — the shard does not duplicate it;
   a row without a value of domain.ref is not a unit (N1.2 no_value)
3b. for each manifest with domain.view_tables and each copy: the generation of the copy has changed since the last pass or the process has not yet
   parsed it — for each table take the object <sub>/snapshot-tables/<table>; not in the copy, or not by the family, or cluster is not the member's name — do not
   touch it, a log entry without the value; otherwise, if rows or refused differs from what is written → write domain/members/<member>/tables/<sub>/<table>
   (chapter 8.10 N1.7 objects); a write error or the queue is not confirmed — the copy is not counted as parsed, the next pass repeats
   regardless of the generation; the view is written independently (N1.4a)
3a. read the placements domain/placement/<sub>/* — once per pass: this read serves step 4 and the shadow report (step 6);
   not read — there are no placements of this pass (ADR-0091 addendum (13) item 1)
4. a ref given by two members or more → contested, remove the units of this ref from units; except a ref all members of which are
   executors of its placement of step 3a (N1.4 executors): its units remain in units
5. complete — N1.4b: the round is closed, all the parts are complete (member ok, contested empty)
6. the shadow report by N1.6: the placements of step 3a; the claims; the kinds; clean_streak and may_write; the placements are not read — the report
   of this pass is not built (N1.6 unread)
7. write domain/view (put of the object); the report — into the memory of the pass and into the object domain/shadow in the same pass; not built —
   domain/shadow and the memory of the report are the former ones
```

**Races.** The copies of the members are read at different moments: the view is an observation, not a snapshot. A member can change its mark between steps 1
and 2: the pass takes the generation of the mark it read, the next pass — the new one. Two passes of the holder (there is one holder, chapter 8.8)
do not overlap; after a restart of the holder the members are `unseen` until the first seen change, `clean_streak` is 0. A write error
of `domain/view` does not change the former object; the door gives the former view with its `as_of`. A break between the write of the view and of the shadow
report — the view of the new pass, the report of the former one: the next pass writes both; a divergence of one pass is admissible. A failure of a read or of a write of a part of the pass
interrupts this pass: what is written remains, the rest is done by the next one; the reconciliation of people ([chapter 8.4](08.4-domain.norms.md) N3: `domain/disabled`, deletion marks, the term, new kinds, the role `admin`, each with its own condition) and the backup are works of their own, they go on (C08-PASS-PART-01).
