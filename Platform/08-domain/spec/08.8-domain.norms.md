The common N1 of the lesson applies (forms — [chapter 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md) N1 «Common to all forms of the chapter»). The holder holds a **term** — a number that grows only by a move or a handover; there is no lease, no renewal and no automatic change of holder. ### N1.1 The term record `domain/holder`

<!-- req: P/08.8-R003 -->
The row `domain/holder` and the form of the document (`doc`: `domain`, `term`, `holder`, `url`, `ca?`, `at`, `restored_from?`, `issuer`,
`kid`, `sig`) — the [term record contract](../../common/contracts/record.md) «The term record `domain/holder`» (owner — this
chapter). Here — who writes and what checks:

```yaml
claim:           domain/holder <the record of its term>
check:           record (N3)
```

<!-- req: P/08.8-R034 -->
`claim` — the process domain after installation (term 1 — the domain founding contract «Term record 1»), after a move and after accepting a handover writes the record of its term. `claim` — a record equal to the one lying there is not written. `claim` — after writing the record of its term, the process enters itself into the member list (chapter 8.3 N3 «the holder in the list»). `claim` — term 1 — only when the absence of the rows `domain/holder` and `domain/member-rows/<part>` in its own store is confirmed (installation does not write them).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R038 -->
`claim` — for term 1: `domain/member-rows/<part>` exists and `domain/holder` does not — a break of a move or a handover before claim: there is no term (N1.8, «Races»), term 1 is not derived. `claim` — for term 1: either of `domain/holder` and `domain/member-rows/<part>` is unreadable or the store did not answer — claim is not written, the reason goes to the journal, a retry at the next start. `claim` — the process's own term record already lies (a break after claim before the list) — it is not rewritten, the former at is kept; the holder's Entry is entered by «the holder in the list» at start.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R041 -->
- `check` — the function record (N3) by the set of this cluster.

### N1.2 The member list at a change of holder

<!-- req: P/08.8-R004 -->
```yaml
holder:          Entry {how: "holder", since, key}
former:          Entry {how: "held the domain until term <N>", since, key}
```

<!-- req: P/08.8-R042 -->
`holder` — the Entry of the holder; the form is one, that of chapter 8.3 N1.1 (without by). `holder` — claim rewrites its own Entry to this form if in the list from the backup copy it is different (chapter 8.3 N3 «the holder in the list» step 1).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R044 -->
`former` — the Entry of the former holder, N — the term at which it held. `former` — the key — holder_member_key of the backup copy from which the term is restored (N1.5), and when there is no copy («from nothing») or no field — the key of its Entry in `domain/member-list/<part>` of this cluster: without the key the agent of the former holder gets 403 forever. `former` — a key in revoked_members of the set or in revoked_keys of the record of this name is not written into key: a copy does not return a revoked key. `former` — the Entry of the former holder stays until it is removed by the administrator (chapter 8.3, DELETE /domain/members/{name}).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R048 -->
- `former` — at stolen — Entry {how, since, revoked_keys} without key: the key of the former one is in its revoked_keys
  (chapter 8.2 step 3a) and in revoked_members of the set (the domain key set contract).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R050 -->

<!-- req: P/08.8-R052 -->

  the holder (chapter 8.3).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R053 -->
- `domain/view.term.copies` — a member that does not keep — no key.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R054 -->

<!-- req: P/08.8-R055 -->

<!-- req: P/08.8-R056 -->
- `domain/view.term.stranded?` — the number of elements of domain/stranded of this cluster (N1.6), if it is deposed or, at the acting holder, the row is non-empty.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R057 -->
- `domain/view.term.stranded?` — at the acting holder the field is counted by the holder's pass.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R058 -->
- `domain/view.term.stranded?` — at a deposed one it is published by the deposed process itself as a separate projection, not by the holder's pass: at yielding —
  after the write of domain/stranded (or a failure of the gathering) — and at start as deposed the process rewrites its own
  domain/view under the queue guard (P/08.10 N1.7 objects).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R059 -->
- `domain/view.term.stranded?` — in this projection: term, holder, url — of the new holder's record,
  stranded — the number of elements of the read domain/stranded (no row — 0; does not read — no field,
  the outcomes of domain/stranded), no frozen_for; the other fields of the view — as they lie (as_of the former one: the holder's view
  at the moment of yielding).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R060 -->
`domain/view.term.stranded?` — the holder's pass, the reconciliation and the backup copy do not run at the deposed one. `domain/view.term.stranded?` — the projection does not wait for forget and does not depend on its outcome: after the attempt of gathering stranded — in the same step of yielding before or after the attempt of forget, and at start as deposed (forgotten or not). `domain/view.term.stranded?` — no own domain/view or it is wholly unreadable — no projection: the view is not invented (neither as_of nor the other fields), the reason goes to the journal, forget is unconditional; the next attempt — at start as deposed.

```yaml
  members:       [<name>…]

  raw:           {<object key>: <base64 of the bytes, RFC 4648 §4, with padding>}
  secrets:       <every *_secret> → <an envelope of the backup copy key>
  sealed:        <an item whose *_secret the holder's ring does not open>
  not_in:        <rows outside the document>

```

<!-- req: P/08.8-R065 -->
- `document` — the holder's object `domain/backups/rev-<rev>` (P/05 N1.1) — JCS {term, rev, holder, at, members, parts?, counts?, state,
  objects, raw?, backup_key_rev, holder_member_key?, issuer, kid, sig}; the signature — as N1.1.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R066 -->
- `document.members` — the names of the member list.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R067 -->
- `document.state` — the rows that the domain decided and that nobody else has:
  `domain/member-rows/<part>`, `domain/members/<member>/ca-certs` (chapter 8.3), domain/topology, domain/placement, domain/placement/*, domain/pending/*,
  domain/outcomes/*, domain/issued/* (chapter 8.2 N1.6a), domain/batches/* (chapter 8.3 N1.1b: at a restore a revocation does not revive, used — the largest, refused — the union), domain/partial/* (N1.8a), domain/folders/*, domain/memberof/*, domain/folder-rev (chapter 8.5 N1.8: the copies at the members are incomplete), domain/grants/*, domain/users/*, domain/groups/*, domain/roles/* (roles and groups are part
  of the domain state, the copy of the table at a member does not replace them), domain/deleting/* (the delete mark of chapter 8.4 N1.1, including
  `role:<name>`: the new holder completes the deletion), domain/break-glass/*, domain/disabled,
  domain/book-seal, domain/from-nothing (the sign of a move «from nothing», N1.8 step 6), domain/revoked (otherwise a move would revive revoked tokens), domain/shared (the pointer
  of the shared document, chapter 8.9 N1.2), the rows kept and of the carried books of the catalogue manifests (P/03.7 N1.6: they are written by
  the worker on the domain, they are not assembled from the members' rows).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R068 -->
`document.objects` — the current shared document `domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>` (chapter 8.9 N1.2) and the alarm history `domain/alarm-history/<member>` of each member (chapter 8.9 N1.9). `document.objects` — here — only an object in the form of its family, by value; after a restore its equality is by value (JCS), not by bytes.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R070 -->
`document.raw` — an object from the list objects not in the form of its family: not JSON or JSON not in form (for example « [ 1.00 ] » at a map object) — by bytes; after a restore equality is by bytes. `document.raw` — an object of 0 bytes is not JSON: raw "" (base64 of empty bytes, the empty string is allowed). `document.raw` — the key — in objects or in raw, not in both (a key in both — the document is not in form: the function record step 1, not_a_record). `document.raw` — the field raw exists only when an object not in the form of its family exists.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R074 -->
- `document.raw` — there is no refusal of a copy because of one
  unreadable object.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R075 -->
`document.secrets` — every `*_secret` at any depth of the value of a state item, including inside the JSON of an item (a walk with the full path, as the sealing contract «AAD path» and P/06.2 N3 «create/edit a unit row, secrets included» step 4) — an envelope of the backup copy key (below, `backup_key`), not of the holder's rings. `document.secrets` — objects carry no secrets (the shared document and the alarm history: a secret is not loaded into shared, P/06.2 N1.4, P/02.1 N1.3a domain).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R077 -->
- `document.sealed` — an item whose `*_secret` the holder's ring does not open — Sealed (P/03.5 N1.2): it does not enter the backup
  copy, journal domain.backup.sealed {key, item}; the pass and the next copies go on.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R078 -->
`document.not_in` — not in the document: domain/signer (the keys travel only by a handover, N1.7), domain/keys (the set at each member), the name index and the assembled books (the pass rebuilds them from the members' reports), the report generations, domain/stranded. `document.not_in` — not in the document either — what the pass restores: the view and domain/view-rows/*, domain/book-keys/*, domain/ref-claims/*, domain/folder-counts/*, domain/folder-log, domain/knocks/*.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R080 -->
- `document.not_in` — domain/relay-members/* is assembled by the restore of N1.8 step 7 from Entry.via, before claim: the pass does not write it.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R081 -->
- `backup_key` — derived from root_key and backup_key_rev — [the domain backup key contract](../../common/contracts/domain-backup-key.md)
  (HMAC-SHA256; vectors common/vectors/domain-backup-key).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R082 -->
- `backup_key.at_holder` — domain/signer.backup_key_secret and backup_key_rev (chapter 8.2 N1.3) — written by the installation.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R083 -->
`backup_key.envelope` — enc:v2 of the sealing contract with this key; AAD — `UTF-8(<row key>) 0x00 UTF-8(<the full JSON Pointer from the root of the row>)`: `/<item>/…/<field>_secret`, the escaping ~0/~1 and the array indices — the sealing contract «AAD path»; for a top-level secret this is `/<item>`. `backup_key.envelope` — the context is one, there is no guessing.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R085 -->
- `backup_key.kid` — the writer sets `b<backup_key_rev>` (b1, b2 …; the kid grammar of the sealing contract, it does not coincide with the ring `k<N>`).

<!-- req: P/08.8-R086 -->
`backup_key.kid` — the reader derives the key from backup_key_rev of the document. `backup_key.kid` — the kid of the envelope ≠ `b<backup_key_rev>` of the document — Sealed «sealed with key `<kid>`» (P/03.5 N1.2 open_without), it does not try to open. `backup_key.kid` — the local ring is never used for the envelopes of the backup copy key.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R089 -->
- `rev` — grows only at a change of contents — JCS of state with open secrets, objects, raw, backup_key_rev,
  holder_member_key.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R090 -->
- `rev` — not more often than BACKUP_EVERY.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R091 -->
- `rev` — the holder keeps the last BACKUP_HEADS documents.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R092 -->
- `rev` — the next rev is the largest of the objects
  domain/backups/rev-* of its own store + 1, none — 1: copies are not inherited by a move, rev is not end-to-end, a copy is named by the pair (term, rev), term — in the document.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R093 -->
`first` — the first backup copy — in the holder's first pass, without waiting for BACKUP_EVERY (this is a limit, not a delay). `first` — after the first backup copy — at a change of contents, not more often than once per BACKUP_EVERY.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R095 -->
- `force` — a backup copy out of the BACKUP_EVERY turn (N1.7 step 2): rev grows only if the contents changed; the former
  contents — the pointers to the former rev.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R445 -->
`BACKUP_EVERY` — max(BACKUP_EVERY_MIN, bytes of the head and of the changed parts / BACKUP_RATE) seconds. `BACKUP_EVERY` — BACKUP_RATE, measurement status: unmeasured.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R097 -->
`BACKUP_HOLDERS` — members of class server, ok (chapter 8.1), except the holder, by descending hex `sha256(UTF-8 «<holder>/<member>»)`. `BACKUP_HOLDERS` — a camera is never a keeper.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R099 -->
`new_camera` — a camera beyond 16 is not accepted while those that took the copy are fewer than BACKUP_HOLDERS: counted is a keeper whose copies equals the last copy, not a chosen one; 409 with an explanation. `new_camera` — up to 16 — a warning in the view, the field backup_short (chapter 8.1 N1.4). `new_camera` — right after a change of holder an acceptance beyond 16 waits for the copy for about 35 s: the price is accepted.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R102 -->
`keepers` — `domain/keepers/<member> {term, rev, sha256}` at the holder — the pointer of a chosen keeper; sha256 — hex sha256 of the JCS of the document. `keepers` — the holder removes the pointer of a keeper not chosen. `keepers` — the pass checks the pointers against the last copy: a break between the object and the pointer in a quiet domain is fixed by the next pass.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R105 -->
- `carry` — the holder's answer to the agent (chapter 8.3 N1.5) carries `backup: {pointer: {term, rev, sha256}, doc: <the JCS text of the head>}`.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R106 -->
- `carry` — the keeper pulls the parts by the door N1.5a when
  the member's pointer exists and ≠ its domain/backup-taken from the member's copy at the holder.

### N1.6 The deposed holder

<!-- req: P/08.8-R008 -->
```yaml
yields:      <a record domain/holder that passes record>
switch:      <the term record contract «The switch point switch»>
start:       <the record of its own store after a restart>
refusal:     409 {error: "deposed", detail, holder}
forget:      domain/signer := {forgotten: "deposed by term <M>"}
stranded:    domain/stranded {doc: JCS {term, holder, base: {term, rev}, items: [{path, key, value}] by (path, key)}}
apply:       POST /domain/stranded/apply {path, key}
```

<!-- req: P/08.8-R132 -->
`yields` — the process domain yields when in its store there is a record domain/holder of a larger term — or of the same term with another holder, or of another holder of any term with its own issuer in revoked_issuing of this row (a change after theft can be of a smaller term, N1.8 limits) — that passes record by the row domain/keys of its own store. `yields` — the follow set is the agent's memory: the process sees it when the agent has written the row, the key set contract «The follow set»; until it has written it, the record does not pass, the process does not yield, the next pass will check again. `yields` — the term record it writes itself at a handover, N1.7 step 6, or its agent brings it at a move, P/09.1 N3 «follow». `yields` — a record of its own term by another holder gets into the row only when the cluster has accepted the set of that move — that is, has lost.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R136 -->

<!-- req: P/08.8-R384 -->

<!-- req: P/08.8-R385 -->
2. A gathering row, the field gathered exists, it is never a source itself, its gathered judges it. In merge step 2, gathered larger than its own source or no own one → gathered becomes the source, members — step 4 over this row: the newest one lived only in it, the copy rolled the source back. In merge step 2, gathered equal to its own source or null → the names that are not in its own row and not in removed go by a union to step 5, term and rev of the source as before: the member already took it instead of the old row, and the copy rolled it back; no such names → nothing.

3. (doc.term, doc.rev) not larger than the source → nothing.

<!-- req: P/08.8-R390 -->

5.
