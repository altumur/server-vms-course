# P/08.7 — The issue of worker tokens: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/08.7-domain.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand (ADR-0121).

The canon of chapter 8.7 of lesson P/08 «Process domain» (the domain process; ADR-0080; module «Platform» — ADR-0083). Lecture (`ru/08-domain.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](08-domain.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),
[acceptance](../acceptance/7/README.md). Here is the side of the holder of worker tokens: the holder's door
`POST /api/tokens/<member>/<sub>/<kind>` with the body limit `TOKEN_HOLDER_BODY` and an answer in the envelope `m1`, the issue in order
(the first rule gives the outcome) by the scope of the kind — assignment, unit, scenario — and the claims of the token.

Token declarations are [P/02.1 N1.2b](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md). The [local token door contract](../../common/contracts/local-token-door.md) is owned by [P/09.1 N1.6](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md); relay forwarding is [P/09.2](../../09-agent/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md). Recipient checks are [P/03.7 N1.5](../../03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md); token form, signature and verification use the [domain token contract](../../common/contracts/domain-token.md).

## N1 Form

seconds of the holder; the form — [domain token contract](../../common/contracts/domain-token.md) «The token».

**Data** ([catalogue](../../common/data/catalog.tsv)):
- reads: `domain/placement/<sub>/<ref>`, `domain/member-rows/<part>`, `domain/topology`, `domain/shared`, `domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>`, `domain/members/<member>/reports/<rev>`, `domain/signer`, `domain/keys`, `domain/revoked` — for the self-check of the issued token: the request door does not accept a token; the check failed or a row does not read — 503 unavailable (C08-TOKEN-SELF-CHECK-01); `domain/revoked` — also step 6a; `domain/issued/<sub>/<kind>/<sha16>` — step 6a.
- writes: `domain/issued/<sub>/<kind>/<sha16>` (cas) — only a kind with keep: true (N3 «issue» step 6a, [chapter 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md) N1.6a).

The issue of a kind without `keep` writes nothing (N3). The assignment records — [P/08.6 N1.1](08.6-domain.norms.md), the list of members —
[P/08.3 N1.1](08.3-domain.norms.md), the topology — [P/08.3 N1.2](08.3-domain.norms.md), the units of members (`units` of a copy) — the generations of reports `…/reports/<rev>` (chapter 8.3),
the shared document — the pointer `domain/shared` and the object `domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>` ([P/08.9 N1.2](08.9-domain.norms.md)), the token key

the revocations `domain/revoked` — [domain key set contract](../../common/contracts/keys.md) «Revocations».

The common N1 of lesson P/08 ([P/08.2 N1](08.2-domain.norms.md) «Common to all forms of the chapter», [P/08.3 N1](08.3-domain.norms.md)) applies. A worker's token is a domain token
([domain token contract](../../common/contracts/domain-token.md) «The token») with `kind` of a kind of the manifest. The holder's signing key
is not handed to the worker, nor is the member's key. The token at the worker (a request through the agent, only in memory) and the check at the recipient —
[P/03.7 N1.5](../../03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md) (stage B).

### N1.3 The holder's door

<!-- req: P/08.7-R004 -->

```yaml
door:       POST /api/tokens/<member>/<sub>/<kind> at the process domain; the agent's signature (agent signature contract); through a relay —
            byte for byte, with the same signature (chapter 9.2 N1.1 `not_author`, N2)
body:       {request_id, role, ref, by?, revoked?} — revoked: <jti> — the recipient refused this token with the word revoked (P/08.2 N1.6a jti); at a kind without keep — refusal 400 bad_request; role — the role the agent certified; the forms as at the local door (local
            token door contract «The door»); more than TOKEN_HOLDER_BODY — 413

answer:     200 {sealed: <m1 P/08.3 N1.5>} — inside, the text of JSON {request_id, token, exp}
```

The envelope `m1` and its content at the token door — [domain envelopes contract](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md)

hand it over) — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.6 `agent`.

## N3 Procedures

<!-- req: P/08.7-R007 -->

### issue (the process domain) — in order, the first rule gives the outcome

<!-- req: P/08.7-R008 -->

```
0. the process is deposed (P/08.8 N1.6) — it has no token keys                → 409 deposed (with holder)
1. member, key, signature — P/08.3 N2                                         → 404 / 403 / 401
2. the body is not of the form of N1.3 — the closed {request_id, role, ref, by?, revoked?}   → 400 bad_request
3. the kind is not declared by the manifest of sub, or has no scope, or role ∉ grant   → 403 not_granted
4. by is present without scenario, or scenario does not have it               → 400 by_misplaced
4a. the scope takes a value of the topology (centre, star_centre, poll_home, via — N1.1 computed), and domain/topology exists but
    does not read → 503 unavailable, nothing is issued, the log without the value (V7 item 3, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-28, P1; row 51 of the list)
5. the scope:
   placement — the record <placement.sub>/<ref>:
     unreadable (including without the field copies, or the field not of the form of chapter 8.6 N1.1; added by the reconciliation of M11, T2)
                                                                             → 403 assignment_unreadable
     absent: if there is unit — to unit; otherwise                           → 403 no_assignment
       (a record key that the store will not accept as a name — longer than the key limit of P/01.1 N1, 400 badkey — means no record,
        not «unreadable»: such a record cannot exist; C08-ASSIGNMENT-BADKEY-01)
     a name of the record (cluster, origin, copies) is not a member, or the member ≠ the field caller → 403 outside_scope
       (origin "" is not a name: it is not checked for membership and does not enter the audience; caller origin with "" — outside_scope)
     audience: the values of target_fields by N1.1 computed: centre without centre → 409 no_centre; star_centre — only
       with the cluster of the record in star (outside star it adds nothing, no refusal), cluster in star without centre → 409 no_centre
       (added by the reconciliation of M11)
   unit — the owners of ref (the members whose copies declare a unit with this ref in units of the subsystem unit.sub, chapter 8.1):
     caller self: the owner is exactly this member; caller poll_home: the owner is one   → otherwise 403 unit_not_unique
     caller poll_home: poll_home(owner) ≠ member                             → 403 not_poll_home
     audience: the values of target_fields — poll_home → poll_home(owner), centre → centre (without centre → 409 no_centre)
   scenario — by:
     the owners of by and ref — in units of the subsystem of the kind (sub of the manifest that declared the kind)
     the owner of by is one: the member or a member behind this relay (via[owner] = member)  → otherwise 403 trigger_not_reported
     the shared document doc (chapter 8.9 N1.2, the checked record — term record contract) → the array items; absent        → 403 no_shared_doc
     the items: a name — a string that occurs in the array once; enabled cuts off only false (no field —
       enabled); target = ref, trigger = by; the path member is declared and the value by it is non-empty and not equal to the owner of by — the item is skipped: ref is unique only within a cluster (C08-SCENARIO-MEMBER-01, the Architect's decision of 2026-11-01, review of the VMS invariants); no field by the path act — the item is skipped → acts; none at all → 403 no_item
     home: the record <placement>/<ref> — its cluster (in star — centre); no record — poll_home(the single owner of ref)
       the record is unreadable, or cluster in star without centre — there is no home, do not fall back to the owner  → none → 403 no_home
     audience (the rules in order; the home is computed earlier): the lift of a relay (the requester is via of the owner of by) — [centre];
                the requester behind a relay R: R = home — [R], otherwise [R] and up = centre;
                otherwise — {home} and centre, if it exists; for the lift and behind a relay ≠ home centre is needed → none → 409 no_centre
6. the audience is empty or has a non-member                                 → 403 no_audience
6a. a kind with keep, under the common side of the lock (P/08.2 N1.6a order): a freeze → 503 frozen; domain/revoked does not read → 503 unavailable; e = now + lifetime; the row domain/issued of the target: CAS of creation or of prolongation max(exp, e); the word revoked equals the jti of the row, within REVOKED_WORDS, and the row domain/revoked is not 80 % full (P/08.2 N1.6a) — a batch with it into domain/revoked, a new jti → the jti of the row; a conflict — reread, up to 3 times, then 503 unavailable
7. the claims of N1.4, exp of a kind with keep — e of step 6a, the signature, m1 to the member   → 200
```

Nothing is written except in step 6a: the issue is a read of records, of the topology, of copies and of the document. The `detail` of a refusal names the reason in words, without
the values of the document and of tokens.

The refusals of step 1 — chapter 8.3 N2 and [agent signature contract](../../common/contracts/agent-signature.md) «Check and refusal words»;
the units of the members' copies (step 5 `unit`) — [P/08.1](08.1-domain.norms.md); the shared document (step 5 `scenario`) —
[P/08.9 N1.2](08.9-domain.norms.md); the assignment record — [P/08.6 N1.1](08.6-domain.norms.md).
