# P/08.7 — The issue of worker tokens: norms

This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/08.7-domain.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand.

The canon of chapter 8.7 of lesson P/08 «Process domain» (the domain process; module «Platform»). Lecture (`ru/08-domain.md`, for people, not in the export),
[contract](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml), [instructions](08-domain.README.md), [life cycle](../lifecycle.md),
[acceptance](../acceptance/7/README.md). Here is the side of the holder of worker tokens: the holder's door
`POST /api/tokens/<member>/<sub>/<kind>` with the body limit `TOKEN_HOLDER_BODY` and an answer in the envelope `m1`, the issue in order
(the first rule gives the outcome) by the scope of the kind — assignment, unit, scenario — and the claims of the token.

Token declarations are [P/02.1 N1.2b](../../02-installer/spec/02.1-installer.norms.md). The [local token door contract](../../common/contracts/local-token-door.md) is owned by [P/09.1 N1.6](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md); relay forwarding is [P/09.2](../../09-agent/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md). Recipient checks are [P/03.7 N1.5](../../03-worker/spec/03.7-worker.norms.md); token form, signature and verification use the [domain token contract](../../common/contracts/domain-token.md).

### N1.3 The holder's door

<!-- req: P/08.7-R004 -->
```yaml
door:       POST /api/tokens/<member>/<sub>/<kind>
body:       {request_id, role, ref, by?, revoked?}
TOKEN_HOLDER_BODY: local token door contract
answer:     200 {sealed: <m1 P/08.3 N1.5>}
```

<!-- req: P/08.7-R024 -->
- `door` — at the process domain; the agent's signature (agent signature contract).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R025 -->
- `door` — through a relay — byte for byte, with the same signature (chapter 9.2 N1.1 `not_author`, N2).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R026 -->
- `body.revoked` — `revoked: <jti>` — the recipient refused this token with the word revoked (P/08.2 N1.6a jti).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R027 -->
- `body.revoked` — at a kind without keep — refusal 400 bad_request.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R028 -->
- `body.role` — the role the agent certified.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R029 -->
- `body` — the forms as at the local door (local token door contract «The door»).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R030 -->
- `body` — more than TOKEN_HOLDER_BODY — 413.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R031 -->
- `TOKEN_HOLDER_BODY` — the value and its meaning are in the local token door contract.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R032 -->
- `answer` — inside, the text of JSON `{request_id, token, exp}`.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R033 -->
The envelope `m1` and its content at the token door — [domain envelopes contract](../../common/contracts/domain-envelopes.md)
«Envelope `m1`», «Content of `sealed`»; `TOKEN_BODY` — [local token door contract](../../common/contracts/local-token-door.md).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R034 -->
What the agent does with the answer (open `m1`; `request_id` ≠ the requested one or `exp` ≤ now — 502 `invalid token reply`; otherwise
hand it over) — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) N1.6 `agent`.

### N1.4 Claims

<!-- req: P/08.7-R005 -->
```yaml
payload:    {iss: <domain>, sub: <requesting member>, kind: <kind>, iat: now, exp: now + lifetime, jti: 16 hex,
             <ref_claim>: ref, <audience_claim>: [<member>…],
             scenario: <trigger_claim>: by, <acts_claim>: [<act>…], <up_claim>?: centre}
names:      ref_claim, audience_claim
signed:     the holder's token key; JCS of header and payload
```

<!-- req: P/08.7-R035 -->
- `payload.<audience_claim>` — ascending, without repeats.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R036 -->
- `payload.<acts_claim>` — without repeats by the JCS text, ascending by the bytes of UTF-8 of this text.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R037 -->
- `names` — ref_claim and audience_claim — from the scope that gave the audience (placement without a record fell to unit — from unit).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R038 -->
- `signed` — by the holder's token key (current of the set, domain key set contract; P/08.2 N1.2), JCS of header and payload (domain token contract «The token»).

## N3 Procedures

### issue (the process domain) — in order, the first rule gives the outcome

<!-- req: P/08.7-R008 -->
0. the process is deposed (P/08.8 N1.6) — it has no token keys → 409 deposed (with holder).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R040 -->
1. member, key, signature — P/08.3 N2 → 404 / 403 / 401.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R041 -->
2. the body is not of the form of N1.3 — the closed `{request_id, role, ref, by?, revoked?}` → 400 bad_request.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R042 -->
3. the kind is not declared by the manifest of sub, or has no scope, or role ∉ grant → 403 not_granted.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R043 -->
4. by is present without scenario, or scenario does not have it → 400 by_misplaced.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R044 -->
4a. the scope takes a value of the topology (centre, star_centre, poll_home, via — N1.1 computed), and `domain/topology` exists but
    does not read → 503 unavailable, nothing is issued, the log without the value.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R045 -->
5. the scope: placement — the record `<placement.sub>/<ref>`: unreadable (including without the field copies, or the field not of the form of chapter 8.6 N1.1)
   → 403 assignment_unreadable.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R046 -->
   - In the scope placement, the record is absent: if there is unit — to unit; otherwise → 403 no_assignment.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R047 -->
   - In the scope placement, a record key that the store will not accept as a name — longer than the key limit of P/01.1 N1, 400 badkey — means no record,
     not «unreadable»: such a record cannot exist.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R048 -->
   - In the scope placement, a name of the record (cluster, origin, copies) is not a member, or the member ≠ the field caller → 403 outside_scope.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R049 -->
   - In the scope placement, origin "" is not a name: it is not checked for membership and does not enter the audience; caller origin with "" — outside_scope.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R050 -->
   - In the scope placement, the audience: the values of target_fields by N1.1 computed.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R051 -->
   - In the scope placement, centre without centre → 409 no_centre.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R052 -->
   - In the scope placement, star_centre — only
     with the cluster of the record in star (outside star it adds nothing, no refusal), cluster in star without centre → 409 no_centre.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R053 -->
   - the scope: unit — the owners of ref (the members whose copies declare a unit with this ref in units of the subsystem unit.sub, chapter 8.1).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R054 -->
   - In the scope unit, caller self: the owner is exactly this member; caller poll_home: the owner is one → otherwise 403 unit_not_unique.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R055 -->
   - In the scope unit, caller poll_home: poll_home(owner) ≠ member → 403 not_poll_home.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R056 -->
   - In the scope unit, the audience: the values of target_fields — poll_home → poll_home(owner), centre → centre (without centre → 409 no_centre).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R057 -->
   - the scope: scenario — by: the owners of by and ref — in units of the subsystem of the kind (sub of the manifest that declared the kind).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R058 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the owner of by is one: the member or a member behind this relay (via[owner] = member) → otherwise 403 trigger_not_reported.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R059 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the shared document doc (chapter 8.9 N1.2, the checked record — term record contract) → the array items; absent → 403 no_shared_doc.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R060 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the items: a name — a string that occurs in the array once.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R061 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the items: enabled cuts off only false (no field — enabled).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R062 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the items: target = ref, trigger = by.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R063 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the items: the path member is declared and the value by it is non-empty and not equal to the owner of by — the item is skipped: ref is unique only within a cluster.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R064 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the items: no field by the path act — the item is skipped.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R065 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the items → acts; none at all → 403 no_item.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R066 -->
   - In the scope scenario, home: the record `<placement>/<ref>` — its cluster (in star — centre); no record — poll_home(the single owner of ref).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R067 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the record is unreadable, or cluster in star without centre — there is no home, do not fall back to the owner → none → 403 no_home.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R068 -->
   - In the scope scenario, the audience (the rules in order; the home is computed earlier): the lift of a relay (the requester is via of the owner of by) — [centre];
     the requester behind a relay R: R = home — [R], otherwise [R] and up = centre;
     otherwise — {home} and centre, if it exists.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R069 -->
   - In the scope scenario, for the lift and behind a relay ≠ home centre is needed → none → 409 no_centre.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R070 -->
6. the audience is empty or has a non-member → 403 no_audience.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R071 -->
6a. a kind with keep, under the common side of the lock (P/08.2 N1.6a order): a freeze → 503 frozen.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R072 -->
   - In step 6a, `domain/revoked` does not read → 503 unavailable.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R073 -->
   - In step 6a, e = now + lifetime; the row `domain/issued` of the target: CAS of creation or of prolongation max(exp, e).

<!-- req: P/08.7-R074 -->
   - In step 6a, the word revoked equals the jti of the row, within REVOKED_WORDS, and the row `domain/revoked` is not 80 % full (P/08.2 N1.6a) — a batch with it into `domain/revoked`, a new jti → the jti of the row.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R075 -->
   - In step 6a, a conflict — reread, up to 3 times, then 503 unavailable.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R076 -->
7. the claims of N1.4, exp of a kind with keep — e of step 6a, the signature, m1 to the member → 200.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R007 -->
Nothing is written except in step 6a: the issue is a read of records, of the topology, of copies and of the document.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R077 -->
The `detail` of a refusal names the reason in words, without
the values of the document and of tokens.

<!-- req: P/08.7-R078 -->
The refusals of step 1 — chapter 8.3 N2 and [agent signature contract](../../common/contracts/agent-signature.md) «Check and refusal words»;
the units of the members' copies (step 5 `unit`) — [P/08.1](08.1-domain.norms.md); the shared document (step 5 `scenario`) —
[P/08.9 N1.2](08.9-domain.norms.md); the assignment record — [P/08.6 N1.1](08.6-domain.norms.md).
