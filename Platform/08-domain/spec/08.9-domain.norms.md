This English text is the source; the Russian translation `ru/spec/08.9-domain.norms.md` (for people, not in the export) is derived from it by translation and is not edited by hand.

The canon of chapter 8.9 of lesson P/08 «Process domain» (the domain process; module «Platform»). Lecture (`ru/08-domain.md`, for people, not in the export),

### N1.2 The shared document

<!-- req: P/08.9-R003 -->
```yaml
document:        the holder's object domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev> — JCS {domain, term, rev, at, by, shared, issuer, kid, sig}
  shared:        {<sub>: {<name>: <value>}}
  term, rev:     the holder's term (P/08.8) and the number of the edit
  by:            the name proven by the gate
  signature:     the issuing key
pointer:         domain/shared {doc: JCS {term, rev, sha256, object}} at the holder
write:           the object, then the pointer
after_move:      rev + 1, by "domain"
readers:         the process domain, the domain console, the subsystem worker, the cluster console
```

<!-- req: P/08.9-R036 -->
`document.shared` carries only what is declared by P/02.1 N1.2b; subsystems and names go ascending (JCS).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R037 -->
The order of documents is (term, rev), term is senior.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R038 -->
After a move, `document.by` is "domain".

<!-- req: P/08.9-R039 -->
`document.signature`: the document is signed by the issuing key, as the term record (term record contract, record.md); the check is the function record.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R040 -->
An unsigned document is never accepted.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R041 -->
In `pointer`, `sha256` is hex sha256 of the text of the object.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R042 -->
In `pointer`, `object` is the key of this object, the string `"domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>"` with rev of the pointer.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R043 -->
`write`: first the object, then the pointer by CAS (two edits at once — one gets 409).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R044 -->
The holder keeps the last SHARED_OBJECTS_KEEP objects.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R045 -->
The process executes the edits of the shared document one at a time (the steps of N1.3 order 3–6 — one edit at a time).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R046 -->
The revision object is written by the order of the objects without a pointer (P/08.10 N1.7 objects): a snapshot
of the pointer domain/shared (by it the object is recognised as nobody's) → get_at of the revision object → the confirmation of its own
turn domain/signer-turn → a write by the index of THIS get_at (object store client contract «Operations» ops).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R047 -->
In the write of the revision object, there was no object — put_new.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R048 -->
In the write of the revision object, it exists with the same bytes — do not write.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R049 -->
In the write of the revision object, it exists with other bytes — the pointer does not name it (rev of the object is higher than
rev of the pointer: the former edit broke off between the object and the pointer), the object is nobody's — put_at over it.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R050 -->
In the write of the revision object, the turn is lost between the read and the confirmation — 503 to the edit by P/08.10 N1.7, the object is not touched.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R051 -->
In the write of the revision object, put_new or put_at got conflict — the object changed after get_at, another edit or another process writes it: 409 to the edit «the shared
settings changed while this edit was made — look again», the object is not touched — the losing edit does not
overwrite the object of the winning one.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R052 -->
`after_move`: the new holder (P/08.8 N1.8 step 8) re-signs the same content as rev + 1, by "domain" — it has another
issuing key.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R053 -->
After a move, when the sum matched at no copy, the new holder does not re-sign (P/08.8 N1.8 step 8).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R054 -->
After a move, at the exception of step 3 the pointer goes in the batch of step 8 (P/08.8 N1.8).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R055 -->
At a planned handover the issuing key is the same, the document does not change.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R056 -->
`readers`: the process domain, the domain console, the subsystem worker on the domain (the role `<sub>domain`) read the pointer domain/shared
(the exact name) and the objects domain/shared-docs/rev-* (the family domain/* of the object door of P/05).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R057 -->
The cluster console reads the row domain/shared (the exact name: at the holder
the pointer, at a member the copy row, N1.4; P/02.5 N1.4 console).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R058 -->
The subsystem worker by worker.reads domain/shared reads at a member the copy row and the object domain/shared,
at the holder's cluster the pointer and the object by its field object.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R059 -->
The subsystem worker checks the sum and the signature itself; a mismatch — the former value.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R060 -->
There are no projections of the shared document by subsystems.

### N1.3 Edit

<!-- req: P/08.9-R004 -->
```yaml
door:            PUT /domain/shared {base_rev?, shared: {<sub>: {<name>: <value> | null}}} and If-Match
order:           1, 1a, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
answer:          200 {term, rev, by}, ETag
```

<!-- req: P/08.9-R061 -->
`door`: the door `PUT /domain/shared` is at the domain console (admin, P/08.4;
lesson P/10 N1.3) → the process domain by the same path (chapter 8.0).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R062 -->
The body of `PUT /domain/shared` is ≤ 1 MiB (otherwise 413 {error: "refused"}).

The steps of `order`:

<!-- req: P/08.9-R063 -->
1. Freeze, deposition — P/08.8 (503 frozen, 409 deposed).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R064 -->
1a. The headers and the repeat memory (chapter 8.0 N1.9).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R065 -->
2. The body is not `{base_rev?: integer ≥ 0, shared: object, every shared.<sub> — object}`, neither If-Match nor base_rev, or they
   differ (chapter 8.0 N1.9 «base_rev») — 400
   `{error: "refused", detail: "the edit is {base_rev: <the revision it was made against>, shared: {<sub>: {<field>: value or null}}}"}`.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R066 -->
   In step 2, `shared.<sub>` not an object is refused also here, before base_rev.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R067 -->
3. The revision before the content: If-Match is not equal to the version of the pointer — 412 revision moved.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R068 -->
   In step 3, no header and base_rev ≠ rev of the pointer (0 — no document) — 409 {error: "refused", detail: "the shared settings are at
   rev N; the edit was made against rev M — look again"}.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R069 -->
4. The content — all refusals in one 400 joined by «; ».

<!-- req: P/08.9-R070 -->
   In step 4, a refusal of the content: «`shared settings for '<sub>': no subsystem on the domain shares a field by that name`».

<!-- req: P/08.9-R071 -->
   In step 4, a refusal of the content: «`'<name>' is not in its domain.shared [<the names of domain.shared of the manifest joined by «, », ascending>]`».

<!-- req: P/08.9-R072 -->
   In step 4, the type of the field — the same check
   of the form as at the unit door (P/06.1 N3 «create» step 4), on this one field, the refusal word — its.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R073 -->
   In step 4, the value of a document is checked against its schema (the words of the schema).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R074 -->
   In step 4, a value the console would seal (P/06.2 N3) (a name *_secret at any depth,
   a password found in an address by P/06.2 N1.1a) — «`'<name>': the shared document is signed, not sealed`».

<!-- req: P/08.9-R075 -->
5. The new document: the former shared, over it — the values of the edit; rev + 1, term — its own, at, by.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R076 -->
   In step 5, null — remove the name.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R077 -->
   In step 5, an empty subsystem — remove.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R078 -->
   In step 5, the former document (the object the pointer names) does not read
   or is not with its sha256 — 503 {error: "refused", detail: "the shared document at rev N does not read: nothing
   is written"}, nothing is written: an edit over an empty one would lose the former values.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R079 -->
6. Write (N1.2 write); the target write is the pointer, the object goes before it and is repeated without harm (chapter 8.0 N1.9 write).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R080 -->
   In step 6, CAS failed — 409 «the shared settings changed while this edit was made — look again».

<!-- req: P/08.9-R081 -->
`answer`: 200 {term, rev, by}, ETag — the index of the pointer.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R082 -->
With the answer — the log domain.shared.edited {term, rev, by}.

### N1.4 Delivery to a member — the holder's side

<!-- req: P/08.9-R005 -->
```yaml
carry:           shared: {pointer: {term, rev, sha256}, doc?: <text>}
holder_self:     the pointer domain/shared and the object domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>
```

<!-- req: P/08.9-R083 -->
`carry`: the holder's answer to the agent (P/08.3 N1.5) carries `shared`.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R084 -->
`carry.doc` is sent only when domain/shared from the member's copy ≠ the pointer, or the copy carries the field object: this is a row of a former holder (P/08.3 N3 «read the copy»).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R085 -->
`holder_self`: at the holder's cluster the row domain/shared is the pointer (N1.2), and the document is the object `domain/shared-docs/rev-<rev>`.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R086 -->
The agent of the holder does not take the document.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R087 -->
The holder's console reads the pointer (the door
GET /domain/shared/{sub} of the OpenAPI of lesson 6).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R088 -->
The agent side — `member` (take the document in order, the object and the row `domain/shared`, a refusal — the row
`domain/shared-refused`) and `in_report` (the rows `domain/shared` and `domain/shared-refused` are report objects) — [chapter 9.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md)
(N1.10, N3 «take the document»).

<!-- req: P/08.9-R089 -->
The holder reads these objects from the member's report for the delivery (N1.6).

### N1.6 Delivered to how many

<!-- req: P/08.9-R006 -->
```yaml
domain/view.shared:  {term, rev, holding, behind, refused, silent, sentence}       # P/08.1 N1.4
  holding:     [<member>…]
  behind:      {<member>: {term, rev}}
  refused:     {<member>: <reason>}
  silent:      [<member>…]
  sentence:    «rev R on H of T members[; not taken yet: …][; not answering: …][; refused by: <member> (<reason>), …]»
  before:      term 0, rev 0, holding [], behind {}, refused {}, silent, sentence «rev 0 on 0 of T members»
```

<!-- req: P/08.9-R090 -->
`holding`: the member's copy ≥ (term, rev) of the pointer; the holder is included.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R091 -->
`behind`: a member that holds an older one, with its {term, rev}.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R092 -->
In `behind`, a member without a copy — {term: 0, rev: 0}, not null.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R093 -->
`refused`: domain/shared-refused of the member with (term, rev) of the pointer.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R094 -->
`silent`: the member is not ok by the view (chapter 8.1); this rule goes before the other rules.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R095 -->
`before`: before the first document (no pointer) the value is term 0, rev 0, holding [], behind {}, refused {}, silent — by the view,
sentence «rev 0 on 0 of T members» with the same tails.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R096 -->
The field view.shared exists from the first pass of the holder — before the first document with the value before.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R097 -->
The door `GET /domain/shared` at the domain console (`view`) → `200 {doc, delivery, declared}`: `delivery` is `view.shared` above.

<!-- req: P/08.9-R098 -->
`doc` (the document as signed — the object of N1.2 | `null`) and `declared` (the items `domain.shared` of the manifests of the catalogue with `schema` at the
document) of `GET /domain/shared` — lesson P/10 ([N1.2f](../../10-domain-console/spec/10-domain-console.norms.md), P/10-14).

### Data families (built from the standard files)

The form of the rows and objects that the chapter owns by the [catalog](../../common/data/catalog.tsv) is the JSON Schema 2020-12 of the family. An instance of a row is `{key, row}`, of an object `{key, object}`; the key is the grammar of the column `key` of the catalog. The fields of `row` are the values of `items` after the [codec](../../common/data/codec.tsv): the writer checks the schema and its `$defs.writer`, the reader the schema (layer 3 — the words `x-*` of the schema). Unreadable — the row did not decode or did not pass the schema in the reader's mode; the outcome is by the table of outcomes of the family: the path is a prefix of the path of the refusal (empty — the whole row); with several paths of refusal the outcome of the least priority applies.
