### Н1.1 The member, the member key, admission (the holder's side)

<!-- req: P/08.3-R003 -->

The member's agent (role `domainagent`), the member key `domain/member-key` (`pub` is a point of the Ed25519 curve not of small order),
the command `join` and the member's copies `domain/member-list/<раздел>`, `domain/member` are the member's side: [P/09.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.1.
The fingerprint of the member key and of the root is [chapter 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md) Н1.1.

```yaml
part:           the first 12 bits of SHA-256(UTF-8 of the member name), a decimal number of four digits, 0000…4095; a function of the name, there is no list of parts (ADR-0113 addendum (1), (3))
domain/member-rows/<раздел>: {doc: <JSON text>}   # at the holder; writer — the process domain (chapter 8.0), CAS of this row; the list is the union of the rows
  doc:          {domain, part, rev, term, members: {<член>: Entry}, gathered?, new?, issuer, kid, sig}    # the signature and the reader's check — the term record contract «The members row»; gathered, new — chapter 8.8 Н1.8а
  part:         the part of this row; the part of every name equals part, otherwise the row is not valid
  rev:          an integer ≥ 1, +1 on every write of this row
  term:         the term of the holder that wrote the row (chapter 8.8 Н1.1; installation — 1); the pair (term, rev) names the row
                unambiguously, after a transfer too (Н1.4а)
  freshness:    the reader — the term record contract «The members row» take; kid in revoked_issuing — the row is not valid, it waits for re-signing
  re-signing:   a new term re-signs all non-empty parts, raising rev, no faster than REPORT_RATE rows per second
  Entry:        {how, since, class?, by?, key?, revoked_keys?, ca?, url?, via?, batch?}   # ca — Н1.6; how of the holder — chapter 8.8
    class:      server — a cluster with servers | camera — a camera cluster: Н1.1а; no field — server: so the entries of the holder, of a former holder and of old domains are read; a change — leaving and a new admission
    url:        the entry address from the object domain/servers after the check (chapter 8.1 Н1.4), at most 256 bytes
    via:        the member's relay (Н1.2); batch — the admission batch
    how:        "admitted" | "holder" | "held the domain until term <N>" — where the member in the list comes from (chapter 8.8 Н1.2 — the two
                last ones); the name of the person is only in by, it is not duplicated
    since:      wall-clock seconds of the holder that wrote the entry
    by:         the person who admitted the member (chapter 8.4; before it — operator, P/06.3 Н1.8 `loopback`); only at admitted
    key:        hex of the key of a member admitted by fingerprint; at holder — the key from the first report of its agent (chapter 8.8)
    revoked_keys: [<hex>…] — the former keys of this name, revoked (chapter 8.2 Н3 «change after theft» step 3а), in the order of revocation: the oldest
                  first, the form names the order itself (the common rule of arrays does not apply; C08-REVOKED-KEYS-ORDER-01); at most the 4 last ones,
                  Entry ≤ ENTRY_MAX 1240 bytes of JCS (chapter 8.10 Н1.5; added by М11/12)

                layout audit, the Architect's decision of 2026-10-25, Б-12)
```

**Admission is two-sided.** On the member the operator pins the root (`join`, [P/09.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.1); on the holder the operator enters
the member key (`POST /domain/members {name, fingerprint}`, Н2), having compared the fingerprint with the one the member's agent printed at
start. The order of the sides is any. The agent reports without pinning too — so the holder sees the knocking one — but it does not take the
set without a pinned root ([domain key set contract](../../common/contracts/keys.md) «The function `keys`» →
`no_pin`). The fingerprint of the member key and of the root is [chapter 8.2](08.2-domain.norms.md) Н1.1.

The member's copy `domain/member-topology` is the body of `GET /api/topology/<член>` (Н1.2 `carry`) as it is, taken at a new `sha256` of the field `topology` of the holder's answer: the agent's side,
[P/09.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.4.

Which road the agent reports by is decided by its environment (`DOMAIN_URL` or `RELAY_URL`), not by the topology; the topology
tells the holder and the relay whom to **accept** through whom. A member whose report came by the wrong road is rejected
(Н3 «the holder on POST /api/relay»; the relay door — [chapter 9.2](../../09-agent/spec/09.2-agent.norms.md) Н2); a direct report of a member whom the topology leads through
a relay is accepted by the holder — this is the second road (Н1.3а).

### Н1.3 Generation and mark

<!-- req: P/08.3-R007 -->

What the agent needs from this subsection (the mark — `sha256` of the JCS of the whole, `MARK_REFRESH`, `BODY_LIMIT`) is also the [report reception contract](../../common/contracts/accept.md) «Generation, mark, limits» (analysis of review of stage B 8–10, № 4: lesson 9 reads the contract, this chapter owns the text).

```yaml
  whole:        {sent_at, objects, key, keys_rev?, pinned?, outcomes?}   # objects — all objects of the member after applying the
                change; outcomes — P/09.1 Н1.5: a change of outcomes is a new generation, the agent sends the field of outcomes of the edits still named by the holder (OUTCOME_HOLD) in every
                publication while the row domain/outcomes is not empty; no field — no outcomes

  # generation fields

  sha256:       hex sha256 of the bytes of generation — sha256 of the JCS of the whole whole
  base:         the rev against which the change came; 0 — a whole came
  delta_sha256: hex sha256 of the bytes of delta (at base 0 — of the body of the whole, as it came)
  signature?:   the header X-Agent-Signature of the direct report that created the generation, as it came (Н1.4); through a relay
                there is no field (Н1.3а); via is a field of the last request, so a mark without signature and without via is lawful:
                the generation came through a relay, then a direct heard (the Architect's decision on the reconciliation of М11, A14)
  key, keys_rev?, pinned?: from the publication — by them the domain console admits the member and shows the pinning
  # fields of the last accepted request
  sent_at:      sent_at of the publication; received_at — wall-clock seconds of the accepting holder
  request_sha256: hex sha256 of the bytes of the body of this request, as it came; a repeat is recognized by (sent_at, request_sha256)
  via?:         the relay — Н1.3а
  stale:        from the publication at every acceptance, «heard» included (report reception contract, step 6); not in the publication — []
  sign_failures?: from the publication at every acceptance with a write of the mark, as stale; it creates no generation and does not
                refresh the mark by itself; not in the publication — no field (Н1.6а; P/09.1 Н1.12; the Architect's decision on the reconciliation of М11, A7, W1)
  every?:       s — the period told to the member by the last answer with a write of the mark (Н1.5; the report reception contract «The answer `carry`: `every`»); none — REPORT_EVERY

MARK_REFRESH:   22.5        # s = LOST_AFTER/2 (chapter 8.1 Н1.3) by the holder's monotonic clock
BODY_LIMIT:     forms/runtime-constants.tsv      # the body limit of the door of the holder and of the relay: larger — 413, the body is not read further; it is also
                            # the limit of the JCS of the whole of step 4а of the report reception contract and of the whole that the agent does not send (P/09.1 Н1.2 body)
```

The mark is the boundary of what is accepted: an object with a number not above `reported.rev` is immutable; an object above it belongs to nobody: it is not read, not
transferred, does not hold the number, the next acceptance writes over it. The readers (the view — chapter 8.1, the book collector — chapter 8.5,
the backup — chapter 8.8) take `reported.rev` and read only the object of this generation; a mark without an object or an object
not with its `sha256` — the member is `silent` (chapter 8.1 Н3 step 2), never a mixture.

The publication — the body of `POST /api/member/<член>` (a closed form, `member_objects`, the fields outside the delta, `ready`, `change`) — is
[P/09.1](../../09-agent/spec/09.1-agent.norms.md) Н1.2; the schema of the door body is the [lesson contract](08-domain.api.openapi.yaml).

### accept(mark, stored, body, via, heard_ago) — the holder, on `POST /api/member/<член>` after the signature

<!-- req: P/08.3-R016 -->

The paragraph on `via` and `heard_ago`, steps 0–7 and the paragraph on `sent_at`, the answer and a failure are the [report reception contract](../../common/contracts/accept.md) «The function «accept»»
(owner P/08, chapter 8.3; participant — the relay 9.2; moved there without a change, the step numbers are the former ones;

The holder executes it on `POST /api/member/<член>` and for each member of a relay batch (Н3 «the holder on POST
/api/relay»); the answer is carry (Н1.5).

The input of the function is read by the holder; what is not known is not «no». If the mark `reported` is not readable (Г-29), or its read, like
the read of the generation `reports/<mark.rev>`, is incomplete — the object is not among those that answered, or it was found with `X-Missing`, the resource door
did not answer ([object client contract](../../common/contracts/object-client.md) «missing»): what is found at an incomplete poll
may be not fresh, — the function is not called, 503 `{error: "unavailable"}`, nothing is written, a journal entry without the value;
substituting absence would give a false rebase or a generation over it (C08-ACCEPT-PARTIAL-READ-01). The write of step 7 failed before
the write of the mark — the same 503: nothing is accepted, the objects above the mark belong to nobody. The mark is written, but the cleanup of step 7 (`reports/<rev−2>`,
its `.delta`, the objects above `rev`) failed — the report is accepted: the answer carry, the cleanup failure is a diagnostic of the process, the remainder
is removed by the next acceptance (`keep`) (C08-ACCEPT-CLEANUP-01).

The doors of agents (`POST /api/member/<член>`, `GET /api/carry/<член>`) read the rows that the answer asserts:
`domain/member-rows/<раздел>` (the member, its key and the revocation of the key), `domain/keys` and `domain/revoked` (the fields of carry) — before the checks of the member and
the key (the order of the row of Н2 and of the report reception contract «The check of the member and the key» is the former one); the mark `reported` (for carry — the `rev`
of the road, Н1.5) — after a successful signature, before «accept» or the answer carry: a stranger gets its 404, 403 or 401 also with
an unreadable mark (C08-AGENT-DOOR-ORDER-01). The row exists but is not readable, or the read is not confirmed (an incomplete poll,
the store did not answer) — 503 `{error: "unavailable"}`: not 404, not 401, not `rev` 0 or the former one, not an omitted field; no knock,
no acceptance and no write, a journal entry without the value (П1; as Г-29 and C08-ACCEPT-PARTIAL-READ-01). A field of carry whose row does not exist is
omitted by Н1.5 (C08-CARRY-UNREADABLE-01).

A write of these doors (the generation and the mark — report reception contract steps 6, 7; the holder in the list step 2; `on_accept` — the row
`ca-certs` and `Entry.ca`) with the guard without an answer — an unknown outcome — is a barrier (chapter 8.10 Н1.7): it does not count as success,
the processing is interrupted, the answer is 503 `{error: "unavailable"}`, for a relay batch — to the whole batch; what was accepted before it is not
rolled back: a repeat of the agent with the same `sent_at` — same or heard, the answer carry. The guard refused — the process is standby, the answer is
503 `standby` (to the whole batch). A confirmed write failure after the mark (`on_accept`, not an unknown outcome and not a refusal of
the guard) — the report is accepted: the answer carry, the failure is a diagnostic of the process without the value, `on_accept` repeats the write at the next
accepted report (`tls_ca` ≠ `Entry.ca`); a failure before the mark — 503 `unavailable` (C08-ACCEPT-CLEANUP-01) (C08-AGENT-WRITE-AMBIGUOUS-01).
