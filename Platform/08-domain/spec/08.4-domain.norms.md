**Deletion writer key context.** The existing key `domain/deleting/role:<name>` selects empty writer items `{}` with no `at`, `done` or person-token hold. A person key selects `{at,done?}`, with required `at`; `{}` is not a positive person-writer form. The two local schema definitions `person_writer` and `role_writer` in the deleting schema require that key-aware selection before writer decoding and checking; an unkeyed union is not proof. Readers retain the existing presence/denial/recovery rule even for garbled mark content.

The common writer name limit is interpreted with the properties of the key-selected writer profile; it does not make the required person `at` universal. The existing unkeyed consumer binding is an explicit dependency, not a permissive fallback.

The references of the block: the list of domain doors and forwarding — the [forwarding contract](../../common/contracts/forwarding.md); `open_routes`,
`login_url`, `/session` — the [gate and pass contract](../../common/contracts/gate-and-pass.md) «The gate»
([P/06.3](../../06-console/spec/06.3-console.norms.md) N1.3); the token and `token` — the [domain token
contract](../../common/contracts/domain-token.md).

**Login time origin (P08 review C5).** When the enabled credential row is confirmed, capture its wall time `credential_at` and a monotonic start before the unlocked PBKDF2 verification. A successful person token uses `iat = credential_at`, `exp = credential_at + TOKEN_LIFETIME`, and response `until = exp`; signing, a later read or a retry cannot refresh this origin. After password verification and immediately before issuance, if the monotonic elapsed duration is at least TOKEN_LIFETIME or wall now is at least exp, return 401 `{error: "refused", detail: "login took too long; try again"}` without a token; a new request rereads its credential and starts anew. A delay after the final time check still cannot extend iat or exp. DELETE and later recreation of the same name therefore cannot turn a suspended verification of the old password into a fresh token for the new person. Ordinary login is unchanged, and keep/kind token issuance is not changed by this login-specific origin.

The revocation window at a member without connection is two limits, separately (the Architect's decision on R41-13 of run 41):
- **an already issued token** of a disabled person or a revoked one — `TOKEN_LIFETIME + TOKEN_SKEW` = 960 s from issue;
- **a grant** removed at the holder, at a member without connection — `GRANT_LIFETIME` (a day) from `as_of` of the copy: a person who was not
  disabled logs in again and with a new token uses the former copy while it is not stale.

The numbers are declared in advance (M12A/04 step 5).

**Public supervision (P08 review C4).** POST `/domain/roles` and PUT `/domain/roles/{name}` accept the closed optional field `supervision: {<sub>: <role>}`; GET `/domain/roles` returns that field when stored. On POST omission or `{}` means no supervision. On PUT omission preserves the old map, `{}` clears it (remove the stored field), and a nonempty map replaces it whole; `null` is 400 bad body. The same rules apply to `fields.supervision` of a role put in POST `/domain/batch`, over its final snapshot. A missing non-self supervisor role is 400 unknown role; an unreadable referenced role is 503 unavailable. Self-reference is allowed, including creation; it needs no pre-existing self row. All other referenced roles are guarded at their read versions. A request to set or clear supervision on builtin admin is 409 builtin. A stored reference from another role prevents DELETE with 409 role referenced; a self-reference leaves with the role. Reads and writes remain ordinary role operations with the same rights, CAS, counters and repeat memory; no manual store edit is required to enable or remove supervision.

A break of step 9 between the person row and `domain/disabled` or the grants rows: the login is already closed to the person (the row), while the members

the mark are removed, the person stays without grants, the deletion is finished by a repeated `DELETE` (C08-DELETING-USER-ROW-01). It does not look for «people who do not exist»
by the absence of a row: a subject of a name index is not a person of the domain either (log of cammeta-go, M11-09 № 1; the Architect's decision).

A subject without a name (C08-GRANTS-ORPHAN-01; the Architect's decision of 2026-10-29 on the request of VMS, analysis of V/03, question 2). By the same pass, after the name indexes of the pass (chapter 8.5 N3

books with `domain.names` — all are known and empty) and the list of people is read: the indexes of this pass are not built (a failure of a part of the pass, chapter 8.1 «Races»; the window after a taken term, chapter 8.5 N1.3), an index is not known, the list of people or an index row is not

CAS of the grants row by the read version; a conflict — reread and repeat at most three times, the rest — by the next
