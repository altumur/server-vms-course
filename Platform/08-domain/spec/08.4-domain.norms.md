### N1.1 Person

<!-- req: P/08.4-R003 -->
```yaml
domain/users/<name>:  {name, how, pwhash_secret, disabled, title?, email?, expires?, pw_by?, at, by, revision}   # at the holder
  name:        the person's name
  how:         "password"
  pwhash_secret: the password hash
  disabled:    true | false
  title:       the display name
  email:       a string
  expires:     0 | wall-clock seconds
  pw_by:       the name of whoever last set the password
  at, by:      wall-clock seconds and the name of the person of the last edit
  revision:    the edit counter
PASSWORD_MIN:  forms/runtime-constants.tsv          # characters
domain/disabled: {names: <JCS text of the array of names>}
delete:        the person row and his grant lines
domain/deleting/<name>: {at, done?}    # the deletion mark of a person
unreadable:    a person row that does not read
```

<!-- req: P/08.4-R038 -->
`domain/users/<name>` is at the holder; the writer is the process `domain`.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R039 -->
`name`: the person's name — the form and the white-space characters — the person name contract common/contracts/person-name.md `name`,
`white_space`; out of form — 400 `bad name`.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R040 -->
`how`: the lesson sets no norm for other ways of login.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R041 -->
`pwhash_secret`: the form, the hash and the check — the password hash contract common/contracts/password-hash.md. `pwhash_secret` is a secret by the suffix (P/02.1 N1.3a), sealed by the holder's ring.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R043 -->
`title`: the display name, a string up to `GROUP_TITLE_MAX` characters; no field — `""`.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R044 -->
`email`: a string of 0..254 characters; no field — `""`. `email`: the platform does not check the address form and sends no mail.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R046 -->
`expires`: 0 — no term; otherwise wall-clock seconds; no field — 0. `expires`: now ≥ expires — the person is «disabled by term».

<!-- req: P/08.4-R048 -->
`pw_by`: the name of whoever last set the password (proven by the gate; at first-admin — the person being created); no field — «set by a non-administrator».

<!-- req: P/08.4-R049 -->
`pw_by` is written at first-admin,
`POST /domain/users`, a password change.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R050 -->
`at`, `by`: wall-clock seconds and the name of the person of the last edit (proven by the gate).

<!-- req: P/08.4-R051 -->
`revision`: chapter 8.0 N1.9 «Versions»: 1 at creation, +1 on every write of the row.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R052 -->
The writer of `domain/users/<name>` does not write a field with the default value (`title`, `email`, `expires`).

<!-- req: P/08.4-R053 -->
`PASSWORD_MIN` counts characters: Unicode code points, not bytes.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R054 -->
`domain/disabled`: the names of the disabled ones ascending in UTF-8 bytes, without repeats; none — `"[]"`; an array, not a string joined by «,»: a name can carry a comma. `domain/disabled`: the writer is the process `domain` at every edit of `disabled` and by the reconciliation at every pass (N3 «expiry»). `domain/disabled`: a name is here if `disabled = true` or now ≥ `expires`: disabled by term = disabled. `domain/disabled`: the login (N1.2 `check`), `may` step 2 and `carry` judge by `domain/disabled` and the field `disabled`, they do not read `expires` directly.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R058 -->
`domain/disabled`: the window «the term has come, there was no pass yet» is no longer than a pass. `domain/disabled` carries no secrets.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R060 -->
`delete`: delete the person row and remove his lines in all `domain/grants/*` (N1.3) — with one edit per grants row. `delete` goes under the deletion mark below (N3 «people door» step 9).

<!-- req: P/08.4-R062 -->
`domain/deleting/<name>`: the deletion mark of a person at the holder; the writer is the process `domain` under the guard `domain/signer-turn`. `domain/deleting/<name>`: the mark stands from the start of `DELETE` until all grant lines with `subject = <name>` are removed and also until `at + TOKEN_LIFETIME + TOKEN_SKEW`: the token of the deleted one lives that long, a new person of this name does not answer for it. `domain/deleting/<name>`: `at` — the moment the mark is written; `done` — the lines are removed. `domain/deleting/<name>`: no `at` or it does not read, at a role mark too, — no term.

<!-- req: P/08.4-R066 -->
`domain/deleting/<name>`: while it stands the name is taken: `POST /domain/users` — 409 `exists`, a new grant line with this subject — 409 `being deleted`. `domain/deleting/<name>`: the reconciliation at start and at every pass of chapter 8.1 removes the grant lines by it and deletes the mark when there are no lines and the term is over.

### Data families (built from the standard files)

The form of the rows and objects that the chapter owns by the [catalog](../../common/data/catalog.tsv) is the JSON Schema 2020-12 of the family. An instance of a row is `{key, row}`, of an object `{key, object}`; the key is the grammar of the column `key` of the catalog. The fields of `row` are the values of `items` after the [codec](../../common/data/codec.tsv): the writer checks the schema and its `$defs.writer`, the reader the schema (layer 3 — the words `x-*` of the schema). Unreadable — the row did not decode or did not pass the schema in the reader's mode; the outcome is by the table of outcomes of the family: the path is a prefix of the path of the refusal (empty — the whole row); with several paths of refusal the outcome of the least priority applies.
